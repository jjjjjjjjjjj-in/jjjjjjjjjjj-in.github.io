Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

No homework needed

It's an origin story for a new hero, with very little Avengers material.

When is it set?

According to Marvel Studios' official timeline book, the story begins after Ant-Man, Strange trains around the time of Civil War, and the final battle happens before Black Widow.

It's about magic

Instead of science and armor, there are spells and other dimensions. Reading the key terms first helps.

A visual feast

Buildings twist and cities fold like kaleidoscopes. Best on a big screen.

There are scenes during and after the credits

The mid-credits scene leads into Thor: Ragnarok.

StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

1. The brilliant surgeon

Strange is a gifted but arrogant surgeon who looks down on people, even his colleague and ex, Christine.

2. The crash

He crashes his car on a mountain road at night and badly damages the nerves in his hands. Surgery after surgery fails, his hands still shake, and he loses everything.

3. To Kathmandu

Hearing of a man who recovered from an incurable injury, Strange travels to Kathmandu, where Mordo takes him to the Ancient One.

4. Learning magic

Shown other dimensions with his own eyes, Strange begins to study magic. With his photographic memory he devours the library and improves fast.

5. Kaecilius attacks

The Ancient One's former student Kaecilius tries to destroy the three Sanctums to bring in Dormammu of the Dark Dimension. Strange fights him at the New York Sanctum, and the cloak chooses him.

6. Battle in the Mirror Dimension

Strange and Mordo fight Kaecilius in a New York that twists and folds around them.

7. Showdown in Hong Kong

The Hong Kong Sanctum has fallen and Dormammu is arriving. Strange uses the power to turn back time and goes to bargain with Dormammu.

8. Strange the sorcerer

Strange gives up surgery and stays on as the sorcerer protecting the New York Sanctum.

Key terms

Kamar-Taj
The training compound of the sorcerers, in Kathmandu, Nepal.
The Ancient One
Leader of the sorcerers, said to have lived for centuries.
The Sanctums
Three buildings in New York, London and Hong Kong that shield Earth from other dimensions.
Sling ring
Worn on the fingers, it opens a portal to a faraway place when you trace a circle in the air.
The Mirror Dimension
A mirror-image copy of the real world. Fighting there doesn't damage reality.
The Dark Dimension
A realm of darkness where time doesn't exist, ruled by Dormammu.
The Eye of Agamotto
An amulet with the power to control time. It holds a green stone.
The Cloak of Levitation
A red cloak with a mind of its own that chooses Strange. It lets him fly.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

What was the Ancient One hiding?

To protect the world, she secretly drew a little power from the Dark Dimension to prolong her life. Mordo can't forgive that.

How does Strange beat Dormammu?

With the Eye of Agamotto, he traps them both in a time loop. Dormammu, who lives outside time, is stuck in a moment that never ends, and agrees to leave Earth alone.

Why does Mordo leave?

Seeing magic used to bend the laws of nature, he loses faith in the sorcerers' ways.

What's the stone in the Eye?

It's the Time Stone, one of the six Infinity Stones.

Easy-to-miss details

Strange's watch

Strange keeps wearing the watch Christine gave him, even after his hands are ruined.

Wong and the Wi-Fi

When Strange asks for the Wi-Fi password, the librarian Wong replies, "We're not savages."

Stan Lee's cameo

Marvel Comics legend Stan Lee appears as a bus passenger laughing at a book.
