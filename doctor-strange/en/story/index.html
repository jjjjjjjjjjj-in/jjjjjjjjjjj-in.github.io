Part 1: The stolen pages

1-1The library at Kamar-Taj

Kamar-Taj, a hidden training ground for sorcerers in Kathmandu, Nepal. The sorcerer Kaecilius and his followers, the Zealots, slip into the library.

Kaecilius was once a student of the Ancient One. He beheads the librarian and tears pages describing a forbidden ritual out of a book belonging to the Ancient One, the Book of Cagliostro.

1-2The chase through the Mirror Dimension

Kaecilius and his followers escape to a distant city through a portal. The Ancient One follows and fights them in the Mirror Dimension, a world where nothing seems to happen in reality, and where buildings and streets fold like a kaleidoscope.

In the end, Kaecilius and the Zealots get away.

Part 2: A surgeon's fall

2-1Strange the star surgeon

A hospital in New York. Neurosurgeon Stephen Strange saves patients other doctors can't and is in demand as a speaker.

He is rich and famous. He lives in a luxury apartment, enjoys music, collects expensive watches and drives a sports car. He also makes no secret that he thinks he's better than everyone.

2-2West's patient

Christine, an ER doctor who is Strange's colleague and ex, calls him to the emergency room. Dr. West, another colleague, has given up on a patient shot in the head.

Strange sees that West is wrong, humiliates him in front of everyone, and removes the bullet to save the patient.

Later Christine criticizes how he treats people. He doesn't listen.

2-3The crash on the mountain road

At night, Strange speeds along a winding mountain road in his sports car, heading to a conference where he is to speak. He is talking on the phone while looking at patients' X-rays on it.

In a moment of distraction his car swerves, tumbles off the road and plunges into the river below. Strange's hands are badly injured.

2-4Shaking hands

He survives, but the nerves in both hands are badly damaged, and his hands are held together with metal pins.

The shaking never stops. He can no longer perform delicate surgery.

2-5Treatment after treatment

Determined to be a surgeon again, Strange tries seven different treatments, but his hands don't recover. He sells his belongings to pay for it, and his savings run out.

Doctors around the world give up on him, and he grows bitter.

2-6Christine walks away

Christine tries to support Strange and urges him to think about a different life.

Instead, he takes his anger out on her and says cruel things. Christine leaves.

Though he sells nearly everything of value, Strange keeps the watch Christine gave him. Her message is engraved on the back.

2-7The man who walked again

Strange learns of Jonathan Pangborn, a man paralyzed by a spinal injury who somehow walked again. He was a patient Strange himself once turned away as hopeless.

Strange finds Pangborn playing basketball. Pangborn tells him that after the doctors gave up on him, he was healed at a place in Kathmandu called Kamar-Taj.

Strange spends the last of his money to travel to Nepal.

Part 3: Kathmandu

3-1Muggers and Mordo

Searching Kathmandu for Kamar-Taj, Strange is cornered in an alley by muggers who want his watch. They beat him badly; the watch face cracks and it stops.

A hooded man appears and drives them off. It is Mordo, a student of the Ancient One. He takes Strange to Kamar-Taj.

3-2The Ancient One

Strange meets the Ancient One, the head of Kamar-Taj. When she talks about the mind and body, Strange the doctor scoffs that he doesn't believe in such things.

So she gives him a light push. His spirit, his astral form, is knocked out of his body, and he sees himself from outside.

Then he is hurled through one bizarre dimension after another, swirling with colors and shapes. He is shown that beyond this world there are many others: the multiverse.

3-3Waiting at the door

Back in the room, Strange begs her to teach him. She refuses: his pride and ambition remind her of Kaecilius, the student who left.

Strange is put out and the door is shut. Still, he refuses to give up and waits outside. Seeing his persistence, the Ancient One takes him in as a student.

3-4Wong and the library

Strange meets Wong, the keeper of the library. The previous librarian was killed by Kaecilius, and now Wong guards the books. The most dangerous ones are kept apart where students can't read them.

After Strange mentions the singer Beyoncé, Wong later takes to listening to her songs.

Part 4: Learning magic

4-1The sling ring and the mountain

Training begins with opening portals using a sling ring. The other students manage, but Strange's shaking hands hold him back.

The Ancient One sends him through a portal onto a high snowy mountain and leaves him there. Close to freezing, Strange finally opens a portal himself and tumbles back into Kamar-Taj, covered in snow.

4-2Fast progress

With a memory that never forgets what he reads, Strange studies the library's books day and night. He trains with Mordo as well, and within months he outpaces the other students.

But as a former doctor, he hesitates to fight in a way that hurts people. Mordo scolds him for it.

4-3The Eye of Agamotto

Strange secretly reads the Book of Cagliostro, the book Kaecilius tore pages from.

It describes how to control time. Using an amulet called the Eye of Agamotto, Strange turns a half-eaten apple back into a whole one.

4-4Mordo and Wong's warning

Mordo and Wong walk in and stop him sharply. Tampering with time, they say, breaks the laws of nature and is dangerous.

They point to Kaecilius, who strayed from the path in search of eternal life, as a warning.

4-5The three Sanctums and Dormammu

Wong explains how Earth is protected. Three buildings called Sanctums, in New York, London and Hong Kong, create a shield against threats from other dimensions. All three can be reached directly through doors in Kamar-Taj. Just as the Avengers protect the world from physical dangers, the sorcerers protect it from mystical ones.

In the Dark Dimension, a realm without time, rules Dormammu. Kaecilius means to use the ritual on the stolen pages to bring Dormammu to Earth.

Strange also learns that Pangborn chose not to protect the world, and uses magic only to heal his own body.

Part 5: The New York Sanctum

5-1London falls

Using the stolen ritual, Kaecilius connects with Dormammu and destroys the London Sanctum. One of Earth's shields is gone.

5-2The attack on New York

Next, Kaecilius attacks the New York Sanctum. Strange goes through the door into the New York Sanctum.

Daniel Drumm, the sorcerer guarding it, is killed by Kaecilius. Strange has to face Kaecilius and the Zealots alone.

5-3The Cloak of Levitation

Cornered, Strange is saved by a red cloak on display in the building, the Cloak of Levitation. It moves of its own will, settles onto his shoulders and lifts him into the air.

Strange fights using the magical objects kept in the Sanctum, and uses a binding relic to pin Kaecilius in place.

5-4Stabbed, to the hospital

But the Zealot Lucian stabs Strange, and he is badly wounded. Strange opens a portal and escapes to his old hospital.

Christine, on duty in the ER, is stunned when he appears out of nowhere, but she starts operating at once.

5-5The invisible fight in the operating room

During the surgery, Strange leaves his body as an astral form. Then Lucian, also in astral form, follows him there.

Their invisible fight makes things in the operating room shake and move. Christine shocks his body with a defibrillator; the jolt powers up his astral form, and he defeats Lucian. Lucian dies.

5-6A doctor who killed

After the surgery, Strange opens a portal and returns to the Sanctum.

A doctor who saved lives, he is shaken at having killed someone, even an enemy. Mordo criticizes him for hesitating to fight.

5-7Kaecilius reveals a secret

Still bound, Kaecilius tells Strange that the Dark Dimension is a realm without time, where no one dies. And the Ancient One herself, he says, has been using the Dark Dimension's power, which she forbids her students, to keep living far beyond a normal life.

When Strange tells Mordo, Mordo, who has always trusted her, is deeply shaken.

Part 6: Losing a teacher

6-1The chase in the Mirror Dimension

Freed, Kaecilius and the Zealots pull Strange and Mordo into the Mirror Dimension. Here, their dark power makes New York's buildings spin, streets fold, and the whole city twist.

Strange and Mordo run for their lives but are cornered.

6-2The Ancient One falls

The Ancient One arrives and fights the Zealots.

But Kaecilius mortally wounds her and throws her out of the Mirror Dimension into the real world. She falls from a great height onto the street below. Kaecilius and his followers head for Hong Kong.

6-3To the hospital

Strange and Mordo rush the Ancient One to the hospital, where Christine and West operate to try to save her.

6-4Last lessons in the snow

During the surgery, the Ancient One appears to Strange in astral form. They talk while watching snow fall over the city.

She admits she drew on the Dark Dimension to protect the world until someone could take her place.

She tells him that only Mordo, who holds firmly to the rules, and Strange, who is willing to bend them, together can stop Kaecilius. She leaves him with a choice: heal his hands and return to his old life, or protect the world.

Then her astral form fades, and she dies.

6-5Parting from Christine

Having lost his teacher, Strange talks with Christine at the hospital. Choosing to fight rather than return to medicine, he says goodbye to her and leaves.

Part 7: Showdown in Hong Kong

7-1Too late

When Strange and Mordo reach Hong Kong, the Sanctum is already destroyed and Wong, who was defending it, is dead.

All of Earth's shields are gone. The Dark Dimension spreads across the sky and begins swallowing the city.

7-2Turning back time

Strange uses the Eye of Agamotto to reverse time across Hong Kong. Shattered buildings piece themselves back together, and Wong comes back to life.

Kaecilius and the Zealots attack to stop him.

7-3"I've come to bargain"

Strange flies alone into the Dark Dimension and calls out, "Dormammu, I've come to bargain." The giant face of Dormammu kills him easily.

But Strange is right back where he started. With the Eye, he has trapped himself and Dormammu in a time loop that repeats forever.

However many times he is killed, Strange keeps offering the same bargain.

7-4Dormammu gives in

For Dormammu, who lives where there is no time, an endless repetition is unbearable. At last he accepts the bargain.

The terms: leave Earth and never return, and take Kaecilius and his followers with him. In exchange, Strange undoes the loop.

Kaecilius and the Zealots are dragged into the Dark Dimension. The eternal life they wanted becomes eternal torment under Dormammu. Hong Kong is restored.

Part 8: Afterward

8-1Mordo walks away

Mordo is disgusted that both the Ancient One and Strange broke the laws of nature. He leaves the sorcerers and walks away.

8-2An Infinity Stone

Strange returns the Eye of Agamotto to its place at Kamar-Taj. Wong tells him the stone inside is one of the Infinity Stones.

Strange moves into the New York Sanctum, to guard it and keep studying with Wong.

8-3The cracked watch

In the New York Sanctum, Strange picks up the cracked watch Christine gave him and puts it on without repairing it. There are signs that magic may help him steady his trembling hands.

8-4Mid-credits

Thor comes to the Sanctum. Strange asks why he has brought his brother Loki to Earth. Hearing they are searching for their missing father Odin, Strange agrees to help.

8-5After the credits

Mordo visits Pangborn and drains the magical energy he uses to walk. Pangborn collapses. Mordo says the world has too many sorcerers.

Summary (spoilers through the ending)

Kaecilius's plan

Kaecilius, a former student of the Ancient One, sought eternal life without death and served Dormammu of the Dark Dimension (Parts 1 and 5).

He stole pages from the Book of Cagliostro (Part 1) and used the ritual to destroy Earth's three Sanctums one by one: London fell (Part 5), New York held, and Hong Kong fell (Part 7).

In the end, under the bargain Strange struck with Dormammu, he and his followers were dragged into the Dark Dimension (Part 7).

The path Strange chose

After losing the use of his hands, Strange came to Kamar-Taj to heal them (Parts 2 and 3).

With her last words, the Ancient One told him to choose between healing his hands and returning to his old life, or protecting the world (Part 6). He chose the world and became the sorcerer guarding the New York Sanctum (Part 8).

Meanwhile Mordo, disillusioned by the Ancient One's secret and Strange's methods, left the sorcerers (Parts 5 and 8).

Who died (in order)

- The Kamar-Taj librarian (Part 1): beheaded by Kaecilius when he came to steal the pages.

- Daniel Drumm (Part 5): the sorcerer guarding the New York Sanctum, killed by Kaecilius during the attack.

- Lucian (Part 5): one of Kaecilius's followers. He stabbed Strange and followed him to the hospital in astral form, but was defeated in the operating room by Strange's astral form, powered up by a defibrillator shock.

- The Ancient One (Part 6): mortally wounded by Kaecilius in the Mirror Dimension and thrown into the real world. She died at the hospital after giving Strange her last lessons.

- Wong (Part 7): killed defending the Hong Kong Sanctum, but brought back when Strange reversed time with the Eye of Agamotto.

- Kaecilius and the Zealots (Part 7): not killed, but taken into the Dark Dimension under the bargain with Dormammu.
