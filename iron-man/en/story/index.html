Part 1: Ambush in Afghanistan

1-1The convoy

A U.S. military convoy crosses the Afghan desert. In one vehicle rides Tony Stark, head of the weapons maker Stark Industries.

Drink in hand, Tony cracks jokes with the nervous young soldiers and even poses for a photo when one of them asks.

1-2The ambush

Suddenly the convoy is hit by explosions. It's an ambush by a terrorist group. The soldiers in Tony's vehicle jump out to fight back and are shot down one after another.

Tony hides behind a rock. A missile lands at his feet, stamped with his own company's name: Stark Industries.

It explodes, and Tony goes down with a serious chest wound.

1-3On camera

Tony comes to with a hood over his head, surrounded by armed men in front of a video camera while one of them reads a statement.

The film then jumps back to "36 hours earlier."

1-4An award in Las Vegas

In Las Vegas, an award ceremony honors Tony. A video recaps his life: a boy genius who took over the company of his late father, Howard, at a young age.

His best friend, Air Force officer Rhodey (James Rhodes), is there to present the award, but Tony never shows. Obadiah Stane, his father's longtime partner, accepts it for him.

Tony is gambling in the casino. Rhodey finds him and hands him the award there.

1-5Reporter Christine

Outside the casino, magazine reporter Christine Everhart confronts Tony, asking how he feels about being called a "merchant of death."

Tony breezily answers that weapons are what keep the peace, and takes her home that night.

1-6Pepper the next morning

The next morning, the house's AI, J.A.R.V.I.S., wakes Christine. Tony's assistant, Pepper Potts, shows her out. Pepper runs everything in Tony's freewheeling life, from his schedule to his errands.

Tony is down in his workshop tinkering with a car, long past his departure time. Pushed by Pepper, he finally leaves for his trip to Afghanistan.

1-7Rhodey waits

At the airfield, Rhodey has been waiting for hours. On the company jet the two bicker, but they relax like the old friends they are.

1-8The Jericho

At a base in Afghanistan, Tony demonstrates his new missile, the Jericho, for military officials, pitching it as the kind of weapon you only have to fire once.

One missile splits into many smaller bombs and levels an entire hillside. The officers are stunned.

On the drive back, the ambush from the opening happens.

Part 2: Prisoner in the cave

2-1A magnet in his chest

Tony wakes up in a cave with an electromagnet in his chest, wired to a car battery.

The surgery was done by Yinsen, a doctor also being held prisoner. Tiny fragments from the missile are still in Tony's body and would kill him if they reached his heart. The magnet holds them back.

Yinsen says they once met at a conference in Bern. Tony, who was drunk at the time, doesn't remember.

2-2The Ten Rings' demand

Tony's captors are a cell of the terrorist group called the Ten Rings. They lead him outside the cave, where Stark weapons are piled high. His own company's weapons have ended up in terrorist hands.

They order him to build a Jericho. When he refuses, they repeatedly dunk his head under water.

2-3A secret plan

Tony doesn't believe they'll let him go even if he builds the missile, and Yinsen agrees.

So Tony pretends to accept and gathers materials. His real plan is to build an iron suit to escape. Watched by the guards' cameras, the two get to work.

2-4A small arc reactor

First, using metal taken from the missiles, Tony builds a palm-sized power source, an arc reactor: a shrunk-down version of the big generator at his company's plant.

Once it's in his chest, he no longer needs the car battery. It will also power the suit.

2-5The plans, and Yinsen's question

Tony draws the suit's plans in separate pieces, so that each sheet on its own looks like part of a missile. Bit by bit, the two build the parts.

Between tasks, Yinsen asks Tony if he has a family. When Tony says no, Yinsen calls him a man who has everything and nothing. Yinsen says his own family is in his home village, Gulmira.

2-6Raza's threat

The cell's leader, Raza, visits the cave. Just as Genghis Khan's bow once ruled these lands, he says, now whoever holds the newest Stark weapons rules them.

Frustrated with the slow progress, Raza threatens to put a hot coal in Yinsen's mouth. Tony protects him, saying he needs his assistant. Raza leaves, warning that they're running out of time.

Part 3: Escape from the cave

3-1Powering up the suit

The iron suit, the Mark I, is assembled. Tony climbs in while Yinsen starts it up on a computer, but it will take time before the suit can move.

3-2The plan is discovered

Meanwhile, the captors realize what the two are up to. Soldiers rush the workshop and try to force the door.

A bomb the two had rigged at the door goes off and holds the soldiers back.

3-3Yinsen's sacrifice

To buy time for the suit to come online, Yinsen grabs a gun and runs out into the tunnels, firing as he goes and drawing the soldiers away.

3-4The Mark I moves

The suit comes to life. In the bulky gray armor, Tony marches through the tunnels. Bullets bounce off, and he knocks the soldiers aside with his iron arms.

3-5Yinsen's last words

Further along, Tony finds Yinsen lying on the ground, shot by the soldiers.

Yinsen says his family is already dead, and now he will see them again. He tells Tony, "Don't waste your life," and dies.

3-6Burning the weapons

Outside the cave, Tony uses the flamethrowers in the suit's arms to set the stockpile of Stark weapons ablaze, and the weapons go up in a chain of explosions.

He fires the suit's rockets and shoots into the sky.

3-7Rescued in the desert

But the suit soon crashes in the desert and falls apart. Tony climbs out and walks across the empty sand.

Then a helicopter appears: Rhodey and his team, who have been searching for him. Tony has been a prisoner for about three months.

Part 4: Home, and a change of course

4-1Back in America

Tony's plane lands in the U.S. Pepper is waiting, her eyes red with tears.

Instead of going to the hospital, Tony says he wants a cheeseburger first, and asks for a press conference.

4-2"No more weapons"

At the company press conference, Tony sits down on the floor and invites the reporters to do the same. He talks about his father, then about seeing young soldiers die in front of him, killed by his own company's weapons.

Then he announces that Stark Industries will stop making weapons. The room erupts.

4-3Obadiah and the big reactor

Obadiah objects that the stock will plunge, while acting like a worried father figure.

Tony takes him to see the large arc reactor at the company's plant, which most people had thought was just for show. Tony shows him the small reactor in his chest.

Obadiah warns that the board will try to push Tony out of running the company.

4-4Swapping the reactor

Back home in his basement workshop, Tony decides to replace his chest reactor with an improved one he has built.

Pepper helps. Nervously, she reaches into the opening in his chest, pulls out the old device and puts in the new one.

Tony asks her to throw the old one away.

Part 5: A new suit

5-1In the workshop

Tony holes up in his workshop and starts building a new suit with J.A.R.V.I.S., beginning with hover tests using thrusters in his hands and feet.

Each time he slams into a wall or the ceiling, the robot arm Dum-E, standing by, sprays him with a fire extinguisher.

5-2First flight in the silver suit

The silver test suit, the Mark II, is finished. Tony flies over the coastal city at night, thrilled.

5-3Icing up

Testing how high he can go, Tony climbs higher and higher until the suit ices over and shuts down, and he plummets.

Just before he hits the ground, the ice breaks off and the suit restarts. Back home, he botches the landing, crashes through the roof and lands on a car in his workshop.

5-4Obadiah's news

Obadiah drops by Tony's house with pizza and tells him the board is moving to lock him out of the company.

Tony keeps the new suit to himself.

5-5The red-and-gold Mark III

Tony solves the icing problem and builds the finished suit, gold with a hot-rod red added. The red-and-gold Mark III is complete.

5-6Pepper's gift

Pepper never threw away the old arc reactor. She has had it mounted in a glass case, inscribed "Proof that Tony Stark has a heart."

Part 6: Gulmira

6-1The charity event

Tony makes a surprise appearance at a charity event for firefighters' families. At the entrance he greets an older guest as if he were someone else (a cameo by Marvel's Stan Lee).

Inside, government agent Coulson approaches. He introduces his agency by its long name and says he wants to debrief Tony about his captivity.

6-2A dance with Pepper

Tony finds Pepper in a blue dress and they dance. They step out onto the terrace and nearly kiss. Tony goes to get them drinks.

6-3Christine's photos

While Tony is getting the drinks, Christine shows him photos: the Ten Rings attacking Yinsen's home village, Gulmira, with Stark weapons recently delivered to them. Tony had no idea.

6-4Obadiah comes clean

Tony confronts Obadiah, who admits he's the one who had the board lock Tony out, claiming it was to protect him.

6-5The battle of Gulmira

At home, Tony sees a news report about Gulmira's people being driven from their homes. He puts on the Mark III and flies to Afghanistan.

In the village, Ten Rings fighters have rounded up the villagers and are dragging the men away from their families. Tony takes out only the fighters, without hitting the villagers.

He hands their leader over to the villagers. When a tank fires on him, he destroys it, and wrecks the weapons in the village.

6-6Chased by fighter jets

On the way home, two Air Force fighter jets chase the unidentified flying object. Rhodey, watching from a base, gets a call from Tony.

Tony reveals that he is the one flying. He collides with a jet, and its pilot ejects, but the parachute won't open. Tony frees it, saving the pilot, and flies off.

Rhodey decides to tell the military it was a training exercise.

6-7Obadiah and Raza

Meanwhile, Raza's men have gathered the pieces of the Mark I and meet with Obadiah. Obadiah is the one who had secretly been selling weapons to the Ten Rings, and had paid them to kill Tony.

Raza offers a new deal based on the Mark I's plans. But Obadiah paralyzes him with a sonic device, and Obadiah's men gun down Raza's fighters.

Obadiah takes the Mark I's pieces back to the U.S.

6-8The reactor they can't build

In a secret lab at the company, Obadiah has his engineers building a giant suit based on the Mark I. But they can't make a miniature arc reactor to power it.

Obadiah yells that Tony Stark built one in a cave, with a box of scraps.

Part 7: Fighting the man behind it

7-1Pepper goes in

To trace where the company's weapons are going, Tony asks Pepper to copy data from the company's computers. Now knowing what he's been doing, she reluctantly agrees.

Copying files from Obadiah's computer, she learns that he paid the Ten Rings to kill Tony, and that they changed their plan, deciding to make Tony build weapons instead.

Obadiah walks into the office. Pepper bluffs her way out.

7-2Turning to Coulson

In the company lobby, Pepper finds Agent Coulson, who has kept trying to meet with Tony. She tells him what Obadiah has been doing.

Coulson's agency is called S.H.I.E.L.D.

7-3The reactor is stolen

Meanwhile, Obadiah visits Tony's house and paralyzes him with the sonic device.

He reveals that he was the one who ordered the hit on Tony, then pulls the arc reactor out of Tony's chest and takes it. Without it, the fragments will reach Tony's heart and kill him.

7-4What Dum-E handed him

Tony drags himself toward the workshop to reach the old reactor Pepper had framed, but he can't get to it.

Then Dum-E, the robot arm, hands it to him. Tony puts it in his chest and survives.

7-5Rhodey and the silver suit

Rhodey arrives at Tony's house. Tony puts on the Mark III and flies to Pepper.

Left in the workshop, Rhodey eyes the silver Mark II and says, "Next time, baby," before leaving it.

7-6Iron Monger

Pepper, Coulson and S.H.I.E.L.D. agents go to the company plant to arrest Obadiah. In the secret lab they find the Mark I's pieces, but the giant suit is gone.

Obadiah appears in the suit, Iron Monger, powered by Tony's reactor. He overpowers the agents and goes after Pepper.

7-7Fighting on old power

Tony arrives and fights Obadiah, but the old reactor can't run his suit at full power.

The fight spills onto a nearby road. Obadiah lifts a car with people inside, and Tony protects them.

7-8Up high

Tony flies high into the sky and lures Obadiah after him. Obadiah's suit ices up and stops: he never fixed the problem Tony ran into before.

Obadiah falls, but he isn't finished.

7-9Overloading the big reactor

The fight moves to the roof of the company building, above the large arc reactor. Tony's suit is nearly out of power.

Tony tells Pepper to overload the big reactor. Though he can't get off the roof, she presses the button.

A huge electrical surge engulfs Obadiah. He falls through the roof into the reactor and is killed in the explosion. Tony survives.

Part 8: "I am Iron Man"

8-1The headline

The next day's newspaper gives the mysterious armored hero a name: Iron Man.

8-2A prepared story

Before the press conference, Coulson hands Tony an alibi saying he was somewhere else on the night of the fight. At the conference, Rhodey reads the military's statement.

8-3"I am Iron Man"

Tony starts reading the prepared cards, but after a question from Christine, he stops.

Then he says it himself: "The truth is... I am Iron Man." The room erupts, and the film ends.

8-4After the credits

Back home, Tony finds a man with an eye patch waiting: Nick Fury, director of S.H.I.E.L.D.

Fury hints that Tony isn't the only hero in the world, and says he's there to talk about the Avengers Initiative.

Summary (spoilers through the end)

Obadiah's scheme

- Behind Tony's back, Obadiah sold Stark weapons to the Ten Rings.

- To take over the company, he paid the Ten Rings to kill Tony (the Part 1 ambush). They changed plans and tried to make Tony build weapons instead (Part 2).

- When Tony stopped making weapons, Obadiah used the board to lock him out (Parts 4-6).

- He got the Mark I's pieces, built Iron Monger and stole the reactor from Tony's chest (Parts 6-7).

The suits

- Mark I (Part 3): the gray suit built in the cave. It breaks apart in the desert after the escape, and its pieces end up with Obadiah.

- Mark II (Part 5): the silver prototype built at home, which ices up at high altitude. Rhodey eyes it at the end.

- Mark III (Parts 5-7): the finished red-and-gold suit, with the icing problem fixed.

- Iron Monger (Part 7): the giant suit Obadiah has built from the Mark I.

Who died (in order)

- The soldiers with Tony (Part 1): shot in the Ten Rings ambush.

- Yinsen (Part 3): runs out with a gun to buy time for Tony's suit to start, and is shot by Ten Rings fighters. His last words to Tony: don't waste your life.

- Ten Rings fighters (Parts 3 and 6): taken down by Tony in the Mark I during the escape, and in the Mark III at Gulmira.

- Raza's fighters (Part 6): gunned down by Obadiah's men after handing over the Mark I's pieces.

- Obadiah (Part 7): when Pepper overloads the big reactor at Tony's request, the surge engulfs him; he falls through the roof into the reactor and is killed in the explosion.

My takeOpinion

The first one, and still simply a great watch. The closing line, "I am Iron Man," is said to have been improvised by Robert Downey Jr. I think that one line changed the mood of superhero films, where hiding your identity used to be the rule.
