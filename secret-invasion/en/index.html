Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why does Gravik rebel?

Fury once promised the Skrulls a new home and never delivered. Angry, Gravik decided to take Earth for themselves.

Was Rhodey real?

No. The Rhodey in this series was a Skrull. The real one had been held captive for a long time.

Who is Fury's wife?

His wife, Priscilla, is a Skrull named Varra.
