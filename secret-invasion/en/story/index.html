Episode 1: Resurrection

1-1Ross and Prescod in Moscow

In Moscow, CIA agent Everett Ross meets former CIA man Prescod, who has worked out that radical shape-shifting Skrulls plan to start a war between Russia and the U.S. and take over Earth.

Prescod suspects Ross himself is a Skrull. They struggle, and Ross kills Prescod and leaves with the evidence.

1-2Rooftop chase

Ross calls Maria Hill for a pickup. A Russian-speaking man chases him across the rooftops. Ross falls from a building and dies in front of Hill's car as she arrives.

Hill points her gun at the pursuer, who turns out to be the Skrull Talos. Ross's body changes into a green Skrull: he was an impostor. Hill calls Nick Fury.

1-3Fury comes back

Since the Blip, Fury has been working in space at S.A.B.E.R. After Hill's call, he returns to Earth.

At a safehouse he reunites with Talos, who says he was pushed off the Skrull Council because Fury never found the Skrulls a new home. Their former ally Gravik now leads the radicals. Talos's wife Soren is dead.

1-4Gravik and G'iah

Gravik's rebels have a base in Russia and operate disguised as humans. Among them is Talos's daughter G'iah, who works close to Gravik.

1-5Sonya Falsworth

Walking alone, Fury is hooded and bundled into a car by MI6 agents, who take him to his old acquaintance Sonya Falsworth.

He proposes they work together to stop Gravik; she refuses. Fury secretly leaves a bug in her office.

1-6Poprishchin, and Talos's daughter

Listening in on Sonya and the British Prime Minister, Fury and Talos find Vasily Poprishchin, who got dirty bombs for the rebels. They fail to get anything out of him, and Fury shoots him dead.

Talos meets G'iah, who has joined the rebels. When he says Gravik killed her mother Soren, she reveals the rebels will attack a Moscow square on Unity Day, and promises to mark the bombs' locations.

1-7A game of chess

The night before the attack, Fury and Hill play chess at the back of a bar. She asks why he left Earth and why he came back. He says he owes it to Talos.

1-8Unity Day bombing

In the square on Unity Day, Fury, Hill and Talos split up to find the marked bombs, but the marked spots are decoys. Gravik sets off the real bombs, and the square descends into chaos.

1-9Hill's death

In the chaos, Gravik, disguised as Fury, walks up to Hill and shoots her. The real Fury reaches her, but she cannot be saved.

Talos pulls Fury away, and they are forced to flee, leaving her body behind.

Episode 2: Promises

2-1The 1997 promise

In 1997, Fury, with Talos and Soren, meets Skrull refugees on Earth. He comforts young Gravik, orphaned in the war with the Kree.

Fury promises to find the Skrulls a new home. In return, they will pose as humans and work as his spies protecting Earth.

2-2Confession on the train

In the present, Fury and Talos flee Moscow by train. Fury tells a story about riding a train with his mother as a boy.

Talos admits about a million Skrulls now live on Earth hidden among humans. Fury is furious, and they part ways.

2-3Hill's mother

The bombing is blamed on America. To make it look that way, the rebels let a Skrull named Brogan be arrested as an American.

Fury meets Hill's mother, Elizabeth, and explains how Maria died: someone killed her to hurt him. She blames him.

2-4The Skrull Council

Gravik meets the Skrull Council, Skrulls posing as powerful humans. They disapprove of the bombing; he reminds them Fury broke his promise.

With majority support, Gravik becomes the new Skrull General. Dissenting member Shirley secretly contacts Talos, who asks her to arrange a meeting with Gravik.

2-5Meeting Rhodey

In London, Fury meets James "Rhodey" Rhodes, who works for the U.S. government, and warns him about the Skrulls. But Rhodes blames Fury for the bombing and Hill's death and dismisses him from his post.

Fury tells him he's still dangerous, title or not.

2-6G'iah at New Skrullos

At Gravik's Russian base, New Skrullos, G'iah secretly learns the rebels are experimenting with DNA from powered beings, such as Groot, Cull Obsidian and Extremis.

2-7Interrogation at the butcher's

Sonya goes to a hidden room behind a butcher shop used by Russian intelligence and questions the captured Brogan. She cuts off a finger to check his blood, and, finding he's a Skrull, tortures him with a drug that raises his body heat.

Brogan reveals that Gravik is building a machine to make Skrulls stronger, with help from a scientist couple, the Daltons.

2-8Brogan's end

Gravik, with G'iah and Pagon, raids the butcher shop, shoots the guards and frees Brogan. Sonya escapes through a hatch in the floor.

But Gravik suspects Brogan talked. They drive into the woods, and Brogan is shot dead among the trees.

2-9Fury's home

Fury comes home. In the kitchen, a woman in green Skrull form is chopping vegetables. She shifts into human form: Fury's wife, Priscilla.

At her prompting, Fury puts on his wedding ring.

Episode 3: Betrayed

3-1Meeting in 1998

In 1998, at a diner, Fury meets Varra, a Skrull in human form. She hands him an envelope of intelligence: "This should put Dreykov's men on their heels."

3-2Breakfast

At home in the present, Fury hints over breakfast that Priscilla may be in contact with Gravik, and they argue. She takes a secret phone call and goes out.

3-3The Super-Skrull machine

At New Skrullos, Gravik shows Council members a machine built by scientist Rosa Dalton. With it, Skrulls can take not only heroes' faces but their powers. Gravik has already succeeded on himself.

He also reveals he has planted Skrulls in the Royal Navy to shoot down a United Nations plane with a missile.

3-4The café meeting

Talos and Gravik meet at a café in London. Talos asks him to stop killing innocent people. Gravik needles him about G'iah and finally threatens to kill her, and Talos stabs Gravik's hand to the table with a knife.

The other customers turn out to be Gravik's people. Gravik pulls his hand free; the wound glows and heals at once. He leaves.

3-5Teaming up again

G'iah secretly sends Talos details of the missile strike.

Fury is angry that Talos let so many Skrulls onto Earth, but asks him for help stopping Gravik.

When Fury calls Sonya, she scolds him for planting a device in her office, but gives him the name of the naval officer in charge: Commodore Robert Fairbanks.

3-6Argument in the car

On the drive to Fairbanks's house, Fury and Talos argue about their 30 years together. Talos says Fury would never have become the world's top spy without his hidden Skrull network.

3-7Fairbanks's house

Fury and Talos break into Fairbanks's home and interrogate him. He is a Skrull impostor. He provokes Talos, who shoots him dead before learning how to stop the launch.

Talos contacts G'iah. From the memories of the real Fairbanks, held prisoner at the base, she gets the abort code. The launch is stopped just in time.

3-8G'iah is shot

Her cover blown, G'iah tries to leave the base. Gravik, who suspected her, catches her and shoots her at point-blank range, leaving her for dead.

3-9Priscilla's call

Priscilla opens a bank safety-deposit box with a gun inside. On the phone she asks to speak with Gravik, but is turned down.

Episode 4: Beloved

4-1G'iah revives

G'iah lies in the road. Before trying to flee, she had secretly used Gravik's machine to give herself Extremis powers. Her wound glows and heals, and she sits up.

4-2Talos and G'iah

G'iah meets her father. Talos says that once he and Fury stop the rebels, he plans to ask U.S. President Ritson to help the Skrulls. G'iah, hoping for a stronger plan to find a new home, walks away disappointed.

4-3Paris, 2012

In 2012, shortly after the Battle of New York, Fury and Priscilla (Varra) dine in Paris. She praises him for assembling the Avengers and recites Raymond Carver's poem "Late Fragment."

4-4Meeting at the church

In the present, Priscilla meets Rhodey at a church. This Rhodey is a Skrull working with Gravik, and orders her to kill Fury.

Fury has been secretly listening.

4-5Showdown with his wife

Fury is waiting when Priscilla gets home. They draw guns. He asks about the woman whose face she wears, and she tells him about the real Priscilla Davis.

Dying of a heart condition, Priscilla befriended Varra and let her take her identity after death, on conditions: bury her at sea, care for her parents, and never harm the man she came to love in that form. That oath is why she cannot kill Fury.

They fire at the same moment, both missing on purpose. As Fury leaves, she asks whether he would have loved her in her true form.

4-6A drink with Rhodey

Fury visits Rhodey's hotel suite with a fine bourbon and they drink together. The drink secretly contains a liquid tracker.

4-7The motorcade attack

Rhodey goes to pick up President Ritson. Fury and Talos follow using the tracker.

On the road, Gravik and the rebels, disguised as Russian terrorists, attack the motorcade. The President's car is overturned and Ritson is knocked out.

4-8Talos's death

Talos tries to reach Ritson in the overturned car, but Gravik's lieutenant Pagon shoots him and he reverts to Skrull form. British troops arrive, and Fury and Talos pull the unconscious Ritson out.

Then Gravik, disguised as a British soldier, approaches and stabs Talos with a knife. Talos dies there.

Fury drives Ritson away, leaving Talos's body on the road.

Episode 5: Harvest

5-1Ritson in hospital

President Ritson is rushed to hospital. Beside his unconscious bed, Fury tells him: "Do not trust Rhodes."

Rhodey (really a Skrull) arrives and tells Fury he has leaked footage of Hill's shooting. Because it shows Gravik disguised as Fury, it looks as if Fury killed her. Fury becomes a wanted man worldwide.

5-2Pagon and Beto's mutiny

At New Skrullos, Pagon criticizes Gravik in front of the others: he failed to kill Fury, and Fury never trusted him with something important, so why should they trust him? Gravik turns his arm into wood and impales Pagon through the chest.

New recruit Beto and a few others attack Gravik, but his powers overwhelm them and he kills them all, killing Beto in front of the others as a warning.

5-3Sonya and Weatherby

Gravik orders the Skrull posing as Rhodey to show Ritson photos of New Skrullos, to make it look as if Russia is siding with the Skrulls and push for a strike on the base.

In London, Sonya shoots her superior, Derrik Weatherby. The wound shows he's a Skrull, and he gives up the location of scientist Rosa Dalton.

5-4The Harvest

At a safehouse, Fury meets the grieving G'iah. She says what Gravik really wants is something called the Harvest.

Fury admits that after the Avengers' final battle on Earth, he had a team of Skrulls, Gravik among them, secretly collect blood and DNA from everyone who fought.

5-5The Daltons' lab

Sonya goes to the Daltons' lab. Rosa is a Skrull impostor. Sonya gets Rosa to explain Gravik's machine.

Victor holds a gun to Rosa, saying he won't let her betray Gravik. Sonya shoots him dead, then burns the lab and takes the research.

5-6Gravik's call

Gravik calls Fury about the coming strike on New Skrullos and offers to have it called off if Fury brings him the Harvest in person.

5-7Talos's funeral, and the attack

Outside the house, G'iah and Varra (Priscilla) hold a small funeral, burning Talos's body on a pyre.

Gravik's men shoot through the windows and break in. G'iah and Varra fight them off and kill them.

5-8The grave in Finland

Now a fugitive, Fury flies to Finland on old contact Rick Mason's jet. Mason gives him an old S.H.I.E.L.D. device.

At a Finnish cemetery, Fury meets Sonya. Hidden in a grave bearing Fury's own name is the Harvest: DNA from those who fought in the Avengers' final battle.

Fury puts on the eyepatch and black coat stored there and sets out for the final showdown.

Episode 6: Home

6-1A farewell call

Fury calls Varra in what sounds like a goodbye; he isn't sure he'll come back.

6-2Talks at New Skrullos

An eyepatched Fury walks into New Skrullos. He gives Gravik the Harvest and asks him to spare Earth and take other planets instead. Gravik refuses.

Gravik blames Fury: the Skrulls worked in the shadows for decades and never got a home. His human face belongs to the first man he killed on his first mission for Fury.

6-3The Harvest's power

Gravik loads the Harvest DNA into the machine and gains even greater powers. Fury is inside the machine at the same time, receiving the same powers.

At the hospital, the Skrull posing as Rhodey (Raava) persuades President Ritson to approve a nuclear strike on New Skrullos.

6-4Who "Fury" really is

The empowered Gravik tries to kill Fury, but "Fury" stops the blow and sends him flying out of the building. It's G'iah, disguised as Fury.

6-5Super-Skrull battle

With the same powers, they fight. Gravik uses Ebony Maw's telekinesis to throw G'iah and hurls a car; she phases through it with Ghost's ability. Both fly with Captain Marvel's powers.

Choked in mid-air, G'iah uses Mantis's power to put Gravik to sleep, and he falls. Finally she blasts through his chest with Captain Marvel's energy and kills him.

6-6The real Fury at the hospital

The real Fury is with Sonya. She calls Rhodey (Raava), warning that Fury is coming to attack the President, and gets Ritson moved to an upper floor.

Fury and Sonya take down the Secret Service agents with tranquilizer darts and enter the room. Fury tells Ritson that Rhodey is a Skrull and that Skrulls staged the motorcade attack.

When Raava lunges, Fury shoots her in the head. Ritson immediately calls off the strike.

6-7The real Rhodey

At New Skrullos, G'iah frees the humans held in the pods, among them Everett Ross and the real James Rhodes. Wearing a hospital gown, Rhodey needs help to walk; he has been held for a long time.

6-8The President's declaration

On TV, President Ritson reveals the Skrull infiltration, pushes a bill declaring all off-world beings hostile, and vows to hunt down the Skrulls left on Earth.

Fear spreads, and people publicly murder officials they suspect of being Skrulls.

Fury warns Ritson the policy will get innocent people killed, but Ritson won't listen.

6-9Sonya and G'iah

Sonya meets G'iah and proposes a partnership to protect the Skrulls from the President's bill.

6-10Into space

Fury tells Varra the Kree are open to peace talks and asks her to come with him to S.A.B.E.R. to help negotiate.

Varra appears in her true Skrull form and agrees. They leave Earth together.

Summary (spoilers through the finale)

Skrulls in disguise

- Everett Ross (Ep. 1): an impostor, exposed when he dies. The real Ross is held at the base and freed in Ep. 6.

- Rhodey (James Rhodes): the Skrull Raava, revealed in Ep. 4 and shot by Fury in Ep. 6. The real Rhodey, held for a long time, is freed in Ep. 6.

- Priscilla (Fury's wife): the Skrull Varra, bound by her promise to the real Priscilla never to harm Fury (Ep. 4). She leaves for space with Fury.

- Commodore Fairbanks (Ep. 3), MI6's Weatherby (Ep. 5) and scientist Rosa Dalton (Ep. 5) were Skrulls too.

Who died (in order)

- Prescod (Ep. 1): ex-CIA man who uncovered the Skrull plot; killed by the fake Ross.

- The fake Ross (Ep. 1): falls from a roof while chased by Talos.

- Poprishchin (Ep. 1): the bomb supplier; shot by Fury after refusing to talk.

- Maria Hill (Ep. 1): shot by Gravik disguised as Fury in the chaos of the Unity Day bombing, which also kills many in the square.

- Brogan (Ep. 2): suspected of talking under interrogation; shot in the woods on Gravik's orders.

- The fake Fairbanks (Ep. 3): provokes Talos and is shot.

- Talos (Ep. 4): stabbed by Gravik, disguised as a British soldier, during the President's rescue.

- Pagon (Ep. 5): impaled through the chest by Gravik's wooden arm for challenging him.

- Beto and his group (Ep. 5): killed by Gravik after their mutiny.

- Victor Dalton (Ep. 5): shot by Sonya after holding a gun to Rosa.

- Gravik (Ep. 6): killed by G'iah, empowered by the Harvest, with an energy blast through the chest.

- Raava, the fake Rhodey (Ep. 6): shot by Fury when she lunges in the hospital room.
