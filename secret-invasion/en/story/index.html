Episode 1: Resurrection

Trouble in Moscow

In Moscow, a CIA agent uncovers evidence of a Skrull rebellion and is killed by his pursuers.

Fury comes home

After years in space, Fury returns to Earth at Talos's request and reunites with Maria Hill.

Gravik's uprising

Young Skrull leader Gravik and his followers, disguised as humans, are plotting to take Earth.

Hill's death

As Fury tries to stop a bombing, Gravik, disguised as Fury, shoots Maria Hill.

Episodes 2–3: Impostors

New Skrullos

Talos's daughter G'iah is undercover at Gravik's base, New Skrullos.

The Harvest

Gravik seeks the Harvest: DNA collected from heroes after the Avengers' last battle.

Fury's wife

Fury has a wife, Priscilla, who is actually the Skrull Varra.

Sonya

British intelligence officer Sonya Falsworth helps Fury.

Episodes 4–5: On the brink

Attack on the President

Skrulls attack the President's motorcade, and 'Rhodey' blames Fury.

Talos falls

Talos is killed in a fight with Gravik.

G'iah's choice

Shot by Gravik, G'iah survives using the Harvest's power.

Episode 6: Home

The real Rhodey

Fury rescues the real Rhodey, who has been held captive for a long time.

Super-Skrull

Gravik uses the Harvest to gain the powers of many heroes.

G'iah vs. Gravik

G'iah, who also absorbed the Harvest, fights Gravik in Fury's form and defeats him.

The President's declaration

The President declares war on all aliens on Earth.

Back to space

Fury heads back into space to find the Skrulls a new home.
