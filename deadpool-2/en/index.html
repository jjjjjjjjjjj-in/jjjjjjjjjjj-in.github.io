Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

The sequel to Deadpool

It's set two years after the first film. Vanessa, Weasel, Blind Al, Colossus, Negasonic and Dopinder are back.

Watch the first one first

Knowing the first film's relationships, and the Skee-Ball token, makes the big moments land harder.

Violent and crude

It's rated R (R15+ in Japan), with plenty of gore and crude jokes.

Watch during the credits

There's a time-travel bonus sequence mid-credits.

StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

Part 1: What he lost

Two years into being Deadpool, Wade and Vanessa decide to start a family on their anniversary, but a target Wade failed to kill attacks them, and Vanessa is shot dead.

Wade takes revenge but is consumed by guilt and keeps trying to die. All he has left is their Skee-Ball token.

Part 2: X-Men trainee

Colossus takes Wade in as an X-Men trainee. On his first mission, he meets Russell, a boy raging at an orphanage.

Learning that the staff abused Russell, Wade shoots staff dead, and both are sent to the Ice Box, a prison for mutants. Meanwhile, Cable arrives from the future to kill Russell.

Part 3: Battle at the Ice Box

Cable attacks the Ice Box, and Wade fights to protect Russell. Cable takes Vanessa's token.

Russell overhears Wade say he doesn't care and shuts him out. Near death, Wade sees Vanessa, who urges him to help Russell.

Part 4: X-Force

Wade forms X-Force and jumps onto Russell's prison convoy from the air, but the wind wrecks the landings and members die one after another. Only the lucky Domino lands safely.

Meanwhile, Russell frees the unstoppable inmate Juggernaut, and they escape together to kill the orphanage headmaster.

Part 5: Teaming up with Cable

Cable proposes teaming up with Wade and Domino. In his future, Russell's first killing, the headmaster, leads to him murdering Cable's wife and daughter.

Wade agrees, on the condition that he gets to try talking Russell down first, and they head to the orphanage.

Part 6: Showdown at the orphanage

Colossus takes on Juggernaut. Wearing a power-suppressing collar, Wade dies taking Cable's bullet for Russell. Moved, Russell spares the headmaster, and the future changes.

Cable spends his last trip home to go back in time and save Wade with the token.

Key terms

Cable
A soldier from the future with a cybernetic arm, who traveled back in time for his family's sake.
The Ice Box
A prison for mutant criminals, where inmates wear power-suppressing collars.
Power-suppressing collar
A collar that shuts off mutant powers. For Wade, it means he can't heal.
X-Force
The team Deadpool puts together to protect Russell, named by Deadpool himself.
The Skee-Ball token
An arcade token from Wade and Vanessa's dates, her anniversary gift that becomes a keepsake.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why does Cable want to kill Russell?

In Cable's future, the grown-up Russell becomes a killer who murders Cable's wife and daughter. It all starts when he kills the orphanage headmaster.

Why does Russell want to kill the headmaster?

Because he was abused by the headmaster and staff at the orphanage.

Why does the bullet kill Wade?

He's wearing a power-suppressing collar, so his healing factor doesn't work.

Why doesn't Cable go home?

He used his last charge to save Wade. With his family's future safe, he decides to stay in the present for a while.

My takeOpinion

A sequel with more jokes and more people than the first. The relationship with Cable and the post-credits "do-overs" are among my favourite moments in the series.
