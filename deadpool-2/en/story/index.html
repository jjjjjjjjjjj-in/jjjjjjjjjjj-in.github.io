Part 1: What he lost

1-1Deadpool on the barrels

The film opens with Deadpool lying on oil barrels in his apartment. He tells the audience he'll explain how he got here, then lights the barrels and blows himself up.

The story then jumps back in time.

1-2Taking out bad guys worldwide

Two years into being Deadpool, Wade travels the world taking out organized crime.

1-3Anniversary night

On his anniversary with Vanessa, Wade fails to kill one of his targets.

That night, the two decide to start a family. Vanessa gives him a Skee-Ball token, a reminder of their arcade dates, as an anniversary gift.

1-4Vanessa's death

The target Wade let slip tracks him down to their home. In the attack aimed at Wade, Vanessa is shot and killed.

Wade hunts the man down and kills him.

1-5Six weeks later

Six weeks later, Wade is still drowning in guilt. He visits Blind Al, but her advice to move on doesn't get through.

He tries again and again to die, but his body keeps healing. The barrel explosion was one of those attempts.

Near death, he sees Vanessa in a vision of the afterlife. All he has left of her is the Skee-Ball token.

Part 2: X-Men trainee

2-1To the X-Mansion

Colossus gathers up the pieces of Wade's body and brings him to the X-Mansion. Once Wade heals, Colossus invites him to join the X-Men.

Believing Vanessa would have wanted it, Wade joins as a trainee. At the mansion he also meets Negasonic's girlfriend, Yukio.

2-2Russell at the orphanage

The first mission is at an orphanage for mutant kids, billed as a mutant re-education center. A boy named Russell is shooting fire from his hands in a standoff with police.

2-3Shooting a staff member

Wade realizes Russell has been abused by the orphanage staff.

Enraged, he shoots one of them dead before Colossus restrains him. Both Wade and Russell are arrested.

2-4The Ice Box

Fitted with power-suppressing collars, they're sent to the Ice Box, a prison for mutant criminals. Without his healing, Wade's cancer starts coming back.

Inside, Russell learns about a powerful inmate called Juggernaut.

2-5Cable arrives from the future

Meanwhile, Cable, a cybernetic soldier, travels back in time from the future. His goal is to kill Russell.

Part 3: Battle at the Ice Box

3-1Cable attacks

Cable storms the Ice Box and goes after Russell. In the brawl, Wade's collar breaks, and he fights Cable to protect the boy.

Cable takes Vanessa's token from Wade.

3-2"He's not my friend"

Wade hurls himself and Russell out of the prison, but not before Russell overhears Wade saying he doesn't care about him.

Hurt, Russell shuts him out and stays behind.

3-3Vanessa's words

Near death again, Wade sees Vanessa, who tells him his heart is in the right place and convinces him to help Russell.

Part 4: X-Force

4-1Building a team

With Weasel's help, Wade puts together a team to protect Russell from Cable, calling it X-Force.

The recruits: Domino, who says her power is being lucky, plus Bedlam, Shatterstar, Zeitgeist, Vanisher, and Peter, a totally ordinary guy.

4-2A disastrous jump

Russell is being moved in a prison convoy. X-Force plans to parachute onto it from a plane.

But high winds wreck the jump, and every member except Wade and Domino dies on landing. Domino lands safely, thanks to her luck.

4-3Juggernaut is freed

While Wade and Domino battle Cable, Russell frees Juggernaut, who's in the same convoy. They agree to team up so Russell can kill the orphanage's headmaster.

Juggernaut wrecks the convoy, tears Wade in half, and escapes with Russell.

Part 5: Teaming up with Cable

5-1Waiting to grow back

At Al's place, Wade waits for the lower half of his body to grow back.

5-2Cable's family

Cable offers to work with Wade and Domino. In his future, after Russell kills the headmaster, he grows into a serial killer who murdered Cable's wife and daughter.

Stopping Russell before that first killing might change the future.

5-3Wade's condition

Wade agrees, on the condition that he gets a chance to talk Russell down first. Cable agrees.

Cable's time-travel device has only one charge left, the one he needs to get back to his family.

5-4Colossus says no

Wade also asks Colossus for help, but Colossus, who objects to Wade's killing, refuses at first.

Wade and the others head for the orphanage in Dopinder's cab.

Part 6: Showdown at the orphanage

6-1No match for Juggernaut

At the orphanage, Juggernaut overpowers Wade and the team, while Russell goes after the headmaster.

6-2Colossus arrives

Colossus, who had turned them down, shows up and takes on Juggernaut, keeping him busy.

6-3Pleading, and a bullet

Wearing the Ice Box collar, Wade pleads with Russell not to kill the headmaster, but Russell won't listen.

Cable fires at Russell. Wade leaps in front of the bullet, and with the collar on, he can't heal.

6-4Wade dies, and the future changes

Wade dies and is reunited with Vanessa in the afterlife.

Moved by Wade's sacrifice, Russell decides not to kill the headmaster, and the future in which Cable's family is murdered is gone.

6-5The last charge

Cable uses his last charge, meant for getting home, to jump back a few minutes and strap Vanessa's Skee-Ball token over Wade's heart.

This time the bullet hits the token, and Wade survives. Cable decides to stay in the present for a while to help make the world better.

6-6The headmaster's end

Afterward, the headmaster who abused Russell is run over and killed by Dopinder's taxi.

6-7Mid-credits

Negasonic and Yukio repair Cable's time-travel device for Wade.

He goes back in time to save Vanessa and X-Force's Peter. In more gag scenes, he takes out a different Deadpool from another film and stops actor Ryan Reynolds himself just as he finishes reading the script for one of his old movies.

Summary (spoilers through the end)

Wade and Russell

1) At the orphanage, Wade meets Russell, who's been abused by the staff (Part 2).

2) At the Ice Box, Wade says he doesn't care, and Russell shuts him out (Part 3).

3) Russell teams up with Juggernaut to kill the headmaster (Part 4).

4) Wade takes a bullet for Russell, who then spares the headmaster, changing the future (Part 6).

Who died (in order)

- Vanessa (Part 1): shot when the man Wade failed to kill attacks them. (Wade goes back in time and saves her in the mid-credits scene.)

- The man who killed Vanessa (Part 1): Wade kills him in revenge.

- An orphanage staff member (Part 2): Wade shoots him, furious at how Russell was treated.

- X-Force (Part 4): everyone except Wade and Domino dies landing the jump. (Wade saves Peter in the mid-credits scene.)

- Wade (Part 6): shot shielding Russell; he dies, but Cable goes back in time and saves him.

- The headmaster (Part 6): run over by Dopinder's taxi.
