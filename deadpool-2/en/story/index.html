Part 1: What he lost

1-1Deadpool on the barrels

The film opens with Deadpool lying on fuel barrels in his apartment, cigarette in mouth, next to a music box that mimics Logan's death.

He tells the audience this is a family story, says he'll explain how he got here, then lights the barrels and blows himself up.

1-2A spy-movie opening

A spy-movie-style title sequence plays, with jokes in the credits, like billing the writers as "the real villains."

Then the story jumps back in time.

1-3Taking out crime bosses worldwide

Two years into being Deadpool, Wade is a paid killer of crime bosses around the world, even slicing bullets in half with his swords.

1-4An anniversary job

It's his anniversary with Vanessa. Wade fails to finish off one of his targets, but eager to get home, he lets it go.

1-5Anniversary night

That night, the two decide to start a family. A Skee-Ball token, a reminder of their arcade dates, becomes a keepsake of the night.

1-6Vanessa's death

The target Wade let slip and his men attack their home for revenge. In the gunfire, Vanessa is shot and killed.

Wade hunts the man down and kills him. All he has left of her is the Skee-Ball token.

1-7Al's place, and the explosion

Consumed by guilt, Wade visits Blind Al. She tells him to move on, but it doesn't get through.

He tries to die, but his body keeps healing; the explosion from the opening was one such attempt. Near death, he sees Vanessa in a vision of the afterlife but can't reach her.

Part 2: X-Men trainee

2-1Picking up the pieces

Colossus gathers the pieces of Wade's blown-apart body and brings them to the X-Mansion, where he heals back together.

2-2The X-Mansion

Colossus invites Wade to join the X-Men. Believing Vanessa would have wanted it, Wade joins as a trainee.

He complains that the mansion only ever has Colossus and Negasonic around, while behind him, Professor X, Beast and other X-Men are in a room whose door quietly closes.

2-3Negasonic and Yukio

At the mansion, Wade meets Negasonic's cheerful girlfriend Yukio, an X-Man who fights with electrified chains.

2-4Russell at the orphanage

Wade's first mission as a trainee. Outside an orphanage for mutant kids, billed as a mutant re-education center, a boy named Russell is shooting fire from his hands in a standoff with police.

Wade goes in with Colossus and Negasonic.

2-5Shooting the staff

Listening to Russell, Wade realizes the boy has been abused by the headmaster and staff.

Enraged, he shoots staff dead before Colossus restrains him. Both Wade and Russell are arrested.

2-6The Ice Box

Fitted with power-suppressing collars, they're sent to the Ice Box, a prison for mutant criminals.

With his healing blocked, Wade's cancer starts coming back. He brushes off Russell's pleas for help and resigns himself to dying.

2-7The inmates, and Juggernaut

The inmates are run by a man called Black Tom. Inside, Russell also learns about Juggernaut, a hugely powerful prisoner kept under heavy restraint.

2-8Cable arrives from the future

Meanwhile, Cable, a soldier who is half machine, travels back from a ruined future about fifty years ahead. On his belt hangs his daughter's burned teddy bear.

He steals a truck from a couple of locals and heads for the Ice Box. His goal is to kill Russell.

Part 3: Battle at the Ice Box

3-1Cable attacks

Cable storms the Ice Box and goes after Russell, and the prison erupts into chaos.

Weak as he is, Wade steps in to protect Russell, but he's no match for Cable.

3-2The collar breaks

In the brawl, Wade's collar breaks and his healing returns, and he fights back.

Cable takes Vanessa's Skee-Ball token from him.

3-3Russell overhears

Wade blows himself up to drive Cable back and is thrown clear of the prison. Before that, Russell overhears Wade saying he doesn't care about him.

Russell survives, shuts Wade out, and goes back into the prison alone.

3-4Vanessa's words

Near death again, Wade sees Vanessa, who tells him his heart isn't in the right place yet.

He takes it to mean he must protect Russell from Cable.

Part 4: X-Force

4-1The interviews

Russell is about to be moved to another prison with other inmates. With Weasel's help, Wade puts out a job ad to recruit a team to protect him from Cable.

Domino comes in and says her power is being extremely lucky. Wade doesn't buy that luck counts as a superpower.

4-2X-Force is born

The recruits: Domino; Bedlam, who disrupts people with electrical waves; Shatterstar, who claims to be an alien; Zeitgeist, who spits acid; Vanisher, who is always invisible; and Peter, a totally ordinary guy with no powers.

Wade calls the team X-Force.

4-3Jumping into the wind

X-Force plans to hit the prison convoy from the air. The wind is strong, but they go ahead and everyone jumps.

4-4One by one

The wind wrecks their landings. Bedlam slams into the windshield of a moving bus. Shatterstar flies into the rotor of a helicopter that's taking off. Vanisher hits a power line, is electrocuted, and becomes visible for the first time.

Zeitgeist lands in a wood chipper. Peter, trying to help, is sprayed with his acid and dies, and Zeitgeist is pulled into the machine.

4-5Domino's luck

Domino alone lands safely, thanks to her luck, and makes it onto the convoy. Wade catches up too.

4-6Fighting on the convoy

Cable shows up at the convoy and fights Wade and Domino. A stray shot from Cable kills Black Tom, the inmates' boss.

4-7Juggernaut is freed

During the fight, Russell frees Juggernaut, who's in the same convoy. They agree to team up so Russell can kill the orphanage's headmaster.

Juggernaut wrecks the convoy and tears Wade in half when he tries to stop them. Ignoring Wade's pleas, Russell escapes with Juggernaut.

Part 5: Teaming up with Cable

5-1Waiting to grow back

At Al's place, Wade waits for the lower half of his body to grow back. His new legs come in tiny, like a small child's.

5-2Cable's family

Cable turns up and offers to work together. In his future, after Russell kills the headmaster, he comes to enjoy killing and keeps on murdering as an adult, eventually killing Cable's wife and daughter.

Stopping Russell before that first killing might change the future.

5-3Wade's condition

Wade agrees, on the condition that he gets a chance to talk Russell down first. Cable gives him thirty seconds.

Cable's time-travel device has only one charge left, the one he needs to get back to his family.

5-4Colossus says no

Wade also asks Colossus for help, but Colossus, who objects to Wade's killing, turns him down.

Wade, Cable and Domino head for the orphanage on their own.

Part 6: Showdown at the orphanage

6-1No match for Juggernaut

At the orphanage, Russell arrives with Juggernaut. Ignoring Wade, he heads inside to kill the headmaster.

Juggernaut overpowers Wade and the others.

6-2Colossus arrives

Colossus, who had turned them down, shows up with Negasonic and Yukio, and decides it's time to fight dirty against Juggernaut.

With the girls' help, Juggernaut gets zapped with a live power line and knocked into a pool.

6-3Chasing Russell

Meanwhile, Wade and Cable go after Russell inside. Russell has cornered the headmaster.

Wade puts a power-suppressing collar on himself and tells Russell to kill him instead, trying to stop him from killing the headmaster. Russell won't listen.

6-4Cable's bullet

Out of patience, Cable fires at Russell. Wade leaps in front of the bullet, and with the collar on, he can't heal.

6-5Wade dies

Colossus tries to take the collar off, but Wade refuses. He thanks Cable, Domino, Russell, Colossus and the others, and dies in Russell's arms.

In the afterlife, he is reunited with Vanessa.

6-6The future changes

Moved by Wade's sacrifice, Russell decides not to kill the headmaster.

Cable's teddy bear changes from burned back to clean, a sign that the future where his family is murdered is gone.

6-7The last charge

Cable uses his last charge, meant for getting home, to jump back a few minutes and secretly strap Vanessa's Skee-Ball token over Wade's heart.

This time the bullet hits the token, and Wade survives. Seeing the dented coin, Wade realizes what Cable did.

6-8Cable stays

Unable to go home now, Cable decides to stay in the present for a while and help change the world for the better.

6-9The headmaster's end

As they leave, the headmaster comes out and insults them, and Dopinder's taxi plows into him, killing him.

Negasonic and Yukio load the orphanage's kids onto the X-Men's jet.

6-10Mid-credits

Negasonic and Yukio repair Cable's time-travel device for Wade.

He goes back in time to save Vanessa, and saves Peter by telling him not to join X-Force. He also shoots the other Deadpool from X-Men Origins: Wolverine in front of Wolverine, and shoots actor Ryan Reynolds himself, just as he finishes reading the Green Lantern script.

Summary (spoilers through the end)

Wade and Russell

1) At the orphanage, Wade meets Russell, abused by the headmaster and staff; Wade shoots the staff and they both go to prison (Part 2).

2) At the Ice Box, Russell overhears Wade saying he doesn't care, and shuts him out (Part 3).

3) X-Force tries to rescue him, but Russell teams up with Juggernaut and escapes (Part 4).

4) Wade teams up with Cable on the condition he can try talking Russell down first (Part 5).

5) Wade takes a bullet for Russell, who then spares the headmaster, changing the future (Part 6).

Who died (in order)

- Vanessa (Part 1): shot when the men Wade failed to kill attack them. (Wade goes back in time and saves her mid-credits.)

- The man behind Vanessa's death (Part 1): Wade kills him in revenge.

- Orphanage staff (Part 2): Wade shoots them, furious at how Russell was treated.

- X-Force (Part 4): Shatterstar, Vanisher, Zeitgeist and Peter die in the jump. (Wade saves Peter mid-credits.)

- Black Tom (Part 4): killed by a stray shot from Cable during the convoy fight.

- Wade (Part 6): shot shielding Russell; he dies, but Cable goes back in time and saves him.

- The headmaster (Part 6): run over by Dopinder's taxi.
