Part 1: The man who hates gods

A father and daughter in the desert

Gorr and his young daughter cross a barren desert. He prays to his god, but she dies.

A god's laughter

In a lush oasis, Gorr meets his god, who laughs off his prayers. The Necrosword, a blade that can kill gods, calls to Gorr. He slays his god and vows to kill them all.

Part 2: Thor's journey

Korg narrates

Korg recounts Thor's life. Having lost so many loved ones, Thor has closed off his heart and gotten back in shape.

Leaving the Guardians

Thor fights alongside the Guardians, but learns gods are being murdered and heads for Earth on his own.

Jane's illness

On Earth, Jane is diagnosed with a serious illness. As treatment fails, she travels to New Asgard in Norway.

Part 3: The Mighty Thor

Attack on New Asgard

Gorr and his shadow monsters attack New Asgard. Thor arrives to find another "Thor" wielding Mjolnir: Jane.

The children taken

Gorr takes the children of New Asgard into his Shadow Realm.

The hammer's secret

Thor remembers asking Mjolnir long ago to always protect Jane.

Part 4: The city of gods

The goat boat

Thor, Jane, Valkyrie and Korg fly to the city of gods on a ship pulled by two endlessly screaming goats.

Zeus

Zeus, king of the gods, refuses to help. After a confrontation, Thor steals Zeus's thunderbolt and escapes.

Jane's secret

Thor learns that every time Jane uses Mjolnir, her illness gets worse.

Part 5: The Shadow Realm

A world without color

In the colorless Shadow Realm, they fight Gorr. His real goal is to use Thor's axe Stormbreaker to open the gate to the center of the universe.

The axe is taken

Gorr steals Stormbreaker. Jane returns to the hospital, and Thor decides to rescue the children alone.

Part 6: Love and thunder

Powering the children

Using Zeus's thunderbolt, Thor gives the children temporary thunder powers, and they fight the shadow monsters.

Jane's last stand

Knowing the cost, Jane takes up Mjolnir one last time to fight beside Thor.

Gorr's wish

At the center of the universe, Gorr is about to wish for the gods' destruction. Thor urges him to wish for his daughter back instead.

Goodbye to Jane

Gorr chooses to revive his daughter, asks Thor to raise her, and dies. Jane dies in Thor's arms.

Love and Thunder

Thor raises Gorr's daughter, and the two help people together. Korg calls them Love and Thunder.

Part 7: The credit scenes

Mid-credits

Zeus is alive and orders his son Hercules to take down Thor.

After the credits

Jane arrives in Valhalla, where Heimdall welcomes her.
