Part 1: Birth of the God Butcher

1-1A father and daughter in the desert

Gorr and his young daughter Love trudge across an endless, barren desert. They are the last of a people who worship the god Rapu. With no food or water left, Gorr keeps praying.

His prayers go unanswered, and Love dies. Gorr buries her in the sand.

1-2Rapu's paradise

Near death himself, Gorr follows a strange voice to a lush green paradise. There Rapu is throwing a feast to celebrate killing the last wielder of the Necrosword, a blade that can kill gods.

Gorr tells him how faithfully he prayed through his suffering and begs for help. Rapu laughs: followers dying for their god is simply how things are, and there is no reward waiting after death.

1-3Taking up the Necrosword

In despair, Gorr renounces Rapu. Enraged, Rapu starts strangling him. Then the Necrosword chooses Gorr as its new wielder and flies into his hand.

Gorr kills Rapu with it and vows to kill every god. The sword lets him command shadows and create monsters, but it also carries a curse that eats away at its wielder and will eventually kill him. Gorr becomes known as the God Butcher.

Part 2: Thor in space

2-1Korg tells the story

Thor's rock-bodied friend Korg recounts Thor's life: his childhood, the loves he has found and lost, and the many loved ones he has lost, including his father Odin, mother Frigga and brother Loki.

After the final battle against Thanos, Thor closed off his heart and got himself back in fighting shape. With Korg, he has been traveling the galaxy with the Guardians of the Galaxy, answering calls for help.

2-2The battle on Indigarr

The sacred temple on the planet Indigarr is under attack by the Booskans, a band of bird-like raiders. The Guardians come to help, and Thor charges in with his axe Stormbreaker.

Thor defeats the Booskans but gets so carried away that he wrecks the temple too.

2-3The goats and Quill's advice

Indigarr's King Yakan thanks Thor with a gift of two giant goats.

Guardians leader Peter Quill talks to the closed-off Thor about finding people to love and holding on to them.

2-4Sif's distress call

Gods are being murdered across the cosmos, and conflicts are breaking out. Then Thor picks up a distress call from the Asgardian warrior Sif. He parts ways with the Guardians and sets off with Korg to find her.

2-5Sif, wounded

Sif is badly hurt and has lost an arm. Nearby lies the body of a god Gorr has killed.

Sif warns that Gorr's next target is New Asgard on Earth. Thor takes her and rushes back to Earth.

Part 3: Jane's illness

3-1A stage four diagnosis

On Earth, Thor's ex-girlfriend, astrophysicist Jane Foster, has been diagnosed with stage four cancer. She goes through chemotherapy, but the treatment isn't working.

Her friend Darcy encourages her to look for help in New Asgard.

3-2A book of Norse myths

In a book of Norse mythology, Jane reads that Thor's hammer Mjolnir is said to give its wielder strength and health.

Mjolnir was shattered by Thor's sister Hela, and its pieces are on display in New Asgard, Norway. Pinning her last hopes on it, Jane travels there.

3-3Mjolnir reborn

When Jane stands before the fragments, Mjolnir begins to glow, and the pieces fly back together. The hammer has chosen Jane as its new wielder.

Long ago, Thor had asked Mjolnir to always protect Jane. Without his realizing it, that request became an enchantment on the hammer (this is shown later in a flashback).

Part 4: Night in New Asgard

4-1A tourist town

New Asgard has become a busy tourist town. Valkyrie, who inherited the throne from Thor, runs it as king.

At the local theater, actors playing Loki, Thor, Odin and Hela stage an over-the-top play about Asgard's recent history.

4-2Gorr attacks

At night, Gorr attacks New Asgard with his shadow monsters. Valkyrie and the townspeople fight back, and Thor and Korg, just back on Earth, join in.

Among the fighters Thor spots another "Thor" swinging Mjolnir. It is Jane. Stunned, Thor fights alongside her.

4-3The children are taken

Thor, Jane, Valkyrie and Korg drive Gorr off. But before he escapes, he uses his shadows to snatch many of the town's children, among them Axl, son of the late gatekeeper Heimdall.

Part 5: The city of the gods

5-1A message from Axl

Axl has the same far-seeing power as his father Heimdall. He uses it to reach Thor's mind and show him where they are: caged in Gorr's Shadow Realm.

They need help to fight Gorr. Thor decides to go to Omnipotence City, where the gods of the universe gather, and raise an army of gods.

5-2The goat boat

Thor, Jane, Valkyrie and Korg board a ship pulled by King Yakan's two goats, and Stormbreaker's power launches them into space. The goats never stop screaming.

Jane tries out one heroic catchphrase after another.

5-3Enter Zeus

In the golden Omnipotence City, the gods are holding council, presided over by Zeus, king of the gods, who wields the thunderbolt.

Thor warns them about the God Butcher and asks for help. Zeus says they are safe hidden in the city and refuses to send an army or lend his thunderbolt.

5-4The story of Eternity

Zeus reveals Gorr's true goal. At the center of the universe is Eternity, which grants a single wish to whoever reaches it first. Gorr means to get there and wish for every god to be wiped out.

Zeus, however, is sure Gorr will never make it.

5-5Taken prisoner

To keep the city's location secret from Gorr, Zeus orders that Thor and his companions not be allowed to leave. When Thor objects, Zeus magically strips his clothes off.

A fight breaks out with Zeus's guards, and Zeus hurls his thunderbolt. Korg's body is shattered, leaving only his face.

5-6Stealing the thunderbolt

Furious, Thor grabs the thunderbolt and throws it back at Zeus, running him through the chest. Zeus falls.

Valkyrie takes the thunderbolt, and the group escapes the city on the goat boat, carrying Korg's face.

5-7A confession on the boat

On the way to the Shadow Realm, Jane tells Thor that she has cancer.

They talk about the years since they split up and how they feel, and they find their way back to each other.

Part 6: The Shadow Realm

6-1No children

The group reaches the Shadow Realm, but the children are nowhere to be found.

6-2Gorr's trap

Jane notices ancient drawings on the wall. They show Stormbreaker summoning the Bifrost to open a path to Eternity.

She realizes the kidnapping was a trap to lure Thor and his axe there, and she throws Stormbreaker far away.

6-3The axe is taken

Gorr appears and overpowers the three of them. He pins Jane and threatens to kill her unless Thor summons the axe, so Thor calls Stormbreaker back.

Gorr seizes it and badly wounds Valkyrie, and the weakened Jane collapses. Thor brings them both back to Earth.

Part 7: Thor's choice

7-1What Mjolnir costs Jane

Back in New Asgard, Jane is treated in the hospital. Thor learns that each time she transforms with Mjolnir's power, her body loses its ability to fight the cancer.

One more transformation could kill her.

7-2Thor goes alone

Jane wants to keep fighting until the children are safe, but Thor asks her to stay and live.

With Valkyrie wounded too, Thor takes Zeus's thunderbolt and goes after Gorr alone. Gorr is already using Stormbreaker to open the gate to Eternity.

Part 8: Eternity's altar

8-1Thunder for the children

At Eternity's altar at the center of the universe, Thor frees the caged children. Using the thunderbolt, he lends them his thunder powers.

Armed with whatever they can grab, the children take on Gorr's shadow monsters.

8-2Thor versus Gorr

Thor battles Gorr, but the Necrosword makes Gorr formidable, and Thor is driven back.

8-3Jane arrives

Sensing that Thor is in danger, Jane takes up Mjolnir one more time, knowing what it will cost her, and arrives as the Mighty Thor.

Thor wins back Stormbreaker and hands it to Axl, who uses it to take the children home to Earth.

8-4The Necrosword shattered

Together, Thor and Jane smash the Necrosword with Mjolnir. Gorr is freed from its control, but the curse means he has little time left.

The gate to Eternity is already open, and Gorr, Thor and Jane are drawn into Eternity's realm.

8-5Thor's words

Gorr prepares to make his wish. Thor doesn't fight him. Instead he urges Gorr to wish for his daughter's life rather than the gods' destruction, then leaves the choice to Gorr and goes to the fallen Jane.

8-6Jane's last moments

Spent, Jane lies in Thor's arms. She tells Gorr that his daughter won't be alone, because Thor will look after her.

With Thor beside her, Jane dies of her cancer and fades away into light.

8-7Gorr's wish

Moved by what he has seen, Gorr wishes not for the end of the gods but for his daughter Love to live again. Eternity grants it, and Love is restored.

Gorr asks Thor to take care of her, and then the sword's curse claims his life.

Part 9: Love and Thunder

9-1New Asgard, afterward

The children return safely to New Asgard, where Valkyrie and Sif start training them to fight. A memorial to Jane is put up in the town.

Korg finds love with a fellow Kronan named Dwayne.

9-2Thor and Love

Thor adopts Love and raises her as his own. Together they travel the cosmos helping people in need, Thor with Mjolnir and Love with Stormbreaker.

Korg closes the story by telling how the two came to be known as Love and Thunder.

Part 10: The credit scenes

10-1Mid-credits scene

Zeus survived. Recovering in Omnipotence City, he takes revenge by sending his son Hercules to kill Thor.

10-2Post-credits scene

Jane arrives at the gates of Valhalla, where the honored dead go. Heimdall is waiting. He thanks her for helping save his son and welcomes her in.

Summary

Thor's allies

- Jane: when treatment failed, she staked her hopes on Mjolnir. Chosen by the hammer, she became the Mighty Thor, but each transformation weakened her body (Parts 3 and 7).

- Valkyrie: king of New Asgard. Badly wounded by Gorr, she ends up training the children (Parts 6 and 9).

- Korg: the narrator. Zeus shattered him down to his face, but he survived and ends up with Dwayne (Parts 5 and 9).

- Sif: lost an arm to Gorr but warned Thor of the danger (Part 2).

Gorr's story

After losing his daughter and being scorned by his god Rapu, Gorr used the Necrosword to kill god after god (Part 1). He kidnapped the children to get Thor's axe (Part 4) and opened the gate to Eternity.

In the end he wished not for the gods' destruction but for his daughter's life (Part 8).

Who died (in order)

- Love (Part 1): Gorr's daughter, who died of hunger and thirst in the desert. Gorr's wish brings her back at the end (Part 8).

- Rapu (Part 1): the god of Gorr's people. He mocked Gorr's plea and began strangling him, and Gorr killed him with the Necrosword.

- Jane (Part 8): using Mjolnir left her body unable to fight her cancer, and she died in Thor's arms in Eternity's realm.

- Gorr (Part 8): the Necrosword's curse ate away at him, and he died after bringing his daughter back.

(Zeus was run through by Thor but survived. Part 10)

My takeOpinion

The constant jokes split opinion, but the villain Gorr's sorrow is real. Jane's choice, and what "love" turns out to mean at the end, make for an honestly good story, I think.
