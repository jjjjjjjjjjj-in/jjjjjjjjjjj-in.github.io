StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

Part 1: Birth of the God Butcher

Gorr loses his daughter Love in the desert and is mocked by his god Rapu when he begs for help.

Chosen by the god-killing Necrosword, he slays Rapu and vows to kill every god.

Part 2: Thor in space

After the fight with Thanos, Thor has been traveling with the Guardians.

Answering Sif's distress call, he leaves them and learns that Gorr is headed for New Asgard.

Part 3: Jane's illness

Thor's ex, Jane, has stage four cancer, and treatment is failing.

Hoping Mjolnir's legend is true, she visits New Asgard, where the shattered hammer reforms and chooses her.

Part 4: Night in New Asgard

Gorr attacks with his shadow monsters, and Thor is reunited with Jane, now the Mighty Thor.

Gorr is driven off but kidnaps the children, including Heimdall's son Axl.

Part 5: The city of the gods

The team flies to Omnipotence City on the goat boat, but Zeus refuses to help and reveals that Gorr is after Eternity, which grants a wish.

After Zeus shatters Korg, Thor fells him and escapes with his thunderbolt. On the way, Jane tells Thor about her cancer.

Part 6: The Shadow Realm

The Shadow Realm is Gorr's trap to get Thor's axe Stormbreaker.

Gorr takes it and badly wounds Valkyrie, and Jane collapses.

Part 7: Thor's choice

Every time Jane uses Mjolnir, her body loses its strength to fight the cancer.

Wanting her to live, Thor goes after Gorr alone.

Part 8: Eternity's altar

Thor gives the children his powers, and with Jane, who arrives to help, he breaks the Necrosword.

Before Eternity, Thor urges Gorr to wish for his daughter, and Jane dies in Thor's arms. Gorr wishes Love back to life, leaves her to Thor, and dies.

Part 9: Love and Thunder

The children return home, and a memorial to Jane is raised.

Thor raises Love, and together they help people as Love and Thunder.

Key terms

The Necrosword
A cursed blade that can kill gods, slowly consuming whoever wields it.
The God Butcher
Gorr's title, earned by killing gods with the Necrosword.
Eternity
A being at the center of the universe said to grant one wish to whoever reaches it.
Omnipotence City
A golden city where gods from across the universe gather.
Stormbreaker
Thor's axe, which seems jealous of Mjolnir.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

How can Jane lift Mjolnir?

Long ago, Thor asked the hammer to protect her. It chose Jane, who was ill.

What does Mjolnir do to Jane's body?

Each time she uses its power, it drains the strength her body needs to fight her illness.

Who's the girl at the end?

Gorr's daughter, brought back by his final wish. Thor raises her.

Easy-to-miss details

A jealous axe

Stormbreaker seems to sulk whenever Thor pays attention to Mjolnir.

Another play

New Asgard stages a play about Asgard's history.

Screaming goats

The two giant goats pulling Thor's ship never stop screaming.

The ending and post-creditsSpoilers

After the end credits

Mid-credits: Zeus sends his son Hercules after Thor. Post-credits: Jane is welcomed into Valhalla.

My takeOpinion

The constant jokes split opinion, but the villain Gorr's sorrow is real. Jane's choice, and what "love" turns out to mean at the end, make for an honestly good story, I think.
