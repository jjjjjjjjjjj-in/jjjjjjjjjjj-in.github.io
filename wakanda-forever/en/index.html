Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

It continues Black Panther

The first film makes Wakanda's people and ways much easier to follow.

It begins with T'Challa's death

After Chadwick Boseman, who played T'Challa, passed away, the story also has T'Challa die. The whole film is a farewell to him.

A new nation: Talokan

Another vibranium-rich civilization, hidden beneath the sea, is introduced.

There's a mid-credits scene

An important scene that points to what comes next.

StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

1. The king dies

T'Challa dies of an illness. His sister Shuri blames herself for failing to save him and buries her grief in her work.

2. Vibranium in demand

A year later, Queen Ramonda rules. Nations around the world start searching for vibranium outside Wakanda.

3. Namor of the sea

When vibranium is found on the ocean floor, Namor, king of Talokan, comes to Wakanda and demands the scientist who built the detector.

4. Riri Williams

Shuri and Okoye find the detector's inventor: Riri, a U.S. college student. Then Namor's warriors capture Shuri and Riri.

5. The kingdom of Talokan

Namor shows Shuri his kingdom and his past, and proposes they join forces against the outside world.

6. The queen falls

Nakia rescues Shuri, but an angry Namor attacks Wakanda, and Queen Ramonda is killed.

7. A new Black Panther

Shuri recreates the herb, takes it herself and becomes the new Black Panther. On the Ancestral Plane, she meets not her brother but Killmonger.

8. Revenge, or something else

In the battle at sea, Shuri gets Namor at her mercy, but remembers her mother's words and chooses peace over revenge. At last she quietly mourns her brother.

Key terms

Talokan
A nation beneath the sea that has secretly thrived for centuries thanks to vibranium.
Namor
The king of Talokan. He has small wings on his ankles and can fly.
The vibranium detector
A machine Riri built that can find vibranium underwater, which sets off the conflict.
The heart-shaped herb
The plant that grants the Black Panther's powers. Killmonger burned it, so Shuri tries to recreate it.
Midnight Angels
New flying armor worn by Wakandan warriors.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why did T'Challa die?

In the film, of an illness. After Chadwick Boseman's death, the filmmakers chose not to recast the role and to say goodbye within the story.

Why does Namor come to Wakanda?

If the world keeps hunting vibranium, Talokan will be found. He wants the detector's inventor gone and Wakanda on his side.

Why did Shuri see Killmonger?

On the Ancestral Plane, you meet who your heart seeks. Shuri's desire for revenge seems to have called him.

What happened to M'Baku?

He helps Wakanda and supports it as a member of the council.

What's the mid-credits scene?

We learn Nakia had a son with T'Challa.

Easy-to-miss details

The Marvel logo

The opening Marvel logo is made up entirely of images of T'Challa (Chadwick Boseman), in silence.

Riri Williams

Riri, the genius teen who builds her own suit, later headlines Ironheart.

Everett Ross and Valentina

CIA agent Ross and his boss Valentina appear. Valentina is important in later films.
