Part 1: The king dies

1-1Shuri in the lab

A lab in the Wakandan palace. Princess Shuri, sister of King T'Challa, is working frantically with her team.

T'Challa is terminally ill. Shuri believes the heart-shaped herb, which gives the Black Panther his powers, can cure him. Killmonger burned the herb in the first film, so she is trying to recreate it synthetically.

1-2Too late

The herb comes out incomplete. Clinging to a slim hope, Shuri rushes toward her brother's room.

Her mother, Ramonda, comes out and tells her that T'Challa has gone to join his father T'Chaka and the ancestors. Shuri was too late to save him.

1-3The white procession

Dressed in white, the people of Wakanda sing and dance as they carry T'Challa's coffin away. Shuri and Ramonda walk in white mourning clothes.

The Marvel Studios logo that follows shows only T'Challa instead of the usual crowd of heroes, and it plays in silence.

Part 2: One year later

2-1A Wakandan outpost under attack

One year later, French mercenaries raid a Wakandan outpost in Mali, trying to steal vibranium.

Wakanda's warriors capture them. With T'Challa gone and no Black Panther to protect it, Wakanda faces nations trying to take its vibranium by force.

2-2The queen at the U.N.

At the United Nations, nations press Queen Ramonda to share Wakanda's vibranium. She protests the raid, and has the Dora Milaje march the captured mercenaries into the hall.

Ramonda warns that if this continues, Wakanda is ready to fight, refuses to let any vibranium leave the country, and walks out.

2-3A mother's request

Ramonda asks Shuri to keep working on the herb so Wakanda can have a new Black Panther.

Shuri refuses. Her brother is gone, and she believes the Black Panther is no longer needed. She shuts herself away in her lab instead of facing her grief.

2-4A dive in the Atlantic

A platform in the Atlantic. The CIA is using a vibranium detector to search for vibranium under the sea. Graham, who runs the operation, watches as two researchers dive.

The detector finds a vibranium deposit on the seabed. Soon after, contact with the two divers is lost.

2-5People from the sea

A strange song rises from the water, and the guards start leaping into the sea one after another.

With the platform left unguarded, blue-skinned warriors who breathe underwater storm it, led by the warrior Namora and the general Attuma. The expedition is wiped out.

Graham tries to escape by helicopter, but Namor, a man who flies, smashes the helicopter down into the sea.

2-6Wakanda under suspicion

The CIA suspects Wakanda was behind the attack.

After all, the whole world believes Wakanda is the only place that has vibranium.

2-7Burning the mourning clothes

Ramonda coaxes Shuri out of the lab and they go for a drive together.

There, Ramonda burns the white clothes she wore to the funeral, marking the end of the year of mourning. She urges Shuri to do the same, but Shuri says she can't yet.

2-8Namor's demand

Then Namor appears, a man with small wings on his ankles. He has slipped past Wakanda's tight border defenses without anyone noticing.

He reveals that the CIA has used a detector to find vibranium under the sea, and blames Wakanda for the race to find it, since it started when Wakanda showed itself to the world.

His demand: find the scientist who built the detector and hand that person over. Refuse, and he will attack Wakanda with his army. He warns that if they tell any other nation about him, he will treat that nation as an enemy too, then leaves.

Part 3: The student who built the detector

3-1Visiting Ross

Shuri and Okoye travel to Alexandria, Virginia, to see their old ally Everett Ross, who works for the CIA.

Ross tells them the detector was built by Riri Williams, a student at MIT. In doing so, he is secretly passing information to Wakanda.

3-2The MIT dorm

The two head to MIT in Cambridge, Massachusetts. Shuri slips into the university and meets Riri in her dorm room.

Riri is young, but a genius who built the detector herself. Once Shuri explains the danger, Riri is reluctant at first, but finally agrees to come.

3-3Riri's workshop

The three go to Riri's workshop. Shuri notices equipment that looks like Stark Industries tech, Tony Stark's company, and asks Riri about it.

3-4Chased by the FBI

The FBI arrives.

Shuri escapes on a motorcycle, then regroups with Okoye and Riri.

3-5Shuri and Riri are taken

Then Namor's warriors ambush them. Okoye fights but is beaten.

Shuri and Riri are captured and taken underwater to Namor.

3-6Okoye loses her rank

Back in Wakanda, Ramonda angrily confronts Okoye for failing to protect Shuri.

Ramonda strips her of her title as general of the Dora Milaje.

3-7Nakia in Haiti

Ramonda travels to Haiti. Nakia, T'Challa's beloved and a former Wakandan spy, has been living there ever since the Blip.

Ramonda asks her to find Shuri, and Nakia agrees.

Part 4: Talokan, the kingdom under the sea

4-1Namor and Shuri

Shuri comes face to face with Namor. Treating her as royalty, he takes her down to see his kingdom.

4-2The kingdom of Talokan

On the seabed lies Talokan, a nation grown rich on vibranium and hidden from the surface world for centuries. Its blue-skinned people live full lives under the sea.

They revere Namor as K'uk'ulkan, the feathered serpent god.

4-3Yucatán, 1571

Namor tells how Talokan began. In 1571, on Mexico's Yucatán Peninsula, smallpox brought by the Spanish was devastating his ancestors.

They found a strange vibranium-laced plant that grows under the sea and took it as medicine. Namor's mother, pregnant with him, took it too.

It protected them from the disease, but turned their skin blue and gave them gills, making it hard to breathe on land. So they moved into the sea and built a new nation, Talokan.

4-4The child without love

Namor was born different: pointed ears, wings on his ankles that let him fly, and the ability to breathe both in water and in air.

When his mother died, he went up onto land to bury her in her homeland. There he found his people enslaved and brutalized by the Spanish. Enraged, he fought them.

The Spanish called him "niño sin amor," the child without love, and that became his name, Namor. His hatred of the surface world began there.

4-5An offer of alliance

Namor proposes that Wakanda and Talokan join forces against the rest of the world, since both nations have vibranium and both are targets.

But if Wakanda refuses, he says, he will destroy Wakanda first. Shuri cannot answer right away.

4-6Nakia's rescue

Nakia tracks down where Shuri and Riri were taken and sneaks in.

While Ramonda is meeting with Namor, Nakia frees Shuri and Riri.

During the escape, Nakia fights and kills Talokanil guards. The deaths of his people send Namor into a fury.

Part 5: The attack on Wakanda

5-1Attack from the sea

Namor strikes back at once. Talokanil warriors attack with water-based weapons, and the surprised Wakandans are overwhelmed.

The capital is flooded, and many lives are lost.

5-2Water in the throne room

Namor reaches the palace and floods the throne room with a water bomb. Ramonda and Riri are swept under.

Ramonda pulls Riri up to the surface.

5-3Queen Ramonda dies

When help arrives, Riri is revived. But Ramonda has already drowned, having saved Riri at the cost of her own life.

Having lost her father and brother, and now her mother, Shuri is consumed by grief and rage.

5-4An answer in a week

Namor leaves, vowing to return with his army in a week for Wakanda's answer: alliance or destruction.

For their safety, the people of Wakanda move to the mountains of the Jabari, led by M'Baku.

5-5Ross is arrested

Back in America, Ross is arrested by CIA director Valentina Allegra de Fontaine, who is also his ex-wife.

She has discovered he was secretly passing classified information to Wakanda.

Part 6: A new Black Panther

6-1The queen's funeral

Ramonda's funeral is held. Only a year after burying her brother, Shuri now buries her mother.

6-2Recreating the herb

Shuri realizes that an ornament Namor gave her contains a piece of the very plant that gave his people their powers.

Using it, she finally succeeds in recreating the heart-shaped herb.

6-3Killmonger on the Ancestral Plane

Shuri takes the herb herself and travels to the Ancestral Plane. She hopes to see her mother or brother, but the one waiting for her is her cousin, Erik Killmonger.

Killmonger suggests she is like him, since she wants revenge on Namor for her mother's death, and asks whether she will seek vengeance or be as honorable as T'Challa. Shuri denies she is like him.

6-4Awakened power

When Shuri wakes, she finds she now has the powers of the Black Panther.

She puts on a new Black Panther suit.

6-5The council of tribes

In the Jabari mountains, the leaders of Wakanda's tribes gather. Shuri arrives wearing the Black Panther suit.

The tribes accept her as the new Black Panther and protector of Wakanda.

6-6M'Baku objects

M'Baku urges Shuri to seek a peaceful way instead of war with Namor.

But with her mother dead, Shuri burns for revenge. She ignores him and orders an immediate counterattack on Talokan.

6-7Preparing for battle

Ayo becomes the new general of the Dora Milaje.

Shuri gives Okoye the new flying Midnight Angel armor, and Okoye recruits Aneka of the Dora Milaje to join her.

Riri, working with Shuri, builds a new Iron Man-like suit and joins Wakanda in the fight.

Part 7: The battle at sea

7-1The decoy ship

The Wakandans head out to sea on their ship, the Sea Leopard. The plan is to run a vibranium detector on deck and lure Namor and his warriors to the surface.

It works: the Talokanil burst from the sea and the battle begins.

7-2Fighting on deck

On deck, the Dora Milaje, Okoye and Aneka in their Midnight Angel armor, and Riri in her suit fight the Talokanil fiercely.

Namora and Attuma are among the attackers, and the battle grows more and more intense.

7-3The trap in the jet

Shuri's goal is to separate Namor from the water, since he weakens as he dries out.

She traps Namor inside a fighter jet and takes off, trying to dry him out and weaken him.

7-4Crash on the beach

Namor fights back inside the jet, and it crashes on a desert beach. Shuri and Namor face each other one-on-one, far from the water.

Namor's spear runs Shuri through. Even so, she fights back, corners him and gains the upper hand.

7-5A vision of her mother

As Shuri is about to finish him, she sees a vision of her mother, Ramonda.

Instead of revenge, she chooses to show what Wakanda truly is.

7-6Surrender and alliance

Shuri demands that Namor yield, and offers a peaceful alliance instead of war.

Namor accepts and calls off his warriors. The battle at sea is over.

Part 8: After the battle

8-1Namora is unhappy

Namor's cousin Namora can't accept Namor's surrender.

But Namor believes the alliance will make Talokan stronger. If the surface nations come for Wakanda's vibranium someday, he says, Wakanda will have to turn to Talokan.

8-2Planting the herb

Shuri plants more of the heart-shaped herb so that the mantle of the Black Panther can be passed on in the future.

8-3Riri goes home, Ross is freed

Riri returns to MIT, leaving her suit behind in Wakanda.

Okoye rescues Ross from custody.

8-4A challenge for the throne

In Wakanda, the ceremony to choose a new ruler is held, but Shuri doesn't attend.

In her absence, M'Baku steps forward to challenge for the throne.

8-5In Haiti

Meanwhile, Shuri has gone to see Nakia in Haiti.

She finally burns the white robe from her brother's funeral. As her mother wished, she at last lets herself grieve for T'Challa.

8-6T'Challa's son (mid-credits)

In a mid-credits scene, Nakia introduces Shuri to a little boy: the son Nakia and T'Challa had together, whom she has been raising in secret in Haiti.

He goes by Toussaint, but reveals that his Wakandan name is T'Challa.

Summary

The main threads (who did what, and what came of it)

- Shuri: fails to save T'Challa (Part 1) and buries herself in her work, unable to burn her mourning clothes (Part 2). After losing her mother, she recreates the herb using the plant in Namor's gift and becomes the new Black Panther (Part 6). Bent on revenge, she orders a counterattack, but in the end spares Namor and forms an alliance (Part 7), then burns her mourning robe in Haiti and grieves her brother (Part 8).

- Namor: demands that Wakanda hand over the detector's inventor (Part 2). He offers Shuri an alliance (Part 4) and, after his people are killed, attacks Wakanda (Part 5). In the end he yields to Shuri and accepts the alliance (Part 7).

- Ramonda: rules and supports Shuri, strips Okoye of her rank and asks Nakia for help (Part 3). She dies saving Riri during Namor's attack (Part 5).

- Okoye: loses her rank after failing to protect Shuri (Part 3), but fights as a Midnight Angel (Part 7) and rescues Ross (Part 8).

- Riri: becomes a target for building the detector (Part 3). She fights alongside Wakanda in her suit (Part 7) and returns to MIT without it (Part 8).

- Ross: arrested by his ex-wife Valentina for passing information to Wakanda (Part 5); rescued by Okoye (Part 8).

Who died (in order)

- T'Challa (Part 1): dies of his illness. Shuri tried to save him by recreating the herb, but was too late.

- The Atlantic expedition (Part 2): wiped out by Talokanil warriors led by Namora and Attuma because they found vibranium. Graham, trying to flee, is smashed into the sea in his helicopter by Namor.

- Talokanil guards (Part 4): killed by Nakia during her rescue of Shuri and Riri, which triggers Namor's attack.

- Wakandans and Queen Ramonda (Part 5): in revenge for his people, Namor attacks Wakanda and floods the capital. Ramonda is engulfed by a water bomb in the throne room, pulls Riri to the surface, and drowns.
