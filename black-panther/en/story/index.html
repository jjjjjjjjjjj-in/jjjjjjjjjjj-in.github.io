Part 1: How Wakanda began

1-1Metal from the sky

In darkness, a child asks his father for a story about home, and the father begins.

Long ago, a meteorite made of the strange metal vibranium fell in Africa, and five tribes fought over the land. One warrior ate a heart-shaped herb changed by the metal and became the first Black Panther, with superhuman strength.

Four of the tribes united under him to form the nation of Wakanda. Only the Jabari, who live in the mountains, refused to join.

1-2The hidden nation

Over centuries, Wakanda used vibranium to build extraordinarily advanced technology.

To stay out of the world's wars, it hid its wealth, posed as a poor farming country, and kept itself apart from the rest of the world.

1-3Oakland, 1992

Oakland, California, 1992. In an apartment, N'Jobu, a Wakandan agent living undercover, plans something with a partner over guns and maps. Outside, kids are playing basketball.

N'Jobu's older brother, King T'Chaka of Wakanda, arrives in the Black Panther suit with two warriors of his guard.

T'Chaka accuses N'Jobu of helping the arms dealer Ulysses Klaue steal vibranium from Wakanda. N'Jobu denies it, but his partner reveals that he is Zuri, a fellow Wakandan who had been watching him.

Outside, the kids look up as a strange glowing aircraft lifts into the sky and disappears.

Part 2: The new king

2-1Rescue in the forest

The present. After his father T'Chaka was killed in a bombing, his son T'Challa is about to return to Wakanda to become king.

First, T'Challa and Okoye, leader of the royal guard the Dora Milaje, head to a forest in Nigeria. Nakia, a Wakandan spy and T'Challa's ex, has slipped into a convoy of kidnapped women, posing as one of them.

As Black Panther, T'Challa attacks the trucks and takes down the armed men. The moment he sees Nakia he freezes, and Okoye teases him. Nakia sends a boy soldier home unharmed.

Nakia is annoyed that her mission was cut short, but T'Challa asks her to attend his coronation and brings her back to Wakanda.

2-2The golden city

Their craft passes through a holographic barrier that looks like forest and enters Wakanda's capital: a city of towers and flying vehicles, more advanced than anywhere on Earth.

His mother Ramonda and younger sister Shuri greet him. Shuri teases her brother as they get ready for the ceremony.

2-3The ritual at the falls

The coronation takes place on the rocks below a huge waterfall, with the tribes watching from the cliffs.

The elder Zuri gives T'Challa a drink that takes away the Black Panther's powers. Zuri asks whether anyone will challenge for the throne, and no tribe steps forward. Then the Jabari, who never joined the nation, appear.

2-4M'Baku's challenge

M'Baku, leader of the Jabari, challenges T'Challa for the throne. Without his powers, T'Challa fights with his hands and a spear.

Pushed to the edge, he rallies when his mother Ramonda calls out to him, and pins M'Baku. T'Challa urges him to yield rather than die, and M'Baku yields.

T'Challa becomes king of Wakanda.

2-5The Ancestral Plane

That night T'Challa drinks the heart-shaped herb and gets the Black Panther's powers back. Zuri and the others lay him in red sand.

His mind travels to the Ancestral Plane, a grassland under a purple sky. Among the black panthers in a tree, his late father T'Chaka appears.

T'Challa admits he doesn't know how to rule without him. His father tells him how hard it is for a good man to be king.

Part 3: The stolen axe

3-1The London museum

A museum in London. A man named Erik asks a staff member about the African exhibits and tells her that one axe, labeled as coming from another country, is really Wakandan and made of vibranium.

She collapses, poisoned by her coffee. The men dressed as paramedics are Klaue and his crew, along with Erik's partner Linda. They take out the guards and steal the axe. Erik also takes a mask from the display.

3-2W'Kabi's anger

News that Klaue has resurfaced quickly reaches Wakanda. W'Kabi, chief of the Border Tribe, T'Challa's best friend and Okoye's husband, is furious: when Klaue stole vibranium years ago, he killed W'Kabi's parents.

T'Challa promises to bring Klaue back to Wakanda.

3-3Nakia's wish

Nakia has traveled the world and seen people suffering. She believes Wakanda should use its power to take in refugees and send aid.

T'Challa wants her to stay in Wakanda, but worries that opening the country could cost it everything it has protected. W'Kabi argues that letting outsiders in would bring their problems too.

3-4Shuri's lab

They learn that Klaue plans to sell the stolen axe in Busan, South Korea.

Before leaving, T'Challa visits Shuri's lab. She shows him shoes that silence footsteps, tools for driving vehicles remotely, and a new Black Panther suit that folds into a necklace. The suit absorbs the energy of blows and can release it back.

Part 4: A night in Busan

4-1Into the casino

T'Challa, Okoye and Nakia slip into an underground casino in Busan. Okoye hates having to wear a wig.

CIA agent Everett Ross is there as Klaue's buyer, ready to pay for the axe with diamonds. T'Challa, who has met Ross before, tells him that he will be taking Klaue.

4-2The shootout

During the deal Klaue realizes Wakandans are present, and a shootout breaks out. Okoye and Nakia fight his men hard.

Klaue escapes outside, firing the sonic cannon built into his artificial arm.

4-3The Busan chase

Klaue and his men flee by car. Nakia and Okoye give chase, while T'Challa rides on the roof of a car that Shuri drives remotely from her lab in Wakanda.

Klaue blasts the pursuing cars with his sonic cannon. T'Challa releases the energy stored in his suit to flip Klaue's car and catches him.

4-4Ross's interrogation

With bystanders filming on their phones, T'Challa spares Klaue and hands him over to Ross.

At a CIA safe house, Ross questions Klaue, who reveals that Wakanda is no poor country but a highly advanced civilization built on vibranium.

4-5Erik's attack

Erik, Linda and their crew attack and break Klaue out. Ross takes a bullet in the back protecting Nakia and is badly hurt.

T'Challa goes after Erik but stops short when he sees the ring hanging from Erik's neck: a ring of the Wakandan royal family. Erik and Klaue get away.

4-6Taking Ross to Wakanda

Ross won't survive without help. T'Challa gives up the chase and takes Ross to Wakanda, where its technology can save him.

Part 5: His father's secret

5-1W'Kabi's disappointment

Shuri begins treating Ross.

W'Kabi, learning that T'Challa failed to bring Klaue back, is deeply disappointed in him.

5-2Zuri's confession

T'Challa goes to Zuri and confronts him, because Erik wore the royal ring.

Zuri tells him the truth about 1992. In America, N'Jobu saw people of African descent suffering around the world and planned, with Klaue's help, to give them Wakanda's technology and weapons so they could rise up.

When T'Chaka moved to bring him home, N'Jobu attacked Zuri, and T'Chaka killed him to protect Zuri. T'Chaka ordered Zuri to say that N'Jobu had disappeared and, to keep that story, left N'Jobu's young son behind in America.

T'Challa is shaken by what the father he revered had done.

5-3The end of Linda and Klaue

Meanwhile, Erik and Klaue's crew have fled to an airfield.

Realizing that Erik means to go to Wakanda, Klaue grabs Erik's girlfriend Linda as a shield and points a gun. Erik shoots Linda without hesitation, then shoots Klaue dead.

5-4A delivery at the border

Erik arrives at Wakanda's border with Klaue's body. W'Kabi, who hated Klaue, brings him to the throne room in the capital.

5-5Who Erik is

Waking up in Shuri's lab, Ross sees Erik and recognizes him: a U.S. Navy SEAL with an American university education, who killed people in secret operations to bring down foreign governments, nicknamed Killmonger.

In the throne room, Erik reveals that his real name is N'Jadaka and that he is N'Jobu's son. As one of royal blood, he challenges T'Challa for the throne, and T'Challa accepts.

Part 6: The throne is taken

6-1The duel at the falls

The ritual at the falls is held again. Erik's body is covered in scars, one for each person he has killed.

Shaken, T'Challa is overwhelmed. When Zuri steps in, Erik stabs him to death, blaming him for not protecting N'Jobu.

Erik defeats T'Challa and throws him off the falls. T'Challa is presumed dead, and Erik becomes king.

6-2Erik's Ancestral Plane

Erik also takes the heart-shaped herb and goes to the Ancestral Plane. For him, it isn't a grassland but the Oakland apartment.

Erik, as a boy, meets his father N'Jobu. As a child, Erik had found his father's body. N'Jobu is saddened that his son sheds no tears for him.

6-3Burning the herb

When he wakes, Erik orders the entire herb garden burned, grabbing the throat of the elderly woman who tries to refuse. Now no one else can become Black Panther.

With W'Kabi's forces, Erik prepares to ship Wakandan weapons to operatives in cities around the world.

6-4Nakia's choice

Nakia manages to save a single herb before the rest are burned.

She asks Okoye to come with her, but Okoye stays, saying her loyalty is to the throne. Nakia, Shuri, Ramonda and Ross flee the capital.

6-5The Jabari mountains

Ramonda urges Nakia to take the herb and fight, but Nakia plans to give it to M'Baku so he can fight, and the four head for the snowy Jabari mountains.

M'Baku leads them to T'Challa, lying unconscious in the snow. A Jabari fisherman found him in the river, and in return for T'Challa sparing M'Baku's life, they have kept him alive packed in snow.

6-6Words for his father

Given Nakia's herb, T'Challa goes to the Ancestral Plane.

He tells his father and the ancestors that it was wrong to turn their backs on the world and to abandon that boy, and resolves as king to set it right.

T'Challa wakes with his powers restored. M'Baku at first declines to join the fight.

Part 7: The battle for Wakanda

7-1The king returns

As planes loaded with weapons prepare to take off, T'Challa appears in a suit Shuri made and declares that the challenge is not over.

Erik meets him in a golden Black Panther suit. Okoye and the Dora Milaje side with the living T'Challa, while W'Kabi and the Border Tribe soldiers side with Erik.

7-2Ross in the sky

Shuri and Nakia fight alongside the Dora Milaje.

Ross, a former Air Force pilot, remotely flies a fighter jet from Shuri's lab and shoots down the weapons planes one by one before they can leave Wakanda.

7-3The Jabari arrive

As T'Challa's side is pushed back, M'Baku arrives leading the Jabari warriors.

7-4Okoye and W'Kabi

W'Kabi charges on an armored rhino, trying to trample M'Baku and the others. Okoye stands in his way with her spear, saying she will choose her country over their love.

Unable to kill her or be killed by her, W'Kabi kneels and surrenders, and the Border Tribe soldiers follow.

7-5The duel underground

T'Challa and Erik fall into the underground vibranium mine and keep fighting.

The mine's tracks disrupt both suits. T'Challa uses this to break Erik's suit and stabs him in the chest with a spear blade.

7-6The sunset

T'Challa carries the wounded Erik up the mountain to see the Wakandan sunset Erik's father N'Jobu told him about when he was a boy.

T'Challa says he can still be healed. But Erik chooses to die free rather than live locked up, and asks to be buried in the ocean with his ancestors who jumped from the ships, because they knew death was better than bondage.

Erik pulls out the blade himself and dies watching the sunset.

Part 8: Opening up to the world

8-1The building in Oakland

Some time later, T'Challa brings Shuri to that apartment building in Oakland. Kids who were playing basketball gather around their ship.

T'Challa explains that he has bought the buildings. It will be Wakanda's first outreach center to the world, run by Nakia and Shuri.

8-2Mid-credits

At the United Nations, T'Challa reveals Wakanda's true nature and announces that it will share its knowledge and technology with the world.

8-3After the credits

In a hut in Wakanda, Bucky Barnes wakes up with Shuri beside him, while children outside call him the White Wolf. Shuri is helping him recover.

Summary

Killmonger's anger and plan

Erik (N'Jadaka) is N'Jobu's son. In 1992 his father was killed by T'Chaka and he was left behind in America (Parts 1 and 5).

He learned to kill as a U.S. soldier, teamed up with Klaue to get close to Wakanda, then killed Klaue and used his body to get into the country (Parts 3-5).

He beat T'Challa in the duel, became king, burned the herbs, and set out to ship Wakandan weapons to operatives worldwide so oppressed people could rise up (Part 6).

The path T'Challa chose

T'Challa was deeply troubled to learn that his father killed N'Jobu and abandoned his son (Part 5).

On the Ancestral Plane he told his father and ancestors they were wrong to turn their backs on the world (Part 6). After defeating Erik, he opened an outreach center in Oakland and announced at the UN that Wakanda would open up to the world (Part 8).

Who died (in order)

- N'Jobu (Part 5, events of 1992): killed by his brother T'Chaka, who was protecting Zuri after N'Jobu attacked him.

- Linda (Part 5): Erik's girlfriend. Klaue took her hostage, and Erik shot her without hesitation.

- Ulysses Klaue (Part 5): shot dead by Erik, whose body became Erik's ticket into Wakanda.

- Zuri (Part 6): stabbed to death by Erik while trying to stop the duel at the falls.

- Erik Killmonger (Part 7): stabbed by T'Challa in the mine duel; he refused healing and died watching the sunset.
