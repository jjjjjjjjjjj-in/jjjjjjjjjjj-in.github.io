Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

It follows Civil War

It begins after King T'Chaka's death in the Vienna bombing. You can follow it without having seen Civil War.

Wakanda is a secret

To the outside world it looks poor, but it's actually the most technologically advanced nation on Earth.

The villain has his reasons

Killmonger's anger is rooted in Wakanda's own past.

There are scenes during and after the credits

The final one features a familiar face.

My takeOpinion

The film's strength is that you find yourself agreeing with the villain, Killmonger. I love that T'Challa, after winning, goes on to change how his own country does things.

StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

1. How Wakanda began

Long ago a vibranium meteorite fell in Africa, and Wakanda was founded under the first Black Panther, empowered by a heart-shaped herb. Wakanda hid its technology and posed as a poor country.

In Oakland, California, in 1992, King T'Chaka confronts his brother N'Jobu for helping the arms dealer Klaue steal vibranium.

2. The new king

After his father dies in a bombing, T'Challa and Okoye bring the spy Nakia back from a forest in Nigeria and return to Wakanda.

At the ritual at the falls, M'Baku of the Jabari challenges him for the throne. T'Challa wins, spares him and is crowned, then meets his late father on the Ancestral Plane.

3. The stolen axe

At a London museum, a man named Erik teams up with Klaue to steal a Wakandan axe made of vibranium.

W'Kabi, whose parents Klaue killed, is furious, and T'Challa promises to bring Klaue back. Shuri gives her brother a new suit.

4. A night in Busan

T'Challa, Okoye and Nakia infiltrate a casino in Busan and run into CIA agent Ross. After a shootout and a car chase they catch Klaue and hand him to Ross.

But Erik attacks and breaks Klaue out, and Ross is badly hurt protecting Nakia. T'Challa takes Ross to Wakanda to be healed.

5. His father's secret

The elder Zuri reveals that in 1992 T'Chaka killed N'Jobu and left his young son behind in America.

Erik kills Klaue, arrives in Wakanda with the body, reveals he is N'Jobu's son N'Jadaka, and challenges T'Challa for the throne.

6. The throne is taken

In the duel at the falls, Erik (Killmonger) kills Zuri when he intervenes, throws T'Challa off the falls and becomes king.

He burns the herbs and prepares to ship Wakanda's weapons worldwide. Nakia flees with the last herb and the others to the Jabari mountains, where they revive the unconscious T'Challa.

7. The battle for Wakanda

Okoye's guard and the Jabari side with the returned T'Challa, and Ross remotely flies a jet to shoot down the weapons planes. Facing Okoye's spear, W'Kabi surrenders.

In a duel in the underground mine, T'Challa stabs Erik, who refuses healing and dies watching the Wakandan sunset.

8. Opening up to the world

T'Challa buys the Oakland building where N'Jobu died and makes it Wakanda's first outreach center, run by Nakia and Shuri.

Then, at the United Nations, he announces that Wakanda will share its knowledge and technology with the world.

Key terms

Wakanda
An African nation with advanced vibranium technology, which has hidden its true nature from the world for generations.
Vibranium
Metal from a meteorite that fell in Wakanda long ago. Incredibly strong, and it absorbs energy.
The heart-shaped herb
A special plant that grants the Black Panther's powers.
The Dora Milaje
The elite all-female guard that protects the king, led by Okoye.
The Jabari
A mountain tribe that keeps its distance from the others, led by M'Baku.
The Ancestral Plane
A mystical place where a king who takes the herb can meet his ancestors.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why does Killmonger hate Wakanda?

T'Chaka killed his father N'Jobu and left him behind in America. He's also furious that Wakanda abandoned people suffering around the world.

Why did T'Challa lose the first duel?

The duel is an old Wakandan tradition, and T'Challa was shaken after learning of his father's mistake.

Who is Everett Ross?

A CIA agent. He's wounded protecting Nakia, is treated in Wakanda, and helps in the final battle.

Who is the man in the final scene?

Bucky, the Winter Soldier. Wakanda took him in after Civil War.

Easy-to-miss details

Shuri's inventions

T'Challa's sister Shuri is a genius who builds his new suit and gadgets.

The final sunset

Killmonger watching the Wakandan sunset his father once described to him is one of the film's most moving scenes.

"Wakanda Forever"

The crossed-arms salute is Wakanda's signature greeting.

Stan Lee's cameo

Marvel Comics legend Stan Lee appears as a gambler at the casino in Korea.
