StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

Part 1: From 5000 BC to 500 years ago

In 5000 BC, ten Eternals sent by Arishem begin protecting humanity from the Deviants, forbidden to interfere in human conflicts.

After they kill the last Deviants at Tenochtitlan in 1521, the group disagrees about humanity and splits up. Ikaris leaves Sersi, the woman he loved.

Part 2: A monster in London and Ajak's death

In present-day London, the Deviant Kro attacks Sersi and Sprite, and Ikaris comes to the rescue.

Their leader Ajak is found dead in South Dakota, and her power passes to Sersi. Connecting with Arishem, Sersi learns that the Celestial Tiamut is growing inside Earth, that its birth will destroy the planet, and that the Eternals were there to help humanity grow for that purpose.

Part 3: Gathering the family

Sersi's group brings back Kingo, a Bollywood star in Mumbai; Gilgamesh and Thena in Australia; and Phastos, who lives with his family in Chicago.

Deviants attack Druig's village in the Amazon. The Eternals win the fight, but Kro kills Gilgamesh.

Part 4: The Domo and the split

They find Makkari at the Domo, buried in Iraq, and Phastos proposes using the Uni-Mind to put Tiamut to sleep.

But Ikaris reveals that he led Ajak to the Deviants and let her die. Sprite sides with him, and Kingo leaves rather than fight his friends.

Part 5: The Emergence in the Indian Ocean

At a volcano in the Indian Ocean, the Eternals fight Ikaris, Sprite and Kro, and Thena kills Kro.

Ikaris can't bring himself to kill Sersi, and he and Sprite join the Uni-Mind. Sersi turns Tiamut to stone and saves Earth. Guilt-ridden, Ikaris flies into the Sun, and Sersi makes Sprite human.

Part 6: Separate paths and Arishem's judgment

Thena, Druig and Makkari set off into space to warn Eternals on other planets.

Sersi, Phastos and Kingo, who stayed on Earth, are taken away by Arishem to await his judgment on whether humanity will live.

Key terms

The Celestials
Colossal cosmic beings who create stars and life.
Arishem
The Celestial who gives the Eternals their orders.
The Deviants
Monsters that prey on humans and animals.
The Emergence
The birth of a new Celestial growing inside the Earth.
The Uni-Mind
A way of channeling all the Eternals' powers through one of them.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

What was the Eternals' real purpose?

A new Celestial, Tiamut, was growing inside the Earth. The Eternals protected humanity until there were enough people for it to be born, which would destroy the planet.

Why didn't they help against Thanos?

Arishem forbade them from interfering in any conflict that didn't involve the Deviants.

What's the giant figure left in the ocean?

Tiamut, the half-born Celestial, turned to stone by Sersi. In Brave New World it's called Celestial Island.

Easy-to-miss details

Kingo's movies

Kingo has been a Bollywood star for generations, posing as his own descendants.

A DC joke

There's a joke mentioning Superman and Batman.

A deaf hero

Makkari is the MCU's first deaf superhero, who speaks in sign language.

My takeOpinion

It divides people, but "Is it enough just to watch over humanity?" is one of the bigger questions in the MCU. For sheer beauty of scenery, I think it's the best in the series.
