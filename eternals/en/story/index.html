Part 1: Seven thousand years ago

Arrival

Mesopotamia, 5000 BC. The ship Domo lands, and ten Eternals emerge. Their leader, Ajak, takes orders from the Celestial Arishem to protect humanity from the Deviants.

Watching over humanity

They fight the Deviants and watch history unfold, but they're forbidden to interfere in human conflicts.

Parting ways

Five hundred years ago, believing the Deviants gone, the Eternals go their separate ways.

Part 2: A monster in London

Sersi today

Present-day London. Sersi works at a museum and is dating Dane Whitman. She lives with Sprite, who looks like a girl and never ages.

The Deviant returns

A Deviant named Kro attacks the city at night. Ikaris, Sersi's old love, flies in, and the three drive it off.

Ajak's death

They go to Ajak in America, only to find her dead. Ajak's power passes to Sersi.

The real mission

As the new leader, Sersi connects with Arishem and learns the truth. A new Celestial is growing inside Earth, and when it's born, Earth will be destroyed. The Eternals were sent to help humanity grow until then.

Part 3: Reuniting the team

Kingo

Kingo, now a Bollywood star, joins them. His valet, Karun, films the journey.

Gilgamesh and Thena

In Australia, Gilgamesh cares for Thena, who suffers from a condition that clouds her mind.

Gilgamesh falls

Kro attacks, and Gilgamesh dies protecting the others.

Phastos

In Chicago, the inventor Phastos lives with his husband and son. He still grieves that humanity used his inventions for war.

Druig and Makkari

In the Amazon, they find Druig, who controls minds. Makkari, the speedster, has been living aboard the Domo.

Part 4: The team divides

The Uni-Mind

Phastos realizes that if all their powers are channeled through Sersi, they could stop the Celestial's birth.

Ikaris's confession

Ikaris reveals he killed Ajak. Loyal to Arishem, he's willing to sacrifice Earth.

Choosing sides

Sprite sides with Ikaris. Kingo refuses to fight either way and leaves.

Part 5: The Emergence

A hand from the sea

In the Indian Ocean, the hand of the Celestial Tiamut begins to break through the Earth.

The final battle

The Eternals fight Ikaris, Sprite and Kro. Thena kills Kro.

Sersi's power

Channeling everyone's power, Sersi turns Tiamut to marble.

Ikaris's choice

Unable to harm Sersi, Ikaris flies into the sun.

Sprite's wish

Sersi turns Sprite human, so she can finally grow up.

Part 6: Afterward

Arishem's judgment

While Thena, Makkari and Druig travel into space, Arishem appears before Sersi, Kingo and Phastos on Earth and takes them, saying he'll judge whether humanity deserves to live.

Mid-credits

Thanos's brother Eros and his companion Pip appear before the three in space.

After the credits

Dane reaches for the black sword he's inherited, and a mysterious voice speaks to him.
