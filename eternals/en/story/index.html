Part 1: From 5000 BC to 500 years ago

1-1Celestials and Deviants

The film opens with text on screen. The giant Celestials have been creating life on planets since the dawn of time.

The Celestial Arishem made the Deviants to wipe out the big predators that threatened life. But the Deviants turned on the planets' people too.

So Arishem created the Eternals and sent them out to protect people from the Deviants.

1-2Mesopotamia, 5000 BC

Mesopotamia, 5000 BC. The starship Domo arrives on Earth.

As villagers are attacked by Deviants, ten Eternals land, kill the monsters and save the people.

They are Ajak, the healer and leader; Sersi, who transforms matter; Ikaris, who flies and fires beams from his eyes; Kingo, who shoots energy from his hands; Sprite, who casts illusions and looks like a child; Phastos, the inventor; Makkari, the speedster; Druig, who controls minds; Gilgamesh, the strongest; and Thena, a warrior who forms weapons of light.

1-3The rule of watching over humanity

Only Ajak can connect with Arishem and receive his orders.

The orders: protect humanity from the Deviants, and stay out of any human conflict that doesn't involve them.

For thousands of years the Eternals fight Deviants around the world and sometimes share their knowledge, watching civilization grow. They live among the people of ancient Babylon, too.

1-4Tenochtitlan, 1521

Tenochtitlan, the Aztec capital, 1521. Spanish forces are attacking the Aztecs.

Here the Eternals kill the last of Earth's Deviants, but they can only watch as the people are slaughtered.

Fed up with the rule, Druig decides to end the fighting with his own power and leaves the group.

1-5Thena's illness

Around this time, Thena falls ill. Her mind is buckling under the weight of countless memories, and at times she loses herself and attacks her friends.

Ajak thinks the only cure is to wipe her memories. Gilgamesh offers instead to stay by Thena's side and look after her.

1-6Five hundred years apart

With the Deviants gone, the Eternals no longer agree on how to deal with humankind.

They scatter, blending in among humans as they wait for Arishem's next orders. Ikaris, who loved Sersi, eventually leaves her without a word.

Part 2: A monster in London and Ajak's death

2-1Sersi in London

Present-day London, some time after the people lost in the Snap have returned.

Sersi works at London's Natural History Museum and is dating Dane Whitman, a human who also works there. At home, she lives with Sprite, who never grows up.

2-2A monster in the night

One night, the Deviant Kro attacks Sersi, Sprite and Dane. The supposedly extinct monster is hunting the Eternals, not humans.

As Sersi and Sprite struggle, Ikaris, whom they haven't seen in centuries, flies in and drives Kro away.

Dane learns that Sersi and Sprite aren't ordinary humans.

2-3Ajak in South Dakota

Why have the Deviants returned? Sersi, Ikaris and Sprite go to see their leader, Ajak, in South Dakota.

But when they arrive, Ajak has already been killed by Deviants.

2-4Sersi is chosen

A glowing sphere rises from Ajak's body and enters Sersi. Ajak had chosen Sersi to succeed her as leader.

Now Sersi can communicate with Arishem.

2-5Arishem reveals the real mission

Connecting with Arishem for the first time, Sersi learns the Eternals' true purpose.

For millions of years Arishem has planted Celestial seeds inside populated planets. When enough life energy builds up, a new Celestial is born: the Emergence.

A Celestial named Tiamut has been growing inside Earth. With the people lost in the Snap restored, humanity is now big enough for it to be born, and its birth will destroy the planet.

2-6What the Eternals really are

Sersi also learns the truth about the Eternals themselves: they were made in a Celestial forge.

Each time a mission on one planet ended, their memories were wiped and they were sent to another. Thena's illness comes from old memories that were never fully erased.

Sersi, Ikaris and Sprite set out to gather their scattered family and find a way to stop the Emergence.

Part 3: Gathering the family

3-1Kingo in Mumbai

First stop: Mumbai, where Kingo has become a Bollywood star. To hide that he never ages, he has kept acting for generations by posing as his own son and grandson.

Hearing the news, Kingo joins them. His valet Karun comes along and starts filming the trip like a documentary.

3-2Gilgamesh and Thena in Australia

Next, the Australian outback, where Gilgamesh lives quietly with Thena, looking after her through her episodes.

The two of them agree to join the fight.

3-3Phastos in Chicago

Sersi and Ikaris go to Chicago for Phastos. Calling himself Phil, he lives with his husband Ben and their ten-year-old son Jack.

Phastos once helped humanity invent many things. But in 1945, when his technology was used for the atomic bomb on Hiroshima, he was crushed and stopped helping. A flashback shows him grieving in the ruins of Hiroshima.

3-4Deciding for his family

At first Phastos refuses; he doesn't want to leave his family.

But with his husband Ben's encouragement, he decides to go and protect the world his family lives in.

3-5Druig in the Amazon

The group seeks out Druig deep in the Amazon rainforest. He has built a small community there and uses his mind control to keep it peaceful.

When he hears the truth about their mission, Druig is furious.

3-6Battle in the forest

Then a pack of Deviants attacks, and the Eternals fight together.

Sersi shows a powerful new ability, turning Deviants into trees. All of the monsters die except Kro.

3-7Gilgamesh's death

Before escaping, Kro kills Gilgamesh. Kro can absorb the powers of the Eternals it kills; it had already taken Ajak's healing. Killing Gilgamesh makes it stronger still, and it grows closer to a thinking being.

The team mourns Gilgamesh, who had protected Thena for so long.

Part 4: The Domo and the split

4-1The Domo and Makkari in Iraq

The group heads to Iraq, near the ancient site of Babylon. The Domo is buried underground there, and Makkari is waiting inside.

Makkari is deaf and talks with the others in sign language. She and Druig have long shared a special bond.

4-2The Uni-Mind plan

Phastos comes up with a plan. The Uni-Mind, a link that channels all the Eternals' powers into one of them, could make Druig's mind control strong enough to put Tiamut to sleep.

That might stop Earth from being destroyed.

4-3Ikaris's confession

But Ikaris objects. He is loyal to Arishem and has no intention of stopping the Emergence.

Then he reveals the truth about Ajak's death. He had known their real mission for a long time; Ajak had told him.

4-4Flashback: Alaskan ice

Flashback. After the people lost in the Snap returned, Ajak changed her mind: humanity must not be destroyed. She told Ikaris she wanted to stop the Emergence.

Seeing that as betrayal of Arishem, Ikaris took her to Alaska, where melting ice was releasing trapped Deviants.

He led Ajak to them and let them kill her, so the others would focus on the Deviants rather than the Emergence.

4-5The family splits

Sersi and the others are shaken. Ikaris means to do his duty and keep them from stopping the Emergence.

Sprite, who has loved Ikaris in silence for ages, goes with him.

Kingo agrees with Ikaris but refuses to fight his friends, and leaves.

4-6Where the Emergence will happen

Sersi, Phastos, Druig, Makkari and Thena remain.

Makkari finds where the Emergence will happen: an active volcano in the Indian Ocean.

Part 5: The Emergence in the Indian Ocean

5-1Ambush at the volcano

The Eternals head to the volcano in the Indian Ocean. The ground shakes, and the giant hand of the half-born Tiamut begins to rise from the sea.

Ikaris and Sprite show up to stop them.

5-2Family against family

The Eternals fight Ikaris and Sprite. Druig knocks Sprite out, and Phastos restrains Ikaris.

5-3Kro's end

Then Kro arrives and attacks the Eternals.

Thena kills Kro, avenging Gilgamesh.

5-4Druig can't stop it

Druig tries to put Tiamut to sleep through the Uni-Mind, but it doesn't work.

So Sersi changes the plan: she will use her power to transform matter to turn Tiamut into stone.

5-5Ikaris can't do it

Ikaris breaks free and goes after Sersi to kill her.

But he cannot bring himself to kill the woman he has loved for thousands of years.

He and Sprite join the Uni-Mind and lend Sersi their power.

5-6Stopping Tiamut

With everyone's power, Sersi turns Tiamut to stone (marble).

The Emergence is stopped and Earth is saved. A colossal stone hand and body are left standing in the sea.

5-7Ikaris's end

Having betrayed both Arishem and his family, Ikaris can't bear the guilt.

He flies up into the sky and straight into the Sun.

5-8Sprite's wish

Sprite has long suffered in a body that can never grow up.

Sersi uses the Uni-Mind's remaining energy to turn Sprite into a human. Now she can age and grow up like anyone else.

Part 6: Separate paths and Arishem's judgment

6-1Three set off into space

Thena, Druig and Makkari leave on the Domo to warn Eternals on other planets about what the Emergence really means.

Sersi, Kingo and Phastos stay on Earth.

6-2Dane's secret

Back in London, Dane tells Sersi he loves her, and he starts to reveal an important secret about his family's history.

6-3Arishem's judgment

In that instant, Sersi, Phastos and Kingo are lifted into space.

Before them looms Arishem, displeased with their treason.

He says he will spare humanity if the Eternals' memories show that humans are worthy of living. Vowing to return for judgment, he takes the three away.

6-4Mid-credits scene

Aboard the Domo, Thena, Druig and Makkari are worried because they can't reach their friends on Earth.

Then a small figure called Pip and a man named Eros appear. Pip introduces Eros as Thanos's brother, and Eros says he knows where their friends have been taken.

6-5Post-credits scene

Dane opens a large wooden crate holding a black sword. When he reaches for it, it shakes and glows.

Then an unseen voice asks whether he is really ready to wield it.

Summary (spoilers through the ending)

The Eternals' real mission

The Eternals believed they were sent to protect humanity from the Deviants. In truth, they were there to help humanity grow until the Celestial Tiamut, growing inside Earth, could be born, destroying the planet (Part 2).

The Eternals themselves were made by the Celestials and had their memories wiped after each planet. Thena's illness came from memories that were never fully erased (Parts 1–2).

Who sided with whom

- To stop the Emergence: Sersi, Phastos, Druig, Makkari, Thena. In the end Ikaris and Sprite joined the Uni-Mind too, and Sersi turned Tiamut to stone (Part 5).

- Loyal to Arishem: Ikaris and Sprite (Parts 4–5).

- Neither: Kingo, who agreed with Ikaris but refused to fight his friends (Part 4).

- At the end: Thena, Druig and Makkari leave on the Domo; Sersi, Kingo and Phastos are taken by Arishem (Part 6).

Who died or was changed (in order)

- Ajak (Parts 2 and 4): because she wanted to stop the Emergence, Ikaris led her to the Deviants in Alaska and let them kill her. Kro gained her healing power.

- Gilgamesh (Part 3): killed by Kro after the Deviant battle in the Amazon. Kro absorbed his power too.

- Kro (Part 5): killed by Thena at the volcano in the Indian Ocean.

- Ikaris (Part 5): unable to kill Sersi and crushed by the guilt of betraying both Arishem and his family, he flew into the Sun.

- Tiamut (Part 5): turned to stone by Sersi through the Uni-Mind before it could be born.

- Sprite (Part 5): not dead. Sersi turned her into a human, so she can finally grow up.
