StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

1. A meeting in 1999

On New Year's Eve 1999, Tony meets scientist Maya. He also brushes off another scientist, Killian, who wanted him to hear his pitch.

2. Tony can't sleep

After the Battle of New York, Tony suffers anxiety attacks and can't sleep, so he keeps building suits. Things get tense with Pepper.

3. The Mandarin strikes

One of the Mandarin's bombings badly injures Happy. Furious, Tony taunts the Mandarin on TV, and his house is destroyed by attack helicopters.

4. Tony without his suit

Crash-landing in rural Tennessee with a broken suit, Tony investigates the bombings with help from a boy named Harley.

5. Who the Mandarin really is

Tony sneaks into the Mandarin's hideout and finds Trevor, an actor playing the part. The real mastermind is Killian, the creator of Extremis.

6. Pepper and the President in danger

Killian kidnaps Pepper and injects her with Extremis, then steals Rhodey's suit and abducts the President.

7. The final battle

Tony and Rhodey call in every suit Tony has built and fight at Killian's base. They save the President, and Pepper, now with Extremis powers, defeats Killian.

8. Even without the suit

Tony destroys all his suits for Pepper and has surgery to remove the shrapnel from his chest. He declares "I am Iron Man," a hero with or without the suit.

Key terms

Extremis
A technology that rewrites the body to make it stronger, even healing injuries. If the body can't handle it, it can explode.
The Mandarin
A mysterious terrorist who claims responsibility for attacks on TV.
A.I.M.
The research company Killian founded, presented as a science firm.
Iron Patriot
Rhodey's War Machine suit, repainted in the flag's colors and renamed by the government.
Mark 42
Tony's new suit, whose pieces fly to him and assemble on his body.
House Party Protocol
Tony's command to call in all the suits he's built at once.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

So what was the Mandarin?

A fake figure Killian created to disguise Extremis failures, people exploding, as terror attacks. Actor Trevor played him on TV.

Why was Tony so anxious?

After facing an alien invasion and gods in The Avengers, he's terrified he can't protect the people he loves.

How could Pepper breathe fire?

Killian injected her with Extremis. In the end Tony removes it and she goes back to normal.

Why did Killian hate Tony?

He never got over being brushed off by Tony back in 1999.

Did Tony quit being Iron Man?

He destroyed his suits and had the reactor removed, but as "I am Iron Man" says, he's still a hero. He returns in later films.
