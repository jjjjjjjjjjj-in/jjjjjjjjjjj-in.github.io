StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

Part 1: Bern, 1999

On New Year's Eve 1999, at a party in Bern, Switzerland, Tony meets botanist Maya and learns of Extremis, her research into rewriting the body so it heals itself.

When a young scientist, Killian, asks for his help, Tony tells him to wait on the roof and never shows up. Killian waits all night.

Part 2: Tony can't sleep

After the Battle of New York, Tony suffers panic attacks and insomnia and keeps building dozens of suits. A suit he summons in a nightmare goes for Pepper.

Meanwhile, the terrorist known as the Mandarin keeps setting off bombings, and Happy, tailing one of Killian's men, is badly hurt in an explosion at a theater.

Part 3: Tony gives his address

Furious, Tony taunts the Mandarin in front of reporters and gives out his home address.

Investigating the theater blast, he finds no sign of a bomb, and a similar case in Rose Hill, Tennessee, where a soldier's body exploded. Then Maya, the woman from 1999, arrives.

Part 4: The house falls into the sea

Gunships attack Tony's house. He puts his suit on Pepper so she can escape with Maya, and sinks into the sea with the collapsing house.

The suit carries him to Tennessee but runs out of power in the snow. The world thinks Tony is dead.

Part 5: The boy in Rose Hill

Tony hides his suit in a boy named Harley's garage and investigates with Harley's help. Chad's mother gives him her son's file on the experiment.

Killian's agents Brandt and Savin attack, but Tony survives without his suit, and Brandt dies in an explosion.

Part 6: Hunting the mastermind

Killian kidnaps Pepper, and Rhodey is captured in Pakistan and loses the Iron Patriot armor.

Tony finds footage of Extremis being tested on veterans and traces the Mandarin's broadcasts to Miami. Armed with homemade weapons, he sneaks into the mansion.

Part 7: Who the Mandarin is

The Mandarin is Trevor, an actor hired to play the part. The real mastermind is Killian, who disguised Extremis explosions as terror attacks.

Killian reveals he gave Pepper Extremis and shoots Maya when she tries to stop him. Tony escapes with the parts of his suit that fly to him.

Part 8: Air Force One

Savin, in the stolen Iron Patriot armor, attacks Air Force One and abducts the President. Flying a suit by remote, Tony kills Savin and saves thirteen people blown out of the plane.

Killian, working with the Vice President, plans to kill the President on live TV.

Part 9: Showdown on the tanker

On an oil tanker, Tony calls in dozens of suits to fight, and Rhodey saves the President. Pepper falls into the flames.

When Tony is cornered, Pepper, alive thanks to Extremis, saves him by taking down Killian. Tony then blows up all his suits like fireworks.

Part 10: Afterward

Tony returns Pepper to normal, has surgery to remove the shrapnel from his chest and throws his chest reactor into the sea. Even without the suits, he says, he is Iron Man.

After the credits, Bruce, who'd been listening to the whole story, turns out to have fallen asleep.

Key terms

Extremis
A technology that rewrites the body to make it stronger, even healing injuries. If the body can't handle it, it can explode.
The Mandarin
A mysterious terrorist who claims responsibility for attacks on TV.
A.I.M.
The research company Killian founded, presented as a science firm.
Iron Patriot
Rhodey's War Machine suit, repainted in the flag's colors and renamed by the government.
Mark 42
Tony's new suit, whose pieces fly to him and assemble on his body.
House Party Protocol
Tony's command to call in all the suits he's built at once.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

So what was the Mandarin?

A fake figure Killian created to disguise Extremis failures, people exploding, as terror attacks. Actor Trevor played him on TV.

Why was Tony so anxious?

After facing an alien invasion and gods in The Avengers, he's terrified he can't protect the people he loves.

How could Pepper breathe fire?

Killian injected her with Extremis. In the end Tony removes it and she goes back to normal.

Why did Killian hate Tony?

He never got over being brushed off by Tony back in 1999.

Did Tony quit being Iron Man?

He destroyed his suits and had the reactor removed, but as "I am Iron Man" says, he's still a hero. He returns in later films.

My takeOpinion

Honestly, I can't help thinking Pepper didn't need to be cured. Her body adapted to Extremis and she became strong enough to finish off the final villain, so she could have been a hero as she was. Then again, choosing to "put things back" is very Tony.
