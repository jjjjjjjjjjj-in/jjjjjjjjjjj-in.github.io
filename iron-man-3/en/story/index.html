Part 1: Bern, 1999

1-1New Year's Eve party

The film opens with Tony's narration: a story about problems you create yourself coming back to haunt you.

New Year's Eve, 1999. Young Tony is at a party during a science conference in Bern, Switzerland. As he drinks and enjoys himself, Yinsen, the man who will later save his life in Iron Man, introduces him to a heart specialist, Dr. Wu.

1-2A promise to Killian

Aldrich Killian, a young scientist who walks with a cane, eagerly approaches Tony. He wants Tony's help with A.I.M., the think tank he has founded.

Tony brushes him off by telling him to wait on the roof and promising to come right up. He has no intention of going.

1-3Maya's research, and the man on the roof

Tony goes back to the room of botanist Maya Hansen. She is working on Extremis, a way to rewrite living things so they heal themselves, and shows him an experiment on a plant.

Tony spends the night with Maya and never goes to the roof. Killian waits there in the cold all night for a man who never comes.

Part 2: Tony can't sleep

2-1The Mark 42 in the workshop

Thirteen years later, the 2012 Christmas season. Ever since he nearly died going through the portal in the Battle of New York, Tony hasn't been able to sleep.

In the workshop under his Malibu house, he goes days without sleep, building dozens of suits. The new Mark 42's pieces fly to him and lock onto his body, but in testing it doesn't quite work yet.

2-2The Mandarin and the Iron Patriot

Meanwhile, a terrorist calling himself the Mandarin has been setting off bombings across America, hijacking TV signals to claim them.

At the President's request, Tony's best friend Rhodey hunts the Mandarin as the Iron Patriot, his War Machine suit repainted in red, white and blue.

2-3Panic at the restaurant

Tony meets Rhodey at a seaside restaurant. Rhodey says that no bomb fragments are ever found at the Mandarin's blast sites.

Some kids come over for autographs and ask about the Battle of New York. Tony has a sudden panic attack and escapes into his suit parked outside.

2-4Killian's visit

Killian visits Pepper, now running Stark Industries. He is unrecognizable, confident and polished. Showing her a hologram of a brain, he proposes they work together on Extremis.

Pepper turns him down because it could be turned into a weapon. Happy, now head of security, scolds Killian's companion Savin for not wearing a visitor badge and grows suspicious of them.

2-5The nightmare and the suit

That night in the bedroom, Tony confesses to Pepper that he's been anxious ever since New York.

Asleep, he has a nightmare. The Mark 42, summoned by Tony in his sleep, goes for Pepper as she tries to wake him. Tony wakes and stops the suit, but the shaken Pepper says she'll sleep downstairs.

2-6The Chinese Theatre blast

Happy tails Savin to the Chinese Theatre in Hollywood, where Savin hands a case of medicine to Taggart, an ex-soldier who has been given Extremis.

When Happy confronts them, Savin throws him aside. Taggart's body glows red with heat and explodes. People nearby are killed, and Happy is badly hurt and falls into a coma.

Part 3: Tony gives his address

3-1Happy in the hospital

The Mandarin claims the theater bombing as his own.

Tony visits Happy in the hospital and puts on Happy's favorite TV show while he lies unconscious.

3-2Taunting the Mandarin

Surrounded by reporters outside the hospital, a furious Tony dares the Mandarin to come for him, and gives out his home address on camera.

Back home, Pepper is angry that he has practically invited the enemy to their door.

3-3Rebuilding the scene

In his workshop, Tony and his AI, J.A.R.V.I.S., recreate the theater scene as a hologram. The blast was over 3,000 degrees, yet there's no trace of a bomb.

Searching for similar cases, he finds that in Rose Hill, Tennessee, a soldier named Chad Davis exploded before the Mandarin ever appeared.

3-4Maya arrives

Soon after, Maya, the woman from 1999, shows up at the door. She says she needs to talk to Tony about Extremis.

Part 4: The house falls into the sea

4-1The helicopter attack

Armed helicopters disguised as news choppers appear and fire missiles into the house.

Tony quickly sends the Mark 42 onto Pepper. Wearing the suit, Pepper protects Maya and the two escape the collapsing house.

4-2Into the sea

In the suit, Tony fights the helicopters and brings two down. But the house collapses off the cliff, and Tony sinks to the bottom of the sea with the wreckage.

J.A.R.V.I.S. flies the suit, and Tony bursts out of the water.

4-3Snowy Tennessee

With Tony unconscious, the suit follows the flight plan from his investigation and flies to Tennessee. Still unfinished, it doesn't have enough power to get back to Malibu and comes down in a snowy field.

Tony drags the dead suit along a snowy road. The world believes he is dead.

Part 5: The boy in Rose Hill

5-1Harley's garage

Tony breaks into a garage in town and hides the suit there. He's caught by Harley, the boy who lives there: a smart kid who loves machines and is an Iron Man fan.

With Harley's help, Tony charges the suit and starts looking into the Rose Hill incident.

5-2Shadows on the wall

Tony and Harley visit the blast site, where six people including Chad died.

The victims' shadows are burned into the wall by the heat. But there's no shadow for Chad, who was at the center of the blast.

5-3Panic in the snow

When Harley asks about the Battle of New York, Tony has another panic attack and crouches in the snow.

Harley reminds him that he called himself a mechanic, and says that if so, he should build something. Tony slowly calms down.

5-4Chad's mother

In a bar, Tony meets Chad's mother, Mrs. Davis. Chad was a soldier badly wounded in combat who signed up for an experimental program. The town thinks he built a bomb, but his mother is sure he never would have.

She gives Tony Chad's file, which shows that Taggart, who exploded at the theater, was in the same program.

5-5Fighting Brandt

Brandt, one of Killian's Extremis-enhanced agents, arrives posing as a government official. She overpowers the deputies who try to stop her and goes after Tony with hands glowing with heat.

Without his suit, Tony leaks gas in the kitchen and uses a microwave to set off a big explosion. Brandt is blown into power lines and electrocuted.

5-6Savin and the water tower

Savin, the other agent, knocks down the town's water tower on top of Tony, then takes Harley hostage and demands the file.

Tony and Harley catch Savin off guard and take him down. Tony leaves town with Savin's car and the file, leaving the half-repaired suit in Harley's garage.

Part 6: Hunting the mastermind

6-1Pepper is taken

Pepper and Maya have escaped the house. Maya admits she works for Killian and that Killian is connected to the Mandarin.

Then Killian shows up and takes Pepper away, and Maya goes back to him.

6-2Rhodey is captured

Rhodey tracks the Mandarin's broadcasts to Pakistan. After a few false leads, he raids a factory, but Extremis soldiers disguised as workers capture him and take the Iron Patriot armor.

6-3The Mandarin goes live

The Mandarin hijacks the airwaves again. This time he has captured Richards, an employee of the oil company Roxxon, and broadcasts himself shooting him live.

He also aims a threat at the President.

6-4The news van at the pageant

Tony goes to a beauty pageant in Chattanooga, Tennessee (Stan Lee is one of the judges). Gary, a young technician in a TV van, is a huge fan and lets Tony use his connection.

Footage in the file shows A.I.M. testing Extremis on wounded veterans. And J.A.R.V.I.S. traces the Mandarin's broadcasts to Miami, Florida.

6-5Homemade weapons

Tony buys supplies at a store and builds homemade weapons. Then he sneaks into the Mandarin's mansion in Miami.

Part 7: Who the Mandarin is

7-1Trevor the actor

Getting past the guards, Tony finally finds the Mandarin. But he turns out to be Trevor Slattery, an English actor who is drunk and clowning around.

Trevor was hired to play the Mandarin and has no idea what is really being done in his name.

7-2Killian revealed

Tony is captured, and Killian appears. Killian created the Mandarin.

He says that on the night he waited on the roof in 1999, he even thought about jumping. Later he used Maya's Extremis to cure his own body, then gave it to wounded veterans. When it failed and people exploded, he covered it up as the Mandarin's terrorism.

7-3Extremis for Pepper

Killian reveals he has given Extremis to the captured Pepper. If Tony wants to save her, he'll have to fix Extremis's flaws.

If her body can't handle it, she could explode.

7-4Maya's end

Maya tries to stop Killian, threatening her own life on the grounds that he can't do without her, the key to the research.

Killian shoots her dead without hesitation, then leaves the mansion, leaving Tony behind.

7-5Escaping the mansion

The Mark 42, repaired in Harley's garage, answers Tony's call and flies to him piece by piece. But only one gauntlet and one boot arrive in time.

With those, Tony fights off the guards and reunites with Rhodey, who was also being held at the mansion.

Part 8: Air Force One

8-1A fake Iron Patriot

Wearing the stolen Iron Patriot armor, Savin boards Air Force One pretending to be Rhodey.

Once the plane is in the air, he takes down the President's guards, seals the President inside the Iron Patriot armor and sends it flying off.

8-2Thirteen in midair

Tony flies the Mark 42 into Air Force One and kills Savin with a blast from its chest. But thirteen people are blown out through a hole in the damaged plane.

Tony has the falling people link hands in a chain, catches them all together and lowers them into the sea.

Then the Mark 42 is hit by a truck and falls to pieces. It was empty: Tony had been flying it by remote.

8-3The Vice President's scheme

Killian plans to kill the President on live TV aboard a damaged oil tanker impounded in port.

The Vice President has agreed to become Killian's puppet once the President is dead, in exchange for Extremis curing his young daughter's disability. Tony and Rhodey head for the tanker.

Part 9: Showdown on the tanker

9-1The House Party

Christmas night. The President, still sealed in the Iron Patriot armor, hangs above the tanker. Tony and Rhodey storm the ship as Extremis soldiers wait for them.

Tony orders J.A.R.V.I.S. to call in the dozens of suits he's built. Flown by J.A.R.V.I.S., they arrive one after another and fight the soldiers.

9-2Saving the President

Rhodey reaches the President, frees him and gets him to safety.

9-3Pepper falls

Tony finds Pepper. Her body has survived Extremis.

But before he can save her, the rigging around them collapses. Pepper falls from a great height into the flames and disappears in front of Tony.

9-4Fighting Killian

Tony fights Killian, jumping from suit to suit. He cuts off one of Killian's arms, but Killian, powered by Extremis, won't go down.

Cornered, Tony locks the Mark 42 around Killian and makes it self-destruct. Killian still survives and comes at Tony.

9-5Pepper strikes

Then Pepper appears, having survived the fall thanks to Extremis. She takes Killian down, and he dies in an explosion.

9-6Suit fireworks

As a sign of his devotion to Pepper, Tony has J.A.R.V.I.S. blow up all the remaining suits in the night sky, like fireworks.

Part 10: Afterward

10-1After it's over

The Vice President and Trevor, who played the Mandarin, are arrested. In the hospital, Happy wakes up.

10-2Pepper, and the surgery

Tony stabilizes the Extremis in Pepper's body and returns her to normal.

Tony himself has surgery by Dr. Wu to remove the shrapnel near his heart. He no longer needs the device in his chest.

10-3The reactor in the sea

Harley's garage is filled with the latest gear, a gift from Tony.

Tony returns to the ruins of his house, salvages his robot arms like Dum-E from the sea, and throws his old chest reactor into the water. Even without the suits or the house, he says, "I am Iron Man."

10-4After the credits

Tony has been telling this whole story to Bruce Banner, who fell asleep partway through and missed most of it.

When Tony starts over from his childhood, Bruce is dismayed.

Summary

The Mandarin and Extremis

Extremis is Maya's technology for rewriting the body so it heals (Part 1). Killian used it to cure himself and gave it to wounded veterans; those whose bodies rejected it overheated and exploded (Parts 3 and 5).

Killian disguised those explosions as attacks by the "Mandarin," played by the actor Trevor, and planned to kill the President and control the Vice President (Parts 7 and 8).

Tony and Pepper

After New York, Tony suffered from anxiety and sleeplessness, and his suit-building came between him and Pepper (Part 2).

Losing his house and suits, he fought on with Harley's help and homemade gear (Parts 5-7). In the end Pepper saved him; he destroyed all his suits and had the shrapnel removed (Parts 9 and 10).

Who died (in order)

- Chad Davis and five townspeople (before the story): Chad's body rejected Extremis and he exploded in Rose Hill (revealed in Parts 3 and 5).

- Taggart and people at the theater (Part 2): Taggart's body rejected Extremis and he exploded at the Chinese Theatre. Happy was badly hurt but survived.

- Brandt (Part 5): blown by Tony's gas-and-microwave explosion into power lines and electrocuted.

- Maya (Part 7): shot dead by Killian when she tried to stop him.

- Savin (Part 8): killed on Air Force One by the chest beam of the Mark 42, which Tony was flying by remote.

- Killian (Part 9): survived the Mark 42's self-destruct, but was taken down by Pepper, who had survived thanks to Extremis, and died in an explosion.
