Part 1: New Year's Eve, 1999

A party in Switzerland

Tony narrates. On New Year's Eve 1999, at a party in Switzerland, young Tony meets botanist Maya Hansen, who is researching Extremis, a way to make the body heal itself.

The man on the roof

Aldrich Killian, a scientist with a disability, asks Tony to hear about his think tank. Tony tells him to wait on the roof, then forgets. Killian waits all night in the cold.

Part 2: Tony can't sleep

After New York

Since the Battle of New York, Tony is haunted by nearly dying in space and suffers panic attacks. Unable to sleep, he builds dozens of suits in his workshop.

The Mandarin

Meanwhile, a terrorist called the Mandarin hijacks TV broadcasts to claim a string of bombings.

Happy is hurt

Happy, now head of security, tails one of Killian's men. An explosion outside a Hollywood theater leaves Happy badly hurt and in a coma.

Tony gives his address

Furious, Tony goes on camera, threatens the Mandarin and gives out his home address.

Part 3: The house falls

Maya visits

Maya, the woman from 1999, shows up at Tony's house. She seems to have something to tell him.

Helicopter attack

Gunships appear and fire missiles into the house. Tony sends his suit to Pepper to protect her, then flies out as the house collapses into the sea.

A strange town

The suit flies him on autopilot to a town in Tennessee he'd been investigating, and runs out of power in the snow. The world thinks Tony is dead.

Part 4: The boy in Tennessee

Harley

Tony hides the suit in a garage and meets Harley, a sharp, cheeky kid who helps him investigate.

Exploding people

The local blast was caused by a former soldier whose body overheated and exploded. The Mandarin's "bombings" weren't bombs at all.

Soldiers made of fire

Soldiers who glow with heat and heal instantly come after Tony. Without a suit, he survives with homemade gadgets and Harley's help.

Part 5: Who the Mandarin is

Extremis

Maya admits she works for Killian. The burning soldiers were enhanced with her Extremis. Killian kidnaps Pepper and injects her with it. When Maya tries to turn against him, Killian shoots her.

The mansion in Miami

Tony tracks the Mandarin to a Miami mansion and sneaks in. He finds Trevor Slattery, an out-of-work actor drinking in front of the TV. The Mandarin is a fake, invented by Killian.

Killian's plan

Killian explains his scheme to the captured Tony: invent a terrifying enemy and profit from the war on terror. And with the Vice President's help, he plans to kidnap the President and kill him on live TV.

Part 6: Saving the President

Air Force One

Tony escapes and teams up with Rhodey. Killian's man, wearing Rhodey's stolen suit, boards Air Force One and abducts the President.

Saving thirteen people

Using a remote-controlled suit, Tony saves the passengers blown out of the plane by having them link hands in midair.

The House Party Protocol

At an oil platform, Tony summons dozens of his suits to fight all at once. Rhodey rescues the President.

Pepper strikes

Pepper falls into the flames, and Tony thinks she's dead. But Extremis keeps her alive, and she rises to defeat Killian.

Fireworks

To show Pepper what she means to him, Tony blows up all his suits in the sky like fireworks.

Part 7: Afterward

Surgery

Tony cures Pepper, then has surgery to remove the shrapnel from his chest and throws his old reactor into the sea. Still, he says: "I am Iron Man."

After the credits

Tony has been telling this whole story to Bruce Banner, who fell asleep partway through.
