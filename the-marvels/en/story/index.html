Part 1: The other bangle

Dar-Benn

Kree leader Dar-Benn finds the twin of Kamala's bangle and uses its power to damage the jump points that connect space.

Three heroes

Carol investigates a jump point, Monica is on a space station, and Kamala is in Jersey City. The moment they all use their powers, they swap places.

Kamala's room

Carol appears in Kamala's bedroom, and Kamala is thrilled.

Part 2: Becoming a team

The space station

The three gather on Fury's space station and practice together to handle the swapping.

Monica and Carol

As a child, Monica loved Carol like family, and she's hurt that Carol never came back.

Dar-Benn's plan

To save the Kree homeworld, Dar-Benn is stealing air, water and even sunlight from other worlds.

Part 3: The singing planet

Aladna

They visit Aladna, where people communicate through song. Carol is 'married' to Prince Yan.

Stealing the sea

Dar-Benn comes to take Aladna's water, and the three fight her.

Flerken evacuation

On the space station, Goose's kittens swallow the crew to carry them to safety.

Part 4: The rift

Two bangles

Dar-Benn takes Kamala's bangle too and uses both to open an enormous rift.

Dar-Benn's end

Overwhelmed by the power, Dar-Benn is destroyed.

Monica's sacrifice

To close the rift, Monica is left behind on the other side.

Relighting the sun

Carol uses her power to reignite the Kree sun.

Part 5: Afterward

New recruits

Kamala visits archer Kate Bishop to recruit her for a team of young heroes.

Mid-credits

Monica wakes up in another universe, where a woman who looks like her mother and the blue-furred Beast are waiting.
