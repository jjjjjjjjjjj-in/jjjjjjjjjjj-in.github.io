Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

A sequel to three stories

It follows Captain Marvel, WandaVision (Monica) and Ms. Marvel (Kamala).

Carol's past

Something Carol did on the Kree homeworld, Hala, sets the story in motion.

A short one

At 105 minutes, it's one of the MCU's shortest films.

Watch the mid-credits

There's an important scene.

My takeOpinion

The fights where the three keep swapping places are a fun idea. It's short and light, so if you like Kamala from Ms. Marvel, I don't think you'll regret it.

StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

Parts 1-2: Swapping places

Kree leader Dar-Benn finds the twin of Kamala's bangle, a Quantum Band, and forces open a jump point.

The disturbance entangles Carol, Monica and Kamala, who now swap places whenever they use their powers.

Parts 3-4: The Marvels

The Khans' house is wrecked in the swapping chaos, and the family goes to the space station with Fury.

When Dar-Benn drains the Skrull planet Tarnax's air into Hala, the three evacuate its people and become the Marvels.

Parts 5-7: Aladna and Carol's past

On the singing planet Aladna, the three fight Dar-Benn alongside Carol's 'husband' Prince Yan, but she steals the planet's water.

Carol confesses to Monica that she destroyed the Supreme Intelligence on Hala, leaving the Kree world in ruins.

Parts 8-9: Battle by the Sun

On the space station, Goose's kittens swallow the crew to evacuate them.

Dar-Benn targets Earth's sun and tears space open with both bands, destroying herself. Monica closes the rupture but is stranded in another universe, and Carol reignites Hala's sun.

Part 10: Afterward and the credits

Kamala visits Kate Bishop to recruit her for a team of young heroes. In the mid-credits scene, Monica wakes in another universe to find a woman who looks like her mother, Maria, and Beast.

Key terms

The Kree
A vast space empire whose homeworld, Hala, is dying as its sun fades.
The Quantum Bands
A pair of bangles. Dar-Benn finds the twin of Kamala's.
Jump points
Gateways connecting distant points in space.
Flerkens
Alien creatures that look like cats, like Carol's companion Goose.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why does Dar-Benn hate Carol?

Carol destroyed the Supreme Intelligence that ruled the Kree. The homeworld fell into civil war, and its sun began to die.

Why do the three swap places?

All three have light-based powers, and the rift Dar-Benn opened entangled them.

Where does Monica end up?

To close the rift, she's left behind in another universe, where a woman who looks like her late mother is alive.

Easy-to-miss details

A planet of song

The people of Aladna communicate through song, and Carol is 'married' to their prince.

Flerken kittens

Goose's many kittens help evacuate the space station.

Kamala's family

Kamala's family get plenty of screen time, even in space.

The ending and post-creditsSpoilers

After the end credits

Mid-credits: Monica wakes up in another universe and meets someone who looks like her mother Maria, along with Beast. Post-credits: Kamala invites Kate Bishop to a new team.
