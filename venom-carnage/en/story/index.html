Part 1: Two kids in an institution

1996

In 1996, at a home for troubled kids, young Cletus Kasady bonds with Frances Barrison, a girl whose scream can shatter anything.

Torn apart

Frances is taken to Ravencroft. During the transfer, young officer Patrick Mulligan is injured by her power.

Part 2: The death row interview

Life with Venom

Eddie and Venom live together. Venom wants to eat people, and Eddie won't let it. They bicker constantly.

The interview

At the request of Mulligan, now a detective, Eddie interviews Kasady. Venom spots clues to where the victims are buried, and Eddie's story becomes a sensation.

Anne's engagement

Anne tells Eddie she's engaged to Dan.

Part 3: Carnage is born

The bite

Just before his execution, Kasady bites Eddie's hand during a final visit and takes in a piece of Venom along with the blood.

The execution chamber

When Kasady is given the lethal injection, a red symbiote bursts out of him. Carnage is born, and the prison descends into chaos.

The breakup

At the same time, Eddie and Venom have a huge fight, and Venom leaves Eddie's body.

Part 4: Separate nights

The costume party

Away from Eddie, Venom hops from body to body and ends up at a costume rave, where it makes a speech and becomes the star of the party.

Freeing Shriek

As Carnage, Kasady breaks Frances, Shriek, out of Ravencroft. They abduct Mulligan.

Anne is taken

Shriek kidnaps Anne too. Kasady plans to hold a wedding in a church.

Part 5: Reunited

Mrs. Chen's help

Venom gets back to Eddie by hitching a ride in Mrs. Chen.

Making up

Eddie and Venom apologize for what they said and bond again.

Battle in the church

In the church, Venom fights Carnage, who's stronger and has the upper hand.

The bells and the scream

Shriek's scream and the church bells weaken both symbiotes. Venom seizes the moment and devours Carnage. Kasady and Shriek die.

Part 6: Afterward

A beach vacation

Now wanted by the police, Eddie and Venom lie low at a beach resort.

Mid-credits

In a hotel room, as Venom talks about worlds beyond theirs, they're suddenly pulled into another universe, where the TV news reveals Spider-Man is Peter Parker.
