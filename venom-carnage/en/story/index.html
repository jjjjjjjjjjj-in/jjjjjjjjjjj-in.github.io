Part 1: St. Estes, 1996

1-1Young Cletus

1996. Young Cletus Kasady has pushed his grandmother down the stairs and killed his mother as well, and has been sent to St. Estes, a home for unwanted children.

There he is badly bullied by the other kids. The one person on his side is a girl, Frances Barrison, and the two fall in love.

1-2Frances is taken away

Frances can shatter things with a powerful scream. Because of that power, she is to be moved to the Ravencroft Institute.

On the way, she uses her scream to try to escape and attacks the young police officer escorting her, Patrick Mulligan. He shoots her in the left eye, and her scream damages his ear. Mulligan believes he has killed her.

1-3Frances survives

But Frances survives. Blind in her left eye, she is locked in a glass cell at Ravencroft, where her power is kept in check.

Cletus grieves losing her and vows they will meet again.

Part 2: The death row interview

2-1Life with Venom

Present-day San Francisco. Reporter Eddie Brock shares his body with Venom, an alien symbiote.

Venom wants to eat human brains, but Eddie, who wants to stay off the police's radar, has banned him from eating even criminals. Venom makes do with chicken brains and grows more and more frustrated.

2-2Mulligan's request

Mulligan, now a detective, comes to Eddie with a request. Serial killer Cletus Kasady, held at San Quentin, refuses to talk to anyone but Eddie.

The police believe there are victims of his who have never been found.

2-3The prison interview

Eddie goes to see Kasady, who asks him to publish a poem he has written. It is really a message to Frances, who he hopes is still alive somewhere.

Inside Eddie, Venom notices the drawings on the walls of Kasady's cell.

2-4The big scoop

From the drawings, Venom works out where Kasady hid his victims' bodies. Eddie's discovery becomes huge news, and his career takes off again.

Because of it, Kasady's death sentence is set.

Part 3: Anne's engagement

3-1Anne wants to talk

Eddie's ex, Anne, asks to meet because she has something to tell him. Venom is sure she has broken up with Dr. Dan Lewis and heads out in high spirits.

3-2The engagement

But what Anne tells him is that she and Dan are engaged.

Venom keeps butting in, urging Eddie to win her back, and that gives away to Anne that Venom is still with Eddie.

3-3The ride in the rain

Leaving, Eddie rides his motorbike recklessly through the rain. When he nearly crashes, Venom saves him.

Part 4: The bite

4-1The last interview

Kasady summons Eddie again. Furious that Eddie's story sealed his death sentence, he starts insulting Eddie partway through the interview.

4-2Venom lashes out, and the bite

Unable to stand the insults to Eddie, Venom lashes out of Eddie's body and grabs Kasady, who briefly glimpses Venom's true form.

In that moment, Kasady bites Eddie's hand hard enough to draw blood, taking in a tiny piece of Venom along with it.

4-3The blood moves

Guards pull them apart and Eddie leaves. Kasady remarks that Eddie's blood tastes different from a normal person's. The blood he wipes from his mouth is squirming.

Part 5: The breakup

5-1A big breakfast

At home, Venom cooks a lavish breakfast to cheer up Eddie, who is still down about Anne. Eddie brushes it off.

5-2The big fight

Fed up with a life where he can't eat people, Venom finally explodes. Venom wants freedom; Eddie wants peace. It turns into a huge fight.

Venom beats Eddie up and trashes the apartment, throwing the TV and dishes out the window.

5-3Venom leaves

Venom detaches from Eddie's body and leaves on his own.

5-4Body to body

Venom looks for a new host, hopping from person to person across the city. But no one's body can hold a symbiote for long.

5-5The costume party

Venom slips into a costume rave, where everyone takes him for a costume and loves the look.

The star of the party, he gives a speech on stage to huge cheers, but mutters that he wishes Eddie could have seen it.

Part 6: Carnage is born

6-1The execution

Kasady's execution begins. The drugs go in one by one, but as he starts to fade, a red symbiote surges out of his body and stops the injection.

6-2Rampage in the prison

The red symbiote, born from Eddie's blood, bonds with Kasady. It tears through the prison, killing guards and freeing inmates, and breaks out.

6-3A deal with Carnage

The red symbiote calls itself Carnage, and it and Kasady strike a deal: Carnage will help free Frances, and in return Kasady will help kill Eddie and Venom.

6-4Raid on Ravencroft

Kasady tracks Frances down and attacks Ravencroft, killing many people as he frees her from her glass cell. The two are reunited after all those years.

6-5St. Estes burns

Kasady and Frances go to St. Estes, where they grew up, and burn it down.

Part 7: Reunited

7-1Eddie is arrested

Mulligan grows suspicious of Eddie, the last person to see Kasady before Carnage appeared, and arrests him.

Eddie calls Anne in as his lawyer and asks her to find Venom.

7-2Mrs. Chen's store

Anne finds Venom inside Mrs. Chen, the owner of Eddie's local store, and talks him into forgiving Eddie.

7-3Anne and Venom

Venom bonds with Anne, and the two head to the police station, where Venom, in Anne's body, breaks Eddie out.

7-4Making up

Eddie apologizes to Venom for what he said. Venom moves from Anne back into Eddie, and the two are bonded again.

7-5Two hostages

Meanwhile, Frances kidnaps Anne and Kasady takes Mulligan, planning to use them as bait to lure out Eddie and Venom.

Kasady and Frances intend to get married in a cathedral. Dan tells Eddie that Anne has been taken.

Part 8: Battle at the cathedral

8-1The cathedral wedding

Grace Cathedral, San Francisco. Kasady and Frances are holding their wedding in front of their hostages. Eddie and Venom arrive, with Dan.

8-2The red one

Carnage engulfs Kasady, calls Venom "father" and means to eat him. Venom is frightened of the red symbiote and doesn't want to fight.

Only when Eddie promises to let him eat people does Venom finally go into battle.

8-3Venom outmatched

Carnage is far stronger, and Venom is pushed back again and again. Dan pitches in, but it barely helps.

When the cathedral bells ring, both symbiotes, which can't stand loud noise, are stunned, and Eddie and Kasady slug it out as humans.

8-4Frances and Carnage

Frances turns her scream on Mulligan, the man who shot her eye, and he goes still as if dead.

But the noise enrages Carnage, which attacks Frances even as Kasady tries to stop it.

8-5Saving Anne

Carnage grabs Anne, climbs high up the cathedral and tries to throw her off. As Venom is about to give up, Dan points out that Kasady and Carnage aren't fully bonded.

Venom catches the falling Anne and lowers her safely to the ground with a tendril.

8-6Shriek's scream

Venom deliberately provokes Frances into screaming. The sound tears both Venom and Carnage away from their hosts.

The cathedral starts to collapse, and Frances is crushed to death by the falling bell. With Anne and Dan's help, Venom gets back into Eddie.

8-7Kasady's end

Carnage tries to bond with Kasady again, but Venom devours it, then bites Kasady's head off.

Part 9: Afterward

9-1Mulligan lives

Mulligan, thought to be dead, is alive, and a piece of Carnage has gotten into him.

9-2To the beach

Now fugitives, Eddie and Venom say goodbye to Anne and Dan and get away. They take the beach vacation Venom always wanted and agree to keep going as a team.

9-3Mid-credits

Watching TV in their hotel room, Venom offers to show Eddie a little of what the symbiotes know about other worlds.

Then a blinding light changes the room, and they find themselves in a different hotel room. On TV, J. Jonah Jameson is reporting that Spider-Man is Peter Parker. Venom seems to recognize the face.

Summary

How it unfolds (in order)

- The start (Parts 1–2): Cletus and Frances are torn apart as kids. Now on death row, Kasady gives Eddie the interview that revives his career.

- Carnage is born (Parts 4 and 6): Kasady bites Eddie and takes in a piece of Venom. At his execution, the red symbiote Carnage emerges.

- Breakup and reunion (Parts 5 and 7): Eddie and Venom split up, but Anne brings them back together.

- The end (Part 8): At the cathedral, Venom uses Frances's scream to tear Carnage from Kasady and devours it.

Who died (in order)

- Cletus's grandmother and mother (Part 1): killed by young Cletus.

- Prison guards (Part 6): killed by Carnage during the breakout.

- People at Ravencroft (Part 6): killed by Kasady and Carnage as they free Frances.

- Frances (Part 8): provoked by Venom into screaming, she is crushed by the falling bell of the collapsing cathedral.

- Carnage (Part 8): torn from Kasady by the scream and devoured by Venom.

- Kasady (Part 8): after losing Carnage, his head is bitten off by Venom.

* Mulligan looked dead after Frances's attack but survived (Parts 8–9).
