Key terms

Carnage
A red symbiote spawned from Venom, bonded with Kasady.
Shriek
A woman whose scream can shatter anything. Kasady's love.
Ravencroft
A facility that locks up people with special powers.
Loud sound
Symbiotes are weak to loud noise, so Shriek's scream hurts both Venom and Carnage.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

How is Carnage created?

When Kasady bites Eddie, he takes in a piece of Venom along with Eddie's blood.

Is Carnage Venom's child?

In a way, since it came from Venom. It's described as even stronger.

What happens mid-credits?

Eddie and Venom are suddenly pulled into a hotel room in another universe, the MCU, where the TV is reporting that Spider-Man is Peter Parker.

Easy-to-miss details

Venom at the rave

After leaving Eddie, Venom becomes the life of a costume party.

Mrs. Chen

Kasady covers his cell walls with drawings of his past.
