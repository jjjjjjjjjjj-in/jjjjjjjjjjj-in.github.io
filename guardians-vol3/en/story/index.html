Part 1: Attack on Knowhere

1-1Baby raccoons in a cage

The film opens on baby raccoons playing in a cage. A hand reaches in and picks one of them out. That one will become Rocket.

1-2Rocket and "Creep"

The present. Knowhere, a city inside the skull of a giant creature. The Guardians have bought it and rebuilt it as their home base.

Rocket walks through the town alone, listening to a quiet acoustic version of Radiohead's "Creep."

1-3Peter drinks himself out

Peter can't get over Gamora, killed by Thanos, and drinks nearly every day. Again he passes out and is carried to bed.

His friends are fed up with him, but worried too. Elsewhere in town, Kraglin keeps practicing with Yondu's arrow without much success, in a running rivalry with Cosmo, the telekinetic space dog.

1-4Adam, the golden youth

Then a golden-skinned young man, Adam Warlock, crashes in from the sky. He was created by Ayesha, queen of the Sovereign, who has held a grudge against the Guardians since the last film, and he has come to take Rocket.

Adam is so strong that even Drax and Groot can't stop him. Mantis's arm is broken.

1-5Rocket is gravely hurt

In the fight, Rocket is badly hurt and falls unconscious. Seeing this, Peter throws himself into the battle.

Just as Adam is about to finish off Drax, Nebula stabs him with the blade in her arm. Wounded, Adam flees, leaving Knowhere in ruins.

Part 2: Rocket's memories, and setting off

2-1Memory (1): 89P13 and his cage mates

As he is carried away, Rocket remembers. Born a baby raccoon on Earth, he was captured by the scientist known as the High Evolutionary, fitted with machinery and remade. He had no name, only a test-subject number: 89P13.

After surgery he is thrown into the cage of his test batch, Batch 89. Inside are an otter with mechanical arms, a walrus with wheels for legs and a rabbit fitted with machinery. They speak kindly to the frightened newcomer, and he is no longer alone. None of the four has a name yet.

2-2A body that can't be healed

The present. When a med-pack is used on Rocket, a device inside his body reacts and only brings him closer to death. Nebula makes them stop: treating him would kill him.

This kill switch turns out to belong to Orgocorp, the company that made Rocket. To shut it off, they need the override code held by the company.

2-3Setting off

Peter decides to go to Orgocorp's headquarters to save Rocket. He leaves Knowhere in the hands of Kraglin and Cosmo the space dog.

The Guardians take off in their new ship with Rocket still unconscious aboard.

2-4The High Evolutionary's anger

It was the High Evolutionary who sent Adam. He is also the creator of the Sovereign, so Ayesha and Adam cannot defy him. Rocket is his most valuable test subject.

He punishes Adam for coming back without Rocket, and Ayesha begs him to stop, since Adam is like a son to her. The High Evolutionary threatens to wipe out the Sovereign if they fail to bring Rocket back, and tells his aide Theel that he knows where the Guardians are headed.

Adam was awakened before he was fully developed, so he is powerful but childlike.

Part 3: Breaking into Orgocorp

3-1The Ravagers and Gamora

The Guardians' ship arrives near Orgocorp's headquarters. Peter tells Mantis he has never been back to Earth since he was taken as a boy; the only person he would know there is his grandfather, and he doesn't know if he is still alive.

Then the Ravagers, led by Stakar, board the ship, and Gamora is with them. Nebula has been secretly in touch with her and asked for help.

This is the Gamora who came from 2014 in Endgame, with no memories of Peter. He is stunned. Stakar hands over spacesuits for the break-in and warns them about the guards.

3-2Memory (2): The genius 89P13

Rocket's memory. He is so bright that the High Evolutionary keeps him close. The High Evolutionary is building Counter-Earth, a planet where he will send his finished creations.

He shows Rocket a machine that rapidly evolves animals. But the creatures it produces turn violent and are disposed of as failures. Rocket pinpoints the cause, which the High Evolutionary himself could not find.

3-3Down to the living headquarters

Orgocorp's headquarters floats in space and is made of living, fleshy material. The Guardians put on the spacesuits and descend from the ship with Gamora. Groot stays aboard to watch over Rocket.

Peter talks to Gamora as if nothing has changed; she brushes him off, saying she is not the Gamora he knew.

3-4Posing as employees

Inside, the Guardians stash their spacesuits. Master Karja and his guards find them almost at once, but they pass themselves off as employees.

They split up: Peter, Gamora and Nebula in one group, Drax and Mantis in the other. Peter's group grabs the receptionist, Ura, and makes her lead them to the records.

3-5Out with the file

Peter's group finds Rocket's file. The alarm goes off at once and the guards close in. The stashed spacesuits, left in the wrong place, have been sucked out into space.

The Guardians fight their way out, get back to the ship where Groot is waiting, and barely escape.

Part 4: Next stop, Counter-Earth

4-1Memory (3): Choosing names

Rocket's memory. In their cage, the four dream aloud about getting out and being free someday. And they choose names for themselves: the otter is Lylla, the walrus is Teefs, the rabbit is Floor.

Seeing a rocket fly across the sky, 89P13 promises to build a ship and take them all up there someday, and names himself Rocket.

4-2The code is gone

The present. The Guardians play back the file and see the cruel experiments Rocket went through. But the override code has been removed from it. They learn it is stored in a device in the head of Theel, the High Evolutionary's aide.

Theel is on Counter-Earth, the planet the High Evolutionary built. Peter wants to go at once. Gamora objects, until Nebula tells her Rocket is still family.

4-3The captured Ravager

Orgocorp's guards hand over a captured Ravager and his small pet to Ayesha and Adam. Ayesha orders Adam to get information about the Guardians out of him.

But Adam can't control his power and burns the man to death before learning anything. Ayesha is furious.

Moments later, a call from Gamora comes in on the dead man's radio, asking to be picked up. From the coordinates she sends, Ayesha learns the Guardians are heading for Counter-Earth. Adam takes a liking to the pet left behind, later called Blurp.

4-4Memory (4): The scientist's rage

Rocket's memory. Using Rocket's insight, the High Evolutionary reruns his experiment and succeeds in making creatures that don't turn violent. But he is enraged that one of his own creations found the answer before he did.

He sneers at Rocket as a failed experiment full of mistakes. Batch 89 was never meant for the new world, he says: he will remove Rocket's brain for study and incinerate the others.

Part 5: Counter-Earth

5-1A planet like Earth

Counter-Earth looks like a suburban American town. Its people are Humanimals, animals remade into people.

Right after landing, Drax hits a child on the head with a ball, and the locals start pelting the Guardians with garbage until Groot makes himself huge and scares them off.

Peter helps a bat woman, and she takes the group into her home. With her family's help they locate Arête Laboratories, where the High Evolutionary is, and borrow the family car.

5-2Splitting up

Peter, Groot and Nebula drive to the lab in the borrowed car, while Drax and Mantis stay on the ship with Gamora to watch over Rocket.

At the entrance, the guards won't let Nebula in because of the weapons built into her body, so she has to wait outside. Only Peter and Groot are escorted in.

5-3Drax talks Mantis into it

Drax talks Mantis into going after Peter's group, and the two leave the ship. Gamora is left alone with Rocket.

5-4Memory (5): Losing his friends

Rocket's last memory. The night before they are to be disposed of, Rocket unlocks the cages with a device he got hold of and tries to free his friends. He and Lylla embrace.

Then the High Evolutionary appears and shoots Lylla dead, and mocks Rocket's wailing grief.

Enraged, Rocket leaps on the High Evolutionary and tears his face apart. Two henchmen rush in and shoot Teefs and Floor dead. Rocket guns the henchmen down, steals a spaceship and escapes, alone.

5-5Gamora protects Rocket

The present. War Pig, a pig warrior serving the High Evolutionary, attacks the ship to take Rocket. Gamora fights to protect him.

Adam, still on the hunt, comes flying in as well, but he fails to carry Rocket off.

Part 6: The scientist who destroys a world

6-1Face to face

Peter and Groot come face to face with the High Evolutionary. When Peter says the people of this world have violence and flaws of their own, the High Evolutionary agrees.

6-2Wiping out a world

The High Evolutionary decides to erase his failed world and start over. Explosions tear across the planet and the people of Counter-Earth are wiped out.

Ayesha is caught in it too, aboard her ship. Adam flies to save her, but he is too late, and she dies before his eyes.

6-3Jumping with Theel

Arête Laboratories is really a giant spaceship. As it lifts off, Peter and Groot fight the High Evolutionary's henchmen, grab Theel and leap out of a window with him. Groot grows wings to keep Peter in the air.

Theel does not survive, and the two take the code from the device in his head. Gamora picks them up in the ship.

6-4Three left behind

Drax and Mantis reach Nebula outside. Not knowing that Peter and Groot have already jumped, the three board the departing Arête to rescue them.

Nebula blames Drax for the mess they're in. Mantis stands up for him.

Part 7: Saving Rocket

7-1His heart stops

Aboard the ship, Peter, Gamora and Groot rush to use the code and shut off the kill switch. But partway through, Rocket's heart stops.

7-2Reunion in the light

In a place full of light, Rocket is reunited with Lylla, Teefs and Floor. He wants to stay with them, but Lylla tells him his time hasn't come yet.

7-3Rocket wakes

Working with Gamora, Peter shuts off the kill switch and gets Rocket's heart going again. Rocket comes back, and his friends celebrate.

Part 8: The battle on Arête

8-1Children in cages

On Arête, Nebula, Drax and Mantis find many children locked in cages: the High Evolutionary's next batch of genetically modified subjects.

Over the comms, the three hear from Peter that Rocket is alive, and they rejoice.

8-2The three are captured

But the High Evolutionary captures the three and lets Peter's group know it. Peter decides to rescue them and calls Kraglin for help.

Drax turns out to speak the children's language. Clowning around the way he used to with his own daughter, he gets the frightened children laughing.

The three are thrown into a chamber with monstrous Abilisks.

8-3Knowhere arrives

Kraglin and Cosmo bring Knowhere itself alongside Arête, and Kraglin opens fire on Arête from Knowhere.

The High Evolutionary's monstrous Hellspawn counterattack. Kraglin sees a vision of the late Yondu, urging him to use his heart, and at last he masters the arrow and cuts down the attackers. Cosmo fights with her telekinesis, and together with the townspeople they defend Knowhere.

8-4A mutiny

Facing defeat, the High Evolutionary's crew wants to retreat and turns on him. He kills them.

8-5Escaping with the Abilisks

In the chamber, Mantis calms the Abilisks with her powers and wins them over. The three break out with the Abilisks.

8-6The Guardians together

Peter's group boards Arête too. Adam, whose life Groot has saved, joins their side with Blurp. Reunited, the Guardians fight their way down Arête's corridors.

Their mission is done, but instead of leaving they decide to save the children.

8-7Cosmo's tunnel

Cosmo uses her telekinesis to make a tunnel between Arête and Knowhere, and the children escape through it. Nebula pilots Arête to keep it close to Knowhere.

Part 9: Rocket and the High Evolutionary

9-1Captive animals

Aboard the ship, Rocket finds animals locked in cages, including baby raccoons. Here he learns for certain that he himself is a raccoon.

9-2The final showdown

As Rocket tries to free the animals, the High Evolutionary attacks him, again calling him a failed experiment. Rocket fights back, declaring that he isn't a number anymore: his name is Rocket Raccoon.

His friends rush in and together they beat the High Evolutionary. Nebula pulls off his face, revealing the wounds Rocket gave him long ago.

9-3Sparing him

Rocket chooses not to kill the High Evolutionary. The Guardians get the animals to Knowhere too. Drax carries the High Evolutionary off the ship, and he is later imprisoned on Knowhere (per the director).

9-4Peter adrift

After the animals are out, Peter goes back for the music player he dropped and is left behind. He can't reach Knowhere and begins to freeze in the cold of space.

Adam flies in and carries him to safety on Knowhere.

Part 10: Separate paths

10-1A new captain

On Knowhere, Peter announces he's leaving the Guardians and names Rocket the new captain. When Groot tells everyone "I love you guys," the audience understands him for the first time.

10-2Farewells

Mantis sets out with the Abilisks on a journey to find herself. Nebula decides to stay and lead Knowhere, and asks Drax to stay too and raise the rescued children. Drax agrees and shares a tearful goodbye with Mantis.

Peter and Gamora accept that things can't be what they were and part ways, and Gamora returns to the Ravagers.

10-3Grandpa on Earth, dancing on Knowhere

Peter gives Rocket his music player, returns to Earth and is reunited with his grandfather, Jason.

On Knowhere, Rocket plays Florence and the Machine's "Dog Days Are Over," and the Guardians, the children and the townspeople dance together.

10-4During and after the credits

Mid-credits, Rocket's new Guardians (Groot, Kraglin, Cosmo, Adam, the rescued child Phyla and Blurp) take on a new job.

After the credits, Peter sits down to breakfast with his grandfather on Earth, and a title promises that the legendary Star-Lord will return.

Summary

Rocket's past

Rocket was a baby raccoon from Earth, remade by the High Evolutionary (Parts 1 and 2). In his cage he befriended the future Lylla, Teefs and Floor (Part 2), and the four chose their names together (Part 4). His genius made the High Evolutionary want his brain (Parts 3 and 4).

When he tried to escape, his three friends were killed and he fled alone (Part 5). That's why he never told anyone about his past.

The quest to save Rocket

Badly hurt in Adam's attack, Rocket couldn't be treated because of his kill switch (Parts 1 and 2). The Guardians stole his file from Orgocorp (Part 3), got the code from Theel on Counter-Earth (Part 6) and saved his life (Part 7).

What becomes of the Guardians

Peter makes Rocket captain and goes home to his grandfather; Mantis sets off on a journey; Nebula and Drax raise the children on Knowhere; Gamora goes back to the Ravagers.

Rocket forms a new team of Guardians with Groot, Kraglin, Cosmo, Adam, Phyla and others (Part 10).

Who died (in order)

- A Ravager (Part 4): burned to death by Adam, who couldn't control his power while trying to question him.

- Lylla (Part 5, Rocket's memory): shot dead by the High Evolutionary during the escape.

- Teefs and Floor (Part 5, Rocket's memory): shot by henchmen who rushed in.

- The people of Counter-Earth and Ayesha (Part 6): killed when the High Evolutionary blew up the planet he considered a failure.

- Theel (Part 6): taken off Arête by Peter and Groot in their leap, he did not survive.

- The High Evolutionary's crew (Part 8): killed by him when they mutinied and tried to retreat.

- The High Evolutionary survives; Rocket spares him (Part 9).

My takeOpinion

Rocket's past is almost too painful to watch, which is why everyone's departure at the end feels so warm. As the close of a trilogy, I think it's hard to fault.
