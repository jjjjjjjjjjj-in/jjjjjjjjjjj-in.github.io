StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

1. Attack on Knowhere

A golden man, Adam, attacks the Guardians' city, and Rocket is gravely injured.

2. Why he can't be healed

A device from the company that made Rocket prevents treatment.

3. Rocket's past

In Rocket's mind, memories of his early life return.

4. The living building

The Guardians infiltrate a company built of living tissue.

5. A made-up Earth

On an Earth-like world, the High Evolutionary's horrifying plans come to light.

6. The last battle

The Guardians fight to save captive children and animals.

Key terms

Knowhere
A city inside a giant creature's skull, now the Guardians' base.
The High Evolutionary
A scientist who modifies animals to create a "perfect" species.
OrgoCorp
The High Evolutionary's company, whose headquarters is made of living tissue.
Counter-Earth
A planet the High Evolutionary built to look like Earth.
Adam Warlock
A powerful young man created by the golden Sovereign.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why doesn't Gamora remember Peter?

This Gamora came from 2014 in Endgame. She never shared those years with him.

How was Rocket made?

The High Evolutionary experimented on him when he was a baby raccoon.

What happens to the Guardians?

They go their separate ways, and Rocket leads a new team of Guardians.
