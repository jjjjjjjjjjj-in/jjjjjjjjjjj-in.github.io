StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

Part 1: Attack on Knowhere

Adam, a golden young man created by the Sovereign queen Ayesha, attacks the Guardians' base on Knowhere to take Rocket. Nebula stabs him and drives him off, but Rocket is gravely injured.

Treating him would trigger a kill switch planted in his body. Shutting it off takes an override code held by Orgocorp, the company that made him.

Part 2: Rocket's memories (1)

Unconscious, Rocket remembers being a baby raccoon remade by the scientist known as the High Evolutionary. In his cage he befriended Lylla the otter, Teefs the walrus and Floor the rabbit, and named himself Rocket.

Part 3: Breaking into Orgocorp

With help from the 2014 Gamora, now a Ravager, the Guardians sneak into Orgocorp's living headquarters. They find Rocket's file, but the recorder Theel has removed the code. Thanks to Peter's quick thinking and Groot's ship, they barely escape.

Part 4: The High Evolutionary's anger

The High Evolutionary threatens to wipe out the Sovereign if Ayesha doesn't bring Rocket back. Adam forces a Ravager to reveal where the Guardians are going and kills him, while the Guardians head for Counter-Earth, where Theel is.

Part 5: Rocket's memories (2)

In his memories, Rocket grows smart enough to find a flaw the High Evolutionary couldn't. Enraged, the scientist decides to take Rocket's brain and dispose of his friends.

Part 6: Counter-Earth

On an Earth-like world populated by remade animals, Peter and Groot enter the High Evolutionary's lab. Nebula has to wait outside, and Drax and Mantis leave the ship too. Left alone, Gamora protects Rocket from War Pig and Adam and takes off in the ship.

Part 7: Destroying a world

The High Evolutionary bombards his failed world, killing its people and Ayesha. His lab lifts off as a spaceship, and Nebula, Drax and Mantis board it. Peter and Groot leap off with Theel, who dies breaking their fall, and take the code from his body.

Part 8: Rocket's memories (3)

In his memories, Rocket escapes his cage to save Lylla, but the High Evolutionary shoots her dead. Rocket tears his face apart, Teefs and Floor are shot dead too, and Rocket escapes alone.

Part 9: Saving Rocket

While they use the code, Rocket's heart stops, and in a world of light he meets Lylla and the others. Told it isn't his time yet, he comes back as Peter and Gamora shut off the kill switch.

Part 10: The battle on Arête

Nebula, Drax and Mantis find caged children, are captured, and escape by winning over the Abilisks. Kraglin brings Knowhere into the fight, and the High Evolutionary kills his mutinous crew. The Guardians fight together and send the children to Knowhere through Cosmo's telekinetic tunnel.

Part 11: Rocket and the High Evolutionary

The High Evolutionary attacks Rocket as he tries to free captive animals. The Guardians beat him together, but Rocket spares his life. Freezing in space, Peter is saved by Adam, whom Groot had rescued.

Part 12: Separate paths

Peter names Rocket the new captain and goes home to his grandfather on Earth. Mantis sets off on a journey, Nebula and Drax raise the children on Knowhere, and Gamora returns to the Ravagers. Rocket forms a new team with Adam, Phyla and others.

Key terms

Knowhere
A city inside a giant creature's skull, now the Guardians' base.
The High Evolutionary
A scientist who modifies animals to create a "perfect" species.
OrgoCorp
The High Evolutionary's company, whose headquarters is made of living tissue.
Counter-Earth
A planet the High Evolutionary built to look like Earth.
Adam Warlock
A powerful young man created by the golden Sovereign.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why doesn't Gamora remember Peter?

This Gamora came from 2014 in Endgame. She never shared those years with him.

How was Rocket made?

The High Evolutionary experimented on him when he was a baby raccoon.

What happens to the Guardians?

They go their separate ways, and Rocket leads a new team of Guardians.
