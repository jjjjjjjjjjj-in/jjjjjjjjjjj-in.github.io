Part 1: Janet disappears

A memory of hide-and-seek

Hank recalls what happened 30 years ago. In 1987, he and Janet, as Ant-Man and the Wasp, set out to stop a nuclear missile. To get inside it, Janet shrank past the point of no return and vanished into the Quantum Realm. Little Hope was told her mother had gone away on a trip.

Part 2: Scott at home

Life indoors

The present. Because he joined the fight in Civil War, Scott is serving two years of house arrest. He practices drums and plays elaborate games with his daughter Cassie at home. Three days remain.

A strange dream

Scott dreams of Janet playing hide-and-seek with young Hope, a scene he's never seen. Troubled, he calls Hank for the first time in years.

Scott is taken

The next day, Hope whisks Scott away, leaving a giant ant wearing his ankle monitor in his place. Because Scott took the suit to Germany, Hank and Hope have been on the run for violating the Accords.

The quantum tunnel

In their shrinkable, portable lab, they've been building a tunnel to the Quantum Realm. When they switched it on once, Scott's dream linked him to Janet.

Part 3: Two enemies

The dealer Burch

Hope tries to buy a missing part from black-market dealer Sonny Burch, but he wants Hank's whole lab. In her Wasp suit, Hope takes down his men.

Ghost

Then a mysterious enemy who phases through walls, Ghost, appears and steals the shrunken lab.

Bill Foster

Hank turns to his old colleague Bill Foster, who helps them find Ghost's hideout.

Part 4: Who Ghost is

Ava's past

Ghost is Ava. As a child, she lost her parents in an accident in her father's lab and her body became unstable, always phasing. She's in constant pain and won't live much longer.

Foster's secret

Bill Foster raised Ava. They believe energy from Janet in the Quantum Realm could heal Ava's body, even if it puts Janet at risk.

Janet's voice

Janet speaks through Scott's body and tells them where she is in the Quantum Realm.

Part 5: Chase through the city

Luis talks

Captured by Burch's men and given truth serum, Luis rambles on and on, and gives away where the lab is.

The lab in play

Ghost takes the lab, and Burch's crew chases them all. On the hills of San Francisco, a wild chase unfolds with cars that shrink and grow.

Giant Scott

Scott grows giant and wades through the bay to get the lab back, with help from Cassie and Luis.

Part 6: Into the Quantum Realm

Hank's journey

Hank travels into the Quantum Realm in a small pod. In its strange landscape, he finally finds Janet.

A family after 30 years

Hank and Janet return, and Hope embraces her mother for the first time in 30 years.

Healing Ava

Using abilities she gained in the Quantum Realm, Janet eases some of Ava's pain.

Home just in time

Scott makes it home seconds before Agent Woo arrives to check on him, and his house arrest ends safely.

Part 7: The credit scenes

Mid-credits

Scott enters the Quantum Realm to collect energy for Ava. But outside, Hank, Janet and Hope turn to dust and vanish. Scott is stranded in the Quantum Realm.

After the credits

In Scott's empty house, the giant ant plays the drums while an emergency broadcast plays on TV.
