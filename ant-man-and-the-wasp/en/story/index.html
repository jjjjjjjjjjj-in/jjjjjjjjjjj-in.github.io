Part 1: Janet disappears

1-1A game of hide-and-seek

Hank Pym narrates a memory. His wife Janet plays hide-and-seek with their young daughter, Hope.

Hank and Janet secretly worked as Ant-Man and the Wasp. Hope was only told that her mother had gone away for work.

1-2The 1987 missile

In 1987, the two are sent to stop a Soviet nuclear missile. The gap inside it is too small to get through.

Janet switches off the regulator that keeps her from shrinking too far, and goes subatomic to get inside. The missile is stopped, but Janet vanishes into the Quantum Realm and never returns.

Part 2: The last days of house arrest

2-1Scott under house arrest

Present-day San Francisco. Scott Lang broke the Sokovia Accords by fighting on Captain America's side in Civil War. After a deal with the German government he was sent home and given two years of house arrest.

He wears an ankle monitor, and leaving the house would mean a long prison sentence. His case officer is FBI agent Jimmy Woo. Three days remain.

2-2Life indoors

Scott passes the time at home. He practices card tricks and plays the drums. Agent Woo, who drops by to check on him, is fascinated by the card tricks. When his daughter Cassie stays over, they turn the whole house into an Ant-Man adventure.

His best friend Luis has started a security company, X-Con Security, with their old crew Dave and Kurt, and Scott is a partner. The company is trying to land a big client.

2-3A falling-out with Hank and Hope

Scott hasn't spoken to Hank or Hope in a long time. He took Hank's suit to Germany without telling them and used it in the Avengers' fight.

Because of that, Hank and Hope were charged under the Accords too, and are now on the run from the FBI.

2-4A strange dream

One night Scott dreams that he is Janet, playing hide-and-seek with little Hope, a moment he has never seen.

Troubled, Scott calls Hank, even though they haven't spoken in years and he's under house arrest.

2-5Scott is taken

The next day Hope shows up and takes Scott out of the house. To fool the FBI, they leave a giant ant wearing his ankle monitor in his place.

Hope is still angry that Scott took the suit without telling them.

2-6The portable lab

Hank's whole lab building can shrink down and be carried by a handle like a suitcase. Inside, he and Hope have been building a tunnel to the Quantum Realm.

The night before, they managed to open it for a moment, which is when Scott had his dream. When Scott went into the Quantum Realm before, he seems to have become entangled with Janet. They take the dream as a message from her, proof that she's alive.

Part 3: The dealer and the Ghost

3-1The deal with Burch

To stabilize the tunnel, they need one more part. Hope goes to the restaurant of black-market dealer Sonny Burch to buy it.

Burch has heard about Hank's research from his contact inside the FBI. Sensing a fortune, he breaks the deal and demands the lab in exchange for the part.

3-2The Wasp in action

Hope puts on the Wasp suit, which lets her fly and shrink or grow, and takes down Burch's men one after another in the restaurant kitchen.

Scott tries to help in a new suit Hank made.

3-3The Ghost takes the lab

Then a masked enemy appears. Her body flickers, and she attacks by phasing through walls and people.

Hope and Scott fight her together, but she disappears with the shrunken lab. They come to call her the "ghost."

Part 4: Who the Ghost is

4-1Bill Foster

To get the lab back, Hank reluctantly visits his old partner Bill Foster. Foster once took part in Hank's growth experiments, but the two fell out. He now teaches quantum physics at UC Berkeley.

Foster tells them that by modifying the regulator from a suit, they might be able to track the lab.

4-2The old suit in the trophy

To track the lab, they need the old suit. Instead of handing it to the FBI, Scott shrank the old suit and hid it inside a trophy Cassie gave him. But Cassie has taken the trophy to school.

Scott and Hope sneak into the school. On the way, Scott's suit malfunctions and leaves him stuck at the height of a child. He hides under a big hoodie and slips out among the kids.

4-3The hideout in the woods

Using a part from the old suit, they track the lab to a hideout in the woods.

But as soon as they get inside, the Ghost captures them and they're all tied up.

4-4Ava Starr's past

The Ghost takes off her mask: she is Ava Starr. Her father, Elihas Starr, was once Hank's partner, until Hank pushed him out.

Her father kept doing quantum experiments, and when Ava was a child, an explosion in his lab killed him and her mother. Ava survived, but her body's molecules were left in constant flux, so she phases through things.

S.H.I.E.L.D. then took her in and, with a promise to cure her, used her as an assassin and spy code-named Ghost. They never kept that promise.

4-5Foster's plan

Then Foster walks in. He is the one who raised Ava. She is in constant pain and doesn't have long to live.

They believe they can cure her by drawing out the quantum energy stored in Janet's body. Hank refuses to help, saying it would kill Janet.

4-6Escape from the hideout

The three of them stage a distraction and escape from the hideout with the lab.

Part 5: A message from Janet

5-1The tunnel opens

With the lab back, they manage to open a stable version of the tunnel. Suddenly, Scott starts acting differently.

5-2Janet speaks through Scott

Janet, in the Quantum Realm, uses her link to Scott to speak through his body. Hank and Hope are stunned to hear Janet's words coming out of Scott's mouth.

She gives them her exact location. She warns that the Quantum Realm is unstable: after two hours, they won't be able to reach her again for a century.

Back to himself, Scott remembers nothing of it.

Part 6: The FBI and Burch

6-1Truth serum and Luis's long story

Burch's crew grabs Luis, Dave and Kurt of X-Con Security and injects them with truth serum. Luis rambles on about all kinds of things nobody asked.

From what he says, Burch learns where Scott and the others are, and passes it on to his contact at the FBI.

6-2Racing home

Luis warns Scott that the FBI is on its way to his house. Scott races home, puts the ankle monitor back on, and gets into the bathtub.

Agent Woo and his team burst in, but Scott is right there. There's no proof he broke his house arrest.

6-3Hank and Hope are arrested

Meanwhile, Hank and Hope are arrested by the FBI. While that happens, Ava takes the lab.

6-4Breakout with ants

Scott slips out of the house again to rescue them. Using ants, he gets the Wasp suit and FBI jackets to the two of them inside the FBI building and gets them out, much like how Hank once got Scott out of jail in the first film.

Part 7: The Quantum Realm and the chase

7-1Getting the lab back

The three find the lab and take it back. They only have a little time left to reach Janet.

7-2Hank's journey

Hank climbs into a small vehicle and travels alone into the Quantum Realm, through strange landscapes and past dangerous creatures like tardigrades.

Scott and Hope stay outside to guard the lab and the tunnel.

7-3The San Francisco chase

Burch's crew arrives, and a chase for the lab begins. Cars shrink and grow as they race over San Francisco's hills.

Even a giant Hello Kitty PEZ dispenser, a gift from Cassie to Luis, gets used in the fight.

7-4Giant Scott

Along the way the lab falls into Burch's hands, and he tries to get away on a ferry. Scott's flying ant gets eaten by a seagull, so he grows giant and swims across the bay after him.

When Scott starts to give out in the water, Hope, as the Wasp, pulls him out and brings him back to normal size.

7-5Ava goes after Janet's energy

In the end Ava gets hold of the lab. Even though Hank is still in the Quantum Realm, she starts forcibly draining Janet's energy.

7-6Luis and the crew fight back

Luis, Dave and Kurt take down Burch and his men, which frees Scott and Hope to stop Ava. They defeat her and get the lab back.

Part 8: A family after 30 years

8-1Reunion in the Quantum Realm

Deep in the Quantum Realm, Hank begins to lose his mind and imagines himself back in his 1980s home.

Then Janet reaches him and steadies his mind with her hands on his head. She explains that the Quantum Realm has changed her. They're together again after 30 years.

8-2Hope and her mother

Hank and Janet return safely, and Hope holds her mother for the first time in 30 years.

8-3Healing Ava

Using powers she gained in the Quantum Realm, Janet willingly gives some of her energy to Ava, stabilizing her body for a while.

Ava and Foster go into hiding.

8-4Free at last

Scott makes it home again in time for the end of his sentence. A suspicious Agent Woo shows up, but finds Scott at home. Woo declares his house arrest over.

Burch and his men are arrested. Dosed with their own truth serum, they confess to everything.

Part 9: The credit scenes

9-1Scott heads into the Quantum Realm

During the credits. To help keep Ava stable, they plan to gather energy from the Quantum Realm. From a tunnel set up on a rooftop, Hank, Janet and Hope send Scott into the Quantum Realm.

Janet warns him to watch out for tardigrades and time vortexes.

9-2Three turn to dust

Before they can bring Scott back, Hank, Janet and Hope turn to dust and vanish (because of what happens at the end of Infinity War).

With no one left to answer him, Scott is stranded in the Quantum Realm.

9-3The empty house

After the credits. In Scott's empty house, the giant stand-in ant plays the drums while an emergency broadcast plays on the TV.

Summary (spoilers through the ending)

Rescuing Janet

In 1987, Janet shrank too far to stop a missile and vanished into the Quantum Realm (Part 1).

Because Scott was entangled with Janet, his dream told Hank and Hope she was alive (Part 2). Janet spoke through Scott's body to give her location (Part 5), and Hank went into the Quantum Realm to bring her home (Parts 7 and 8).

The Ghost's story (Ava)

Ava lost her parents in a lab accident as a child and was left with a phasing body in constant pain. After S.H.I.E.L.D. used her, she and Foster tried to cure her with Janet's energy (Part 4).

They fought over the lab, but in the end Janet willingly shared her energy and stabilized Ava for a while (Part 8). Ava and Foster went into hiding.

Scott's sentence and making amends

Taking the suit without telling them had broken Scott's bond with Hank and Hope (Part 2). By working with them to save Janet, he made amends, and he finished his house arrest without the FBI catching him (Parts 6 and 8).

Who died or vanished

- Elihas Starr and his wife (before the story): Ava's parents, killed in the explosion during a quantum experiment.

- Hank, Janet and Hope (Part 9): right after sending Scott into the Quantum Realm, they turned to dust because of what happened at the end of Infinity War.

- No one dies during the main story. Burch and his men were arrested.
