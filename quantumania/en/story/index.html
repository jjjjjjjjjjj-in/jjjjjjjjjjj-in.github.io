Part 1: A meeting 30 years ago

Janet stranded

Thirty years ago, stranded in the Quantum Realm, Janet meets a man beside a crashed ship. They work together to repair its power source.

Part 2: Scott the celebrity

Fame

The present. Scott is a famous hero with a memoir, recognized everywhere he goes.

Cassie's device

Cassie, now 18, has been building a device with Hank to send signals into the Quantum Realm. Janet panics and shouts for them to shut it off.

Pulled in

Light bursts from the device, and all five are sucked into the Quantum Realm.

Part 3: The Quantum Realm

Separated

Scott and Cassie are taken in by a band of rebels. Hope, Hank and Janet land elsewhere.

A bustling city

Janet's group visits a city where they meet an old acquaintance of hers, to Hank's jealousy.

Janet's secret

Janet finally explains: the man she helped 30 years ago, Kang, is a conqueror who destroyed world after world. She shrank his ship's power core so he could never escape.

Part 4: Kang's deal

Scott captured

Kang captures Scott and Cassie. He offers to send the family home if Scott retrieves the shrunken power core.

The probability storm

Inside the core, Scott is surrounded by countless possible versions of himself. They work together to reach it.

Betrayal and revolt

Kang breaks his word. The family and the people of the realm rise up against his army.

Part 5: The final battle

The ant army

Hank arrives leading ants that have evolved over ages in the Quantum Realm.

M.O.D.O.K. turns

Kang's henchman M.O.D.O.K. turns out to be Darren Cross from the first film. In the end, he sides with Scott.

Kang's fall

Scott and Hope send Kang tumbling into his ship's power core.

The way home

Cassie opens a portal, and the family makes it home just in time.

Part 6: The credit scenes

Mid-credits

A vast council of Kangs learns their exiled variant has been defeated and begins to act.

After the credits

In 1901, Loki and Mobius watch a presentation by the inventor Victor Timely.
