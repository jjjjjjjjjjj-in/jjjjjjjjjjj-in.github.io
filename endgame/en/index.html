Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

It picks up right after Infinity War

Be sure to watch that first.

Lots of scenes from earlier films

Because it involves time travel, you revisit moments from The Avengers, The Dark World, Guardians and more from new angles.

It's three hours long

Taking a break partway is a fine idea.

There's no post-credits scene

Just a sound at the very end.

StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

Part 1: Twenty-three days later

Thanos's snap erases half of all life, including Clint's family. Tony and Nebula, adrift in space, are rescued by Carol and brought back to Earth.

The remaining heroes find Thanos, but he has already destroyed the stones. With no way to undo it, an enraged Thor cuts off his head.

Part 2: Five years later

Five years later, Scott escapes the Quantum Realm and wonders if it could be a way into the past.

Tony, living by a lake with his wife Pepper and daughter Morgan, says no at first. But that night he finds a safe way to time travel, returns Steve's shield, and makes peace with him.

Part 3: Bringing the team back

Bruce and Rocket bring back Thor, who has been drinking his days away in New Asgard. Natasha fetches Clint, who has been killing criminals in Tokyo.

Together they work out where the six stones were in the past, split into three teams, and jump back in time.

Part 4: 2012 and 1970

In New York in 2012, Bruce receives the Time Stone from the Ancient One, and Steve gets the scepter with the Mind Stone.

But Loki escapes with the Tesseract. Tony and Steve go to a base in 1970 for the Tesseract and Pym Particles. Tony talks with his young father Howard, and Steve watches Peggy from afar.

Part 5: 2013 and 2014

In Asgard in 2013, Rocket gets the Reality Stone, and Thor, encouraged by his mother Frigga, gets Mjolnir back.

On Morag in 2014, Rhodey and Nebula get the Power Stone. But through Nebula's cybernetics, the 2014 Thanos learns of the plan, captures her, and swaps in his own Nebula.

Part 6: Vormir, and another snap

On Vormir, the Soul Stone demands the life of a loved one. Natasha lets herself fall from the cliff so that Clint can live.

Back in the present, the grieving team builds a new gauntlet, and Bruce snaps to bring everyone back. Moments later, the 2014 Thanos, summoned by the swapped Nebula, destroys the Compound.

Part 7: The final battle

Tony, Steve and Thor take on Thanos. Steve lifts Mjolnir, but his shield is shattered.

Then the returned heroes and armies pour through portals, and with Steve's "Avengers, assemble" the final battle begins. In the end, Tony, having moved the six stones onto his own suit, says "I am Iron Man" and snaps, wiping out Thanos and his army.

Part 8: New paths

Burned by the stones' power, Tony dies with Pepper and the others at his side, and his funeral is held at the lake house.

Thor leaves New Asgard to Valkyrie and heads into space with the Guardians. Steve, sent to return the stones, stays in the past to live his life, then reappears as an old man and hands Sam the shield.

Key terms

The Snap
Thanos snapping his fingers with the stones, wiping out half of all life.
The time heist
The plan to travel through the Quantum Realm into the past and borrow the stones before Thanos got them.
Time travel rules
Changing the past doesn't change your present; it creates a separate branch. So the stones must be returned afterward.
Pym Particles
Hank Pym's size-changing particles, needed to travel to the past and back. There are only a few.
New Asgard
The new town Asgard's survivors built in Norway on Earth.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why doesn't changing the past change the present?

In this film, changing the past creates a new branch of reality. The original present stays the same.

How did Steve end up old?

After returning the stones, he stayed in the past and lived a life with Peggy.

What about Loki escaping with the Tesseract?

The 2012 Loki slipped away in the chaos. His story continues in the series Loki.

What did the vanished people experience for five years?

For them it was an instant. When they came back, five years had passed.

What happens to Thor?

He makes Valkyrie ruler of New Asgard and leaves to travel with the Guardians.

Easy-to-miss details

Who lifts Mjolnir

The hero who barely budged the hammer in Age of Ultron finally lifts it.

"Avengers, assemble"

The famous rallying cry is finally said in full for the first time in the series.

The final sound

At the very end of the credits, you hear the clang of metal being hammered, from the first Iron Man.

Stan Lee's cameo

Marvel Comics legend Stan Lee appears driving a car in a 1970s scene, one of his final film cameos.

The ending and post-creditsSpoilers

After the end credits

There is no post-credits scene; only the sound of Tony hammering his suit plays at the end.

What the 2026 Encore re-release addsSpoilers

From September 2026, the film returned to theaters as Avengers: Endgame – Encore. The story is the same as the 2019 version, but new footage leading into Avengers: Doomsday was added. This summary is based on reports from US trade outlets and others (checked 2026-11-02).

The ending: a knock at the door

In the final scene, Steve, who chose to stay in the past, dances with Peggy. There's a knock, and Loki, in his TVA outfit, says he's there to help them. Most reports describe this as a new ending, but at least one says no new footage was added to the main story, so it may depend on the screening format.

Mid-credits: Banner underground

Bruce Banner, alone in an underground facility, hears Doctor Doom's voice as Doom comes to take him. Reports place it after Spider-Man: Brand New Day.

Warnings at the TVA

TVA screens light up with warnings of incursions, Earths from different universes colliding with each other.

The final clang and Doom

The 2019 version ended the credits with no image, just the clang of Tony hammering his first suit. In the Encore, that metal-on-metal sound plays over images of Doom, a big change in what that sound seems to mean.

Differences by format

Reports differ on the length of the new footage (about 4 minutes vs. 5–6). Some say all four scenes were only in IMAX and Infinity Vision screenings, with standard screenings showing two. In Japan, Infinity Vision screenings also had an extra Japan-only featurette of about 3 minutes (Marvel Japan).

On Disney+

The 2019 version is on Disney+. As of November 2, 2026, we couldn't confirm any official announcement about streaming the Encore version.
