Part 1: Twenty-three days later

1-1Clint's family vanishes

Clint is spending the day with his family at their farm. He teaches his daughter Lila archery, and his wife Laura calls them in for lunch.

He looks away for a moment, and Lila is gone. His wife and sons have turned to dust too. Thanos's snap has wiped out half of all life in the universe.

1-2Tony and Nebula adrift

After the battle on Titan, Tony and Nebula drift through space in a broken ship. Its fuel cells were damaged in the battle, and a fix bought them a little flight time, but now the ship is dead. They pass the time flicking a paper football.

With no food or water left, Tony records a farewell message to Pepper on his helmet. He says the oxygen will run out by morning, then lies down, wasted and weak.

1-3Carol to the rescue

A glowing Carol Danvers appears and carries the whole ship back to the Avengers Compound on Earth.

Tony and Pepper embrace. To Steve, Tony admits that he lost the kid, Peter.

1-4Tony's anger

At the Compound, the survivors take stock. Tony lashes out at Steve: he said they needed armor around the world, and Steve was the one who opposed it.

He pulls the arc reactor from his chest, shoves it at Steve, and collapses. Bruce and the others get him to rest.

1-5Where Thanos went

Nebula remembers Thanos saying he would rest at "the Garden" when his work was done. Rocket and the others detect a burst of energy like the snap, used two days earlier on a planet.

If they can get the stones back, maybe they can undo it. Leaving the injured Tony behind, Steve, Natasha, Bruce, Thor, Rhodey, Rocket, Nebula and Carol head there.

1-6Thanos without the stones

Thanos is alone on a quiet, empty planet, farming. The heroes attack together, and Thor cuts off the arm wearing the gauntlet.

But the gauntlet is empty. Thanos says he used the stones to destroy the stones, and it nearly killed him. Nebula confirms her father doesn't lie.

1-7Thor's blow

There's no way left to undo it. Driven by rage and regret, Thor cuts off Thanos's head with Stormbreaker.

In the last film, Thor aimed for the chest and Thanos got to snap. This time he mutters that he went for the head, but nothing is won back.

Part 2: Five years later

2-1Five years on

Five years pass. In New York, Steve runs a support group for people who lost loved ones.

Natasha stays at the Compound, keeping in touch with allies by screen: Rocket and Nebula in space, Okoye in Wakanda, Carol, and Rhodey. Rhodey reports that Clint has been attacking criminal groups around the world and killing them.

When Steve visits, Natasha tears up: this job and these people were everything she had.

2-2Scott comes back

In a storage unit, a rat happens to trigger the machinery in an old van, and Scott Lang, who had been stuck in the Quantum Realm, bursts out.

A memorial wall for "the vanished" lists his own name. He rushes home and hugs his daughter Cassie, now much older.

2-3The time travel idea

Scott goes to the Compound and tells Steve and Natasha: for him, only five hours passed in the Quantum Realm, but five years passed outside.

Time works differently there. So could they pass through it into the past and get the stones before Thanos collected them?

2-4Tony at the lake house

The three visit Tony at his house by a lake. He has married Pepper, and they have a daughter, Morgan.

Tony turns them down: he can't put the family he finally has at risk.

2-5Bruce, and a failed test

Next, they see Bruce. After 18 months of research, he has merged the Hulk's body with his own mind, and at a diner he poses for photos with kids.

At the Compound they try it with Scott's van, but it goes wrong: Scott turns into a teenager, an old man, and a baby.

2-6Tony's breakthrough

That night, Tony stares at a photo of himself with Peter. Running calculation after calculation on his computer, he finds a safe way through the Quantum Realm.

Pepper understands: even if she stopped him, he wouldn't be able to rest. Tony tucks Morgan in, and she tells him she loves him 3000.

2-7Returning the shield

Tony arrives at the Compound and says he has worked out time travel. He also hands back the shield Steve left behind after their old fight.

His condition: bring back what they lost without losing what he has. After years apart, the two make peace.

Part 3: Bringing the team back

3-1Thor in New Asgard

Bruce and Rocket go to New Asgard, the Asgardians' town in Norway. Valkyrie, who looks after it, says Thor keeps to himself.

Thor has grown heavy and spends his days drinking and playing video games with Korg and others. He flinches at Thanos's name. Bruce and Rocket mention beer on the ship and bring him along.

3-2Clint in Tokyo

Natasha goes to Tokyo. On a rainy night, Clint cuts down the yakuza boss Akihiko and his men with a sword. Since losing his family, he has been killing criminals.

Natasha tells him they've found a chance. Clint agrees to come back.

3-3Clint's test run

At the Compound, Tony and Rocket finish a new quantum tunnel. Clint goes first.

For a few moments he's back at his farm in the past, and he hears Lila's voice. He returns holding her baseball glove. The test works.

3-4Where the stones were

Together they work out where each stone was, and when. Bruce realizes that three stones were in New York during the battle there. Thor talks about the Reality Stone inside Jane.

Nebula explains the Power Stone was on the planet Morag, and that Thanos took Gamora to Vormir and came back alone.

3-5Three teams

There are only enough Pym Particles for one round trip each. They split into three teams.

1) New York, 2012: Tony, Steve, Bruce and Scott. 2) Asgard, 2013: Thor and Rocket. 3) Space, 2014: Natasha, Clint, Rhodey and Nebula. Rocket hands Clint a shrunken spaceship.

3-6Departure

Steve tells the team: five years ago they lost, and this is their one chance. Whatever it takes.

They all step onto the quantum tunnel and jump to their points in the past.

Part 4: 2012 and 1970

4-1New York, 2012

They arrive in New York right after the first Avengers film's victory over Loki's army. The Avengers of the past have just captured Loki.

The four split up. Steve, Tony and Scott go to Stark Tower, where the stones are being taken away. Bruce heads to the sorcerers' Sanctum, which guards the Time Stone.

4-2The Ancient One's promise

The Ancient One, leader of the sorcerers, confronts Bruce. Taking a stone away would split her time into a separate branch and leave it in danger, she says, so she refuses.

Bruce promises to return every stone to its own time when they're done. Learning that in the future Strange gave his stone away by choice, she entrusts Bruce with the Time Stone.

4-3The password in the elevator

Steve rides an elevator with the men carrying Loki's scepter. Knowing they are secretly members of Hydra, he whispers "Hail Hydra."

Thinking he's one of them, they hand over the scepter, which holds the Mind Stone.

4-4Two Captain Americas

Steve runs into his 2012 self, who thinks he's Loki in disguise and attacks.

They're evenly matched until Steve shouts that Bucky is alive. His past self freezes in shock, and Steve knocks him out with the scepter.

4-5Losing the Tesseract

The past Tony and Thor are arguing with the official Pierce over who takes Loki and the case holding the Tesseract, with the Space Stone inside. A shrunken Scott slips into the past Tony's chest device and triggers a heart attack, and in the confusion Tony grabs the case.

But the past Hulk bursts out of a stairwell and knocks into him, and the case falls. The Tesseract slides across the floor, and the captive Loki picks it up and vanishes.

4-6To 1970

Scott and Bruce head back with two stones, but Tony and Steve don't have the Pym Particles to try again. Tony remembers that in 1970 the Tesseract was kept at a military base, Camp Lehigh.

Hank Pym, who created the particles, worked there too. Tony and Steve jump to 1970.

4-7Talking with Howard

Tony takes the Tesseract from the base's vault. On his way out, he runs into his young father, Howard, who is waiting for his child to be born. That child is Tony himself.

Hiding who he is, Tony listens to his father's worries about being a dad. Before they part, Tony hugs him. Out on the road, Stan Lee calls out from a passing car.

4-8Pym Particles, and Peggy through the window

Steve takes the Pym Particles they need from Hank Pym's lab.

Hiding in an office, he watches Peggy Carter, the woman he loved, through a glass window. Then he and Tony return to the present with what they came for.

Part 5: 2013 and 2014

5-1Asgard, 2013

Thor and Rocket arrive in Asgard during the time of Thor: The Dark World. It is the day Thor's mother Frigga will die.

Seeing his old love Jane, Thor falls apart. Rocket goes on alone and uses a device to draw the Reality Stone out of Jane's body.

5-2Frigga's words

Frigga realizes this Thor has come from the future. He tries to warn her of her death, but she won't hear it: he's here to fix his own future, not hers.

She tells him that everyone fails at who they're supposed to be. What matters is how well you succeed at being who you are.

5-3Mjolnir returns

Before leaving, Thor holds out his hand, and this time's Mjolnir flies into it.

"I'm still worthy!" His spirit restored, Thor returns to the present with Rocket, Mjolnir in hand.

5-4Morag, 2014

Natasha and Clint fly to Vormir, while Rhodey and Nebula stay on the planet Morag.

Peter Quill, the future Guardians leader, arrives dancing to his music. Rhodey knocks him out, and the two take the orb holding the Power Stone from the ruins.

5-5Two Nebulas

Meanwhile, the Nebula of this time is aboard Thanos's ship with Gamora. Her cybernetics link with those of the Nebula from the future, and the future Nebula's memories flow in.

Thanos has the memories projected and learns that his future self won, and that the Avengers are trying to undo it.

5-6Nebula captured

On Morag, the future Nebula collapses as her systems fail and can't go home. Rhodey returns to the present without her.

Thanos's forces capture her. Thanos sends this time's Nebula to the present in her place, disguised as her future self, to prepare for his arrival.

Part 6: Vormir, and another snap

6-1The Soul Stone's keeper

Natasha and Clint reach the top of the mountain on Vormir. Waiting for them is Red Skull, now the stone's keeper.

He tells them the stone demands the life of someone you love. They realize this is where Thanos killed Gamora.

6-2Who will fall

Each wants to be the one to die so the other can live. Clint feels that after all the killing of the past five years, it should be him. Natasha believes Clint must go home to his family.

They struggle at the cliff's edge, each racing to jump first.

6-3Natasha's end

As Natasha goes over the edge, Clint catches her hand. She tells him to let her go, that it's okay, and lets go herself.

Clint wakes lying in water, with the Soul Stone in his hand.

6-4Return, and grief

Everyone returns to the Compound with all six stones. No one notices that Nebula has been swapped. Only Natasha is missing.

By the lake, Thor insists the stones can bring her back, but Clint says they can't. In fury, Bruce hurls a bench.

6-5A new gauntlet

Tony, Bruce and Rocket build a new gauntlet to hold the six stones. Snapping with it will unleash more power than a body can bear.

Thor insists on doing it, but Bruce, who can best withstand gamma radiation, takes it on. He will bring back everyone who vanished and change nothing else.

6-6Bruce snaps

As Tony shields the team, Bruce puts on the gauntlet. The stones' power burns his arm, and in agony he snaps. His arm is badly burned.

Outside, birds start singing, and Clint's phone rings. It's his wife Laura. Those who vanished are back.

6-7The Compound falls

Moments later, the impostor Nebula activates the quantum tunnel and brings the 2014 Thanos, with his huge warship, into the present.

Missiles rain down, and the Avengers Compound collapses. Rhodey, Rocket and Bruce are pinned under rubble in a flooding underground level.

Part 7: The final battle

7-1Rescue under the rubble

Underground, Bruce holds up the collapsing ceiling as Rhodey and Rocket nearly drown. Scott grows giant and pulls them out.

Clint runs through the tunnels with the stone-filled gauntlet, protecting it from Thanos's beasts.

7-2Nebula and Nebula

Aboard Thanos's ship, the present-day Nebula convinces this time's Gamora to join her. Underground, they find the past Nebula, who has taken the gauntlet from Clint.

The present Nebula tells her past self she can break free of their father, but she won't listen. When the past Nebula aims a gun at Gamora, the present Nebula shoots her past self dead.

7-3Three against Thanos

On the rubble, Tony, Steve and Thor face Thanos. He says he will tear this universe down to its last atom and build a new one that will be grateful.

They fight, but Thanos is stronger. With Thor pinned, Mjolnir flies past him into Steve's hand. Thor is thrilled: he knew it.

7-4The shattered shield

Steve fights well with Mjolnir and his shield, but Thanos splits the shield with his huge blade. Tony and Thor are down too.

Thanos calls down his army from the ship. Battered, Steve straps on the broken shield and stands alone before the army.

7-5"On your left"

Sam's voice comes over Steve's radio: "Cap, can you hear me? On your left." A glowing portal opens behind him, and T'Challa, Shuri, Okoye and the armies of Wakanda step through.

Strange, Wong and the other sorcerers open portal after portal. The heroes who came back from the snap, the Guardians, the Ravagers, Asgard's warriors, and Pepper in a suit of armor from Tony all arrive.

7-6Avengers, assemble

With everyone gathered, Steve raises Mjolnir and calls out: "Avengers... assemble." The final battle begins.

If the stones go back to the past, Thanos can't touch them. The heroes pass the gauntlet from hand to hand, from Clint to T'Challa to Peter, trying to get it to Scott's van with its quantum tunnel.

7-7Wanda and Carol

Wanda goes after Thanos, and her power nearly overwhelms him. Thanos orders his ship to fire on the battlefield, his own troops included.

Then Carol flies in and smashes straight through the warship. But the van is destroyed in the fighting. Thanos grabs Carol as she holds the gauntlet, pries out the Power Stone, and blasts her away with it.

7-8Strange's signal

Tony looks at Strange, who holds up one finger: of the 14,000,605 futures he saw in the last film, there's only one where they win, and this is it.

Tony grapples with Thanos for the gauntlet but is thrown aside.

7-9"I am Iron Man"

Thanos says "I am inevitable" and snaps, but nothing happens. The stones are gone from the gauntlet.

While grappling, Tony moved all six stones onto his own suit's hand. "And I... am... Iron Man," he says, and snaps. Thanos's army turns to dust, and finally Thanos sits down quietly and fades away too.

Part 8: New paths

8-1Tony's end

The stones' power has burned the right side of Tony's body. As he slumps against the rubble, Rhodey, Peter and Pepper come to his side. Peter cries that they won.

Pepper tells him they'll be okay and he can rest now. Tony quietly dies.

8-2The message he left

Before the heist, Tony recorded a message. Pepper and the others watch it.

He says he had planned for things not working out, and that part of the journey is the end. Finally, he tells Morgan he loves her 3000.

8-3Tony's funeral

Tony's funeral is held at the lake house. Heroes and friends gather, including those who came back.

Pepper sets the arc reactor from the first Iron Man, ringed with flowers, afloat on the lake.

Around this time, Peter goes back to school and hugs his best friend Ned when they meet again.

8-4Thor sets out

In New Asgard, Thor asks Valkyrie to be the new ruler.

Then he leaves for space with the Guardians. Peter Quill is searching for the 2014 Gamora, who has gone off somewhere.

8-5Returning the stones

Steve sets off to return the six stones and Mjolnir to their own times. Bruce runs the machine, and Sam and Bucky see him off.

He's supposed to be back in five seconds, but he doesn't return.

8-6The old man on the bench, and the shield

Bucky notices an old man on a nearby bench. It's Steve, grown old. After returning the stones, he stayed in the past and lived his life.

Steve calls Sam over and hands him the shield. Sam says it feels like it belongs to someone else, and Steve says that's why it's his. When Sam asks about his life in the past, Steve won't say.

8-7The promised dance

In the final scene, inside a home in the past, a young Steve and Peggy dance together, the dance he promised her in Captain America: The First Avenger.

8-8After the credits

There's no scene at the end of the credits, only the clang of hammering from when Tony built his first suit in a cave in Iron Man.

Summary (spoilers through the end)

The six stones: who got them, and where

- Time Stone: Bruce, in New York in 2012, from the Ancient One after promising to return it (Part 4).

- Mind Stone: Steve, at Stark Tower in 2012, handed the scepter by Hydra agents (Part 4).

- Space Stone: the 2012 attempt failed and Loki vanished with the Tesseract. Tony got it at Camp Lehigh in 1970 (Part 4).

- Reality Stone: Rocket, in Asgard in 2013, drawing it out of Jane (Part 5).

- Power Stone: Rhodey and Nebula, on Morag in 2014, ahead of Quill (Part 5).

- Soul Stone: on Vormir in 2014, Natasha gave her life and Clint got the stone (Part 6).

In the end, Steve returned every stone to its own time (Part 8).

Who died (in order)

- The 2018 Thanos (Part 1): once it's clear he destroyed the stones and nothing can be undone, an enraged Thor cuts off his head with Stormbreaker.

- Akihiko and his yakuza men (Part 3): Clint, killing criminals since losing his family, cuts them down with a sword in Tokyo.

- Natasha (Part 6): the Soul Stone demands the life of a loved one, so to let Clint live and get the stone, she lets go and falls from the cliff on Vormir.

- The 2014 Nebula (Part 7): the present-day Nebula shoots her when she aims a gun at Gamora.

- The 2014 Thanos and his army (Part 7): Tony snaps with the six stones he moved onto his suit, turning them to dust.

- Tony (Part 8): the power of snapping with all six stones burns his body, and he dies.

Note: those erased by the snap, including Clint's family at the start, are brought back when Bruce snaps (Part 6).

My takeOpinion

A reward for anyone who followed ten years of films. "Avengers, assemble" and Tony's final line carry their weight because you've been there since the first film.
