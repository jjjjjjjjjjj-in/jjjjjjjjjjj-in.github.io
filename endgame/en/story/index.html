Part 1: After the Snap

A family vanishes

Clint is at his farm with his family, teaching his daughter archery. In an instant, his wife and children turn to dust.

Adrift in space

Tony and Nebula drift through space in a broken ship. Food and air running out, Tony records a farewell message for Pepper.

Carol to the rescue

A glowing Carol Danvers appears and carries their ship back to Earth. Tony reunites with Pepper, unloads his anger on Steve, and collapses.

Finding Thanos

The remaining heroes find Thanos farming on a quiet planet. But he has already destroyed the stones. In fury, Thor cuts off his head.

Part 2: Five years later

Moving on

Five years pass. Steve leads a support group for people who lost loved ones. Natasha stays at Avengers HQ, keeping in touch with allies on Earth and in space.

Scott returns

Scott, stranded in the Quantum Realm, is accidentally freed. For him, only five hours have passed. He wonders if the Quantum Realm could be a way into the past.

Tony's home

Steve and the others visit Tony, who lives by a lake with Pepper and their daughter Morgan. Afraid of losing what he has, he says no, but that night he works out how time travel could be done.

Gathering the team

Bruce has merged the Hulk's strength with his own mind. Thor is drowning in drink and video games in New Asgard. Natasha goes to Tokyo to bring back Clint, who's been killing criminals.

Part 3: The time heist

Planning

They work out where each stone was in the past and split into three teams: New York in 2012, Asgard in 2013, and space in 2014.

New York, 2012

Just after the Battle of New York, Bruce persuades the Ancient One to lend him the Time Stone. Steve fights his past self and gets Loki's scepter. But the plan for the Tesseract fails, and the 2012 Loki vanishes with it.

A base in 1970

Tony and Steve go to a military base in 1970 to get the Tesseract and more Pym Particles. Tony talks with his young father Howard, and Steve watches Peggy through a window.

Asgard, 2013

Rocket draws the Reality Stone out of Jane. Thor talks with his mother Frigga before her death, finds himself again, and summons Mjolnir.

Space, 2014

Rhodey and Nebula get the Power Stone. But Nebula's cybernetics link with her 2014 self, and the 2014 Thanos learns of the plan. The 2014 Nebula takes our Nebula's place.

Part 4: The cliff on Vormir

The price of the Soul Stone

Natasha and Clint reach Vormir and learn the stone demands the life of someone they love.

Who will go

Each fights to be the one who dies. In the end, Natasha throws herself from the cliff, slipping from Clint's grip. Clint is left holding the Soul Stone.

Grief

Everyone returns to the present except Natasha. The heroes mourn her by the lake.

Part 5: Another snap

A new gauntlet

Tony and the others build a new gauntlet. Bruce, strong enough to survive its power, puts it on and snaps. His arm is badly burned, but Clint's phone rings: everyone has come back.

The compound falls

Moments later, the 2014 Thanos, brought forward by his Nebula, bombards the compound and turns it to rubble.

Three against Thanos

Tony, Steve and Thor face Thanos. Steve lifts Mjolnir, stunning Thor, but Thanos is overwhelming and even splits Steve's shield.

Part 6: Avengers, assemble

Portals open

Steve stands alone before the army when he hears "On your left." Portals open everywhere, and the heroes who came back, with the armies of Wakanda, Asgard and more, pour through.

The final battle

With Steve's call of "Avengers, assemble," the final battle begins. The heroes pass the gauntlet from hand to hand to keep it away from Thanos.

Strange's signal

Tony looks to Strange, who holds up one finger: the one future out of 14 million where they win.

"I am Iron Man"

Thanos takes the gauntlet and snaps, but nothing happens. The stones are already on Tony's hand. "I am Iron Man," he says, and snaps. Thanos and his army turn to dust.

Tony's end

Burned by the stones' power, Tony is surrounded by Peter, Rhodey and Pepper. Pepper tells him he can rest now, and he dies.

Part 7: New paths

Tony's funeral

Pepper and the others watch a message Tony recorded before the heist. Then every hero gathers by the lake for his funeral.

A new ruler

Thor names Valkyrie ruler of New Asgard and leaves to travel the stars with the Guardians.

Returning the stones

Steve goes to return every stone to its time. He doesn't come back on schedule, and then an elderly Steve is found sitting on a nearby bench.

Passing the shield

The old Steve gives his shield to Sam. In the final scene, a young Steve dances with Peggy in the past.

The final sound

There's no scene after the credits, only the clang of hammering from when Tony built his first suit in Iron Man.
