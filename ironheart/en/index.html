Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Where does the Hood's power come from?

Parker's cloak draws its power from a demonic source.

Who is Natalie?

Riri's best friend, who was killed in a shooting. Riri built her armor's AI based on her.

Who appears at the end?

In the finale, the demon lord Mephisto appears and offers Riri a deal.
