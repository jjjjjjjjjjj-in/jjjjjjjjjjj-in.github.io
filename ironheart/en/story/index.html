Episodes 1–2: Back to Chicago

Expelled

Riri Williams, a genius at MIT, is expelled over how she's been funding her armor research.

Home to Chicago

Riri returns to her mother, Ronnie, in Chicago. She still can't move on from losing her best friend Natalie years ago.

The AI

Riri installs an AI in her armor, and it starts talking with Natalie's face and personality.

Parker's offer

Short on money, Riri accepts an offer from the mysterious Parker Robbins to join his crew of thieves.

Episodes 3–4: The Hood's crew

The heist

Riri uses her armor to help the crew with a big heist.

The Hood's power

In his cloak, Parker can vanish and wield strange powers. Riri senses something science can't explain.

A death on the job

A crew member dies during a job, and Riri realizes how dangerous Parker is.

Joe

Riri meets engineer Joe McGillicuddy and they work together.
