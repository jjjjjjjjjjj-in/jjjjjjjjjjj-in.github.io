Part 1: A desert storm and an ancient war

1-1A storm in the night desert

Puente Antiguo, a small desert town in New Mexico. Astrophysicist Jane Foster, her mentor Dr. Erik Selvig and her assistant Darcy, a college student, are chasing a strange phenomenon in the night sky.

A man stumbles out of a swirling column of light, and their vehicle hits him. The story then jumps far back in time.

1-2The war of 965 AD

Norway, 965 AD. The Frost Giants are trying to freeze the Earth and take it over.

Odin, king of the realm of Asgard, leads his army to defend Earth, pushes on into the Frost Giants' realm of Jotunheim and defeats their king, Laufey. He takes away the Casket, the source of the giants' power, and brings it home.

1-3Two brothers in the vault

In Asgard's vault, Odin tells the story to his two young sons, Thor and Loki, as he shows them the Casket.

Thor declares he'll hunt down all the giants. Odin tells them that only one of them can take the throne, but both were born to be kings.

Part 2: The coronation

2-1Coronation day

Many centuries later, in the present day. The grown Thor is about to be crowned king.

Holding his hammer, Mjolnir, he swaggers into the great hall as the crowd cheers. His brother Loki, his mother Frigga, his childhood friend the warrior Sif, and the Warriors Three, Volstagg, Fandral and Hogun, watch.

2-2Frost Giants in the vault

Just as Odin is about to crown Thor, he senses someone has broken into the vault.

Frost Giants have frozen the guards and are trying to take back the Casket. The Destroyer, the giant metal guardian of the vault, wakes up and kills them with fire from its face. The coronation is called off.

2-3Father and son argue

Standing over the dead giants, Thor insists they must strike back at Jotunheim at once.

Odin refuses: he has a truce with Laufey, the Frost Giant king. Thor thinks his father is being weak and won't accept it.

2-4The overturned table

In the banquet hall, Thor flips a table in anger.

Loki, beside him, warns that defying their father would be unwise while quietly egging him on. Thor decides to go to Jotunheim and takes Loki, Sif and the Warriors Three. His friends object but go along anyway.

2-5The gatekeeper of the bridge

The group rides to the observatory at the end of the Bifrost, the rainbow bridge. Heimdall, the gatekeeper who sees all the worlds, is troubled that the giants slipped past his sight.

He warns them he won't open the bridge for their return if it would put Asgard in danger, then sends them to Jotunheim.

Part 3: Jotunheim

3-1Laufey, king of the Frost Giants

Jotunheim is dark and frozen. Thor demands that King Laufey explain how his giants got into Asgard.

Laufey replies that there are traitors in Odin's house, and offers to let them leave if they go now. Loki urges Thor to back down.

3-2The fight begins

As they turn to leave, one of the giants taunts Thor. He can't let it go, smashes the giant with his hammer, and a huge battle breaks out.

Volstagg's arm is burned by a giant's freezing touch, and Fandral is run through by a spike of ice. The others carry the wounded and run.

3-3Loki's blue arm

In the fighting, a giant grabs Loki's arm. It should have badly hurt him, but his skin just turns blue where he was touched, and he is unharmed.

Loki stares at his arm, shaken.

3-4Odin arrives

Thor battles a huge beast while the others are surrounded. Then Odin appears on his eight-legged horse, Sleipnir.

Odin asks Laufey to end this, but Laufey answers that war can no longer be avoided. Odin takes everyone back to Asgard. The truce is broken.

3-5Banished

At the Bifrost observatory, Thor lashes out at his father, and Odin furiously condemns his son's arrogance. Thor even calls him an old fool.

Odin declares him unworthy, strips away his armor and his power, and casts him down to Earth through the bridge. Then he whispers an enchantment over the hammer: "Whosoever holds this hammer, if he be worthy, shall possess the power of Thor." He throws it to Earth as well.

Part 4: The man who fell to Earth

4-1The man they hit

Back to the opening scene. The man Jane's team hit gets up and shouts at the sky, calling out to someone.

When he comes toward them talking nonsense, Darcy knocks him out with her taser. They take him to the hospital.

4-2Gone from the hospital

In the hospital he wakes, says he is Thor, and throws staff members around as they try to hold him. He is finally sedated, but he soon slips out of the hospital.

Meanwhile Jane realizes he appeared exactly where her readings were centered, and goes back to the hospital to find him.

4-3Hit again

Outside the hospital, their vehicle accidentally hits Thor a second time, though again he isn't badly hurt.

They bring him to their lab. In Jane's photos of the event, a human figure can be seen inside the vortex.

4-4The mug at the diner

At the town diner, Thor eats a mountain of breakfast. When he likes his coffee, he smashes the mug on the floor and shouts "Another!" Jane explains that people don't do that here, and he agrees.

Then a local mentions a "satellite" that fell in the desert. Thor realizes it is his hammer and asks where it is.

4-5A hammer no one can lift

The hammer is stuck in a crater in the desert, and the locals have turned it into a party. One after another they try to lift it, and finally hook it up to a pickup truck, but the truck breaks instead.

Then Agent Coulson of S.H.I.E.L.D. arrives and seals off the crater.

4-6The research is seized

S.H.I.E.L.D. takes everything from Jane's lab: equipment, data, even her notebooks. Coulson hands her a check in return. Jane protests furiously, but she can't stop it.

Selvig mentions a colleague in the same field who got involved with S.H.I.E.L.D. and was never heard from again. Thor promises that when he gets his hammer back, he'll recover Jane's research too.

Part 5: Loki's secret

5-1Skin turning blue

In Asgard, Loki goes into the vault alone and lifts the Casket. His skin turns blue from his hands to his face, and his eyes glow red. It is how a Frost Giant looks.

Then Odin comes in.

5-2His true birth

Pressed by Loki, Odin tells the truth. After the old war, he found an abandoned baby in a temple in Jotunheim and took him home. It was Laufey's son: Loki.

Odin says he hoped one day to use him to bring peace between the realms, but Loki accuses him of seeing him as just another stolen relic. In the middle of the argument, Odin collapses.

5-3The Odinsleep

Odin falls into the Odinsleep, a deep sleep to restore his strength. No one knows when he will wake.

With Thor banished and Odin asleep, Loki is next in line. Holding the royal spear Gungnir, he begins to rule Asgard.

5-4A request refused

Sif and the Warriors Three ask King Loki to bring Thor back from Earth.

Loki refuses, saying he cannot undo the All-Father's last command as his first act as king. The four begin to suspect him.

Part 6: Going after the hammer

6-1A horse at the pet store

To reach the hammer, Thor walks into a pet store and asks for a horse. Told they only have dogs, cats and birds, he asks for one large enough to ride.

In the end, Jane drives him there.

6-2Breaking in in the rain

By night, S.H.I.E.L.D. has built a large temporary facility around the hammer. In pouring rain, Thor leaves Jane in the car, climbs the fence and fights his way past guard after guard to the center.

Coulson has an agent with a bow, Clint Barton, waiting up high, and watches.

6-3The hammer won't move

Thor finally reaches the hammer and confidently grabs the handle. But however hard he pulls, it doesn't move at all.

He roars at the sky in the rain and sinks to his knees. With no fight left in him, he is captured by S.H.I.E.L.D.

6-4Loki's lie

In the interrogation room, Coulson asks Thor where he got his training, but Thor won't answer. When Coulson leaves, Loki appears.

Loki tells him their father is dead, that his exile is the condition of the truce with the giants, and that their mother forbids his return. It's all lies. Thor blames himself, apologizes to Loki, and they part.

6-5Donald Blake

Selvig comes to the facility with a fake ID and claims Thor is a colleague, a doctor named Donald Blake. Coulson pretends to believe it, lets Thor go, and has them followed.

On the way back, Selvig has a drinking contest with Thor at a bar. Thor carries the drunk Selvig home. Selvig asks him to stay away from Jane.

Part 7: Worthy

7-1Loki's deal

Loki secretly travels to Jotunheim to meet Laufey. It was Loki who let the giants into the vault on coronation day.

Now he offers to let Laufey into Asgard to kill the sleeping Odin, and to give the Casket back in return. Laufey accepts.

7-2A night by the fire

On the lab's roof, Thor and Jane sit by a fire. He tells her about the Nine Realms connected by a great tree and sketches it in her notebook.

Jane's research and the worlds he describes begin to fit together. They grow close, and when Jane falls asleep, Thor covers her with a blanket.

7-3Heimdall's choice

Sif and the Warriors Three go to Heimdall to bring Thor home. It means defying the king, but Heimdall distrusts Loki too, and lets them go to Earth.

When Loki finds out, he sends the Destroyer to Earth. And when Heimdall turns his blade on him, Loki freezes him with the power of the Casket.

7-4Friends in a small town

In the morning, Sif and the Warriors Three appear in armor on the main street of Puente Antiguo. They tell Thor that his father isn't dead, only asleep. Thor realizes Loki lied to him.

Then the Destroyer descends from the sky. It burns the S.H.I.E.L.D. vehicles that approach and starts wrecking the town.

7-5Protecting the town

Powerless, Thor helps Jane's team get the townspeople out. Sif and the Warriors Three fight the Destroyer, but its fire blasts and strength knock them down one by one.

Thor tells his friends to fall back and leave it to him.

7-6Giving his life

Thor walks alone toward the Destroyer. He tells Loki, who is controlling it, that if he has wronged his brother he is sorry, but these people are innocent, and he should take Thor's life and end this.

The Destroyer seems to pause, then knocks Thor flying with one blow. He falls and lies still, near death, as Jane rushes to him.

7-7The hammer returns

At that moment, the hammer still stuck at the S.H.I.E.L.D. site begins to move. It flies across the sky into Thor's hand, and with a strike of lightning he is revived with his armor and power restored. By giving his life for others, he has proven himself worthy.

Thor whips up a whirlwind that lifts the Destroyer into the air, then dives down and smashes it with the hammer.

7-8A promise to Jane

Thor tells Coulson they fight for the same cause, protecting this world, and asks him to return Jane's research.

He promises Jane he will come back for her, and they kiss. Frozen Heimdall hears Thor's call, breaks free of the ice with all his strength, and opens the bridge. Thor and his friends return to Asgard.

Part 8: Battle on the Rainbow Bridge

8-1Odin's bedchamber

In Asgard, with Loki's help, Laufey and his giants get into Odin's chamber. Frigga fights with a sword but is struck down by Laufey.

Just as Laufey is about to kill the sleeping Odin, Loki blasts him with Gungnir and kills him. Frigga embraces Loki as the son who saved his father.

8-2Loki's real plan

Thor arrives and tells their mother that Loki sent the Destroyer to kill him. Loki's plan was to kill the Frost Giant king who had tried to murder Odin, then wipe out the giants' realm and win his father's approval.

Loki has aimed the Bifrost's power at Jotunheim and locked it in place with Gungnir. The beam begins to destroy the whole realm.

8-3Brother against brother

Thor and Loki fight on the rainbow bridge. Loki cries that he never wanted the throne; he only wanted to be his brother's equal.

Thor sees that the only way to stop the Bifrost is to destroy the bridge itself, which would mean never reaching Jane on Earth again. Asking Jane to forgive him, he strikes the bridge again and again with his hammer.

8-4Loki falls

The bridge explodes and shatters, and Thor and Loki are about to fall into the void beyond. Thor grips one end of Gungnir with Loki hanging from the other, and the awakened Odin catches Thor by the ankle.

Loki pleads that he could have done it, for his father and for all of them. Odin answers, "No, Loki." Loki lets go and falls into the darkness of space as Thor screams.

Part 9: Afterward

9-1Not ready to be king

Peace returns to Asgard. Still grieving for Loki, Thor faces his father.

He tells Odin he is not ready to be king; he has a lot to learn.

9-2At the end of the bridge

Thor visits Heimdall at the broken end of the bridge and asks about Jane. Heimdall tells him she is searching for him.

On Earth, Jane, with her research back, works with Selvig and Darcy to find a way to open a path to Asgard.

9-3After the credits

At a S.H.I.E.L.D. facility, Nick Fury opens a briefcase and shows Selvig a glowing blue cube. Fury says it may hold untold power and asks him to study it.

A mirror shows Loki standing beside Selvig. Loki is alive and, unseen, nudges Selvig into agreeing.

Summary (spoilers through the ending)

How Thor changed

Proud of his strength, Thor defied his father, started a fight in Jotunheim and broke the truce with the giants (Parts 2 and 3). Odin judged him unworthy, took his power and banished him to Earth (Part 3).

Unable to lift the hammer, and believing Loki's lie that their father was dead, Thor accepted his punishment (Part 6). By offering his life to the Destroyer Loki controlled, to protect the townspeople and his friends, he was found worthy and regained his power (Part 7).

In the end, to save the giants' realm, he gave up his way back to Jane by destroying the bridge, and told his father he was not ready to be king (Parts 8 and 9).

What Loki did

On coronation day, Loki let the Frost Giants into the vault and egged Thor into going to Jotunheim (Parts 2 and 7).

He learned he was Laufey's son, taken in by Odin (Part 5), and took the throne when Odin fell asleep. He lied to Thor (Part 6), offered Laufey a deal to kill Odin, sent the Destroyer to kill Thor, and froze Heimdall (Part 7).

Finally he killed Laufey himself and tried to destroy the giants' realm with the Bifrost to win his father's approval. Thor stopped him, Odin rejected him, and he let himself fall into space (Part 8). The post-credits scene shows he is alive (Part 9).

Who died (in order)

- The Frost Giants in the vault (Part 2): killed by the Destroyer's fire while trying to take back the Casket.

- Giants in Jotunheim (Part 3): killed by Thor and his friends in the fight Thor started after being taunted.

- Thor (Part 7): not dead. He offered himself to protect the townspeople and was left near death by the Destroyer Loki controlled, but was revived when the hammer returned.

- Laufey (Part 8): came to kill Odin with Loki's help, then was shot dead by Loki with Gungnir, a betrayal meant to make Loki look like the hero who saved his father.

- Loki (Part 8): not dead. He let himself fall from the broken bridge into space, but the post-credits scene shows he survived.

My takeOpinion

An arrogant prince learns kindness only after losing his power. I think showing Loki as a brother who wanted to be acknowledged, not just a villain, is what made him so loved later.
