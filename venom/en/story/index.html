Part 1: Life from space

1-1The crash in Malaysia

The Life Foundation, a powerful company, has been sending probes into space to look for worlds people could live on. One finds a comet covered in black, slimy life forms, symbiotes, and brings four of them back.

On the way home one gets loose, and the ship crashes in the forests of Malaysia. Rescue workers rush to the burning wreck.

1-2The one that got away

The escaped symbiote slips into the body of a female paramedic. This is Riot, the leader of its kind.

The Life Foundation recovers the other three and takes them to its lab in San Francisco.

1-3Experiments at the lab

CEO Carlton Drake believes Earth won't last much longer and wants humans to be able to survive in space.

The lab learns that symbiotes can't live without an oxygen-breathing host, but most hosts reject them and die. Drake keeps experimenting on people anyway.

1-4Eddie and Anne

San Francisco reporter Eddie Brock has a TV news show and is popular for standing up for ordinary people. His fiancée, Anne Weying, is a lawyer who is currently defending the Life Foundation in a lawsuit.

Eddie's boss sends him to interview Drake. It's supposed to be a friendly piece about the company's space program.

1-5Confronting Drake

The night before, Eddie secretly reads a confidential file on Anne's laptop describing deadly human trials at the Life Foundation.

At the interview, Eddie drops the planned questions and accuses Drake of killing people in experiments. Drake ends the interview at once.

1-6Losing everything

Eddie is fired from his show, and Anne, blamed for the leak, loses her job at her firm too.

Furious that Eddie used her work behind her back, Anne calls off the engagement and leaves him.

Part 2: The lab's secret

2-1Eddie, six months later

Six months later, Eddie can't find work and is stuck in a small apartment.

At the corner store, owner Mrs. Chen is being shaken down by a thug, and Eddie looks the other way. He is friendly with Maria, a homeless woman he chats with on the street.

2-2Dora's request

At the lab, the trials are getting closer to success, but one symbiote dies through carelessness.

Life Foundation scientist Dora Skirth can no longer stand Drake using homeless people as test subjects. She comes to Eddie, asks him to expose the experiments, and offers to get him inside.

2-3Breaking in

At night, while Dora distracts a guard, Eddie slips into the lab and photographs the people locked up there.

In one cell he finds Maria, who had gone missing. She begs for help, and he breaks the glass to free her.

2-4Maria dies

But Maria is hosting a symbiote. Out of her mind, she attacks Eddie, and in that instant the symbiote jumps into him. Without it, Maria dies on the spot.

Alarms go off and Eddie gets away from the guards. He doesn't yet realize something is inside him.

2-5Something's wrong

At home, Eddie is ravenously hungry. He empties the fridge and even eats scraps from the trash. He starts hearing a strange voice in his head.

He stumbles into a restaurant where Anne is dining with her new boyfriend, Dr. Dan Lewis. He climbs into the lobster tank and bites into a live lobster.

2-6Dora's end

At the lab, security chief Roland Treece discovers that Dora let Eddie in and tells Drake.

Drake locks the traitor in with the last captive symbiote. Dora dies, and so does that symbiote, leaving the one inside Eddie as the only known survivor.

2-7The raid

Guessing that Maria's symbiote is now in Eddie, Drake sends Treece and his men to Eddie's apartment.

When they point their guns at him, black tendrils burst out of Eddie's body on their own and take the men down one by one.

Part 3: Venom

3-1The chase

Eddie flees on a motorcycle, chased by Treece's men in cars and by drones overhead.

He tears through San Francisco's steep streets with the symbiote's help, its tendrils fending off his pursuers.

3-2The monster

Cornered, Eddie is covered completely by the symbiote and becomes a huge black monster with big white eyes and razor teeth. It takes down the pursuers and bites one man's head off.

When Eddie comes back to himself, he is horrified.

3-3It calls itself Venom

Once things calm down, the symbiote speaks to Eddie and introduces itself as Venom.

Venom explains that the comet is looking for worlds where symbiotes can take over and devour the inhabitants, and offers to spare Eddie if he helps. Scared as he is, Eddie starts to enjoy his new strength.

3-4Back to the old office

Eddie wants to get his lab photos to his old boss and expose Drake. Using Venom's powers, he scales a tall building and leaves a phone with the photos in his boss's office.

From the top of the building, Venom marvels at the San Francisco skyline.

3-5Fighting the SWAT team

Waiting below is a police SWAT team, called in about the break-in. Eddie turns into Venom again and fights his way out.

Anne, who has come to pick him up, sees it all.

3-6An apology in the car

Anne drives Eddie to Dan's hospital. On the way, prompted by Venom, Eddie apologizes for what he did to her.

Part 4: Riot

4-1Eating him alive

Tests at the hospital show the symbiote is slowly damaging Eddie's organs. Eddie mentions its two weaknesses: high-pitched sound and fire.

Venom insists the damage can be fixed, but Anne switches on an MRI machine. Unable to stand the noise, Venom is torn out of Eddie's body.

4-2Eddie is captured

Separated from Venom, Eddie is grabbed by Drake's men after leaving the hospital.

4-3Riot's journey

Meanwhile, Riot, which escaped in Malaysia, moves from the paramedic to an old woman, then into a little girl, and travels to America.

Still in the girl's body, Riot gets into the Life Foundation in San Francisco and appears before Drake.

4-4Drake and Riot

Riot bonds with Drake. Drake welcomes it, promising to fly Riot to the comet aboard a Life Foundation ship and bring the rest of the symbiotes back to Earth.

He gets the launch ready.

4-5In the woods

Treece and his men take the captured Eddie into the woods.

There, Treece gets ready to kill him.

4-6Anne and Venom

Then Anne appears, bonded with Venom. To save Eddie, she reluctantly agreed to let it into her body.

Covered in the symbiote, Anne takes down the men and bites Treece's head off, killing the man who was about to kill Eddie. Anne is shaken by what she has done.

4-7Back together with a kiss

Anne kisses Eddie, and Venom passes from her mouth back into him. Eddie and Venom are one again.

4-8Venom changes sides

Venom admits that on its home world it's a loser, just like Eddie. It has come to like Earth, and Eddie, and wants to stay.

The two head for the Life Foundation launch site to stop Drake and Riot from bringing the other symbiotes to Earth.

Part 5: The launch pad

5-1Storming the launch site

At the launch site, the spacecraft is waiting to lift off. Eddie and Venom go after Drake before he can board.

Riot emerges from Drake's body, a monster even bigger than Venom.

5-2Battle with Riot

On the launch pad, Venom and Riot clash. Riot is stronger, and at one point swallows Venom and Eddie into its own body.

Anne blasts a high-pitched sound through the launch site's speakers, and the two symbiotes are pulled apart in pain.

5-3The explosion

The ship lifts off with Drake and Riot aboard. Venom leaps onto it as it rises and wrecks it.

The ship explodes in midair, and Drake and Riot die in the blast. Venom wraps Eddie up to shield him from the blast and seems to burn away.

5-4Venom lives

Later, Eddie meets Anne. She is sorry Venom is gone, and Eddie agrees.

But Venom is alive inside him. Eddie and Venom agree that it can't eat just anyone, only bad guys.

5-5At Mrs. Chen's store

Eddie goes back to reporting. At Mrs. Chen's store, the same thug is shaking her down again.

This time Eddie doesn't look away. He becomes Venom, terrifies the thug, and eats him. Eddie and Venom will protect the city by devouring its criminals.

5-6Mid-credits

Back on the job, Eddie is called to San Quentin prison. Serial killer Cletus Kasady will talk only to him.

Kasady promises: "When I get out of here, and I will, there's gonna be carnage."

5-7After the credits

Finally, a scene from the animated film Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse plays.

Summary

What Eddie did

He lost his job and his fiancée trying to expose Drake's human trials (Part 1). He bonded with Venom while breaking into the lab (Part 2), and despite his fear, ended up teaming with Venom to stop Drake and Riot (Part 5).

What Venom did

At first it meant to help its kind come to Earth, offering to spare Eddie if he helped (Part 3). Living with Eddie and Anne, it grew to like Earth and turned against the invasion (Part 4). It shielded Eddie from the blast and agreed to eat only bad guys (Part 5).

What Anne did

She used an MRI to separate Venom from Eddie (Part 4), then let Venom into her own body to save Eddie, killed Treece and passed Venom back with a kiss (Part 4). At the launch pad she blasted a high-pitched sound to help against Riot (Part 5).

Who died (in story order)

- Maria (Part 2): A Life Foundation test subject hosting a symbiote. When Eddie tried to free her, it jumped to him and she died.

- Dora Skirth (Part 2): Caught letting Eddie into the lab, she was locked in with the last captive symbiote by Drake and died.

- One of Treece's men (Part 3): Chasing Eddie, he had his head bitten off by Venom.

- Roland Treece (Part 4): About to kill Eddie in the woods, he had his head bitten off by Anne while she was bonded to Venom.

- Carlton Drake and Riot (Part 5): They launched to bring more symbiotes to Earth, but Venom wrecked the ship and they died in the explosion.

- The thug (Part 5): Eaten by Venom while shaking down Mrs. Chen's store.

My takeOpinion

You expect something scary and get a buddy comedy built on Eddie and Venom's banter. Whether you enjoy their arguing decides what you think of it.
