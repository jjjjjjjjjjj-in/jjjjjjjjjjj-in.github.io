Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

Starts outside the MCU

It's a Sony Marvel film set in its own world at first. The series links up with the MCU later on.

No Spider-Man

Venom is the star; Spider-Man doesn't appear.

Lots of comedy

Eddie and Venom bicker like a comedy duo.

Watch the mid-credits

There's a scene setting up the sequel.

Key terms

Symbiotes
Alien life forms that live inside a host, giving them great strength.
The Life Foundation
A big company working on space exploration, secretly running dangerous experiments.
Venom
The name of the symbiote that bonds with Eddie.
Riot
A powerful symbiote, the leader of its kind.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why does Venom help Eddie?

At first it just needs a host, but over time it comes to like Eddie, and Earth.

What was Drake planning?

The Foundation's CEO, Drake, wanted to merge humans with symbiotes so people could survive in space, and experimented on many people to do it.

Who's the man in the mid-credits scene?

Cletus Kasady, a serial killer in prison. He's the villain of the next film.

Easy-to-miss details

Mrs. Chen's shop

Mrs. Chen, who runs the corner store near Eddie's place, appears throughout the series.

Snacks

Venom craves chocolate and 'bad guys.'
