Part 1: Back home

1-1Knull on Klyntar

On Klyntar, the symbiotes' home world, Knull, their creator, lies imprisoned after the symbiotes rebelled against him long ago.

To get the Codex, the key that would free him, he sends his monstrous Xenophages out across the universe.

1-2Two bars

In another universe (the MCU), Eddie and Venom are in a bar in Mexico, hearing the bartender talk about things like a "purple alien." Suddenly a light swallows them and they are sent back to their own universe.

They keep drinking in a similar bar in their own world, never noticing that a small bit of Venom has been left behind there.

1-3Wanted

The TV news tells them they are wanted. After their battle with Carnage in the last film, Detective Mulligan is believed murdered, and Eddie is the prime suspect.

They can't go back to San Francisco. Eddie decides to see an old acquaintance in New York to clear his name, and they set off for New York.

1-4Area 51 shutting down

Area 51 is being decommissioned. Under soldier Rex Strickland, a huge machine that sprays powerful acid wipes out the remaining evidence.

But underground, a government program called Imperium carries on, capturing and studying symbiotes that have fallen to Earth. Many are held there.

1-5Mulligan is alive

Mulligan, believed killed in the fight with Carnage, survived. Left for dead by the symbiote inside him, he was captured by Imperium and brought here.

Imperium scientists Dr. Teddy Paine and her colleague Sadie Christmas bond him with a green symbiote to learn why the symbiotes came to Earth.

1-6Dr. Paine's nightmare

Dr. Paine dreams of her childhood: running through a storm holding her twin brother's hand, she was struck by lightning, and the current passed into her brother and killed him. Her left arm was left paralyzed.

Part 2: Hunted

2-1On the side of a plane

The pair ride to New York clinging to the outside of a plane. A Xenophage appears and attacks, and they dodge it and drop into the Nevada desert.

2-2The secret of the Codex

Venom explains: Knull sent the Xenophages to find a Codex, which forms when a symbiote brings its host back from death.

Venom once revived Eddie in the first film, so they carry a Codex. A Xenophage can only sense it when Venom fully shows himself, so they decide to travel as Eddie as much as possible.

2-3Venom the horse

Strickland's team arrives in the desert. Venom bonds with a horse and gallops across the wilderness with Eddie on its back, to Queen's "Don't Stop Me Now."

The horse leaps off a cliff and the pair fall into a river.

2-4Two hunters

The soldiers keep coming. When Venom fully emerges to fight them, the Xenophage senses it and appears too.

Fighting off both, the pair barely escape.

2-5The Moon family

Out of the river, Eddie meets Martin Moon's family, alien-loving hippies on a road trip to see Area 51 before it closes.

Martin has dreamed of meeting an alien his whole life. The family offers Eddie a ride as far as Las Vegas.

2-6A sing-along

On the road to Las Vegas, Eddie and Venom sing David Bowie's "Space Oddity" with the family.

2-7What Mulligan's symbiote says

At Area 51, the symbiote bonded to Mulligan tells Strickland what Knull wants. The Codex would set Knull free, and it can only be destroyed if either Eddie or Venom dies.

Strickland moves to capture Venom and kill Eddie.

2-8The bartender taken

Tracking Eddie to the bar in Mexico, Strickland takes the bartender into custody.

Part 3: Las Vegas

3-1Mrs. Chen again

In Las Vegas, the pair unexpectedly run into Mrs. Chen, the shopkeeper from San Francisco. She has won big at the casino and invites them up to her suite.

3-2Dancing with Mrs. Chen

In the suite, Venom comes out in full form and happily dances with Mrs. Chen.

3-3Through the ceiling

Because Venom showed himself, the Xenophage smashes through the ceiling. Venom withdraws just in time, but the creature searches the building.

Strickland's team arrives too. Eddie is captured, Venom is separated from him, and both are taken to Area 51.

Part 4: Area 51

4-1The program shut down

Having seen how dangerous the Xenophage is, Imperium's head gives Strickland full authority over the program, and he orders Dr. Paine to shut it down.

4-2Reunion with Mulligan

Separated from Venom, Eddie meets Mulligan in the base. Mulligan's symbiote warns him that the Xenophages must never get the Codex.

4-3Together again

Sadie, who sympathizes with the symbiotes, frees Venom. When Strickland shoots Eddie, Venom rebonds with him.

4-4The Xenophage gets in

Venom's appearance draws the Xenophage into the base. In the fight, Mulligan dies at its hands while helping Venom get away.

4-5The symbiotes fight back

The captive symbiotes are released and bond with Sadie, the researchers and soldiers to fight the Xenophage. Sadie bonds with a dark green symbiote (Lasher).

The Xenophage signals to Knull that the Codex has been found.

4-6Saving the Moons

The Moon family, who had come to see Area 51 before it closed, are caught up in the chaos. With Dr. Paine's help, Eddie and Venom get them out.

4-7More Xenophages

Knull sends many more Xenophages to Earth through portals, and the symbiotes fall one after another.

Scorn, the symbiote bonded to lab technician Jade Clark, is killed by a Xenophage.

Part 5: The last dance

5-1The acid plan

Strickland tells Eddie and Venom his plan: wipe out the Xenophages with the giant acid machine used to destroy evidence.

5-2Venom's decision

Venom realizes Knull will keep coming as long as they're together, and resolves to sacrifice himself to save Eddie.

Venom says sadly that he wanted to see the Statue of Liberty. Eddie encourages him that they're going to see her.

5-3The motorcycle run

To lure the Xenophages to the machine, Eddie and Venom race through the base on a motorcycle, risking their lives.

5-4Goodbye

At the machine, Venom leaves Eddie's body, bonds with one Xenophage after another and drags them into the acid tanks.

He covers Eddie with a steel door to protect him and says goodbye. His last words are "Bye for now."

5-5The end of Venom and Strickland

Venom pulls the badly wounded Strickland back with a tendril to start the machine. Strickland, who saw the symbiotes as enemies, has a change of heart and sets off his grenades, destroying the Xenophages and himself.

Venom dissolves in the acid along with the Xenophages, and the Codex is gone.

5-6Paine and Agony

In the explosion, Dr. Paine bonds with a purple symbiote (Agony) to save Sadie.

Part 6: Afterward

6-1In the hospital

Eddie wakes in a hospital. A military official tells him that, for his brave actions at Area 51, his record will be wiped clean, on the condition that he keeps everything secret.

6-2The Statue of Liberty

Eddie goes to New York alone. Walking the city and remembering his days with Venom, he gazes at the Statue of Liberty, which Venom wanted to see. Maroon 5's "Memories" plays.

6-3Mid-credits: Knull

On Klyntar, Knull declares that with Venom gone, the universe is no longer safe from him.

6-4After the credits: what remains

The Mexican bartender crawls out of the burned ruins of Area 51 and runs off. Nearby, a black cockroach crawls out beside a broken glass vial.

Summary

Key threads (who did what, and what happened)

- Knull sends the Xenophages to get the Codex (Part 1).

- Venom revived Eddie in the first film, so the Codex is inside them; only one of them dying will destroy it (Part 2).

- Strickland hunts Venom and means to kill Eddie, but in the end changes his mind and gives his life to destroy the Xenophages (Parts 2–5).

- Venom sacrifices himself to protect Eddie, and the Codex is destroyed. Eddie's record is cleared and he visits the Statue of Liberty alone (Parts 5–6).

Who died (in order)

- Mulligan (Part 4): killed by the Xenophage that breaks into the base, while helping Venom get away.

- Jade Clark (Part 4): bonded with Scorn to fight, but killed by a Xenophage.

- Venom (Part 5): to stop Knull's release and protect Eddie, he bonds with the Xenophages, drags them into the acid tanks and dissolves.

- Strickland (Part 5): mortally wounded, he sets off his grenades to destroy the Xenophages, killing himself.
