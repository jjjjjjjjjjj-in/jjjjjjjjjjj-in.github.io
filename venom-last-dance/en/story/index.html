Part 1: Deep in space

The imprisoned creator

On a distant world, Knull, creator of the symbiotes, lies imprisoned. He sends the monstrous Xenophages to find the Codex, the key to his freedom.

Part 2: On the run

A bar in Mexico

Back from the MCU, Eddie and Venom are in a Mexican bar, wanted by the police after the events of the last film.

Heading for New York

They decide to go to New York to clear their names.

The first monster

On the way, a Xenophage attacks. They learn the monster can track them whenever Venom shows himself.

The military

Soldiers led by Rex Strickland are also hunting the symbiote.

Part 3: Across America

A family road trip

Eddie hitches a ride with Martin Moon's family, who dream of meeting aliens and are traveling near Area 51.

Las Vegas

Venom detours to Las Vegas, runs into Mrs. Chen and dances with her.

The scientist

At Area 51, scientist Teddy Paine studies captured symbiotes.

Part 4: Area 51

To the base

Eddie is captured and taken to Area 51.

The monsters attack

Xenophages swarm the base. Captive symbiotes are released and bond with soldiers and scientists to fight back.

The last dance

The Codex can only be destroyed if Eddie or Venom dies. Venom protects Eddie and sacrifices himself, taking the monsters with him.

Part 5: Afterward

The Statue of Liberty

Alone now, Eddie visits the Statue of Liberty, which Venom always wanted to see.

Extra scenes

There are short scenes during and after the credits.
