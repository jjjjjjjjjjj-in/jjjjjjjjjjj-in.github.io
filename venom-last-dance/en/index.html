StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

Part 1: Back home

Back in their own world, Eddie and Venom learn they're wanted for the supposed murder of Detective Mulligan, and head for New York.

Knull, the imprisoned creator of the symbiotes, sends Xenophages to find the Codex that would free him. Beneath the closing Area 51, a surviving Mulligan is bonded with a symbiote.

Part 2: Hunted

The Codex is inside them, and the Xenophages can find it whenever Venom fully emerges.

Escaping both Strickland's soldiers and the monster, they ride to Las Vegas with the alien-loving Moon family. The Codex can only be destroyed if one of them dies.

Part 3: Las Vegas

In Las Vegas they reunite with Mrs. Chen, and Venom dances with her.

The Xenophage and the soldiers strike, and the pair are separated and taken to Area 51.

Part 4: Area 51

Venom rebonds with Eddie, and freed symbiotes bond with researchers and soldiers to fight the Xenophage in the base.

Knull sends many more Xenophages, and the symbiotes fall one by one.

Part 5: The last dance

To protect Eddie, Venom drags the Xenophages into the acid tanks and sacrifices himself.

Strickland blows up the monsters with his grenades, and the Codex is destroyed.

Part 6: Afterward

His record cleared, Eddie goes to New York alone and gazes at the Statue of Liberty, which Venom wanted to see.

Key terms

Knull
The creator of the symbiotes, imprisoned deep in space.
The Codex
The key to freeing Knull, which formed inside Eddie and Venom.
Xenophages
Monsters Knull sends to hunt for the Codex.
Area 51
A secret US military base that studies alien life.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why do they have the Codex?

A Codex forms when a symbiote brings its host back from death, which Venom once did for Eddie.

How can the Codex be destroyed?

If either Eddie or Venom dies, it disappears.

What happened to Mulligan?

The last film hinted something had happened to him. This one shows what.

Easy-to-miss details

Dancing with Mrs. Chen

In Las Vegas, Venom and Mrs. Chen dance together.

Venom the horse

Venom bonds with a horse and other animals to get around.

The Statue of Liberty

At the end, Eddie visits the Statue of Liberty, which Venom always wanted to see.

My takeOpinion

As the end of a trilogy, I think focusing on Eddie and Venom's goodbye was the right call. The plot is rough, but the final scene is properly sad.
