Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

It follows Hawkeye

Maya Lopez was introduced in Hawkeye.

A deaf hero

Maya is deaf and signs. Some scenes drop out all sound.

More mature

It's more violent than most MCU series.

Daredevil appears

Daredevil makes a brief appearance in Episode 1.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Didn't Kingpin die?

Maya shot him at the end of Hawkeye, but he survived.

What is the ancestors' power?

Passed down through the women of Maya's family, it lets her reach into and share someone's pain. In the end she uses it on Kingpin.

What happens after the credits?

Back in New York, Kingpin watches the news and makes a big decision.

Easy-to-miss details

Silent scenes

Some scenes drop the sound to show how Maya experiences the world.

The roller rink

Her uncle Skully's roller rink becomes an important place.

How each episode opens

Each episode begins with the story of one of Maya's ancestors.
