Episode 1: Chafa

1-1Chafa, the first

It opens with the Choctaw origin story. Long ago the people emerge from under the earth and take human form, led by a woman named Chafa, the first Choctaw.

Then, in the small town of Tamaha, Oklahoma, young Maya and her cousin Bonnie act out Chafa's story with shadow puppets inside a makeshift tent.

1-22007: The crash

In 2007, Maya is riding with her mother, Taloa, when their car fails to stop at an intersection and is hit from the side.

Taloa is killed and Maya loses a leg. Her father, William, later says the brakes had been cut.

Maya's grandmother Chula blames William for her daughter's death.

1-3New York and 'Uncle' Fisk

William takes Maya to New York to work for crime lord Wilson Fisk, the Kingpin, and becomes a commander of the Tracksuit Mafia.

Fisk becomes something like an adoptive uncle to Maya.

1-4Her father's death (from Hawkeye)

Years later, the masked swordsman Ronin attacks the Tracksuit Mafia at an auto shop, and William dies in front of Maya (as seen in Hawkeye).

Fisk promises to find William's killer, tells her to channel her rage more constructively, and puts her to work for him.

1-5Fighting Daredevil

Maya's first real job is to help two of Fisk's men take out rivals trying to move into his territory.

Daredevil, the blind lawyer who fights crime at night, drops in, and the two have a long, brutal fight through the building before he gets away.

Fisk is impressed that she held her own.

1-6December 2024: Shooting Fisk

In December 2024, Maya meets Clint Barton, the real Ronin, and learns that Fisk set up her father's death by tipping off Ronin.

To avenge William, she shoots Fisk in the eye and leaves New York (as seen in Hawkeye's finale).

1-7Five months later: Tamaha

Five months later, Maya returns to Tamaha. She runs into her cousin Biscuits and his dog and asks him to keep her return a secret.

She goes to her uncle Henry to get patched up. Vickie, who works for Henry, recognizes her.

Henry suggests she talk to Bonnie, but Maya has other plans.

1-8Asking Henry

Maya asks Henry to help her tear down Fisk's operation so she can take over his empire.

Not wanting to put the family in danger, Henry refuses.

1-9Fisk survived

Meanwhile, in a hospital bed, his face wrapped in bandages, Fisk is alive. He survived Maya's shot.

Episode 2: Lowak

2-11200 AD: Lowak's game

In 1200 AD, in what is now Alabama, a Choctaw woman named Lowak plays in a stickball game. If her side loses, they will be exiled.

As her team nears victory, the other side sends in a formidable warrior who evens the score.

In the middle of the melee, Lowak has a vision. Her hands glow with a spiral pattern, and she breaks free with the ball to win the game.

2-2Recruiting Biscuits

Maya enlists Biscuits for her first strike on Fisk's empire and sends him to Skully's pawn shop, part antique store, part repair shop, for supplies.

Skully is like a grandfather to Maya.

2-3Robbing the train

Maya boards a cargo train guarded by Fisk's men. Biscuits assumes she's there to steal something.

She finds a munitions container and plants a homemade bomb in one of its crates. Her real goal is to strike back at Fisk.

2-4Caught in the coupling

As she tries to get off, her prosthetic leg gets stuck in a coupling between cars.

Maya sees a vision of her ancestors. Her hands begin to glow, and she pushes the coupling apart and frees her leg.

2-5The armory explodes

The shipment arrives at one of Fisk's armories in New York. When Zane, one of his lieutenants, has his men unload the crate, Maya's bomb goes off and destroys the building.

2-6Skully's shop and the Chafa statue

Skully gives Maya a new prosthetic leg to replace the one damaged on the train.

In the shop, Maya notices a statue of Chafa. Skully tells her one of the women in her vision was Chafa, the first Choctaw, and that the ancestors watch over the family in times of need.

2-7Henry's warning

Henry hears about the armory explosion and warns Maya that if she keeps this up, she'll end up hurting the people close to her. She brushes him off.

Meanwhile, Chula learns that Maya is in town, and Bonnie hears it from Biscuits. Maya refuses to see either of them.

Episode 3: Tuklo

3-1Late 1800s: Tuklo and her father

Shot like a black-and-white silent film. In the late 1800s, Tuklo practices shooting with her father, a Choctaw Lighthorseman.

She wants to join the Lighthorse, but he forbids it, telling her "Women are life givers, men are life takers."

3-2Tuklo saves her father

Her father rides out to confront some local criminals, while Tuklo braids her hair like a Choctaw warrior.

She has a vision of Chafa and Lowak, and her hands begin to glow. The criminals ambush her father's group, but Tuklo hears the attack and arrives in time to save them.

3-3Chula and Skully

Chula visits Skully, who encourages her to reach out to Maya.

3-4Vickie's betrayal

Maya is suddenly hit by a vision of Chafa, Lowak and Tuklo, and in that moment she is knocked out and captured by two masked people.

She wakes up in Henry's roller rink. Henry's employee Vickie has sided with Fisk's organization and tipped them off to where she was. Henry is a hostage too.

Bonnie shows up to see Henry and is captured, then locked in the storeroom with Maya.

3-5Zane arrives

Zane arrives to collect Maya, then betrays and kills Vickie.

In the storeroom, Bonnie confronts Maya about leaving without a word all those years ago.

3-6The fight at the rink

Maya builds an automatic weapon out of junk in the storeroom, breaks out and fights Zane's men, using the arcade cabinets too.

But Zane threatens Bonnie and Henry and holds Maya at gunpoint. Just as he's about to shoot her and Henry, he gets a phone call, and he and his men leave.

3-7Fisk is alive

Maya sends Bonnie away, promising they'll make up later.

That evening, Henry tells Maya that Fisk is still alive, and promises to help her.

3-8A new leg, and a man waiting at home

Skully gives Maya a new prosthetic decorated with Choctaw designs and nudges her to reconnect with her family.

On her way out of town, Maya stops at her old house. Fisk is waiting for her.

Episode 4: Taloa

4-12008: The ice cream vendor

In 2008, young Maya tries to buy ice cream, but the vendor, not realizing she's deaf, can't understand her and mocks her.

Fisk, watching from the car, tells her to stay inside, walks up behind the man and beats him savagely. Maya watches, then kicks the man herself, showing no remorse.

4-2A final lesson, and the interpreter

Years later, as Maya is about to start working for Fisk, they share a dinner at home, and he gives her a final lesson: they can only trust each other.

When dinner is over, the ASL interpreter who has been translating for them is dismissed, and soon killed.

4-3Fisk's offer

In the present, Fisk and his men surround Maya. He gives her an augmented reality contact lens so they can talk without an interpreter.

He offers to hand her his criminal empire if she comes back to New York with him, and gives her one day to decide.

4-4A shared vision

Maya tells Henry about the offer, but is suddenly hit by a vision of her ancestors. At the same time, at the Choctaw powwow grounds, Chula has the same vision.

4-5Chula's story

Henry takes Maya to Chula, who tells her the ancestors help them when they need it most.

She recounts the night she gave birth to Maya's mother, Taloa, in the forest surrounded by family instead of in a hospital, and the vision she had then. Taloa was a healer, she says, and Maya can do the same.

But Maya, who felt abandoned by Chula as a child, storms out. Afterward, Chula starts working on a special garment.

4-6The night at the hotel

That night, Maya goes to Fisk's suite at the casino hotel intending to kill him, and holds him at gunpoint.

Fisk tells her that as a boy he killed his father after seeing him beat his mother (shown in the Daredevil series), and says that if this is what she needs, she should go ahead.

She doesn't shoot. He repeats his invitation to come to New York.

4-7Maya doesn't show

The next morning, Fisk waits on his plane, but Maya never comes. Finding out she has left Tamaha without him, he is enraged.

Episode 5: Maya

5-1Young Maya and the woodpecker

It opens with a memory from Maya's childhood: she hits a woodpecker with a slingshot.

Taloa scolds her for hurting an innocent life, then heals the bird with glowing hands. Together they release it back into the wild.

5-2Family missing

Biscuits messages Maya that Chula and Bonnie are missing, and she heads back to Tamaha.

5-3Her mother's spirit, and Chula's garment

Maya hurries to Chula's house, where a vision of her mother, Taloa, appears.

Taloa tells her she is the embodiment of their people's legacy, and that it will echo through what she does. She is not alone; the ancestors are with her.

When the vision fades, Chula's finished garment is waiting for her.

5-4At the powwow

Fisk's men have surrounded the Choctaw powwow festival. Maya finds Fisk, who has taken Chula and Bonnie.

Furious at her betrayal, he threatens to kill her entire family.

5-5Biscuits and Henry fight back

Biscuits takes out Fisk's men with a monster truck, and Henry kills Zane.

5-6Three women

Maya shares the power of her ancestors with Chula and Bonnie, and the three of them overpower Fisk's men.

5-7Into Fisk's mind

Using her power, Maya takes Fisk back to the memory of his father beating his mother, trying to heal that trauma and help him let go of his anger.

Back in reality, an outraged Fisk demands to know what she did to him, and leaves the festival before the police arrive.

5-8Goodbye to family

The next day, Maya shares a meal with her family and says goodbye before leaving Tamaha.

5-9Mid-credits: the mayoral race

On his plane, Fisk watches a news report about the New York mayoral race having no clear front-runner, and takes a keen interest.

Summary

Maya and Fisk (in order)

1) Her father (Ep. 1): Fisk got William killed by tipping off Ronin. When Maya learns this, she shoots him (Hawkeye's finale).

2) Striking back (Ep. 2): Maya plants a bomb on a train and destroys his New York armory.

3) The offer (Ep. 4): Fisk comes to Tamaha and offers her his empire if she returns. She holds him at gunpoint in his hotel but doesn't shoot, then leaves town without him.

4) The powwow (Ep. 5): Fisk threatens her family. Instead of killing him, Maya uses the ancestors' power to reach his childhood memory and try to heal it. He leaves and sets his sights on the mayor's race.

Maya and her family (in order)

- Chula: blamed William for Taloa's death (Ep. 1). Maya felt abandoned by her, but takes the garment Chula made (Ep. 5) and they fight side by side.

- Bonnie: confronts Maya for leaving without a word (Ep. 3), then fights beside her with the power Maya shares (Ep. 5).

- Henry: refuses to help at first (Ep. 1) and warns her to stop (Ep. 2), but promises to help once he learns Fisk is alive (Ep. 3).

- Skully: makes her prosthetic legs (Eps. 2–3), tells her about the ancestors and urges her to reconnect.

- Biscuits: helps with the train job (Ep. 2), warns her that family is missing, and fights with a monster truck (Ep. 5).

Who died (in order)

- Taloa (Ep. 1): Maya's mother, killed in a crash after her brakes were cut.

- William (Ep. 1): Maya's father, killed by Ronin (Clint) after Fisk tipped him off (as seen in Hawkeye).

- The ASL interpreter (Ep. 4, flashback): dismissed after Fisk and Maya's dinner, then killed.

- Vickie (Ep. 3): sold Maya out, then was betrayed and killed by Zane.

- Zane (Ep. 5): killed by Henry at the powwow.

- Fisk's father (told in Ep. 4, seen as a memory in Ep. 5): killed by the young Fisk for beating his mother.
