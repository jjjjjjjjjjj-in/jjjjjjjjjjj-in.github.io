Episode 1: Chafa

The first ancestor

It opens long ago, with the story of the first Choctaw people emerging into the world.

Maya's childhood

Young Maya lives with her family in Oklahoma. An accident takes her mother's life and injures Maya's leg. Her father, William, takes her to New York.

'Uncle' Fisk

Her father's boss, Wilson Fisk, Kingpin, treats Maya like a daughter and trains her to fight. As she grows up, she works for his organization.

Fighting Daredevil

On a job, Maya fights Daredevil, the blind lawyer turned vigilante.

Shooting Kingpin

Learning the truth about her father's death, Maya shoots Kingpin and heads home to Oklahoma.

Episodes 2–3: Home

Family in Tamaha

In Tamaha are her uncle Henry, her grandmother Chula, her cousin Bonnie and her uncle Skully, who runs a roller rink. Maya's reunion with them is awkward.

Hitting the empire

Maya robs a train carrying Kingpin's goods, striking at his operation.

The ancestors

Each episode opens with a story of Maya's female ancestors. Maya begins to sense their power within her.

Episode 5: Maya

The powwow

On the day of the town's powwow, Kingpin's men attack. Maya's family and neighbors fight beside her.

Sharing the pain

Joining with the women of her family, Maya uses the ancestors' power to reach into Kingpin's pain. Shown his own childhood memories, he leaves town.

After the credits

Back in New York, Kingpin watches the news and sets his sights on a new ambition.
