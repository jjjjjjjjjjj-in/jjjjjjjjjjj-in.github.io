StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

1. The ship is attacked

Thanos attacks the ship carrying Asgard's people, takes the Space Stone and beats the Hulk.

2. A warning to Earth

Sent to Earth, Bruce warns Strange and Tony. Thanos's lieutenants arrive in New York and abduct Strange.

3. The Guardians and Thor

The Guardians rescue Thor drifting in space and split into two teams.

4. Gamora and the Soul Stone

Thanos takes Gamora and forces her to reveal where the lost Soul Stone is.

5. The battle on Titan

Tony, Peter, Strange and the Guardians fight Thanos on his home world, Titan, and come within inches of winning.

6. The battle of Wakanda

On Earth, the Avengers and Wakanda's army fight a vast horde to protect the stone in Vision's forehead. Thor arrives with a new weapon.

7. The snap

With all six stones, Thanos snaps his fingers.

Key terms

Infinity Stones
Six stones born with the universe. Together, they can do anything with a snap of the fingers.
The Infinity Gauntlet
The golden glove made to hold all six stones.
The Black Order
Thanos's lieutenants, called his children, including Ebony Maw and Proxima Midnight.
Where the stones are
Space is in the Tesseract with Loki, Mind in Vision's forehead, Reality with the Collector, Power on Xandar, Time in Strange's amulet, and Soul is lost.
Stormbreaker
Thor's new axe-like weapon, which can also summon the Bifrost.
Wakanda
The vibranium nation, site of the final great battle.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why does Thanos want to wipe out half of all life?

He truly believes the universe's resources are limited, and that halving life lets the rest thrive.

Why does Strange give up the stone?

He looked at over 14 million possible futures and found just one where they win. Giving up the stone was part of it.

What does the Soul Stone require?

Sacrificing the one you love most. Thanos sacrifices Gamora.

Are the people who vanished dead?

They turned to dust. What happens next is told in Endgame.

What's the post-credits scene?

Just before he vanishes, Fury sends a message on a pager. The symbol on the screen is Captain Marvel's.

Easy-to-miss details

"Peter tingle"

Peter's hair stands on end as his spider sense warns him of danger.

Tony vs. Strange

Both proud, they trade jabs throughout, even about each other's facial hair.
