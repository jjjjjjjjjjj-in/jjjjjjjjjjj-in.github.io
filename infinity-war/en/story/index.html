Part 1: The Asgardian ship

1-1A distress call

A call for help goes out across space: the ship carrying the survivors of Asgard, destroyed in the previous film, is under attack.

Asgardians lie dead throughout the ship. The attackers are Thanos and the four lieutenants he calls his children: Ebony Maw, Cull Obsidian, Proxima Midnight and Corvus Glaive.

Thanos wears a golden gauntlet that already holds the purple Power Stone, taken from the planet Xandar.

1-2Loki and the Tesseract

Thanos tortures the captured Thor by pressing the Power Stone to his head and demands that Loki hand over the Tesseract.

Loki first acts as if he doesn't care, but he can't bear to watch his brother suffer and produces the hidden Tesseract.

1-3The Hulk is beaten

At Loki's signal, the Hulk leaps at Thanos. He has the upper hand at first, but Thanos beats him with his bare hands, without using a stone, and slams him to the floor.

Thor attacks too, but Maw binds him in the ship's twisted metal and gags him.

1-4Heimdall's last strength

The badly wounded Heimdall summons the Bifrost one last time and sends the Hulk alone to Earth.

Seeing this, Thanos takes his lieutenant's spear and stabs Heimdall to death while Thor, bound, can only watch.

1-5The second stone

Thanos crushes the Tesseract in his hand, takes out the blue Space Stone and sets it in the gauntlet. He now has two stones.

He orders his children: there are two more stones on Earth; find them and bring them to him on Titan.

1-6Loki's end

Saying Thanos will need a guide on Earth, Loki steps forward and pretends to pledge loyalty, naming himself a son of Odin.

Then he tries to stab Thanos with a hidden dagger. Thanos stops it with his power, seizes Loki by the throat and strangles him. Loki dies, and Thanos tosses the body in front of Thor, saying there will be no resurrections this time.

1-7The ship explodes

Thanos and his children vanish using the Space Stone, and the ship explodes.

Thor, lying beside Loki's body, is flung into space with the wreckage.

Part 2: Attack on New York

2-1Something crashes through the roof

At the Sanctum in New York, Strange and Wong are talking when something crashes through the roof. It's Bruce Banner, back in human form.

He warns them that Thanos is coming. Thanos means to wipe out half of all life in the universe.

2-2Tony in the park

Tony is walking in Central Park with his fiancée Pepper, talking about having a child someday.

Strange steps out of a portal and asks him to come, because the universe is in danger. Bruce appears behind him; he and Tony haven't seen each other in years.

2-3The six stones

At the Sanctum, Wong and Strange explain the six Infinity Stones. Strange's amulet holds the green Time Stone, and he has sworn to protect it with his life.

Bruce says Thanos was also behind Loki's attack on New York in 2012. Another stone, the Mind Stone, is in Vision's forehead, but Vision has gone missing.

Bruce says they should call Steve. Tony pulls out the old flip phone Steve sent him, but after their falling-out in Civil War, he can't bring himself to call.

2-4A ship over Bleecker Street

A wind tears through the streets and people run. A huge ring-shaped spaceship hangs overhead.

Thanos's lieutenants Ebony Maw and Cull Obsidian land on the street, and Maw demands the Time Stone.

Tony's suit forms around him from the device on his chest. Bruce tries to become the Hulk, but the Hulk refuses to come out.

2-5Peter joins in

On a school bus, Peter Parker spots the spaceship. His friend Ned creates a distraction so Peter can slip away as Spider-Man.

Tony fights Obsidian while Strange and Wong take on Maw. Wong opens a portal and sends Obsidian far away.

2-6Strange is taken

Maw tries to take the Time Stone from Strange's amulet, but Strange's spell burns his hand and he can't.

So Maw binds Strange in metal and lifts him up into the ship. Strange's cloak tries to pull him free, but it's not enough.

2-7Clinging to the ship

Tony and Peter chase the rising ship. Tony tells Peter to go home, but Peter clings to the hull and is carried high into the sky.

As the air thins, Peter can't breathe. Tony sends him a new suit, the Iron Spider, which wraps around him.

Tony's call with Pepper cuts out. The two get inside the ship as it leaves Earth.

2-8The two left on Earth

Wong stays behind to guard the Sanctum.

In the rubble, Bruce picks up the phone Tony dropped: the one that reaches Steve.

Part 3: The Guardians and Gamora

3-1Thor picked up in space

Answering the distress call, the Guardians of the Galaxy reach the wrecked Asgardian ship. Thor, drifting in space, slams into their windshield.

They pull him aboard, and Mantis wakes him with her power.

3-2What Thanos is after

Thor explains that Thanos is collecting the six stones. He took the Power Stone by attacking Xandar, and now he has the Space Stone too.

Thor says the red Reality Stone is with the Collector on Knowhere. Gamora, raised as Thanos's daughter, knows him well.

3-3Splitting up

Thor decides to go to Nidavellir, home of a legendary forge, to make a new weapon that can kill Thanos. Rocket and Groot go with him in a small pod.

Peter Quill, Gamora, Drax and Mantis head to Knowhere to secure the Reality Stone before Thanos.

3-4Gamora's request

On the way to Knowhere, Gamora makes Quill promise that if Thanos captures her, he'll kill her.

She says she knows something Thanos doesn't, and if he learns it, the universe is in danger. Torn, Quill promises.

3-5The illusion on Knowhere

On Knowhere, they find Thanos torturing the Collector for the stone's location. Gamora leaps at Thanos and stabs him. He falls, and she breaks down in tears.

But it was an illusion: Thanos already had the Reality Stone. The real Knowhere is burned and in ruins.

3-6The bubble gun

Using the stone, Thanos breaks Drax into blocks and turns Mantis into ribbons, then grabs Gamora.

Reminded of his promise, Quill tearfully fires, but the Reality Stone turns his shots into bubbles. Thanos vanishes with Gamora.

3-7Gamora's childhood

Aboard his ship, Thanos talks with Gamora. On her home world, he killed half the people and took the young Gamora to raise. Back then, he gave her a perfectly balanced knife.

He tells her the survivors on her world now live in plenty, and insists that what he did was right.

3-8Nebula's torment

Gamora claims she doesn't know where the lost Soul Stone is. So Thanos takes her to Nebula, captured earlier when she tried to kill him and now being tortured.

From Nebula's memories, Thanos knows Gamora found the Soul Stone. Unable to watch her sister suffer, Gamora reveals it is on the planet Vormir.

3-9The cliff on Vormir

Thanos and Gamora climb to a mountaintop on the misty planet Vormir. The Red Skull, now the stone's keeper, tells them the Soul Stone can only be had by sacrificing someone you love.

Gamora laughs that Thanos loves no one, but he is weeping. She tries to kill herself with her knife, but it turns to bubbles.

Thanos throws Gamora off the cliff and she dies. He receives the orange Soul Stone.

3-10Nebula escapes

While Thanos is away, Nebula escapes his ship and contacts Mantis and the others, telling them to meet her on Titan, Thanos's home world.

Part 4: The Avengers on Earth

4-1Together in Edinburgh

In Edinburgh, Scotland, Wanda and Vision are secretly living together. Vision, disguised as a human, talks with her about staying together.

News of the New York attack is on TV, and Vision says the stone in his head is sensing something.

4-2The night attack

On the city streets at night, Corvus Glaive stabs Vision with his glaive. The weapon disrupts Vision's powers, and he grows weaker.

Proxima Midnight and Corvus try to pry the stone from Vision's forehead. Wanda fights them with her powers but is overwhelmed.

4-3Help at the station

At a train station, Steve, Natasha and Sam arrive and drive off Proxima and Corvus. Since Civil War, the three have been living as fugitives.

They take the wounded Vision and fly to the Avengers compound.

4-4Rhodey and Secretary Ross

At the compound, Rhodey (War Machine) is talking with Secretary Ross by hologram when Steve's group walks in.

Ross orders Rhodey to arrest the fugitives. Rhodey refuses and cuts the call. Bruce also meets Natasha again after a long time.

4-5A life and a stone

Vision asks Wanda to destroy the stone, and him with it, so Thanos can't have it. Wanda refuses.

Steve says they don't trade lives. Bruce says it might be possible to remove the stone without killing Vision, but it would take advanced technology. Steve knows a place.

4-6To Wakanda

They fly to Wakanda. King T'Challa welcomes them, and Bucky is given a new arm.

T'Challa's sister Shuri examines Vision and starts work on removing the stone, but it will take time. Wanda stays nearby to guard the work.

Part 5: Three places in space

5-1Rocket and Thor

In the pod, Rocket gives Thor an artificial eye, and Thor puts it in to replace the one he lost in his last battle.

Thor talks about losing his father, mother, brother and best friend, but says he will still take down Thanos.

5-2On Maw's ship

Aboard his ship, Maw tortures Strange with needles to make him undo the Time Stone's spell. Strange holds out.

Tony and Peter are hiding aboard. Using a plan Peter takes from an old movie, Tony blasts a hole in the hull. Maw is sucked out into space and freezes to death. Peter saves Strange from being sucked out too.

5-3Tony's decision

Tony decides not to go home but to take the fight to Thanos, and he accepts Peter as an Avenger.

Strange warns that if it comes down to their lives or the stone, he will protect the stone.

5-4The dead forge

Thor's group reaches Nidavellir, but the great forge around the star has gone dark. Only Eitri, the giant dwarf king, remains.

Eitri tells them Thanos forced him to make the gauntlet for the six stones, then killed all the other dwarves and turned Eitri's hands to metal so he could never make anything again.

5-5Taking the star's fire

Thor restarts the forge's rings himself to let the star's light through, but a shutter jams. He holds it open with his own body and takes the full blast of the star.

Thor collapses near death, but in the mold, a new axe-shaped weapon, Stormbreaker, has formed.

5-6Groot's arm

Stormbreaker still has no handle. Groot grows out his own arm and cuts it off to make one.

When Thor grips Stormbreaker, his strength returns. With it, he can summon the Bifrost.

Part 6: The battle on Titan

6-1Meeting on Titan

The ship carrying Tony, Peter and Strange crashes on the ruined planet Titan. There they meet Quill, Drax and Mantis, who came at Nebula's call.

At first each side thinks the other works for Thanos, but they realize they're both there to fight him.

6-214,000,605 futures

Strange uses the Time Stone to look at possible futures. He sees 14,000,605 of them, and they win in only one.

Tony's group plans to pin Thanos down and pull off the gauntlet.

6-3Thanos's home world

Thanos appears using the Space Stone. He shows Strange how beautiful Titan once was.

When it grew overcrowded and food ran short, Thanos proposed wiping out half its people at random to save it. They refused, and the planet died. That is why, he says, he will wipe out half of all life in the universe.

6-4Everyone attacks

Tony's group attacks Thanos one after another. Thanos uses the stones to rain down pieces of a shattered moon.

Nebula then rams her ship into Thanos and joins the attack.

6-5So close

The heroes pin Thanos down, and Mantis puts him in a daze. Tony and Peter begin working the gauntlet off his hand.

Quill asks where Gamora is. From Nebula's words and what Mantis senses in Thanos's mind, he learns Gamora is dead.

6-6Quill's rage

Enraged, Quill keeps hitting Thanos, and Thanos snaps out of the daze and gets the gauntlet back.

Thanos beats the heroes one by one. Strange fights with magic but is defeated.

6-7Strange's bargain

Tony fights Thanos alone and is stabbed through the side with a blade. As Thanos moves to finish him, Strange offers the stone if he spares Tony's life.

Strange hands Thanos the Time Stone. Thanos sets the fifth stone in the gauntlet and vanishes to Earth.

Part 7: The battle of Wakanda

7-1The army arrives

Ships from Thanos's lieutenants crash outside the invisible barrier that protects Wakanda, and hordes of monstrous Outriders pour out.

T'Challa, Steve, Natasha, Bucky, Sam, Rhodey, Bruce in the Hulkbuster armor, Okoye and the Wakandan warriors, and M'Baku of the mountain tribe line up before the barrier. T'Challa cries, "Wakanda forever!"

7-2Opening the barrier

The Outriders throw themselves at the barrier and try to go around it. To protect the place where Vision is, T'Challa orders them to deliberately open part of the barrier and funnel the creatures to one spot.

There are too many, and the heroes are slowly pushed back.

7-3Thor arrives

Just as they're about to fall, Thor lands on the Bifrost with Stormbreaker and wipes out a mass of Outriders. Rocket and Groot are with him.

Bruce is overjoyed to see Thor again.

7-4Cull Obsidian's end

Cull Obsidian fights Bruce in the Hulkbuster. Bruce grabs him, rockets them both into the barrier, and Obsidian is blown apart by its energy and dies.

7-5Proxima's end

Wanda, who had been guarding Vision, joins the fight. Proxima Midnight is battling Natasha and Okoye.

Wanda lifts Proxima with her powers and throws her into the path of one of the army's spinning-blade machines. Proxima is caught in the blades and dies.

7-6Corvus's end

With Wanda gone, Corvus Glaive slips into Shuri's lab. Shuri's work is interrupted, and Vision gets up to fight him.

Vision falls out of the lab and is cornered, but Steve steps in. Vision takes Corvus's own glaive and kills him with it.

7-7Thanos arrives

The air shifts and Thanos steps out of a rift in space. The heroes charge him one after another, but with the stones he easily throws off Steve, Bruce, T'Challa and the rest.

Steve holds back the gauntlet with his bare hands, but Thanos punches him down.

7-8Wanda's tears

Vision begs Wanda to destroy the stone now. Weeping, she holds Thanos back with one hand and pours her power into the stone with the other.

The stone shatters, and Vision falls dead in a burst of light. Thanos tells Wanda he understands her pain.

7-9Time rewound

But Thanos turns back time with the Time Stone. The stone is restored, and so is Vision.

Thanos rips the stone from Vision's forehead. Vision turns gray, falls and dies. Thanos sets the sixth stone in the gauntlet.

7-10"You should have gone for the head"

As the power of all six stones surges, Thor flies in and drives Stormbreaker into Thanos's chest.

Badly wounded, Thanos tells him he should have gone for the head, and snaps his fingers.

Part 8: Those who vanish

8-1Young Gamora

In the instant of the snap, Thanos finds himself in a strange place with Gamora as a child. She asks if he did it; he says yes. She asks what it cost; he answers, "Everything."

Back in the battlefield, the gauntlet scorched, Thanos opens a rift and disappears.

8-2Vanishing in Wakanda

Thor shouts, asking where Thanos went. Then Bucky, calling Steve's name, turns to dust.

Next, T'Challa as he speaks to Okoye, Groot in front of Rocket, Wanda beside Vision, and Sam all crumble to dust.

8-3Vanishing on Titan

On Titan, Mantis says something is happening and fades away, followed by Drax and Quill. Strange tells Tony there was no other way and vanishes.

Last, Peter Parker, saying he doesn't want to go, crumbles to dust in Tony's arms. Only Tony and Nebula are left on Titan.

8-4Those left behind

On the battlefield in Wakanda, Steve, Natasha, Thor, Bruce, Rhodey, Rocket, Okoye and M'Baku remain. Steve sinks down beside Vision's body and whispers, "Oh, God."

8-5Thanos at sunrise

In a quiet hut on a distant world, the wounded Thanos sits and watches the sun come up.

8-6After the credits

In a city on Earth, Nick Fury and Maria Hill are driving when drivers around them vanish, cars crash and a helicopter hits a building.

Hill turns to dust. Fury pulls out a modified pager and sends a signal before he too crumbles away. On the dropped pager's screen is a red, blue and gold star emblem.

Summary (spoilers through the ending)

The order Thanos got the stones

1) Power (purple): taken from Xandar before the film begins.

2) Space (blue): from the Tesseract Loki hands over on the Asgardian ship (Part 1).

3) Reality (red): taken from the Collector on Knowhere (Part 3).

4) Soul (orange): won on Vormir by sacrificing Gamora (Part 3).

5) Time (green): handed over by Strange on Titan in exchange for Tony's life (Part 6).

6) Mind (yellow): destroyed by Wanda in Wakanda, then restored when Thanos turns back time and rips it from Vision's forehead (Part 7).

Who died (in order)

- People on the Asgardian ship (Part 1): attacked by Thanos's forces.

- Heimdall (Part 1): Thanos stabs him with his lieutenant's spear for sending the Hulk to Earth.

- Loki (Part 1): strangled after trying to stab Thanos with a dagger.

- Gamora (Part 3): Thanos throws her off the cliff on Vormir to get the Soul Stone.

- Ebony Maw (Part 5): sucked out into space through a hole Tony blasts in the hull, and frozen.

- Cull Obsidian (Part 7): Bruce, in the Hulkbuster, slams him into Wakanda's barrier.

- Proxima Midnight (Part 7): Wanda throws her into a spinning-blade machine.

- Corvus Glaive (Part 7): Vision stabs him with his own glaive.

- Vision (Part 7): Wanda destroys the stone at his request and he dies, but Thanos turns back time and kills him by ripping the stone out.

(Before the film, Thanos had also attacked the dwarves of Nidavellir and the people of Xandar.)

Who vanished in the snap, and who was left

Vanished (main characters): Bucky, T'Challa, Groot, Wanda, Sam, Mantis, Drax, Quill, Strange, Peter Parker, Maria Hill and Nick Fury. Half of all life in the universe was gone.

Left: Tony and Nebula on Titan; Steve, Natasha, Thor, Bruce, Rhodey, Rocket, Okoye and M'Baku in Wakanda.
