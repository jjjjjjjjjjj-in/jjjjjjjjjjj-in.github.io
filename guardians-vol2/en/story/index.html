Part 1: Lovers in 1980

Two in a car

1980, Missouri. Peter's mother Meredith drives happily with the man she loves. He plants a glowing seedling deep in the woods.

Part 2: A job for the Guardians

Monster-fighting and Baby Groot

Thirty-four years later, the Guardians fight a giant space monster to protect valuable batteries for the golden-skinned Sovereign. Behind them, Baby Groot happily dances to the music.

Collecting Nebula

Their payment is Nebula, Gamora's captured adopted sister. But Rocket has secretly stolen some batteries, and Queen Ayesha's fleet chases them.

A mysterious savior

Just as the swarm of ships closes in, a mysterious man wipes them all out on his own. The Guardians crash-land on a forest planet.

Meeting his father

The man introduces himself as Ego, Peter's father. With him is Mantis, who can sense people's feelings.

Part 3: The team splits up

To Ego's planet

Peter, Gamora and Drax go to Ego's planet. Rocket and Groot stay behind to fix the ship and guard Nebula.

Yondu hesitates

Yondu's crew, hired by Ayesha, comes to capture Peter. But Yondu hesitates to hand him over, his crew mutinies, and he and Rocket are captured.

Yondu's arrow

With Baby Groot's help, Yondu gets back his whistle-controlled arrow and takes out the mutineers. He reveals he knows why Ego wanted Peter, and that's why he never delivered him.

Part 4: What Ego is

A living planet

Ego is a godlike being: the planet itself is his body. Peter has inherited his power and learns to form a ball of light in his hands. Playing catch with his father, he's as happy as a child.

Ego's plan

But Ego's goal is to grow the seedlings he planted across the universe and turn everything into himself. For that, he needs Peter's power.

The truth about his mother

Ego admits he put the tumor in Peter's mother's head. Enraged, Peter shoots him.

The cave of bones, and sisters

Warned by Mantis, Gamora and Drax find a cave full of the bones of Ego's other children. Nebula fights Gamora, then admits all she ever wanted was a sister, and they join forces.

Part 5: Fighting his father

Groot's bomb

Yondu and Rocket arrive, and the whole team is together. Rocket builds a bomb, and tiny Groot carries it to Ego's core, after being told over and over which button not to press.

Father against son

Peter fights Ego with his own powers to buy time. The bomb goes off and Ego dies. With him goes Peter's power, and he becomes an ordinary man.

Yondu's end

Escaping the dying planet, Yondu puts the only spacesuit on Peter and dies in space. His last words: Ego may have been Peter's father, but he wasn't his dad.

Part 6: Afterward

Fireworks in space

At Yondu's funeral, the ships of his old Ravager comrades gather and light up the sky. Peter realizes Yondu was his real dad.

Moving on

Nebula leaves alone to hunt Thanos. Rocket, realizing how much he and Yondu had in common, cries.

The credit scenes

There are five scenes during and after the credits, including Yondu's old team reuniting, Ayesha creating a new being named Adam, and Groot as a moody teenager.
