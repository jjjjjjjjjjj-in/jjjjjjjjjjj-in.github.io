Part 1: Missouri, 1980

1-1Two in a car

1980, Missouri. Young Meredith Quill, Peter's future mother, and the man she loves drive along happily with music playing.

1-2The seedling in the woods

He takes Meredith deep into the woods and shows her a small seedling he has planted. It glows with a strange light no Earth plant could give off.

Part 2: A job for the Sovereign

2-1Waiting for the monster

2014. Famous for saving Xandar, the Guardians (Peter, Gamora, Drax, Rocket and tiny Baby Groot) are on the world of the golden-skinned Sovereign.

Their job is to protect valuable Anulax batteries from the Abilisk, a monster from another dimension. Rocket sets up speakers so they can have music while they fight.

2-2Groot dances

The monster arrives and the four of them scramble to fight it. In the middle of it all, Baby Groot happily dances to the music.

Drax leaps into the monster's mouth. Gamora spots a wound in the skin of its neck and slices it open with her sword, killing it.

2-3Ayesha and Nebula

The Sovereign's high priestess Ayesha pays them with Nebula, Gamora's adopted sister, who was caught trying to steal the batteries. The Guardians plan to take her to Xandar for the bounty.

Ayesha senses that Peter has unusual heritage.

2-4The Sovereign fleet

Soon after the Milano takes off, a Sovereign fleet comes after them. Rocket has secretly stolen some of the batteries.

The swarm of drone ships is piloted remotely by Sovereign people at home, like an arcade game. Fighting over who should fly, Peter and Rocket flee through a dangerous field of floating rocks.

2-5A mysterious rescuer, and a crash

Just as they're cornered, someone in a small ship appears and wipes out the entire enemy swarm single-handedly.

The battered Milano jumps away and crash-lands on the forest planet Berhert. Peter and Rocket have a furious argument about the theft.

2-6The man who says he's his father

An egg-shaped ship lands. The man who steps out says his name is Ego and that he is Peter's father. With him is Mantis, who can sense the feelings of anyone she touches.

Peter isn't sure he believes it, but at Gamora's urging he agrees to visit Ego's planet.

Part 3: Yondu, and Ego's planet

3-1The team splits up

Peter, Gamora and Drax board Ego's ship. Rocket and Groot stay on Berhert to repair the Milano and guard Nebula.

On the ship, Mantis demonstrates her power on Drax. She also touches Peter and blurts out his feelings for Gamora, to his embarrassment.

3-2Yondu on Contraxia

On the snowy pleasure planet Contraxia, Yondu, who abducted and raised Peter as a boy, is relaxing with his crew.

Stakar Ogord, leader of all the Ravagers, shows up. Long ago Yondu broke the Ravager code by trafficking children, and was cast out for it. Stakar refuses to have anything to do with him and leaves.

3-3Ayesha's offer

Afterward, Ayesha comes to Yondu and hires him to capture the Guardians. He takes the job.

His crewman Taserface and others grumble that Yondu went soft on Peter, keeping the boy instead of delivering him to the client long ago.

3-4Ego's planet

Ego's planet is beautiful and full of color. In his palace, Ego tells his origin story using moving figures.

Ages ago he came into being as a mind alone in space, and gathered matter around himself to build this planet. He made a human form to travel the universe, and on Earth he fell in love with Meredith. After she died he hired Yondu to bring him his son, but Yondu never delivered Peter.

Ego describes the Earth song "Brandy" as being about a sailor who can't give up the sea, and says that, like him, he couldn't give up his travels to stay with Meredith.

3-5A game of catch with light

Ego tells Peter he has inherited Ego's Celestial power and teaches him to use it. Peter learns to form a ball of light in his hands, and he and his father play catch with it.

Peter admits that as a lonely, fatherless kid he used to tell people his dad was David Hasselhoff.

Part 4: The Ravagers mutiny

4-1Rocket's traps

Night on Berhert. Yondu's crew surrounds the Milano. With traps set in the forest and his weapons, Rocket takes down Ravager after Ravager.

4-2Nebula's shot

Yondu appears and catches Rocket. He suggests just selling the batteries himself; he has no intention of handing over Peter.

Seeing this, his lieutenant Kraglin questions his judgment, and Taserface leads a mutiny. Nebula, who has tricked Groot into freeing her, shoots off Yondu's head fin, the device he uses to control his arrow. Yondu, Rocket and Groot are captured.

4-3Crewmates thrown into space

On Yondu's ship, the Eclector, Taserface takes over. Tullk and the others loyal to Yondu are thrown out into space to die. Kraglin is shaken; he never signed up for this.

The Ravagers torment Baby Groot like a toy. Rocket laughs at the name Taserface.

4-4Nebula goes after her sister

Nebula takes a ship as her share of the bounty and leaves the Eclector. She blames Gamora for the torture Thanos put her through, and she's going to kill her.

4-5Two in a cell

In their cell, Yondu tells Rocket he knows exactly who he is, because he's the same. Rocket pushes people away even though he cares about them. So did Yondu.

4-6Groot's errand and the whistling arrow

Regretting the mutiny, Kraglin comes with Groot to free them. Little Groot is sent to fetch Yondu's spare fin, but keeps bringing back the wrong thing.

With the fin finally in place, Yondu whistles his arrow through the ship and kills the mutineers one after another. The four escape in a small ship and blow up the Eclector. Before he dies, Taserface tells the Sovereign where they're headed.

4-7Jump after jump

To reach Ego's planet quickly, the four make jump after jump in a short time. The strain is enormous, and their faces stretch and distort.

During the jumps, on one world, an old astronaut (Stan Lee) is telling the Watchers stories from his past.

Part 5: The secret of Ego's planet

5-1Gamora's doubts

In Ego's palace, Peter asks Gamora to dance. She tells him she senses that Ego and Mantis are hiding something.

Overjoyed to have found his father, Peter won't listen, and they argue. Gamora walks off alone.

5-2Drax and Mantis

Drax tells Mantis about his murdered wife and daughter. She touches him, feels the depth of his grief, and weeps.

Mantis tries to warn Drax of some danger, but stops herself.

5-3Sisters at war

Nebula arrives on Ego's planet and hunts Gamora, firing from her ship. Gamora fires back, Nebula's ship goes down, and the two fight savagely.

In the middle of it, Nebula finally says what she feels: Gamora was the one who always wanted to win; Nebula just wanted a sister. Thanos made them fight, and every time Nebula lost, he replaced part of her body with machinery. The sisters agree to work together.

5-4The cave of bones

In a cavern beneath the palace, Gamora and Nebula discover piles of skeletons.

Mantis and Drax arrive. Mantis reveals that the bones belong to Ego's other children.

5-5Ego's plan

Holding Peter's mind in his grip, Ego shows him his true purpose. In his travels he planted seedlings on thousands of worlds. When they grow, those worlds become part of him.

But waking the seedlings takes the power of two Celestials. Ego fathered children with countless women and had Yondu collect them, killing every child who didn't inherit his power.

5-6The truth about his mother

Ego reveals that he put the tumor in Meredith's head. He loved her too much; staying with her might have made him give up his plan.

Enraged, Peter breaks free of Ego's control and shoots him. Ego smashes Peter's beloved Walkman, seizes him and starts draining his power. The seedlings begin swelling on worlds across the universe, swallowing towns on Earth too.

Part 6: Fighting his father

6-1The others arrive

Yondu, Rocket, Kraglin and Groot reach the palace, open fire on Ego and pull Peter free. Gamora, Nebula, Drax and Mantis join them, and the whole group is together.

Mantis touches Ego and, with all her strength, puts him to sleep.

6-2To the brain at the core

Ego's true body is a huge brain at the center of the planet, and the group heads for it.

On the way, Yondu explains why he never delivered Peter: he knew what had happened to the other children, and kept Peter to protect him.

6-3Groot's bomb

Rocket builds a bomb from the stolen batteries. Only Groot is small enough to get it into the brain.

Rocket explains again and again which button not to press, and nobody is sure Groot has it.

6-4The Sovereign attack

Thanks to Taserface's tip, a swarm of Sovereign drone ships descends. Ego wakes up and attacks the group by moving rock and earth.

When Gamora nearly falls, Nebula saves her. Mantis is knocked out by Ego's attack, and Drax protects her.

6-5Father against son

Peter fights Ego with the power he inherited, buying time. He even forms a giant figure like Pac-Man, the game he loves, to slam into him.

Yondu tells Peter that Ego may be his father, but he isn't his dad. Peter focuses his power by thinking of the people he cares about.

6-6The explosion

Groot plants the bomb on the brain and the others escape. It goes off, and Ego dies.

With Ego gone, Peter's power vanishes too. The seedlings stop across the universe, and the planet starts to fall apart.

6-7Yondu's end

There is only one spacesuit left. Yondu puts it on Peter and carries him out into space.

Peter survives, but Yondu, with no suit, freezes and dies in space. Peter remembers moments when Yondu protected him growing up.

Part 7: Afterward

7-1Nebula leaves

Though she and Gamora have made peace, Nebula chooses to leave alone to hunt down Thanos. Gamora tells her she will always be her sister.

7-2Yondu's funeral

With Kraglin and their new member Mantis, the Guardians hold a funeral for Yondu. Peter says he had a real dad all along.

Kraglin gives Peter a new music player, a Zune, that Yondu had bought for him. Peter passes Yondu's arrow to Kraglin.

7-3Fireworks in space

Having heard from Rocket, Ravager ships arrive one after another and fill the sky with fireworks, honoring Yondu as one of their own again despite his exile. Stakar salutes.

Rocket cries. Peter and Gamora watch the sky side by side.

7-4Mid-credits

There are three scenes during the credits.

1) Kraglin practices whistling the arrow and accidentally stabs Drax. 2) Stakar reunites with the members of his old team, the one Yondu was part of. 3) Ayesha is creating a new being to destroy the Guardians, and names him Adam.

7-5After the credits

4) Some time later, Groot, now a teenager, sits in a messy room absorbed in a video game and sulks when Peter scolds him.

5) An old man (Stan Lee) grumbles as the Watchers he was talking to leave without him.

Summary (spoilers through the ending)

Ego's plan and Peter

Ego is a Celestial whose body is a planet. He planted seedlings on worlds across the universe to make everything part of himself (Part 5). That took the power of two Celestials, so he searched for Peter, the child who inherited his power (Part 3).

The tumor that killed Meredith was his doing (Part 5). Peter fought his father and the bomb destroyed Ego, but Peter lost his own power (Part 6).

Yondu and Peter

Yondu ferried children for Ego, but once he learned what happened to them, he kept Peter and raised him instead (Part 6). That dealing got him exiled from the Ravagers, and his softness on Peter got him betrayed by his crew (Parts 3 and 4).

In the end he gave Peter the only spacesuit and died, and Peter realized Yondu, who raised him, was his real father (Parts 6 and 7).

Who died (in order)

- The Abilisk (Part 2): Gamora cut open the wound in its neck.

- Tullk and Yondu's other loyalists (Part 4): thrown out into space by Taserface's mutineers.

- The mutinous Ravagers (Part 4): killed one after another by Yondu's whistling arrow once he got a fin back.

- Taserface (Part 4): died when Yondu's group blew up the Eclector, after telling the Sovereign where they were going.

- Ego's other children (before the story): killed by Ego for not inheriting his power; their bones are found in the cave (Part 5).

- Ego (Part 6): killed by Rocket's bomb, planted by Groot on the brain at the planet's core.

- Yondu (Part 6): gave Peter the only spacesuit while escaping the collapsing planet, and froze to death in space.
