Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

It continues the first film

It's set a few months after Guardians of the Galaxy. Watch that first.

About the Japanese title

In Japan it was released as Guardians of the Galaxy: Remix. The English title is Vol. 2.

It's about family

Peter and his father, Gamora and her sister Nebula, Peter and Yondu: it explores many kinds of family.

Lots of extra scenes

There are five extra scenes during and after the credits.

StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

1. Trouble with the Sovereign

The Guardians defeat a monster for the golden Sovereign, but Rocket steals some valuable batteries, and the Sovereign fleet comes after them.

2. Meeting his father

After a crash landing, they're met by Ego, who says he's Peter's father, and his companion Mantis.

3. The team splits up

Peter, Gamora and Drax go to Ego's planet. Rocket and Baby Groot stay with the ship and their prisoner, Nebula.

4. Yondu in trouble

Hired by the Sovereign to capture the Guardians, Yondu is blamed for protecting Peter, and his crew mutinies. With help from Rocket and Groot, he takes his ship back.

5. Sisters face off

Nebula reaches Ego's planet and fights Gamora fiercely. Then she admits she always just wanted to beat her sister, and they start to understand each other.

6. Ego's true plan

Ego plans to turn worlds across the universe into part of himself, and reveals he's the one who gave Peter's mother her illness.

7. The battle with Ego

Peter fights his father, while Rocket and Groot destroy Ego's core with a bomb.

8. His real father

As the planet collapses, Yondu saves Peter at the cost of his own life. Peter realizes that Yondu, who raised him, was his real father. The Ravagers light up the sky with fireworks to honor him.

Key terms

The Sovereign
A proud, golden-skinned people led by Ayesha.
Ego
Peter's father. Not just a man, but a godlike being who is a living planet.
Celestial
The name for ancient, enormously powerful beings like Ego.
The Ravagers
Yondu's space outlaws. Some of his crew, fed up with his soft spot for Peter, mutiny.
Yondu's arrow
A flying arrow Yondu controls by whistling.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

What is Ego?

A living planet that can also take human form. He wanted to use Peter's power to spread himself across the universe.

Why didn't Yondu deliver Peter to Ego?

Yondu had been bringing Ego children, until he learned what Ego did to them. So he kept Peter and raised him himself.

Why does Nebula resent Gamora?

Thanos made them fight, and Nebula always lost. Each time, he replaced part of her body with machinery.

Why is Groot bigger at the end?

In an extra scene, Groot has grown into a moody teenager.

What is the "Adam" Ayesha creates?

A new being the Sovereign are creating to destroy the Guardians. He appears in a later film.

Easy-to-miss details

Baby Groot's opening

While the others fight a monster, Baby Groot dances to the music in a delightful opening.

"He may have been your father, but he wasn't your daddy"

Yondu's words to Peter sum up the film's theme.

Mantis and Drax

Empathic Mantis and blunt Drax make a very funny pair.

Stan Lee's cameo

Marvel Comics legend Stan Lee appears telling stories to a group of cosmic watchers.
