Episode 1: Filmed Before a Live Studio Audience

1-1Newlyweds in the 1950s

A black-and-white sitcom with a studio laugh track begins. Wanda and Vision are newlyweds who have just moved to Westview, a small town in New Jersey.

Wanda can move things with her mind; Vision can phase through objects and fly. Wanting to fit in as an ordinary couple, they are careful to hide their powers from the neighbors.

1-2A heart on the calendar

One morning they notice a heart drawn on the calendar, but neither can remember what it means.

Vision heads off to work.

1-3Vision's office

Vision works at Computational Services Inc., where he amazes his co-workers by processing stacks of paperwork at incredible speed. Yet he isn't sure what the company actually does.

His boss, Mr. Hart, reminds him that Vision and Wanda are hosting him and his wife for dinner tonight. That is what the heart on the calendar really meant.

1-4Agnes next door

At home, their cheerful neighbor Agnes drops by to introduce herself. The two women decide the heart must mean an anniversary, and Agnes helps Wanda get ready to celebrate.

1-5A last-minute dinner

When Vision comes home, they realize the boss and his wife are coming tonight. Wanda scrambles to make dinner.

She uses magic in the kitchen while they try to keep Agnes from seeing, and with Agnes's help they get the meal together just in time.

1-6Mr. Hart chokes

At the table, the Harts ask the couple about themselves and where they came from. Neither Wanda nor Vision can answer.

Mr. Hart grows angry and begins choking on his food, while Mrs. Hart just laughs and repeats "stop it" over and over. The laugh track and music vanish, and the mood turns cold.

When Wanda tells Vision to help him, Vision phases his hand into Mr. Hart's throat and removes the food. Everything snaps back to a cheerful sitcom, and the Harts thank them and leave.

1-7Someone is watching

Alone again, the two reaffirm their love.

As the credits roll, we see that someone is watching this program, WandaVision, on modern equipment. Earlier, a retro commercial advertised a Stark Industries toaster.

Episode 2: Don't Touch That Dial

2-1Noises in the night

In a black-and-white 1960s setting, Wanda and Vision hear a loud noise outside one night but find nothing.

2-2Rehearsing for the show

The next day they rehearse a magic act for a neighborhood talent show, hoping to win over the neighbors.

Vision then heads off to a neighborhood watch meeting with the men of the town.

2-3A helicopter in color

In the bushes outside, Wanda finds a red-and-yellow toy helicopter, the only thing in color in the black-and-white world. She picks it up.

Agnes arrives and invites her to a women's committee meeting, and Wanda is distracted.

2-4Dottie's meeting

The meeting is run by Dottie Jones, the rather snooty queen bee of the neighborhood. There, Wanda becomes friends with a woman named Geraldine.

2-5Vision swallows gum

The men at the watch meeting welcome Vision. He accidentally swallows a stick of gum, which his body can't process, leaving him acting as if he were drunk.

2-6A voice on the radio, and red blood

After the meeting, as Wanda talks with Dottie, a man's voice comes through a radio, calling Wanda's name and asking who is doing this to her.

Dottie asks Wanda who she really is. Then the radio breaks and the glass in Dottie's hand shatters. In the black-and-white picture, only the blood on her hand is red, yet Dottie acts as if nothing happened.

2-7Covering with real magic

On stage, the "drunk" Vision accidentally shows his real powers. Wanda secretly uses her magic to make everything look like simple stage tricks, including Vision sawing her in half.

The act is a hit, and Dottie gives them the award for Comedy Performance of the Year.

2-8The beekeeper and the rewind

Back home, Wanda is suddenly and visibly pregnant. As they celebrate, they hear the noise outside again.

They go out and see a man in a beekeeper suit climbing out of a manhole. Wanda refuses it and "rewinds" the scene to the pregnancy reveal.

The world bursts into color, now styled like the 1970s. Earlier, a commercial advertised Strücker watches bearing the Hydra logo.

Episode 3: Now in Color

3-1Dr. Nielsen's checkup

Now in 1970s color, Wanda's pregnancy is advancing remarkably fast. Town doctor Dr. Nielsen examines her, finds everything fine, and prepares to leave with his wife on vacation to Bermuda.

Seeing him out, Vision notices neighbor Herb cutting right through the concrete wall between their driveways.

3-2The nursery and a name

They build a nursery and debate names: Wanda wants Tommy, and Vision prefers Billy.

3-3Wanda's powers go haywire

The pregnancy suddenly speeds up and Wanda's powers spin out of control. Strange things happen around the house, and a surge of her energy knocks out power across Westview.

Vision rushes off to fetch Dr. Nielsen.

3-4The twins are born

Geraldine drops by. Wanda tries and fails to hide her pregnancy, then goes into labor, and with Geraldine's help gives birth to twin boys.

They are named Tommy and Billy.

3-5People who can't leave

Dr. Nielsen hints that his vacation is really a way to escape Westview.

Outside, Herb and Agnes gossip about Geraldine. Agnes says Geraldine has no home or family in Westview. Herb starts to tell Vision something about their situation, but Agnes stops him.

3-6Pietro and Ultron

At the house, Geraldine mentions that Wanda's twin brother Pietro was killed by Ultron, something no one in town should know.

Wanda turns cold and interrogates her, and notices a pendant with a sword emblem around Geraldine's neck.

3-7Cast out of town

When Vision returns, Geraldine is gone. Wanda tells him she had to rush home.

Outside Westview, Geraldine is thrown out through a wall of TV-like static and is surrounded by agents of S.W.O.R.D.

Episode 4: We Interrupt This Program

4-1Return at the hospital

S.W.O.R.D. agent Monica Rambeau comes back from the Blip in a hospital amid the chaos of people reappearing. She learns that her mother, Maria, died of cancer three years earlier.

4-2S.W.O.R.D. and Hayward

Three weeks later, Monica returns to work. S.W.O.R.D., which her mother founded, is now run by Acting Director Tyler Hayward. Following Maria's own order before her death, he restricts anyone who returned from the Blip to missions on Earth.

Monica is sent to help FBI agent Jimmy Woo with a missing-persons case.

4-3A town that doesn't exist

A witness under Woo's protection has gone silent in Westview. Two local police officers insist the town doesn't exist, even though it's right behind them. Woo explains that some unknown force keeps him from entering.

Monica sends in a small drone, which vanishes. They find the town surrounded by an invisible hexagonal field. Reaching toward it, Monica is pulled inside.

4-4Darcy and WandaVision

Within 24 hours, S.W.O.R.D. sets up a base around the town. Astrophysicist Dr. Darcy Lewis is brought in and finds old-style TV broadcast signals coming from the field.

Hooked up to vintage TVs, the signal shows a sitcom called WandaVision. The team learns that the real residents have been cast as characters, that Monica is "Geraldine," and that Vision, who died five years ago, is alive. Darcy was the one watching in Episode 1.

4-5The radio voice and the beekeeper

Darcy and Woo try to reach Wanda by radio; Woo was the voice in Episode 2.

Agent Franklin tries to enter through the sewers, but past the boundary his hazmat suit becomes a beekeeper outfit and his tether turns into a jump rope. The toy helicopter in Episode 2 was Monica's drone, transformed to fit the era.

4-6The scene that wasn't aired

The moment from Episode 3 when Geraldine mentioned Ultron and Wanda threw her out was never shown on the broadcast. Darcy and Woo realize the show is censoring itself.

Inside, the sitcom illusion drops and Wanda sees Vision as he was when he died, gray and broken. Horrified, she quickly restores the illusion.

4-7It's Wanda

Monica wakes up at the S.W.O.R.D. base, surrounded by agents. She tells them that Wanda is the one controlling all of it.

Episode 5: On a Very Special Episode...

5-1Crying twins and Agnes

In a 1980s-style setting, Wanda and Vision struggle to calm baby Tommy and Billy. Agnes comes to help, but seems to break character and asks Wanda whether they should take the scene again from the top.

Vision finds this odd. Then the twins suddenly age up to about five. Agnes isn't surprised at all.

5-2Hayward's version

At the base, Hayward calls Wanda a terrorist, while Monica doesn't believe she means harm.

Hayward shows footage he says proves Wanda broke into S.W.O.R.D. headquarters, stole Vision's body and resurrected him, against Vision's living will.

Since her clothes changed to fit the era when she entered, Monica suggests sending in something that already belongs to that era.

5-3Sparky the dog

A dog shows up and the boys beg to keep it; Agnes suggests the name Sparky. Wanda nearly uses her powers in front of Agnes, worrying Vision.

Told they're too young to care for a dog, the twins age themselves up to about ten.

5-4Who Norm really is

At work, Vision reads an email from S.W.O.R.D. describing what is going on in Westview.

He uses his power to break the trance over his co-worker Norm, revealing a real Westview resident named Abilash Tandon, who begs Vision to stop "her." Vision puts him back as Norm.

5-5Wanda walks out

S.W.O.R.D. sends in a 1980s-era drone, and on Hayward's orders it tries to kill Wanda.

Wanda walks out of the barrier carrying the wrecked drone, drops it in front of Hayward, and warns him to leave her alone. With her power she turns the soldiers' guns toward Hayward, then goes back inside.

5-6Sparky dies

Sparky gets out, eats leaves from Agnes's azalea bushes, and dies. The boys ask their mother to bring him back to life.

Wanda tells them she can't, and that they have to face their grief.

5-7Vision confronts Wanda

Vision accuses Wanda of controlling the town and admits he can't remember anything about his life before Westview.

The end credits start to roll, but Wanda stops them and the argument continues. She says she isn't in control of everything, and doesn't know how it began.

5-8Her brother is back

The doorbell rings. Standing there is her dead brother Pietro, but with a different face from the brother she knew.

Watching the broadcast, Darcy remarks that Pietro has been recast.

Episode 6: All-New Halloween Spooktacular!

6-1A family Halloween

In a late-1990s to early-2000s setting, Wanda wants to spend the twins' first Halloween together, but Vision says he's going out with the neighborhood watch.

Wanda, the boys and "Pietro" go trick-or-treating in costume.

6-2Pietro's pranks and Tommy's speed

"Pietro" causes mischief around town with his super-speed, and Tommy turns out to have the same power.

Wanda asks why he looks different, but he insists he's her brother. Later he says he knows Wanda is controlling the town and he's fine with it. He keeps asking how she did it; she says she doesn't know.

6-3Thrown out of the base

Outside, Hayward prepares to strike Wanda. Monica, Darcy and Woo warn him it would start a war they can't win, and he has them thrown off the base.

They sneak back in to find out what he's hiding.

6-4Hayward's computer

Darcy hacks Hayward's system and finds he has been tracking the vibranium signature of Vision's body.

They also find Monica's blood test results: having passed through the barrier twice, her cells are changing at a molecular level.

Darcy stays behind while Monica and Woo go to meet a contact who can help Monica get back inside.

6-5The frozen townspeople

On patrol, Vision walks far from home. At the edge of town he finds residents frozen in place, including Agnes, sitting motionless in her car.

When he frees her, the real Agnes tells him he is dead and that Wanda is controlling them all. Vision puts her back under.

6-6Vision tries to leave

Vision pushes through the static wall, but his body begins to disintegrate.

Outside, Darcy begs Hayward to help him; instead she is handcuffed to a car.

6-7The Hex expands

At home, Billy senses that Vision is dying and tells Wanda, who expands the barrier.

The new boundary restores Vision but also swallows Darcy and several S.W.O.R.D. agents, turning them into circus performers. Hayward, Monica and Woo escape.

Episode 7: Breaking the Fourth Wall

7-1Wanda wants a day off

In a late-2000s style, characters talk to the camera in documentary-style interviews.

Wanda decides to take a day for herself. Agnes agrees to babysit Billy and Tommy and takes them to her house.

7-2A house out of control

Around Wanda's house, objects keep reverting to versions from earlier "episodes," and she can't control it.

7-3Vision wakes in a circus

Vision wakes up in a circus run by spellbound S.W.O.R.D. agents. He finds Darcy and frees her.

Darcy tells him about his death and everything that led up to the current situation.

7-4A road that won't let them through

Driving back to his house, Vision and Darcy keep getting held up at the same junction. Vision thinks Wanda is keeping him away.

Still, he realizes his wife needs him, and he flies the rest of the way, leaving Darcy behind.

7-5Monica crosses the barrier

Outside, Monica and Woo meet S.W.O.R.D. personnel loyal to Maria rather than Hayward and get a vehicle that should be able to cross. But half of it turns into a truck, and the attempt fails.

Since she's already passed through twice, Monica decides to go in on her own. She passes through the static and emerges with glowing eyes and new powers.

7-6Monica's warning

Monica goes to warn Wanda about Hayward. Wanda doesn't believe her and attacks, but Monica's new powers let her withstand it.

Agnes interrupts, tells Monica to leave, and takes Wanda to her house.

7-7The basement and Agatha

Agnes says the twins are in the basement. Going down, Wanda finds a mysterious lair and is trapped.

Agnes reveals herself as the powerful witch Agatha Harkness and shows Wanda how she manipulated events: she killed Sparky and sent "Pietro" to Wanda. It plays out as the opening of a new show, Agatha All Along.

7-8Mid-credits scene

In a mid-credits scene, Monica discovers Agatha's lair, but is caught by "Pietro."

Episode 8: Previously On

8-1Salem, 1693

Salem, Massachusetts, 1693. Agatha is put on trial by a coven led by her mother, Evanora, for practicing dark magic.

They try to kill her with their power, but Agatha absorbs their magic and drains the life from them all, her mother included.

8-2Into Wanda's memories

In the present, Agatha demands to know how Wanda is controlling Westview, and forces her to relive key moments of her life.

8-3The bomb in Sokovia

First, a small apartment in Sokovia. The family is watching an American sitcom together when a shell hits and kills the parents.

Young Wanda and Pietro are trapped in the rubble beside an unexploded bomb. From this memory, Agatha deduces that Wanda was born a witch who unknowingly used simple magic as a child, and that her power kept the bomb from going off.

8-4The Mind Stone experiment

Next, a Hydra lab, where Wanda is experimented on with the Mind Stone. As she approaches, it reacts and shows her a vision of a figure inside the stone.

Agatha concludes the stone amplified magic that was already within her.

8-5Vision at the Avengers Compound

Third, life at the Avengers Compound. Grieving Pietro, Wanda is watching a sitcom when Vision joins her and they talk about loss.

Vision tells her: "What is grief, if not love persevering?" It's a tender memory of the two growing close.

8-6What she saw at S.W.O.R.D.

After Vision's death, Wanda goes to S.W.O.R.D. to recover his body. Hayward shows her the disassembled remains but refuses to give them back, and she leaves without the body.

Hayward's claim in Episode 5 that she stole it was a lie.

8-7The empty lot in Westview

Wanda drives to Westview, to a lot Vision had bought so they could grow old there together. It's just an empty, rundown lot.

Overwhelmed by grief, her power pours out. A house rises, a new Vision appears, and the spell spreads across the whole town, turning it into a black-and-white 1950s sitcom. This is where Episode 1 began.

8-8The Scarlet Witch

As the memories end, Wanda hears Tommy and Billy crying for help and finds Agatha holding them captive.

Agatha mocks her for not knowing the extent of her own power and using it to build a sitcom world. She tells her this is chaos magic, which makes her the legendary Scarlet Witch.

In a mid-credits scene, Hayward uses Wanda's energy to activate the reassembled, all-white body of the original Vision.

Episode 9: The Series Finale

9-1Agatha and the White Vision

Agatha tries to take Wanda's magic, but the White Vision arrives and attacks Wanda, under Hayward's orders to eliminate her.

The Vision Wanda created steps in, and the two Visions battle across Westview while Agatha keeps attacking Wanda.

9-2The townspeople beg

Agatha frees the residents from the sitcom roles Wanda forced on them. Themselves again, they surround Wanda and beg her to set them free.

9-3Opening, and closing, the Hex

Wanda opens the barrier so the residents can leave, but her Vision and the twins begin to disintegrate. She closes it again.

Before it closes, Hayward drives in with S.W.O.R.D. personnel.

9-4Who "Pietro" really is

Monica fights "Pietro" and pulls off his magical necklace, freeing him from Agatha's control. He is really Ralph Bohner, the "Ralph" Agnes kept mentioning.

9-5Two Visions in the library

The fight moves into the town library, where Wanda's Vision raises the Ship of Theseus: if every part of a ship is replaced, is it still the same ship?

White Vision concludes, "Neither is the true ship. Both are the true ship." The conversation unlocks his memories. Declaring himself Vision, he flies away.

9-6Monica, the twins and Darcy

When his plan fails, Hayward opens fire on the twins. Monica jumps in front of them and stops the bullets with her new powers, and Billy stops one that gets past her.

Hayward tries to drive off, but Darcy rams his car with a food truck, and he's detained.

9-7The trap of runes

In the sky, Agatha drains Wanda's magic. But Wanda has carved runes, a trick she learned from Agatha, around the barrier, so that only the witch who cast them can use magic inside.

Powerless, Agatha can't stop Wanda from taking back all her magic. Wanda transforms into the Scarlet Witch.

She traps Agatha in Westview as the nosy neighbor "Agnes."

9-8Goodbye

Wanda takes her family home, and she and Vision put the boys to bed as she slowly takes down the barrier.

Vision asks what he is. Wanda tells him he is the part of the Mind Stone that lives in her, made from her love. They say goodbye, believing that since they've said goodbye before, they'll say hello again.

As the barrier vanishes, Vision, the twins and the house disappear with it.

9-9Wanda leaves

The residents glare at the woman who hurt them, but Monica sympathizes with her grief, and the two make peace. Vowing to understand her powers, Wanda flies away.

9-10Credit scenes

Mid-credits, Hayward is arrested. At a movie theater, a Skrull posing as an FBI agent tells Monica that a friend of her mother's wants to meet her in space.

Post-credits, at a remote cabin, Wanda studies the Darkhold in her astral form and hears her sons calling for help.

Summary (spoilers through the finale)

What was going on in Westview

- How it began (Ep. 8): After Vision's death and S.W.O.R.D.'s refusal to release his body, Wanda went to the lot Vision had bought for them. Her grief overflowed; she created a house and a new Vision, and turned the whole town into a sitcom.

- The townspeople (Eps. 1–9): forced by Wanda's power to play roles while suffering inside (Norm in Ep. 5, Agnes in Ep. 6, the residents in Ep. 9). Wanda finally frees them by taking down the barrier.

- Hayward (Eps. 5–9): lied that Wanda stole the body, and reactivated Vision's remains as the weaponized White Vision. He fires on the twins and is arrested.

- Agatha (Eps. 1–9): posed as Agnes to learn the secret of Wanda's power. She killed Sparky and sent Ralph Bohner in as "Pietro." Defeated by Wanda's runes, she is trapped in town as "Agnes."

- Vision and the twins (Ep. 9): created by Wanda's power, they vanish with the barrier. The White Vision regains his memories and flies off.

Who died or vanished (in order)

- Wanda's parents (Ep. 8 memory): killed when a shell hits their home in Sokovia.

- Pietro (mentioned in Eps. 3 and 8): died in the battle against Ultron. The "Pietro" in Westview was really Ralph Bohner.

- Vision (mentioned in Eps. 4 and 8): died in Infinity War. The Westview Vision was created by Wanda's power.

- Monica's mother, Maria (Ep. 4): died of cancer while Monica was gone in the Blip.

- Sparky the dog (Ep. 5): dies after eating azalea leaves. Episode 7 reveals Agatha killed him.

- The Salem coven (Ep. 8, 1693): tried to execute Agatha, but she drained their magic and their lives, including her mother Evanora's.

- Vision and the twins (Ep. 9): vanish with the barrier. In the post-credits scene, Wanda hears only the twins' voices.

My takeOpinion

It starts as a pastiche of old sitcoms and slowly reveals itself as a story about grief; that structure is clever. What Wanda does can't be excused, and yet you understand her, which is the painful part.
