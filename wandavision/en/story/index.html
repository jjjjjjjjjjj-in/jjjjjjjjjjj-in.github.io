Episode 1: Filmed Before a Live Studio Audience

A 1950s town

A black-and-white sitcom with a laugh track begins. Wanda and Vision are newlyweds who've moved to Westview, hiding her magic and his phasing from the neighbors.

Dinner with the boss

At dinner with Vision's boss and his wife, the boss chokes. Wanda says "stop it," and everything snaps back to normal.

Someone is watching

At the end, we see someone watching the show on a monitor somewhere.

Episode 2: Don't Touch That Dial

The talent show

Wanda and Vision perform a magic act at a town talent show. Vision acts drunk, and Wanda covers with real magic.

A colored helicopter

A red and yellow toy helicopter appears in the black-and-white world. Then a man in a beekeeper suit crawls out of a manhole, and Wanda "rewinds" the scene.

Wanda is pregnant

Suddenly, Wanda is visibly pregnant.

A voice on the radio

A voice on the radio asks, "Wanda, can you hear me?"

Episode 3: Now in Color

Twins

Now in 1970s color, Wanda gives birth the same day to twin boys, Tommy and Billy.

Geraldine

A neighbor named Geraldine mentions Wanda's brother Pietro and Ultron. Wanda's face changes.

Thrown out

Geraldine is blasted out of town.

Episode 4: We Interrupt This Program

Back from the Blip

Geraldine is really S.W.O.R.D. agent Monica Rambeau. Freshly returned from the Blip, she was swallowed by the town while investigating it.

The base outside

Outside, S.W.O.R.D., FBI agent Jimmy Woo and scientist Darcy have set up a base. They discover the town is broadcasting Wanda's sitcom.

The real Vision

For a moment, Vision appears as he was when he died. Wanda quickly makes it go away.

Episode 5: On a Very Special Episode...

Growing twins

The 1980s. The twins age to about ten in minutes.

Vision suspects

Vision suspects the townspeople are being controlled and argues with Wanda.

S.W.O.R.D.'s secret

S.W.O.R.D. Director Hayward reveals they have the real Vision's body.

Her brother returns

At the door stands her late brother Pietro, though played by a different actor than before.

Episode 6: All-New Halloween Spooktacular!

Halloween in the 2000s

A 2000s-style episode. The family goes trick-or-treating in costume.

The edge of town

Vision walks to the edge of town, where people stand frozen. He tries to leave, and his body begins to come apart.

The Hex expands

To save Vision, Wanda expands the barrier, swallowing the S.W.O.R.D. base too.

Episode 7: Breaking the Fourth Wall

Mockumentary

A 2010s mockumentary-style episode. Wanda speaks candidly to the camera.

Monica's powers

Monica forces her way through the barrier again and gains powers.

The neighbor's secret

Neighbor Agnes reveals she is the witch Agatha Harkness, as a theme song announces it was Agatha all along.

Episode 8: Previously On

A journey into the past

Agatha takes Wanda through her memories: losing her parents in Sokovia, touching the Mind Stone, her time with the Avengers.

The heart of grief

Grieving Vision, Wanda unknowingly remade the entire town and created a new Vision.

The Scarlet Witch

Agatha declares Wanda is the legendary Scarlet Witch. Outside, S.W.O.R.D. activates a white Vision.

Episode 9: The Series Finale

Two battles

Wanda and Agatha battle with magic in the sky while Vision fights the White Vision in town.

Restoring memories

Vision talks the White Vision into recovering his memories, and he flies off.

Wanda wins

Wanda turns Agatha's power against her and traps her as the town's nosy neighbor.

Goodbye

Wanda chooses to release the town. As the barrier shrinks, she says goodbye to the twins and Vision.

Credit scenes

Mid-credits, a Skrull invites Monica into space. Post-credits, in a cabin, Wanda studies a book and hears her sons calling for help.
