Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

It's set after Endgame

Vision died in Infinity War. Keep that in mind and the strangeness makes sense.

It starts as a classic sitcom on purpose

Black-and-white, laugh track and all. Give the first few episodes time: the real story emerges.

It's a TV series

Nine episodes, streaming on Disney+.

Two scenes after the finale

Key terms

Westview
The small American town where Wanda and Vision live.
The Hex
A giant invisible barrier around the town. Inside, the world runs by different rules.
S.W.O.R.D.
An agency handling threats such as those from space, investigating the town from outside.
The Scarlet Witch
The name of a legendary, immensely powerful witch.
Chaos magic
A rare magic that can reshape reality itself.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why is Vision alive?

The Vision in town is one Wanda created with her own power out of grief.

What happened to the townspeople?

Wanda's power forced them to play roles in her sitcom, and they suffered for it.

What's the White Vision?

S.W.O.R.D. rebuilt the real Vision's body. In the end, he regains his memories and flies away.

What happened to Monica?

Passing through the Hex gave her powers. Her story continues in The Marvels.
