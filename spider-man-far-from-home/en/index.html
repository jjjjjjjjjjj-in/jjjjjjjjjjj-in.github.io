StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

1. A world without Tony

Grieving Tony, Peter is worn out by everyone expecting him to be the next Iron Man. On a school trip to Europe, he plans to confess to MJ and leave hero work behind, even ignoring Fury's calls.

2. Monster in Venice

A water monster attacks Venice and a green-caped man destroys it. He is Quentin Beck, who says he comes from another Earth that the monsters destroyed.

3. Tony's glasses

Fury gives Peter E.D.I.T.H., glasses Tony left him that control vast data and armed drones. After the fire monster in Prague, Peter decides Beck deserves them more and hands them over.

4. Mysterio's secret

Beck is a former Stark engineer Tony dismissed; the monsters and the hero were faked with holograms and drones. Tipped off by a device MJ found, Peter is ambushed by illusions in Berlin and hit by a train.

5. Showdown in London

With Happy's help Peter builds a new suit and faces Beck's biggest illusion in London. Trusting his spider sense, he downs the drones and takes back the glasses. Beck is killed by his own drone's bullet. Peter and MJ start dating.

6. The credits

A video Beck left frames Spider-Man as a killer and reveals to the world that he is Peter Parker. Finally, a twist about Fury is revealed.

Key terms

The Blip
The five years people were gone after the Snap, and their return.
The Elementals
Giant monsters of water, fire, earth and air.
E.D.I.T.H.
The AI-powered glasses Tony left Peter, giving access to vast data and weaponry.
Mysterio
A green-caped hero who claims to come from another Earth. Real name: Quentin Beck.
"Peter tingle"
Peter's spider sense, which warns him of danger.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Is Mysterio really from another world?

No. He's a former Stark employee with a grudge against Tony, faking the monsters with holograms and drones.

What was going on with Fury and Hill?

The post-credits scene reveals they were the Skrulls Talos and his wife in disguise. The real Fury was in space.

What happens mid-credits?

A video Mysterio left behind is broadcast, revealing to the world that Spider-Man is Peter Parker.
