Part 1: A world without Tony

A storm in Mexico

Nick Fury and Maria Hill investigate a small Mexican village. A giant monster bursts out of a sudden storm, and a mysterious man in a green cape appears to fight it.

The memorial video

At Peter's school, a student-made tribute video plays. People who vanished for five years have returned, and the world is slowly adjusting.

Tony's shadow

Peter is grieving Tony and tired of being expected to be the next Iron Man. Images of Tony are everywhere.

The trip plan

Peter's class is going to Europe for summer. He plans to tell MJ how he feels and leave the hero business behind. He even ignores Fury's call.

Part 2: The monster in Venice

The water monster

In Venice, a huge monster rises from the canals. Peter tries to help without his mask, and the man in the green cape defeats it.

Fury and Beck

That night, Fury confronts Peter. The caped man is Quentin Beck, who says he comes from another Earth destroyed by these monsters.

Tony's glasses

Fury gives Peter a pair of glasses Tony left him. Inside is an AI called E.D.I.T.H., with access to vast data and weapons.

Part 3: A night in Prague

A change of plans

Fury secretly reroutes the trip to Prague, where he expects the next monster.

The fire monster

During a festival, a fire monster attacks Prague. Peter and Beck team up, and Beck destroys it.

Handing over the glasses

Talking with Beck at a bar, Peter decides an adult hero like Beck should have Tony's glasses, and gives them to him.

Beck's secret

After Peter leaves, Beck's team celebrates. Beck is a former Stark employee whose work Tony dismissed. The monsters, and the hero, were all faked with holograms and drones.

Part 4: The lie uncovered

MJ's find

MJ shows Peter a small device she picked up at the battle site: a projector. Peter is impressed, and the two grow closer.

Illusions in Berlin

Peter goes to Berlin to warn Fury, but it's one of Beck's illusions. He's battered inside the illusions and finally hit by a train.

Waking up in the Netherlands

Peter wakes up in a Dutch jail cell and calls Happy to come get him.

A new suit

On Happy's jet, Peter designs his own new suit with Tony's technology. Happy tells him Tony struggled too.

Part 5: Showdown in London

The biggest illusion

In London, Beck unleashes his biggest illusion yet while drones cause real destruction. MJ, Ned and the others are in danger.

Peter tingle

Trusting his spider sense, Peter closes his eyes, ignores the illusions and takes out the drones.

Getting the glasses back

Peter retrieves the glasses. Beck is hit by his own drone's fire and goes down.

Back in New York

Back home, Peter takes MJ on a web-swinging ride over New York.

Part 6: The credit scenes

Mid-credits

News plays on giant screens across the city. A video Beck left behind makes it look like Spider-Man killed him, and ends by revealing that Spider-Man is Peter Parker.

After the credits

Fury and Hill turn out to be the Skrull Talos and his wife in disguise. The real Fury is relaxing aboard a Skrull ship in space.
