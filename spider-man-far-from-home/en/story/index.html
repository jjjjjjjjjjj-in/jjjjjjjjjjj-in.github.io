Part 1: A world without Tony

1-1A storm and an earth monster in Mexico

Ixtenco, Mexico. Nick Fury and Maria Hill come to investigate a village hit by an unnatural storm.

A giant creature of earth and rock rises up. Then a man in a green cape and a fishbowl-like helmet flies in, fires green bolts from his hands and destroys it. Fury and Hill recruit him.

1-2The school's memorial news

Midtown High in New York, eight months after the fight with Thanos.

On the student news show run by Betty and Jason, a tribute plays for the fallen Iron Man, Captain America, Black Widow and Vision.

Betty then explains that those who vanished for five years in the Snap came back without aging, so Jason is now younger than his little brother. The school restarted the year for returning students.

1-3Aunt May's fundraiser

Aunt May is running a fundraiser for people who lost their homes in the Snap, and Peter helps out as Spider-Man.

Reporters ask if he'll be the next Iron Man, and he doesn't know what to say. Tony's face is everywhere; Peter is still grieving and worn out by everyone's expectations.

Happy is there too, acting close with May, which throws Peter. Happy tells him Fury wants to get in touch.

1-4The plan for Europe

For summer break, Peter's class heads to Europe for two weeks, led by Mr. Harrington and Mr. Dell.

Peter has decided to tell MJ how he feels. He tells his best friend Ned the plan: buy her a gift in Venice and confess at the Eiffel Tower in Paris.

He means to take a break from hero work and leave the suit at home. When Fury calls, he doesn't pick up.

1-5Seats on the plane

On the plane, Peter tries to sit next to MJ, but classmate Brad takes the seat. Brad didn't vanish in the Snap, so he used to be younger than Peter and is now in his grade.

Meanwhile Ned hits it off with Betty in the next seat, and the two start dating during the trip.

Part 2: The water monster in Venice

2-1A black dahlia necklace

First stop: Venice, Italy. At a glass shop, Peter buys MJ a necklace shaped like a black dahlia, because she once brought up the flower.

2-2A water monster from the canals

As the class tours the city, the canal swells and a giant creature made of water rises up and starts wrecking the city.

Without his suit, Peter tries to protect his classmates with only his web-shooters, but the webs pass right through the water.

Aunt May had secretly packed his suit, but he left it at the hotel.

Then the green-caped man flies in and destroys the creature.

2-3They call him Mysterio

On the news, the mysterious hero is called "Mysterio," and the class is thrilled with the new hero. Classmate Flash, as usual, is livestreaming on his phone.

2-4Fury's summons

That night in the hotel room, Ned is mid-story when he suddenly drops: Fury has shot him with a tranquilizer dart.

Fury is irritated that Peter dodged his calls, and takes him to a secret base.

2-5Beck's story

Yesterday's hero is there: Quentin Beck. He says he comes from another Earth, Earth-833.

Creatures of earth, water, air and fire, the Elementals, destroyed his world and killed his family, and now they've come to Peter's Earth, Earth-616.

Beck predicts the Fire Elemental will strike Prague, in the Czech Republic, next.

2-6Tony's glasses

Fury hands Peter a pair of glasses Tony left him. Inside is the AI E.D.I.T.H., which can search all of Stark Industries' data and command armed drones kept in orbit. Tony meant for Peter to have it.

2-7Peter says no

Fury asks Peter to fight alongside them, but Peter says he's just a high schooler who wants his trip back, turns him down and returns to the hotel.

Fury has no intention of taking no for an answer.

Part 3: The fire monster in Prague

3-1A change of route

The next morning the trip is suddenly rerouted from Paris to Prague. Fury pulled the strings. The new bus driver is Fury's operative, Dimitri.

3-2The black stealth suit

Along the way, a woman working for Fury fits Peter with a black stealth suit, so he can fight abroad without being recognized as Spider-Man.

3-3A drone strike on the bus

On the bus, Peter asks E.D.I.T.H. about Brad. The AI treats it as an order to strike, and calls down a drone to target the bus.

Peter frantically cancels and just barely stops it. The glasses, he learns, can launch a real attack with one careless word.

3-4Festival night in Prague

Prague is holding a festival of lights. Knowing the Fire Elemental is coming, Peter tries to keep his classmates indoors.

But when it appears, Ned and Betty are up on the festival's Ferris wheel and get stuck high above the ground.

3-5The Fire Elemental and Night Monkey

In the black suit, Peter fights the Fire Elemental with Beck but is no match for it. In the end Beck destroys it.

Ned names the black-suited hero "Night Monkey," and Prague cheers for Mysterio and Night Monkey.

3-6Handing the glasses to Beck

After the fight, Fury invites Peter and Beck to Berlin to discuss a new team of heroes.

Peter talks with Beck alone in a bar. He decides he's just a kid, and that an adult hero like Beck should take Tony's place. He gives Beck the E.D.I.T.H. glasses and transfers control to him.

3-7Beck's secret

Once Peter leaves, the bar dissolves: it was a set made of holograms, and Beck's team appears, celebrating.

Beck is no hero from another world. He's a former Stark employee who built hologram tech until Tony dismissed him and he was pushed out. He created B.A.R.F., the memory-projection device Tony showed students in Civil War.

His team are ex-Stark people with grudges against Tony too, including William Ginter Riva, who once worked for Obadiah Stane, Gutes Guterman, who writes the story, and Janice, who made the costume.

3-8The monsters were all lies

The monsters and Mysterio's powers were all faked by Beck's team with holograms and armed drones.

Now that he has E.D.I.T.H.'s drones, Beck plans to stage even bigger illusions and pose as an Avenger-level hero, the greatest in the world.

Part 4: The lie uncovered

4-1MJ's confession

That night, Peter walks through Prague with MJ. As he tries to confess, she tells him she has figured out he's Spider-Man.

She shows him a small device she picked up at the battle site, with his webbing still stuck to it.

4-2What the device showed

The device switches on and projects an image of the Fire Elemental they supposedly destroyed. It's a part from one of Beck's drones.

Peter realizes Beck has been lying, and admits to MJ that he's Spider-Man. He asks MJ and Ned to cover for him and heads to Berlin, where Fury is.

4-3Fury in Berlin

In Berlin, Fury picks Peter up by car, and Peter tells him Beck is a fraud. Fury asks who else knows, and Peter names MJ and Ned.

But this Fury is an illusion projected by Beck.

4-4Nightmare illusions

Beck hits Peter with illusion after illusion. The ground falls away, the world flips, and Tony's grave appears, even a corpse-like Iron Man rising from the earth.

Inside the illusions, the drones are really attacking him, and Peter can't tell what's real.

4-5Hit by a train

Lured onto the tracks, Peter is struck by an oncoming train. Beck assumes he's dead.

Beck heads to London, the trip's last stop, to get rid of MJ, Ned and the others who know the truth.

4-6A Dutch jail cell

Unconscious on the train, Peter has been carried all the way to the Netherlands. He wakes in a police cell with some soccer fans.

He slips out and phones Happy for help.

4-7A talk with Happy

Happy picks him up in a tulip field in a Stark jet. Peter admits he was fooled by Beck and that he's lost without Tony.

Happy tells him Tony second-guessed himself all the time and couldn't live up to the Tony everyone imagined either, but he chose Peter.

4-8Building a new suit

The jet has a suit-making machine Tony left behind. Peter designs his own red and black suit and builds it.

As he starts, Happy puts on AC/DC's "Back in Black." Peter mistakes it for Led Zeppelin. Watching Peter work, Happy sees Tony in him.

Part 5: Showdown in London

5-1The combined monster

In London, Beck unleashes his biggest illusion: a giant creature made of all the Elementals combined. Inside it, swarms of armed drones are tearing up the city for real.

Under cover of the chaos, Beck means to kill the friends of Peter's who know the truth.

5-2Peter arrives from the sky

MJ, Ned, Betty and Flash are lured onto a bridge over the Thames by Beck's people.

Reaching London aboard Happy's jet, Peter leaps out in his new suit and glides down into the city.

5-3Friends in the tower

Happy reaches the kids and they all take cover inside an old tower.

Drones follow them in, but MJ grabs an old mace on display and knocks one down.

5-4Fighting with his eyes closed

Peter gets inside the creature and starts wrecking drones, but Beck's illusions throw him off.

He closes his eyes and trusts only his spider sense, dodging drones he can't see and taking them out one by one.

5-5Beck's end

Peter tracks Beck down. Cornered, Beck orders the drones to fire at Peter, but a stray drone bullet hits Beck himself.

He tries one last illusion to fool Peter, who sees through it. Before dying, Beck tells his associate Riva to retrieve the data from the drones.

Peter takes back the glasses and has E.D.I.T.H. call off every drone.

5-6Confessing to MJ

After the battle, Peter gives MJ the black dahlia necklace, now broken. She happily takes it, broken or not.

They tell each other how they feel, and kiss.

5-7Back home in New York

The trip is over and the class flies home. At the airport, May and Happy are waiting for Peter.

Ned and Betty decide to split up, on friendly terms. Peter and MJ start dating.

Part 6: The credit scenes

6-1Over New York

Mid-credits: Peter, as Spider-Man, swings across the New York sky with MJ in his arms.

6-2His identity revealed

Back on the ground, they see the news on a giant street screen. J. Jonah Jameson of TheDailyBugle.net is airing a video Beck left behind.

The doctored footage makes it look as if Spider-Man caused the London attack and killed Beck. Then Beck, in the video, reveals that Spider-Man is Peter Parker. Peter is stunned.

6-3The real Fury

After the credits: riding in a car, Fury and Hill change form. They're the Skrulls Talos and his wife Soren, disguised at the real Fury's request.

Talos phones the real Fury, who is in space aboard a Skrull ship, relaxing in front of a beach projection. Hearing the report, Fury gets up and tells the Skrulls to get back to work.

Summary

What Peter did

Grieving Tony, he tried to take a break from hero work and enjoy the trip (Part 1). Fury handed him Tony's glasses, but feeling unworthy, he gave them to Beck (Parts 2 and 3).

He saw through the lie thanks to MJ's device, but was overwhelmed by illusions in Berlin and hit by a train (Part 4). Encouraged by Happy, he built his own suit, trusted his spider sense to beat the drones in London, and took back the glasses (Parts 4 and 5).

Beck's lies

Beck was a former Stark employee whom Tony dismissed (Part 3). The monsters and the other-Earth story were lies he built with his team using holograms and drones. His goal: get Tony's E.D.I.T.H. glasses and pose as the world's greatest hero (Part 3).

Even after his death, the video he left framed Peter as a killer and revealed his identity to the world (Part 6).

Who Fury and Hill really were

The Fury and Hill in this film were the Skrulls Talos and Soren in disguise. The real Fury was in space (Part 6).

Who died

- Quentin Beck (Mysterio) (Part 5): in London, he's hit by a bullet from his own drone, fired on his order at Peter. Before dying, he had Riva take the drone data.
