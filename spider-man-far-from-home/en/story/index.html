Before you start: Who's who

Peter Parker (Spider-Man)

A New York high schooler. A spider bite gave him the power to cling to walls and a sense for danger he calls his "Peter tingle." He looked up to Tony Stark like a father.

Spoiler-free note

In Endgame, Tony died in the fight against Thanos. This film takes place shortly after.

Peter's people

MJ: Peter's classmate, sarcastic but very observant. Peter has a crush on her.

Ned: Peter's best friend, who knows his secret.

Aunt May: the aunt raising Peter.

Happy: Tony's former driver and bodyguard, who still looks out for Peter.

Flash: a classmate who teases Peter.

Spoiler-free note

Aunt May and Ned both know Peter is Spider-Man.

Nick Fury and Maria Hill

The former director of the peacekeeping agency S.H.I.E.L.D. and his deputy, the people who brought the Avengers together.

Spoiler-free note

Fury is the eyepatch-wearing man from Captain Marvel and The Avengers.

The Blip

Thanos's Snap wiped out half of all people, who came back five years later. This is called the Blip.

Spoiler-free note

Those who vanished didn't age, so some of Peter's former classmates who stayed behind are now five years older.

Part 1: A world without Tony

1-1A storm in Mexico

Nick Fury and Maria Hill investigate a strange storm in the Mexican village of Ixtenco. A giant creature of earth and rock rises out of it. Then a man in a green cape and a gold, fishbowl-like helmet appears and fights it.

Spoiler-free note

Fury and Hill don't yet know what this creature is.

1-2The memorial video

At Peter's school, a student news show plays a tribute to the fallen heroes: Iron Man, Captain America, Black Widow and Vision. It also covers the odd chaos of students returning from the Blip.

Spoiler-free note

Captain America didn't die; he retired. The student show lumps him in anyway, a small joke.

1-3Aunt May's charity event

Aunt May is running a fundraiser for people who lost their homes in the Blip. Peter helps, but a reporter asks if he'll be the next Iron Man, and he squirms. Happy is there too, and seems close to May.

Spoiler-free note

Many people who returned from the Blip found their homes gone, a problem also seen in other stories.

1-4Tony's shadow

Images of Tony are everywhere. Peter is grieving him and tired of being expected to be the next Iron Man.

Spoiler-free note

Tony and Peter's relationship is told in Homecoming.

1-5The trip plan

Peter's class is going to Europe for the summer. He plans to confess to MJ at the Eiffel Tower in Paris and leave the hero business behind. He even ignores Fury's call.

Spoiler-free note

Aunt May secretly packs Peter's Spider-Man suit in his bag.

1-6On the plane

On the flight, Peter tries to sit next to MJ but is outmaneuvered by classmate Brad, who grew up during the Blip and is friendly with MJ. Meanwhile Ned hits it off with Betty and they start dating.

Spoiler-free note

Brad didn't vanish in the Blip, so he used to be younger than Peter but is now in his grade.

Part 2: The monster in Venice

2-1A necklace in Venice

First stop: Venice, Italy. Peter buys MJ a glass necklace shaped like a black dahlia, since she once mentioned the flower.

Spoiler-free note

"Black Dahlia" is also the nickname of a famous real unsolved murder. MJ likes dark trivia like that.

2-2The water monster

A huge monster made of water rises from the canals. Without his suit, Peter tries to help using only his web-shooters. Then the green-caped man from Mexico arrives and destroys it. The crowd cheers for the new hero.

Spoiler-free note

The Italian crowd starts calling him "Mysterio."

2-3Fury's summons

That night, Fury shows up in Peter's hotel room and takes him to a secret base. The caped man is there.

Spoiler-free note

Fury is a little annoyed that Peter ignored his calls.

2-4Beck's story

The man is Quentin Beck. He says he comes from another Earth, destroyed by the Elementals, creatures of water, earth, fire and air, and that he lost his family. The last one, a fire creature, is coming next.

Spoiler-free note

"Another Earth" introduces the idea of the multiverse, said out loud here for the first time.

2-5Tony's glasses

Fury gives Peter a pair of glasses Tony left for him. Inside is an AI called E.D.I.T.H., with access to all of Stark's data and a fleet of armed drones.

Spoiler-free note

E.D.I.T.H. stands for "Even Dead, I'm The Hero," one of Tony's jokes.

2-6Drone mishap on the bus

On the bus, Peter accidentally orders E.D.I.T.H. to target Brad, who has a photo of Peter he plans to show MJ. Peter frantically calls it off.

Spoiler-free note

This shows how dangerous E.D.I.T.H. is: one careless word can launch a real strike.

Part 3: A night in Prague

3-1A change of plans

Fury has his agent Dimitri secretly reroute the trip to Prague, where he expects the fire creature. Peter is given a new black stealth suit.

Spoiler-free note

Dimitri is Fury's silent operative, who joins the trip as the bus driver.

3-2The fire monster

During a festival, the fire creature attacks Prague. Peter, in the black suit, and Beck fight it together, and Beck destroys it.

The news dubs the black-suited Peter a new hero, "Night Monkey."

Spoiler-free note

"Night Monkey" is the name Ned came up with to hide Peter's identity.

3-3Handing over the glasses

Talking with Beck at a bar, Peter decides he's just a kid and that an adult hero like Beck should have Tony's glasses. He gives them to him.

Spoiler-free note

Part of Peter wants to escape the weight of replacing Tony.

3-4Beck's secret

After Peter leaves, the bar's surroundings vanish and Beck's team celebrates. Beck is a former Stark engineer who left after Tony dismissed his work.

The monsters and the other-Earth story were all lies, faked with holograms and armed drones. Beck wanted Tony's glasses so he could be accepted as the world's greatest hero.

Spoiler-free note

Beck's hologram tech is what powered the memory-projection device Tony demonstrated to students in Civil War.

3-5MJ's find

MJ reveals she figured out Peter is Spider-Man. She shows him a small device she picked up at the battle site: a projector. Peter realizes Beck has been lying.

Spoiler-free note

MJ has been watching Peter closely all along. Peter admits the truth to her.

Part 4: The lie uncovered

4-1Illusions in Berlin

Peter goes to Berlin to warn Fury, but it's an illusion created by Beck. Inside it, Peter is shown nightmare after nightmare, including Tony's grave and a zombie-like Iron Man, and attacked.

Spoiler-free note

Beck is trying to find out who Peter has told.

4-2Hit by a train

Beck learns Peter told Ned and MJ. He tries to have his drones finish Peter off, and Peter is struck by an oncoming train.

Spoiler-free note

Beck's illusions are mixed with real attacks from his drones.

4-3Waking up in the Netherlands

Peter wakes up in a police cell in the Netherlands, carried there on the train. He calls Happy for help.

Spoiler-free note

For once, Peter properly asks an adult for help.

4-4A new suit

On Happy's jet, Peter builds himself a new red and black suit using Tony's technology. Happy tells him Tony struggled too, and chose Peter for a reason.

Spoiler-free note

Watching Peter work to music, Happy is reminded of Tony, who did the same.

Part 5: Showdown in London

5-1The biggest illusion

The trip ends in London. Beck unleashes his biggest illusion yet, all the creatures combined, while drones cause real destruction. He also means to get rid of the kids who know his secret.

Spoiler-free note

MJ, Ned and the others flee toward Tower Bridge, protected by Happy.

5-2Trusting the Peter tingle

Peter gets inside the creature but is fooled by illusions. He closes his eyes, trusts only his spider sense, and takes out the hidden drones one by one.

Spoiler-free note

Aunt May teased him about his "Peter tingle" early on, and here it pays off.

5-3Beck's end

Peter finds Beck and takes back the glasses. Cornered, Beck has his drones fire at Peter, but a bullet hits Beck himself. He tries one last illusion to trick Peter, but Peter sees through it. Beck dies.

Spoiler-free note

Beck meets his end inside the world of lies he built.

5-4MJ's answer

After the battle, Peter confesses to MJ and gives her the now-broken black dahlia necklace. MJ says she likes him too, and they start dating. Fury and his people arrive in London too.

Spoiler-free note

The necklace broke in the Berlin fight, but MJ is happy to take it anyway.

5-5Back in New York

Back in New York, Peter is welcomed by May and Happy at the airport. Later he takes MJ web-swinging over the city. She's terrified but laughing.

Spoiler-free note

By now, Peter is a hero in his own way rather than Tony's replacement.

Part 6: The credit scenes

6-1Mid-credits

On a giant screen, J. Jonah Jameson of the news site The Daily Bugle airs a video Beck left behind, edited to make it look like Spider-Man murdered him.

At the end of the video, Beck reveals that Spider-Man is Peter Parker. Peter is stunned.

Spoiler-free note

Jameson is played by J.K. Simmons, who played him in Sam Raimi's Spider-Man films. This leads straight into No Way Home.

6-2After the credits

Fury and Hill turn out to be the Skrull Talos and his wife Soren in disguise. The real Fury is relaxing aboard a Skrull ship in space.

Spoiler-free note

Skrulls are shape-shifting aliens. Talos met Fury in Captain Marvel.

Summary (spoilers through the end)

Who died

- Quentin Beck (Mysterio): in London, he orders his drones to fire at Peter and is hit by one of the bullets. Before dying he left a video framing Peter as a killer and revealing his identity.

Beck's lies

The Elementals and the other-Earth story were all lies made by Beck's team with holograms and drones. The goal: get Tony's glasses, E.D.I.T.H., and be hailed as the world's greatest hero.
