Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

It follows Civil War

Peter returns to everyday life in New York after the fight in Germany.

It skips the origin

There's no spider bite story. He's already active as a hero when it starts.

It's a high-school movie too

School, friends, a first crush and the homecoming dance are all part of the fun.

There are scenes during and after the credits

The last one is a cheeky bonus.

StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

1. Cleaning up after the battle

After the Battle of New York, Toomes's salvage crew loses its job to a new agency. They steal leftover alien tech and start building and selling weapons.

2. Peter can't wait

After Germany, Peter is eager for his next mission, but Tony and Happy never call. It's school and neighborhood patrols every day.

3. Alien weapons

Peter finds robbers using alien-tech weapons and starts tracing where they came from. His best friend Ned discovers his secret.

4. The Washington Monument

On the academic decathlon trip to Washington, his friends are trapped in an elevator about to fall. Peter unlocks the suit's full features and saves them.

5. The ferry disaster

Chasing the Vulture alone, Peter causes a ferry to split in two. Tony comes to help, and takes the suit away.

6. Homecoming night

Peter takes his crush Liz to the homecoming dance and discovers her father is the Vulture.

7. Fighting without the suit

In his homemade suit, Peter takes on the Vulture. Pinned under rubble, he lifts it himself, stops the Vulture's raid on a plane carrying Avengers gear, and saves his life.

8. Friendly neighborhood hero

Tony offers Peter a place in the Avengers, but he chooses to stay a neighborhood hero. Back home, Aunt May walks in on him in the suit.

Key terms

The Stark suit
The high-tech suit Tony made for Peter. At first most of its features are locked.
Karen
The AI inside the suit, which Peter names himself.
The Vulture
Toomes's flying persona, using wings built from alien tech.
Damage Control
The agency that cleans up after superhero battles. It cost Toomes his job.
Academic decathlon
The school quiz competition Peter and his friends compete in.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why no spider bite?

It's such a familiar story that the film skips it. Peter's already Spider-Man.

Why did Toomes turn to crime?

A new agency tied to Tony took over his cleanup job, and he told himself it was to provide for his family and crew.

Why did Tony take the suit away?

Peter kept ignoring him and doing dangerous things that put many people at risk.

Why did Peter turn down the Avengers?

He decided he wanted to be a neighborhood hero first, and to grow on his own.

Why didn't Toomes reveal Peter's identity?

It seems he felt he owed Peter for saving his life and protecting his daughter.
