Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why is Maya after Ronin?

Clint, as Ronin, killed her father. But someone set things up so she'd find out.

Why does Yelena go after Clint?

Valentina told her Clint was responsible for Natasha's death. In the end, she learns Natasha chose to sacrifice herself.

Is Kate's mother a villain?

Her mother, Eleanor, had been working with Kingpin for her company's sake.

Easy-to-miss details

Rogers: The Musical

A Broadway musical about Captain America appears in the first and final episodes.

Trick arrows

Clint's trick arrows, including one with Pym Particles that grows giant, get plenty of use.

Hearing aid

Years of fighting have damaged Clint's hearing, and he wears a hearing aid.
