Episode 1: Never Meet Your Heroes

1-12012: Kate and Hawkeye

2012, the day of the Battle of New York. A Chitauri attack blows open the wall of young Kate Bishop's penthouse home.

Through the hole, Kate sees Hawkeye (Clint) fighting with a bow. When he shoots down a nearby Chitauri, her life is saved.

But her father, Derek, is killed in the battle. After the funeral, Kate promises her mother, Eleanor, that she'll protect them both.

1-2Kate grows up, and the bell tower

For years after, Kate trains in archery, martial arts and fencing.

Present day (2024, just before Christmas). Now in college, Kate makes a bet with her friends Franny and Greer and tries to ring the bell of the campus clock tower, Stane Tower, with an arrow. The bell falls, part of the tower collapses, and campus security catches her.

1-3Mom's fiancé, Jack

At home, Eleanor has talked the college out of expelling Kate in exchange for paying for the repairs. As punishment, she shuts off Kate's credit cards.

Eleanor tells Kate to come to a charity auction gala that night, where Kate learns her mother is engaged to a wealthy man named Jack Duquesne. Kate doesn't like him.

1-4Clint's vacation

Meanwhile Clint, who now wears a hearing aid, is in New York for Christmas with his kids Lila, Cooper and Nathaniel.

They see Rogers: The Musical, a song-and-dance show about the Avengers' Battle of New York. The show makes Clint uneasy and reminds him painfully of Natasha. On the theater bathroom wall is the graffiti "Thanos was right." Clint leaves early.

1-5The gala and the secret auction

Stepping outside for air during the gala, Kate meets a one-eyed dog eating pizza (later named Lucky).

Back inside, Kate sees Jack and his uncle Armand III acting suspiciously and follows them. Below the gala is a secret black-market auction of items recovered from the ruined Avengers Compound. Jack and Armand bid on the sword of Ronin, the vigilante Clint once was.

1-6Kate in the Ronin suit

The Tracksuit Mafia, a gang in matching tracksuits, bursts in, takes the guests hostage and searches for a certain wristwatch.

In the chaos, Jack makes off with Ronin's sword. Kate puts on the Ronin suit on display to hide her face, fights off the gang and frees the hostages.

Outside, she saves the dog from oncoming traffic and takes him home to keep. The rescue ends up on the news.

1-7Ronin on the news, and Armand's death

At his hotel, Clint sees the news: someone is wearing his Ronin suit, and the past he left behind comes rushing back.

Kate leaves the dog at her apartment and, suspicious of Jack, goes to check out Armand's house. She finds Armand murdered inside and flees.

1-8Meeting Clint

Outside, the Tracksuit Mafia ambushes and corners Kate.

Clint shows up, takes out the gang and saves her. Then he pulls off the Ronin mask and comes face to face with Kate.

Episode 2: Hide and Seek

2-1Meeting her hero

Kate is thrilled to meet Clint and can't stop talking. She's idolized him and practiced archery since she was a kid.

Clint worries that, having worn the suit, she's now a target for the gang. She takes him back to her apartment, where they patch up their wounds.

2-2The apartment burns

The Tracksuit Mafia shows up and throws Molotov cocktails into the apartment, setting it on fire. Clint throws one back.

Clint, Kate and Lucky escape, but the Ronin suit is left behind. When Clint goes back for it, it's gone: a firefighter at the scene took it home.

2-3The aunt's apartment, and a promise

The two of them and Lucky hide out in the old apartment of Kate's aunt, who's away in Florida for the winter.

The next morning, Clint sends his kids home ahead of him, promising to be back by Christmas.

2-4Suspecting Jack

Clint walks Kate to her mother's firm, Bishop Security.

Kate suspects Jack killed Armand and tells her mother, who doesn't believe her. Learning Jack is an expert swordsman, Kate challenges him to a fencing match and grows even more suspicious.

2-5Getting the suit back from the LARPers

Clint tracks down Grills, the firefighter who took the suit. Grills plays in a live-action role-playing (LARP) group in Central Park, where people dress up and act out battles.

Clint joins the game, wins the fight, and gets the Ronin suit back.

2-6Clint lets himself get caught

To find out who's hunting Ronin, Clint deliberately lets the Tracksuit Mafia capture him and drive him away.

Unable to reach him, Kate traces his phone and heads off to rescue him.

2-7The gang's hideout

Clint is taken to the gang's hideout in an abandoned toy store and tied to a toy pony ride.

Kate arrives to help but is caught too. The gang tells their leader, Maya Lopez, that they have both of them. Finally, Maya appears.

Episode 3: Echoes

3-1Maya's childhood

2007. Maya, a deaf girl, wants to attend a school for the deaf. Her father, William, signs with her and raises her with love.

Maya learns karate at a dojo, training alongside her childhood friend Kazi. Her father's associate, her "uncle," dotes on her.

3-2Ronin kills her father

Years later, after the Blip, Ronin attacks the Tracksuit Mafia's hideout, a used-car shop.

Ronin cuts through the gang and kills Maya's father, William, while Maya watches. She vows revenge on Ronin.

3-3Maya's interrogation

Back in the present, Maya demands to know from the bound Clint and Kate who Ronin is and where he is.

Clint says Black Widow (Natasha) killed Ronin. Maya knows Natasha is dead too and doesn't buy it. Believing Kate is Ronin, she goes after her.

3-4The broken hearing aid

Insisting Kate isn't Ronin, Clint slips his bonds and fights the gang. In the fight, Maya crushes his hearing aid underfoot.

Clint frees Kate, and the two escape the hideout.

3-5The trick-arrow car chase

They steal a car and flee, with Maya and Kazi in pursuit. Clint drives while Kate fires his trick arrows from the passenger seat.

A putty arrow covers Kazi's windshield, a grappling arrow pulls a row of Christmas trees into a pursuing car, and an acid arrow drops a traffic light.

Cornered on a traffic-jammed bridge, they leap off on a rope arrow and land on the roof of a train passing below.

3-6Kazi's warning

As the gang moves its hideout, Kazi warns Maya that more trouble will cause problems with her "uncle."

3-7Clint can't hear

Without his hearing aid, Clint can barely hear. When his son Nathaniel calls, Kate writes down what he says so they can talk.

Kate takes Clint and Lucky to an audiologist in Chinatown who fixes the hearing aid.

3-8Breakfast, and sneaking into Mom's place

Over breakfast at a deli, Kate tells Clint he's a role model for kids and should put himself out there more.

To investigate the gang and Jack, they sneak into Eleanor's penthouse. Kate tries to search Bishop Security's criminal database on her mother's laptop, but gets locked out.

Meanwhile, in another room, Jack points Ronin's sword at Clint.

Episode 4: Partners, Am I Right?

4-1Clint the Avenger

Jack and Eleanor recognize Clint as an Avenger, and the standoff ends. The four of them sit down and talk.

Eleanor asks Clint to keep Kate out of danger. Clint quietly takes back Ronin's sword, which Jack had taken.

Kate stays behind at her mother's. Afterward, Eleanor phones someone and fills them in.

4-2Laura helps

Clint talks on the phone with his wife, Laura, at home. With her help, he learns Jack is CEO of Sloan Limited, a shell company that launders money for the Tracksuit Mafia.

4-3A Christmas for two

That night, Kate and Lucky come back to the aunt's apartment with decorations, movies and food, and throw a little Christmas party for Clint, who's away from his family.

They bond over games and talk.

4-4Talking about Natasha, and who Ronin was

Clint tells Kate about his memories of Natasha.

Listening, Kate realizes Clint lost his family in the Blip and works out that he was Ronin. Understanding the pain he went through, she doesn't hold it against him.

4-5A request to Kazi, and getting the arrows back

The next day, Clint finds Maya's lieutenant, Kazi, and asks him to talk Maya out of her revenge on Ronin. Kazi refuses.

Meanwhile, at Clint's request, Kate takes Lucky to the LARPers and asks them to help get Clint's arrows back. Clint picks up the arrows at Grills's apartment.

4-6The watch and Maya's apartment

Laura calls: the stolen watch is sending out a signal from an apartment building.

Clint keeps watch from a neighboring rooftop while Kate sneaks inside. It's Maya's apartment. Kate finds the watch, along with Maya's notes on Clint and his family.

4-7Rooftop melee

Maya comes home and attacks Kate, and they fight. At the same time, on the roof, a masked assassin in black ambushes Clint.

In the four-way brawl, Maya is hurt by Kate and pulls back. Clint unmasks his attacker: it's Natasha's sister, Yelena Belova. She escapes.

Deciding he can't keep putting Kate in danger, Clint ends their partnership.

Episode 5: Ronin

5-1Yelena in 2018

2018. Yelena and her fellow former Widow Sonya are still freeing brainwashed Black Widows. They break into the home of Anna, another ex-Widow, only to find she's already free.

They apologize, and Yelena steps into the bathroom, where she vanishes in the Blip.

5-2Yelena returns five years later

2023. Yelena reappears in the same bathroom. The house has changed and five years have gone by. Reunited with a stunned Anna, she wants to find her sister Natasha.

But she learns Natasha is already dead.

5-3Jack is arrested

Present day. Kate tells Eleanor that Jack runs a company that handles the gang's money. Eleanor calls the police, and Jack is arrested.

5-4Yelena at Kate's place

Kate returns to her burned-out apartment to find Yelena waiting for her, helping herself to mac and cheese.

Yelena explains she's Natasha's sister and came to New York to kill Clint, whom she's been told is to blame for Natasha's death. Kate defends him.

5-5Clint at the memorial

The injured Clint gets patched up at Grills's apartment.

Then he visits the Avengers memorial to the Battle of New York and speaks to Natasha. To protect his family, he decides to become Ronin one more time and settle things with Maya.

5-6Ronin vs. Maya

Clint summons the gang with a note tied to an arrow and waits at the used-car shop where Maya's father died. In the Ronin suit, he takes down the gang members one by one and faces Maya alone.

Clint unmasks and tells her to drop her revenge and stay away from his family. He reveals that the tip that led Ronin to the hideout that night came from someone working for Maya's boss. Maya doesn't believe him and attacks again.

Kate swoops in with arrows, and Clint gets away.

5-7Maya's suspicion

Maya remembers Kazi wasn't there the night her father was killed. When she asks about that night, he dodges the question, and she begins to suspect him.

5-8Yelena's message, and Kingpin

The next day, Yelena texts Kate: the one who hired her to kill Clint is Kate's mother, Eleanor. The image she sends shows Eleanor with a huge man.

Clint knows him: Maya's "uncle," Wilson Fisk, the Kingpin who secretly runs New York's underworld.

Episode 6: So This Is Christmas?

6-1Footage of Mom's secret

Eleanor meets Fisk and tells him she wants out. Fisk warns her that's not going to happen.

Clint and Kate watch a recording of the meeting sent by Yelena and learn that Eleanor, not Jack, killed Armand, and that she set Jack up to take the fall for Sloan Limited. Kate is shaken.

The two make trick arrows to prepare for the night ahead.

6-2Maya and Fisk

Christmas Eve. Fisk summons Maya. She plays along and asks for a few days off, and he agrees.

6-3The party at Rockefeller Center

That night, Eleanor's company throws its Christmas party at Rockefeller Center, and Clint and Kate go.

Kate confronts her mother, who admits that Kate's late father, Derek, owed money to Fisk, which forced her to work with him.

Kate spots Yelena at the party and fights her to keep her away from Clint, but Yelena slips away.

6-4Kazi takes aim, and the evacuation

On Fisk's orders, Kazi aims a rifle at Eleanor. When Clint spots him, Kazi opens fire on Clint instead.

Clint calls on Grills and the LARPers, and on Jack, to get the party guests out. Jack joins the fight with a sword.

6-5Battle beneath the Christmas tree

Around the skating rink and the giant Christmas tree, Clint and Kate battle the Tracksuit Mafia with a whole range of trick arrows, including an electromagnetic arrow that yanks away the gang's guns.

When Clint gets stuck in the tree, Kate fells it with an acid arrow to bring him down. Together they beat the gang.

6-6Maya and Kazi

Maya goes to Kazi and asks him to leave everything behind and start over with her. He refuses, and they struggle. In the struggle, Kazi is killed.

6-7Yelena and Clint

After Kate goes to look for her mother, Yelena confronts Clint on the ice rink and demands the truth about how Natasha died.

Clint says Natasha gave her life to save the universe. Yelena can't accept it, and they fight.

Clint whistles the secret signal that only Natasha and Yelena shared as girls. Realizing how close he and Natasha were, Yelena spares him and walks away.

6-8Kate vs. Kingpin

Meanwhile, Fisk sets out to deal with the fleeing Eleanor himself. Kate steps in his way.

Fisk is enormously strong, shrugs off arrows and throws her around. But she keeps firing trick arrows, pinning him with a grappling arrow, and finally knocks him out with explosive arrows.

The police arrive, and Eleanor is arrested for murdering Armand. Kate knows her mother did it for her, but can't forgive her.

6-9Maya and Fisk in the alley

Fisk limps away, but Maya is waiting for him in an alley. He tries to soothe her with gentle words, but she is sure he had her father killed and raises her gun. A gunshot rings out.

Clint tells Kate that hardly anyone survives a fight with Kingpin, and praises her courage.

6-10A family Christmas

Christmas Day. Clint brings Kate and Lucky home to Iowa. The kids tear into their presents, and Kate and Lucky are welcomed into the family.

Clint gives the recovered watch back to Laura: it's a memento from her days at S.H.I.E.L.D.

Outside, Clint and Kate burn the Ronin suit and talk about a hero name for Kate.

6-11After the credits

A full number from Rogers: The Musical, the show Clint saw in Episode 1, plays.

Summary (spoilers through the finale)

Who was behind it all

- Wilson Fisk (Kingpin): the boss secretly running New York's underworld. While raising Maya as her "uncle," he had a tip passed to Ronin through an informant so that her father, William, would be killed (revealed in Ep. 5).

- Kazi: Maya's childhood friend and lieutenant. He wasn't there the night her father died and was working for Fisk.

- Eleanor: Kate's mother. Her husband Derek owed Fisk money, so she worked with Fisk. When she tried to get out, she killed Armand and framed Jack for that and for Sloan Limited (revealed in Ep. 6).

- Yelena: told Clint was to blame for Natasha's death, she was hired by Eleanor to kill him.

Who died (in order)

- Derek Bishop (Ep. 1, 2012): Kate's father, killed in the Battle of New York.

- Armand Duquesne III (Ep. 1): Jack's uncle. Kate finds him murdered at home. The killer is Eleanor (revealed in Ep. 6).

- William Lopez (Ep. 3 flashback): Maya's father, killed by Ronin (Clint), who raided the hideout on a tip Fisk planted.

- Kazi (Ep. 6): killed in a struggle with Maya after she learns the truth about her father's death.

- Fisk (Ep. 6): Maya points a gun at him in an alley and a shot rings out (what happens next isn't shown).

My takeOpinion

A pleasantly light series that makes you want to watch it at Christmas. Powerless, worn-out Clint paired with energetic Kate is a good balance.
