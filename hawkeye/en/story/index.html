Episode 1: Never Meet Your Heroes

How Kate began

During the 2012 Battle of New York, young Kate Bishop's home is wrecked and her father dies. Seeing Hawkeye in action, she takes up archery.

Clint's vacation

Now, Clint is in New York with his kids for Christmas. At a Captain America musical, he's reminded of Natasha and feels down.

The auction

Kate, now in college, attends a gala with her mother Eleanor and Eleanor's fiancé, Jack. An underground auction is selling the Ronin suit. When a gang crashes it, Kate dons the suit and flees.

Ronin on the news

Seeing Ronin on the news, Clint goes looking for Kate to get the suit back.

Episodes 2–3: The gang and Maya

The meeting

Clint saves Kate from the gang. Kate is thrilled to meet her hero.

The LARPers

The suit has ended up with live-action role-players. Clint joins their game to get it back.

Maya Lopez

The gang's leader, Maya Lopez, is deaf and a formidable fighter. Ronin killed her father, and she wants revenge.

The car chase

Chased through the streets, Clint and Kate fight back with trick arrows.

Episode 4: Partners, Am I Right?

Christmas preparations

Kate decorates for Christmas to cheer up Clint, who's away from his family. A mentor-and-student bond forms.

Remembering Natasha

Clint talks to Kate about Natasha.

The assassin in black

During a rooftop fight, an assassin in black attacks Clint. It's Yelena, Natasha's sister.

Episode 5: Ronin

Kate and Yelena

Yelena shows up at Kate's apartment and they talk over mac and cheese. Yelena has been told Clint is responsible for Natasha's death.

Her mother's secret

Kate learns her mother, Eleanor, has been working with the crime boss Kingpin.

Episode 6: So This Is Christmas?

Rockefeller Center

At a Christmas party, the gang, Yelena and Kingpin all converge for a battle.

Kate vs. Kingpin

Kate takes on the enormously strong Kingpin and fights back with a Pym Particle arrow.

Yelena and Clint

On the ice rink, Yelena fights Clint. He tells her Natasha chose to sacrifice herself, and shares a signal only the sisters knew. Yelena stops.

Maya's choice

Maya realizes Kingpin has been manipulating her and turns her gun on him.

A family Christmas

Clint brings Kate home to celebrate Christmas with his family. The Ronin suit is burned.

After the credits

A full number from the Captain America musical plays.
