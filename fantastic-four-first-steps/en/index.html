Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

A different world

It's set on Earth-828, not the main MCU Earth (Earth-616).

No homework needed

You don't need to know other MCU films.

A retro future

The world looks like the future as imagined in the 1960s.

A family story

They're a team, but above all a family.

Watch through the credits

A mid-credits scene sets up what's next, and there's another at the end.

Key terms

Cosmic rays
The radiation the four were exposed to in space, which gave them their powers.
Galactus
A cosmic giant who devours entire planets.
The Silver Surfer
Galactus's herald, a silver being who flies through space on a surfboard.
H.E.R.B.I.E.
The friendly robot who helps around the family's home.
The Baxter Building
The tower where the family lives and Reed works.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why does Galactus want the baby?

The unborn child already carries tremendous cosmic power. Galactus wants to pass on his endless hunger to him.

Why does the Silver Surfer serve Galactus?

Shalla-Bal agreed to become his herald in exchange for sparing her own world.

How does it connect to the rest of the MCU?

It takes place in a separate universe, but the mid-credits scene is widely seen as setting up Avengers: Doomsday.

Easy-to-miss details

Retro design

Cars, gadgets and TVs all look like a 1960s vision of the future.

A kids' show

There's a children's TV show about the family.

Johnny and the signals

Johnny works to decode mysterious signals from space.
