Part 1: Heroes of Earth-828

Good news

In their Baxter Building home, Sue Storm tells her husband Reed Richards she's pregnant. They've hoped for a child for a long time.

How it began

A TV special recounts their story. Four years ago, cosmic rays struck them in space. Reed can stretch, Sue can turn invisible and make force fields, Johnny can burst into flame and fly, and Ben became a being of rock.

Beloved heroes

The four are beloved around the world for protecting the city. Even their old foe, the Mole Man, now cooperates with them.

Part 2: The silver herald

A visitor from the sky

One day a silver herald appears over New York and announces that Earth will soon be devoured by Galactus.

Into space

The four fly into space to stop Galactus, with Sue pregnant.

Meeting Galactus

They meet the colossal Galactus, who offers to spare Earth if they give him their unborn child.

A birth in space

Escaping Galactus, Sue gives birth aboard the ship to a boy, Franklin.

Part 3: Earth's anger

The world reacts

Back on Earth, people learn the family refused the deal and turn angry and afraid.

Sue speaks

Sue addresses the public: they won't give up their child, but everyone can face this together.

Moving the planet

Reed devises a plan to move the entire planet. When it fails, they switch to sending Galactus far away instead.

Shalla-Bal

Johnny talks with the herald, Shalla-Bal, and learns she began serving Galactus to save her own world.

Part 4: The final battle

Galactus in New York

Galactus lands on Earth and marches through New York toward Franklin.

A family fight

Reed, Johnny, Ben and even the Mole Man team up to lure Galactus to the teleporter.

Sue's power

Sue pushes Galactus into the device with all her strength. She collapses, but baby Franklin's power brings her back.

Shalla-Bal's choice

Shalla-Bal disappears into deep space along with Galactus.

Part 5: Afterward

Family life

Earth is saved, and the family begins a new life with Franklin.

Mid-credits

Some time later, Sue finds a masked man beside young Franklin, widely understood to be Doctor Doom.

After the credits

A short bonus scene closes the film.
