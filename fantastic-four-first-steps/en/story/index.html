Part 1: Heroes, four years on

1-1Good news

The setting is New York in the 1960s, on Earth-828, a world separate from the main MCU. In their home in the Baxter Building, Sue Storm stares at a pregnancy test.

Her husband Reed Richards is rummaging through the cabinets for medicine. When Sue shows him the result, Reed is stunned. They have wanted a child for a long time.

1-2Four years on TV

To mark the team's fourth anniversary, Ted Gilbert's TV show looks back on their story.

Four years earlier, the four went into space as astronauts and were hit by cosmic rays. Back on Earth, Reed could stretch like rubber, Sue could turn invisible and create force fields, Johnny could burst into flame and fly, and Ben had become a super-strong being of rock.

As the Fantastic Four, they have fought villains attacking the city. The show includes their battle with a giant monster sent by the Mole Man (Harvey Elder), ruler of the underground realm of Subterranea.

1-3Beloved heroes

The four are celebrities around the world. Reed's inventions have pushed technology forward, and Sue's diplomacy through the Future Foundation has led much of the world to disarm and live in peace.

Even their old enemy the Mole Man now keeps a truce with them.

1-4Sunday dinner

The four always have dinner together on Sunday. This time Sue and Reed show up late.

Waiting for them are Sue's brother Johnny Storm and Reed's best friend Ben Grimm. Sue and Reed share their big news: they're having a baby.

1-5The world awaits the baby

When news of the baby gets out, the whole world looks forward to the birth and wonders whether the child will have powers too.

Reed can't stop worrying about the baby. He builds his own machine and runs test after test on Sue and the baby. Sue is frustrated that Reed always imagines the worst.

Part 2: The silver herald

2-1A herald over Times Square

Months later, a figure with a shining silver body, riding a surfboard-like craft, appears above Times Square: the Silver Surfer.

She announces that Galactus, a being who devours planets, has marked this world for destruction: "Your planet is now marked for death."

2-2The vanished planets

Reed checks whether the Surfer's warning is true. Going through records of space, he finds that other planets have disappeared. Earth really is in danger.

2-3Deciding to go

The four decide to go into space and stop Galactus before he reaches Earth. Sue, though pregnant, goes with them.

It will be their first trip back into space since the flight that gave them their powers.

2-4The Excelsior launches

The four board their spaceship, the Excelsior. It launches from a platform that juts out from the Baxter Building over the river.

The launch is shown at length before the ship lifts off into space.

2-5Faster than light

In space, the four track the energy trail the Silver Surfer leaves behind. Using Reed's faster-than-light drive, they jump far across the galaxy.

Part 3: Galactus's ship

3-1A planet destroyed

The four arrive at a planet (LHS-275) just as Galactus's enormous ship destroys it.

3-2Before Galactus

The four are captured and brought before Galactus, a towering cosmic giant.

3-3An endless hunger

Galactus explains that he has a hunger that can never be satisfied, and that it has driven him to devour planets for eons.

3-4A deal for the baby

Galactus senses immense cosmic power in Sue's unborn child. The child could take on his hunger and set him free of it.

He offers to spare Earth in exchange for the baby, and uses his power to send Sue into labor.

3-5Refusing and escaping

The four refuse the offer, break out of Galactus's ship and flee in the Excelsior. The Silver Surfer chases them.

3-6The neutron star

The four flee past a neutron star. Its powerful gravity holds back the Silver Surfer, and they use that same gravity to slingshot the ship toward Earth.

But the gravity destroys their faster-than-light drive, so they must make the rest of the trip home at normal speed.

3-7A birth in space

On the way back to Earth, in zero gravity aboard the ship, Sue gives birth to a boy, Franklin.

Part 4: Earth turns against them

4-1Home after a month

A month later, the four return to Earth with baby Franklin.

4-2The press conference

Reed holds a press conference and tells everything that happened in space: that Galactus offered to spare Earth in exchange for Franklin, and that they refused.

4-3Public outrage

People are outraged that the family chose one child over the billions of people on Earth. Calls to hand Franklin over to Galactus spread around the world.

Protesters gather around the Baxter Building.

4-4Reed and Sue clash

When Reed says he can see the protesters' side, Sue snaps. She storms off to another room to hold Franklin.

Reed can't stop thinking through every worst case, and Sue is hurt that he turns that way of thinking on their own family.

4-5Johnny and the alien language

Johnny works to decode the Silver Surfer's native language, using his encounters with her and deep-space transmissions from her planet that Reed had picked up.

Among the recordings are transmissions from planets Galactus destroyed.

4-6Sue speaks

As Galactus draws near and the protests grow, Sue carries Franklin out to the crowd in front of the Baxter Building.

She tells them they will not sacrifice their child, but they will not give up on everyone else on Earth either. Her words win back the public's trust.

4-7A plan to move the planet

Moved by Sue's words, Reed turns to the teleportation system he has been building. The plan: build huge teleportation bridges around the world and move the entire Earth to another solar system, out of Galactus's reach.

4-8The world comes together

Through the Future Foundation, Sue rallies the world's nations. They build the bridges and save up energy to power them.

Part 5: The battle of Times Square

5-1The Surfer destroys the bridges

Just as the bridges are being switched on, the Silver Surfer returns and destroys them one after another. The plan to move Earth fails.

5-2Johnny saves the last bridge

Johnny stops the Silver Surfer from destroying the last bridge, in Times Square.

He tells her what he learned from the transmissions: her name is Shalla-Bal, and she became Galactus's herald so that he would spare her world, Zenn-La.

5-3Shalla-Bal wavers

Johnny plays her transmissions from the worlds she helped destroy. Shalla-Bal shows remorse, but refuses to help and flies away.

5-4Franklin as bait

Only one bridge is left. The team plans to use Franklin, the child Galactus wants, as bait to lure him to the Times Square bridge and teleport him far away.

5-5Evacuation underground

Sue negotiates with the Mole Man (Harvey Elder) to shelter New York's people in his underground realm, Subterranea.

5-6Galactus arrives

Galactus lands on Earth and strides through New York. The four fight with everything they have, but Galactus avoids the trap and seizes Franklin.

5-7Sue's full power

Sue pours all her power into a force field and pushes Galactus toward the portal. Meanwhile, Reed stretches around Galactus and rescues Franklin.

5-8Johnny and Shalla-Bal

To give Galactus the final push into the portal, Johnny prepares to sacrifice himself. But Shalla-Bal returns and stops him.

She slams into Galactus herself and drives him into the portal, which closes behind them both. Galactus and Shalla-Bal are sent somewhere far from Earth.

5-9Sue dies, and returns

Having spent all her strength, Sue collapses in the street, dead. Reed, Johnny and Ben rush to her side.

Then baby Franklin uses his power for the first time and brings his mother back to life.

Part 6: Afterward

6-1The fifth anniversary

Earth is saved. As the world celebrates the team's fifth anniversary, the four head off on a new mission.

6-2Mid-credits

Four years later, at home in the Baxter Building, Sue is reading to the now-older Franklin. She goes to get a different book, and when she comes back, a man in a green cloak holding a metal mask is with Franklin.

He isn't named on screen, but the filmmakers identify him as Doctor Doom (played by Robert Downey Jr.).

6-3After the credits

Finally, an old-fashioned cartoon of the Fantastic Four plays on a vintage TV: an animated show made within their world, featuring a lineup of classic comic-book villains.

Summary (spoilers through the ending)

Galactus and Franklin: how it plays out

The Silver Surfer announces Earth's end (Part 2). In space, Galactus offers to spare Earth for Franklin, and the four refuse (Part 3).

The public turns on them, then trusts them again after Sue's speech. Reed's plan to move Earth through teleportation bridges fails when the Surfer destroys the bridges (Parts 4–5).

Finally, using Franklin as bait, they lure Galactus to Times Square, and Sue's force field and Shalla-Bal's final push send him away through the last bridge (Part 5).

Shalla-Bal (the Silver Surfer)

She became Galactus's herald to save her home world, Zenn-La (revealed by Johnny in Part 5). She announced Earth's end (Part 2), chased the four (Part 3) and destroyed the teleportation bridges (Part 5).

Hearing the worlds she helped destroy, she feels remorse but leaves at first. In the end she returns, takes Johnny's place, and pushes Galactus into the portal, vanishing with him (Part 5).

Who died, and what vanished

- A planet (LHS-275) (Part 3): destroyed by Galactus's ship just as the four arrive.

- Sue Storm (Part 5): she dies after spending all her power on the force field that pushes Galactus toward the portal. Franklin's power brings her back moments later.

- Galactus and Shalla-Bal (Part 5): not killed, but sent through the last bridge to somewhere far from Earth.

My takeOpinion

A story about one family that you can enjoy without knowing any other title. The bright 60s-style world looks unlike anything else in the MCU, which is refreshing.
