Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Which Vision is this?

The White Vision rebuilt by the government in WandaVision, who received the memories of the Vision Wanda created.

How is Tommy alive?

When Wanda's town vanished, his soul seems to have ended up in another boy's body. More may be revealed in the remaining episodes.

Why is Ultron inside Vision?

Both were born from the Mind Stone's power, and Ultron has been hiding in Vision's mind.

Easy-to-miss details

Tony's AIs in person

F.R.I.D.A.Y., E.D.I.T.H., J.A.R.V.I.S. and more are played by human actors.

The ship of Theseus

Episode 2's title comes from the famous question of whether a ship with every part replaced is still the same ship, also discussed in WandaVision.
