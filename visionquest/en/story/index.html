Episode 1: No Strings (Oct. 13)

The man at the pub

Vision lives as a human named Sherwood Ellis and works at a pub he once visited with Wanda. The owner, Fiona, looks out for him.

The bounty hunter

Men working for the bounty hunter Paladin attack the pub, and Fiona is killed.

A son alive

In the final moments, it's revealed that Paladin has found Tommy, Wanda and Vision's son.

Episode 2: Theseus (Oct. 13)

A year earlier

A year earlier, Ultron, inside Vision's mind, talks to him about the ship of Theseus.

A prison trap

Paladin holds Tommy in a prison hospital to lure Vision in, armed with special bullets that can hurt him.

Escape with Tommy

After some hesitation, Vision goes after Tommy and is badly wounded. Tommy drives them away from Paladin in a stolen car.

Episode 3: Reboot (Oct. 20)

A night at the motel

Badly hurt, Vision hides out with Tommy in a motel. The only people who could fix him, like Bruce Banner and Tony Stark, are out of reach.

The reboot

They use Tommy's super-speed to reboot Vision's body. It works, but the signal draws the attention of a mysterious killer called Butcherbird.

Ultron

At the end, Ultron takes control of Vision's body.

Episode 4: Malware (Oct. 20)

A trail of victims

Butcherbird's victims are linked to the company that built the prototypes behind Tony's AIs, pointing to its CEO, Katerina Wells.

The gala

At a big gala that night, the tension builds. Butcherbird is revealed to be Jocasta, a synthetic being.

Tommy and Ultron

Vision, under Ultron's control, leaves the wrecked gala with Tommy.

Episode 5: Firewall (Oct. 27)

Locked in the attic

Ultron locks Vision and the AIs in the attic of his mind.

Ultron and Tommy

Ultron takes Tommy to a pub to talk. Tommy calls him a monster.

Tipping off Paladin

Ultron feeds information to Paladin, who abducts Tommy.

To Madripoor

Back in control, Vision is on a plane to Madripoor to save Tommy.

Episode 6: Deadlock (Oct. 27)

Jocasta's past

Jocasta reveals that losing her father to a brain disease as a child is what drew her to AI.

Ultron and Jocasta

Ultron enters Jocasta's mind and seeks an alliance with her.

Tommy in Madripoor

Paladin threatens Tommy with an explosive in his arm, but Tommy realizes it's a toy.

Father and son

Vision tells Tommy what happened in WandaVision, and Tommy realizes Vision is his father.

E.D.I.T.H.'s persuasion

E.D.I.T.H. hacks Paladin's AI and convinces her to abandon him.
