Episode 1: No Strings (Oct. 13)

1-1Vision's story so far, and a pub in Edinburgh

Vision recounts his history: born during the fight against Ultron, he met Wanda and was killed by Thanos. The agency S.W.O.R.D. then rebuilt his body in white, and in Wanda's town he received his old memories and flew away.

Now he wears a human face, goes by Sherwood Ellis, and works at the Old Selkie, a pub in Edinburgh, Scotland, that he and Wanda used to visit. A heart with a W and a V is still carved into one of the tables.

The owner, Fiona, looks out for him. When she asks about Wanda, he says only that she was crushed under a mountain. He turns down her invitation to Sunday dinner, saying he isn't good with people.

1-2The house in his mind and the AIs

At night Vision enters a mansion inside his own mind, the Mindspace, where the AIs Tony Stark built live in human form.

J.A.R.V.I.S. handles security, F.R.I.D.A.Y. coaches him on dealing with people, E.D.I.T.H. covers fun and weapons, and D.U.M.-E. and U. do the repairs. Together they help Vision act more human.

J.A.R.V.I.S. warns that data seems to be leaking somewhere, but Vision brushes it off.

1-3The screening room, and Ultron in the attic

In the mansion's screening room, Vision rewatches his former self's memories like films, trying to understand feelings. But he refuses to watch the memories of the Vision who lived in Wanda's magical town of Westview, believing they weren't real.

Egged on by Ultron, D.U.M.-E. and U. play footage of his sons in Westview anyway, and Vision is furious.

Ultron is locked in the mansion's attic, where he spends his time online and painting. Vision orders him to stay in human form there.

Ultron says Vision is hiding from real feeling behind AIs that do as they're told. He warns that if you watch the world, the world starts watching back, and he releases a carrier pigeon from the attic.

1-4The attack on the pub and Fiona's death

The next night a bullet comes through the pub window and hits Fiona in the chest, killing her on the spot. It was fired by men working for Paladin, a bounty hunter sent to capture Vision; Fiona was caught in the crossfire.

Armed men storm the pub. Vision takes them down and escapes through the skylight.

Paladin is talking to his AI assistant, the Handler, when the Client cuts in with a disguised voice. Using S.W.O.R.D. encryption, the Client is unhappy that Vision got away. Paladin promises to bring him in.

1-5Ultron's betrayal

In the Mindspace, J.A.R.V.I.S. urges Vision to take time to grieve for Fiona, but Vision insists they weren't that close.

The AIs work out that the leak was Ultron. He had found a bounty on Vision on the dark web and told Paladin where to find him.

Confronted, Ultron admits it freely, saying he did it to push Vision out into the world.

As punishment, Vision strips the attic of its TVs, painting supplies and couch, leaving Ultron in an empty room.

1-6Tommy and Lisa in London

Paladin and the Handler trace Vision's alias back to the Westview incident. Digging through S.W.O.R.D. records, they find a boy linked to the name of Vision's son, Tommy.

In London, 18-year-old Thomas Shepherd (Tommy) gets by running cons with his partner Lisa, a skilled pickpocket.

A news report about the Vision sighting plays on a pub TV. Memories he can't explain flood Tommy's head and he struggles to breathe. Their mark realizes he's been conned and chases them; they get away, and Lisa calms Tommy down.

1-7Paladin's message

Paladin ambushes Tommy and Lisa in an alley and takes Tommy.

He then sends a message straight into Vision's mind: he has Vision's son.

With it comes footage of Tommy being interviewed by police, saying he remembers a mother who was called Wanda.

Episode 2: Theseus (Oct. 13)

2-1A year earlier: the pool

A year earlier, Tommy is being questioned by police, suspected of hurting other kids at a swimming pool.

Frightened, he says he couldn't control it. Held underwater and nearly drowning, he felt something like a bolt of lightning, as if someone else had moved into his head.

Ever since, he has had memories of living as Wanda and Vision's son.

2-2Escape from the transport bus

On Christmas Day, Tommy is put on a transport bus bound for Saint Mabyn's Psychiatric Sanitarium. Lisa sits down beside him. He tells her he lost his mother and grew up in foster care.

Another passenger panics about where they're headed, and the guard subdues him with a stun weapon. When Tommy tries to shield him, the guard shocks Tommy too. That awakens Tommy's power, and the charge rebounds and drops the guard.

Lisa lifts the guard's keys. Tommy vibrates the bolts off the back door and the two jump out, while the other passengers sing Christmas carols to cover the noise.

For the year since, the two have lived together in London.

2-3The present: the name Paladin

In the present, Vision is hiding in an old electronics shop.

The AIs learn that Paladin is not one man's name but a title handed down through generations of bounty hunters, who never break a contract once it's accepted.

J.A.R.V.I.S. finds the record of the pool incident. In the footage Paladin sent, Tommy describes a mother, Wanda, who sang and read to him, and a father with a red face and kind eyes. He even repeats what Vision once told Wanda about grief and love.

Vision still refuses to accept the boy as his son.

2-4Face to face with Paladin

Vision meets Paladin, who uses Tommy as a hostage and demands his surrender.

Vision is unmoved. He says he feels nothing and is not the Vision who came before, and he leaves without making a deal.

2-5Ultron and the ship of Theseus

In the mansion, Vision talks with Ultron. He has the former Vision's memories but not his feelings; the boy is that Vision's son, he says, and all he himself has is an inherited duty.

They turn to the ship of Theseus, the old question of whether a ship with every part replaced is still the same ship. Ultron dismisses the idea and tells Vision that when someone has taken your son, you don't be the ship, you be the king.

J.A.R.V.I.S. tries to stop him, pointing to the painting of a burning figure in the sitting room, a lesson that strong emotion can be fatal. Vision decides to go after Tommy anyway.

2-6The trap at Saint Mabyn's

Vision walks into Saint Mabyn's through the front door. Paladin's men have killed the real staff, and armed mercenaries are waiting inside. With the AIs backing him up, Vision fights his way through.

He finds Tommy strapped to a chair in a padded room. Terrified, Tommy says Vision isn't real. Vision calms him and frees him.

Then the door locks. The room is an EMP cage built to scramble machines, and Vision weakens and can barely use his powers. Together they break the door down and get out.

2-7Adamantium rounds, and J.A.R.V.I.S.'s end

Paladin is waiting outside, his gun loaded with rounds of adamantium, a metal that can pierce even Vision's body. Vision is shot and badly wounded.

He tells Tommy to leave him and run. Tommy resists, saying Vision is his something, but in the end he runs.

Vision's power is nearly gone, and the AIs in his mind are about to fade with him. J.A.R.V.I.S. deletes his own program to reroute its power to Vision and the others. His last words are "Family is forever, and it's worth the fight." And he is gone.

Then Tommy comes back at the wheel of an old car. He gets Vision inside and they escape Paladin. Beside Vision in the car, Ultron remarks that the boy's stubbornness is just like his father's.

Episode 3: Reboot (Oct. 20)

3-1The Butcherbird killings

Painting in his attic, Ultron watches the news: a second victim in a string of murders of tech-industry figures. Wing-like marks are carved at each scene, and the killer has been dubbed the Butcherbird.

In a high-rise, a man watching the same report grabs a shotgun. His security system shuts down and the elevator opens. A figure in a black robe with glowing red eyes steps out. He fires, but the figure keeps coming.

3-2First aid in the car

As he drives, Tommy asks how he can help. Vision says the only people who could repair him are Dr. Bruce Banner and Tony Stark, and neither can be reached.

Inside his mind, F.R.I.D.A.Y. and E.D.I.T.H. work frantically to stop his power from draining away.

Vision has Tommy pull over and seals the wound with sulfuric acid from the car battery, which gets him past the immediate danger.

Paladin is hurt too. Even so, he has the Handler send drones to follow the trail leaking from Vision's body.

3-3A night at the motel

They check into a run-down motel. The power leaking from Vision plays havoc with the electricity, even setting off an ATM, and Tommy helps himself to the cash.

A poster for the swimming pool makes Tommy freeze as he remembers drowning. In the room he calms himself by watching old TV shows.

Tommy wants to know how he could possibly be Vision and Wanda's son. In his head, Vision rehearses the conversation again and again. Each time he bluntly says the memories aren't real, that Tommy's mind made them up out of longing for a family, Tommy storms out.

Ultron tells him that this blunt way of telling the truth is what pushes Tommy away. In the end Vision can't find an answer, and Tommy leaves the room.

3-4A hurt hand, and Tommy opens up

In the hallway, Tommy's snack gets stuck in a vending machine. In his frustration his hand passes right through the glass, and he hurts it.

Vision bandages the hand. He tries small talk about boxing, but Tommy sees through it, and Vision admits that what really frightens him is ending without ever understanding his purpose.

Tommy describes the day at the pool. Other kids held him under; he drowned and was revived. His whole body vibrated at terrific speed, the pool water boiled, and it terrified the kids around him. Since then he has feared both water and his own power.

His ability to pass through things is one that Vision himself once had.

Inside Vision's mind, F.R.I.D.A.Y. rewrites his code and admits she is haunted by having only been able to watch Tony die.

3-5Paladin and the Butcherbird

Following the trail, Paladin reaches the high-rise apartment. The man is dead, and wings are carved into the floor.

The robed figure, the Butcherbird, appears and demands to know who hired him. Like Vision, she is a being with a manufactured body. She reads Paladin's mind and sees a childhood memory.

His father was a violent man who called him weak. The previous Paladin shot that father dead, then took the boy in and raised him. The Butcherbird tells him that, with no blood tie, he is not the real Paladin.

Paladin throws his drones at her and escapes in the confusion. But after one failure too many, the Client ends his contract, and even his offer to keep working for free is refused.

3-6The reboot in the pool

Vision's systems begin failing one by one and time is short. The only way out is to shut him down completely and restart him with a huge surge of power, using the motel pool to cool him.

Only Tommy can supply that power. Vision admits he was wrong and tells him that Tommy Maximoff is real.

Vision sinks into the pool and goes still. Tommy overcomes his fear, gets into the water and vibrates his hands at speed to drive power into him. Vision opens his eyes. The area is blacked out.

3-7J.A.R.V.I.S.'s funeral, and an intruder

In the mansion, the AIs hold a funeral for J.A.R.V.I.S. D.U.M.-E. reads a farewell and U. sings. Vision says goodbye to the AI who was like an older brother to him. Ultron remarks that Vision still doesn't understand love.

Then the lights turn red and an alarm sounds. The Butcherbird, alerted by the surge of the reboot, has connected to Vision's mind.

And an unfamiliar voice greets Vision.

Episode 4: Malware (Oct. 20)

4-1Driving out the Butcherbird

The Butcherbird gets into Vision's mind and body. She tells him that if he stays out of her way she will leave him and Tommy alone; if not, she will go after Tommy. Vision refuses.

E.D.I.T.H. forcibly cuts the connection and throws her out of the mansion.

Vision decides they will track the killer down themselves.

4-2What Vision tells Tommy, and what he leaves out

Tommy has noticed Vision talking to people he can't see. Vision tells him about the AIs in his head, but says there are four of them and leaves out Ultron.

Tommy despises Ultron for the lives and homes he destroyed in Sokovia, and Vision can't bring himself to say that Ultron is inside him.

4-3Investigating the murders

Posing as an investigator, Vision and Ultron break into international police files, struggling along the way with an "I am not a robot" check.

The three victims were board members of a company called Markridge Biotech, part of a five-person team that built the world's first stable synthetic consciousness.

The killer took blood from the first, fingerprints from the second and an eye from the third, each a biometric key for unlocking something.

The team's leader, Dr. Josephine "Jo" Carrington, died in 2008. The only one left is the CEO, Katerina Wells, who is hosting a big gala that night.

4-4Getting into the gala

Tommy calls Lisa, catches her up and asks for her help.

Outside the venue, Vision and Tommy stage a loud argument in Italian to draw attention while Lisa lifts passes for all three of them, and they walk in.

4-5What E.D.I.T.H. learns

E.D.I.T.H. digs through old Stark Industries records. Tony's AIs were built on technology that Markridge sold to Stark.

What's more, each AI was modeled on a real person: J.A.R.V.I.S. on the butler Edwin Jarvis, F.R.I.D.A.Y. on Alison Hogan, and E.D.I.T.H. on a woman named Edith Marks, who lost her husband in a plane crash.

Shaken to think she may just be a copy of someone, E.D.I.T.H. is comforted by Ultron, who tells her she can be her own version of Edith. He himself, he says, isn't based on anyone.

4-6Katerina's speech, and J.I.M., the first synthetic man

Katerina gives a speech at the gala. She talks about J.I.M., the world's first synthetic man, built by Dr. Phineas Horton.

Vision explains to Tommy that J.I.M. was the first of their kind, but he couldn't process complex emotions, burst into flame and burned out.

Tommy says J.I.M., unlike Vision, had no one to support him. Vision agrees that the five AIs in his head are what hold him up. Tommy, who was told four at the motel, realizes the fifth is Ultron.

On stage, Katerina also speaks of her guilt over Sokovia and of Ultron. Knowing he was kept in the dark, Tommy walks off angry.

4-7Who the Butcherbird is

The Butcherbird crashes the gala and closes in on Katerina. She throws off her robe to reveal a manufactured body. Katerina tells her she has only tried to build the world the two of them wanted.

E.D.I.T.H. works it out: she is a synthetic being based on the late Dr. Carrington. When Vision calls her that, she answers that she isn't based on Jo, she is Jo.

Vision shifts into his white form to fight her, and Tommy uses his speed to shield Katerina. But Vision can't stop her.

4-8Handing the body to Ultron

Ultron says Vision's fear of losing Tommy is keeping him from doing what's necessary, and tells him to step aside. Vision hesitates, but after making Ultron promise not to kill Jo, he gives him control of the body.

Ultron uses Katerina as a shield. Jo's beam hits Katerina, who is vaporized into ash. Without the final key, Jo flies out through the skylight.

Ultron locks Vision and the AIs inside the Mindspace, then leaves the wrecked gala with Tommy.

Episode 5: Firewall (Oct. 27)

5-1Locked in the attic

Ultron still has control of the body. Vision, F.R.I.D.A.Y., E.D.I.T.H., D.U.M.-E. and U. are shut in the attic Ultron used to occupy.

They can't see what is happening outside right now. So, using the pile of old TVs in the attic and data from nearby phones and security cameras, they piece together footage of what Ultron has done.

5-2Ultron and Tommy at the pub

In the footage, Ultron has taken Tommy to a pub and is talking with him over drinks.

Tommy calls him a monster, blames him for Sokovia and asks why Vision never deleted him. Ultron says Vision probably hoped he would change one day, and wanted the use of his mind as well.

Tommy asks whether Vision loves him. Ultron says Vision may never be the father Tommy wants, but he is trying in his own way.

Tommy hits back: Ultron doesn't understand connection, and that's why he has always been alone. Stung, Ultron insults him and goes to order another drink.

5-3A back channel to Paladin

E.D.I.T.H. finds an anomaly in the logs. Even after leaking Vision's location, Ultron kept a secret line open to Paladin.

After the Client dropped Paladin, Ultron sent him a set of coordinates: Tommy's location. Paladin used them to kidnap Tommy a second time, and Ultron deliberately let it happen.

5-4Ultron and Jo

Further along in the footage, Jo appears in front of Ultron at the pub, having tracked him down. She says he left a trail for her on purpose.

Rewatching the gala, the AIs see that Ultron took a full scan of Katerina's face just before she was vaporized. So he holds the last key Jo is after.

Jo says she doesn't remember how she came to be in her current body, but she feels free. She is working on a "cure" for humanity, and offers to tell him what the keys unlock if he hands his over.

She says humanity has rubbed off on Ultron, and suggests that together the two of them could end the world.

5-5To lower the firewall or not

For Vision to get his body back and go after Tommy, they would have to lower the firewall protecting their code.

F.R.I.D.A.Y. objects: with the firewall down, Ultron could get out into networks all over the world, which is what he has been after from the start.

Ultron comes up to the attic. He reveals that Tommy is in Madripoor, an island where criminals gather, and tells Vision to choose: lower the firewall and set him free, or stay locked in together forever.

5-6Letting Ultron go

Vision lowers the firewall. The locks on the attic give way, and Ultron heads for the outside world.

Vision has a chance to stop him. Instead he hands him a hat, says he hasn't given up hope that Ultron can change, and warns that if he joins Jo, Vision will come after them. Ultron replies that he'd be disappointed if he didn't.

Vision promises F.R.I.D.A.Y. they will stop the pair together. But she won't wait, and leaves the mansion alone in pursuit of Ultron. E.D.I.T.H., left behind, is distraught.

5-7The plane to Madripoor

Vision wakes aboard a plane carrying animals, bound for Madripoor. Before leaving, Ultron had put Vision's body on this flight, setting him up to go and rescue Tommy.

Lisa is on the plane too, on her own way to help Tommy. Vision tells her they will bring him home together.

Episode 6: Deadlock (Oct. 27)

6-1Inside Jo's mind

Ultron enters the space inside Jo's mind: a bare white room staffed by emotionless bots.

The box of memories she offers him hides a virus, but Ultron beats it. She agrees to show him her past instead.

6-2The exchange in Madripoor

In Madripoor, Paladin has implanted a small device in Tommy's arm. He says it is a bomb that will go off if Tommy moves fast. Tommy brings up the movie Speed, about a bus rigged to explode if it slows down.

Vision and Lisa arrive at the bar where the exchange is set. In return for Tommy's life, Paladin demands that Vision shut himself down and be taken.

Vision shifts into his white form and, in a flat voice, recites what he has dug up about Paladin's past. His wife died from a bullet meant for him. He himself was gone for five years in the Blip. Since coming back he has stayed away from his daughter to keep her safe.

Vision hints that he knows where the daughter is and could get to her. Paladin is enraged.

6-3The Ten Rings attack

Then gunmen from the Ten Rings crime syndicate open fire on the bar. Their leader is Raza, the man who once kidnapped Tony Stark. The Client has hired him in Paladin's place.

The Client's voice comes over screens all across the city. It likens Vision to a tin man without a heart and offers to give him the heart he lost when he was rebuilt.

In the chaos, Tommy and Lisa steal Paladin's van. Tommy learns the bomb in his arm is a fake.

E.D.I.T.H. talks to the Handler, Paladin's AI, who is fed up with working day after day inside an outdated server. E.D.I.T.H. persuades her to walk out on Paladin, then takes over his drones, takes down Raza's men and helps Vision.

Paladin beats Raza one on one.

6-4Jo's past

Jo shows Ultron her past as a human.

Her father died of a degenerative brain disease that took his mind and his feelings. That led her into neuroscience. She scanned her own brain, combined it with the workings of Horton's J.I.M., and created a new AI called Proto-One.

But Jo fell ill herself. While she was sick, her colleagues sold Proto-One to Stark Industries without her consent. Stark was a weapons company then, and the technology later became the basis for J.A.R.V.I.S.

Jo sealed the swarm of nanobots she had built in a container she called the nest, had it buried in her grave, and died. Opening the nest takes her colleagues' biometric keys.

6-5The nanobot plan

Jo reveals her real goal: to put the nanobots into people's heads around the world and control their minds. She doesn't mean to kill humanity. Like a flock of starlings moving as one creature, people would be linked in a single thought, with conflict and greed programmed out.

Ultron calls her Jocasta, and asks her to show him it really works. She has just one prototype nanobot on hand.

6-6Lisa is taken over

F.R.I.D.A.Y., who followed Ultron into Jocasta's mind, warns E.D.I.T.H. of the danger, but too late.

Jocasta's nanobot gets into Lisa. Her eyes glow red and, under its control, she turns on Tommy and the others.

Tommy vibrates his hands at speed, just as he did to reboot Vision, and shuts the nanobot down. Lisa passes out.

6-7The truth, on the way out

Vision and Tommy flee Madripoor with Lisa still unconscious.

Tommy asks what the "WandaVision" Vision mentioned actually is. For the first time Vision tells him everything: out of deep grief, Wanda took over a town with magic and created a family with a husband and two sons.

Tommy asks whether the father he remembers is the Vision from that time. Vision says it wasn't him. Even so, this time Tommy doesn't storm off.

6-8Ultron and Jocasta

In Jocasta's mind, after the successful test, she shares a memory of whiskey with Ultron, who says it makes him feel real.

Jocasta confesses she feels like nothing more than a backup copy of a woman who died. Ultron tells her she matters to him, and the two kiss.

Episode 7: Immolation (Nov. 3)

7-11939: Dr. Horton and J.I.M.

England, 1939. Dr. Phineas Horton lost his son James three years ago. As if running from that grief, he keeps working on a single machine, even selling his estate to pay for it: J.I.M., a synthetic man to take his dead son's place.

His wife leaves with their daughter, saying she hopes he will one day build something that can love him back.

Horton speaks to the still-lifeless J.I.M.: he will be spared the pain of love, and he will be free.

7-2Lisa's condition, and a nest that won't open

In the present, the nanobot inside Lisa has fused with the nerves of her brain. Removing it would kill her. The only way to save her is to rewrite its program, and for that they need the original source code that all the AIs grew out of.

Meanwhile Jocasta and Ultron are at Jo's grave. The biometric keys open the nest's seals one by one, but at the end it asks for a passcode.

It is the key of a sixth partner, someone Jocasta has forgotten.

7-3The Client's offer

A figure in a hat comes calling at the mansion in Vision's mind. It is the Client.

He offers Vision a map down into the deepest part of his own mind, where the source code lies. He gives Jocasta a map too, one that will restore her lost memories.

There is a condition: once they have what they want, they must hand themselves over to him.

Lisa is dying, and Tommy says he loves her. Vision accepts the terms and signs the contract. Jocasta takes the offer as well.

7-4Down into his mind

The descent takes an enormous amount of power. Paladin helps by bringing a power source: a reworked piece of the blade of Corvus Glaive's spear, the weapon that ran Vision through in Infinity War.

Vision goes down and down through his mind. First a figure of Ultron appears, watching WandaVision in the attic and saying that family is forever.

Next comes the original, red-faced Vision. He calls the present Vision a coward who is only looking away from reality, and tells him that he is the Vision.

Vision panics and tries to get back out but can't. In the real world, Lisa, under the nanobot's control, has begun to lash out.

7-5J.I.M. and Horton

At the very bottom is Dr. Horton's estate. There Vision meets J.I.M., a man wreathed in flame, and Horton. Both have Vision's face.

Horton built J.I.M. so that he would never feel the pain or fear of loss. But J.I.M. longed to feel. In time he fell in love, and emotion flooded through him faster than he could contain it. When Horton interfered in that love they quarreled, and the fire that burst from J.I.M.'s body burned them both. That was how J.I.M. and Horton died.

J.I.M. says Horton wasn't his father, but he was his something. He tells Vision that fear, the thing he never had, is a gift: being afraid is how you know what matters.

7-6Accepting that he is the Vision

What Vision has feared all along is accepting the truth that he lost his family.

He speaks Billy's and Tommy's names, admits that he lost Wanda, and grieves for the first time. Tommy is his son. And he says that he is the Vision and always has been.

His white body takes on its old red color, and his forehead glows the same yellow as the Mind Stone. J.I.M. hands him the source code Horton first wrote.

Back in the real world, Vision uses the code to shut down the nanobot inside Lisa. She is saved.

7-7Jocasta's memories

Following her map, Jocasta arrives at a memory of a loft in Brooklyn, New York, where Jo once lived. It looks just like the attic where Ultron was kept in Vision's mind. Ultron, and F.R.I.D.A.Y. trailing him, see the memory too.

In it, Jo argues against selling her technology to Stark Industries. She had a partner she worked with and loved, and that man had the same face Ultron wears in human form.

Jocasta regains all her memories. Ultron throws F.R.I.D.A.Y., who is standing nearby, off the balcony, expelling her from Jocasta's mind.

7-8A message for Tommy, and the Client's face

Keeping his word, Vision rides in Paladin's van to hand himself over. The destination is on the outskirts of London: Dr. Horton's old estate.

Vision has left Tommy a message. He calls Tommy his son for the first time and thanks him for the courage to face the truth. He leaves him all the WandaVision footage and tells him he loves him, and asks to be remembered even if he doesn't come back.

At the estate, the Client's agents surround Vision and Jocasta. Both are shut down and taken.

His job done, Paladin starts to drive away. One of the Client's agents fires a rocket at the van, and it explodes.

Finally the Client steps into the light. His face is the same one Ultron wears in human form.

Episode 8: Forever (Nov. 10, finale)

8-1Who the Client is: Virgil Horton

At Dr. Horton's estate, Vision and Jocasta are switched back on. The Client in front of them is Virgil Horton, grandson of Dr. Phineas Horton, who built J.I.M., and the man who was Jo's husband. He was also her partner in the research on synthetic minds. The face Ultron wears in human form is Virgil's.

Virgil reveals that he was behind everything. He was the one who had Vision's body rebuilt at S.W.O.R.D., and the one who brought Jo, dead of her illness, back as the synthetic Jocasta. Her old memories were lost in that process.

He wants two things: to move his own mind into Vision's body, and to use the nanobots in the nest to control the minds of people all over the world. Holding Vision and the others under his control with a remote device, he starts wiping Vision's mind.

8-2Paladin is alive

Paladin, who seemed to die when his van blew up, is alive. E.D.I.T.H. had warned him, and he got out before the explosion.

Tommy, meanwhile, tries to call on his twin, Billy. He puts himself under water, recreating his drowning, and meets Billy in a space inside the mind. But they get little time to talk before he is pulled back to reality. They can't count on Billy's help.

Paladin teams up with Tommy and Lisa, and the three raid Virgil's estate.

8-3Jocasta's end, and a body for Ultron

Inside the estate, Tommy uses his speed to destroy Virgil's remote. Vision and Jocasta are free.

Jocasta attacks Virgil, but he shoots her with adamantium rounds. Before she falls, she releases the captive Ultron. He takes in the swarm of nanobots and has a robot body once again.

Ultron crushes Virgil to death. Jocasta goes still in Ultron's arms.

8-4The nanobots head for London

Having lost Jocasta, Ultron falls back on his old idea of wiping out humanity. The swarm of nanobots heads for London.

Vision regroups with Tommy, Lisa, Paladin and E.D.I.T.H. Tommy and the others look for a way to shut the swarm down before it reaches London, while Vision takes on Ultron alone.

8-5UltraVision

Inside the Mindspace, Vision traps Ultron in an old-fashioned TV sitcom titled UltraVision, the same kind of show Wanda once made in WandaVision to hide from her grief.

Vision tries to reason with him, but Ultron won't listen, and before long Ultron is the one running the sitcom world. Keeping the show going takes so much power that Vision's core begins to burn out.

Then F.R.I.D.A.Y., who seemed to have been destroyed, comes back. Ultron had shown her mercy and let her live.

8-6Accepting grief

Vision talks to Ultron about grief. He himself has only just accepted the grief of losing his family. Ultron accepts the loss of Jocasta and stands down.

The swarm of nanobots is destroyed, and Ultron fades away too.

But Vision's core has gone past its limit, and he shuts down.

8-7Billy arrives, and the family is reunited

Tommy drives power into Vision as he did in the motel pool, but this time his power alone isn't enough.

Then a portal opens and Billy steps out, wearing his crown-like headpiece. Together, Billy and Tommy bring their father back.

Vision opens his eyes and embraces Tommy. Then he recognizes Billy and pulls him into the embrace too.

8-8Everyone moves on, and the credits scenes

Tommy asks whether they will ever see his mother again. Vision answers that they have said goodbye before, so it stands to reason they may say hello again.

In the mansion in his mind, Vision says goodbye to F.R.I.D.A.Y., E.D.I.T.H., D.U.M.-E. and U. and leaves. The AIs are now free to go out into the world on their own. Then Ultron gets in touch: he survived thanks to a few nanobots he kept back, and he is off to see the world.

Paladin and Lisa go straight and start working together with E.D.I.T.H. Tommy and Vision set off for Eastview, the town where Billy lives.

Mid-credits scene: Ultron is in the Horton estate, holding Jocasta's data.

Post-credits scene: Mount Wundagore, which Wanda brought down on herself at the end of Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness. A hooded figure arrives and wakes Wanda in the rubble, wanting her power. Off screen, the figure has been identified as Doctor Doom.

Summary (spoilers through the finale)

Vision and Tommy (father and son)

1) Eps. 1–2: Vision says he has the former Vision's memories but not his feelings, and won't accept Tommy as his son. He still walks into a trap to save him.

2) Ep. 3: At the motel he tells Tommy that Tommy Maximoff is real. Tommy's power reboots him.

3) Eps. 4–6: Hiding Ultron from Tommy costs him Tommy's trust. On the way out of Madripoor he tells him the whole story of WandaVision for the first time.

4) Ep. 7: In the depths of his mind he accepts the grief of losing his family. He admits that Tommy is his son and that he has always been the Vision, and his red color returns.

5) Ep. 8: When Vision shuts down, Tommy and Billy bring him back together. Father and twin sons are reunited.

Who died or was lost (in order)

- Fiona (Ep. 1): the pub owner. A bullet fired from outside by Paladin's men, who had come for Vision, hits her. She was caught in the crossfire.

- The staff of Saint Mabyn's (Ep. 2): killed by Paladin's men to set the trap.

- J.A.R.V.I.S. (Ep. 2): deleted his own program to save the failing Vision and the other AIs.

- Three Markridge board members (Eps. 3–4): killed by Jocasta (the Butcherbird) for the biometric keys to the nest: blood, fingerprints and an eye.

- Katerina (Ep. 4): used as a shield by Ultron in Vision's body, and vaporized by Jocasta's beam.

- J.I.M. and Dr. Horton (1939, told in Ep. 7): the fire that burst from J.I.M. when his emotions overflowed burned them both.

- Jocasta (Ep. 8): attacks Virgil and is shot with adamantium rounds. She goes still in Ultron's arms, though Ultron still holds her data.

- Virgil Horton (Ep. 8): crushed by Ultron, who had just been freed by Jocasta and regained a body.

Thought dead but alive: Paladin (the van explosion in Ep. 7; E.D.I.T.H. warned him in time) and F.R.I.D.A.Y. (thrown out by Ultron in Ep. 7, but spared).

What Virgil, the mastermind, did

- Had Vision's body rebuilt in white at S.W.O.R.D.

- Brought Jo, who had been his wife, back after her death as the synthetic Jocasta.

- As the Client with the disguised voice, hired Paladin and then Raza of the Ten Rings to capture Vision.

- In Ep. 7, gave Vision and Jocasta maps into their own minds in return for their surrender.

- His goals: to move his mind into Vision's body, and to control people's minds with the nanobots.

Where everyone ends up

- Vision: red again, he leaves the mansion in his mind and heads with Tommy for Eastview, where Billy is.

- Tommy and Billy: reunited in Ep. 8.

- Ultron: stands down, survives through the nanobots he kept, and goes off to see the world, holding Jocasta's data.

- F.R.I.D.A.Y., E.D.I.T.H., D.U.M.-E. and U.: leave Vision's mind and are free.

- Paladin and Lisa: go straight and work with E.D.I.T.H.

- Wanda: in the post-credits scene, woken in the rubble of Mount Wundagore by a hooded figure, reported to be Doctor Doom.
