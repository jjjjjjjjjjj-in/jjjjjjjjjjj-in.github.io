Part 1: The Tesseract is stolen

A deal in space

Somewhere in space, Loki makes a deal with a mysterious power and its messenger: he'll deliver the Tesseract from Earth, and in return he gets the Chitauri army to conquer it.

Attack on the base

At a S.H.I.E.L.D. research facility, the Tesseract suddenly flares. Loki steps out of the light and uses his scepter to take control of Dr. Selvig and master archer Clint Barton. He escapes with the Tesseract as the base collapses.

Part 2: Assembling the heroes

Natasha and Bruce

Natasha is pulled off a mission and sent to India, where Bruce Banner is hiding out as a doctor, to ask him to help track the Tesseract.

Steve, awake

Steve, newly awakened after 70 years, is alone in a gym, pounding a punching bag. Fury asks him to help save the world.

Tony and Pepper

Tony and Pepper are celebrating his new tower in New York when Coulson arrives with files on the team and the Tesseract research. Tony agrees to help.

The flying carrier

Steve and Bruce are brought aboard the Helicarrier, an enormous flying aircraft carrier.

Part 3: Capturing Loki

A square in Germany

In Germany, Loki forces a crowd to kneel, telling them they were made to be ruled. When an old man refuses, Captain America steps in, Iron Man joins, and they take Loki prisoner.

Thor arrives

Thor drops onto the jet and grabs Loki. He wants to take his brother back to Asgard, and fights Iron Man in a forest until Captain America breaks it up.

The glass cell

Loki is locked in a special cell aboard the Helicarrier. Natasha, talking with him, realizes his plan is to unleash the Hulk.

Part 4: A team falling apart

The argument

The heroes discover S.H.I.E.L.D. was using the Tesseract to build weapons and start turning on each other. With Loki's scepter in the room, the argument escalates.

Attack on the Helicarrier

The brainwashed Clint leads an attack on the carrier. Caught in an explosion, Bruce turns into the Hulk, chases Natasha and fights Thor. Tony and Steve fix a dead engine to keep the carrier from falling.

Coulson falls

Loki escapes, traps Thor and drops him from the sky. Coulson tries to stop him and is stabbed. He goes down cracking a joke, still holding a gun on Loki.

A reason to come together

Fury tells the heroes Coulson is dead and shows them his Captain America trading cards, giving them a reason to fight together. Clint, freed from Loki's control, joins them.

Part 5: The Battle of New York

A hole in the sky

On top of Stark Tower, Loki uses the Tesseract to open a portal in the sky. The Chitauri army and giant flying creatures pour into the city.

Six heroes

All six assemble, and Captain America gives the orders. Bruce arrives on a motorbike, says his secret is that he's always angry, and turns into the Hulk on the spot, stopping a giant creature with one punch.

Hulk vs. Loki

Inside the tower, Loki shouts that he is a god. The Hulk slams him into the floor over and over.

The nuke

Officials above Fury fire a nuclear missile at the city. Tony grabs it and carries it through the portal into space, where it destroys the enemy mothership and the whole army.

Tony falls

Natasha closes the portal with the scepter just as Tony falls back through, unconscious. The Hulk catches him and roars, and Tony wakes up.

Part 6: Afterward

Loki's end

Battered, Loki looks up at the heroes standing over him and says he'll have that drink now. Thor takes him and the Tesseract back to Asgard.

Separate ways

The heroes go their separate ways. Tony and Bruce drive off together. Fury is confident they'll come back when they're needed.

Mid-credits

The messenger who sent the Chitauri reports their defeat to his master. The master turns and smiles: it is Thanos.

After the credits

The exhausted heroes sit in a wrecked shawarma restaurant, eating in silence.
