Part 1: The stolen Tesseract

1-1A deal in space

Somewhere in space, Loki meets the Other, the messenger who speaks for an alien army called the Chitauri.

The deal: Loki will bring them the Tesseract from Earth, and in return the Other will lend him an army to conquer it. Loki holds a scepter with a glowing blue stone.

1-2Trouble at the lab

On Earth, in an underground S.H.I.E.L.D. research facility, Dr. Selvig's team is studying the Tesseract, and it starts giving off energy on its own.

Director Nick Fury arrives by helicopter. Agents Coulson and Maria Hill are there, and Fury also checks in with master archer Clint Barton, who has been watching from up high.

1-3Loki arrives

The Tesseract opens a hole in space, and Loki steps out of it. He strikes down the guards who try to stop him with his scepter.

He touches the scepter to the chests of Barton and Selvig, taking control of their minds. Fury tries to leave with the cube and is shot by the brainwashed Barton, but his vest saves him.

1-4The base collapses

Loki and his brainwashed followers flee by truck with the cube. Hill gives chase, but the unstable portal brings the whole facility down.

Fury and Hill make it out, but Loki is gone. Fury tells his people that as of now, they are at war.

Part 2: Assembling the heroes

2-1Natasha in Russia

In Russia, Natasha Romanoff is tied to a chair and being interrogated. In fact, she's letting them think they've caught her so she can pull information out of them.

Coulson calls to tell her that her partner Barton has been taken over. She takes down the men in the room in seconds and cuts the mission short.

2-2Bruce in India

In Kolkata, India, Bruce Banner is living under the radar as a doctor treating the poor. A little girl begs him to help her sick father, and he follows her to a house on the edge of town.

Natasha is waiting there. The Tesseract gives off gamma radiation, she explains, and Bruce, an expert in it, could track it down.

When Bruce suddenly shouts, Natasha pulls a gun on him: everyone is afraid of the Hulk. Bruce agrees to come with her.

2-3Steve at the gym

In New York, Steve Rogers, awake after 70 years, is alone in a gym at night, punching one bag after another off its chain. He can't shake his memories of the war.

Fury arrives with files on the Tesseract and asks him to help save the world.

2-4Tony at Stark Tower

Tony Stark has just got his new New York skyscraper, Stark Tower, running entirely on his own arc reactor power, and he and Pepper are celebrating.

Coulson arrives with a file: the Tesseract research, plus information on the Hulk, Captain America, Thor and Loki. Pepper heads off to Washington, and Tony digs into the files.

2-5To the Helicarrier

Coulson flies Steve and Natasha out on a Quinjet. He's a huge Captain America fan and shyly mentions his collection of vintage trading cards.

They land on a giant aircraft carrier at sea, where Steve meets Bruce on the deck. The carrier rises out of the ocean, flies, and turns invisible: it's S.H.I.E.L.D.'s flying base, the Helicarrier.

In the lab, Bruce starts searching for the Tesseract's gamma signature using sensors around the world.

2-6Loki's hideout

Meanwhile, in Loki's hideout, the brainwashed Selvig is building a machine to open a portal to space with the Tesseract. To keep its power stable, he needs a metal called iridium.

Loki also speaks with the Other, who warns him that if he fails to deliver the Tesseract, there is nowhere he can hide from their punishment.

Part 3: Capturing Loki

3-1The gala in Stuttgart

In Stuttgart, Germany, Loki shows up at a museum gala in a suit and attacks the scientist hosting it, using a device on his eye to get past an eye-scanning vault.

While everyone is distracted, Barton steals the iridium from that vault.

3-2The kneeling crowd

Outside, Loki surrounds the fleeing crowd with copies of himself and forces them to kneel, telling them people are happier being ruled.

One old man stands up and refuses to kneel to men like him. Loki fires at him, and Captain America leaps in and blocks the blast with his shield.

Natasha's Quinjet takes aim from above and Iron Man arrives. Surrounded, Loki surrenders without much of a fight.

3-3Thor in the storm

Outside the Quinjet carrying Loki, thunder suddenly booms, and Loki looks uneasy.

Someone lands on the plane, grabs Loki and flies off: it's his brother Thor. On a mountaintop, Thor urges Loki to give up his plan and come home to Asgard, but Loki won't listen.

3-4The fight in the forest

Tony slams into Thor, and the two fight fiercely in the forest. Tony won't hand Loki over; Thor insists that he will deal with his brother in Asgard.

Steve steps in, but Thor brings his hammer down anyway. It strikes Steve's shield, and the shockwave flattens the trees around them. The three stop fighting and take Loki to the Helicarrier.

3-5The glass cell

Loki is locked in a glass cell aboard the Helicarrier, one originally built to hold the Hulk.

Fury shows Loki that, at the push of a button, the floor opens and the cell drops out of the sky.

3-6Tony and Bruce in the lab

In the lab, Tony and Bruce hunt for the Tesseract. Tony pokes Bruce with a small electric prod to see if he'll turn into the Hulk, which angers Steve.

When he was on the bridge, Tony secretly planted a small device to hack S.H.I.E.L.D.'s computers. Steve, suspecting S.H.I.E.L.D. is hiding something, goes to search the storage decks himself.

3-7Natasha and Loki

Natasha visits Loki's cell and asks about Barton. Loki throws her past as a spy in her face and threatens her cruelly.

She seems to break down in tears, but she is listening, and Loki gives away his plan: to turn Bruce into the Hulk and let him loose inside the carrier.

Part 4: The Helicarrier in danger

4-1A team at odds

In storage, Steve finds weapons built with the Tesseract's power, and Tony's hack turns up the same secret: S.H.I.E.L.D. planned to use the cube to make weapons against threats from space.

Everyone gathers in the lab and argues. Fury says Thor's arrival proved there are beings out there far stronger than Earth; Thor answers that the cube is exactly why Earth is being targeted.

With Loki's scepter in the room, the argument grows uglier. Bruce, who has picked up the scepter without thinking, has his computer find the Tesseract just then.

4-2The Hulk breaks loose

At that moment, Barton and other brainwashed agents attack the Helicarrier by air, and an explosion knocks out one of the carrier's engines. Bruce and Natasha fall through to the deck below.

In pain and anger, Bruce turns into the Hulk. He chases Natasha, then fights Thor. Finally he leaps onto a fighter jet that's firing at him and falls with it to the ground.

4-3Fixing the engine

With one engine dead, the Helicarrier starts to tilt and fall. Tony goes outside in the Iron Man suit and pushes the stalled rotor around with his own body.

Steve, out on the catwalks, pulls the lever Tony needs while dodging enemy fire. Together they get the engine running and keep the carrier in the air.

4-4Natasha vs. Barton

Barton gets aboard and targets the bridge and the ship's systems with his arrows. Natasha fights him one-on-one and knocks him out with a hard blow to the head.

When he comes to, he is free of Loki's control.

4-5Thor falls

Loki tricks Thor into stepping into the cell using a copy of himself, and locks him in.

Loki hits the button and drops the cell, with Thor inside, out of the sky. Thor breaks out before it hits the ground.

4-6Coulson's death

Coulson faces Loki with a big gun built from parts of the Asgardian Destroyer. But that Loki is a copy too, and the real Loki stabs Coulson through the chest from behind with the scepter.

Down but not done, Coulson tells Loki he's going to lose, then fires and blasts Loki through a wall. Loki escapes the carrier with his followers.

Coulson dies with Fury at his side.

4-7A reason to fight

Fury tells Tony and Steve that Coulson is dead and tosses them the Captain America cards, stained with blood, that Coulson had wanted Steve to sign. Fury says the heroes needed something to bring them together.

Later, when Hill points out that the cards were in Coulson's locker, Fury says they needed the push.

Meanwhile, Bruce wakes up, human again, in the warehouse where he fell.

Part 5: The Battle of New York

5-1Loki's target

Tony realizes Loki wants an audience: he wants to win where everyone can see. And Selvig's machine needs huge power, the kind Stark Tower has.

Tony heads there first in his damaged suit. Barton, now himself again, Natasha and Steve follow in a Quinjet.

5-2Tony and Loki

On the roof of Stark Tower, Selvig's machine is already being powered by the Tesseract. Tony takes off his suit, walks in and offers Loki a drink as they talk.

Loki touches his scepter to Tony's chest to control him, but the arc reactor blocks it. Furious, Loki throws Tony out the window.

As Tony falls, a new Iron Man suit flies out and wraps around him in midair.

5-3A hole in the sky

A beam from the machine shoots into the sky and tears open a huge portal. Chitauri soldiers and flying craft pour into New York.

Thor lands on the roof and fights Loki while trying to reason with him, but Loki stabs him with a small blade and flies off into the city on a Chitauri craft. The machine is protected by a barrier that even Thor's hammer can't break.

5-4All six together

Steve's Quinjet is shot down in the streets. Steve tells the police how far to clear people out.

Bruce arrives on a motorbike as a gigantic flying creature, like a whale, comes through the portal. When Steve says now might be a really good time to get angry, Bruce says his secret is that he's always angry, turns into the Hulk on the spot, and stops the creature with one punch.

Captain America, Iron Man, Thor, the Hulk, Black Widow and Hawkeye stand back to back together for the first time.

5-5Captain America's plan

Steve gives orders: Barton watches from a rooftop and picks off enemies with his arrows, Tony draws off the enemies in the sky, Thor uses lightning to hold back those coming through the portal, and the Hulk is simply told to smash.

Steve and Natasha protect people on the ground. Natasha decides the only way to close the portal is to get to the top of the tower, and she uses Steve's shield as a springboard to leap onto a passing Chitauri craft.

5-6Hulk vs. Loki

Loki retreats into Stark Tower and shouts at the pursuing Hulk that he is a god.

The Hulk grabs Loki by the legs, slams him into the floor over and over, and walks away from the stunned god with a parting insult.

On the roof, Natasha finds Selvig, freed from Loki's control, who tells her Loki's scepter can shut down the machine.

5-7The nuke

The World Security Council, Fury's superiors, decides to fire a nuclear missile at New York to wipe out the enemy along with the city. Fury refuses and stops one jet with a rocket launcher, but another one launches the missile.

Tony catches the missile and pushes it up toward the portal. Thinking he may not come back, he tries to call Pepper, but the call doesn't go through.

Tony flies through into space and lets the missile go at the Chitauri mothership. When it explodes, the Chitauri on Earth all drop at once.

5-8Tony falls

His suit out of power, Tony falls back through the portal unconscious, just as Natasha uses Loki's scepter to shut down the machine and close the portal.

The Hulk catches Tony in midair. Tony isn't moving, but when the Hulk roars at him, he wakes up and suggests they all go get shawarma.

Part 6: After the battle

6-1Loki's last stand

Loki crawls out of the floor to find all six heroes standing over him, weapons ready.

All he can say is that he'll take that drink after all.

6-2Separate ways

In a New York park, Thor takes the muzzled, handcuffed Loki and the Tesseract back to Asgard, where Loki will face judgment.

The heroes go their own ways: Tony and Bruce drive off together, Steve leaves on his motorbike, and Natasha and Barton drive away.

The Council criticizes Fury for letting Loki and the cube go, but Fury is sure the heroes will come back when they're needed.

6-3Mid-credits

The Other reports the failure on Earth to his master, warning that challenging the humans means courting death.

The master turns and smiles: it is Thanos.

6-4After the credits

The exhausted heroes sit in a wrecked shawarma restaurant, eating in silence.

Summary (spoilers through the ending)

Loki's plan and how it ends

Loki agreed to hand the Tesseract to the Other in exchange for an army to conquer Earth (Part 1).

He stole the cube and used the scepter to control Barton and Selvig (Part 1). He let himself be captured to get aboard the Helicarrier and tried to break the team apart from the inside by unleashing the Hulk (Parts 3–4).

In the end he opened the portal from Stark Tower and brought in the Chitauri, but the six heroes beat him, and Thor took him back to Asgard (Parts 5–6).

The people Loki controlled

- Clint Barton: taken over by the scepter in Part 1; he stole the iridium and attacked the Helicarrier. In Part 4, Natasha knocks him out and he returns to himself. In Part 5 he fights as one of the heroes.

- Dr. Selvig: taken over in Part 1; he built the machine that opens the portal. In Part 5 he is free again and tells Natasha the scepter can shut it down.

Who died (in order)

- Guards at the research facility (Part 1): struck down by Loki's scepter when they tried to stop him.

- Phil Coulson (Part 4): he tried to stop Loki from escaping the Helicarrier. Loki fooled him with a copy of himself and stabbed him through the chest from behind with the scepter. Fury uses Coulson's death to bring the heroes together.
