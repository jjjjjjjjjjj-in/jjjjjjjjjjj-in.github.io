Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

It brings everything together

Heroes from Iron Man, The Incredible Hulk, Thor and Captain America: The First Avenger all meet here. At the very least, watching Thor and Captain America first makes it much easier to follow.

Hulk has a new actor

Mark Ruffalo plays Bruce Banner from this film on. Same character.

They don't get along at first

The heroes mostly argue at first. Watching them come together is the fun of it.

There are scenes during and after the credits

The mid-credits scene sets up the much bigger story to come.

StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

1. The cube is stolen

At a S.H.I.E.L.D. facility, the Tesseract activates and Loki arrives from space. With his scepter he takes control of Clint (Hawkeye) and Dr. Selvig, and escapes with the cube.

2. Assembling the heroes

Fury puts the Avengers Initiative into action. Natasha brings in Bruce from India, Coulson recruits Tony, and Fury calls on the newly awakened Steve.

3. Capturing Loki

Captain America and Iron Man catch Loki in Germany. Thor arrives to take his brother home, and the heroes end up fighting each other.

4. Chaos on the Helicarrier

On the flying Helicarrier, the team keeps arguing. Then the mind-controlled Clint and others attack, Bruce turns into Hulk, and Loki escapes.

5. Coulson's death

Coulson tries to stop Loki and is killed. His death finally brings the divided heroes together.

6. The Battle of New York

Loki opens a portal from the roof of Stark Tower and the Chitauri army pours through. All six heroes stand together to defend the city.

7. Tony's choice

Higher-ups launch a nuclear missile at the city. Tony carries it through the portal and destroys the enemy mothership. Natasha closes the portal and the battle ends.

8. After the battle

Thor takes Loki and the cube back to Asgard, and the heroes go their separate ways. During the credits, the powerful figure behind Loki shows himself.

Key terms

The Tesseract
The blue cube of limitless power. S.H.I.E.L.D. was studying it until Loki stole it.
Loki's scepter
A staff with a glowing blue stone. It can control the minds of the people it touches.
The Chitauri
The alien army Loki brings to Earth.
The Helicarrier
A giant flying aircraft carrier that serves as S.H.I.E.L.D.'s headquarters.
The Avengers Initiative
Fury's plan to bring together people with special abilities to protect Earth.
Stark Tower
Tony's skyscraper in New York. Loki opens the portal to space from its roof.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why does Loki attack Earth?

He wants to rule Earth. He was promised an alien army in exchange for handing over the cube.

Why do Clint and Dr. Selvig work for Loki?

His scepter controls their minds. Natasha snaps Clint out of it with a hard blow to the head.

How could Bruce control Hulk at the end?

He says his secret is that he's always angry, and turns into Hulk on purpose. He's starting to use Hulk's power for the team.

Who is the figure at the end?

Thanos. He's the one who lent Loki the army, and he becomes the MCU's biggest villain.

Why do Tony and Steve argue?

They're opposites: Tony is free-wheeling and cocky, Steve values discipline. They clash at first, but come to respect each other in battle.

Easy-to-miss details

The shawarma scene

After the credits, the exhausted heroes silently eat at the restaurant Tony mentioned during the battle.

Coulson is a Captain America fan

Coulson has been a huge Captain America fan since childhood and collects his trading cards.

Hulk and Loki

The moment Hulk slams Loki into the floor is one of the film's biggest laughs.

Stan Lee's cameo

Marvel Comics legend Stan Lee appears in a news interview after the battle.
