StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

Part 1: Wenwu and Ying Li

A thousand years ago, Wenwu found the Ten Rings, which gave him an ageless body and the power to shape history.

In 1996, searching for the legendary village of Ta Lo, he is beaten by its guardian, Ying Li, and they fall in love. Li leaves the village to marry him, and they have Shang-Chi and Xialing. For his family, Wenwu locks the rings away.

Part 2: A mother's death, a son's training

When Shang-Chi is seven, Li is killed by the Iron Gang, Wenwu's old enemies.

Wenwu puts the rings back on, takes his revenge, and trains Shang-Chi as an assassin. At 14, Shang-Chi carries out the mission to avenge his mother, but it scars him, and he runs away to San Francisco, leaving his sister behind.

Part 3: Shaun in San Francisco

Going by "Shaun," Shang-Chi lives a carefree life as a hotel valet with his best friend Katy.

One morning, Ten Rings agents attack him on a bus. He fights them off with kung fu, but they take his mother's pendant. Fearing his sister is next, he tells Katy his real name and past and heads to Macau.

Part 4: Macau

At a secret fight club in Macau, Shang-Chi fights his sister Xialing and loses. She resents him for leaving her behind.

The Ten Rings attack, and the siblings fight together on scaffolding outside the tower, but her pendant is taken. Wenwu appears and takes the three of them to his compound.

Part 5: The Ten Rings compound

Wenwu hears his late wife Li's voice and believes she is trapped behind a gate in Ta Lo.

Using the two pendants to reveal a map, he vows to burn the village and locks up Shang-Chi and the others when they object. In the cell are Trevor, the actor who played the fake Mandarin, and Morris, a creature from Ta Lo. They escape and head for Ta Lo.

Part 6: Ta Lo

Their aunt, Ying Nan, welcomes them. Behind the gate is sealed a soul-eating creature, the Dweller-in-Darkness, and Li's voice is the creature's imitation, meant to make Wenwu break the gate.

Shang-Chi learns his mother's village style, Katy learns archery, and they prepare for battle with the villagers.

Part 7: The battle of Ta Lo

Wenwu attacks the village, knocks Shang-Chi into the lake and starts pounding the gate. When Soul Eaters pour out, the Ten Rings and the villagers fight side by side.

Saved by the dragon, Shang-Chi gets the better of his father, but the gate breaks. Wenwu shields his son, has his soul drained and dies after passing on the rings. With the dragon, Xialing and Katy's arrow, Shang-Chi destroys the creature.

Part 8: Afterward

Back in San Francisco, Shang-Chi and Katy are summoned to Kamar-Taj by the sorcerer Wong.

Examining the rings with Bruce Banner and Carol, they find the rings are sending out a signal somewhere. Meanwhile, Xialing doesn't disband the organization; she becomes the new leader of the Ten Rings.

Key terms

The Ten Rings
Ten mysterious rings that grant great power and long life, and also the name of the organization Wenwu built with them.
Ta Lo
A hidden village of legendary creatures, Shang-Chi's mother's home.
The pendants
Necklaces their mother left Shang-Chi and his sister. Together, they're a map to Ta Lo.
The Golden Daggers Club
A secret fight club in Macau.
The Dweller-in-Darkness
A soul-eating creature sealed behind Ta Lo's gate.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Is this the Mandarin from Iron Man 3?

That Mandarin was a fake played by actor Trevor. The real one is Wenwu, the bearer of the Ten Rings.

Why does Wenwu attack Ta Lo?

He believes he hears his late wife calling from behind the gate. It's really the creature imitating her.

Where did the Ten Rings come from?

The film doesn't say. At the end, experts admit they can't identify them.

Easy-to-miss details

Trevor returns

Trevor, the actor who played the Mandarin in Iron Man 3, shows up again in a surprising way.

Abomination and Wong

At the Macau fight club, you'll spot the Abomination from The Incredible Hulk, and Wong.

Katy's bow

Karaoke-loving Katy learns archery in Ta Lo and makes a big difference.

The ending and post-creditsSpoilers

After the end credits

Mid-credits: Wong, Bruce and Carol study the Ten Rings and find they are sending a mysterious signal. Post-credits: Xialing becomes the new leader of the Ten Rings.
