Part 1: Wenwu and Ying Li

1-1The man who found the rings

The story opens with narration by Ying Li, Shang-Chi's mother.

A thousand years ago, Xu Wenwu finds ten mysterious rings. They give him godlike power and a body that never ages.

He builds an organization named after them, the Ten Rings, and over the centuries conquers kingdoms and topples governments.

1-21996: the fight in the forest

1996. Wenwu searches for Ta Lo, a village said to be home to mythical beasts, and makes his way through a magical forest to its entrance.

There he is stopped by the village's guardian, Ying Li, who fights with power over the wind, a gift from Ta Lo's guardian dragon. Even with the rings, Wenwu can't beat her. As they fight, they fall for each other.

1-3Li leaves the village

But the people of Ta Lo reject Wenwu for his dark past. Li chooses to leave and make a life with him.

They marry and have two children, a son, Shang-Chi, and a daughter, Xialing. For his family, Wenwu gives up his organization and locks the rings away, and Li gives up the dragon's gift.

Li tells the children stories of her village. She tells Shang-Chi that what beat the rings was the power of the dragon that guards Ta Lo, and gives him a pendant.

Part 2: A mother's death, a son's training

2-1The Iron Gang

When Shang-Chi is seven, the Iron Gang, old enemies of Wenwu, come to the house while he is away and kill Li.

Wenwu puts the rings back on, slaughters the Iron Gang, and takes command of the Ten Rings once again.

2-2Brutal training

Wenwu puts young Shang-Chi through brutal martial arts training, taught by the masked assassin Death Dealer and others.

He doesn't let Xialing train. She watches in secret and teaches herself.

2-3The mission at 14

When Shang-Chi is 14, Wenwu orders him to kill the leader of the Iron Gang, the man behind his mother's death. Blood must be repaid with blood, his father believes.

Shang-Chi carries out the mission, but it scars him. Instead of going home, he runs away to San Francisco and starts a new life as "Shaun." His sister Xialing is left behind with their father.

Part 3: Shaun in San Francisco

3-1Two valets

The present, after the people Thanos erased have come back.

Shaun works as a hotel parking valet with his best friend from high school, Katy. The two have a carefree life, joyriding in their customers' fancy cars.

3-2Dinner and karaoke

They have dinner with their friends Soo and her husband John. Soo asks why two capable people are still parking cars and urges them to do more with their lives.

That night, Shaun and Katy go out and sing karaoke. Shaun has told even Katy almost nothing about where he comes from or his family.

3-3The bus fight

The next morning, on their bus to work, Ten Rings agents led by Razor Fist, a man with a blade for a right arm, come for the pendant around Shaun's neck.

In front of a stunned Katy, Shaun fights them in the cramped bus with dazzling kung fu. Partway through, Katy takes the wheel. A passenger, Klev, livestreams the fight.

Shaun drives them off without any passengers getting hurt, but they get away with the pendant.

3-4His real name

Xialing has a matching pendant. Fearing she'll be next, Shaun plans to go to Macau, to the address on a letter he received from her.

When Katy demands answers, he tells her the truth: his real name is Shang-Chi, his father raised him to be an assassin, and he ran away at 14. Katy insists on coming along.

Part 4: Macau

4-1The secret fight club

In Macau, the address leads them to the Golden Daggers, a secret fight club high in a tower. The sorcerer Wong is there too, fighting the monster Abomination in the cage.

Shang-Chi ends up in a match himself.

4-2A match with his sister

His opponent is Xialing herself. When he warns her she's in danger, she tells him he shouldn't have come back, and knocks him out.

She owns the club: she escaped from their father at 16 and built it herself. She resents her brother for leaving her behind without a word, and she says she never sent him a letter.

4-3The club is raided

Alarms sound: Razor Fist and a crowd of agents have come for Xialing's pendant.

Shang-Chi and Katy try to get out, but Xialing, who fled at first, comes back and the siblings fight side by side.

4-4Fight on the scaffolding

The fight spills out onto tall bamboo scaffolding on the outside of the tower. Katy clings on, nearly falling.

Shang-Chi faces Death Dealer, his childhood teacher, who takes Xialing's pendant. He corners him but hesitates to finish him.

4-5Father arrives

In that moment, Wenwu appears and puts a stop to the fight with the rings. Shang-Chi, Xialing and Katy are taken, along with the pendant, to the Ten Rings' compound.

Part 5: The Ten Rings compound

5-1Family, ten years on

Wenwu is glad to have his children back after ten years and welcomes them to dinner.

He tells them he hears his late wife Li's voice. He believes she is alive, trapped behind a sealed gate in Ta Lo, and calling to him for help.

5-2The pendant map

Wenwu uses the two pendants to reveal a mystical map showing the way to Ta Lo. The way opens only once a year, he says.

If the villagers don't release Li, he says, he'll burn the village down, and he wants his children to help him save their mother. Shang-Chi, Xialing and Katy object, and he locks them up.

5-3Trevor in the cell

Deep in the prison is Trevor, the actor who once played a fake "Mandarin," the organization's leader (the events of Iron Man 3). Captured for impersonating the real Wenwu, he escaped execution by entertaining Wenwu with Shakespeare and has lived on as his jester.

Trevor has befriended Morris, a strange faceless, winged creature the Ten Rings had captured. Morris doesn't speak, but Trevor understands him. Morris comes from Ta Lo, wants to go home, and knows the way.

5-4Escape

They break out, take Trevor and Morris with them, and flee in a car stolen from the compound. Trevor passes on what Morris tells them, and Morris shows the way. The goal: reach Ta Lo before Wenwu and warn the village.

Part 6: Ta Lo

6-1Through the moving forest

Ta Lo's entrance is guarded by a bamboo forest that moves on its own. Morris guides the group through.

Beyond it lies Ta Lo, a village in another realm, home to creatures out of Chinese myth.

6-2Their aunt, Ying Nan

They are welcomed by Ying Nan, Li's sister and the siblings' aunt.

She tells them the village's history. Thousands of years ago, the soul-eating Dweller-in-Darkness and its Soul Eaters attacked this world. A great dragon, the Great Protector, helped the people seal the creature behind the Dark Gate, and Ta Lo has guarded it ever since.

6-3The truth about the voice

When Shang-Chi tells her his father's plan, Ying Nan explains that the voice Wenwu hears is the Dweller imitating Li. It promises people their deepest wish, to trick Wenwu into using the rings to break the Gate.

The day the way opens is tomorrow.

6-4Dragon-scale armor

Ying Nan gives the siblings outfits and weapons made from dragon scales, which Li left for them. Dragon scale works against the Soul Eaters. She tells them they are family of Ta Lo too.

The villagers start preparing for Wenwu's arrival.

6-5Training

Shang-Chi learns his mother's village style from Ying Nan.

Xialing sharpens her skill with the rope dart, and Katy learns archery. Trevor and Morris help the villagers prepare.

6-6Telling Katy

That night, Shang-Chi tells Katy what really happened at 14: he did kill the man who murdered his mother.

Believing his father is to blame for her death, he says he means to kill him.

Part 7: The battle of Ta Lo

7-1The Ten Rings attack

The next day, Wenwu arrives with the Ten Rings army. Ying Nan tries to reason with him, but he believes the voice completely and won't listen. The battle begins.

7-2Shang-Chi sinks

Shang-Chi takes on his father but can't match the rings. Wenwu sends him into the nearby lake, and he sinks into the depths.

Wenwu reaches the Gate and starts pounding it with the rings.

7-3The Soul Eaters break out

Cracks open in the Gate, and Soul Eaters burst out and start draining people's souls. Death Dealer is the first to fall.

Seeing their weapons are useless, Razor Fist orders the Ten Rings to fight alongside the villagers, and swaps his own arm blade for one made of dragon scale.

Katy, who had stayed out of the fight, joins in with her bow. Still, more Soul Eaters keep coming, and the losses mount.

7-4The dragon's help

At the bottom of the lake, Shang-Chi remembers his mother's teachings and her story of the dragon's power. The Great Protector appears, revives him and carries him up out of the water.

The dragon joins the battle and starts tearing through the Soul Eaters.

7-5Father and son, round two

At the Gate, Shang-Chi faces his father again. This time, just as his mother once did, he gets the better of his father.

But he doesn't kill him. Instead, he tells him the voice is a lie.

7-6His father's end

The weakened Gate finally gives way, and the Dweller-in-Darkness bursts out and lunges at Shang-Chi.

Wenwu shields his son. Realizing he was deceived, he passes the ten rings to Shang-Chi as the Dweller drains his soul, and dies.

7-7Destroying the Dweller

Shang-Chi seals the Gate for the moment with the rings, then rides the dragon with Xialing after the Dweller. Swollen with the souls it has eaten, the creature starts draining the dragon's soul.

Then Katy's arrow pierces its throat, and the tide turns. The dragon pins it down, and Shang-Chi drives the rings into its body and, guiding them with his mother's style, blows it apart from within.

7-8Lanterns

After the battle, the survivors set lanterns afloat for the dead, Trevor and Morris, villagers and Ten Rings soldiers among them. Shang-Chi and Xialing light lanterns for their mother and father.

Xialing tells her brother she will disband the Ten Rings.

Part 8: Afterward

8-1Back in San Francisco

Back in San Francisco, Shang-Chi and Katy tell Soo and John about their adventure, who can hardly believe it.

Then the sorcerer Wong appears and asks them to come with him and bring the rings. They step through his portal to Kamar-Taj, home of the sorcerers.

8-2Mid-credits

At Kamar-Taj, Wong introduces them to Bruce Banner and Carol Danvers, who joins by hologram.

Neither knows where the rings came from. What they find is that the rings are sending out a signal to somewhere.

Afterward, Shang-Chi, Katy and Wong sing karaoke together.

8-3After the credits

Despite what she told her brother, Xialing hasn't disbanded the organization: she has become the new leader of the Ten Rings. Sitting in her father's seat, she starts training women as well as men.

A title card says the Ten Rings will return.

Summary (spoilers through the ending)

How Wenwu was deceived

After losing Li, Wenwu began hearing her voice and believed she was trapped in Ta Lo (Part 5).

The voice was the Dweller-in-Darkness, sealed behind the Dark Gate, imitating her so he would break the Gate with the rings (Part 6). Wenwu took his children's pendants to find the way to the village (Parts 3–5), attacked it and opened the Gate. In the end he realized he'd been deceived and died shielding his son (Part 7).

Who died (in order)

- Ying Li (Part 2): killed by the Iron Gang, Wenwu's old enemies, when Shang-Chi was seven, before the main story.

- The Iron Gang (Part 2): Wenwu slaughtered them with the rings to avenge Li.

- The Iron Gang's leader (Part 2): killed by 14-year-old Shang-Chi on his father's orders.

- Death Dealer (Part 7): the first to have his soul drained by the Soul Eaters that escaped the Gate.

- Villagers and Ten Rings soldiers (Part 7): their souls drained by the Soul Eaters and the Dweller.

- Wenwu (Part 7): died shielding Shang-Chi from the Dweller, which drained his soul. He passed the rings to his son before he died.

- The Dweller-in-Darkness (Part 7): Katy's arrow pierced its throat, the dragon pinned it, and Shang-Chi drove the rings into its body and destroyed it.

My takeOpinion

The bus fight alone makes it worth watching. It's also satisfying to finally learn what the Ten Rings, named since the first Iron Man, really are.
