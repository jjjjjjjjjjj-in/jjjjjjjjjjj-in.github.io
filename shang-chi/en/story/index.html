Part 1: The man who lived a thousand years

The Ten Rings

Long ago, Wenwu found ten mysterious rings, the Ten Rings, which gave him long life and the power to shape nations.

Meeting Ying Li

In 1996, searching for the legendary village of Ta Lo, Wenwu fights its guardian Ying Li and falls for her. He sets aside the rings, and they have two children, Shang-Chi and Xialing.

Her death

But Ying Li is killed by Wenwu's old enemies. He puts the rings back on and puts young Shang-Chi through brutal training. At 14, Shang-Chi is sent on a mission of revenge, and disappears.

Part 2: Life in San Francisco

Shaun the valet

The present. Shang-Chi goes by Shaun and works as a hotel valet with his best friend Katy, living carefree.

The bus fight

One morning on a bus, Ten Rings agents attack. Shaun fights back with stunning kung fu, to Katy's amazement, but they take the pendant his mother gave him.

The truth

Shaun tells Katy his real name and past. Realizing his sister, who has a matching pendant, is in danger, he heads to Macau.

Part 3: Macau

The fight club

At a secret fight club atop a Macau tower, Shang-Chi ends up fighting his sister Xialing, who runs the club.

Battle on the scaffolding

The Ten Rings attack, and a fierce fight breaks out on scaffolding high outside the tower.

Father arrives

Wenwu appears and takes Shang-Chi, Xialing and Katy away.

Part 4: Father's plan

Mother's voice

Wenwu tells his children their mother is trapped behind a gate in Ta Lo and is calling to him. The two pendants form a map to the village.

The captive actor

When they refuse to help, they're locked up with Trevor, the actor who once played the Mandarin, and Morris, a strange faceless creature.

Escape

They break out, and Morris guides them through a shifting forest to Ta Lo.

Part 5: Ta Lo

The legendary village

Ta Lo is home to creatures of legend. Their aunt, Ying Nan, welcomes them.

Behind the gate

Ying Nan explains that a soul-eating creature is sealed behind the gate. The voice Wenwu hears is the creature imitating Ying Li to trick him.

Their mother's style

Shang-Chi and Xialing learn their mother's fighting style. Katy learns archery.

Part 6: The battle of Ta Lo

Father and son

Wenwu leads his army into the village. Shang-Chi fights his father and is thrown into the lake.

The guardian dragon

From the depths, the village's guardian dragon rises and saves him. Shang-Chi awakens to a style that combines his mother's and father's techniques.

His father's end

The gate opens and the creature emerges. Wenwu dies saving his son from it, passing the rings to Shang-Chi.

Defeating the creature

Shang-Chi teams up with the dragon, helped by Katy's arrow, and destroys the creature.

Part 7: The credit scenes

Mid-credits

Wong summons Shang-Chi and Katy to examine the rings with Bruce Banner and a hologram of Carol. The rings are sending out a signal somewhere.

After the credits

Xialing takes over her father's organization as the new leader of the Ten Rings.
