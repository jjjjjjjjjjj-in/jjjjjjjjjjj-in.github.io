Part 1: Two flashbacks

1-1Jean, twenty years ago

Twenty years earlier, Charles Xavier (Professor X) and Erik Lehnsherr (Magneto) visit young Jean Grey's home together. Her parents called them, worried about what they think is an 'illness'.

Without touching anything, Jean lifts several cars and other objects outside the house into the air. The two men, still friends at this point, invite her to their school.

1-2Warren, ten years later

Ten years later, industrialist Warren Worthington II finds his son desperately trying to shave off the feathers of the wings growing from his back.

His son is a mutant. The father is speechless.

Part 2: The cure

2-1Danger Room drills

In the present, Storm and Logan run a training session in the Danger Room beneath the school for Rogue, Bobby (Iceman), Kitty and Colossus.

Their opponent is a hologram of a giant mutant-hunting robot, a Sentinel. It ends when Colossus hurls Logan at the robot and Logan cuts off its head.

2-2Scott in mourning

Scott (Cyclops) still can't get over losing Jean, who vanished into Alkali Lake in the last battle. At night, he hears her voice.

2-3Hank at the White House

Hank McCoy (Beast), the government's Secretary of Mutant Affairs, is a blue-furred mutant with a long history with the X-Men.

Meeting with the President and his staff, Hank learns that Worthington Labs has found a way to remove mutant powers. Meanwhile, Mystique has been captured by the government and is being interrogated.

2-4The cure is announced

Worthington Labs announces an injection that suppresses the gene that gives mutants their powers, a 'cure', and offers it to any mutant who wants it.

The cure is made from a young mutant named Jimmy, whose presence switches off the powers of mutants near him. He lives at the Worthington facility on Alcatraz Island in San Francisco Bay.

2-5A divided community

Mutants split between those who want the cure and those outraged at being treated as if they were sick.

At the school, Storm is firmly against it. Rogue, whose touch drains the life and powers of anyone she touches, can't even touch her boyfriend Bobby. Seeing Bobby grow close to Kitty, she finds herself drawn to the cure.

Part 3: Magneto's army

3-1The church meeting

In an old church, mutants have gathered to discuss the cure. Magneto and Pyro walk in.

Magneto warns that the cure will be forced on them and used as a weapon to wipe mutants out. The mutants there decide to follow him.

3-2Callisto and the others

One of the mutants at the meeting, Callisto, is super-fast and can sense where other mutants are and how strong they are.

Magneto recruits her along with others, like Kid Omega, who grows sharp quills, and Arclight, who makes shockwaves by clapping. He uses Callisto's power to track down the captured Mystique.

3-3The convoy attack

Magneto's group attacks a mobile prison carrying mutant prisoners and frees Mystique.

Also released are Juggernaut, a super-strong man who can't be stopped once he gets moving, and Multiple Man, who can make endless copies of himself. Both join up.

3-4Mystique's sacrifice

A cure dart is fired at Magneto. Mystique throws herself in front of it and loses her powers, turning into an ordinary human woman.

Magneto says she is no longer one of them and leaves her there.

3-5Hank resigns

Guns that fire the cure were made without Hank's knowledge. The cure isn't just for those who want it; it has become a weapon.

Learning this, Hank resigns his cabinet post.

Part 4: Jean returns

4-1Alkali Lake

Unable to bear Jean's voice any longer, Scott goes alone to Alkali Lake, where she disappeared.

At the shore he takes off his glasses and fires his optic blast across the water. Then Jean, thought dead, rises out of the lake.

They embrace and kiss, but in the middle of it her face changes.

4-2The glasses left behind

Sensing trouble, Professor X sends Logan and Storm to the lake.

They find rocks floating in the air, Scott's glasses on the ground, and Jean lying unconscious. Scott is nowhere to be found.

4-3The Phoenix

With Jean back at the school, Professor X confesses to Logan: her power was so great that when she was a child, he built walls in her mind to hold it back.

That buried power grew into a separate, purely instinctive personality called the Phoenix. When Jean sacrificed herself at the lake, the walls came down.

Logan is disgusted that Xavier tampered with her mind.

4-4Jean wakes up

When Jean wakes, she acts strange and aggressive, unlike herself. Asked where Scott is, she can't remember, and fears she killed him.

She begs Logan to kill her before she hurts anyone else. When he refuses and says Xavier can help her, the Phoenix takes over and slams him into a wall with her mind.

Jean flees the school and heads for her childhood home.

Part 5: The Grey house

5-1Both sides arrive

Through Callisto, Magneto learns of a powerful mutant and realizes Jean has returned.

Professor X, Logan and Storm reach the Grey house at the same moment as Magneto's group. Xavier and Magneto go inside alone.

5-2Two men, one woman

Inside, Professor X and Magneto each speak to Jean, trying to win her to their side. She is torn between them.

5-3Fighting outside

Outside, Logan and Storm face off against Magneto's followers until a fight breaks out. Storm takes on the lightning-fast Callisto.

5-4Xavier's end

When Professor X tries to reach into Jean's mind, the Phoenix, furious after twenty years locked away, bursts out.

The house starts to break apart. After a battle of minds, the Phoenix disintegrates Professor X into dust.

Jean leaves with Magneto.

5-5The funeral

The school holds Professor X's funeral. The X-Men have lost Xavier, Scott and Jean all at once.

Part 6: Choices

6-1Warren spreads his wings

At the Worthington lab, the adult Warren (Angel) is strapped to a table in front of his father, about to receive the cure.

At the last moment he breaks free and crashes through the window. Spreading his huge wings, he flies off toward Professor X's school.

6-2Rogue's choice

With Professor X gone and still unable to touch Bobby, Rogue decides to take the cure and leaves the school.

Outside the clinics handing out the cure, long lines of mutants wait for their turn.

6-3Mystique talks

Abandoned by Magneto, Mystique tells federal agents where his hideout in the woods is.

6-4The plan in the woods

Magneto's army, now with Jean, is camped in the forest. He decides to attack the Alcatraz lab and kill Jimmy, the boy the cure is made from, so no more can be produced.

6-5An empty camp

Government teams raid the camp. Heat sensors showed many people there, but they find only Multiple Man and his copies. Magneto's army is already on its way to San Francisco.

Part 7: The battle of Alcatraz

7-1Moving the bridge

Magneto tears the Golden Gate Bridge loose, moves it through the air and sets it down against Alcatraz. It becomes a road for his army to cross to the island.

7-2Plastic guns

The soldiers guarding the island carry plastic guns that fire cure darts, which Magneto can't control with magnetism.

The first wave of mutants he sends in is hit with the cure one after another and loses their powers. But Arclight's shockwaves wreck the soldiers' weapons, and Magneto's army begins to overrun them.

7-3The X-Men arrive

Logan, Storm, Hank, Bobby, Kitty and Colossus arrive. They are badly outnumbered.

Magneto calls them traitors for siding with humans and sends his army at them.

7-4Kitty and Juggernaut

Juggernaut smashes through wall after wall of the lab to reach and kill Jimmy. Kitty phases through the walls ahead of him, finds Jimmy and gets him out.

When Juggernaut catches up, he loses his power near Jimmy and charges headfirst into a wall, knocking himself out.

7-5Bobby and Pyro

Bobby fights Pyro, who controls fire, one on one. For the first time, Bobby turns his whole body to ice. He freezes Pyro's hands to stop the flames and knocks him out with a headbutt.

7-6Storm and Callisto

Storm faces the lightning-fast Callisto again and strikes her down with lightning. Callisto is killed.

7-7Angel to the rescue

Magneto's followers throw Worthington II off a lab building. His son Warren swoops in from the sky and catches him as he falls.

7-8Magneto is cured

Logan has Colossus throw him at Magneto to keep him busy. Meanwhile Hank jabs Magneto with a cure syringe.

Magneto's powers are gone. He is an ordinary man.

Part 8: The Phoenix

8-1Jean is shot at

Just as Logan reaches Jean and begins to calm her, army reinforcements arrive and open fire on her.

Enraged, the Phoenix disintegrates the soldiers. Then she starts tearing apart the lab and the whole island, and everyone and everything near her turns to dust, including one of Magneto's followers.

8-2Only Logan can reach her

Logan realizes that with his adamantium skeleton and healing power, he alone can get near the Phoenix.

While Storm and the others get people off the island, Logan pushes toward Jean step by step, his body torn apart and healing over and over.

8-3The hardest choice

The Phoenix asks Logan whether he would die for the others. He answers that he wouldn't die for them, but he would for her.

For a moment Jean is herself again and begs him to save her. Logan tells her he loves her and, in tears, stabs her with his claws. She dies in his arms, and the Phoenix's rampage is over.

Part 9: Afterward

9-1A new chapter

Some time later, mutant rights are finally won. The school stays open, with Storm as headmistress.

The President appoints Hank as ambassador to the United Nations.

9-2Rogue and Bobby

Rogue, having taken the cure, returns to the school and tells Bobby what she did. Without her powers, she can now touch people without hurting them.

9-3The chessboard

Magneto, powerless, sits alone at a chessboard. When he holds his hand toward a metal chess piece, it wobbles slightly. His powers may not be completely gone.

9-4After the credits

Dr. Moira MacTaggert checks on a patient in a coma. Moving a dying man's mind into the body of someone with no higher brain function had come up earlier in a class at the school.

The patient greets her in Professor X's voice. Startled, she says, "Charles?"

Summary (spoilers through the end)

What happened with the cure

1) Worthington Labs announces a cure made from the boy Jimmy (Part 2).

2) A cure dart meant for Magneto hits Mystique, who loses her powers. Learning the cure has been turned into a weapon, Hank resigns (Part 3).

3) Warren escapes just before receiving it; Rogue chooses to take it (Part 6).

4) Magneto attacks Alcatraz to kill Jimmy, but Hank injects him with the cure and his powers are gone (Part 7).

5) In the end, Magneto's power seems to be faintly returning (Part 9).

Jean and the Phoenix

1) When Jean was a child, Professor X walled off her overwhelming power inside her mind (Parts 1 and 4).

2) Jean rises from Alkali Lake, and Scott disappears (Part 4).

3) Waking at the school, she begs Logan to kill her, then the Phoenix takes over and she flees (Part 4).

4) At her old home she destroys Professor X and joins Magneto (Part 5).

5) Shot at on Alcatraz, she runs wild, and Logan ends her life (Part 8).

Who died

- Scott (Part 4): he vanishes after kissing the resurrected Jean at Alkali Lake. It later turns out she killed him.

- Professor X (Part 5): trying to hold back Jean's mind, he is disintegrated by the enraged Phoenix. After the credits, his mind turns out to live on in another body.

- Callisto (Part 7): struck by Storm's lightning in the battle of Alcatraz.

- The soldiers and one of Magneto's followers (Part 8): disintegrated by the Phoenix after the soldiers fire on Jean.

- Jean (Part 8): to stop the Phoenix, Logan stabs her with his claws, answering her plea.
