Key terms

The cure
A drug that removes mutant powers, made from one boy's DNA.
The Phoenix
An immensely powerful and dangerous force locked deep inside Jean's mind.
The Brotherhood
Magneto's army of mutants.
Alcatraz
The island in San Francisco Bay housing the cure lab, and the site of the final battle.
The Danger Room
The training room beneath the school where the X-Men practice combat.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why does Jean act like a different person?

Professor X had locked away an overwhelming force inside her, the Phoenix. Alkali Lake broke that lock.

Where did the cure come from?

From a boy, Leech, whose power cancels the powers of mutants near him. He was kept in the lab.

Why does Rogue take the cure?

Her touch drains people, so she can't even touch Bobby. She chooses it so she can live a normal life.

What does the final chess scene mean?

Magneto, stripped of his powers by the cure, makes a metal chess piece twitch, hinting the cure may not be permanent.

Easy-to-miss details

Twenty years earlier

The film opens with a younger Professor X and Magneto visiting young Jean together, back when they were friends.

The Golden Gate Bridge

Magneto rips the Golden Gate Bridge loose and uses it as a road to the island.

Kitty vs. Juggernaut

Kitty, who phases through walls, races the unstoppable Juggernaut in a highlight of the final battle.
