Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

Thor's third film

It follows Thor and The Dark World, picking up from Loki posing as Odin at the end of the last film.

Where the Hulk went

It reveals where the Hulk ended up after flying off alone at the end of Age of Ultron.

Lots of laughs

It's much funnier and lighter than the earlier Thor films.

There are scenes during and after the credits

The mid-credits scene leads straight into the next big battle.

StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

Part 1: From the land of fire to Asgard

Two years after Sokovia, Thor is a prisoner of the fire demon Surtur, who tells him of Ragnarok, the day Asgard will burn.

Thor defeats Surtur, takes his crown and returns home, where he exposes Loki, who has been posing as Odin and enjoying a play in his own honor.

Part 2: Farewell to a father

With Doctor Strange's help, the brothers find Odin in Norway. He warns that his death will free his imprisoned firstborn, Hela, and fades into light.

Hela appears, crushes Thor's hammer in one hand, and knocks Thor and Loki out of the Bifrost into space.

Part 3: Hela's Asgard

In Asgard, Hela kills Thor's friends, crushes the army and makes Skurge her henchman. She reveals the conquests she shared with Odin and raises her ancient army and a giant wolf.

Heimdall escapes with the sword that controls the Bifrost and hides the people in the mountains.

Part 4: Sakaar, the junk planet

Thor lands on the junk planet Sakaar, is captured by Scrapper 142, a former Valkyrie, and sold to the Grandmaster, where Loki has already landed.

Shorn of his hair and sent into the arena, Thor faces the missing Hulk. His lightning returns, but the Grandmaster's disc takes him down.

Part 5: The Revengers

The Hulk has no wish to go home. Thor finds the Quinjet, and a recording of Natasha turns the Hulk back into Bruce.

142 decides to face Hela and joins them, bringing the captured Loki. Thor names the team the Revengers.

Part 6: Escape from Sakaar

Loki tries to betray Thor, but Thor sees it coming and leaves him behind. Korg and the gladiators rise up.

Thor, Bruce and 142 steal one of the Grandmaster's ships and fly through a wormhole toward Asgard.

Part 7: The battle for Asgard

Thor's team reaches the people trapped on the bridge by Hela's army. The Hulk battles the giant wolf, and Loki arrives with the rebels in a huge ship to take the people aboard.

Skurge turns on Hela and dies protecting the people.

Part 8: Ragnarok

Losing his right eye to Hela, Thor is encouraged by a vision of Odin and unleashes lightning without a hammer. But Hela can't be beaten on Asgard.

Realizing Asgard is its people, not a place, Thor has Loki revive Surtur, who destroys Asgard and Hela with it. Now king, Thor sets course for Earth with his people.

Key terms

Ragnarok
The prophecy of Asgard's destruction, in which the fire demon Surtur burns it to the ground.
Hela
The goddess of death, Odin's firstborn and Thor's older sister, imprisoned for ages.
Sakaar
A planet where junk and people from across the universe wash up. The Grandmaster rules it.
The Contest of Champions
Gladiator fights staged on Sakaar, the Grandmaster's favorite entertainment.
The Valkyries
An elite force of women warriors from Asgard's past.
The Eternal Flame
The fire that restores Surtur to full power, kept in Asgard's vault.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why is Hela so powerful?

She draws her power from Asgard itself. As long as she's there, she's nearly unbeatable, so Thor chooses to destroy Asgard.

Why was the Hulk on Sakaar for two years?

He stayed as the Hulk and became the star of the arena. Bruce never got to come back out.

What happened to Thor's eye?

He loses it fighting Hela, leaving him one-eyed like his father.

What was Heimdall doing?

He refused to serve Hela and hid Asgard's people in a secret refuge.

Easy-to-miss details

Loki's play

In Asgard, Loki has staged a play about his own heroics. Look closely at the actors.

Korg's voice

The rock warrior Korg is played by the film's director, Taika Waititi.

Stan Lee's cameo

Marvel Comics legend Stan Lee appears as the barber who cuts Thor's hair on Sakaar.

The battle music

Led Zeppelin's "Immigrant Song" plays in the big fight scenes.

The ending and post-creditsSpoilers

After the end credits

Mid-credits: Thanos's ship appears in front of the Asgardian ship. Post-credits: a Grandmaster gag.

My takeOpinion

A bright, funny film quite unlike the earlier Thor entries. It seems to be fooling around, yet it properly delivers a heavy ending, the loss of a homeland, and I like that.
