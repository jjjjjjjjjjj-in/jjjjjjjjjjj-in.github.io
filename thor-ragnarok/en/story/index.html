Part 1: From the land of fire to Asgard

1-1Thor in chains

Two years after the battle against Ultron in Sokovia. Thor has been traveling the universe, and is now a prisoner of the fire demon Surtur in Muspelheim.

As Thor hangs in chains, Surtur tells him that Odin is no longer on Asgard. Once Surtur's crown is placed in the Eternal Flame in Odin's vault, he will return to full size and burn Asgard to the ground. That is the prophesied Ragnarok.

1-2Taking Surtur's crown

Thor breaks free, summons his hammer Mjolnir, and smashes through Surtur's forces, tearing the crown from Surtur's head. He believes he has stopped Ragnarok.

He calls for Heimdall, keeper of the Bifrost, but gets no answer, and keeps calling as a fire dragon chases him.

1-3The new gatekeeper

At the Bifrost observatory, the new gatekeeper Skurge is showing off things he picked up on Earth to some women. He finally hears Thor and opens the bridge, and Thor returns to Asgard. The dragon chasing him is cut off as the bridge closes.

Thor asks about Heimdall and learns that Odin removed him as a traitor, and that he has disappeared.

1-4Loki's play

In Asgard, "Odin" is happily watching a play that honors Loki as a hero. Loki was thought to have died in The Dark World.

Thor throws Mjolnir into the distance and pins "Odin" in its path. Just before the hammer returns, "Odin" drops the disguise: he is Loki.

Part 2: Farewell to a father

2-1The demolished retirement home

Loki admits he put a spell on the real Odin and left him in a retirement home in New York. When they get there, the building has been torn down.

Then a hole opens under Loki's feet and he vanishes, leaving behind a card with an address.

2-2Doctor Strange's house

The card leads to the Sanctum Sanctorum, home of the sorcerer Doctor Strange. He keeps watch on dangerous visitors to Earth, he says, and Loki is on his list.

Strange agrees to help as long as they leave Earth once they find Odin, who is in Norway. He pulls Loki back out of the endless fall he had been stuck in and sends the brothers to Norway.

2-3A confession on the Norwegian coast

Odin is waiting on a seaside cliff, already free of Loki's spell. He tells his sons that he is dying and that Ragnarok has already begun.

He then reveals a sister they never knew: his firstborn, Hela, the goddess of death. She once fought at his side, but she grew so bloodthirsty that he imprisoned her, and his life is what holds her prison shut.

Telling his sons he loves them, Odin fades into golden light.

2-4The hammer is shattered

A dark portal opens at once and Hela steps out, ordering them to kneel.

Thor throws Mjolnir. Hela catches it with one hand and crushes it to pieces.

2-5Thrown off the Bifrost

Loki calls on Skurge to bring them back. The brothers escape into the Bifrost, but Hela leaps in after them.

Inside the beam she knocks Thor and Loki out into space, and arrives in Asgard alone.

Part 3: Hela's Asgard

3-1The deaths of Thor's friends

When Hela arrives at the observatory, Thor's old friends Volstagg and Fandral stand against her, and she kills them both.

She cuts down almost the whole Asgardian army by herself. Hogun, the third of Thor's companions, is also killed by her.

3-2Skurge kneels

To save his own life, Skurge kneels before Hela. She makes him her executioner.

3-3History beneath the ceiling

In the palace, Hela tears away the peaceful murals on the ceiling. Underneath is the true history: Hela and Odin conquering realm after realm together.

She tells Skurge that Asgard's riches came from those conquests. But Odin later chose to be a peaceful king, and when she wanted to keep expanding, he locked her away and erased her from history.

3-4The dead rise

Hela goes to Odin's vault, dismisses the Infinity Gauntlet there as a fake, and uses the Eternal Flame to raise the ancient warriors who once fought beside her, along with her giant wolf Fenris.

She plans to use the Bifrost to conquer the other realms.

3-5Heimdall takes the sword

But the sword that controls the Bifrost has already been taken by Heimdall, who has been in hiding.

He shelters the people fleeing Hela in a hidden refuge in the mountains.

Part 4: Sakaar, the junk planet

4-1Scrapper 142

Thor crash-lands on Sakaar, a planet ringed by wormholes where the universe's junk rains down. Scavengers catch him in a net.

A hard-drinking woman called Scrapper 142 arrives in a small ship and drives them off. She sticks an obedience disc on Thor's neck, leaving him helpless.

4-2The Grandmaster, and Loki got there first

142 sells Thor to Sakaar's ruler, the Grandmaster, for a fortune. The Grandmaster is whimsical, and he has a device that melts anyone who displeases him.

Standing beside him is Loki, who fell onto Sakaar weeks earlier and is already one of the Grandmaster's favorites.

4-3The haircut

Thor's long blond hair is cut off, and he is entered in the Grandmaster's Contest of Champions. The Grandmaster promises to grant his wish if he can beat the champion.

In the holding area Thor meets Korg, a warrior made of rock, and his friend Miek. Korg says he once tried to start a revolution, but not enough people joined in.

4-4Once a Valkyrie

Before the fight, Thor notices the mark on 142's arm and realizes she is one of the Valkyries, Asgard's legendary women warriors, said to have been wiped out fighting Hela long ago.

He asks for her help, but she ignores him.

4-5The champion is the Hulk

In front of a roaring crowd, the champion turns out to be the Hulk, missing since the battle of Sokovia. Thor is delighted, calling him "a friend from work," but the Hulk attacks for real.

Thor tries the words Natasha used to calm him, "The sun's getting real low," but they work for only a moment.

4-6Lightning, then defeat

Cornered, Thor feels lightning surge through him and starts to overpower the Hulk.

But just before he can win, the Grandmaster shocks him through the disc on his neck, and the Hulk knocks him out.

Part 5: The Revengers

5-1The Hulk's quarters

Thor wakes up in the Hulk's lavish quarters. The Hulk is a star here and has no wish to return to Earth. Thor suggests they escape together, but the Hulk refuses.

5-2Heimdall's message

Through Heimdall's far-seeing power, Thor sees what is happening on Asgard. Heimdall is hiding the people in the mountains.

Heimdall says he will lead them out of Asgard if its defenders fall, and tells Thor to come home through one of the wormholes around Sakaar.

5-3Valkyrie's past

Thor tells 142 that Odin is dead and Hela has seized Asgard, and asks again for her help.

But she lost every one of her fellow Valkyries fighting Hela long ago and resents Asgard's royal family. She refuses again.

5-4The Quinjet and Bruce

Catching 142 off guard, Thor gets his own disc off and slips out. He finds the Quinjet, the Avengers' jet the Hulk arrived in.

He tries voice passwords until "Point Break," Tony's nickname for him, works. The Hulk comes to stop him, but a recording of Natasha on the screen turns him back into Bruce Banner for the first time since Sokovia.

5-5Bruce, two years later

Bruce thinks he is still in the middle of the battle of Sokovia. Learning that two years have passed and that he is on a distant planet throws him into confusion.

He fears that if he becomes the Hulk again, he may never come back.

5-6142 joins

The Grandmaster orders 142 and Loki to bring Thor back. Loki uses magic to look into 142's memories and sees the ancient battle where Hela slaughtered the Valkyries, but 142 overpowers him.

She brings the captured Loki to Thor and says she will fight Hela with him. Thor names the team the "Revengers."

Part 6: Escape from Sakaar

6-1The brothers' plan

Loki offers to help in exchange for a way off Sakaar, and the group plans to steal one of the Grandmaster's ships.

Thor and Loki take out the guards with "Get Help," a trick they say they used as kids.

6-2Seeing through Loki

Thor tells Loki he thinks it's best they go their separate ways. Sure enough, Loki tries to sell him out and calls the guards.

But Thor has already stuck an obedience disc on Loki's back. Loki is left on the floor, twitching from the shocks.

6-3The gladiators revolt

Thor's group frees the gladiators from their discs. Led by Korg and Miek, they rise up, and the city erupts.

The abandoned Loki is found by Korg's rebels and joins them.

6-4Through the wormhole

Thor, Bruce and 142 take off in one of the Grandmaster's ships. Thor shoots down the pursuers while 142 dodges the rest.

They dive into a huge wormhole around Sakaar and head for Asgard.

Part 7: The battle for Asgard

7-1The people on the bridge

On Asgard, Hela's soldiers have found the people's refuge. Heimdall leads them across the Bifrost bridge to escape.

But Hela's undead army and the wolf Fenris catch up with them, trapping the people on the bridge.

7-2Thor's team arrives

Thor lands on the bridge in a blast of lightning and starts cutting through the army, while 142 fights to protect the people.

Bruce leaps from the ship to transform into the Hulk, but simply face-plants on the bridge as Bruce. Later he becomes the Hulk and takes on Fenris.

7-3Loki returns

As Hela's army keeps coming, Loki arrives in a huge spaceship carrying Korg and the rebel gladiators.

He announces that their savior is here and starts getting the people aboard.

7-4Skurge's choice

Watching the people flee, Skurge, Hela's executioner, has a change of heart. With the two rifles he brought back from Earth, he stands alone against Hela's army.

He buys time for the people to board the ship, and dies doing it.

Part 8: Ragnarok

8-1Losing an eye

In the palace, Thor faces Hela alone. Without his hammer he is outmatched, and she slashes out his right eye.

8-2A vision of Odin

As Thor lies beaten, a vision of Odin appears. He asks, "Are you Thor, the God of Hammers?" and reminds him the hammer was only ever a way to help him control his power.

Thor wakes with lightning pouring out of him like never before. He comes down on the bridge and wipes out Hela's soldiers in one blow.

8-3Asgard is its people

Hela still won't fall. She draws her power from Asgard, and as long as she is there she keeps getting stronger.

Thor realizes that Asgard is not a place but its people, and decides the only way to stop Hela is to let Ragnarok happen.

8-4Surtur rises

Thor asks Loki to put Surtur's crown in the Eternal Flame. In the vault, Loki pauses at the Tesseract stored there.

When the crown meets the flame, Surtur is reborn at full size. The Hulk leaps at him, but Thor calls him back and everyone escapes to the ship.

8-5The end of Asgard

From the ship, the people watch Asgard burn. Hela tries to stop Surtur, but he drives his sword into Asgard, which explodes, taking Hela with it.

8-6The new king

Aboard the ship, Thor takes the throne at the urging of Heimdall and the others. He tells Loki that if he were really there, he might even give him a hug, and tosses something at him. Loki catches it and says, "I'm here."

Thor decides to take his people to Earth.

Part 9: The credit scenes

9-1Mid-credits

On the ship, Loki wonders whether going to Earth is really a good idea. Thor says it will be fine.

Then an enormous spaceship appears in front of them.

9-2After the credits

On Sakaar after the revolt, the Grandmaster, surrounded by the rebels on a junk heap, tries to tell them the revolution was a tie.

Summary

How Ragnarok came about

Surtur said that once his crown met the Eternal Flame, he would burn Asgard to the ground (Part 1). Thor thought he had stopped it by taking the crown.

But Hela drew her power from Asgard and couldn't be beaten there (Part 8). Deciding that Asgard was its people, not a place, Thor got the people onto the ship and had Loki put the crown in the flame. The reborn Surtur destroyed Asgard and Hela with it (Part 8).

Who joined Thor

- Hulk / Bruce: spent two years after Sokovia as Sakaar's champion. A recording of Natasha turned him back into Bruce (Part 5), and in the end he fought Fenris as the Hulk (Part 7).

- Valkyrie (Scrapper 142): lost all her sisters to Hela and had been living on Sakaar. She faced her memories of Hela and joined Thor (Part 5).

- Loki: tried to betray Thor and was left behind (Part 6), but came back with the rebel gladiators in a ship and helped save the people of Asgard (Part 7).

- Skurge: became Hela's executioner to save his own life, but died protecting the people in the end (Parts 3 and 7).

Who died (in order)

- Odin (Part 2): his life came to an end on the Norwegian coast and he faded into golden light. His death freed the imprisoned Hela.

- Volstagg and Fandral (Part 3): Thor's friends, killed when they stood against Hela on her arrival in Asgard.

- Hogun (Part 3): Thor's friend, killed standing up to Hela.

- Skurge (Part 7): stood alone against Hela's army with his two rifles to buy time for the people to board the ship, and died.

- Hela (Part 8): destroyed when Surtur, reborn at Thor's request through Loki, drove his sword into Asgard and blew it apart.
