Thor: Ragnarok: The Full Story, Scene by Scene

Part 1: Back from the land of fire

Thor in chains

In the fiery realm of Muspelheim, a chained Thor listens as the fire demon Surtur describes Ragnarok, the day he'll burn Asgard to the ground. Thor breaks free, defeats Surtur, and takes his crown, the source of his power.

A new gatekeeper

The Bifrost's keeper is no longer Heimdall but a man named Skurge. Thor has him open the bridge and returns to Asgard.

Loki's play

In Asgard, "Odin" is enjoying a play celebrating Loki as a hero. Thor uses his hammer to force the truth out: "Odin" is Loki.

Part 2: A father's end, a sister's arrival

A detour in New York

Loki says he left Odin in a retirement home on Earth, but the building has been demolished. Thor meets Doctor Strange, who helps them find their father.

The Norwegian coast

On a cliff in Norway, Odin tells his sons he has a firstborn daughter, Hela, who will return once he dies. Then he fades into light.

The hammer shatters

Hela appears at once, catches Thor's hammer in one hand and crushes it. Thor and Loki flee on the Bifrost, but Hela follows and knocks them out into space.

Part 3: Hela's Asgard

Taking Asgard

Hela arrives in Asgard and cuts down every soldier who stands against her. Skurge becomes her henchman to save his own life.

Hidden history

Hela strips away the ceiling murals to reveal the history Odin hid. Long ago, she and Odin conquered realm after realm together, until Odin chose to become a peaceful king and locked her away.

The dead rise

Hela raises her ancient army and a giant wolf from beneath the palace. Heimdall secretly leads the people to a hidden refuge in the mountains.

Part 4: The junk planet

Sold

Thor lands on Sakaar, a planet where the universe's junk piles up. A hard-drinking scrapper puts a control disc on his neck and sells him to the planet's ruler, the Grandmaster.

Loki got there first

At the Grandmaster's side is Loki, who arrived weeks earlier and is already in his good graces.

The haircut

Thor's long hair is cut off and he's entered in the gladiator games. In the holding area, he meets the rock warrior Korg.

The opponent

Thor's opponent is the Grandmaster's champion: the Hulk. Thor is thrilled, but the Hulk attacks for real. Just as Thor's lightning flares, the Grandmaster's disc knocks him out.

Part 5: The ragtag team

The Hulk's room

The Hulk is a star here and doesn't want to leave. Thor learns the scrapper was once one of the Valkyries, Asgard's women warriors, all wiped out by Hela.

Bruce comes back

Thor finds the Quinjet the Hulk arrived in. A recording of Natasha turns the Hulk back into Bruce for the first time in two years.

Joining up

Valkyrie decides to face her past and joins Thor. Loki tries to betray them, but Thor sees it coming and leaves him behind.

Escaping Sakaar

Loki helps Korg's gladiator revolt and takes one of the Grandmaster's ships. Thor's team dodges their pursuers and flies through a giant wormhole toward Asgard.

Part 6: The battle for Asgard

The people on the bridge

Heimdall's refugees are cornered on the bridge by Hela's army. Thor lands with a bolt of lightning, the Hulk and Valkyrie join in, and Loki arrives with a huge ship to evacuate the people.

God of Thunder

Fighting Hela, Thor loses an eye. A vision of Odin tells him he was never the god of hammers. Thor realizes the thunder is within him and unleashes it without Mjolnir.

Skurge's choice

To protect the ship full of refugees, Skurge turns on Hela and dies fighting her army alone.

Part 7: Ragnarok

Asgard is its people

Hela draws her power from Asgard itself and can't be beaten there. Thor realizes Asgard is not a place, but its people.

Surtur rises

Loki places Surtur's crown in the Eternal Flame, and the fire demon is reborn. Surtur destroys Asgard, and Hela with it.

The new king

Aboard the ship, Thor takes the throne. With his people, he sets course for Earth.

Part 8: The credit scenes

Mid-credits

As Thor and Loki talk aboard the ship bound for Earth, an enormous spaceship appears in front of them.

After the credits

In the wreckage on Sakaar, the Grandmaster tries to tell the rebels that the revolution was a tie.
