Episode 1: Glorious Purpose

1-1Loki vanishes from New York, 2012

New York, 2012. Loki, defeated and captured by the Avengers, sees the Tesseract slide across the floor in the chaos caused by the time-traveling Avengers. He grabs it and vanishes. He was supposed to be taken back to Asgard, so this moment creates a branch in the flow of time.

He lands in the Gobi Desert in Mongolia and starts grandly telling the locals he is destined to rule.

1-2Arrested by the TVA

Armed agents step out of a glowing door: the TVA, the organization that guards the flow of time. Their leader, Hunter B-15, strikes Loki with a time stick that slows him down. He is arrested for crimes against the Sacred Timeline, and the Tesseract is confiscated.

At TVA headquarters, Loki's magic does not work. He is passed from desk to desk: his clothes are taken, a machine scans him, and he must sign an enormously long record of every word he has ever said. The Tesseract ends up on the desk of a clerk named Casey.

1-3Mobius's crime scene

Meanwhile, TVA agent Mobius M. Mobius is investigating a church in Aix-en-Provence, France, in 1549. TVA soldiers sent after a variant have been killed there, one of a string of similar attacks.

A local child who wanders in is asked who killed them and points to a stained-glass devil. The child's teeth are stained blue from "Kablooie," a blue candy that should not exist in that era. Mobius takes it as a clue.

1-4Miss Minutes and the trial

In a waiting room, the TVA's animated clock mascot, Miss Minutes, explains in a video: long ago, timelines fought a great multiversal war. The three Time-Keepers ended it, merged time into a single flow, and created the TVA to protect it. Anyone who strays from that flow is a "variant," and stray branches are "reset."

At his trial before Judge Ravonna Renslayer, Loki doesn't take it seriously at first, then insists the Avengers were the ones who time-traveled. Ravonna answers that the Avengers' actions followed the set timeline while his did not. He is found guilty and sentenced to be reset.

Just then Mobius, back at the TVA, steps in. Thinking this variant Loki might be useful, he takes custody of him.

1-5His life on screen

Mobius brings Loki to a room where scenes from his life can be replayed. He shows Loki's defeat by the Avengers in New York and asks why he hurts people. One clip even shows Loki as the famous hijacker D. B. Cooper, jumping from a plane.

Loki says he wants to rule so he can free people from the burden of making wrong choices, and he rejects the idea that the TVA decides his fate.

So Mobius shows him the future he was meant to have: his adoptive mother Frigga killed because of him. Loki is furious and shaken.

1-6Infinity Stones in a drawer

Loki escapes and takes the Tesseract back from Casey's desk. But the desk drawer holds several Infinity Stones tossed in carelessly. Their power doesn't work inside the TVA; here they're just paperweights.

Overwhelmed by the TVA's power, Loki returns to the screening room and watches the rest of his future alone: making peace with his brother Thor, the death of his father Odin, and his own death at Thanos's hands. He realizes he can never return to his timeline.

1-7The job

Mobius returns. Loki admits that everything bad he has done came from wanting control over his own life.

Mobius makes him an offer: a variant has been killing TVA soldiers and stealing the devices the TVA uses to reset timelines. He wants Loki's help catching it. The culprit is another Loki.

1-8Oklahoma, 1858

Oklahoma, 1858. TVA soldiers are examining an object that should not exist in that era. A hooded figure appears in the distance, and the soldiers are engulfed in flames.

Episode 2: The Variant

2-1A Renaissance fair, 1985

Oshkosh, Wisconsin, 1985. A TVA squad arrives at a Renaissance fair and is ambushed by the variant, who uses magic to possess people and turn the soldiers against each other, then takes Hunter C-20.

2-2Bargaining at the scene

Loki joins Mobius and B-15 at the scene. B-15 takes away the knives he has hidden. Loki stalls, claiming the variant is still nearby, and tries to bargain: let him meet the Time-Keepers, and promise not to erase him once the variant is caught.

Mobius sees through the lie, and the TVA resets that branch.

2-3Loki reads the files

Back at the TVA, Ravonna objects to using Loki. Mobius asks for one more chance.

Loki reads the variant's case files all night, and along the way learns that Asgard was destroyed in Ragnarok.

Then he has an idea: the variant may be hiding in places just before an apocalypse. If everything there is about to be destroyed, nothing done there changes the timeline, so the TVA never notices.

2-4Testing it in Pompeii

To test the idea, Loki and Mobius go to Pompeii in 79 AD, just before Mount Vesuvius erupts. Loki runs around shouting warnings to the townspeople, but the TVA's instruments register no branch. He was right.

They debate whether free will exists if everything is already decided.

2-5The gum clue

The Kablooie candy found at the 1549 crime scene in France becomes the key. Loki and Mobius work out that the variant is hiding in a town in Alabama in 2050, just before it is destroyed by a huge hurricane.

2-6The superstore in the storm

Loki, Mobius, B-15 and the others enter Roxxcart, a superstore where people have taken shelter from the storm. Loki searches the store on his own.

The variant possesses B-15, then jumps from body to body through nearby shoppers and staff as she talks to him.

Meanwhile, the soldiers find the missing C-20. Terrified, she says she told the variant where the Time-Keepers are.

2-7The variant revealed

The variant lowers her hood: she is a female Loki. Loki proposes they overthrow the Time-Keepers together, but she refuses.

She activates the stolen TVA reset charges and sends them to many points along the timeline. Branches erupt across many eras at once, throwing the TVA into chaos.

She escapes through a time door, and Loki jumps in after her.

Episode 3: Lamentis

3-1C-20's memory

Before Alabama: the variant had projected a memory from C-20's life before the TVA into her mind. The two chat over drinks at a bar, and that is how the variant got the Time-Keepers' location out of her.

3-2A fight at the TVA, and an escape

Through the door from Alabama, the two arrive at TVA headquarters. The variant heads for the Time-Keepers while Loki tries to capture her, and they fight.

Ravonna appears and attacks them both. Loki uses the variant's TemPad, a time-travel device, to jump away with her.

3-3Lamentis-1, 2077

They land on Lamentis-1 in 2077, a moon about to be crushed by its planet. But the TemPad's battery is dead.

Only the variant knows how to recharge it, and Loki has hidden the TemPad with magic. Reluctantly, they team up. The Ark, a ship evacuating people from the moon, might give them the power they need.

3-4The train to the Ark

They sneak onto a train headed for the Ark. Hostile at first, they slowly open up. Loki talks about learning magic from his mother Frigga. The variant says her name is Sylvie and that she taught herself magic. They also talk about love.

3-5Loki's drunken song

In the dining car, Loki gets drunk and loudly sings an Asgardian song. The passengers love it, but the two, who have no right to be aboard, draw attention.

Guards fight them, and Loki is thrown off the train. Sylvie jumps after him. In the process, the TemPad breaks.

3-6Who the agents really are

The two set out on foot, planning to steal the Ark itself.

On the way, Sylvie tells Loki something important: everyone working at the TVA is a variant who once lived in the timeline. Their memories have been wiped, so they don't know it. Loki, told the agents were created by the Time-Keepers, is stunned.

3-7The Ark falls

Fragments rain from the sky onto a town full of panicking people. Fighting through the guards, the two get right up to the Ark.

But before their eyes, the Ark is struck by a falling fragment and destroyed. They are stranded on the dying moon.

Episode 4: The Nexus Event

4-1Young Sylvie

Long ago. Ravonna, then a TVA hunter, arrests a young Sylvie on Asgard for crimes against the Sacred Timeline and erases her timeline.

Brought to the TVA, Sylvie steals Ravonna's TemPad during her trial and escapes into the timeline.

4-2What happened to C-20

At the present-day TVA, Mobius asks Ravonna to let him see C-20. Ravonna says C-20 died after her mind broke under the variant's enchantment.

4-3A bond on a dying moon

On Lamentis-1, amid the falling debris, Loki and Sylvie sit side by side and talk. Sylvie describes a life spent on the run, hiding in apocalypses.

A strong emotional bond forms between them, and in that moment it creates a huge, unprecedented branch: a Nexus Event. The TVA detects it, finds them, and brings them back.

4-4Sif's time loop

At the TVA, Loki and Sylvie are separated. Mobius locks Loki in a time-loop cell, replaying the moment on Asgard when Loki cut the warrior Sif's hair as a prank. Each time, Sif beats him and tells him he will always be alone.

Mobius pulls him out to question him about the branch. Realizing Loki has fallen for Sylvie, he mocks him for being so in love with himself.

Loki tells him the TVA's staff are all variants, but Mobius sends him back into the loop.

4-5Mobius's discovery, and B-15

Mobius secretly takes Ravonna's TemPad and finds a recording: C-20, supposedly broken, calmly answering Ravonna's questions and saying the TVA's staff are variants. Loki was telling the truth.

Meanwhile B-15, who glimpsed memories when the variant possessed her, takes Sylvie to Alabama in 2050 and asks to see her own life before the TVA. She learns she too is a variant.

4-6Mobius is pruned

Mobius frees Loki from the cell, but Ravonna and her soldiers confront them. Mobius admits he betrayed her and that he knows he is a variant.

On Ravonna's order, Mobius is pruned. He vanishes and seems to be dead.

4-7The truth about the Time-Keepers

Ravonna takes Loki and Sylvie before the Time-Keepers. On the way, Sylvie asks why she was arrested as a child; Ravonna says she doesn't remember.

The Time-Keepers order them deleted. At that moment B-15 releases their restraints. Loki and Sylvie fight together and defeat Ravonna and the guards; B-15 is knocked out.

Sylvie cuts off a Time-Keeper's head: it's an empty mechanical puppet. All three are fakes.

4-8Loki is pruned

Loki tries to tell Sylvie how he feels. But Ravonna gets up and prunes him, and he vanishes.

Enraged, Sylvie overpowers Ravonna and demands the truth about the TVA.

4-9Mid-credits

Loki, supposedly erased, wakes up in a place strewn with ruins. Four other Lokis stand before him and tell him to come with them if he wants to survive.

Episode 5: Journey into Mystery

5-1Sylvie and Ravonna

At the TVA, Sylvie interrogates Ravonna, who says even she doesn't know who created the TVA. Pruned things aren't destroyed: they're sent to the Void, a wasteland at the end of time, and no one has ever come back. They conclude that the TVA's creator must be hiding beyond the Void.

Ravonna and Miss Minutes stall until soldiers arrive. About to be caught, Sylvie prunes herself.

After interrogating the imprisoned B-15, Ravonna tells Miss Minutes to help her find the TVA's creator.

5-2The Lokis of the Void

In the Void, Loki is with four other Lokis: the elderly Classic Loki, the child Kid Loki, the bragging Boastful Loki, and Alligator Loki.

Then Alioth appears, a gigantic cloud-like monster that devours anything in the Void. The Lokis flee from it.

5-3Classic Loki's story

The Lokis share their stories. Classic Loki says that when Thanos was about to kill him, he faked his death, escaped, and lived alone in hiding, until he grew lonely.

5-4Sylvie and Mobius

Sylvie arrives in the Void and is attacked by Alioth right away. She enchants it for a moment, but is in danger until Mobius, supposedly erased, appears and helps her escape. Mobius is alive.

5-5Lokis betraying Lokis

Boastful Loki betrays the group, selling them out to another gang of Lokis led by President Loki so he can rule.

But in President Loki's gang, the Lokis start betraying one another too, and a huge brawl breaks out. In the confusion, Classic Loki helps Loki, Kid Loki and Alligator Loki escape. They meet up with Sylvie and Mobius.

5-6Mobius goes back

Sylvie has the TemPad she took from Ravonna. Mobius uses it to return to the TVA, determined to tell its staff the truth. He invites the Lokis to come along, but Kid Loki and the others stay in the Void.

Loki and Sylvie decide to enchant Alioth and reach whoever is beyond it.

5-7Classic Loki's end

Loki distracts Alioth while Sylvie tries to enchant it, but both fail and they are nearly devoured.

Then Classic Loki comes back. Straining all his magic, he conjures a life-size illusion of the city of Asgard to draw Alioth's attention. The two are saved, but Classic Loki is consumed by Alioth and dies, giving his life to protect them.

5-8The citadel beyond the monster

Loki and Sylvie work together and enchant Alioth with their combined magic. As the cloud clears, a citadel at the end of time appears beyond it, and they walk toward it.

Episode 6: For All Time. Always.

6-1Miss Minutes at the entrance

Miss Minutes greets Loki and Sylvie as they enter the Citadel at the End of Time. She relays an offer from her master, He Who Remains: he will return them to the timeline, giving Loki sovereignty and Sylvie happiness. They refuse.

6-2Ravonna, Mobius and B-15

At the TVA, Ravonna receives information that He Who Remains has sent through Miss Minutes. Mobius confronts her, and they accuse each other of betrayal. Mobius tries to prune her, but she overcomes him and leaves, saying she is going in search of free will.

Meanwhile, in Fremont, Ohio, in 2018, B-15 is chased by TVA soldiers. She shows them a school vice-principal who is a variant of Ravonna from before the TVA: proof that its staff are all variants.

6-3The master of the Citadel is just a man

Deep in the Citadel, Loki and Sylvie meet He Who Remains, and are surprised he is neither god nor monster, just a man.

When Sylvie attacks, he dodges with a TemPad. He says he has seen all of the past, present and future, so he knows everything they will do. He even guided them here.

6-4The war, and the TVA's beginning

He Who Remains explains why the TVA exists. In the 31st century, variants of himself in different universes discovered one another and made contact. Some of them set out to conquer other universes, and a multiversal war began.

He used Alioth to end the war, keeping only his own timeline, and created the TVA to make sure no new branches ever formed.

6-5Two paths

Saying he has grown tired, He Who Remains offers them a choice. Kill him, and time will no longer be a single flow; worse variants of himself may emerge and start the war again. Or take his place running the TVA and protecting the timeline.

Then the timeline starts to diverge, and he says he no longer knows what comes next.

6-6Loki and Sylvie fight

Sylvie moves to kill He Who Remains. Loki, afraid he may be telling the truth, fights her to stop it, and tells her he just wants to keep her safe.

They kiss, but right after, Sylvie uses the TemPad to send Loki back to the TVA.

6-7The end of He Who Remains

Alone with him, Sylvie drives her sword into He Who Remains, who no longer resists, to end the TVA that stole her life as a child at its source. He smiles and dies saying she'll see him soon.

The timeline splits into countless branches. A multiverse is born that pruning can no longer contain.

6-8A different TVA

Back at the TVA, Loki finds Mobius and B-15 and desperately warns them about He Who Remains' variants. But they don't know him. Mobius asks who he is.

The statues of the Time-Keepers in the TVA's hall have been replaced by a statue of a man who looks like He Who Remains. Loki has arrived at a TVA in a slightly different world.

Summary (spoilers through the finale)

The truth about the Time-Keepers and the TVA

- The three Time-Keepers (Ep. 4): mechanical puppets. The TVA's real creator is He Who Remains in the Citadel at the End of Time (Ep. 6).

- TVA staff (Eps. 3–6): as Sylvie said, they once lived in the timeline and had their memories wiped. B-15 (Ep. 4) and Mobius (Ep. 4) discover this. In Ep. 6, B-15 finds Ravonna's pre-TVA self, a school vice-principal, as proof.

- The pruned (Ep. 5): not destroyed but sent to the Void, a wasteland at the end of time.

Who was erased or died (in order)

- TVA soldiers (Ep. 1): soldiers hunting the variant (Sylvie) were killed one after another, including in a church in France in 1549. In Oklahoma in 1858, more soldiers are engulfed in flames before a hooded figure.

- Hunter C-20 (Ep. 4): Ravonna says her mind broke under the variant's enchantment and she died. But a recording shows a clear-headed C-20 answering Ravonna's questions.

- Mobius (Ep. 4): pruned on Ravonna's order, but alive in the Void (Ep. 5).

- Loki (Ep. 4): pruned by Ravonna before the Time-Keepers, but alive in the Void (Ep. 5).

- Classic Loki (Ep. 5): to protect Loki and Sylvie, he distracts Alioth with a huge illusion of Asgard and is devoured by it.

- He Who Remains (Ep. 6): the TVA's creator. To end the TVA that stole her life, Sylvie stabs him to death with her sword.
