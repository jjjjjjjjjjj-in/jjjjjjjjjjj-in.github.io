Episode 1: Glorious Purpose

The Loki who fled with the Tesseract is caught by the TVA. Agent Mobius asks him to help hunt down a different Loki.

Key terms

The TVA
The Time Variance Authority, which finds and erases anything that strays from the set flow of time.
The Sacred Timeline
The one "correct" flow of time the TVA protects.
Variant
Someone who acts outside the set timeline. The TVA arrests them.
Pruning
Erasing a variant or a stray branch of time.
The Time-Keepers
The three beings said to have created the TVA and decided the timeline.
Miss Minutes
The TVA's animated clock mascot and guide.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Is this a different Loki from the one who died?

Yes. It's the Loki who escaped from 2012, not the one who died in Infinity War.

Where did the TVA agents come from?

They were people from the timeline whose memories were wiped so they could work for the TVA.

Who was in the Citadel?

The one who created the TVA and kept time on a single path, he says to prevent a war between versions of himself.

Why is the TVA different at the end?

Time has branched, and Loki has landed in a slightly different TVA. Season 2 picks it up from there.

Easy-to-miss details

Alligator Loki

Episode 5 even features a Loki who's an alligator.

Infinity Stones as paperweights

At the TVA, Infinity Stones are used as paperweights.

Mobius and jet skis

Mobius loves jet skis.

The ending and post-creditsSpoilers

After the end credits

As a series, there are no film-style post-credits scenes. The finale ends with the TVA completely changed.
