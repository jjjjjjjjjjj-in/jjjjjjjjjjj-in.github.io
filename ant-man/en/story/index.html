Part 1: Walking away in 1989

1-1The Triskelion under construction

Washington, 1989. S.H.I.E.L.D.'s headquarters, the Triskelion, is still being built.

Scientist Hank Pym storms into a meeting room where S.H.I.E.L.D.'s leaders Howard Stark and Peggy Carter sit with senior official Mitchell Carson. Hank has found out that S.H.I.E.L.D. secretly tried to copy the shrinking particles he invented, the Pym Particles, and he is furious.

1-2Slamming Carson into the desk

Carson needles Hank about his wife, Janet. Hank grabs Carson's head and slams it into the desk, leaving him with a bloody nose.

Hank quits S.H.I.E.L.D. and vows that as long as he lives, no one will get his technology. Then he walks out.

Part 2: Scott gets out

2-1A prison send-off

The present day. San Quentin State Prison, near San Francisco. Scott Lang finishes his sentence.

Scott used to be an engineer. When he found out his company was cheating its customers, he broke in and gave the money back to them, and went to prison as a burglar.

Before he leaves, his fellow inmates send him off with a farewell punch.

2-2Luis picks him up

Waiting outside the gate in his van is Luis, Scott's old cellmate. Scott moves in with him for now and meets Luis's friends Dave and Kurt.

Luis right away pitches a big score, but Scott turns him down. He's done stealing and wants to go straight.

2-3Fired from the ice cream shop

Scott gets a job at an ice cream chain, but the manager, Dale, finds out he's been in prison and fires him on the spot. With a record, no one will keep him.

2-4Cassie's birthday

Without calling ahead, Scott shows up at his ex-wife Maggie's house for his daughter Cassie's birthday. His present is a deliberately hideous stuffed rabbit, and Cassie loves it.

But Maggie and her fiancé, police officer Jim Paxton, scold him for not paying child support. Until he has a place, a job and the money to pay, he can't see Cassie.

2-5Cross's presentation

Meanwhile, at Pym Technologies, the company Hank founded. His former protégé Darren Cross, who now runs it, invites Hank in. At Cross's side as a board member is Hank's daughter Hope, long estranged from him. Years ago, Cross and Hope forced Hank out of his own company.

In front of the board, Cross unveils his plan for the Yellowjacket, a combat suit that can shrink its wearer. He is close to recreating the shrinking particles himself, but his tests on living things still fail. Hank is horrified.

2-6Murder in the restroom

After the meeting, a man named Frank, who has been questioning Cross's work, runs into Cross in the restroom. Cross acts friendly, but when Frank keeps doubting the research, Cross shoots him with a gun loaded with his unstable particles.

Frank's body shrinks into a tiny, shapeless lump and he dies. Cross wipes it up with a paper towel and flushes it away.

Part 3: The suit in the safe

3-1Luis's long story

Broke and barred from seeing his daughter, Scott finally agrees to Luis's job.

Luis tells, at great length, how the tip passed from friend to friend to reach him: a rich old man is away on a trip, and there's a big safe in his basement.

3-2Breaking into the house

That night, with Dave driving and Kurt helping keep watch, Scott slips into the house. He gets past a fingerprint-locked door and cracks a very old, very sturdy safe.

But there's no money inside, just an old leather suit and a helmet. Disappointed, Scott takes them home anyway.

3-3Shrinking in the bathtub

At Luis's place, Scott tries on the suit and presses a button on the glove. He shrinks to the size of an ant. The bathtub becomes an ocean; he's chased by rushing water, falls through cracks in the floor and is pursued by a rat.

A stranger's voice speaks to him through the helmet and talks him through it. Scott manages to return to normal size.

3-4Caught returning the suit

Frightened, Scott takes the suit back to the house. On his way out, the police are waiting, Paxton among them, and Scott ends up in a holding cell.

3-5An old man posing as a lawyer

A visitor posing as Scott's lawyer turns out to be the owner of the house: Hank. He offers Scott a chance to change his life.

Hank has the suit smuggled into the cell. Scott shrinks, slips out of jail, and escapes on the back of a flying ant.

3-6It was all Hank's test

Scott wakes up in Hank's house. Hank explains that he set it all up, from the tip that reached Luis to leaving the house empty. It was a test of Scott's skills.

Hope is there too, and she makes no secret of her resentment: why him, and not her?

Part 4: Ant-Man training

4-1Hank's request

Hank reveals that he once worked for S.H.I.E.L.D. as Ant-Man. If Cross perfects the Yellowjacket, he says, it will end up with dangerous people and the world will be at risk.

Since learning of Cross's plans, Hope has secretly been working with her father, spying on Cross. Hank asks Scott to become the new Ant-Man, steal the Yellowjacket and destroy the research.

When Scott hesitates, Hank tells him it beats spending his life as a petty thief his daughter can't look up to.

4-2Through the keyhole

Scott practices shrinking and growing. Even tiny, he keeps a grown man's strength, so his punches pack a huge wallop.

Practicing shrinking through a keyhole, he fails again and again.

4-3Making friends with ants

Hank teaches him to steer ants by talking to them through the helmet. Several kinds join the team: ants that conduct electricity, ants with a powerful sting, ants that link their bodies to make bridges, and big winged ants that fly.

Scott names the flying ant he rides Antony.

4-4Sparring with Hope

Hope teaches Scott to fight, and she's tough. He gets thrown and punched again and again.

Hope is angry. She could use the suit better than Scott, so why won't her father let her wear it?

4-5The secret about Janet

Hank finally tells Hope what he's hidden from her. Her mother, Janet, didn't die in a plane crash. She was a hero called the Wasp and went on missions with Hank.

In 1987, they went to stop a Soviet nuclear missile launched at the United States. The only way inside was to switch off the regulator that limits shrinking and keep getting smaller. Janet did it and stopped the missile, but she kept shrinking and vanished into the Quantum Realm.

Hank was afraid of losing Hope the same way. Hope understands her father at last, and they reconcile.

4-6A warning about the Quantum Realm

Hank warns Scott too: if he ever overrides the regulator, he could be lost in the Quantum Realm and never come back.

Part 5: The Avengers' base

5-1To the old warehouse

The plan needs a signal decoy, a small device Hank built long ago. He believes it's in an old Stark warehouse and sends Scott to get it.

But when Scott arrives, the place has become the new Avengers facility.

5-2Facing the Falcon

Sam Wilson, the Falcon, spots Scott landing on the roof. Scott fights him, shrinking and growing.

Finally Scott crawls inside Sam's wing pack and wrecks its parts, grounding him, then grabs the device and gets away. Sam hopes word of this doesn't get around.

5-3Cross succeeds

Meanwhile, Cross finally perfects his particles: he shrinks a live lamb and brings it back unharmed.

He schedules an unveiling of the Yellowjacket at the company and invites Hank and Hope. Among the guests are Carson and his men, who are secretly tied to Hydra.

5-4Planning the heist

Luis, Dave and Kurt join Hank's plan. Kurt will tamper with the company's security system, Dave will wait in the van, and Luis will go in disguised as a security guard.

Scott will sneak in at tiny size, wipe the company's data, steal the Yellowjacket and plant explosives to bring down the building, research and all.

Part 6: Inside Pym Tech

6-1The night of the unveiling

On the night of the unveiling, Hank and Hope are there as invited guests. Scott shrinks and, with a swarm of flying ants, gets into the building.

Luis keeps watch dressed as a guard, while Kurt and Dave help from outside.

6-2Wrecking the servers, planting bombs

Using the electricity-conducting ants, Scott wrecks the company's servers and wipes the data. Then he plants explosives throughout the building.

6-3The plan is uncovered

Scott reaches the room where the Yellowjacket is kept, but Cross is already onto them. Scott is trapped, and Hank and Hope are held at gunpoint.

Cross says he plans to sell both the Yellowjacket and the Ant-Man suit to Hydra.

6-4Hank is shot

Scott breaks free, and he and Hope take down the Hydra men one after another. In the chaos, Cross shoots Hank, wounding him.

Carson escapes with a vial of Cross's particles.

6-5Antony's end

Cross flees to the rooftop helicopter with the Yellowjacket, and Scott chases him riding Antony. Cross shoots Antony down, killing him.

6-6The building comes down

The explosives go off, and the Pym Tech building collapses inward, taking the research with it. Hank and Hope make it out.

6-7Fighting in a helicopter and a briefcase

Aboard the helicopter, Cross puts on the Yellowjacket and fights Scott. At tiny size, the two keep fighting even inside Cross's briefcase.

The helicopter ends up crashing into the swimming pool of a suburban home.

Part 7: Showdown in Cassie's room

7-1Arrested by Paxton

Back at full size, Scott is arrested by Paxton and the police and put in a patrol car.

Cross, knowing Cassie is Scott's weak spot, heads for her house.

7-2Out of the patrol car

Learning that Cross is after Cassie, Scott escapes the patrol car with the ants' help and races to her house. Paxton follows.

7-3On the toy train

Cross holds Cassie hostage in her bedroom. Scott shrinks down and fights him on Cassie's Thomas the Tank Engine train set.

At ant scale it's a ferocious battle; at normal size, it's just a toy train tipping over. When Scott enlarges the toy engine, it smashes through the wall of the house.

7-4Stopping the Yellowjacket

Cross's armor is too tough to break with ordinary blows, and he closes in on Cassie.

Knowing he may never come back, Scott overrides his regulator. Shrinking smaller than an atom, he slips inside the Yellowjacket and wrecks its mechanism.

The broken suit keeps shrinking uncontrollably, and Cross shrinks away and vanishes with it.

7-5The Quantum Realm

Scott keeps shrinking too, down into the Quantum Realm, a strange world of light and shapes where time and size lose their meaning.

Hearing Cassie call for him, he loads the growth particles into his suit and manages to make his way back.

7-6Scott comes back

Back at full size in Cassie's room, Scott is hugged by Cassie. Paxton saw with his own eyes that Scott saved her.

Back at Hank's house, Hope kisses Scott. Seeing that Scott came back from the Quantum Realm, Hank begins to wonder whether Janet might still be alive, and might be brought back.

Part 8: Afterward

8-1A new family

Paxton covers for Scott and keeps him out of prison. Scott now shares meals with Maggie, Paxton and Cassie.

In the yard lives an ant Scott enlarged during the fight, now something like Cassie's pet.

8-2The word Luis heard

Scott meets Luis, who tells another long story of how a rumor reached him from person to person: a hero with wings is looking for Ant-Man.

8-3Mid-credits

Hank takes Hope to a room downstairs and shows her a new Wasp suit he and Janet had been working on. He offers it to her, and she is glad to take it.

8-4After the credits

Somewhere, Steve Rogers and Sam stand over Bucky, whose metal arm is clamped in a machine. Steve says they can't call Tony because of the Accords.

Sam says he knows a guy who could help.

Summary

What Scott did

Jailed for punishing a crooked company, he couldn't find work after release because of his record, and couldn't see his daughter Cassie (Part 2).

He stole the suit as Hank's test (Part 3), became the new Ant-Man, broke into Pym Tech, wrecked its data and blew up the building (Part 6). Finally, risking the Quantum Realm, he stopped Cross and saved Cassie (Part 7).

Hank and Hope

Fearing misuse of the Pym Particles, Hank left S.H.I.E.L.D. in 1989 (Part 1). He had hidden the truth about losing Janet from Hope, but finally told her and they made up (Part 4).

Hope worked beside Cross while secretly teaming with her father, and trained Scott (Parts 3 and 4). In the end her father offers her a new Wasp suit (Part 8).

What Cross did

Hank's former protégé, he forced Hank out of the company and built the Yellowjacket (Part 2). He killed Frank for doubting his work (Part 2) and tried to sell the suit to Hydra (Part 6). In the end he took Cassie hostage, but Scott wrecked his suit (Part 7).

Who died (in order)

- Frank (Part 2): for doubting Cross's work, he's shot with Cross's unstable particle gun and shrinks into a lump.

- Antony (Part 6): the flying ant Scott rode, shot down by Cross during the chase.

- Darren Cross (Part 7): the Yellowjacket, sabotaged by Scott, keeps shrinking, and he vanishes with it.

- Janet (told in Part 4): in 1987 she kept shrinking to stop a missile and vanished into the Quantum Realm. Hank believed she was dead, but at the end there's hope she's alive.

My takeOpinion

I like that it's about a father who wants to see his daughter, not about saving the world. Turning smallness into comedy, like a fight on a toy train, is something only this film does.
