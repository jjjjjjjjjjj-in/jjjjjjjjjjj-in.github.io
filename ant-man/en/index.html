StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

1. An old split

In 1989, scientist Hank Pym breaks with S.H.I.E.L.D. after it tries to copy his invention.

2. Scott gets out

Fresh out of prison, Scott looks for work so he can see his daughter Cassie, but his record keeps getting in the way.

3. Dangerous research

Hank's former protégé Darren Cross has nearly perfected a shrinking suit, Yellowjacket, and plans to sell it to bad people.

4. A strange suit

On a tip from his friend Luis, Scott breaks into a rich man's house, but the safe holds only a strange suit. Putting it on shrinks him to the size of an ant. It was all Hank's test.

5. Training with ants

Under Hank and his daughter Hope, Scott learns to fight while tiny and to command ants. Hope learns the truth about her mother, who shrank too far and never came back.

6. Running into an Avenger

Sent to fetch gear for the job, Scott ends up fighting the Falcon, Sam, at the Avengers' base.

7. The heist

Scott's crew breaks into Cross's company to steal Yellowjacket. Cross puts the suit on himself, and the fight ends up in Cassie's bedroom.

8. Back from the Quantum Realm

Scott shrinks even further to destroy Cross's suit and slips into the Quantum Realm, but manages to return, and gets to see his family again.

Key terms

Pym Particles
The particles Hank Pym discovered that can change the size of things.
The Ant-Man suit
A suit that uses Pym Particles to shrink the wearer to the size of an ant, keeping a full-grown person's strength.
Yellowjacket
The weaponized shrinking suit Darren Cross builds.
The Quantum Realm
An impossibly tiny realm you can slip into by shrinking too far. You might never come back.
Pym Tech
The company Hank founded, now run by Cross.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why doesn't Hank wear the suit himself?

He's older, and years of using it have taken a toll. He also doesn't want to put his daughter Hope in danger.

What happened to Hope's mother?

She was a hero alongside Hank, and shrank too far to stop a missile, vanishing into the Quantum Realm.

How did Scott get back from the Quantum Realm?

He used his growth disc and made it back just in time. This matters a lot in later films.

What's the final extra scene?

Steve and Sam talk about the captured Bucky, and Sam hints he knows a guy, Ant-Man, leading into Civil War.

Easy-to-miss details

Luis's stories

Whenever Scott's friend Luis explains a tip, the story spirals off on tangents. A highlight every time.

The toy train

The final fight happens on a toy train set: epic at ant scale, adorable at normal size.

Young Hank with Peggy and Howard

The 1989 opening features Peggy Carter and Howard Stark.

Stan Lee's cameo

Marvel Comics legend Stan Lee appears as a bartender in one of Luis's stories.

The ending and post-creditsSpoilers

After the end credits

Mid-credits: Hope is shown the Wasp suit. Post-credits: Steve and Sam talk over the rescued Bucky (leading into Civil War).
