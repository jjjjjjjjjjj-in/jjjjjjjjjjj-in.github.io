Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

You don't need to know the other films

It's set almost entirely in space, with hardly any Earth heroes. It stands on its own.

The music is a star

Seventies songs from the mixtape Peter's mother gave him play at key moments.

Thanos shows up

Thanos, glimpsed at the end of The Avengers, appears briefly here.

Stay until after the credits

There's a fun little scene at the very end.

StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

1. Goodbye in 1988

On Earth, young Peter's mother dies of illness. Running outside the hospital, he's taken by a spaceship.

2. Stealing the Orb

Twenty-six years later, Peter, now the treasure hunter Star-Lord, steals an orb on a distant planet, and others who want it start chasing him.

3. Meeting in prison

Fighting over the orb with assassin Gamora and bounty hunters Rocket and Groot, they all end up in prison. There, Drax, who wants revenge on Ronan for killing his family, joins them.

4. The breakout

The five work together to break out and head for a buyer who'll pay big for the orb.

5. What's inside the orb

In Knowhere, the Collector reveals the orb holds an Infinity Stone. Then Ronan arrives and takes it.

6. Becoming a team

Ronan plans to use the stone to destroy the planet Xandar. Setting aside their selfishness, the group decides to fight to protect it.

7. Groot's choice

As Ronan's ship crashes, Groot wraps his body around his friends to protect them.

8. The Guardians are born

Holding hands, the five withstand the stone's power together and defeat Ronan. They become known as the Guardians of the Galaxy, and Groot starts growing again from a small sprout.

Key terms

The Orb
The round treasure Peter steals. Inside is one of the Infinity Stones.
Infinity Stones
Six special stones born with the universe, each with incredible power. The one in the orb is strong enough to destroy whatever it touches.
The Ravagers
A band of space outlaws led by Yondu, who took young Peter and raised him.
Xandar
A peaceful planet protected by the Nova Corps, and Ronan's target.
The Nova Corps
The police-like force that protects Xandar.
Knowhere
A town built inside the skull of a giant creature, home to the Collector.
Awesome Mix
The cassette of favorite songs Peter's mother made for him.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why is Peter in space?

The night his mother died, Yondu's Ravagers took him, and he grew up among them.

What's the deal between Ronan and Thanos?

Ronan agreed to get the orb for Thanos, but once he learns what the stone can do, he betrays Thanos and keeps it for himself.

Who are Gamora and Nebula?

Both were raised by Thanos as his "daughters." Gamora wants out, and Nebula has a fierce rivalry with her sister.

How did Peter survive touching the stone?

At the end we learn his father wasn't an ordinary human. The secret is revealed in the sequel.

Did Groot die?

The big Groot falls protecting his friends, but a little Groot begins to grow from one of his branches.

What happened to the stone?

The Nova Corps takes it into safekeeping.

Easy-to-miss details

"I am Groot"

Groot can only say "I am Groot," but his friends understand him. At one moment near the end, the words change once.

A dance-off

In the final battle, Peter suddenly starts dancing to distract Ronan.

Mom's last gift

At the end, Peter finally opens the present from his mother he could never bring himself to open: a new mixtape.

Stan Lee's cameo

Marvel Comics legend Stan Lee appears on Xandar chatting with a young woman.
