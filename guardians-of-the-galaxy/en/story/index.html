Part 1: Goodbye, 1988

1-1The hospital hallway

1988, Missouri. Young Peter Quill sits in a hospital hallway with Walkman headphones on, listening to his mother's favorite songs.

His grandfather calls him into the room.

1-2His mother's last moments

His mother, Meredith, is dying of a brain tumor. She gives Peter a wrapped present and reaches out, asking him to take her hand.

Frightened by how sick she is, Peter can't take it. She dies.

1-3A light from the sky

Peter runs out of the hospital and collapses crying in a field. A spaceship descends and beams him up into the sky.

The ship belongs to the Ravagers, a gang of space thieves and smugglers led by Yondu.

Part 2: The Orb on Morag

2-1The abandoned planet Morag

Twenty-six years later, in 2014, the grown-up Peter lands on Morag, an abandoned planet.

Playing a song from his mother's mixtape on his Walkman, he sings and dances his way through the ruins.

2-2Stealing the Orb

Deep inside an old building, Peter finds what he came for, a silver sphere called the Orb, and takes it. He doesn't know what's inside.

2-3Star-Lord

Outside, Peter is surrounded by armed men led by Korath, an enforcer for Ronan, a fanatical Kree renegade who wants the Orb.

Peter introduces himself as Star-Lord, but they've never heard of him. He escapes in the confusion and leaves the planet in his ship, the Milano.

2-4Bereet and Yondu

Bereet, a woman Peter spent the previous night with, is still aboard the Milano. He had completely forgotten about her.

Then Yondu calls. Furious that Peter cut the Ravagers out of the job and took the Orb for himself, Yondu puts a bounty on his head.

Part 3: Chaos on Xandar

3-1Ronan's ship

Ronan is aboard his huge warship, the Dark Aster. Unable to accept that the Kree have made peace with their old enemy Xandar, he executes a Xandarian prisoner himself.

He is working with Thanos: in return for the Orb, Thanos will destroy Xandar for him.

When he learns the Orb got away, Gamora, an assassin and Thanos's adopted daughter, volunteers to get it. Her adopted sister Nebula is also at Ronan's side.

3-2The Broker

Peter goes to Xandar, a peaceful planet protected by the Nova Corps, to sell the Orb to a dealer called the Broker.

But as soon as he hears that Ronan is after the Orb too, the Broker panics, calls off the deal and throws Peter out.

3-3Rocket and Groot

Meanwhile, two bounty hunters are in a plaza on Xandar: Rocket, a genetically and cybernetically modified raccoon, and Groot, a tree-like being who only says "I am Groot."

They spot the bounty Yondu has put on Peter.

3-4A four-way brawl

As Peter leaves, Gamora attacks him and snatches the Orb. While he fights to get it back, Rocket and Groot join in to grab him for the bounty.

The Orb passes from hand to hand, and the plaza is torn apart.

3-5Arrested

Nova Corps officers arrive and arrest all four. Officer Rhomann Dey, who knows Peter well, reads out their records.

The four are sent to the Kyln, a prison in space.

Part 4: The Kyln

4-1The confiscated Walkman

Everything they own is taken away when they enter the prison. A guard starts listening to Peter's mother's tape on his Walkman, and Peter is subdued when he angrily protests.

4-2Gamora becomes a target

Everyone knows Gamora worked for Ronan, and prisoners whose families Ronan took turn their hatred on her.

Gamora tells Peter and the others that she plans to betray Ronan, unwilling to let him use the Orb's power to destroy whole planets. She says she knows a buyer who will pay a fortune, and Peter, Rocket and Groot agree to work with her and split the money.

4-3Drax

At night, a huge inmate named Drax tries to kill Gamora with a blade. Ronan killed his wife and daughter.

Peter stops him by pointing out that if Gamora lives, Ronan will come for her, and Drax will get his revenge. Drax lowers the knife and joins them.

4-4Rocket's plan

Rocket comes up with an escape plan. They need a security band the guards use to open gates, and a battery mounted high up in the watchtower. Removing the battery will set off the alarm, so it was meant to be the last step.

But before Rocket finishes explaining, Groot stretches up and pulls the battery out. Alarms blare, and they have to rush the whole plan.

4-5Taking the watchtower

Fighting the guards, they break into the watchtower. Rocket shuts off the prison's artificial gravity, leaving the guards floating.

Then they fly the watchtower itself to where Peter's Milano is held.

4-6Going back for the Walkman

Just before they leave, Peter goes back into the prison alone. He takes down the guard who was listening to his music, recovers his Walkman, and rejoins the others on the Milano.

The five escape the prison in the Milano.

Part 5: To Knowhere

5-1Ronan and Thanos

Ronan is summoned before Thanos. When Thanos's servant, the Other, scolds Ronan for disrespecting his master, Ronan snaps the Other's neck and kills him.

Thanos, who had his back turned, swings his throne around to face Ronan. Told of Gamora's betrayal, he warns Ronan never to come back to him empty-handed again. Nebula, too, sets out after Gamora.

5-2Five aboard the Milano

Aboard the Milano, the five don't get along. Drax takes everything literally and doesn't understand figures of speech, and he and Rocket argue.

Gamora says the Orb's buyer is in a place called Knowhere.

5-3Knowhere

Knowhere is a town built inside the severed head of a long-dead giant being, a Celestial. It's full of thieves and traders, and its insides are being mined.

5-4Peter and Gamora

While they wait for the buyer, Peter and Gamora talk alone. He happily tells her about the Earth movie Footloose and lets her listen to his Walkman.

They start to grow close, but when Peter leans in, Gamora puts a knife to his throat.

5-5The bar fight

Meanwhile, at a bar, a drunk Rocket and Drax argue and come to blows.

Tired of waiting for his chance at Ronan, Drax storms off alone and sends Ronan a message telling him where to come and fight.

Part 6: The Collector

6-1The Collector

Peter, Gamora, Rocket and Groot visit Taneleer Tivan, the Collector, who gathers rare creatures and objects from across the universe. His slave Carina works there.

6-2The six stones

The Collector opens the Orb, revealing a glowing stone inside. Using images, he explains that six Infinity Stones were created when the universe began, and this is one of them.

Its power is immense: anyone but the mightiest beings who tries to wield it is destroyed.

6-3Carina and the explosion

Carina, who has suffered under the Collector, decides she won't obey him anymore and grabs the stone.

Its power sets off a huge explosion that kills her and engulfs the Collector's collection. Peter and the others get the stone back into the Orb and flee.

6-4Ronan and Drax

Gamora says they can't sell something this dangerous and should hand it to the Nova Corps. Just then Ronan, summoned by Drax, arrives with Nebula and his soldiers.

Drax challenges Ronan to fight one on one but is no match for him. Ronan leaves him to drown in a pool of fluid from the mining.

6-5A chase through space

Peter and the others flee in small mining pods, with Nebula and Ronan's fighters in pursuit. Nebula blows apart the pod Gamora is in, carrying the Orb, leaving her floating in open space.

Ronan's forces take the Orb.

6-6Giving up his helmet

Peter calls Yondu, who has been hunting him, and tells him where he is. Then he leaves his pod, reaches Gamora, and puts his own helmet on her to keep her alive.

He begins to freeze without air, but at the last moment Yondu's ship picks them both up.

6-7Groot saves Drax

Back on Knowhere, Groot pulls Drax out of the fluid. Rocket wants to run, but Groot talks him out of it, and Drax apologizes for what he caused.

The three go after Peter and Gamora.

Part 7: Guardians

7-1A deal with Yondu

Rocket, Drax and Groot show up in the Milano and threaten to attack Yondu's ship unless he lets Peter and Gamora go.

Peter strikes a deal with Yondu, promising to get the Orb back and hand it over, and the standoff ends.

7-2Standing up

Facing Ronan could mean certain death. Even so, Peter tells the others that they are all people who have lost everything, and that this time they should stay and do something good.

Gamora, Rocket, Drax and Groot stand up one by one. The five decide to fight to stop Ronan.

7-3Ronan's betrayal

On the Dark Aster, Ronan sets the stone into his warhammer and takes its power for himself.

He contacts Thanos and declares that once he has destroyed Xandar, he will come for Thanos next. Nebula, who hates her adoptive father, sides with Ronan.

7-4Warning the Nova Corps

Peter contacts Rhomann Dey and warns that Ronan is heading for Xandar. Nova Prime, the leader of the Nova Corps, believes them, prepares for battle and starts evacuating the city.

Part 8: The Battle of Xandar

8-1Battle in the sky

The Dark Aster appears over Xandar and releases swarms of small fighters. The Ravagers and the Nova Corps fight back.

The Milano punches a hole in the Dark Aster's hull, and Peter, Gamora, Drax and Groot get inside. Rocket stays outside and keeps fighting in the air.

8-2The Nova Corps net

The Nova Corps fighters link up into a giant net and hold back the descending Dark Aster.

But Ronan smashes the net with his stone-powered hammer. Nova ships are destroyed one after another, and their commander, Garthan Saal, is killed.

8-3Groot and Drax

Inside the ship, Groot extends his branches and knocks down a whole group of waiting soldiers at once.

Drax fights Ronan's enforcer Korath and kills him.

8-4Gamora and Nebula

Gamora and Nebula are sisters Thanos made fight each other since childhood. They fight inside the ship, and Gamora wins.

Nebula nearly falls off the ship but escapes in a stolen Ravager ship.

8-5Rocket rams the ship

Peter and the others reach Ronan, but with the stone's power he is far too strong for them.

Then Rocket crashes a Ravager ship into the Dark Aster from outside. Badly damaged, the warship begins to fall toward the surface of Xandar.

8-6Groot's shield

As the ship falls, Groot grows his branches into a sphere around his friends. Rocket begs him to stop, knowing he will die.

Groot answers, "We are Groot," and shields them with his own body.

8-7After the crash

The Dark Aster crashes into the city on Xandar. The others survive, but Groot's body has been shattered.

Rocket cries over a piece of him, and Drax gently pets him.

8-8The dance-off

Ronan emerges alive from the wreckage. Just as he is about to destroy Xandar with the stone, Peter suddenly starts singing and dancing to "O-o-h Child" from his tape.

While Ronan stares in confusion, Drax fires a cannon Rocket built and destroys Ronan's hammer.

8-9Holding hands

Peter grabs the stone as it flies out of the hammer. As its power starts tearing him apart, he sees a vision of his mother reaching out to him.

Gamora takes his hand, and Drax and Rocket join hands too. Sharing the stone's power among the four of them, Peter turns it on Ronan and destroys him.

Part 9: Afterward

9-1The fake Orb

As promised, Peter gives Yondu the Orb. But he has left the real stone with the Nova Corps, and the container he hands Yondu actually holds a little troll doll.

As the Ravagers leave Xandar, Yondu's first mate Kraglin remarks that it was a good thing they never delivered Peter to his father as the contract said.

9-2Peter's father

The Nova Corps wipes the five's criminal records clean.

After testing Peter, Rhomann Dey tells him that he is only half human, and that his father seems to belong to an ancient species they have never seen before.

9-3His mother's gift

At last, Peter opens the present from his mother that he couldn't bring himself to open for 26 years. Inside is a new cassette filled with her favorite songs.

9-4Setting off

The five set off in the rebuilt Milano. Asked what they'll do next, Peter answers: something good, something bad, a bit of both.

They have become the Guardians of the Galaxy.

9-5Baby Groot

The twig of Groot that Rocket planted in a pot has grown into a tiny Groot. He dances to the music, but freezes the moment Drax turns to look.

9-6After the credits

The Collector sits in his ruined archive with two of his living exhibits: a dog in a cosmonaut suit and a talking duck.

Summary

Peter and his mother

In 1988, Peter couldn't take his dying mother's hand, and was taken into space (Part 1).

Twenty-six years later, as the stone threatened to tear him apart, he saw his mother reaching out and this time took Gamora's hand. Holding hands with his friends, he shared the stone's power and defeated Ronan (Part 8). Then he finally opened his mother's gift (Part 9).

How the five became a team

Peter, Gamora, Rocket and Groot met fighting over the Orb, and Drax joined them in prison (Parts 3 and 4). At first they teamed up only for money from the Orb or for revenge.

After losing the Orb on Knowhere, they answered Peter's call and decided to fight to protect the galaxy, not just themselves (Part 7).

Where the stone went

The Orb Peter stole on Morag held one of the Infinity Stones (Parts 2 and 6).

Ronan took the stone and betrayed Thanos (Part 7), but was defeated by the Guardians. The stone was left with the Nova Corps, and Yondu got a container holding a troll doll (Part 9).

Who died (in order)

- Meredith Quill (Part 1): Peter's mother. She died of a brain tumor in the hospital.

- The Other (Part 5): Thanos's servant. He scolded Ronan, who snapped his neck in anger.

- Carina (Part 6): the Collector's slave. She grabbed the stone to break free of him and died in the explosion.

- Garthan Saal (Part 8): a Nova Corps commander. He tried to hold back the Dark Aster with the Nova net and was killed when Ronan's hammer smashed it.

- Korath (Part 8): Ronan's enforcer, killed by Drax aboard the Dark Aster.

- Groot (Part 8): he wrapped his body around his friends to protect them in the crash and was shattered. A tiny new Groot grew from a twig of him (Part 9).

- Ronan (Part 8): destroyed by the stone's power, which Peter and his friends shared by holding hands.
