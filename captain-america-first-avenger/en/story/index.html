Part 1: A shield in the ice, a treasure in Norway

1-1The present: an Arctic blizzard

The present day. In the Arctic snow and ice, a team finds the wreck of a huge aircraft.

Breaking into it, they find what looks like a frozen figure, and a round shield painted red, white and blue with a star, locked in the ice.

The story then jumps back about 70 years, to World War II.

1-21942: a small town in Norway

March 1942, Tønsberg in German-occupied Norway. Troops led by Johann Schmidt, a Nazi officer and head of the science division Hydra, raid an old church.

Schmidt is hunting a glowing blue cube, the Tesseract, said to be a treasure of the Norse god Odin.

1-3The fake cube and the real one

The cube in the tomb is a fake. Schmidt finds the real one hidden inside a carving of the world tree on the wall.

Holding the Tesseract, he remarks that while Hitler digs for trinkets in the desert, he has found real power. His goal is to rule the world himself, beyond even the Nazis.

Part 2: A small young man from Brooklyn

2-1Rejected again

Brooklyn, New York. Steve Rogers is small and has a long list of illnesses, asthma among them.

He wants to serve his country in the war and keeps trying to enlist, only to be rejected every time. He even lies about his hometown to try again at another office.

2-2The theater and the alley fight

In a movie theater, Steve tells a man heckling the newsreel to be quiet. The man drags him into an alley and knocks him down again and again.

Holding a trash-can lid like a shield, Steve keeps getting up: "I can do this all day." His best friend Bucky Barnes, in uniform, arrives and chases the man off.

2-3Bucky's last night

Bucky is now an Army sergeant and ships out to the war in Europe the next day.

He knows Steve has lied on his forms again. For one last night out, he brings two girls along and takes Steve to an expo of future technology.

2-4The Stark Expo

At the expo, young inventor Howard Stark demonstrates a flying car. It lifts off for a moment, then crashes down.

Steve spots an enlistment booth inside and decides to try again. Bucky tries to stop him, but Steve won't change his mind, and the two part ways.

2-5Dr. Erskine's question

Dr. Abraham Erskine, a scientist who overheard them, comes to see Steve in the exam room and asks whether he wants to kill Nazis.

"I don't want to kill anyone. I don't like bullies," Steve answers. Erskine likes the answer, approves him, and brings him into a special program.

Part 3: The super-soldier program

3-1Training camp

Steve joins a training camp for the SSR, the Strategic Scientific Reserve, a secret unit commanded by Army Colonel Chester Phillips, with British agent Peggy Carter on the staff.

When a recruit named Hodge mocks Peggy, she knocks him flat with one punch. Steve can't keep up with the others at running or carrying anything.

3-2Getting the flag

Phillips says whoever brings down the flag from the top of a pole gets a ride back. The others try to climb it and fail.

Steve pulls the pin holding the pole up, lets it fall, and takes the flag. It's brains, not muscle.

3-3The grenade

Phillips is against choosing someone as frail as Steve. As if to test Erskine's view, he tosses a grenade into the group of recruits.

Hodge and the others scatter, but Steve throws himself on the grenade and yells at everyone to get away. It's a dummy and doesn't go off. That settles it: Steve is chosen.

3-4Hydra's laboratory

In Europe, Dr. Arnim Zola, a scientist working under Schmidt, is drawing power from the Tesseract to fuel new weapons.

Schmidt learns where Erskine is and sends an assassin after him.

3-5The night before

On the night before the procedure, Erskine sits down with Steve over a drink. He once worked under Schmidt in Germany, he says. Schmidt took an unfinished version of the serum; it gave him strength but warped both his looks and his mind.

The serum amplifies what is already inside a person: good becomes great, bad becomes worse. Erskine chose Steve because he knows what weakness feels like, and asks him to stay who he is: "not a perfect soldier, but a good man." He drinks Steve's glass himself, since Steve has the procedure in the morning.

3-6Beneath an antique shop

The next morning, Peggy drives Steve to an antique shop in Brooklyn. Through the back, a passage leads down to a huge secret lab.

Senator Brandt and other observers watch from a booth above. Howard Stark runs the machinery.

3-7The procedure

Steve lies down in a large pod and Erskine injects the serum. The pod closes and Howard's machine floods him with vita-rays.

Steve screams, and Peggy and Erskine try to stop it, but Steve tells them not to. When the light fades and the pod opens, he is taller and powerfully built.

Part 4: Erskine's death, and life on stage

4-1The assassin Kruger

The joy doesn't last. Heinz Kruger, a Hydra assassin hidden among the observers, sets off an explosion in the lab.

He shoots Erskine and runs off with the last vial of serum. Dying, Erskine points to Steve's chest, over his heart.

4-2The Brooklyn chase

Kruger escapes by car, and Steve runs after him in his new body. Peggy stands in the road firing at Kruger's car, and Steve pulls her out of the way just in time.

Still getting used to his sudden strength, Steve keeps up the chase through the streets.

4-3A tiny submarine at the docks

Cornered at the docks, Kruger grabs a boy as a hostage and throws him into the water. The boy calls out that he can swim, so Steve keeps going.

Kruger tries to escape in a small submarine, but Steve dives in, tears it open and drags him ashore. The vial of serum has been smashed during the chase.

4-4Hail Hydra

When Steve presses him, Kruger names Hydra and says that cut off one head, two more shall take its place.

Then he bites down on a hidden cyanide capsule and kills himself. With Erskine dead, the formula is gone too. Steve is the only super soldier there will be.

4-5A lab, or a stage

Phillips says he wanted an army of super soldiers, not one, and plans to send Steve off to be studied.

Senator Brandt offers another job: tour the country as "Captain America" in a star-spangled costume, selling war bonds in a stage show.

4-6The Captain America show

Steve performs songs and skits with a chorus line, and the show is a smash. There are movies and comic books, and the bonds sell.

Kids see a hero, but Steve is playing a make-believe one, far from the soldiers who are actually fighting.

Part 5: A one-man rescue

5-1The Italian front

1943, an army camp on the Italian front. Steve performs for real soldiers, who jeer and throw things at him.

As he sits alone in the rain, Peggy comes to find him and reminds him he was meant for more than a stage show.

5-2Bucky's unit

Peggy tells Steve that Bucky's unit, the 107th, fought Schmidt's forces and many of its men are missing.

Steve goes to Phillips and asks about Bucky. Phillips says there will be no rescue of the prisoners held at a factory deep behind enemy lines, and turns him down.

5-3Howard's plane

Steve decides to go alone, still in his stage costume with his prop shield. Peggy asks Howard Stark for help, and Howard flies Steve close to the enemy lines.

The plane comes under anti-aircraft fire, and Steve parachutes out.

5-4The Hydra factory

Steve slips into the Hydra factory hidden on a truck. He takes out guards and frees the prisoners from their cages one after another.

Asked who he is, he answers, "Captain America." Among the men he frees are Dum Dum Dugan and others who will later fight beside him.

5-5Bucky on the table

In a lab deep inside the factory, Steve finds Bucky strapped to a table. Zola has been experimenting on him, and he is dazed, repeating his name and serial number.

Steve gets him up, and they look for a way out.

5-6Red Skull

On a catwalk in the burning factory, Steve comes face to face with Schmidt, who sizes up the man the serum made of him.

Then Schmidt peels off his face. Beneath it is a red, skull-like head: the reason he is called the Red Skull. He escapes with Zola, and the factory starts exploding.

5-7Leaping over the flames

Steve and Bucky escape across a narrow beam high above the floor. Bucky makes it over, but the beam gives way and leaves Steve stranded.

Bucky refuses to leave without him. Steve takes a running leap across the flames, and the two get out of the factory.

5-8Four hundred men come home

At the base, Peggy and Phillips have given Steve up for lost. Then he walks in, leading about 400 men he has rescued.

Steve offers to accept discipline for disobeying orders, but Phillips says it won't be necessary. The soldiers cheer, led by Bucky, for Captain America. Steve is a real hero now.

Part 6: The Howling Commandos

6-1A pub in London

London. Steve memorized a map of Hydra's bases at the factory, and he sets out to form a unit to destroy them.

In a pub he recruits the best of the men he rescued: Dugan, Gabe Jones, Jim Morita, Falsworth and Dernier. Bucky signs on too. The Howling Commandos are born.

6-2Peggy in red

Peggy walks into the pub in a red dress and talks with Steve about dancing. He admits he has never danced; she says she is waiting for the right partner.

The two are slowly growing closer.

6-3The vibranium shield

In Howard's lab, Steve picks out a new shield and settles on a round one among the prototypes. It is made of vibranium, a rare metal that is nearly indestructible and absorbs almost any impact.

Just before, Peggy has seen a woman from the office kiss Steve. Annoyed, she fires a pistol at him while he holds the shield. Every bullet bounces off, proving how strong it is.

6-4Taking down Hydra bases

With a new uniform and the shield, Steve and the Howling Commandos attack Hydra bases across Europe and destroy them one by one.

Meanwhile Schmidt declares that Hydra will no longer hide in Hitler's shadow. He breaks with the Nazis and prepares to take on the world with his Tesseract weapons.

6-5The mountain train

1945, the snowy Alps. To stop a train carrying Zola, Steve, Bucky and Gabe zip-line onto its roof as it races along.

Inside the cars, Steve and Bucky fight Hydra soldiers.

6-6Bucky falls

In the fight, Bucky picks up Steve's shield and takes a hit from an enemy weapon. The blast tears open the side of the car and throws him out.

Bucky hangs from a rail on the outside of the train as Steve reaches for him. The rail snaps, and Bucky falls screaming into the deep ravine. Zola is captured.

6-7Zola's interrogation

The captured Zola is questioned by Phillips. He reveals that Schmidt plans to bomb America's major cities, and where Hydra's last base is.

6-8In a bombed-out pub

Having lost his best friend, Steve drinks alone in a pub wrecked by bombing. But his changed body won't let him get drunk, however much he drinks.

Peggy joins him and tells him Bucky's death is not his fault. Steve resolves to keep fighting until Schmidt is stopped.

Part 7: The final battle, and a promise to Peggy

7-1Straight through the front door

Steve rides a motorcycle straight into Hydra's last base in the mountains. He lets himself be captured and is brought before Schmidt.

Asked what makes him so special, Steve says, "Nothing. I'm just a kid from Brooklyn."

7-2The team storms in

At that moment the Howling Commandos swing in on ropes, and Phillips and Peggy's forces storm the base. A full battle breaks out.

Schmidt boards the Valkyrie, a gigantic aircraft loaded with bombs, and gets ready to take off.

7-3A kiss, and a leap

Steve chases the moving Valkyrie in a car with Phillips and Peggy. In the car, Peggy kisses him.

Steve jumps from the car, grabs onto the Valkyrie's landing gear, and is carried aboard as it lifts into the sky.

7-4Bombs marked with city names

Inside the Valkyrie are small bomb-carrying planes marked with the names of American cities, New York among them.

Fighting off the Hydra crew, Steve makes his way toward the cockpit.

7-5The Tesseract's light

In the cockpit, Steve and Schmidt fight fiercely. The device holding the Tesseract breaks, and the cube tumbles free.

Schmidt picks it up with his bare hand. Light pours out of the cube and opens a portal into space, and Schmidt is pulled into it and vanishes. The Tesseract burns through the floor and falls into the ocean.

7-6Toward the Arctic

The Valkyrie is on course for New York. There's no way to stop it, and trying to land could set off the bombs.

Steve decides to bring it down in the empty Arctic ice.

7-7The radio promise

Steve talks to Peggy over the radio. They make a date: Saturday night, eight o'clock, a dance at the Stork Club. "Don't you dare be late," she tells him.

The plane nears the ice and his voice cuts out. Peggy weeps, listening to nothing but static.

7-8The war ends

Howard keeps searching the sea and recovers the Tesseract from the ocean floor. But neither the Valkyrie nor Steve is found, and Steve is presumed dead.

The war ends and the streets erupt in celebration. The Howling Commandos raise a quiet drink, and Peggy looks at the photo in Steve's file.

Part 8: Waking up 70 years later

8-1A 1940s room

Steve wakes in a 1940s-style hospital room. A baseball game is playing on the radio.

But it is a 1941 game he went to in person. When a woman comes in, he demands to know where he is, having realized the room is a setup.

8-2Times Square

Steve bursts through a wall and runs out into the street. He ends up in Times Square, modern New York, crowded with giant screens, traffic and people.

As he stands frozen, cars pull up and S.H.I.E.L.D. Director Nick Fury steps out to tell him he has been asleep for almost 70 years.

8-3The date

Asked if he's all right, Steve says he is, then adds quietly, "I just... I had a date." The Saturday night with Peggy will never happen.

8-4After the credits

Steve is alone in a gym, pounding a punching bag, when Fury arrives and offers him a mission with worldwide stakes.

A preview of the next film, The Avengers, follows.

Summary (full spoilers)

Steve's journey

Frail and rejected again and again, Steve was chosen by Erskine for his urge to protect others, and the serum gave him a powerful body (Parts 2–3).

But Erskine was killed and the formula lost, so at first Steve was kept off the battlefield and played "Captain America" in a promotional show (Part 4).

Disobeying orders to rescue Bucky and the other prisoners on his own made him a real hero (Part 5), and he led the Howling Commandos against Hydra (Part 6). In the end he brought the Valkyrie down in the Arctic, slept in the ice for almost 70 years, and woke up in the present (Parts 7–8).

Where the Tesseract went

Schmidt took it in Norway (Part 1), and Zola used its power to run Hydra's weapons (Part 3).

In the final fight Schmidt grabbed it bare-handed; it opened a portal into space and he vanished. The cube fell into the ocean, and Howard recovered it from the sea floor (Part 7). It turns up again and again in later MCU films.

Who died or disappeared (in order)

- Dr. Erskine (Part 4): shot right after the procedure succeeds by Kruger, a Hydra assassin posing as an observer. He was the only one who knew how to make the serum.

- Kruger (Part 4): caught by Steve, he bites a hidden cyanide capsule and kills himself before he can be questioned.

- Bucky (Part 6): during the fight on Zola's train, an enemy blast throws him out of the car; the rail he hangs from snaps and he falls into the ravine. The film treats him as dead.

- Schmidt (Part 7): grabs the Tesseract bare-handed and is pulled into its light and vanishes. The film leaves unclear whether he died.

- Steve (Parts 7–8): presumed dead after bringing the Valkyrie down in the Arctic. In fact he lay frozen in the ice and wakes up nearly 70 years later.
