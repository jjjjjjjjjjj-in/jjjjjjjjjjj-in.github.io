Key terms

Weapon X
The secret program that bonded metal to Logan's skeleton.
Adamantium
The unbreakable metal coating Logan's bones and claws.
Alkali Lake
The dam where Stryker's secret base is hidden.
The second Cerebro
Stryker's copy of Professor X's device.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

What was Stryker's plan?

To make Professor X use a copy of Cerebro to kill every mutant on Earth.

Why does Magneto team up?

Stryker threatens all mutants. But at the last moment, Magneto turns the plan against humans instead.

What's in the lake at the end?

A shape like a fiery bird appears, setting up The Last Stand.

Easy-to-miss details

Nightcrawler's faith

The devout Nightcrawler is seen praying with a rosary.

Bobby's family

Bobby, Iceman, tells his family he's a mutant.

Claws against claws

Logan fights a woman working for Stryker who also has claws.

The ending and post-creditsSpoilers

After the end credits

No post-credits scene. At the end of the film, the shadow of a phoenix appears in the lake.
