X2: The Full Story, Scene by Scene

Part 1: Attack on the White House

A blue blur

In the White House, a blue-skinned mutant teleports past the guards and reaches the President, but is shot and vanishes, leaving behind a note: Mutant freedom now.

Logan returns

Logan comes back to the school after finding nothing at Alkali Lake.

Visiting Magneto

Professor X and Cyclops visit Magneto in prison. He admits Stryker drugged him into revealing secrets about Cerebro. On their way out, the two are captured.

Part 2: The raid on the school

A night raid

Stryker's soldiers storm the school at night. Logan fights to get the kids out, and seeing Stryker stirs old memories.

Bobby's home

Logan, Rogue, Bobby and John (Pyro) take refuge at Bobby's family home. Bobby tells his family he's a mutant.

Under fire

Picked up by Jean and Storm's jet, they're shot by fighter planes. Magneto saves them from crashing.

Part 3: Strange allies

Nightcrawler

Jean and Storm find the blue-skinned mutant, Nightcrawler. He'd been controlled and remembers nothing.

Magneto's escape

Mystique tricks a guard and injects him with metal, letting Magneto escape his plastic prison.

Stryker's plan

Magneto explains that Stryker built a copy of Cerebro to make Professor X kill every mutant on Earth. The X-Men and Magneto join forces.

Part 4: Alkali Lake

The base in the dam

They infiltrate Stryker's base hidden inside the Alkali Lake dam.

Logan's past

Logan finds the lab where metal was bonded to his bones, and fights Stryker's agent, a woman with the same claws.

Cyclops controlled

A brainwashed Cyclops attacks Jean, and her power begins to surge out of control.

Magneto's betrayal

Magneto reprograms the fake Cerebro to target humans instead of mutants. Storm and Nightcrawler free Professor X from the illusion.

Part 5: The dam breaks

A wall of water

The dam bursts and a flood rushes toward them. The jet won't take off.

Jean's sacrifice

Jean steps off the jet, holds back the water with her mind and lifts the plane. She saves her friends and disappears beneath the flood.

A message for the President

The X-Men appear before the President and urge him to seek a future where mutants and humans live together.

A shape in the lake

In the final shot, a shape like a fiery bird glimmers beneath the lake.
