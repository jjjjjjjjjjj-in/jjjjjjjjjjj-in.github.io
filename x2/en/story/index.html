Part 1: Attack on the President

1-1A blue blur in the White House

A tour is underway at the White House when a blue-skinned mutant appears. Vanishing in a puff of smoke and reappearing somewhere else, he takes down the Secret Service agents one after another.

He reaches the President and lunges at him with a knife, but at the last moment an agent shoots him, and he disappears.

The attack makes the public even more hostile toward mutants.

1-2The museum trip

Students from Professor X's school are on a museum field trip. Some boys pick on Rogue, Bobby (Iceman) and John (Pyro).

John, who can control fire, makes one boy's cigarette flare up and sets his jacket on fire. Bobby puts it out with ice.

Suddenly everyone around them freezes in place. Professor X appears and scolds the students for showing off.

1-3Logan returns from Alkali Lake

Searching for clues to his past, Logan has been exploring an abandoned military site at Alkali Lake in Canada. He finds nothing.

He comes back to the school. Since the last battle, Jean's powers have been growing stronger and harder to control. She is engaged to Cyclops, though Logan is drawn to her too.

Rogue has started dating Bobby, but because her touch drains people, they can't even hold hands.

1-4Searching with Cerebro

Using Cerebro, the device that finds mutants, Professor X tracks the blue-skinned attacker to Boston.

Jean and Storm head to Boston, while Professor X and Cyclops go to question the imprisoned Magneto about the attack.

Logan is left in charge of the students.

1-5Stryker and the President

Military scientist Colonel William Stryker meets the President. Citing the attack, he asks for permission to investigate Professor X's school, and the President agrees.

1-6The plastic prison

Magneto is held in a prison made entirely of plastic, with no metal anywhere. Professor X sits down with him.

Magneto reveals that Stryker drugged him and made him give up information about Cerebro and the school.

1-7Professor X and Cyclops captured

On their way out, Professor X and Cyclops are captured by Stryker's soldiers and his assistant Yuriko, a mutant with long metal claws who obeys Stryker's every order.

1-8Nightcrawler in the church

In an old church in Boston, Jean and Storm find the blue-skinned mutant praying. He is Kurt Wagner, Nightcrawler, a devout man from Germany.

His body is covered with tattoos, one for each of his sins. He had been controlled and barely remembers the attack on the President.

Jean and Storm bring him aboard their jet.

Part 2: The raid on the school

2-1A night raid

That night, Stryker's soldiers break into the school and sedate every student they find.

Logan wakes up and fights the soldiers with his claws.

2-2Colossus leads the kids

Peter (Colossus), a student who can turn his body to metal, leads the other students to safety.

Even so, several students are taken by the soldiers.

2-3Face to face with Stryker

Logan confronts Stryker, who calls him "Wolverine" and seems to know all about his past. Fragments of old memories stir in Logan's mind.

Logan escapes from the school with Rogue, Bobby and John.

2-4Mystique and the guard

Mystique, Magneto's shapeshifting ally, has been using Senator Kelly's identity to dig through government files, finding the location of Magneto's prison and plans for Cerebro.

She seduces Mitchell Laurio, a guard at Magneto's prison, and secretly injects him with liquid metal.

2-5Magneto escapes

The next day, when Laurio comes to the cell, Magneto draws the metal out of his body and forms it into small balls.

With those, Magneto smashes the plastic prison and escapes.

2-6Bobby's home

The four take refuge at Bobby's family home in Boston. His family doesn't know he's a mutant; they think he goes to an ordinary boarding school.

Bobby tells his parents and his brother Ronny the truth. His mother asks, "Have you tried... not being a mutant?" Ronny sneers at him and secretly calls the police.

2-7Showdown with the police

Police officers surround the house. One of them, feeling threatened by Logan, shoots him. Logan goes down, but heals right away.

John attacks the officers with fire, setting police cars ablaze. Rogue grabs his hand, absorbs his power and uses it to put the fire out.

2-8The jet arrives

Storm, Jean and Nightcrawler arrive in the jet and pick up Logan and the kids.

Bobby has to leave his family behind.

Part 3: An alliance with the enemy

3-1Fighter jets attack

On the way back to the school, fighter jets chase them and fire missiles. Storm whips up tornadoes to dodge them, but a missile hits.

The hull is torn open and Rogue is sucked out. Nightcrawler teleports outside, catches her in mid-air and brings her back.

3-2Who stopped the crash

The jet goes down, but just before it hits the ground, it stops in mid-air and is set down gently.

Waiting in the woods are Magneto and Mystique.

3-3Stryker's plan

Magneto explains Stryker's plan: he has built a copy of Cerebro and intends to make Professor X use it to kill every mutant on Earth.

Professor X will be controlled by Stryker's son Jason, a mutant who can trap people in illusions. Stryker used the same control on Nightcrawler, making him attack the President to give himself an excuse to raid the school.

Magneto also tells Logan that Stryker was the one who bonded adamantium to his bones and took his memory. The X-Men and Magneto's side agree to stop Stryker together.

3-4Nightcrawler's memories

Jean reads Nightcrawler's mind and finds the location of Stryker's base: underground, inside the dam at Alkali Lake.

3-5Before setting out

Before they set out, Mystique makes a pass at Logan.

Nightcrawler asks Mystique why she doesn't stay in disguise all the time and look like everyone else. She answers, "Because we shouldn't have to."

3-6Professor X inside the dam

At the Alkali Lake base, Stryker shows Professor X his son Jason, who was once a student at Xavier's school.

Stryker says Jason's powers drove his wife to take her own life. Jason has been operated on and can no longer even speak.

The captured Cyclops is under the control of Stryker's serum.

3-7The little girl

Jason appears to Professor X in the illusion of a little girl. Lost in the illusion, Professor X is led step by step into preparing the copy of Cerebro.

Part 4: The base at Alkali Lake

4-1Mystique as Logan

The jet lands at Alkali Lake. Disguised as Logan, Mystique gets inside the base and opens the way for the others.

Rogue, Bobby and John are told to wait in the jet.

4-2Splitting up

Magneto and Mystique head off to stop the copy of Cerebro. Storm and Nightcrawler go to find the kidnapped students.

4-3Jean versus Cyclops

The brainwashed Cyclops attacks Jean. Their fight is fierce, and his beams damage the dam.

Jean stops him and frees his mind, but water starts leaking through the damaged dam.

4-4The room from Logan's past

Deep in the base, Logan finds a lab for melting metal and remembers that this is where adamantium was bonded to his bones.

Stryker is there.

4-5The fight with Yuriko

Yuriko attacks Logan to protect Stryker. Like Logan, she has adamantium claws and a body that heals fast.

They fight claw to claw. In the end, Logan pumps her full of liquid adamantium, and she dies.

4-6Chasing Stryker

Logan chases Stryker to the helicopter pad and catches him. He demands answers about his past but gets none.

Logan chains Stryker up and leaves him there.

4-7Turning Cerebro around

Magneto and Mystique stop the copy of Cerebro. Disguised as Stryker, Mystique gives Jason new orders, and Magneto rewires the machine so that Professor X's power targets every human on Earth instead of mutants.

Magneto and Mystique leave in Stryker's helicopter. John goes with them.

4-8A snowstorm inside Cerebro

Storm and Nightcrawler rescue the kidnapped students.

Then Nightcrawler teleports them inside the copy of Cerebro. Storm conjures a snowstorm that breaks Jason's concentration, and Professor X is freed from the illusion.

Part 5: The dam breaks

5-1The dam gives way

The dam, damaged in Jean and Cyclops's fight, finally bursts. Stryker, still chained where Logan left him, is killed by the flood.

5-2The jet won't fly

The X-Men and the students board the jet to escape, but it loses power and can't take off. A wall of water rushes toward them.

5-3Jean's goodbye

Jean slips off the jet without anyone noticing. With her mind, she holds back the flood and lifts the jet into the air.

Through Professor X, she telepathically says goodbye to her friends. Once the jet is safe, she lets go, and the water crashes down on her.

5-4A message for the President

The President is about to address the nation on television when everyone in the room freezes and the X-Men appear.

Professor X hands him Stryker's files and warns that there can be no peace unless humans and mutants come together.

5-5Back at the school

At the school, Professor X, Cyclops and Logan remember Jean.

Professor X begins a class with his students.

5-6A shape in the lake

In the final shot, a shape like a fiery bird rises over the flooded Alkali Lake.

Summary (spoilers through the end)

Stryker's plan and how it ended

1) He drugs Magneto to learn about Cerebro (Part 1).

2) He makes Nightcrawler attack the President, uses that as an excuse to raid the school, and captures Professor X (Parts 1–2).

3) Through Jason's illusions, he tries to make Professor X kill every mutant with the copy of Cerebro (Part 3).

4) Magneto turns it on humans instead, but Storm and Nightcrawler stop it (Part 4).

5) Stryker is killed when the dam bursts (Part 5).

Logan's past

1) He searches Alkali Lake and finds nothing (Part 1).

2) He learns Stryker knows about his past (Part 2).

3) Magneto tells him Stryker put the metal in his bones (Part 3).

4) In the base's lab he remembers it happening there, but gets no answers from Stryker (Part 4).

Who died

- Yuriko (Part 4): Stryker's enforcer. Logan pumps liquid adamantium into her during their fight.

- Stryker (Part 5): left chained by Logan, he's killed when the dam bursts.

- Jean (Part 5): after holding back the water to save her friends' jet, she is swept under. In the last shot, a shape like a fiery bird appears over the lake.
