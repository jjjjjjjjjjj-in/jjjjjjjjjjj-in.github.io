Part 1: Attack on the President

1-1A blue blur in the White House

A tour is underway at the White House. A blue-skinned mutant hidden among the visitors starts vanishing in puffs of smoke and reappearing elsewhere, taking down the Secret Service agents one after another.

He reaches the Oval Office, pins the President to his desk and raises a knife. At that moment a downed agent shoots him in the arm, and he disappears.

The knife he leaves behind carries a message: "Mutant Freedom Now!"

1-2Alkali Lake in the snow

In the Canadian Rockies, Logan arrives at snow-covered Alkali Lake, looking for clues to his past. Professor X had told him a military facility once stood here.

All he finds are abandoned, crumbling buildings near a dam. There is nothing left for him.

1-3The museum trip

Students from Professor X's school are on a museum field trip. Jean is overwhelmed as the thoughts of everyone around her flood into her head at once. Cyclops is worried: ever since the last battle at Liberty Island, her powers have kept growing. Jean admits she feels something terrible is about to happen.

In the food court, two young men pick on Rogue, Bobby (Iceman) and John (Pyro). John, who can control fire, makes the flame of his lighter leap onto one boy's clothes. Bobby puts it out with ice.

Suddenly everyone around them freezes in place. Professor X wheels in and scolds John for showing off. On a TV, the news reports a mutant attack on the President. The group leaves the museum.

1-4A meeting at the school

Back at the school, the X-Men discuss the attack. Cyclops thinks Magneto is behind it, but Professor X and Jean point out that he couldn't arrange it from prison, and that it would only hurt his cause.

Professor X fears the government will crack down hard on mutants. They need to reach the attacker before the authorities do, so once he has a location, Jean and Storm will go and pick him up.

1-5Stryker and the President

Colonel William Stryker, a military scientist, is in the Oval Office. Senator Kelly joins them (the real Kelly died in the last film; this is Magneto's shapeshifting ally Mystique in disguise).

Stryker shows photos of the school and says it is no ordinary school: there is even a jet hidden under the basketball court. He got the information by interrogating the imprisoned Magneto. The President authorizes him to go in and investigate, but warns that he doesn't want to see a dead mutant child on the evening news.

In the hallway, Kelly (Mystique) asks Stryker to arrange a visit to Magneto's prison and is refused. At Stryker's side is his assistant, Yuriko.

1-6Logan comes home

At the school, Rogue and Bobby have started dating. But because Rogue's touch drains a person's power and life, they can't even kiss.

Then Logan rides up on Cyclops's motorcycle. Rogue runs to hug him. Bobby shakes his hand and frosts it over a little.

Storm and Jean are just about to leave for Boston to pick up the attacker. Logan flirts with Jean, and trades barbs with Cyclops when he walks in.

1-7Inside Cerebro

Professor X is using Cerebro, the device that finds mutants, to track the President's attacker. When Logan walks in, countless lights appear around them: white for humans, red for mutants. Through Cerebro his mind is linked to everyone on Earth. He could find the attacker faster by concentrating harder, he says, but that would kill him.

Then the attacker stops moving, a voice is heard praying in German, and Professor X has his location. Logan reports that there was nothing at Alkali Lake and asks the Professor to read his mind again. Professor X answers that some things the mind has to discover for itself.

He asks Logan to watch the children tonight. He and Cyclops are going to visit an old friend.

1-8Magneto, drugged in his cell

That old friend, Magneto, is held in a prison made entirely of plastic, with no metal anywhere. His visitor now is Stryker.

The guard, Laurio, beats Magneto and holds him down while Stryker drips a chemical onto the back of his neck. It is a drug that makes mutants obey. Stryker orders him to tell everything about the house Xavier built and the machine called Cerebro.

1-9Mystique in Stryker's office

Now disguised as Yuriko, Mystique slips into Stryker's office. On his computer she finds the details of Magneto's prison, and plans for building a second Cerebro.

The real Yuriko comes back. Mystique turns into a janitor and walks out with the trash, unnoticed.

1-10Nightcrawler in the church

Night. Jean and Storm reach an old church in Boston. A voice speaks German from above, but it keeps coming from a different place: he is a teleporter.

Storm brings down a bolt of lightning that breaks his perch, and Jean catches him in mid-air. The blue-skinned mutant gives his name as Kurt Wagner. During the attack on the President, he says, he could see what he was doing but couldn't stop himself, like a bad dream.

On the back of his neck is a wound. Someone had been controlling him.

1-11The visit, and the trap

Professor X is wheeled into Magneto's cell in a plastic wheelchair. Cyclops waits outside.

Magneto says Stryker has been visiting him. Professor X knows the name: Stryker's son Jason was once a student at the school. Magneto confesses that he couldn't resist the drug and told Stryker everything.

Then gas hisses into the cell. The visit itself was the trap, and both men pass out. Outside, Cyclops takes down the guards, but Yuriko leaps on him and knocks him out. Professor X and Cyclops are taken away.

Part 2: The raid on the school

2-1A sleepless night

That night at the school, Logan wakes from a nightmare about the old experiments. In the living room a boy named Jones is still up, changing TV channels by blinking.

In the kitchen, Bobby is eating ice cream. He chills Logan's bottle of soda with a breath. His parents, he says, think he goes to an ordinary prep school.

2-2The night raid

Logan hears footsteps. Stryker's soldiers have crept into the school and are shooting the sleeping students with tranquilizer darts.

A girl named Siryn wakes and lets out an ear-splitting scream that rouses the whole school. Logan cuts down soldier after soldier with his claws.

Peter (Colossus), a student who can turn his body to metal, offers to fight, but Logan tells him to look after the kids, and he leads students out through hidden tunnels. Even so, six students are captured.

2-3Face to face with Stryker

Logan sends Rogue, Bobby and John ahead and stays to face the soldiers. Stryker appears and orders them not to shoot. He calls Logan "Wolverine" and says it has been fifteen years. Logan can't remember who he is.

Before Logan can get any answers, Bobby raises a thick wall of ice between the two men. Logan runs to the garage with the three kids and they escape in Cyclops's car.

Stryker's men cut their way into Cerebro and strip out the parts they need. In the car, Rogue gives Logan back the dog tag he left with her. They find a communicator in the car and decide to head for Boston, where Storm and Jean went. Bobby's family lives there.

2-4Mystique at the bar

Disguised as an attractive woman, Mystique approaches the guard Laurio in a bar. She drugs his beer, lures him into the restroom, and he passes out.

Then she injects the sleeping Laurio with a large dose of a silver liquid: iron.

2-5Professor X inside the dam

Professor X wakes up in the base at Alkali Lake. A device on his head blocks his powers. Somewhere else in the base, Cyclops is being put under Stryker's drug.

Years ago Stryker sent his mutant son Jason to the school to be "cured." When Jason came home he resented his parents and tormented them with illusions, and Stryker says that drove his wife to kill herself. Yuriko, too, is a mutant kept under control by the drug on her neck. Professor X realizes that Stryker arranged the attack on the President.

Stryker wheels in a young man in a wheelchair: Jason, operated on so that he can no longer even speak. The mind-control drug comes from Jason's brain. It isn't strong enough for someone as powerful as Xavier, so Stryker will use Jason's illusions on him directly.

2-6Bobby's house

The four arrive at Bobby's family home in Boston. Nobody is home. Bobby finds a change of clothes for Rogue. They share their first real kiss, but her power begins to take hold and Bobby has to pull away.

In the kitchen, Logan takes a beer and, sensing something, pops his claws. It is the family cat, which licks them.

Then Bobby's parents and his brother Ronny come home and run right into Logan. Bobby hurries down, introduces him as "Professor Logan," and says there is something he needs to tell them.

2-7Magneto escapes

The next day, Laurio brings Magneto's meal to the cell. The scanner at the entrance gives a faint blip, but the other guards ignore it.

Magneto notices that something about Laurio is different. Then, with his power, he rips the iron out of Laurio's body. Laurio dies.

Magneto shapes the iron into small balls and fires them like bullets, shattering the plastic cell. He flattens one into a disc, rides it across the deep gap around the cell, and kills the other guards on his way out.

2-8Bobby tells his family

In the living room, Bobby has just told his family he is a mutant. His mother can hardly bring herself to say the word, and asks, "Have you tried... not being a mutant?"

At Rogue's suggestion, Bobby shows them what he can do and freezes the drink in his mother's cup. His brother Ronny storms out of the room.

From upstairs, Ronny calls the police and tells them mutants are holding his family hostage.

2-9Storm and Kurt on the jet

On the jet, Storm asks Kurt about the markings on his body. They are angelic symbols he carved himself, he says, one for every sin.

Kurt says he doesn't hate the people who fear him; he pities them. Storm tells him anger can help you survive, and the devout Kurt replies that so can faith.

Jean picks up an active communicator. On the Drakes' porch, Logan answers. They tell each other the news, that soldiers raided the school and that no one can reach Professor X or Cyclops, and the jet heads for Bobby's house.

2-10The police standoff, and the jet

When Logan and the kids step outside, police have surrounded the house. An officer orders Logan, claws out, to drop the knives. As Logan retracts them and tries to explain, a frightened officer shoots him in the head. He drops.

Bobby and Rogue are forced to the ground. John, furious, hurls fire and blows up one police car after another. Rogue grabs his ankle with her bare hand, drains his power and puts the flames out.

Logan's wound heals and the bullet falls out of his forehead. The jet touches down on the lawn. Bobby looks back at his family watching from the window, then boards.

Part 3: An alliance with the enemy

3-1Fighter jets attack

On the jet, Kurt introduces himself to Logan: in the circus he was known as Nightcrawler.

As they near the school, two Air Force fighters fall in behind them and order them to descend. Storm refuses, darkens the sky and whips up a field of tornadoes. One fighter loses control and goes down, but the other fires two missiles before its pilot ejects.

Jean, her eyes glowing, uses her power to throw one missile off course. The other explodes right behind them and tears a hole in the hull. Rogue, who wasn't strapped in, is sucked out. Nightcrawler teleports outside, catches her in mid-air and brings her back.

3-2Who stopped the crash

The jet plunges toward the ground. Just before impact, the hole in the hull seals itself and the jet stops dead in the air. It isn't Jean's doing.

Waiting in the woods are Magneto and Mystique.

3-3By the campfire

That night they gather around a campfire in the woods, and Magneto explains Stryker's plan. Stryker raided the school to take enough of Cerebro to build one of his own.

Only Professor X can operate Cerebro. But if he, linked to every mind on the planet, were forced to concentrate hard enough on one group, such as mutants, he could kill them all at once.

Magneto tells Logan that Stryker is the one who put the adamantium on his bones. Mystique has found plans of Stryker's base, but they don't know where it is. Magneto looks up at Nightcrawler, perched in a tree.

3-4Nightcrawler's memories

Jean reads Nightcrawler's mind. What she sees, an operation, is very much like what she once saw in Logan's.

The base is at Alkali Lake. When Logan says there was nothing there, Jean answers that there is nothing on the surface: the base is underground.

3-5Logan and Jean

Logan and Jean have a moment alone. Jean is worried about the captured Cyclops.

Logan kisses her. She lets him for a moment, then pulls away, tells him it is Cyclops she loves, and leaves.

3-6Mystique's trick

Nightcrawler asks Mystique why, if she can look like anyone, she doesn't stay in disguise all the time. She answers, "Because we shouldn't have to."

Later, Jean comes into Logan's tent and kisses him. But Logan feels the scars his own claws once left: it is Mystique. "No one's ever left a scar quite like you," she says. She shifts through Storm, Rogue and others, but Logan throws her out.

3-7The little girl

At the base, Professor X is inside Jason's illusions. He finds himself in his study at the school, standing on his own legs. Realizing it isn't real, he shouts at Jason to stop, but the illusions go on.

In the next one, a little girl is crying. Professor X says they will find the other students with Cerebro, and takes her there. In reality the girl is Jason, and the room they are entering is the second Cerebro Stryker has built.

3-8The morning they set out

The next morning they fly to Alkali Lake, and the X-Men suit up. On board, Magneto needles Rogue about the white streak in her hair, which he gave her in the last film. To John he says, "You are a god among insects. Never let anyone tell you different," and wins him over.

The way into the base is through the dam's spillway. If Stryker floods it while they are inside, it is over. And Nightcrawler can't teleport to a place he can't see.

Logan volunteers, guessing Stryker wants him alive. But Magneto says whoever goes in has to be able to operate the controls, and looks at Mystique.

Part 4: The base at Alkali Lake

4-1Mystique as Logan

Logan walks alone down the spillway, shouting Stryker's name. Watching on a monitor, Stryker tells his men not to flood it and has him brought in.

The moment they are inside the base, "Logan" turns back into Mystique, kicks down the soldiers around her and slips away. Then, disguised as Stryker, she walks into the control room, knocks out the operator and seals the door.

She opens the spillway doors.

4-2Splitting up

The X-Men and Magneto enter the base and gather in the control room. Mystique brings up a map: the large round room is the second Cerebro, and it can't be shut down from here.

Magneto and Mystique will go and stop it, and Jean goes with them to keep an eye on them. Storm, seeing the kidnapped children on a monitor, sets off with Nightcrawler to free them. Rogue, Bobby and John have been told to wait in the jet.

Logan spots Stryker on one of the screens and slips away on his own without a word.

4-3Stryker's order

Stryker posts his soldiers around Cerebro and goes inside. He leans down and whispers in Jason's ear that it is time to find all the mutants, everywhere.

In the illusion, the little girl says the same thing to Professor X. He agrees and puts on the helmet.

Outside, Stryker orders his men to shoot anyone who approaches, even if it looks like him.

4-4Cyclops, under control

On the way to Cerebro, Jean, Magneto and Mystique run into Cyclops, who is under Stryker's drug and about to fire. Jean shoves the other two out of the way with her power and tells them to go on.

She hurls Cyclops down to a lower level, then goes after him, worried he is hurt.

4-5The kidnapped children

Storm and Nightcrawler find the six kidnapped children in a cell beneath a grate in the floor.

Nightcrawler teleports in. The children back away, startled by the blue figure who has appeared out of nowhere.

4-6The dam is damaged

On the lower level, Cyclops fires at Jean from hiding at full power. She holds the beam back with her mind; her eyes glow and she repels it. Both are thrown backward.

The scattered blast smashes the walls and machinery of the base and cracks the dam. Its collapse can no longer be stopped.

Cyclops comes back to himself, holds Jean and apologizes. Her leg is injured.

4-7The room from Logan's past

Logan goes down a stairway and finds the room from his nightmares. X-rays of bones and claws hang on the walls. In the middle is a tank, and beside it a vat of molten adamantium and heavy injection needles.

Fragments of memory come back to him. This is where the metal was put on his bones, and where he burst out of the tank and fled, naked.

Stryker and Yuriko walk in. Stryker explains that adamantium has to be kept hot and liquid, because once it cools it is indestructible. He used to think Logan was one of a kind, he says as he leaves. He was wrong.

4-8The fight with Yuriko

Logan goes after Stryker, but Yuriko blocks his way. Long adamantium claws slide out of her fingertips. Like Logan, she heals almost instantly.

She stabs him again and again and throws him across the room. She tosses him onto a metal grate hanging over the tank and keeps stabbing him from below, until he slices through its supports and drops the whole platform on her. She still bursts out and stabs him again.

Logan grabs one of the injection needles, drives it into her and pumps her full of molten adamantium. The metal runs from her eyes and nose and hardens inside her. Yuriko sinks into the tank, dead.

Part 5: The fight over Cerebro

5-1Cerebro turns on mutants

On the jet, John gets tired of waiting and walks out, ignoring Rogue's protests.

Inside the illusion, Professor X begins to concentrate on every mutant in the world. In the base, the X-Men, the children and Logan all clutch their heads and fall. Mutants everywhere are suffering the same way.

Magneto, wearing the helmet that shields him from telepathy, reaches Cerebro and forces the doors open with his power. Cerebro stops, and the mutants' pain ends.

5-2Turning Cerebro around

But Magneto doesn't rescue Professor X. He rises into the air and rearranges the metal panels on the chamber walls, reconfiguring the machine.

Mystique, disguised as Stryker, walks in and tells Jason there has been a change of plans, whispering in his ear. Magneto says goodbye to his old friend and seals the doors behind them.

In the illusion, the little girl tells Professor X that now they must find all the humans.

5-3Chasing Stryker

Stryker is outside, about to escape in his helicopter. Logan catches up, punches him and drives his claws into him.

"You cut me open. You took my life," Logan says. Stryker answers that Logan volunteered for the procedure, and that he was an animal then and is an animal now; Stryker only gave him claws.

An alarm sounds and water bursts from the dam. Stryker says it is too late for anyone inside, and offers Logan the answers if he comes along. Logan replies that if they die, Stryker dies, ties him to the helicopter and runs back into the base.

5-4Magneto takes the helicopter

Cerebro's power turns on humans. Now it is the humans, all over the world, who clutch their heads and fall. Stryker is in agony, and so is the President in the White House.

Magneto and Mystique walk up to the suffering Stryker. Magneto chains him to a nearby concrete wall instead and takes the helicopter.

John appears. He leaves the X-Men behind and flies away with Magneto and Mystique.

5-5At the sealed doors

Storm and Nightcrawler, with the rescued children, meet Jean and Cyclops at Cerebro's doors. Jean scans the room with her mind and realizes that Magneto has reversed the machine: it is now targeting humans.

Cyclops gets ready to blast the doors, but Jean stops him. With Professor X still connected to Cerebro, breaking in could kill him and everyone his mind is linked to.

Storm asks Nightcrawler to take her inside. Teleporting somewhere he can't see is dangerous, but she tells him she has faith in him. Jean warns them not to believe anything they see in there.

5-6A snowstorm inside Cerebro

Praying, Nightcrawler holds Storm and the two appear inside the chamber. They don't see Professor X, only a little girl standing alone. Nightcrawler starts toward her, but Storm stops him.

Her eyes turn white, and she fills the room with fierce wind and freezing cold. The girl screams at her to stop. The real Jason can't bear the cold, and the illusion breaks.

Professor X comes to his senses and takes off the helmet. The ceiling starts to collapse, and Nightcrawler teleports Storm and Professor X out. Jason is left behind in the crumbling chamber.

5-7Out of the base

The group runs for the spillway. But Logan appears and slams the spillway door shut by stabbing his claws into a fuse box. A moment later, water sprays through the cracks. He leads everyone to another way out.

Outside in the snow, the helicopter is already gone. Then the jet appears, lurching through the air. Rogue is at the controls, and she just manages to set it down in the snow. Everyone climbs aboard.

Part 6: The dam breaks

6-1Leaving Stryker behind

Carrying a boy named Artie, Logan walks toward the woods instead of the jet. There he finds Stryker, chained to a concrete wall.

Stryker says he is the only one with the answers. But Logan says, "I'll take my chances with him," drops the dog tags Stryker once gave him, and turns his back.

"One day! Someone will finish what I've started!" Stryker shouts after him. Artie sticks out his tongue at him.

6-2The jet won't fly

Logan boards the jet. Rogue asks where John is; Jean searches with her mind and says he is with Magneto.

The jet has lost power and can't take off. Then the dam finally gives way, and a wall of water comes rushing toward them.

6-3Jean's goodbye

Jean looks at her friends one last time, then slips off the jet without anyone noticing. By the time Professor X realizes, she has sealed the ramp from outside with her power.

As the dam goes, Stryker, chained to his wall, is swept away and killed. Jean holds back the flood with one hand and powers up and lifts the jet with the other. When Nightcrawler tries to teleport out to her, she blocks him too.

Speaking through Professor X, she tells her friends this is the only way, and says goodbye to Cyclops. Her body is wrapped in a fiery glow. Once the jet is in the air, she lets go, and the water crashes down on her. Cyclops breaks down in tears.

6-4A message for the President

Washington. The President begins a televised address about the mutant threat. The lights go out and everyone else in the room freezes. In a flash of lightning, the X-Men are standing before him.

Rogue lays files from Stryker's private office on the desk. They show that, without the President's knowledge, someone tried to start a war, and that there were losses on both sides, human and mutant.

This is not a threat, Professor X says, but an opportunity: to repeat the mistakes of the past, or to work together for a better future. The next move is the President's. Then the X-Men vanish.

6-5Back at the school

At the school, Professor X, Cyclops and Logan talk about Jean. Cyclops asks whether they could have done more; Professor X says she made a choice.

As they leave the room, Logan tells Cyclops that Jean did make a choice: it was him.

Students file into the classroom, and Professor X begins his lesson.

6-6A shape in the lake

In the final scene, Jean's voice speaks about evolution. On the surface of the flooded Alkali Lake, a shape like a fiery bird appears.

Summary (spoilers through the end)

Stryker's plan and how it ended

1) He drugs Nightcrawler into attacking the President and wins permission to investigate the school (Part 1).

2) He drugs Magneto to learn about Cerebro, and traps Professor X and Cyclops when they come to visit (Part 1).

3) He raids the school and takes parts of Cerebro and six students (Part 2).

4) Through his son Jason's illusions, he tries to make Professor X kill every mutant with a second Cerebro (Parts 3–5).

5) Magneto stops it but turns it on humans; Storm and Nightcrawler stop that too (Part 5).

6) Stryker is killed when the dam bursts (Part 6).

Logan's past

1) He searches Alkali Lake and finds nothing (Part 1).

2) He learns that Stryker, who raids the school, has known him for years (Part 2).

3) Magneto tells him Stryker put the metal on his bones (Part 3).

4) In a room at the base, he remembers the procedure and his escape (Part 4).

5) Stryker tells him he volunteered, but gives no real answers (Part 5).

6) He chooses his friends over the answers and throws away his dog tags (Part 6).

Who died

- Laurio (Part 2): the guard at Magneto's prison. Mystique injects him with iron, and the next day Magneto rips it out of his body.

- Yuriko (Part 4): Stryker's assistant, controlled by his drug. Logan pumps molten adamantium into her during their fight.

- Stryker (Part 6): tied to the helicopter by Logan, then chained to a concrete wall by Magneto. Logan leaves him there, and he is swept away when the dam bursts.

- Jean (Part 6): she steps outside and holds back the water so her friends' jet can escape, then is swept under. In the last shot, a shape like a fiery bird appears on the lake.

My takeOpinion

Much better than the first, and near the top of the series, I think. The opening Nightcrawler scene and the enemies joining forces still hold up.
