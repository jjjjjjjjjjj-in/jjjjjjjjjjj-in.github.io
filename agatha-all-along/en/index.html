Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

It follows WandaVision

At the end of WandaVision, Wanda trapped Agatha in Westview as a nosy neighbor. The story starts there.

Songs matter

The Ballad of the Witches' Road is key to the story.

A trial each episode

On the road, each witch faces a trial tied to her own craft.

A link to Wanda's family

Someone connected to Wanda's family plays a big role.

StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

Episode 1: Seekest Thou the Road

In Westview, Agatha believes she is Detective Agnes, investigating a Jane Doe murder. Working with FBI agent Rio Vidal, she catches a teen who breaks into her house.

The teen's chant and Rio's prodding wake Agatha from Wanda's three-year spell, but her magic is gone. Rio, a witch, attacks her, then leaves, saying she'll tell the Salem Seven where Agatha is.

Episode 2: Circle Sewn with Fate / Unlock Thy Hidden Gate

The teen asks Agatha to take him down the Witches' Road, which rewards witches who survive its trials. Intrigued by the spell that hides his name, she starts building a coven.

She gathers the diviner Lilia, the potions witch Jen, whose magic is bound, the protection witch Alice, and her non-magical neighbor Sharon. As the Salem Seven attack, a door appears and the group escapes onto the Road.

Episode 3: Through Many Miles / Of Tricks and Trials

The first trial is a beach house. The wine everyone but the teen drank is poisoned, and the witches battle visions of their pasts while gathering antidote ingredients.

As the house sinks, Jen finishes the antidote just in time, and they escape through a tunnel in the oven. Back on the Road, they find Sharon dead.

Episode 4: If I Can't Reach You / Let My Song Teach You

Summoning a new green witch brings Rio out of Sharon's grave. The next trial is a 1970s studio tied to Alice's late mother, Lorna.

When the teen unleashes the Wu family curse, the coven destroys it by performing Lorna's ballad, a protection spell. Jen heals the injured teen, and Rio tells Agatha the boy is not hers.

Episode 5: Darkest Hour / Wake Thy Power

Chased by the Salem Seven, the coven reaches a 1980s cabin for Agatha's trial. A Ouija board summons the ghost of Evanora, the mother Agatha killed, who possesses her.

Alice drives the ghost out, but Agatha absorbs her power and Alice dies. Furious, the teen controls the others' minds and sinks the three witches in mud as a crown like the Scarlet Witch's forms on his head.

Episode 6: Familiar by Thy Side

In 2023, William Kaplan, a boy from the next town, dies in a car crash the day Westview collapses, and the soul of Wanda's son Billy enters his body.

After three years without memories, Billy learns who he is from Ralph Bohner, with his boyfriend Eddie at his side. He freed Agatha to find his twin, Tommy. Out of the mud, Agatha persuades him to go on with her.

Episode 7: Death's Hand in Mine

Told out of order. In a castle, a tarot trial drops a sword for every wrong card.

Lilia, who lives her life out of sequence, finally understands her gift and passes the trial by reading for herself. She reveals that Rio is Death, then stays behind and flips the castle, taking the Salem Seven with her.

Episode 8: Follow Me My Friend / To Glory at the End

Agatha makes a deal with Rio, who is Death, to hand over Billy. The Road turns out to be a circle; in the last trial Jen regains her power and Billy moves Tommy's soul into another boy's body.

Fighting Rio in her backyard, Agatha kisses her and dies so Billy can live. At home, Billy realizes he created the Road, and hears Agatha laughing.

Episode 9: Maiden Mother Crone

In 1750, Agatha begs Death to spare her newborn son, Nicholas. The song they make together becomes the ballad, but Death takes Nicholas six years later.

For centuries Agatha used the made-up Road to con witches. Now a ghost, she admits to Billy she isn't ready to face her son, and the two set out to find Tommy.

Key terms

The Witches' Road
A legendary road from a song: witches who survive its trials are said to get their heart's desire.
Coven
The group of witches needed to walk the road.
The Green Witch
One of the roles a coven needs.
Westview
The town where WandaVision took place.
The Darkhold
The book of dark magic from WandaVision and Multiverse of Madness.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Who is the teen?

Billy Maximoff, one of the twins Wanda created in WandaVision. When Westview vanished, his soul ended up in the body of a teenager dying in a nearby accident.

Who is Rio Vidal?

Agatha's former lover, who is Death itself.

Was the Witches' Road real?

Agatha originally made it up, but Billy's power brought it into being.

What happens to Agatha?

She accepts Death to protect Billy, but later appears beside him as a ghost.

Easy-to-miss details

A cop-show opener

Episode 1 is styled like an old TV detective drama.

Many versions of the ballad

The same song returns in different styles throughout the series.

Looking for Tommy

At the end, Billy sets out to find his twin brother Tommy.
