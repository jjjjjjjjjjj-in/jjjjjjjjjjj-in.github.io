Episode 1: Seekest Thou the Road

Detective Agnes

In Westview, Agatha has forgotten she's a witch and lives as a detective named Agnes, in a world that looks like an old TV cop show.

The mysterious teen

One day a teenager shows up, and the spell breaks. Agatha realizes Wanda's magic has trapped her for almost three years.

A witch without power

Wanda took Agatha's powers, and they're still gone. The teen asks her to take him to the Witches' Road. Hoping to regain her power at its end, she agrees.

Rio Vidal

Rio Vidal, Agatha's former lover, also appears. There's a dangerous history between them.

Episode 2: Circle Sewn With Fate

A coven is needed

Walking the road takes a coven. Agatha and the teen look up witches she once knew.

Three witches

Lilia, a diviner; Jennifer, Jen, a potions expert; and Alice, a protection witch, join them. Their neighbor Sharon is brought along to fill the last spot.

The entrance

When the witches sing the Ballad of the Witches' Road, an entrance opens in the ground and the road appears.

Episodes 3–5: The trials

The first trial

Trial houses wait along the road. The witches must work together and solve each puzzle to go on.

Alice's trial

Episode 4 is Alice's trial. Her mother once recorded the ballad. Right after they get through it, Alice is killed.

Sharon's fate

After the trial in Episode 5, Sharon, who has no magic, also dies on the road.

The teen's secret

At the end of Episode 5, the teen reveals who he is: Billy Maximoff, Wanda's son.

Episode 6: Billy's story

The accident

The episode flashes back. When Westview vanished, the soul of Wanda's son Billy slipped into the body of a teenager dying in a nearby car crash.

Billy Kaplan

Living as that boy, Billy discovers powerful magic within him as he grows.

Searching for his brother

His twin brother, Tommy, should be out there somewhere. Billy wanted the road to find him.

Episode 7: Lilia's trial

Out of order

Lilia experiences time out of order, past and future mixed together. Her strange remarks begin to make sense.

The tarot trial

Lilia's trial is a tarot puzzle. Accepting her fate, she saves the others and dies.

Episode 8: The end of the road

Jen's power

Jen learns Agatha once bound her powers. In the final trial, Jen gets them back.

A made-up road

Agatha admits the Witches' Road was a story she spread herself. It became real because of Billy's power.

Death revealed

Rio Vidal is Death itself, and she comes for Billy.

Agatha's choice

Agatha gives up her own life in Billy's place and dies in Rio's arms.

Episode 9: Agatha's past

The first ballad

Agatha's past is revealed: she had a son, Nicky, and tried to protect him. Together they created the song of the Witches' Road.

Ghost Agatha

Later, Agatha appears to Billy as a ghost.

A new journey

Billy and Agatha's ghost set off together to find Tommy.
