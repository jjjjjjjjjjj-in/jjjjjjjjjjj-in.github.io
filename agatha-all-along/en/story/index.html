Episode 1: Seekest Thou the Road

1-1Detective Agnes O'Connor

The episode opens with fake opening titles for a crime show, Agnes of Westview, set to a true-crime version of "The Ballad of the Witches' Road," the song that will return again and again.

A Jane Doe's body is found outside Westview. Detective Agnes O'Connor arrives on the scene. She is really Agatha, but she believes she is a detective.

Searching nearby, Agnes finds a mysterious locket and decides the murder happened somewhere else.

1-2The library and Agent Rio

Following a card found on the body, Agnes goes to the town library. The book it lists was stolen three years ago, and a fire scorched the shelf where it stood, destroying every copy.

At the station, FBI agent Rio Vidal shows up, an old acquaintance, and Agnes reluctantly teams up with her.

Later, Agnes realizes the initials of the stolen book's title spell "Darkhold."

1-3A teen breaks in

That night Rio visits Agnes at home to talk about the case. Agnes admits she can't remember why the two of them don't get along.

A teenage burglar breaks into the house. Agnes catches him, pulls off his mask, and interrogates him about the murder.

1-4The illusion cracks

During the interrogation, the teen chants in Latin. Agnes realizes the "crime-scene photos" are pictures of flowers and the one-way mirror is just a painting.

At the morgue, the victim is identified as Wanda Maximoff. Encouraged by Rio, Agnes peels away the layers of the spell. Agnes falls away, and the witch Agatha Harkness comes back.

1-5Back in Westview after three years

Agatha comes to in her Westview home, and her magic is gone.

Her neighbor Herb tells her she has been under Wanda's spell for three years and that her life as a detective was an illusion. In the basement, all her magical equipment has been taken.

In a closet she finds the teen, tied up, and realizes she kidnapped him while under the spell.

1-6Rio attacks

Rio arrives, revealed to be a witch, and attacks Agatha.

As the fight escalates, Agatha asks Rio to wait until she has her powers back so it can be a fair fight. Rio backs off, but says she'll tell the Salem Seven, witches who hate Agatha, where she is.

The teen is still tied up in the closet, and Agatha doesn't know what to do with him.

Episode 2: Circle Sewn with Fate / Unlock Thy Hidden Gate

2-1The teen's request

Agatha is about to leave Westview when the teen asks her to take him down the Witches' Road, a series of trials that rewards witches with what they lack.

She refuses at first. But he admits he undid the Scarlet Witch's spell, and he unknowingly shows a spell on him that stops him from saying his name. Agatha agrees.

Walking the Road takes a coven, so the two leave Westview to recruit witches.

2-2Lilia, the diviner

First they visit the divination witch Lilia Calderu, who is wary of Agatha's reputation as a witch killer.

Agatha reveals she can only absorb power from witches who attack her first. A vision leads Lilia to write a list of witches fated to join the coven, and Agatha points out Lilia's own name is on it.

2-3Jen, the potions witch

Next is the potions witch Jennifer "Jen" Kale, whose magic is bound so she can't use it.

The teen brings up a lawsuit over her skincare products, and the pair pitch the Road as her way out of a life sentence.

2-4Alice, the protection witch

At a mall, they approach the protection witch Alice Wu-Gulliver, daughter of the rock singer who recorded the best-known version of the ballad.

Alice turns them down, insisting the Road is a fiction invented by her late mother's fans.

2-5Words she can't hear

On the drive back to Westview, the teen talks about his life, but the spell on him means Agatha can't hear any of the personal details.

2-6Neighbor Sharon makes five

The coven gathers at Agatha's house, and Alice unexpectedly shows up too.

They still need a green witch. Lilia's note lists an unnamed fifth member marked with a black heart. Agatha brings in her neighbor Sharon Davis, a keen gardener with no magic, who was "Mrs. Hart" under Wanda's spell.

2-7The Salem Seven and the door

In the basement, the five women chant the ballad to conjure the entrance to the Road, and the Salem Seven attack the house.

The Road doesn't appear. Agatha taunts the witches, but Lilia stops them from striking back, which would only give Agatha power to absorb.

Suddenly a door appears. The coven and the teen go through it and escape the Seven.

2-8The Witches' Road

Now on the Witches' Road, the group take off their shoes, pause to take in their surroundings, and begin the journey.

Episode 3: Through Many Miles / Of Tricks and Trials

3-1A hidden name

On the Road, Sharon accuses the coven of kidnapping her. When the teen tries to introduce himself, the witches recognize the spell hiding his identity as a sigil, which keeps any witch from learning who he is.

Agatha explains that each witch's specialty will be tested in its own trial. Sharon strays off the Road and nearly sinks into a mud pool before the coven pulls her out.

3-2The beach house and the wine

The first trial is a stylish coastal house. Inside, everyone's clothes change to match it.

A riddle appears, and Sharon solves it by spotting a bottle of wine. Everyone but the teen drinks, and a countdown timer starts.

Jen privately warns the teen not to trust Agatha, who is rumored to have given up her child for the Darkhold. While the teen and Alice swap stories, Agatha secretly pours her wine away.

3-3Poisoned

After a few minutes, everyone's faces briefly swell. Jen realizes the wine was poisoned and identifies the toxin.

Agatha's glass magically refills, and when the teen threatens to drink it for her, she has no choice but to drink. Under the poison, Sharon hallucinates the trauma she lived through under Wanda's spell and faints.

3-4Visions of the past

Following Jen's instructions, they split into pairs to search the house for antidote ingredients, and each witch is hit by vivid visions of her past.

Jen relives having her magic bound. Lilia sees her younger self and a shadowy figure over her old maestra. Alice sees her mother, Lorna Wu, about to die.

They gather the ingredients, but the house is now underwater, and water leaks through cracks Agatha made in a window when she tried to flee earlier.

3-5The antidote, just in time

They move the unconscious Sharon into the kitchen and brew the antidote under Jen's direction. Agatha hallucinates a crib holding the Darkhold.

Spurred on by Agatha, Jen finishes the antidote just before time runs out. They all drink it and give a dose to Sharon. As the house floods, they escape through a tunnel inside the kitchen oven.

3-6Sharon is dead

They're back on the Road. As Lilia tells Agatha and Jen to stop arguing, the group realize Sharon is dead.

Episode 4: If I Can't Reach You / Let My Song Teach You

4-1Sharon's grave and a new green witch

Alice and the teen bury Sharon. Agatha wants to hurry on, but the others refuse: they need a green witch for one of the trials.

They cast a summoning spell for a replacement, and Rio Vidal rises out of Sharon's grave. Agatha is dismayed; the rest of the coven are glad to have someone who annoys her.

4-2Alice's house

A house appears, and Alice won't go in. When they change direction, it reappears in their path.

Inside is a 1970s recording studio, and they're all dressed as a rock band. The house is tied to Alice's late mother, Lorna Wu, a famous witch musician killed by the Wu family's generational curse.

4-3Overheard in the booth

In a sound booth, Rio and Agatha flirtatiously talk about betraying the group, but the intercom is on and the others hear every word.

4-4The curse returns

The teen plays a record that spins backward and unleashes the curse. Lilia and Jen are scorched until Alice protects them with a magic circle.

The coven realizes the curse should have killed Alice years ago. Lorna's famous cover of the ballad is a protection spell, kept working by being played over and over.

4-5The band plays the ballad

To break the curse, they perform Lorna's version of the song, with Rio on drums. The curse takes the form of a winged demon and sets the house on fire.

Alice finishes the song with the others and destroys the curse for good.

4-6The teen is hurt

Outside, the teen collapses from a wound he took during the trial. On the Road, at Agatha's urging, Jen heals him.

Recovering, he talks with Agatha about his sigil. She claims not to know who cast it, adding that even the witch who did wouldn't remember. When he asks about her son, she doesn't answer.

4-7Scars, and Rio's warning

The rest of the coven bond over the scars of their pasts. When Agatha joins them, Rio hints at their old romance and at how she once hurt Agatha. Agatha walks away.

Rio follows to comfort her. When Agatha tries to kiss her, Rio stops her and tells her the teen is not hers.

Episode 5: Darkest Hour / Wake Thy Power

5-1The Salem Seven close in

Resting on the Road, Lilia wakes from a vision of the Salem Seven closing in.

She explains that the Seven are the children of Agatha's original coven, spared when Agatha killed their parents, and that they entered the Road through the gateway opened when Rio was summoned.

5-2Brooms in the sky

The coven tries to flee on makeshift flying broomsticks, but the Road drags them back to the ground.

Narrowly escaping the Seven, they enter a 1980s-style forest cabin. Rio identifies the trial as Agatha's.

5-3The Ouija board

The teen finds a Ouija board and reads the rules aloud, and the coven uses it to contact the dead.

Agatha first fools everyone by pretending to be possessed by Sharon, but then a spirit truly takes her over. Possessed, she attacks the teen and Jen until Lilia turns on the lights and the ghost is briefly driven out.

5-4Evanora's ghost

The spirit is Agatha's mother, Evanora Harkness, whom Agatha murdered along with her original coven.

Evanora demands they abandon Agatha to finish the trial. Rio and the teen object. As Jen and Lilia get ready to leave, Evanora possesses Agatha again.

5-5Alice dies

Alice strikes Agatha with her magic and drives Evanora out of her body. But Agatha seizes the moment and absorbs Alice's power, killing her.

The teen uses the Ouija board to spell out "Nicholas Scratch," the name of Agatha's son. A disembodied voice breaks Agatha's trance, and an exit appears.

5-6The teen's fury

Back on the Road, the teen confronts Agatha over Alice's death, which she insists was an accident.

When he condemns the witches for chasing power, Agatha gleefully mocks him and says he takes after his mother.

5-7The mud and the crown

In a surge of emotion, the teen seizes control of Lilia's and Jen's minds, makes them hurl Agatha into a mud pool, and then blasts them in too. The three begin to sink.

A crown like the Scarlet Witch's headpiece forms on his head.

Episode 6: Familiar by Thy Side

6-1William's bar mitzvah

In 2023, 13-year-old William Kaplan celebrates his bar mitzvah in Eastview, New Jersey.

Lilia is there as a fortune teller. Reading his palm, she sees his lifeline split in two, and sees something more that she keeps to herself. As he leaves her tent, she casts a protective sigil on him and instantly forgets meeting him.

6-2The crash and a new life

The party breaks up as the Westview anomaly, Wanda's town, begins to collapse. Driving home with his parents, William is distracted by a radio signal from the anomaly, and the car crashes.

William dies of his injuries, but as he passes, the soul of Billy Maximoff, Wanda's son, enters his body and he comes back to life.

He has no memory of either life, and his sudden ability to read minds leaves him distraught.

6-3Eddie and Ralph Bohner

Three years later, Billy confesses to his boyfriend Eddie that his memories have never come back.

Looking into the Westview anomaly, they meet an online poster who claims to have lived there: Ralph Bohner. He describes being mind-controlled by Agatha, and tells them about Wanda and Vision's twins, Billy and Tommy.

As he listens, Billy begins to realize who he really is.

6-4Going to Agatha

Billy researches Agatha and the Witches' Road and goes to Westview, hoping to free her from the Scarlet Witch's spell and find his twin brother, Tommy.

Still under the spell, "Agnes" captures and interrogates him (Episode 1's scenes, seen from Billy's side). Billy breaks the enchantment and introduces himself as both William Kaplan and Billy Maximoff.

6-5Out of the mud

Back in the present on the Road, Agatha drags herself out of the mud. Realizing the sigil is broken, she claims she had an inkling of his identity all along.

Teasing Billy for his poor control of his powers and guessing his goal, she persuades him to go on to the next trial with her.

Episode 7: Death's Hand in Mine

7-1Lilia falls

This episode is told out of order, the way Lilia experiences it.

It opens with a distraught Lilia falling through a black void.

7-2The castle and the tarot

On the Road, Billy questions Agatha about Wanda's death and starts to doubt she has ever walked the Road before, as she claims.

They find a castle and go inside. Dressed as witches from classic fairy tales, they're presented with the next trial: a tarot reading table. They try reading for each other, and every wrong card brings a sword down from the ceiling.

7-3In the tunnels

In the tunnels under the Road, Lilia and Jen follow a path Jen says Lilia suggested. Lilia doesn't remember, but Jen recounts everything she says Lilia told her minutes ago.

Lilia explains her secret: since childhood she has lived her life out of order, her mind shifting between points in her own timeline. That is why she says strange things and forgets things.

7-4The first lesson

One shift takes Lilia to her first lesson in divination. Her maestra laments that Lilia has no faith in her own power, ever since she foresaw the end of her first coven and couldn't stop it.

7-5Swords keep falling

Flash forward: Lilia and Jen are in the castle with Billy and Agatha. Lilia reads for Billy, but the swords keep falling.

Back at the lesson, her maestra urges her to believe her gift has a purpose, and Lilia understands what falling through the void meant.

7-6Back to the beginning

Lilia finds herself at the start of the tunnels and tells Jen everything Jen will later say she learned from her.

Evading the Salem Seven, they set off to join Agatha and Billy at the trial.

7-7Lilia's reading, and Rio's secret

In the castle, Lilia realizes she must do the reading for herself. She lays the cards in the right order and passes the trial.

She reveals that Rio is Death itself, and that Agatha knew.

7-8Lilia's end

Lilia tells everyone to run and stays behind despite Jen's pleas. The Salem Seven ambush her.

She turns the Tower card from her reading upright. Gravity in the castle flips, and the Seven are impaled on the swords. Lilia lets go and falls.

In the final scene, young Lilia happily heads to her first divination lesson.

Episode 8: Follow Me My Friend / To Glory at the End

8-1Alice's spirit

Alice wakes as a spirit in the cabin where she died. Death, as Rio, comes to claim her and tells her she died trying to save Agatha.

Distraught, Alice accepts her fate and passes on.

8-2A deal with Death

On the Road, Rio confronts Agatha. She has learned who Billy is and opposes his return from the dead.

Agatha promises to hand Billy over, in exchange for never seeing Rio again, even in death. Rio agrees and cuts her way out of the Road.

8-3The Road is a circle

Jen and Billy mourn Lilia and talk about who Rio really is before rejoining Agatha.

They reach the spot where they first took off their shoes: the Road is a circle. Agatha furiously insists they walk it again, but Billy defiantly puts his shoes back on.

8-4The last trial and Jen's power

The three are taken to a morgue-like version of Agatha's basement. To pass, they must grow a plant before the grow lights overhead go out.

Jen learns Agatha was the one who bound her magic, a spell Agatha had cast for money. Jen performs an unbinding ritual and gets her power back. Having what she came for, she vanishes from the Road.

8-5Tommy's soul

Agatha helps Billy find Tommy's soul, and he moves it into the body of a drowning boy. Then Billy vanishes too.

Out of time, Agatha uses a seed from her locket and her own tears to grow a flower in a crack in the floor, passes the trial, and escapes the Road.

8-6Fighting Death

Agatha emerges in her backyard in Westview. Rio confronts her for failing to deliver Billy and for rejecting her.

Billy comes to the rescue and lets Agatha draw some of his magic to restore hers. She fights Rio but soon admits it is futile to fight Death. Rio demands that either Agatha or Billy go with her.

8-7Agatha's end

Billy volunteers, and Agatha surprises him by agreeing. Speaking into her mind, Billy asks if this is how her son, Nicholas, died.

That changes her heart. Agatha kisses Rio and dies, and flowers grow where her body falls. Death shows Billy her true form and tells him to go.

8-8Billy's room

Back home, Billy is shocked to see that many objects in his bedroom match parts of the Witches' Road. He realizes he created it with his magic without knowing.

Then he hears Agatha laughing behind him.

Episode 9: Maiden Mother Crone

9-11750: a birth in the woods

In 1750, Agatha goes into labor in the wilderness, and Death appears to claim the child.

Agatha pleads, and Death relents, but warns that she will come back for him.

9-2Traveling with Nicholas

Six years later, Agatha travels with her son, Nicholas Scratch, who helps her lure gullible witches so she can feed on their magic.

On the road, the two come up with the first version of "The Ballad of the Witches' Road." It spreads as a folk song, and Agatha uses it in her killing schemes, which Nicholas grows to oppose.

9-3Nicholas dies

One night, Death wakes Nicholas. He kisses his sleeping mother goodbye and goes with her to the afterlife.

The next morning, Agatha finds his body and is devastated.

9-4A made-up road

After the burial, a curious witch asks Agatha how to find the Witches' Road.

For centuries afterward, Agatha uses the Road to con witches and grow her power. Her latest marks were Jen, Alice, Lilia, and Sharon, but she was blindsided when the Road actually appeared, and she worked out that Billy had made it.

9-5Agatha's ghost

In the present, Agatha's ghost confronts Billy. She explains that his magic created the Road, which tipped her off that he was Wanda's son.

Billy is horrified to have caused the deaths of Sharon, Alice, and Lilia. Agatha points out she meant to kill them from the start, and credits him with Jen's survival. Meanwhile Jen climbs out of the ground near Westview and flies away.

9-6The banishing spell

Billy goes to Westview and searches Agatha's basement for his spell book. He begins a spell to send her to the afterlife, but she stops him, admitting she isn't ready to face her son. Billy relents.

At first the ghost can't touch anything, but soon she manages to pick up her locket.

9-7Sealing the Road, and looking for Tommy

Billy seals the entrance to the Witches' Road with an epitaph honoring their fallen coven.

Then Billy and Agatha set out to find his brother, Tommy.

Summary (spoilers through the finale)

What the Witches' Road really was

- Origins (Ep. 9): Agatha and her son Nicholas made up the ballad on the road in the 1750s. After his death, Agatha used it to spread the legend of the Road and con witches out of their power.

- A real Road (Eps. 2, 8, 9): Billy unknowingly brought the made-up Road into being with his magic, which is why objects from his bedroom turned up on it.

- The teen (Eps. 5, 6): Billy Maximoff, Wanda's son, whose soul entered the dying William Kaplan the day Westview vanished. Lilia cast the sigil hiding him.

- Rio Vidal (Eps. 1, 7, 9): Agatha's former lover, and Death. She spared Nicholas at birth in 1750 and took him six years later.

- The trials: the poisoned-wine house in Ep. 3 (passed with Jen's antidote), Alice's protection in Ep. 4, Agatha's in Ep. 5, Lilia's divination in Ep. 7; Agatha passes the last trial in Ep. 8 by growing a flower.

- Survivors: Billy and Jen. Agatha, now a ghost, sets out with Billy to find Tommy.

Who died (in order)

- Sharon Davis (Ep. 3): drank the poisoned wine in the first trial and fainted; though given the antidote, she was found dead back on the Road.

- Alice Wu-Gulliver (Ep. 5): struck the possessed Agatha to drive out Evanora's ghost, and Agatha absorbed her power, killing her. Death guides her spirit on in Ep. 8.

- The Salem Seven (Ep. 7): impaled on the castle's swords when Lilia flipped the room upside down.

- Lilia Calderu (Ep. 7): stayed behind so the others could escape, taking the Seven with her as she fell.

- Agatha Harkness (Ep. 8): when Death demanded her or Billy, she kissed Rio and died so Billy could live. She returns as a ghost at the end of Ep. 8.

- Earlier deaths (Eps. 5, 9): Agatha killed her mother Evanora in 1693. Her son Nicholas, sickly from birth, was taken by Death at about six.
