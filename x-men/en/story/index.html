Part 1: Two beginnings

1-1The camp gates

The film opens with Professor X's narration about evolution and mutation.

1944, German-occupied Poland. In the rain, a boy named Erik is separated from his parents as he enters a concentration camp.

When soldiers hold him back, the iron gates his parents were taken behind begin to bend toward him, as if pulled by a magnet. The boy can move metal with magnetism. A soldier knocks him unconscious.

1-2Marie's kiss

Years later, in the near future. In Meridian, Mississippi, 17-year-old Marie is in her room, telling her boyfriend David about the trip she wants to take after high school. They lean in and kiss.

David starts to shake, collapses and falls into a coma. Her parents rush in at her scream, but Marie cries out for them not to touch her. Her touch drains the life force, and the powers, of anyone she touches.

1-3The Senate hearing

In Congress, a hearing is being held on the Mutant Registration Act, a bill that would force mutants to reveal their identities and powers.

Testifying, Dr. Jean Grey warns that exposing mutants would leave them open to hostility and violence. But Senator Robert Kelly, the bill's champion, cuts her off and demands to know whether mutants are dangerous. What is to stop a girl who can walk through walls from entering a bank vault or someone's home, he asks, and there are rumors of mutants who can control minds. The room applauds him.

1-4Two old friends

Listening in the audience, Professor Charles Xavier notices a man getting up to leave and follows him into the hall in his wheelchair. The man is Erik, the boy from the camp, who now calls himself Magneto. The two are old friends.

Xavier asks him not to give up on humans, but Magneto says he has heard these arguments before. He asks only that Xavier stay out of his way, and walks off: "We are the future, Charles, not them."

Part 2: A meeting in Canada

2-1The cage fight

Having run away from home, Marie hitches rides until she reaches Laughlin City, a snowbound town in northern Alberta, Canada. In a bar, men are fighting inside a cage.

The man who keeps winning is called the Wolverine. Even when a new challenger gets the better of him, he recovers and flattens the man in seconds. Marie watches him.

2-2A knife and claws

After the fights, the man sits down near Marie at the bar. The TV reports on the coming summit of world leaders in New York, where the mutant question will also be discussed.

The beaten fighter comes over and demands his money back: no man takes a beating like that without a mark. Brushed off, he pulls a knife and attacks from behind. Marie shouts a warning, and the man spins around with three metal claws out of his knuckles, right at the fighter's throat.

When the barman levels a shotgun at him, he slices it apart with his claws and walks out.

2-3Rogue in the trailer

Realizing the man is a mutant like her, Marie stows away in the trailer behind his truck. He finds her once they are on the road, puts her out in the snow and starts to drive off, but stops a little way on and lets her in.

In the cab she introduces herself as Rogue. When he reaches to warm her hands she pulls them away, explaining that people who touch her skin get hurt. She asks whether it hurts when the claws come out. "Every time," he says. His name is Logan, and she tells him her real name is Marie.

2-4Ambush in the snow

Just after Rogue tells him he should wear his seat belt, the truck slams into a tree lying across the road. Logan is thrown through the windshield into the snow. His wounds close before Rogue's eyes.

Rogue is pinned inside, and the truck begins to burn. Heading back to help her, Logan catches an enemy's scent. The giant mutant Sabretooth leaps out of the woods and hurls him around until he is knocked out.

2-5Cyclops and Storm

Two more mutants appear: Cyclops, who fires beams from his eyes, and Storm, who controls the weather. Cyclops blasts Sabretooth away and cuts Rogue free.

They get Logan and Rogue clear seconds before the truck explodes.

2-6Magneto's lair

Sabretooth returns to Magneto's hideout, surrounded by sea. Toad, a mutant with a long tongue, is at work on a large machine.

Sabretooth reports that the others knew they were coming, and that the mutant they wanted is now with them. All he has brought back is Logan's dog tags. Magneto reminds him that the summit is close and they must be ready.

Part 3: The school for gifted youngsters

3-1Logan wakes up

Logan wakes in an unfamiliar infirmary. The woman about to give him an injection is Jean, the doctor from the hearing. Taking her for an enemy, he grabs her and bolts from the room.

Lost in the underground corridors, he hears a voice in his head. It guides him to an elevator and up into a school full of running children. The classroom he ducks into is Professor X's, teaching from his wheelchair. A student named Kitty comes back for something she forgot, then leaves by walking straight through the closed door.

3-2Professor X explains

Professor X tells him this is Xavier's School for Gifted Youngsters, a school for young mutants in Westchester, New York, and that Rogue is safe. In come the two who rescued him, Cyclops (Scott) and Storm, and then Jean.

Sabretooth, he says, works for a very powerful mutant called Magneto. Logan finds it all ridiculous and starts to leave. But Xavier, reading his mind, knows that Logan has no memory of anything before fifteen years ago, and says he can help him find out who he is.

3-3The school, and 48 hours

Xavier shows him around. Here, mutant children with nowhere else to go learn to use their powers. Rogue starts classes too, and meets Bobby, a boy who can make ice.

The school is only the public face: below it is a hangar with a jet. Its teachers, Cyclops, Storm and Jean, are also the X-Men, a team that works for peace between mutants and humans. Xavier tells Logan about Erik, the friend of his youth who came to believe humanity would never accept mutants, and became Magneto.

He offers a deal: give him 48 hours to find out why Magneto is after Logan, and in return he will help Logan uncover his past.

3-4Kelly abducted

Cheered by an anti-mutant crowd, Senator Kelly boards a helicopter with his aide, Henry Gyrich. On board he says that if it were up to him he would lock every mutant away; this is a war.

Glancing out the window, he sees only ocean. Beside him, his aide's body ripples into that of a blue-skinned woman: Mystique, who can take anyone's form. She kicks him unconscious. The pilot is Toad. The helicopter heads for Magneto's island hideout.

3-5The secret of Logan's body

At the school, Jean shows Professor X and the others what her tests on Logan have found. A supposedly indestructible metal called adamantium has been bonded to his entire skeleton.

Only his healing power could have let him survive such an operation. It also makes his age impossible to tell. Logan himself has no memory of who did it.

3-6Jean and Scott

That night Jean shows Logan to his room. Besides moving things with her mind, she tells him, she has some telepathic ability. At his urging she looks into his mind, and sees flashes of fire and an operation.

Scott, her boyfriend, walks in. After Jean leaves, he and Logan trade taunts, and Scott warns him to stay away from his girl.

3-7The mutation machine

In the hideout, Kelly is strapped to a chair, with Mystique, Toad and Sabretooth standing around him. Magneto appears and tells him the real Gyrich has been dead for some time; Mystique has been taking his place.

In front of the senator, Magneto starts a large machine powered by his own magnetism. The light that spreads from it turns ordinary humans into mutants. It washes over Kelly.

When the machine stops, Magneto is so drained that Mystique has to hold him up.

Part 4: Rogue runs away

4-1The nightmare

That night Logan is caught in a nightmare about the metal being bonded to his bones. Rogue, unable to sleep, hears him, comes into his room and tries to wake him.

He jolts awake and his claws go into her chest. Coming to his senses, he pulls them back and shouts for help. Rogue reaches up and touches his cheek. Her wounds close, and Logan, drained, passes out.

Scott, Jean, Storm and the students have rushed in and seen it. Rogue leaves the room saying it was an accident. The students shrink back from her in fear.

4-2Rogue's power

When Logan comes around, Professor X is sitting beside him. Rogue is all right, he says.

He explains that Rogue absorbs the energy of anyone she touches, and with mutants, their gifts as well. This time she took Logan's ability to heal and closed her own wounds. Had she held on any longer, she could have killed him.

4-3Kelly's escape

Locked in a cell in the hideout, Kelly discovers that his body has become soft and stretchy. He pushes his head between the bars and squeezes himself out.

When Magneto and Sabretooth come to look in on him, the cell is empty. Kelly is clinging to the cliff outside. Sabretooth grabs his arm to haul him back, but the hand stretches and slips through his grip, and Kelly falls screaming into the sea.

In daylight, he comes up out of the water onto a beach.

4-4Mystique as Bobby

The next morning Rogue is sitting alone on a bench in the school grounds. Bobby comes over and tells her that mutants should never use their powers on each other, that the students are scared and Professor X is furious, and that in her place he would get out. Close to tears, Rogue walks away.

As he watches her go, Bobby's eyes flash yellow. He is Mystique, who has slipped into the school.

4-5Cerebro

Professor X and Scott are trying to work out what Magneto wants with Logan when Logan comes in asking where Rogue is. Xavier searches with his mind: she is no longer in the school.

He goes into Cerebro, a round chamber that amplifies his power so he can find mutants far away. He cannot find Magneto with it, he says, because Magneto knows how to shield himself. He helped Xavier build it. Jean says it would be too dangerous for her to use.

Xavier locates Rogue at the train station. He tells Logan, the one Magneto is after, to stay put, and sends Scott and Storm to bring her back. But Scott's motorcycle is missing. Logan has taken it and gone ahead.

4-6Mystique's sabotage

In the school, the real Bobby tells his friends that Rogue was supposed to meet him for lunch and never came.

Meanwhile Mystique, still looking like Bobby, goes down to the basement. At Cerebro's door she changes her eyes into Professor X's to pass the scanner, goes in and tampers with the machine.

4-7On the train

Rogue sits on a train waiting to leave, watching the people around her touch each other without a thought. Logan arrives and sits beside her. They each apologize for the night before.

She tells him that the first boy she ever kissed was in a coma for three weeks, and that she can still feel him in her head, and Logan too. He holds her through her clothes and tells her Xavier truly wants to help her. Give these people one more chance, he says, and promises to take care of her.

4-8Magneto attacks

The train starts to move, then jolts to a halt and the lights go out. Magneto tears the carriage open with magnetism and floats in. Logan pops his claws, but with metal all through his skeleton he is simply lifted off the floor and held helpless.

Logan asks what Magneto wants with him. It isn't him he wants, Magneto answers. It is Rogue. He flings Logan aside, puts Rogue out with a tranquilizer dart and takes her.

Meanwhile, inside the station, Storm is asking after Rogue when Sabretooth seizes her by the throat. Scott runs to help, but Toad, clinging to the wall, whips off his visor with his tongue, and Scott's beam blows a hole in the ceiling. Storm calls down lightning and sends Sabretooth flying through a wall.

4-9Rogue taken

When Magneto's group walks out of the station with Rogue, police cars are all around it. He lifts cars with magnetism and drops them, then pulls the officers' guns out of their hands and hangs them in the air, pointed at the officers themselves.

Then Sabretooth and Toad turn on him, telling him to stop. Professor X, in a car nearby, is speaking through their minds. Asked what he needs Rogue for, Magneto gives no answer, and the helmet he wears keeps Xavier out of his thoughts.

Magneto fires one gun and stops the bullet an inch short of an officer. Xavier cannot stop them all, he says, and Xavier has no choice but to let the two go. A helicopter flown by Mystique comes down, and the Brotherhood leaves with Rogue.

Part 5: Kelly's end and getting ready

5-1Kelly at the school

Back at the school, Logan is furious with Professor X for saying Magneto was after him. Xavier apologizes: he made a terrible mistake. Logan means to go after Rogue alone. Storm asks him to fight alongside them, but he says he won't fight for a world that hates them.

He opens the front door, and Senator Kelly is standing there, barely on his feet, asking for Dr. Jean Grey.

5-2What Magneto wants

In the infirmary below, Kelly says he was afraid to go to an ordinary hospital. Professor X reads his mind and sees his memory of the machine, and of how weak Magneto was once it stopped.

Xavier and the others talk it through. The machine's radiation turns ordinary people into mutants. But Kelly's body is rejecting the forced mutation, and his cells are breaking down.

So why Rogue? Scott sees it. The machine runs on Magneto's power, and running it nearly killed him. He means to have Rogue absorb his power and run it in his place, at the cost of her life.

5-3The senator's end

In the infirmary, Storm is sitting with Kelly. Water is running off his body, and he asks her not to leave him alone. Does she hate normal people, he asks. Sometimes, she says, because she is afraid of them. Then she has one less person to be afraid of, he tells her.

His body begins to shake, breaks down into water and spills across the floor. Storm goes to tell the others that Senator Kelly is dead.

5-4Professor X falls

Professor X goes into Cerebro to search for Rogue and puts on the helmet. But Mystique's tampering turns the machine against him. He slumps out of his wheelchair and does not wake.

Jean examines Cerebro and removes what was planted in it. At Xavier's bedside, Scott thanks him for everything he has done, and promises to look after the school if anything happens.

5-5Jean uses Cerebro

Knowing the danger, Jean uses Cerebro herself. Scott sees and runs, but the door closes in his face.

The strain is too much and she collapses. When Scott reaches her, she tells him she knows where Magneto is going.

5-6Magneto and Rogue on the boat

That night Magneto's group heads by boat for Liberty Island and the Statue of Liberty. On Ellis Island next to it, the summit of world leaders is about to begin.

On the boat, Magneto tells Rogue he first saw the statue in 1949. She asks if he is going to kill her. "Yes," he says. He will turn the world's leaders into mutants like themselves, and her sacrifice will mean the survival of their kind. He orders Mystique to put her in the machine, which is mounted in the statue's torch.

5-7Heading out

Scott, Storm, Jean and Logan plan the attack. Magneto does not know his machine kills. If Rogue runs it at full power, not only the leaders at the summit but everyone in New York will die.

Logan puts on a black uniform too. The four take off from the school in the jet and, hidden in fog that Storm calls up, set down on the water by Liberty Island.

Part 6: Battle at the Statue of Liberty

6-1Into the statue

The four enter the Statue of Liberty. Above them, the machine sits on the torch. The metal detector at the entrance goes off at Logan, and he silences it with his claws.

As they move through the museum hall, Logan catches someone's scent. Then a second Logan appears and attacks him: Mystique in disguise. The two Logans slash at each other, and the other three cannot tell which is real. The fight carries Logan away from them.

6-2Fighting Toad

Toad leaps on the remaining three. He shuts Scott behind a door and throws Storm with his tongue. Jean catches him in mid-air with her telekinesis, but he spits slime over her face. It hardens at once over her mouth and nose, and she cannot breathe.

Scott blasts his way out, then uses a low-powered beam to break the slime off Jean's face.

Storm, who had been kicked down an elevator shaft, comes back up. She blows Toad out of the statue with a gale. He hangs on by his tongue, until she calls down a lightning bolt and he is flung away toward the water.

6-3Seeing through Mystique

Logan's fight with Mystique goes on. She shifts back to her own blue form, runs rings around him and vanishes.

A little later Storm comes up to Logan and says they need to regroup. He sniffs the air, then drives his claws into her stomach. Storm's shape melts back into Mystique, and she drops to the floor.

Logan rejoins Scott and Jean, and the real Storm. Asked to prove he is himself, he just tells Scott, "You're a dick." That is proof enough.

6-4Caught by Magneto

As the four climb higher in the statue, Logan suddenly cannot move. He is lifted and slammed against the wall, and flying metal binds the other three in place. Magneto floats down and turns Logan's fists toward his own chest so he cannot use his claws.

Scott is pinned facing Jean, and Sabretooth takes his visor, so he has to keep his eyes shut. Inside a copper statue, Storm cannot use her lightning either.

Jean and Storm tell Magneto that Senator Kelly is dead, and that the people at the summit will die too. He does not believe them. If he were really so righteous, Logan says, he would be the one in the machine. Magneto does not answer, and flies up to the torch.

6-5Giving Rogue his power

On the torch, as Rogue screams for help, Magneto lays his hand on her cheek and makes her absorb his magnetic power.

Below, Sabretooth closes in on Storm. Logan forces his claws out even though it wounds him, cuts his bonds and gets free. He leaps at Sabretooth, and the two end up fighting on top of the statue's head.

6-6Defeating Sabretooth

In the fight, Logan snatches back his stolen dog tags. But Sabretooth throws him, and he goes over the side of the statue.

Sabretooth comes back in to finish Storm. Then Logan, who caught hold of the statue, returns with Scott's visor. Jean floats it in front of Scott's eyes and gives the word. He opens them, and the beam blasts Sabretooth out through the wall. He crashes onto a boat below.

Logan cuts the other three free.

6-7Up to the torch

The machine in the torch comes to life, and a ring of light begins to spread. If Scott blasts the machine, Rogue will die with it.

So Storm raises a wind to lift Logan, and Jean steadies him with telekinesis, carrying him up to the torch. He jumps onto the machine and tries to cut Rogue's chains. But Magneto, who has regained some strength, holds his claws back with magnetism.

6-8Destroying the machine

The ring of light spreads over the water toward Ellis Island and the summit. Scott takes aim while Jean tells him to wait, then fires the instant he has a clear shot and blasts Magneto down.

Freed, Logan slashes the machine apart. The ring of light dies away before it reaches anyone.

6-9Saving Rogue

Logan cuts the chains and catches Rogue, but she is not moving. A streak of her hair has turned white.

He puts his hand to her cheek so that she will absorb his healing power. For a while nothing happens, and he holds her close. Then the wounds of the night's fighting begin to open across his face and body. Rogue gasps back to life, and Logan, drained and bleeding, collapses.

Part 7: Afterward

7-1Logan wakes

Logan wakes in the school's infirmary, his wounds fully healed. Jean, who has been watching over him, tells him Rogue is fine and that what he did was very brave.

He starts to say how he feels about her, reads her reaction, and asks after the professor instead. Xavier has recovered too.

7-2Kelly on the news

The X-Men watch Senator Kelly on the TV news, announcing that he is withdrawing his support for the Mutant Registration Act. For a moment his eyes flash yellow. It is Mystique, who survived, posing as him.

7-3Logan sets out

As promised, Professor X gives Logan a lead: near where he was found, at Alkali Lake in the Canadian Rockies, there is an abandoned military base.

Rogue, who has been playing with the other students, sees him leaving and runs after him. She doesn't want him to go. He promises he will be back, and leaves his dog tags with her. Then he rides away from the school on Scott's motorcycle.

7-4The plastic prison

Magneto is held in a prison made entirely of plastic, with no metal at all. Professor X visits, and the two play chess.

Magneto asks whether it doesn't worry him that they may pass that law one day. Xavier answers that he pities the poor soul who comes to his school looking for trouble.

As Xavier leaves, Magneto tells him this plastic prison won't hold him forever; the war is still coming, and he intends to fight it. Xavier replies that he will always be there to stop him.

Summary (spoilers through the end)

Magneto's plan and how it ended

1) He sends Sabretooth after Rogue, but the X-Men stop him (Part 2).

2) He tests a machine that turns humans into mutants on the kidnapped Kelly, and is badly weakened by running it (Part 3).

3) To have Rogue run the machine in his place, he has Mystique drive her out of the school and seizes her at the station (Part 4).

4) He sets the machine in the Statue of Liberty's torch to change the world leaders at the Ellis Island summit, and refuses to believe it kills humans (Parts 5–6).

5) Cyclops blasts him down and Logan destroys the machine (Part 6).

6) He is locked in a plastic prison (Part 7).

Rogue and Logan

1) Rogue's kiss puts her boyfriend in a coma, and she runs away (Part 1).

2) She meets Logan in Canada, and the X-Men rescue them (Part 2).

3) Logan stabs her during a nightmare, but she survives by absorbing his power. Mystique, posing as Bobby, tricks her into leaving, and Magneto seizes her at the station (Part 4).

4) Forced to absorb Magneto's power and run the machine, she nearly dies, but Logan saves her with his healing (Part 6).

5) Logan heads for Alkali Lake in search of his past, leaving Rogue his dog tags (Part 7).

Logan's dog tags

1) Sabretooth carries them off after the ambush in the snow (Part 2).

2) Logan takes them back in the fight on top of the statue (Part 6).

3) He gives them to Rogue when he leaves, as a promise to return (Part 7).

Who died

- Senator Kelly (Part 5): his body, forcibly mutated by Magneto's machine, can't hold together, and he dissolves into water in the school's infirmary in front of Storm.

- His aide Gyrich (revealed in Part 3): dead for some time, with Mystique taking his place.

My takeOpinion

One of the films that started the modern superhero wave. Professor X and Magneto being enemies and friends at once is a charm that never changes from here on.
