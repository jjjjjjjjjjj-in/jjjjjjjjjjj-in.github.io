Part 1: Two beginnings

1-1The camp gates

1944, German-occupied Poland. As he enters a concentration camp, a boy named Erik is separated from his parents.

When soldiers hold him back, the iron gates his parents were taken behind begin to bend toward him, as if pulled by a magnet. The boy can move metal with magnetism. A soldier knocks him unconscious.

1-2Marie's kiss

Years later, in the near future. In Meridian, Mississippi, 17-year-old Marie kisses her boyfriend David at her house.

He collapses and falls into a coma. Marie's touch drains the life force, and the powers, of anyone she touches.

Terrified, she runs away from home and starts calling herself Rogue.

1-3The Senate hearing

In Congress, a hearing is being held on the Mutant Registration Act, a bill that would force mutants to reveal their identities and powers.

Testifying, Dr. Jean Grey warns that the bill will only make things worse. But Senator Robert Kelly, its champion, stirs up the room by insisting that mutants are dangerous.

1-4Two old friends

In the audience are the telepath Professor Charles Xavier and the boy from the camp, Erik, who now calls himself Magneto.

The old friends talk outside about humans and mutants. Xavier still hopes the two can live side by side; Magneto believes humans will one day turn on mutants.

Xavier is worried about how Magneto will respond to the bill.

Part 2: A meeting in Canada

2-1The cage fight

Rogue reaches Laughlin City, Alberta, in Canada. In a bar, men are fighting inside a cage.

The man who keeps winning is Logan. Every wound he takes heals almost at once.

When a beaten opponent accuses him of cheating, three metal claws spring out from between Logan's knuckles. He leaves the bar.

2-2Rogue in the trailer

Rogue has stowed away in Logan's trailer. He finds her and puts her out, but changes his mind and lets her back in.

As they drive, the two talk a little about their powers. Logan has no memory of his past and has been drifting for years.

2-3Ambush in the snow

On the snowy road at night, the truck slams into a large tree blocking the way. The giant mutant Sabretooth attacks.

Logan fights back with his claws, but Sabretooth is stronger. Rogue is still trapped in the burning truck.

2-4Cyclops and Storm

Cyclops, who fires beams from his eyes, and Storm, who controls the weather, arrive and drive Sabretooth off.

They carry the unconscious Logan and Rogue to Professor X's mansion in Westchester, New York.

Part 3: The school for gifted youngsters

3-1Logan wakes up

Logan wakes in the infirmary beneath the mansion. Treating him is Jean, the doctor from the hearing.

He slips out and wanders the halls, until Professor X's voice speaks inside his head.

3-2Professor X explains

This is Xavier's School for Gifted Youngsters, a school for young mutants. Its teachers, Cyclops, Storm and Jean, are also the X-Men, a team that works toward peace between mutants and humans.

Sabretooth belongs to the Brotherhood, a band of mutants led by Magneto.

Believing Magneto is after Logan, Xavier asks him to stay until they find out why. Logan agrees, on the condition that Xavier helps him uncover his past.

3-3Rogue at school

Rogue enrolls in the school. She meets Bobby Drake, a boy who can make ice, and develops a crush on him.

Logan, meanwhile, finds himself drawn to Jean, who is Cyclops's girlfriend.

3-4Kelly abducted

Senator Kelly is flying by helicopter with his aide, but the aide is really Mystique, a mutant who can take anyone's form.

With her partner Toad, who has a long tongue, she abducts the senator and takes him to the Brotherhood's island hideout.

3-5The mutation machine

Magneto has built a machine powered by his own magnetism. Its radiation turns ordinary humans into mutants.

He tests it on Kelly, who is transformed. But running it leaves Magneto badly weakened.

3-6Escape from the cell

Locked in a cell, Kelly discovers that his body has become soft and stretchy.

He squeezes himself through the bars, falls from the island's cliff into the sea, and washes up on a beach.

Part 4: Rogue runs away

4-1The nightmare

One night Logan is caught in a nightmare. When Rogue tries to wake him, he lashes out by reflex, and his claws stab her in the chest.

She grabs him and absorbs his healing power, and her wound closes, while Logan collapses. The other students see it all.

4-2Mystique as Bobby

Bobby comes to Rogue and tells her that Professor X is angry with her and that she should leave the school.

But this Bobby is Mystique, who has slipped into the school. Rogue believes her and leaves on her own.

4-3Cerebro and Mystique's sabotage

Professor X uses Cerebro, his device for finding mutants anywhere, and locates Rogue at a train station. The X-Men set out to bring her back.

While they are gone, Mystique slips into Cerebro and sabotages it.

4-4On the train

Setting out ahead of Cyclops and Storm, Logan finds Rogue on a train about to leave.

He talks her into coming back to the school, and she agrees.

4-5Magneto attacks

Then Magneto arrives. Discovering that Logan's bones are coated with metal, he pins him helplessly with magnetism.

It turns out Magneto was after Rogue, not Logan. Toad and Sabretooth join in, and outside the station a fight breaks out with Cyclops and Storm.

4-6Rogue taken

Professor X takes control of Sabretooth's mind to stop Magneto.

But Magneto lifts the guns of the police surrounding the station into the air and turns them on the officers themselves. To save them, Xavier has to let Sabretooth go.

The Brotherhood escapes with Rogue.

Part 5: Kelly's end and getting ready

5-1Kelly at the school

Kelly makes it to the school. Professor X reads his mind and learns about Magneto's machine.

Running it nearly killed Magneto. The X-Men realize he means to have Rogue absorb his power and run the machine in his place, at the cost of her life.

5-2The senator's end

A body changed by force cannot hold together for long. Kelly's body breaks down into water, and he is gone.

5-3Professor X falls

Professor X uses Cerebro to search for Rogue. But Mystique's sabotage makes the machine go haywire, and he falls into a coma.

5-4Jean uses Cerebro

Jean repairs Cerebro and, despite the danger, uses it herself.

Magneto has set his machine on the Statue of Liberty on Liberty Island. He plans to turn the world leaders gathering for a summit on nearby Ellis Island into mutants.

5-5Heading out

Logan joins Cyclops, Storm and Jean. The four put on black uniforms and fly to the Statue of Liberty in their jet.

Part 6: Battle at the Statue of Liberty

6-1Into the statue

The four enter the Statue of Liberty at night. Logan follows the enemy's scent.

6-2Fighting Mystique

Mystique takes Logan's form and attacks Cyclops. When the real Logan appears, it's hard to tell who is who, but Logan knows her by scent.

She then approaches disguised as Storm, but Logan sees through it and stabs her with his claws. Mystique goes down.

6-3Fighting Toad

Toad traps Cyclops behind a thick steel door and spits slime over Jean's face, smothering her. Cyclops breaks out and saves her.

Storm summons wind and lightning and strikes Toad down.

6-4Caught by Magneto

At the statue's head, Magneto and Sabretooth are waiting. Magneto bends the statue's metal around the four and pins them in place.

He forces Rogue to absorb his power, and part of her hair turns white. He binds her into the machine in the torch and makes her start it. A ring of light begins to spread.

6-5Defeating Sabretooth

Logan breaks free and fights Sabretooth, but is thrown off the statue.

Just as Sabretooth moves to finish off the other three, Logan returns. With Jean steadying his aim, Cyclops blasts Sabretooth out of the statue.

6-6Up to the torch

Time is running out. Storm whips up a wind and Jean steadies him with telekinesis, lifting Logan to the machine in the torch.

He tries to stop it, but Magneto, who has regained some strength, holds his claws back with magnetism.

6-7Destroying the machine

While Logan keeps Magneto distracted, Cyclops lines up a clean shot and blasts him down.

Freed, Logan slashes the machine apart. The ring of light stops, and the leaders at the summit are safe.

6-8Saving Rogue

Logan frees Rogue from the machine, but she is dying. He places his hand on her face and lets her absorb his healing power.

Rogue comes back to life. Drained, Logan collapses unconscious.

Part 7: Afterward

7-1Two awakenings

Professor X and Logan both come out of their comas.

7-2Kelly on the news

The X-Men see Senator Kelly on the news. It is Mystique, who survived, posing as him. The false Kelly withdraws the Mutant Registration Act.

7-3Logan sets out

As promised, Professor X gives Logan a lead: near where he was found in Canada, at Alkali Lake, there is an abandoned military base.

Logan gives Rogue his dog tags, promises they'll meet again, and leaves the school.

7-4The plastic prison

Magneto is held in a prison made entirely of plastic, with no metal at all. Professor X visits, and the two play chess.

Magneto warns that one day he'll get out and continue the fight. Xavier answers that he and the X-Men will always be there to stop him.

Summary (spoilers through the end)

Magneto's plan and how it ended

1) He builds a machine that turns humans into mutants and tests it on the kidnapped Kelly, nearly dying himself (Part 3).

2) He kidnaps Rogue at the station so she can run the machine in his place (Part 4).

3) He sets the machine on the Statue of Liberty to change the world leaders at the Ellis Island summit (Parts 5–6).

4) Cyclops blasts him down and Logan destroys the machine (Part 6).

5) He is locked in a plastic prison (Part 7).

Rogue and Logan

1) Rogue's kiss puts her boyfriend in a coma, and she runs away (Part 1).

2) She meets Logan in Canada, and the X-Men rescue them (Part 2).

3) Logan stabs her during a nightmare, but she survives by absorbing his power. Mystique, posing as Bobby, tricks her into leaving (Part 4).

4) Forced to absorb Magneto's power and run the machine, she nearly dies, but Logan saves her with his healing (Part 6).

5) Logan heads for Alkali Lake in search of his past, leaving Rogue his dog tags (Part 7).

Who died

- Senator Kelly (Part 5): his body, forcibly mutated by Magneto's machine, can't hold together, and he dissolves into water at the school.
