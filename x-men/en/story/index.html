Part 1: Two beginnings

The boy in the camp

1944, a Nazi camp in Poland. Torn from his parents, young Erik screams, and the iron gate bends on its own. He can move metal.

Rogue's kiss

The near future. When Marie kisses her boyfriend, he collapses into a coma. Her touch drains life and power. She runs away and calls herself Rogue.

The Senate debate

In Congress, Senator Kelly pushes a law to register mutants. Jean Grey argues against it, while Professor X and Erik, now called Magneto, watch.

Part 2: Logan and Rogue

A bar in Canada

In a Canadian bar, Rogue meets Logan, a cage fighter with metal claws who heals instantly and can't remember his past.

Ambush in the snow

On a snowy road, the giant Sabretooth attacks them. Cyclops and Storm arrive and rescue them.

The school

They wake at Professor X's school for young mutants, where he teaches toward peace between mutants and humans.

Logan and Jean

Logan is drawn to Jean, who is Cyclops's girlfriend.

Part 3: Magneto's plan

The senator abducted

Magneto's followers kidnap Senator Kelly, and Magneto uses a machine to turn him into a mutant.

The senator's end

Kelly escapes to the school, but his body dissolves into water. The machine's effect is unstable.

Rogue runs again

Mystique, who can take any form, poses as a student and tricks Rogue into leaving. Logan finds her at the station, but Magneto takes her.

Why Rogue

Running the machine nearly kills Magneto. He plans to make Rogue absorb his power and run it in his place.

Part 4: Battle at the Statue of Liberty

Cerebro

Sabotage by Mystique knocks Professor X into a coma. Jean uses Cerebro and finds Magneto: world leaders are gathering for a summit near the Statue of Liberty.

Fighting on the statue

The X-Men battle Magneto's followers inside the statue. Logan defeats Mystique, and Cyclops blasts Sabretooth away.

Saving Rogue

Logan destroys the machine and frees Rogue. He presses his face to hers, letting her draw on his healing power.

Part 5: Afterward

The plastic prison

Magneto is locked in a cell with no metal. Professor X visits, and the two old friends talk over a game of chess.

Logan's journey

Logan heads north to a lake in search of clues about his past.
