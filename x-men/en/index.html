The first X-Men film

The start of 20th Century Fox's X-Men series, set in a different world from Marvel Studios' films.

No homework needed

You can start right here.

Two friends, two philosophies

Professor X wants peace with humans; Magneto wants to fight them. Their conflict drives the whole series.

No post-credits scene

You can leave when the credits start.

StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

Part 1: Two beginnings

In a Polish concentration camp in 1944, a boy named Erik, torn from his parents, bends an iron gate with magnetism.

In the near future, Marie's kiss puts her boyfriend in a coma, and she runs away, calling herself Rogue.

In Congress, a hearing on the Mutant Registration Act is watched by Professor X and Erik, now called Magneto.

Part 2: A meeting in Canada

In a Canadian bar, Rogue meets Logan, a man with metal claws who heals almost instantly.

He has no memory of his past. On a snowy road Sabretooth attacks them, but Cyclops and Storm come to the rescue.

Part 3: The school for gifted youngsters

They have been brought to Professor X's school for young mutants. Logan agrees to stay if Xavier helps him find his past.

Meanwhile, Magneto's followers kidnap Senator Kelly, and Magneto tests a machine that turns humans into mutants on him.

Running it leaves Magneto badly weakened, and the transformed senator escapes his cell.

Part 4: Rogue runs away

Caught in a nightmare, Logan stabs Rogue when she tries to wake him. She survives by absorbing his healing power.

Tricked by Mystique posing as Bobby, Rogue leaves the school. Logan finds her on a train, but Magneto arrives and takes her.

It was Rogue, not Logan, that Magneto wanted.

Part 5: Kelly's end and getting ready

From Kelly's mind, the X-Men learn Magneto's plan: to make Rogue absorb his power and run the machine at the cost of her life.

Kelly's body dissolves into water. Mystique's sabotage puts Professor X in a coma, and Jean uses Cerebro to find the target: the Statue of Liberty.

Part 6: Battle at the Statue of Liberty

Inside the statue the X-Men defeat Mystique and Toad, but Magneto captures them. He forces Rogue to absorb his power and run the machine, aimed at the world leaders at the summit.

Cyclops blasts Sabretooth and then Magneto, and Logan destroys the machine.

Logan saves the dying Rogue by giving her his healing power, collapsing himself.

Part 7: Afterward

Mystique, who survived, poses as Senator Kelly and withdraws the registration bill.

Professor X gives Logan a lead to his past, an abandoned military base at Alkali Lake, and Logan leaves Rogue his dog tags and sets out.

Over chess in his plastic prison, Magneto vows to keep fighting, and Xavier answers that he will always stop him.

Key terms

Mutants
People born with special powers.
Xavier's School for Gifted Youngsters
Professor X's school for young mutants, and the X-Men's base.
The Brotherhood
Magneto's group of mutants who fight humans.
Cerebro
A device that lets Professor X locate mutants anywhere in the world.
The Mutant Registration Act
A proposed law requiring mutants to register their identities and powers.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why does Magneto fight humans?

Having suffered terribly at human hands as a child, he believes humans will always persecute mutants.

What's the relationship between Professor X and Magneto?

Old friends who, despite opposing views, still care for each other.

Why does Magneto want Rogue?

Powering the machine nearly kills him, so he plans to make Rogue absorb his power and run it instead.

Easy-to-miss details

Black leather suits

The X-Men wear black leather uniforms.

A game of chess

The film ends with Professor X and Magneto playing chess.

Stan Lee's cameo

Marvel Comics legend Stan Lee appears as a hot dog vendor on the beach.
