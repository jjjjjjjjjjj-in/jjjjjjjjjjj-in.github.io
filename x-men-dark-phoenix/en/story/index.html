Part 1: The accident, 1975

Jean in the car

1975. Eight-year-old Jean is riding with her parents. During a squabble over the radio, her power lashes out and the car crashes. Her mother dies; Jean survives.

Meeting Charles

Charles visits Jean at the hospital and takes her to his school, telling her she isn't broken.

Part 2: Rescue in space

Heroes now

1992. The X-Men are celebrated worldwide, and Charles even has a hotline to the President.

Endeavour

The space shuttle Endeavour is caught by a strange glowing cloud. The X-Men fly into space to rescue the crew.

The cloud

Kurt and Peter get the crew out, but the commander is left behind. As Jean holds the shuttle together, the glowing cloud pours into her.

Coming home

Jean returns alive and seems fine, but her power is far stronger than before.

Part 3: Jean changes

The party

The students celebrate with a party in the woods. Jean is enjoying herself until her power suddenly explodes outward, knocking everyone back.

The wall in her mind

The walls Charles built in Jean's mind begin to crumble, revealing that her father, whom she believed dead, is alive.

A mysterious woman

Meanwhile, the shape-shifting D'Bari arrive on Earth in human form. Their leader, Vuk, is after the force inside Jean.

Part 4: Her father's house

Facing her father

Jean visits her father, who admits he was afraid of her and gave her up. She's devastated.

Tragedy at the house

The X-Men come to bring her home. Jean's power spirals out of control, throwing police and friends aside, and Raven is killed.

Hank's anger

Shattered by Raven's death, Hank blames Charles for hiding Jean's past and leaves the school.

Part 5: Genosha and New York

Erik's island

Jean goes to Genosha, the island Erik built for mutants, but he sends her away, unwilling to risk his people.

Vuk's offer

With nowhere to go, Jean is approached by Vuk, who explains the force and tries to win her over.

Erik and Hank

Hank tells Erik that Raven is dead. The two head to New York to stop Jean.

Fight in New York

Charles, Scott and the others stand against Erik and Hank. Charles makes his way to Jean and apologizes for what he hid.

Part 6: Battle on the train

Taken prisoner

The mutants are arrested and loaded onto a prison train, which the D'Bari attack.

Old enemies, together

On the train, the X-Men and Erik's group fight the D'Bari side by side.

Jean's choice

Jean embraces her power and joins the battle. She carries Vuk into space and destroys her, and then vanishes into a blaze of light shaped like a firebird.

Part 7: Afterward

A new name

The school is renamed the Jean Grey School. Charles steps away, and Hank takes over.

Chess in Paris

In a Paris café, Charles and Erik play chess. In the sky above, a firebird of light appears.
