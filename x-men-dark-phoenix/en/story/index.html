Part 1: The crash, 1975

1-1Jean in the car

1975. Eight-year-old Jean Grey sits in the back seat of her parents' car while Glen Campbell's "By the Time I Get to Phoenix" plays on the radio.

Jean wants a different song, but her parents won't change it. So Jean flips the stations herself, without touching the dial, using only her mind.

1-2The crash

But her power starts to run wild, and the car leaves the road in a terrible crash.

Her mother, Elaine, is killed. Jean survives.

1-3Meeting Charles

Charles Xavier comes in his wheelchair to see Jean. He takes her into his School for Gifted Youngsters, a home for mutant children, and promises to teach her to control her power.

Jean grows up at the school believing that both her parents died in the crash.

Part 2: Rescue in space

2-1Heroes now

1992. The X-Men are accepted by the public as heroes, and Charles is now someone the President of the United States calls on directly.

2-2A call from the President

The space shuttle Endeavour is damaged in orbit by strange energy like a solar flare, and its crew is stranded. The President asks Charles for help.

With Hank McCoy flying the X-Men's jet, Raven, Jean, Kurt, Peter and the rest of the team head into space.

2-3Getting the crew out

Kurt, who can teleport, takes the super-fast Peter into the shuttle, and Peter carries the crew to the jet one by one.

But one of the astronauts says their commander is still inside.

2-4Back to the shuttle

The energy is closing in. Raven objects, but Charles talks Jean and Kurt into going back.

While Jean uses her mind to hold off the oncoming energy, Kurt finds the commander and gets him out.

2-5Jean absorbs the light

Left alone on the shuttle, Jean is hit by the energy. The shuttle breaks apart, but instead of burning her up, the energy pours into her body.

Kurt teleports out to Jean as she floats in space and brings her back to the jet. She is alive.

Part 3: Jean changes

3-1Coming home heroes

Everyone returns safely, and the X-Men are welcomed as heroes.

Hank examines Jean. Her readings are off the charts, and he can't tell whether anything is wrong.

3-2Raven's anger

Raven confronts Charles, accusing him of putting the team's lives at risk so he can win praise and time with the President. Charles won't listen.

That night, Raven and Hank talk about leaving the X-Men and the school.

3-3Party in the woods

The students throw a party in the woods outside the school to celebrate the rescue. Jean joins in with Scott and has a good time.

Meanwhile, ships from space come down near a lakeside house where a dinner party is going on.

3-4Vuk arrives

They are the D'Bari, shape-shifting aliens. Their leader, Vuk, kills Margaret, the woman of the house, and takes her form. Margaret's husband is killed when his heart is stopped, and the others kill the dinner guests.

The D'Bari have come to Earth following the force Jean absorbed in space.

3-5Power out of control

That night Jean breaks down, loses control, and turns her power on the students who were celebrating.

3-6Inside Cerebro

Charles, Hank and Raven use Cerebro, the machine that reaches into minds, to try to reach Jean and look into her mind.

Hank and Raven learn that long ago Charles built walls in Jean's mind and locked away her memory of the crash. He did it to shield her from the pain, but the force inside her is now breaking those walls down.

3-7Her father is alive

Jean says she has heard the mind of the father she was told was dead. He is alive, and Charles hid it from her.

Jean turns on Charles for lying to her and forces him out of her mind, knocking him unconscious.

3-8Scott is put to sleep

Her boyfriend Scott tries to stop her from leaving. Jean says she won't put his life at risk, puts him to sleep with her power, and leaves alone.

Part 4: Tragedy at Red Hook

4-1Facing her father

Jean goes to her childhood home in Red Hook, New York. Her father, John Grey, lives there alone.

Looking into his mind, Jean finds that he blamed her for his wife's death and gave her up to Charles himself. She also remembers that it was her own power that caused the crash.

4-2The police

Her father calls the police. When the officers gathered outside point their guns at her, Jean loses control again and wrecks their patrol cars with her power.

4-3The X-Men arrive

Charles and the X-Men catch up. Peter rushes at Jean, but her power throws him back, leaving him badly hurt and unconscious.

When Hank and the remaining officers aim their guns at Jean, Charles freezes everyone with his mind so Raven can talk to her.

4-4Raven dies

Raven walks up to Jean and tries to calm her down. But when the distraught Jean pushes her away with her power, Raven is thrown into something sharp and impaled.

Raven tells Hank she loves him and dies. Horrified at what she has done, Jean flies away.

4-5Raven's funeral

A funeral for Raven is held at the school, and Charles shares memories of their childhood.

Hank blames Charles for Raven's death, saying his meddling with Jean's mind and his lies caused it. Charles insists the walls were for Jean's good.

4-6Hank leaves

Hank takes the X-Men's jet and leaves the school.

Part 5: Genosha

5-1Erik's island

Jean reaches Genosha, the island Erik Lehnsherr made into a home for mutants. Erik has left fighting behind and lives quietly there with his people.

5-2How do you stop?

Jean asks Erik how he managed to stop hurting people. He tells her he killed many people for revenge, and it never made him feel any better.

Erik notices blood on Jean's shirt and asks whose it is, but she won't say.

5-3Military helicopters

U.S. military helicopters arrive and demand that Jean be handed over. Jean fights back with her power, and Erik has to use his own power to stop her.

5-4Banished

After Jean drives the troops away, Erik banishes her from the island to protect his people.

5-5Her father's death

Meanwhile, Vuk visits Jean's father, John, to get information about Jean. She crushes his chest with her power and kills him.

5-6Vuk's offer

With nowhere to go, Jean is found by Vuk. Vuk explains that the cosmic force Jean absorbed wiped out the D'Bari's world, consuming everything in its path until it was drawn to Jean.

Vuk offers to help Jean learn to use the power safely.

Part 6: Battle in New York

6-1Hank and Erik

Hank flies to Genosha, tells Erik that Jean killed Raven, and asks for his help in stopping her.

Erik takes out a copy of the helmet Apocalypse once made for him and gets ready to face Jean.

6-2Finding Jean

Selene, a telepath, tracks Jean to New York City. Erik and Hank head there to kill her, bringing Selene, Ariki, who controls his hair, and others.

6-3The X-Men move

Learning of Erik's plan, Charles decides to save Jean. Kurt teleports Charles, Scott and Storm to New York.

6-4Outside the building

In front of the building where Jean is, Charles warns Erik that if people see mutants fighting each other in the street, they will see mutants as dangerous, and everything they have built could be lost.

Erik's group storms the building anyway, and the X-Men and Erik's faction fight in the street.

6-5Erik and Jean

Inside, Erik finds Jean with Vuk, a woman he doesn't know. He tries to kill Jean, but she breaks his helmet and throws him out of the building.

6-6Charles on the stairs

Charles goes inside to reach Jean. Her power forces him out of his wheelchair and makes him climb the stairs on his own legs.

He can no longer read her mind, but he apologizes for tampering with it and lets her see his memories, including the day her father gave her up and how much Charles wanted to protect her.

6-7Herself again

After seeing the memories, Jean's old self comes back, and she stops hurting Charles.

Full of regret, Jean tries to let Vuk take the force from her. But when Vuk reveals that the D'Bari mean to use it to take over Earth, Scott stops her.

6-8Everyone arrested

A government unit for containing mutants arrives. Both the X-Men and Erik's group are captured and fitted with collars that block their powers. Vuk escapes.

Part 7: Battle on the train

7-1The prison train

The captured mutants are taken away on a train under guard, and Jean is on board too.

On the train, Charles and Scott tell Hank, Erik and the others that Jean can still be saved.

7-2The D'Bari attack

Vuk and her D'Bari attack the train and overpower the soldiers one after another. One soldier unlocks the mutants' collars, and their powers return.

The X-Men and Erik's group join forces to fight off the D'Bari before Vuk arrives.

7-3Fighting through the cars

When a soldier whose son is a big fan of Kurt is killed by the D'Bari, Kurt flies into a rage. He takes down every D'Bari he meets, and teleports the one who killed the soldier onto the tracks to be run over.

Erik kills D'Bari with handcuffs and pieces of metal, and Storm fights with lightning. Erik's allies Selene and Ariki are killed in the fight.

7-4A talk inside the mind

Inside his own mind, Charles talks with Jean, and she forgives him for what he did to her mind.

7-5Jean rises

As the train is wrecked, Jean joins the fight and saves the mutants from the crash. Then she turns the remaining D'Bari to dust with her power.

7-6Into space

Vuk tries again to drain the force from Jean. Jean carries her into space and unleashes all of her power, destroying Vuk.

Jean then becomes a being of light shaped like a phoenix, a firebird, and is gone.

Part 8: Afterward

8-1The Jean Grey School

The school is renamed the Jean Grey School for Gifted Youngsters. Charles steps down as head, and Hank takes his place.

8-2Chess in Paris

Charles is sitting at a café on the Rue de la Paix in Paris when Erik surprises him and invites him to a game of chess. The two make peace.

High in the sky above, a flaming phoenix appears.

Summary

Who died

- Elaine Grey (Jean's mother): killed in 1975 in the car crash caused by Jean's power.

- Margaret, her husband and the dinner guests: killed by the D'Bari when they arrive. Vuk takes Margaret's form.

- Raven: at Red Hook, she tries to talk Jean down, but the distraught Jean pushes her away with her power and she is impaled.

- John Grey (Jean's father): Vuk crushes his chest and kills him while trying to learn where Jean is.

- Soldiers on the train: killed by the D'Bari who attack it.

- Selene and Ariki (Erik's allies): die fighting the D'Bari on the train.

- The D'Bari: cut down by the X-Men and Erik's group on the train; Jean turns the rest to dust.

- Vuk: tries to drain Jean's power; Jean carries her into space and destroys her.

Where everyone ends up

- Jean: after unleashing her power in space, she becomes a phoenix-shaped light and is gone. At the end, a phoenix appears in the sky over Paris.

- Charles: apologizes for tampering with Jean's mind and is forgiven. He steps down from the school and lives in Paris.

- Erik: sets out to kill Jean, but fights beside the X-Men on the train. He makes peace with Charles over chess in Paris.

- Hank: breaks with Charles over Raven's death, but in the end becomes the school's new head.

- Peter: badly hurt when Jean throws him aside at Red Hook.

- The school: renamed the Jean Grey School for Gifted Youngsters.
