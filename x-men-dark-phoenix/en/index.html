Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

About ten years after Apocalypse

It's set in 1992, when the X-Men are celebrated heroes.

Jean's story

Jean, who showed great power in the last film, takes center stage.

A new take

It retells the Jean and Phoenix story from The Last Stand with the younger cast.

No post-credits scene

There's nothing after the credits.

Key terms

The Phoenix Force
An immensely powerful cosmic force that hits Jean as a burning, solar-flare-like cloud.
The D'Bari
Shape-shifting aliens who lost their homeworld and come to Earth seeking the Phoenix force.
Genosha
The island Erik founded as a home for mutants.
Endeavour
The space shuttle that suffers an accident in orbit, which the X-Men set out to rescue.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

What did Charles hide?

Jean was told both parents died after a childhood accident. In fact her father lived, and Charles walled off those memories to protect her.

Why do the D'Bari want Jean?

The Phoenix force destroyed their world. They want its power to make Earth their new home.

How is it different from The Last Stand?

Both follow Jean being consumed by overwhelming power, but here the force comes from space and aliens are involved. The cast and ending differ too.

Easy-to-miss details

The school's new name

By the end, the school has a new name.

Chess in Paris

In the final scene, Charles and Erik play chess at a Paris café, echoing the original films.

A hotline to the President

The X-Men are so trusted they have a direct line to the President.

The ending and post-creditsSpoilers

After the end credits

No post-credits scene. At the end, Professor X and Magneto play chess in Paris as the Phoenix appears in the sky.
