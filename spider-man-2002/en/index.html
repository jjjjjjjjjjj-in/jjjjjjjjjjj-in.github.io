Not the MCU

First of a trilogy

Key terms

Spider-sense
Peter's uncanny sense of incoming danger.
Webs
In this world, Peter shoots webs from his own wrists, without any gadget.
Oscorp
Norman Osborn's company, which develops military technology.
The Daily Bugle
The New York newspaper where Peter sells photos of Spider-Man.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Who is the Green Goblin?

Norman Osborn, Harry's father. He tested a strength-enhancing formula on himself, and it created a terrifying second personality.

Why does Uncle Ben die?

The thief Peter deliberately let go later carjacks Ben and shoots him. It's what drives Peter to become a hero.

Why does Peter turn MJ away at the end?

He believes being close to him would keep putting her in danger.

Easy-to-miss details

The upside-down kiss

Spider-Man hanging upside down in the rain as MJ pulls up his mask for a kiss is one of the most famous movie scenes ever.

New Yorkers step in

During the bridge fight, ordinary New Yorkers pelt the Goblin to help Spider-Man.

Who named him?

The ring announcer at the wrestling match is the one who calls him Spider-Man.
