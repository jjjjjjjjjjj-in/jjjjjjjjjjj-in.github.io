Part 1: The bite

1-1Chasing the school bus

Queens, New York. High school senior Peter Parker is brilliant but shy, and bullied at school. Orphaned young, he was raised by his Uncle Ben and Aunt May.

One morning Peter chases the school bus as it pulls away. The driver won't stop until his next-door neighbor Mary Jane Watson (MJ) asks him to. Peter has had a crush on MJ since childhood, but she is the girlfriend of popular athlete Flash.

1-2The lab trip

The class visits a genetics lab at Columbia University. Peter's best friend, Harry Osborn, is dropped off by his father, Norman. Harry has flunked out of several private schools and ended up at Peter's school.

Harry introduces Peter to his father. Norman, the head of the big company Oscorp and a scientist himself, is impressed by Peter's intelligence and takes an instant liking to him.

1-3The bite

The lab houses genetically engineered spiders. Peter takes photos of MJ for the school paper, and one of the spiders bites him on the hand.

When he gets home, the bite is badly swollen. He feels sick and collapses into bed.

1-4Norman's experiment

Meanwhile at Oscorp, a performance-enhancing formula is behind schedule. General Slocum warns that unless it shows results fast, the vital military contract will go to rival company Quest Aerospace.

To save his company, Norman overrules his scientist Dr. Stromm and tests the formula on himself. It gives him superhuman strength, but it drives him insane, and he kills Dr. Stromm.

Part 2: New powers

2-1A new body

The next morning, Peter wakes up to find he can see perfectly without his glasses. His skinny frame has turned muscular.

2-2In the cafeteria

In the cafeteria, Peter catches MJ as she slips, moving impossibly fast.

But he can't control his new powers yet; webbing even shoots from his wrist without warning. Flash gets caught up in the mess and, furious, picks a fight with Peter.

2-3The fight with Flash

Flash swings at him. A strange sense warns Peter of danger before it comes, and he dodges punch after punch, then sends Flash flying.

Peter runs out of the school.

2-4Testing his powers

Peter realizes he has spider-like powers. He climbs walls with his fingertips and leaps huge distances from rooftop to rooftop.

He experiments with the webbing from his wrists and, after plenty of failures, learns to swing between buildings.

2-5MJ's dream

Peter talks with MJ. When he apologizes for the fight, she confides that she wants to get away from her abusive, drinking father and become an actress.

Peter thinks that if he had a car, MJ might notice him.

Part 3: Uncle Ben

3-1The talk in the car

Peter goes out, claiming he's going to the library. Driving him, Uncle Ben, worried about how Peter has been acting, tells him that with great power comes great responsibility.

Peter snaps at him.

3-2The wrestling match

His real destination is a wrestling arena: anyone who lasts three minutes in the ring with star wrestler Bonesaw McGraw wins $3,000. Peter enters in a homemade mask and costume.

In a caged ring, Bonesaw goes after him hard, but Peter uses his powers to beat him. The ring announcer introduces Peter under the name "Spider-Man."

3-3Letting the thief go

But the promoter pays him only $100. Peter protests and is brushed off.

Moments later a man robs the promoter and runs past. Peter could stop him, but deliberately steps aside and lets him escape.

3-4Uncle Ben's death

Outside, Peter finds that Uncle Ben has been shot by a carjacker.

Ben dies in front of Peter. The killer has fled in Ben's car.

3-5Chasing the killer

Peter pulls on his costume and pursues the carjacker across the rooftops. The man hides in an old warehouse.

Peter corners him and disarms him, and then sees his face: it's the thief he let go at the arena.

Trying to flee, the man falls out of a window to his death. On a rooftop, Peter grieves, knowing Ben died because of him.

3-6Attack on Quest's test

Meanwhile the deranged Norman puts on Oscorp's green armor and, riding a flying glider, attacks a product test held by rival company Quest Aerospace.

He wrecks the test with bombs, killing General Slocum and the scientists.

Part 4: Spider-Man is born

4-1Graduation

At graduation, Norman congratulates Harry, then turns to Peter. He tells him to try to enjoy the day despite this hard time, that he's like a brother to Harry, and that he should call if he ever needs anything.

Harry notices that MJ has broken up with Flash.

4-2With Aunt May

At home, Peter cries over missing Uncle Ben. Aunt May comes in, and they remember him together.

Holding on to Ben's words, Peter decides to use his powers to help people.

4-3The apartment with Harry

Peter moves into a big apartment with Harry, bought by Norman.

MJ works as a waitress while chasing acting jobs. Peter learns from MJ herself that she's dating Harry, which Harry never told him. Peter is crushed but hides it.

4-4The city's hero

Peter makes a red-and-blue suit and starts stopping crimes and helping people as Spider-Man. He becomes the talk of the city.

Daily Bugle editor J. Jonah Jameson paints Spider-Man as a menace. But when he sees that Spider-Man stories sell papers, he puts out an ad offering to buy good photos of him.

4-5Selling the photos

Peter photographs himself as Spider-Man and takes the pictures to the Bugle. No one else has clear shots like these, so Jameson takes him on as a freelance photographer.

4-6Forced out

At an Oscorp board meeting, the directors tell Norman they've decided to sell the company to Quest, and that he'll be stepping down once the deal goes through.

Norman is enraged at having the company he founded taken from him.

Part 5: The Green Goblin

5-1Attack at the festival

A huge festival celebrating world unity is held in Times Square. Oscorp's board watches from a balcony, along with Harry and MJ. Peter is there taking photos for the Bugle.

Norman appears in a green mask on his glider and hurls pumpkin-shaped bombs, killing every board member. The festival descends into chaos.

5-2Saving MJ

Spider-Man arrives and fights Norman. On the collapsing balcony, Harry tries to save MJ but is knocked out by falling debris.

MJ falls, and Spider-Man catches her just in time and carries her to safety. Norman escapes.

5-3The name "Green Goblin"

Jameson dubs the mysterious masked killer the "Green Goblin."

At home, Norman talks with his other self, the Goblin, in a mirror. Norman doesn't remember the killings. The Goblin says only Spider-Man can stop them, so why not make him an ally?

5-4The Bugle attack and the rooftop offer

The Goblin storms the Bugle and demands Jameson tell him who takes the Spider-Man photos. Peter, who is there, swings in as Spider-Man.

The Goblin knocks him out with gas and carries him to a rooftop. With Spider-Man unable to move, the Goblin proposes they team up, and warns him that the city will eventually turn on him.

5-5The kiss in the rainy alley

One night Peter runs into MJ, they talk, and they part. Walking alone in the rain, MJ is cornered in an alley by four thugs.

Spider-Man shows up and beats them. As he hangs upside down, MJ peels his mask up just past his mouth and kisses him. He leaves without revealing who he is.

5-6The burning building

On Thanksgiving, Spider-Man rescues a baby from a burning apartment building. Told a woman is still trapped inside, he goes back, but the "woman" is the Goblin imitating her voice.

The Goblin wants his answer, and Spider-Man refuses. In the brutal fight that follows, Spider-Man's arm is cut and bleeding, but he gets away.

Part 6: Unmasked

6-1Thanksgiving dinner

Thanksgiving dinner is held at Peter and Harry's apartment, with Aunt May, MJ, Harry and Norman. Peter joins them.

Norman notices the cut on Peter's arm, in the same place where he just wounded Spider-Man. He realizes Peter is Spider-Man.

6-2Norman leaves

Norman abruptly says he has to go. In the hallway, when Harry begs him to stay, Norman insults MJ and tells Harry to dump her once he's had his fun.

MJ overhears and calls Norman a creep; Harry defends his father and snaps at her. MJ walks out.

6-3Aunt May attacked

That night the Goblin attacks Aunt May at home as she says her bedtime prayers, and she ends up in the hospital.

Peter still doesn't know who the Goblin is, but he realizes the Goblin is going after the people he loves.

6-4At the hospital

At the hospital, MJ confesses to Peter that she's drawn to Spider-Man, who has saved her twice, and asks whether he ever said anything about her.

Talking as if about Spider-Man, Peter expresses his own feelings for her. They hold hands.

6-5What Harry saw

Harry arrives with flowers and sees Peter and MJ holding hands. Peter drops her hand, but Harry has seen.

Hurt, Harry goes to his father, says he was right about everything, and mentions that Peter loves MJ. Without meaning to, he tells Norman who matters most to Peter.

Part 7: Choice on the bridge

7-1MJ and the children

The Goblin kidnaps MJ. On the Queensboro Bridge, he also seizes a Roosevelt Island tram car full of children.

When Spider-Man arrives, the Goblin tells him he can save only one, MJ or the children, and makes him choose. Then he drops them both.

7-2Saving both

Spider-Man dives after them and, using his webs, catches both MJ and the tram car.

The Goblin attacks while he's trapped, but people on the bridge pelt the Goblin with whatever they can find.

Spider-Man lowers MJ and the tram car safely onto a nearby barge. Everyone is saved.

7-3The fight in the ruin

Enraged, the Goblin grabs Spider-Man and hurls him into an abandoned building nearby, where he beats him savagely.

But when the Goblin boasts that he'll kill MJ slowly afterward, Spider-Man finds the strength to fight back and pounds him into defeat.

7-4The face under the mask

The Goblin pulls off his mask. It's Norman. He desperately begs Peter's forgiveness.

7-5The glider

But as he talks, Norman is secretly sending his glider at Peter's back.

Warned by his spider-sense, Peter leaps aside at the last second, and the glider impales Norman instead.

Norman asks Peter not to tell Harry that he was the Goblin, and dies.

7-6Seen by Harry

Spider-Man carries Norman's body to the Osborn home. Harry walks in and sees Spider-Man standing over his father.

Harry grabs a gun to shoot him, but Spider-Man escapes.

Part 8: Afterward

8-1Harry's vow

At Norman's funeral, Harry, convinced Spider-Man killed his father, swears revenge. He thanks Peter for his friendship and tells him he's all he has left.

Keeping his promise to Norman, Peter says nothing about the truth.

8-2MJ at the cemetery

At the cemetery, MJ tells Peter she loves him. When she thought she was going to die on the bridge, she says, she thought of him. They kiss.

But to protect her from his enemies, Peter hides his true feelings and says he'll always be her friend, and that's all he can be.

As he walks away, MJ looks as if something about the kiss seems familiar.

8-3Great power, great responsibility

Peter recalls Uncle Ben's words: with great power comes great responsibility.

Accepting that this is his calling, and that he is Spider-Man, Peter swings through the skyscrapers of New York in his suit.

Summary

How Peter becomes Spider-Man (in order)

- Getting powers (Parts 1–2): bitten by a genetically engineered spider at a lab, he gains spider-like abilities.

- Uncle Ben (Part 3): to get back at the promoter who cheated him, he lets a thief escape, and that man kills Ben. Peter learns the weight of Ben's words about power and responsibility.

- Becoming a hero (Part 4): after graduation, he helps people as Spider-Man and sells photos of himself to the Bugle.

- The end (Part 8): to protect MJ from his enemies, he turns down her love and chooses life as Spider-Man.

Norman and the Goblin (in order)

- The start (Part 1): to save his company he tests the formula on himself, and a vicious second self, the Goblin, emerges.

- Over the company (Parts 3 and 5): he attacks a rival's weapons test, then kills the board who planned to sell Oscorp at the festival.

- The offer and the secret (Parts 5–6): he invites Spider-Man to join him and is refused. At Thanksgiving he sees the cut on Peter's arm and learns who he is.

- The end (Part 7): he takes MJ and the children hostage, loses to Spider-Man in the ruin, and dies on his own glider.

Who dies (in order)

- Dr. Stromm (Part 1): killed in the lab by Norman, driven insane by the formula.

- Uncle Ben (Part 3): shot by a carjacker, the thief Peter let go.

- The carjacker (Part 3): cornered by Peter in a warehouse, he falls out of a window while trying to flee.

- General Slocum and Quest's scientists (Part 3): bombed at the weapons test by the Goblin, who wanted to sink the rival company.

- Oscorp's board (Part 5): killed by the Goblin's bombs on the festival balcony for selling the company and forcing Norman out.

- Norman (Part 7): Peter dodges the glider Norman aimed at his back, and it impales Norman instead.
