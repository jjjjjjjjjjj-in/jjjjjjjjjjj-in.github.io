Part 1: The bite

A shy teenager

New York high schooler Peter Parker is smart but shy, and sometimes bullied. Orphaned young, he was raised by Uncle Ben and Aunt May. He's long had a crush on his neighbor, Mary Jane (MJ).

The lab trip

On a school trip to a university lab with his best friend Harry Osborn, Peter is bitten by a genetically engineered spider.

Norman's experiment

Harry's father, Norman Osborn, runs the big company Oscorp. To save a military contract, he tests a strength-enhancing formula on himself, and it leaves him violently changed.

Part 2: New powers

A new body

The next morning, Peter no longer needs glasses and he's suddenly fit. He can cling to walls, shoot webs from his wrists and sense danger before it happens.

A fight at school

When the bully Flash picks a fight, Peter beats him with lightning reflexes.

Practice on the rooftops

Peter practices swinging across the city's rooftops.

Part 3: Uncle Ben

Ben's words

Driving Peter, Uncle Ben tells him that with great power comes great responsibility. Peter pushes back.

The wrestling prize

Wanting money for a car to impress MJ, Peter fights in a masked wrestling match and wins, but the promoter cheats him out of the prize.

Letting the thief go

Right after, a thief robs the promoter and runs past Peter, who deliberately lets him go.

Tragedy

Outside, Uncle Ben has been carjacked and shot. He dies in Peter's arms. Peter hunts down the killer, and it's the thief he let escape.

Part 4: Spider-Man is born

A hero begins

After graduating, Peter makes a red-and-blue suit and starts helping people across the city as Spider-Man.

Photos for the Bugle

Daily Bugle editor J. Jonah Jameson paints Spider-Man as a menace. Peter earns money selling photos of himself to the paper.

The Green Goblin

A villain in a green mask, riding a flying glider, attacks Oscorp's board members.

Part 5: Battling the Goblin

Attack at the festival

At a city festival, the Goblin blows up a balcony. Spider-Man saves MJ from falling.

A kiss in the rain

Later, Spider-Man rescues MJ from thugs in an alley. Hanging upside down, he lets her lift his mask just enough for a kiss.

The Goblin's offer

The Goblin offers Spider-Man a partnership. He refuses.

Thanksgiving dinner

At Thanksgiving dinner, Norman notices a wound on Peter's arm and realizes Peter is Spider-Man.

Aunt May attacked

The Goblin attacks Aunt May at home, putting her in the hospital.

Part 6: Choice on the bridge

Two lives

The Goblin kidnaps MJ and dangles her from a bridge alongside a cable car full of children, telling Spider-Man he can save only one.

Saving both

Spider-Man tries to save everyone. As the Goblin attacks, people on the bridge throw things at him, and Spider-Man rescues both MJ and the children.

The final fight

In a brutal fight in a ruin, the Goblin unmasks and, as Norman, begs forgiveness, while secretly aiming his glider at Peter. Peter dodges, and the glider impales Norman.

Last words

Norman's last words are, 'Don't tell Harry.'

Part 7: Afterward

Harry's vow

Peter brings Norman's body home. Harry sees and, believing Spider-Man killed his father, vows revenge.

At the cemetery

After the funeral, MJ tells Peter she loves him. To keep her safe, he says they can only be friends.

Responsibility

Remembering Uncle Ben's words, Peter accepts his life as Spider-Man.
