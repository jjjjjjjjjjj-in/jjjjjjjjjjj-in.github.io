Key terms

OXE Group
Valentina's company, which has been running projects it can't make public.
The Sentry Project
A program to create a hero by giving an ordinary person immense power.
The Void
The darkness inside someone's mind made real, which traps people in shadows.
The Red Room
The program that trained Yelena and Natasha as spies from childhood.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Who is Bob?

A man given immense power by Valentina's company. He becomes the radiant hero Sentry, but the darkness in his mind also takes shape as the terrifying Void.

How does Yelena stop the Void?

Not with force. She goes into Bob's mind and refuses to leave him alone in his worst memories. The rest of the team joins her and holds him.

What does the asterisk mean?

At the end, Valentina presents the team to the world as the New Avengers. The asterisk hinted at the name change.
