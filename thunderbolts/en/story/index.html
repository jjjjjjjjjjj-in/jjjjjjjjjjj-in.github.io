Part 1: Empty days

1-1The Kuala Lumpur job

The film opens with Yelena Belova's voice-over: "There is something wrong with me. An emptiness."

In Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, Yelena steps off the top of a skyscraper and parachutes down. She has been sent by CIA Director Valentina Allegra de Fontaine to destroy a lab belonging to the O.X.E. Group.

Valentina used to run O.X.E. The lab was part of "Project Sentry," an experiment to give a person immense power, and Valentina is wiping out the evidence. Yelena gets the job done, but feels no better.

1-2Seeing Alexei again

Yelena visits her father figure Alexei (Red Guardian). He has stepped away from hero work and now makes a living as a limo driver.

She admits that ever since Natasha, the sister she loved, died, she hasn't been able to shake the feeling of emptiness.

1-3Valentina under pressure

In Washington, Valentina is being grilled by Congress over what she did at O.X.E. Congressman Gary and others are pushing to impeach her.

Among the lawmakers is Bucky Barnes, now a congressman, who is also after Valentina.

1-4One last job

Yelena tells Valentina she wants a public-facing role, not more work in the shadows.

Valentina promises to consider it if Yelena handles one more job: go to a secret O.X.E. facility in the desert and take out Ava Starr (Ghost), an operative going after it. Yelena agrees.

Part 2: The vault trap

2-1Down into the desert

Yelena enters the O.X.E. facility in the Utah desert and makes her way down to an underground vault.

2-2Four-way fight

Three others are already in the vault: former Captain America John Walker (U.S. Agent), Ava, who can phase through things, and Antonia Dreykov (Taskmaster), who copies her opponents' moves.

All four have done covert work for Valentina. Yelena was told to kill Ava, Walker was told to kill Yelena, and Antonia was told to kill Walker. A chaotic fight breaks out.

2-3Taskmaster falls

During the fight, Ava shoots Antonia, killing her.

2-4A man named Bob

A pod that kept someone in suspended animation opens, and a man steps out. His name is Bob, and he has no idea how he got there.

2-5A room built to burn

The three survivors realize the room is an incinerator.

Valentina meant for the four people who knew about her misconduct to kill each other, and for whatever was left to burn along with the evidence. Realizing they've been set up, the three reluctantly team up.

2-6The escape, and Bob's strange effect

Yelena, Walker and Ava get out of the vault, taking Bob with them, before the room burns.

When Yelena and Walker touch Bob, each briefly relives one of their worst memories: for Yelena, her childhood in the Red Room; for Walker, his family.

Part 3: Escape from the desert

3-1What Valentina learns

Valentina learns that three of the four survived. She also learns that Bob was a volunteer in the Sentry trials who was presumed dead. She heads to the facility with armed troops.

3-2Bob draws the fire

When the troops close in, Bob steps out and draws their fire so Yelena, Walker and Ava can get away.

The bullets don't hurt him at all. But he rises into the sky against his will, then crashes back down at the compound, where he is captured.

3-3The Watchtower

Bob is taken to the former Avengers Tower in Manhattan, which Valentina now owns and has renamed the Watchtower.

She plans to unveil Bob to the press as an Avengers-style protector, hoping the publicity will save her from impeachment.

3-4Alexei's limo

As the three walk through the desert, Alexei pulls up in his limo. While driving Valentina, he overheard the plan to get rid of Yelena in the vault, and he came to the rescue. He's even wearing his Red Guardian suit.

3-5A name for the team

In the car, the subject of a team name comes up. They end up being called the Thunderbolts, after the youth soccer team Yelena played on as a kid.

3-6Car chase, and Bucky

Valentina's people give chase. The limo is slow and gets riddled with bullets.

Then Bucky rides in on a motorcycle and blows up the pursuers with a grenade launcher. Tipped off by Valentina's assistant Mel, he tracked Mel's phone to Utah.

His attack flips the limo as well, and Bucky takes the four into custody, planning to have them testify at Valentina's impeachment.

3-7Change of plans: New York

Mel calls again, worried: Bob was the subject of Valentina's secret experiment, and something is wrong. Bucky changes plans and takes the group to the Watchtower in New York.

Part 4: Battle at the Watchtower

4-1The Sentry is born

At the Watchtower, Valentina has convinced Bob to join her. He emerges as the radiant hero "the Sentry": he can fly, has enormous strength and speed, can't be hurt, and can move things with his mind.

4-2Hopelessly outmatched

The Thunderbolts storm the tower and come at the Sentry with knives, guns and fists. Nothing works.

He rips off Bucky's metal arm and folds Walker's shield in half. Walker carries Bucky to safety.

4-3Let go

Valentina orders the Sentry to kill them all. Bob ignores her and lets them retreat, because he doesn't see them as any threat.

4-4Bob changes

With so much power, Bob starts to believe he is something like a god. He turns on Valentina and goes after her.

At that moment, a failsafe kill switch built into him is triggered, and Bob collapses.

4-5The Void emerges

But from the fallen Bob rises the Void, a pitch-black figure. It is the dark side of Bob's mind, his depression and insecurity, magnified by the experiment.

People it reaches vanish, leaving only shadows on the ground. Trapped in a dark place, each one is made to relive their worst memories. The darkness spreads across New York.

Part 5: Into Bob's mind

5-1Saving people on the streets

The Thunderbolts head into the streets to save whoever they can, each using their own skills to pull people out of danger. For the first time, the misfits act like real heroes.

5-2Yelena goes in alone

Yelena realizes the only way to stop the Void is from the inside, by reaching Bob himself. She walks into the darkness alone.

5-3Yelena's past

Inside the darkness, Yelena passes through rooms of her painful past, when she was raised as a Black Widow in the Red Room.

5-4Where Bob is hiding

Yelena finds Bob hiding in a re-creation of his childhood bedroom, where he used to hide from his abusive father.

5-5The others catch up

Bucky, Walker, Ava and Alexei fight their way into the dark world and reach Yelena and Bob.

Together they move through Bob's painful memories: his abusive father, his drug addiction, and finally the day he underwent the Sentry procedure in the Kuala Lumpur lab.

5-6Facing the Void

The Void is waiting there, and it quickly overpowers the Thunderbolts. Bob tries to fight it, but the harder he fights, the closer it comes to swallowing him.

5-7The hug

The Thunderbolts stop fighting. They all wrap Bob in a hug and tell him they believe in him and that he isn't alone.

Bob takes back control and overcomes the Void. Light returns to New York, and the people who had become shadows are freed.

Part 6: The New Avengers

6-1Valentina's press conference

The Thunderbolts set out to bring Valentina in. But she has already staged a press conference.

In front of a crowd of reporters, Valentina introduces the team as the New Avengers and takes the credit for saving the city.

6-2The tables turn

The team plays along and stands before the press. But Yelena quietly tells Valentina, "We own you now."

In the end credits, the title changes to The New Avengers. That is what the asterisk was pointing to.

6-3Mid-credits

In a grocery store, Alexei is thrilled to find a cereal box with the New Avengers on it. He tries to get another shopper to buy one, but she puts it down and walks off.

6-4After the credits

Fourteen months later, at the Watchtower, the team talks about Sam Wilson, who has his own Avengers and is suing them over the name, and about a problem out in space. Bob is with them.

An alarm goes off: a spacecraft from another dimension has arrived near Earth. Yelena zooms in, and the ship bears a large "4" emblem.

Summary (spoilers through the ending)

Valentina's plan and how it ends

Valentina wanted to erase the evidence of O.X.E.'s Project Sentry. She had Yelena destroy the lab (Part 1), then sent Yelena, Walker, Ava and Antonia, who all knew about her misconduct, to kill each other in a vault that would burn whatever was left (Part 2).

She tried to escape impeachment by unveiling the surviving Bob as the Sentry (Parts 3–4), but he turned on her and the Void was born.

In the end she saves her position by presenting the Thunderbolts, who saved the city, as the New Avengers, but the team now knows the truth and holds it over her (Part 6).

Bob (the Sentry and the Void)

Bob had a painful childhood and a drug problem, and volunteered for the Sentry experiment. Presumed dead, he was kept in suspended animation in the vault (Parts 2–3).

He becomes the Sentry on Valentina's side, but when the kill switch is triggered, the dark side of his mind emerges as the Void and plunges New York into darkness (Part 4).

Inside the darkness, Yelena and the others hug him and tell him he isn't alone, and Bob overcomes the Void (Part 5).

Who died (in order)

- Antonia Dreykov (Taskmaster) (Part 2): shot by Ava during the fight in the vault that Valentina set up.

She is the only main character who dies. The New Yorkers turned into shadows by the Void are all freed at the end (Part 5).
