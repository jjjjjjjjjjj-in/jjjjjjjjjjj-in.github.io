Part 1: Empty days

Off the rooftop

Yelena Belova jumps from a skyscraper roof into a lab and finishes a job for Valentina. It goes fine, but she feels empty inside.

Missing her sister

Since losing Natasha, the closest thing she had to a sister, Yelena has lost her sense of purpose.

Valentina's problem

Valentina is under congressional investigation over her company OXE's secret projects. To erase the evidence, she sends Yelena on one last job.

Part 2: The trap

The underground vault

In the underground vault, Yelena finds John Walker, Ghost and Taskmaster. Each was ordered to kill the others.

Taskmaster falls

In the chaos, Taskmaster is killed. The other three realize they were all set up to be erased.

Bob

In the vault, a man named Bob wakes up, unsure how he got there.

Breaking out

Reluctantly, the four work together to escape the vault.

Part 3: The misfits

Alexei's ride

Yelena's father figure Alexei, Red Guardian, picks them up in a limo.

Bucky investigates

Bucky, now a congressman, has been investigating Valentina. He joins up with the group.

Who Bob is

Valentina's company experimented on Bob, giving him immense power. He awakens as the radiant hero Sentry.

Too powerful

Bob's power as Sentry is overwhelming, and the team is no match. He ends up on Valentina's side.

Part 4: Shadows over New York

The Void

The darkness in Bob's mind takes shape as the Void, a black shadow that swallows New Yorkers one by one.

Saving people

Yelena and the others pull people out of the shadows, acting like real heroes for the first time.

Into Bob's mind

Yelena dives into the shadow and walks through Bob's painful memories, which mirror her own.

Not alone

Instead of fighting Bob, Yelena tells him he's not alone. The others join her, and together they hug him. The darkness lifts and the people return.

Part 5: A new name

Valentina's announcement

In front of the press, Valentina introduces the team as the New Avengers, using their popularity to save herself.

The asterisk

This is what the asterisk in the title was hinting at.

Mid-credits

A lighthearted scene of the new team's everyday life.

After the credits

Fourteen months later, a spaceship that seems to come from another universe appears before the team.
