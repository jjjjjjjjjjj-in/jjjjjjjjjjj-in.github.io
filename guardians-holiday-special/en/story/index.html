Part 1: Christmas on Knowhere

1-1Kraglin's story (animated)

The special opens with a storybook-style animated scene. Long ago, aboard the Ravagers' ship, young Peter Quill tries to celebrate Christmas. He puts up a tree and tries to show his foster father Yondu and crewmate Kraglin how much fun Christmas can be.

But Yondu mocks the whole idea, wrecks Peter's tree and throws away the presents he'd prepared.

It turns out to be a story the grown-up Kraglin is telling the current Guardians.

1-2The Guardians and Knowhere today

In the present, the Guardians have bought Knowhere, a town inside the skull of a giant cosmic being, from its former owner, the Collector, and made it their base.

Cosmo, a telepathic space dog, has joined the team, and the Guardians have a new ship, the Bowie. On Knowhere, an alien band cheerfully plays a Christmas song even though they aren't quite sure what Christmas is.

1-3A gloomy Quill

Christmas is coming, but Quill is still down. He hasn't recovered from losing Gamora, the woman he loved, after the war with Thanos.

1-4Mantis's secret

After hearing Kraglin's story, Mantis wants to give Quill the best Christmas ever.

She confides to Drax that she and Quill are siblings, both children of Ego. Because Ego caused Quill so much pain, she hasn't told him, afraid of reminding him of their father.

1-5Kevin Bacon as a gift

The two try to decide what to give Quill. Drax suggests bringing him Kevin Bacon, the Earth hero Quill has talked about so often; as a kid, Quill loved Bacon's movie Footloose.

The two fly to Earth.

Part 2: To Hollywood

2-1Landing in Hollywood

Mantis and Drax land their ship in Los Angeles. Drax forgets to turn on the cloaking, so a few onlookers see them touch down in broad daylight.

Searching for Bacon, they walk along Hollywood Boulevard, where people in superhero and movie costumes pose for photos with tourists.

2-2Mistaken for costumed performers

Because of how they look, the two are taken for costumed performers too. Tourists pose with them and hand them tips. They spend time outside the Chinese Theatre and along the Hollywood Walk of Fame.

2-3A wild night at the bar

They go into a bar, where Mantis dances to a Christmas song with the crowd and Drax drinks with the regulars. Both end up very drunk.

Afterward, someone on the street gives them a star-homes map, which lists Bacon's house.

2-4Bacon's house

They find Bacon's home in Beverly Hills. Bacon is waiting for his family to come home.

Drax takes a liking to an inflatable elf decoration in the yard and tries to bring it along.

Terrified by the two aliens who've appeared out of nowhere, Bacon runs around the house and tries to escape.

2-5Trouble with the police

Police officers arrive. One shoots at Drax, but he's so tough it only tickles him. Drax flips a police car onto its back.

During the chase, Drax loses the inflatable elf. He wants to go back for it, but Mantis snaps at him and he gives up.

2-6Mantis takes Bacon away

Mantis uses her power over people's minds to put the police and Bacon into a trance.

Bacon stops resisting and boards the ship with them, and they fly off to Knowhere with him.

2-7An actor, not a hero

On the way back to Knowhere, the two are disappointed to learn that Bacon isn't a real-life hero, just an actor who played heroes in movies.

Part 3: The party on Knowhere

3-1The Christmas surprise

Meanwhile, the others have covered Knowhere in Christmas decorations, and the Guardians surprise Quill with a Christmas celebration.

3-2Quill is horrified

Quill is horrified to learn that Mantis and Drax have kidnapped Bacon from Earth, and demands that he be taken home right away.

3-3Bacon and Kraglin on the Bowie

Aboard the Guardians' ship, the Bowie, Kraglin talks with Bacon. He explains how Bacon's movies inspired young Quill and helped make him a hero.

Moved, Bacon decides to celebrate Christmas with the Guardians before going home.

3-4Bacon's song and the gift exchange

At the party, Bacon sings the Christmas song "Here It Is Christmastime" with the Knowhere band.

During the song, the Guardians exchange presents. Quill gives Groot a new handheld game console (his old one was destroyed in the battle in Endgame). Nebula gives Rocket a mechanical arm. Cosmo gives Kraglin a dead little creature, and he thanks her even though he's disgusted. Groot hands out handmade gifts to the team. Mantis gives Drax an inflatable elf just like the one he lost on Earth.

3-5Goodbye to Bacon

After the party, Bacon says goodbye to the Guardians and is taken back home to Earth.

3-6Yondu's gift (animated)

Quill tells Mantis how the Yondu story really ended, and the special returns to animation.

After wrecking the tree, Yondu secretly opened the present Peter had made for him: a little figurine, which he set on his ship's dashboard. In return, Yondu gave Peter a pair of blasters, the same ones Quill still fights with today.

For Quill, it's a precious memory of Yondu finally coming around on Christmas.

3-7The real gift

Mantis tells Quill the secret she's kept for so long: they're siblings, both children of Ego. She explains that she held back because she didn't want to remind him of their father.

Quill is stunned, then overjoyed. It becomes the best Christmas present of all.

3-8After the credits

After the credits, Rocket and Cosmo are decorating Groot like a Christmas tree. But Groot lowers his arms and all the ornaments fall off.

Cosmo says Groot has ruined Christmas again, and Rocket grumbles that now they'll have to make another special.

Summary (spoilers through the ending)

The key threads

- Mantis and Drax: to cheer up Quill, they go to Hollywood and use Mantis's power to bring back his hero, Kevin Bacon.

- Quill: horrified that Bacon was kidnapped, he demands he be sent home, but in the end enjoys Christmas with his friends.

- Kevin Bacon: after Kraglin tells him how his films inspired Quill, he sings at the party before being sent back to Earth.

- Yondu (in memory): he ruined Peter's Christmas, but secretly accepted his gift and gave him his blasters in return.

- Mantis: she reveals she and Quill are siblings, both children of Ego, and Quill is overjoyed.

What this special sets up (before Vol. 3)

- The Guardians have bought Knowhere and made it their base.

- Cosmo the space dog has joined the team, and their new ship is called the Bowie.

- Mantis and Quill are siblings, both children of Ego.

- No one dies in this special.

My takeOpinion

A silly, fun short about kidnapping Kevin Bacon as himself. It's also surprisingly important, since it reveals how Mantis and Peter are related.
