Part 1: The freeway ambush

1-1A joke of an opening

The camera glides through a frozen moment of a car crash. Instead of real names, the credits read things like "God's Perfect Idiot" and "A British Villain."

1-2Dopinder the cab driver

A man in a red suit, Deadpool, rides a taxi to the freeway. The driver, Dopinder, confides that his cousin is stealing the girl he loves, and Deadpool gives him some violent advice.

On an overpass, Deadpool sits on the railing and waits for a convoy of cars below.

1-3Twelve bullets

Deadpool drops onto the convoy and fights the men inside. They work for Ajax, the man Deadpool has been hunting.

Counting his last twelve bullets, he takes them down with swords and guns. When he's shot, his wounds close up right away.

He turns to the audience and starts explaining how he ended up like this.

Part 2: Wade and Vanessa

2-1Wade Wilson, mercenary

Years earlier: Deadpool's real name is Wade Wilson. Dishonorably discharged from Canadian special forces, he works in New York as a mercenary for hire.

One job: scaring off a pizza delivery guy who's been stalking a teenage girl.

2-2The bar and Vanessa

Wade hangs out at a mercenaries' bar run by his best friend Weasel. There he meets Vanessa, a sex worker.

They try to top each other's miserable childhood stories and hit it off.

2-3A year of love, and a proposal

They fall for each other and spend holiday after holiday together. Skee-Ball at the arcade becomes one of their things.

A year later, Wade proposes, and Vanessa says yes.

2-4The diagnosis

Soon after, Wade collapses and learns he has terminal cancer that has spread through his body.

Vanessa searches desperately for treatments, but Wade doesn't want her to watch him waste away.

2-5The recruiter, and leaving at night

At the bar, a mysterious recruiter offers Wade an experimental treatment that would cure his cancer and give him powers. Wade turns him down at first.

But one night, he quietly leaves the sleeping Vanessa and takes the offer.

Part 3: The lab

3-1Ajax and Angel Dust

Wade is taken to a lab run by Ajax, who can't feel pain, and the super-strong Angel Dust.

He's injected with a serum meant to awaken hidden mutant genes in his body.

3-2Days of torture, and "Francis"

For days they torture Wade to trigger a mutation, with no result.

Wade learns Ajax's real name is Francis and won't stop mocking him for it.

3-3The airless chamber

Furious, Ajax locks Wade in a chamber that brings him to the edge of suffocation over and over, all weekend.

The ordeal finally triggers a healing factor that holds back the cancer, but it leaves his whole body scarred as if badly burned.

Ajax's real business is turning people into super-powered slaves to sell to the rich.

3-4Left in the burning lab

Wade breaks out and attacks Ajax, but hesitates when Ajax says his face can be fixed.

Ajax overpowers him and leaves him for dead in the burning lab. Thanks to his healing factor, Wade survives.

Part 4: Deadpool is born

4-1Too scared to go home

Wade watches Vanessa from a distance, but he's too afraid of how she'll react to his face to approach her.

He shows himself to Weasel, who mocks his face mercilessly but sticks by him.

4-2Living with Blind Al

Wade moves in with Al, an elderly blind woman. They become foul-mouthed but oddly well-matched roommates.

4-3The red suit and the name

Wade decides to find Ajax and force him to fix his face. He makes a red suit so enemies can't see him bleed.

He takes the name from the bar's dead pool, a betting board on who dies next, where Weasel had bet on him.

4-4Hunting down Ajax

Over the following year, Deadpool questions and kills Ajax's associates one by one.

Finally the recruiter gives up Ajax's whereabouts, which leads to the freeway ambush in Part 1.

Part 5: Showdown on the freeway, and Vanessa

5-1Ajax cornered

Back on the freeway, Deadpool takes out all of Ajax's men, corners Ajax, and demands the cure.

5-2Colossus and Negasonic

X-Men member Colossus and his trainee, Negasonic Teenage Warhead, arrive. Colossus urges Deadpool to stop the killing and join the X-Men.

Deadpool refuses and punches Colossus, which does nothing to his steel body.

5-3Cutting off his own hand

Colossus handcuffs Deadpool to himself, and in the confusion, Ajax escapes.

Deadpool cuts off his own hand to slip the cuffs and gets away.

5-4Vanessa is taken

Ajax and Angel Dust go to Weasel's bar, where Ajax hears about Vanessa.

He kidnaps Vanessa from the club where she works.

5-5Gathering help

At Al's place, Wade's hand is growing back as a tiny baby hand.

He goes to the X-Mansion and asks Colossus and Negasonic for help, and they agree.

They head out in Dopinder's cab, but Wade forgets his bag of guns, leaving him with only his swords.

Part 6: Battle at the scrapyard

6-1The wrecked helicarrier

Ajax waits at a scrapyard holding a huge decommissioned helicarrier, with Vanessa captive on top.

Colossus and Negasonic take on Angel Dust and the henchmen while Deadpool goes after Ajax.

6-2The helicarrier falls

Negasonic's blast accidentally destroys the supports holding the helicarrier, and it tips over and collapses.

Deadpool shields Vanessa as they fall, while Colossus carries Negasonic and Angel Dust to safety.

6-3"There is no cure"

In the wreckage, Ajax attacks again and loses. He reveals there was never a cure for Wade's face.

Colossus begs him to act like a hero and spare Ajax, but Deadpool shoots him dead.

6-4Reunited with Vanessa

Vanessa is furious that Wade disappeared on her. He takes off his mask and explains his face and why he never came back.

She accepts him, and they kiss.

6-5After the credits

Deadpool appears in a bathrobe, imitating the ending of a classic teen comedy, and announces that Cable will be in the sequel.

Summary (spoilers through the end)

How Wade became Deadpool

1) Diagnosed with cancer, he leaves Vanessa and takes the treatment (Part 2).

2) In Ajax's lab, his powers awaken, but his body is left badly scarred (Part 3).

3) To get his face fixed, he hunts Ajax as Deadpool, in a red suit (Part 4).

4) Learning there's no cure, he kills Ajax, and Vanessa accepts him as he is (Part 6).

Who died

- Ajax's men (Parts 1 and 5): taken out by Deadpool on the freeway.

- Ajax's associates (Part 4): killed one by one as Deadpool hunts for him.

- Ajax (Part 6): after admitting there's no cure, he's shot dead by Deadpool, despite Colossus's pleas.

- Angel Dust survives; Colossus carries her to safety.
