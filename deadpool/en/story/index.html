Part 1: The freeway ambush

1-1A joke of an opening

The camera glides through a frozen moment of a car being wrecked, with a man in a red suit in the middle of fighting thugs inside.

Instead of real names, the credits read things like "God's Perfect Idiot," "A British Villain" and a jab at the "overpaid" director.

1-2Dopinder the cab driver

The man in the red suit, Deadpool, is riding a taxi to the freeway. Uncomfortable in the back, he moves up front to chat.

The driver, Dopinder, says the girl he loves, Gita, is falling for his cousin Bandhu, who is as handsome as he is rotten. Deadpool gives him some violent advice.

1-3Waiting on the overpass

On a freeway overpass, Deadpool sits on the railing, looking at a crayon drawing he made himself, and waits for a convoy below.

The man he is after is called Ajax.

1-4Dropping onto the convoy

Deadpool leaps off the railing and crashes into one of the moving cars. The men inside work for Ajax, and a brawl breaks out in the car.

The frozen moment from the opening is this scene. The car wrecks and comes to a stop.

1-5Twelve bullets

Counting his last twelve bullets, Deadpool takes down the men climbing out of the cars with guns and swords. One shot goes through three heads; he also wastes a few and kicks himself for it.

When he's shot, his wounds close up right away.

1-6Talking to the audience

Mid-fight, the picture freezes and Deadpool speaks to the audience. He says this is a love story and starts explaining how he ended up like this.

The story jumps back several years.

Part 2: Wade and Vanessa

2-1Wade Wilson, mercenary

Deadpool's real name is Wade Wilson. Dishonorably discharged from Canadian special forces, he works in New York as a mercenary for hire.

His jobs are small ones, like scaring off a stalker.

2-2Sister Margaret's

Wade hangs out at Sister Margaret's, a bar where mercenaries look for work, run by his best friend Weasel.

The bar keeps a betting pool on which of its regulars will die next, and Weasel has his money on Wade.

2-3Meeting Vanessa

At the bar, Wade meets Vanessa, a sex worker.

They try to top each other's miserable childhood stories. Both were abused growing up, both love crude jokes, and they hit it off.

2-4A year of holidays

They start dating. Over the song "Calendar Girl," a montage shows them spending holiday after holiday together in bed.

Skee-Ball at the arcade becomes one of their things.

2-5The proposal

A year in, Wade tells Vanessa they work because they're both a little crazy, and proposes with a ring.

She says yes, then asks where exactly the naked Wade pulled the ring from.

2-6The diagnosis

Soon after, Wade suddenly collapses and is told at the hospital that he has terminal cancer, spread through his body.

Vanessa searches desperately for treatments, but Wade doesn't want her to watch him waste away.

2-7The recruiter, and goodbye

A mysterious recruiter in a black suit approaches Wade at the bar. He offers a secret treatment that would cure the cancer and give him powers. Wade turns him down at first.

But not wanting Vanessa to watch him die, Wade quietly leaves her and takes the offer.

Part 3: The lab

3-1Ajax and Angel Dust

Wade is taken to a lab run by Ajax, who can't feel pain, and the super-strong Angel Dust, who always has a match in her mouth.

He's injected with a serum meant to awaken hidden mutant genes in his body.

3-2Days of torture

The mutation is supposed to need extreme stress. Wade is tortured for days, with no result.

He still won't stop joking, telling them that if he gets a suit, it had better not be green.

3-3His real name is Francis

Wade learns that Ajax's real name is Francis. Ajax hates being called that, so Wade keeps doing it.

3-4The airless chamber

Furious, Ajax locks Wade in a sealed chamber that pumps out the air, bringing him to the edge of suffocation over and over, all weekend.

The ordeal finally triggers a healing factor that holds back his cancer, but it leaves his whole body scarred as if badly burned.

3-5What the lab is really for

Ajax's people gather the seriously ill, like Wade, with promises of a cure, and force their powers to awaken.

The real business is selling them as super-powered slaves to the rich.

3-6Left in the burning lab

Wade grabs Angel Dust's match and deliberately starts a fire to break out, then attacks Ajax, but hesitates when Ajax says his condition can be cured.

Ajax overpowers him and leaves him for dead in the burning lab. Thanks to his healing factor, Wade survives.

Part 4: Deadpool is born

4-1Too scared to go home

Wade finds Vanessa and watches her from a distance, but afraid she'll reject him because of how he looks, he doesn't let her know he's alive.

4-2Showing Weasel

Wade shows his face only to Weasel, who mocks it mercilessly but sticks by him and suggests a mask.

Wade decides to hunt down Ajax and make him fix his face.

4-3The name

He takes his new name, Deadpool, from the bar's betting pool on who dies next, where Weasel had bet on him.

4-4Living with Blind Al

Wade moves in with Al, an elderly blind woman who keeps trying to assemble furniture on her own, without much luck.

They become foul-mouthed but oddly well-matched roommates.

4-5The red suit

Wade makes his own costume. It starts out white, but blood stains show and Al has some advice, so he switches to red, which keeps enemies from seeing him bleed.

4-6Hunting Ajax's people

Over the following year, Deadpool questions and kills Ajax's associates one after another.

4-7The recruiter talks

Finally he tracks down the recruiter, who is busy pitching the treatment to another sick person.

Roughed up by Deadpool, the man gives up Ajax's whereabouts. Deadpool then "fast-forwards" the story back to the freeway.

Part 5: Showdown on the freeway, and Vanessa

5-1Ajax cornered

Back on the freeway now, Deadpool takes out all of Ajax's men, pins Ajax down and demands the cure.

5-2Colossus and Negasonic

Having seen the chaos on TV, X-Men member Colossus and his trainee, Negasonic Teenage Warhead, arrive.

Colossus tells him to stop this and urges him to join the X-Men and become a hero.

5-3No match for steel

Deadpool refuses: he'll do this on his own. When Colossus keeps talking, Deadpool punches him, but against a steel body he only breaks his own hands.

While they argue, Ajax gets away.

5-4Cutting off his own hand

Colossus handcuffs Deadpool to himself to take him to Professor X.

Deadpool cuts off his own hand with a knife to slip the cuffs and heads home to Al's.

5-5Ajax learns about Vanessa

Meanwhile, Ajax and Angel Dust walk into Weasel's bar, where Ajax finds out that Wade has a girlfriend named Vanessa.

5-6The baby hand

At Al's place, Deadpool's hand is growing back as a tiny baby hand.

Weasel warns him that Ajax is after Vanessa.

5-7Vanessa is taken

Vanessa now works at a strip club, where Stan Lee is the emcee. Ajax shows up and kidnaps her.

5-8To the X-Mansion

Deadpool goes to the X-Mansion and asks Colossus and Negasonic for help, joking that such a huge mansion only seems to have two X-Men.

They agree to come.

5-9Off in the cab

The three ride out in Dopinder's cab. Dopinder has tied up his cousin Bandhu and stuffed him in the trunk; Deadpool pretends to object but lets it go.

Then Deadpool leaves his bag of guns in the cab, so he'll have to fight with just his swords and his fists.

Part 6: Battle at the scrapyard

6-1The wrecked helicarrier

Ajax waits at a scrapyard holding a huge decommissioned helicarrier, with Vanessa captive on top.

Colossus and Negasonic take on Angel Dust and the henchmen while Deadpool goes after Ajax.

6-2Superhero landing

Angel Dust drops in with a one-knee superhero landing. Deadpool tells the audience it's terrible for your knees, but everyone does it.

She knocks Colossus flying with a single blow.

6-3Fighting through the henchmen

Without his guns, Deadpool cuts his way through the henchmen with swords. One of them is Bob, an old mercenary acquaintance; Deadpool just headbutts him unconscious and drags him out of the way.

6-4Negasonic's blast

Colossus struggles against Angel Dust. Negasonic unleashes a huge explosive blast, which accidentally destroys the supports holding up the helicarrier.

6-5The helicarrier falls

The ship tips over and starts to collapse. Deadpool grabs Vanessa and shields her as it comes down around them.

Colossus carries Negasonic and Angel Dust to safety.

6-6"There is no cure"

In the wreckage, Ajax attacks again and loses. Deadpool demands the cure, and Ajax reveals there never was one.

6-7The end of Ajax

Colossus begs him to spare Ajax if he wants to be a hero. Deadpool ignores him and shoots Ajax dead.

6-8Reunited with Vanessa

Vanessa is furious that Wade disappeared on her. He takes off his mask and shows her his face, explaining why he never came back.

She accepts him as he is, and they're together again. Even Negasonic thinks a little better of him.

6-9After the credits

Deadpool appears in a bathrobe, imitating the end of Ferris Bueller's Day Off, and tells the audience to go home.

There's no money left for a big bonus scene, he says, but Cable will be in the sequel, and he rattles off casting ideas.

Summary (spoilers through the end)

How Wade became Deadpool

1) Right after getting engaged, he learns he has cancer, leaves Vanessa and takes the recruiter's treatment (Part 2).

2) In Ajax's lab, the airless chamber awakens his powers but leaves his body badly scarred (Part 3).

3) Too afraid to face Vanessa, he hunts Ajax as Deadpool, in a red suit (Part 4).

4) The X-Men get in the way on the freeway, Ajax escapes, and Vanessa is kidnapped (Part 5).

5) Learning there's no cure, he kills Ajax, and Vanessa accepts him as he is (Part 6).

Who died

- Ajax's men (Parts 1 and 5): taken out by Deadpool on the freeway.

- Ajax's associates (Part 4): killed one by one as Deadpool hunts for him.

- Ajax (Part 6): after admitting there's no cure, he's shot dead by Deadpool, despite Colossus's pleas.

- Angel Dust and Bob survive: Colossus carries Angel Dust to safety, and Deadpool knocks Bob out and drags him clear.

My takeOpinion

Behind the mischief and crude jokes, it's a straightforward love story. Laughing at its own low budget is the film's best weapon.
