Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

Deadpool's first story

The origin of Ryan Reynolds's Deadpool, continued in Deadpool 2 and Deadpool & Wolverine.

Fox's X-Men world

It's set in the same world as Fox's X-Men films. The X-Mansion and Colossus appear, but you don't need to have seen the others.

A hero who talks to you

Deadpool knows he's in a movie and talks straight to the audience.

Violent and crude

It's rated R (R15+ in Japan), with plenty of gore and crude jokes.

Now and then

The film cuts back and forth between a freeway fight (now) and how Wade became Deadpool (then).

Stay after the credits

There's a bonus scene teasing the next film.

StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

Part 1: The freeway ambush

Deadpool takes a cab to the freeway and ambushes a convoy carrying Ajax's men.

Counting his last bullets, he takes them down one by one, then starts telling the audience how he got this way.

Part 2: Wade and Vanessa

Ex-special forces mercenary Wade meets Vanessa at a bar and falls in love. A year later they get engaged, and then he learns he has terminal cancer.

A mysterious recruiter offers a treatment that will cure him and give him powers, and Wade leaves Vanessa to take it.

Part 3: The lab

At the lab, Ajax and Angel Dust torture Wade for days. An airless chamber finally awakens a healing factor.

But his whole body is left scarred. He attacks Ajax, but is left for dead in the burning lab.

Part 4: Deadpool is born

Wade survives, but he's too afraid of his new face to go back to Vanessa.

He moves in with Blind Al and, as Deadpool in a red suit, starts hunting Ajax to make him fix his face.

Part 5: Showdown on the freeway, and Vanessa

Deadpool corners Ajax on the freeway, but Colossus and Negasonic of the X-Men get in the way, and Ajax escapes.

Ajax learns about Vanessa and kidnaps her. Wade asks Colossus and Negasonic for help.

Part 6: Battle at the scrapyard

The final fight breaks out at a scrapyard holding a wrecked helicarrier. The ship collapses, and Deadpool protects Vanessa.

Ajax admits there's no cure, and Deadpool shoots him dead. Vanessa accepts Wade as he is.

Key terms

Deadpool
Wade's name in the red suit, taken from the bar's dead pool, a bet on who'll die next.
Healing factor
The power to heal from any wound. The experiment awakened it but left his skin badly scarred.
Mutants
People born with special powers. The lab forces hidden mutant genes to awaken.
The X-Men
A team of mutants based at Professor X's mansion. Here, only Colossus and his trainee Negasonic appear.
Helicarrier
A giant flying ship. The final battle takes place on a decommissioned one in a scrapyard.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why does Deadpool talk to the audience?

He's a character who knows he's in a movie or comic, so he talks to the viewer and jokes about the film itself.

What was Ajax's lab really for?

Forcing hidden mutant powers to awaken through drugs and torture, then selling the people as super-powered slaves to the rich.

Why didn't Wade go back to Vanessa?

He was terrified she'd reject him because of his face, so he hunted Ajax to get it fixed first.

How does it connect to the X-Men films?

It's the same world as Fox's X-Men movies. The X-Mansion appears, but almost no X-Men besides Colossus and Negasonic show up, which Deadpool jokes about.

Easy-to-miss details

The joke credits

The opening credits list jokes like "God's Perfect Idiot" and "A British Villain" instead of real names.

Why red

Deadpool explains that red keeps bad guys from seeing him bleed.

The baby hand

After he cuts off his hand, it grows back as a tiny baby hand at first.

Skee-Ball

Arcade Skee-Ball is Wade and Vanessa's thing, and it matters again in the sequel.

Stan Lee

Marvel legend Stan Lee has a cameo as a strip club DJ.

The ending and post-creditsSpoilers

After the end credits

After the credits, Deadpool appears in a bathrobe and announces that Cable will be in the sequel.
