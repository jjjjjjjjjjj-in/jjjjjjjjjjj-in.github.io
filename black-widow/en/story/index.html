Part 1: Ohio, 1995

1-1Fireflies at dusk

Ohio, 1995. In a quiet suburb, young Natasha and her little sister Yelena live with their father Alexei and mother Melina.

At dusk, Melina explains to the girls how fireflies make their light. They look like any happy family.

1-2A sudden departure

But they're a fake family: spies sent from Russia. Alexei, the 'dad,' is a Soviet super soldier; Melina, the 'mom,' is a Widow trained in the Red Room. Only little Yelena doesn't know.

Alexei has stolen the research data he was after from a S.H.I.E.L.D. facility. Their three-year mission is over, and the family leaves home that very night.

1-3Escape by plane

In the car, Alexei plays "American Pie" for the frightened Yelena.

A small plane is hidden in a greenhouse at the edge of town. S.H.I.E.L.D. vehicles chase them, firing. Alexei holds them off, then grabs onto the moving plane and climbs aboard.

Melina, at the controls, is shot. With Melina talking her through it, Natasha takes the controls and gets the plane into the air.

1-4Torn apart in Cuba

The family lands on an airstrip in Cuba. Waiting for them are Dreykov, head of the Red Room, and Russian soldiers.

Natasha and Yelena are separated from Alexei and Melina and taken away. The fake family is broken up that day.

1-5The opening titles

Over the titles, girls are trucked away, mistreated by Dreykov's soldiers, and trained to become spies called Widows. Natasha and Yelena are among them.

Part 2: Natasha on the run

2-1Ross gives chase

2016. Having sided with Captain America in Civil War, Natasha is wanted by Secretary of State Ross for violating the Sokovia Accords.

She slips past Ross's people and disappears.

2-2A trailer in Norway

Natasha arrives at a trailer by a Norwegian fjord, where her old contact, the fixer Rick Mason, is waiting, lying on the bed.

He brings her passports, visas, a stack of driver's licenses and a generator, plus the mail that piled up at her old Budapest safe house.

Alone, she drinks a beer and watches the Bond film Moonraker, reciting the lines along with it.

2-3Yelena in Morocco

Meanwhile, in Tangier, Morocco, the grown-up Yelena is still working as a mind-controlled Red Room Widow. Her target is Oksana, a Widow who went rogue.

Yelena fatally wounds Oksana, but Oksana sprays her with a red gas: an antidote that undoes the Widows' mind control.

Freed, Yelena hears Oksana's dying plea to free the others. She cuts the tracker out of her own leg and goes into hiding.

2-4Vials in the mail

Yelena had mailed vials of the red gas to Natasha's Budapest safe house, trusting that Natasha and the Avengers would free the remaining Widows. The vials were in the mail Mason brought.

Natasha drives out to buy gas for the generator, unaware the vials are in her car.

2-5Taskmaster attacks

The masked fighter Taskmaster attacks Natasha's car. As they fight, Taskmaster copies even Natasha's own moves.

Natasha barely escapes and realizes Taskmaster was after the vials. Seeing they came from Budapest, she heads there.

Part 3: Reunion in Budapest

3-1The old safe house

Budapest is where Natasha and Clint (Hawkeye) once fought on an old mission. Natasha enters the apartment safe house she used with Clint; arrow holes from back then still mark the kitchen wall.

Yelena is waiting for her.

3-2A sisterly brawl

The sisters fight before they talk. Furniture breaks, plates shatter, and knives come out, but neither wins.

Finally calm, they pour drinks and talk. Yelena resents Natasha for escaping the Red Room alone and leaving the rest behind.

3-3Dreykov is alive

Natasha believed she'd killed Dreykov with a bomb in Budapest years ago, ending the Red Room. It was her final test to join S.H.I.E.L.D.

But Yelena tells her Dreykov is alive, and the Red Room still sends Widows all over the world. With the red gas, the controlled Widows could be saved.

3-4The Widows strike

Widows sent to recover the vials burst in. Yelena blows up the safe house with the explosives she rigged, and the sisters flee across the rooftops.

3-5Chase through Budapest

They flee on Yelena's motorcycle; Natasha has pickpocketed the key and drives. Taskmaster follows in an armored vehicle, crushing cars along the way.

A Widow's shots send the bike into a wall, and they grab a passerby's car. Yelena kicks off the passenger door into a Widow.

Then Taskmaster's arrow flips the car, and it tumbles down into a subway station.

3-6Inside the vent

They crawl into a ventilation shaft in the station and wait out their hunters. Natasha says she once hid there with Clint for two days; their old tic-tac-toe games are still scratched inside.

Part 4: Breaking Dad out

4-1Mason's helicopter

To find the Red Room, the sisters decide to find Alexei, their old 'dad,' who is locked up in a prison in the Russian snowfields.

When Natasha asks Mason for a jet, he says that would take more time and money, and gets them a helicopter instead. He also supplies two white combat suits.

4-2Arm wrestling in prison

Alexei, now bearded and heavy, arm-wrestles inmate Ursa Major while bragging he once fought Captain America. Told that Cap was frozen in ice back then, Alexei hurts the man's arm.

Then a Red Guardian action figure arrives for him. Hidden inside is a tiny earpiece so the sisters can talk to him.

4-3Breakout and avalanche

The sisters open the prison's doors remotely, and Alexei kicks his way out through the south door. In the chaos of inmates and guards, Natasha drops in to fight her way to him.

Yelena destroys a guard tower, setting off an avalanche that buries the prison. Alexei is hauled into the helicopter at the last second.

4-4In the helicopter

Dreykov had sent him to prison after the mission. He doesn't know where the Red Room is now, but says Melina, their old 'mom,' would.

4-5A field of purple flowers

The helicopter runs out of fuel and crashes in a field of purple flowers outside Saint Petersburg. All three walk away and head for Melina's home.

Part 5: Mom's farm

5-1Melina and the pigs

Melina lives on a farm in the fields, raising pigs. In fact, she uses them to refine the Red Room's mind-control process. She is still connected to Dreykov.

5-2An awkward family dinner

For the first time in 21 years, the four sit down to dinner together. Alexei squeezes into his old Red Guardian suit.

But nothing goes right. Yelena is furious that the Ohio family was real to her but just a mission to the others, and she leaves the table.

5-3"American Pie"

Alexei follows Yelena to her room. At a loss for words, he starts singing "American Pie," and Yelena joins in: the song they sang together in the getaway car when she was little.

5-4A call to Dreykov

Melina has already told Dreykov that Natasha and the others are there. Dreykov's forces arrive at the farm, and the three are captured and taken away.

Part 6: The Red Room in the sky

6-1The flying headquarters

The Red Room's headquarters is a huge base flying around the world. 'Melina' stands before Dreykov, who praises her for finding Natasha and the others.

But Dreykov sees through it: under a face mask is Natasha. At the farm, Natasha and Melina teamed up, swapped faces with masks, and let themselves be captured.

6-2Melina, in disguise

The 'Natasha' in the cell is really Melina. She frees Alexei and Yelena.

6-3Dreykov talks

Dreykov boasts that he directs Widows all over the world from his control desk here. Natasha learns the Red Room girls weren't orphans: they were stolen children.

Dreykov gives off a pheromone that makes it impossible for any Widow to harm him. Natasha can't strike him either.

6-4Taskmaster unmasked

Dreykov reveals who Taskmaster is: his daughter Antonia, thought killed in the Budapest bombing.

Back then, Natasha went ahead with the bombing with Clint, knowing Dreykov's young daughter was in the office. Antonia survived, terribly injured, and Dreykov saved her by putting technology in her head. It made her a soldier who can copy anyone's moves, under her father's control.

6-5Breaking her own nose

Dreykov, overconfident, has revealed too much. Natasha smashes her own nose, breaking it and severing the nerve that lets her smell.

No longer bound by the scent, she goes after Dreykov.

6-6The engine and the cell

Melina takes out one of the base's engines, and it starts to tilt.

Alexei fights Taskmaster and is losing. Natasha leaps in to help and lands in her signature pose, to Yelena's delight. Together they lock Taskmaster in a cell.

Part 7: The base falls

7-1The Widows are freed

Dreykov escapes as Widows surround Natasha and beat her down. Yelena arrives and sets off the red gas. The Widows are freed from mind control.

7-2The data and two vials

Natasha copies the locations of Widows around the world from Dreykov's system onto a drive as the base starts exploding and falling from the sky.

She picks up the two vials of the red gas that survived, then lets Taskmaster out of the locked cell.

7-3Dreykov's end

Melina and Alexei escape the base in a plane.

Dreykov tries to flee in his own aircraft. Yelena blows it up, killing him, and is thrown into the open sky.

7-4Battle in freefall

Amid the falling wreckage, Natasha dives after Yelena, who has no parachute, and hands her one in midair.

Then Natasha fights Taskmaster, who has followed her, as they fall.

7-5Antonia is freed

On the ground, Natasha uses one vial on Taskmaster. The mind control lifts, and Antonia is finally herself again.

7-6The family says goodbye

The freed Widows gather, and Melina and Alexei arrive. Then Ross's forces approach.

Natasha gives Yelena the last vial and the drive with the Widows' locations, asking her to free the rest. After their goodbyes, Yelena, Melina and Alexei leave with Antonia and the Widows, and Natasha stays behind alone.

Part 8: Afterward

8-1Two weeks later

Two weeks later, Natasha wears Yelena's many-pocketed vest and has a new hairstyle. This time, Mason has gotten her a Quinjet.

She flies off to free her captured Avengers teammates.

8-2After the credits

After Endgame, Yelena visits Natasha's grave in Ohio with a dog.

Valentina shows up and tells her Clint Barton (Hawkeye) is to blame for Natasha's death, then hands her Clint's photo as her next target.

Summary

What Natasha did

As a child she lived in Ohio as part of a fake family, then was trained as a spy in the Red Room (Part 1). Years ago in Budapest, she bombed Dreykov's office knowing his young daughter was there (Part 6).

On the run, she reunited with Yelena and learned Dreykov was alive (Part 3), brought the family back together (Parts 4 and 5), and broke her own nose to defeat Dreykov's lock (Part 6). In the end she freed Antonia and entrusted the Widows to Yelena (Part 7).

Yelena and the 'parents'

Freed by the red gas (Part 2), Yelena mailed the vials to Natasha. In the end she blew up Dreykov's aircraft (Part 7) and took on the job of freeing the remaining Widows.

Alexei had been sent to prison by Dreykov after the mission (Part 4). Melina kept working for Dreykov, but teamed up with Natasha, swapped faces with her, and took out the base's engine (Parts 5 and 6).

Dreykov and Antonia

Dreykov controlled kidnapped girls with drugs and a pheromone lock, using them as Widows worldwide (Part 6). He controlled his own daughter Antonia too, making her fight as Taskmaster.

Natasha freed Antonia with the red gas (Part 7), and she left with Yelena and the others.

Who died (in order)

- Oksana (Part 2): a Widow who escaped the Red Room. Fatally wounded by the mind-controlled Yelena, she frees Yelena with the red gas before she dies.

- Dreykov (Part 7): fleeing the falling base in his aircraft, he dies when Yelena blows it up.

- Natasha (mentioned after the credits): she died in Endgame. Valentina tells Yelena that Clint is to blame.
