Key terms

The Red Room
A secret program that kidnaps girls and trains them as spies, called Widows.
Widows
Spies raised by the Red Room, their minds chemically controlled.
The red gas
An antidote that frees the Widows from mind control.
Budapest
Where Natasha and Clint fought on an old mission, often mentioned between them.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Were they a real family?

No. They pretended to be a family for a mission. But to Natasha and Yelena, it felt like a real one.

Who is Taskmaster?

A fighter who can copy anyone's moves. It turns out to be Antonia, Dreykov's daughter.

Why does Natasha break her own nose?

Dreykov gives off a scent that stops any Widow from attacking him. Severing the nerve in her nose breaks the lock.

Easy-to-miss details

The vest

Yelena loves Natasha's many-pocketed vest and ends up taking it.

The pose

Yelena teases Natasha about her signature superhero landing pose.

The blonde look

By the end, Natasha starts moving toward her blonde look from Infinity War.
