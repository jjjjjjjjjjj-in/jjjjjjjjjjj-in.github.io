Part 1: Moscow and the Expo

1-1A father's death

A shabby apartment in Moscow. On TV, the news shows Tony Stark announcing that he is Iron Man.

In front of it, the elderly scientist Anton Vanko dies. Years ago Anton worked in America with Tony's father, Howard, but he was sent back home and ended his life in poverty. His son Ivan is at his side as he dies.

1-2Ivan at work

Ivan spreads out old Stark Industries blueprints his father left behind. They show the same miniature arc reactor that sits in Tony's chest.

Shut away in the apartment, Ivan builds one with his own hands, then starts on a harness with two whips charged by the reactor's power.

1-3Tony drops in from the sky

Six months later. In New York, Tony revives the Stark Expo, the fair of future technology his father Howard once held.

Tony lands on stage from the sky in his Iron Man suit, flanked by dancers, to a roaring crowd. He wants to carry on his father's legacy and make it a place where companies from around the world show off the technology of the future.

1-4Poison in the chest

Off stage, Tony quietly presses a small device to his finger to measure the toxicity in his blood.

The palladium at the core of his chest reactor is slowly poisoning him. He hasn't told Pepper or Rhodey.

Part 2: The hearing and the poison

2-1The Senate hearing

A Senate hearing in Washington. Senator Stern presses Tony to hand over the Iron Man suit, arguing that it is a weapon.

Stern has Rhodey, Air Force Lieutenant Colonel James Rhodes, read part of his own report saying the suit could threaten national security. Rhodey adds that the report also concludes the benefits outweigh the risks.

2-2Footage of failures

Justin Hammer, head of a rival weapons company, also testifies that other countries are copying the suit.

Tony hijacks the screens in the chamber and plays footage of every copied suit failing, embarrassing Hammer. Tony says no one will catch up for years, declares, "I have successfully privatized world peace," and walks out to applause.

2-3No substitute

In his workshop at his Malibu home, Tony tries every material he can think of to replace the palladium, but nothing works.

His AI, J.A.R.V.I.S., warns that if he keeps using the reactor, the poison will kill him. Dark lines are starting to spread from his chest along his veins.

2-4Pepper becomes CEO

Expecting to die, Tony hands control of the company to his assistant, Pepper.

Natalie Rushman from the legal department arrives to handle the paperwork. Tony is boxing with Happy, and when Natalie steps into the ring she flips Happy to the mat in an instant. Tony decides to make her his new assistant.

Part 3: Attack at Monaco

3-1The race at Monaco

Tony, Pepper, Natalie and Happy go to Monaco for a car race Stark Industries sponsors. Hammer is there too, with the reporter Christine Everhart.

Just before the start, Tony suddenly decides to drive his company's car in the race himself. Pepper is exasperated.

3-2Electric whips

Mid-race, Ivan, disguised as a track worker, walks onto the course. His chest reactor glowing, he swings his electric whips and slices oncoming cars in half.

Tony's car is cut apart and wrecked. Without a suit, Tony is cornered by Ivan beside the wreck.

3-3The suitcase suit

Happy and Pepper drive onto the track and ram Ivan again and again, but he slices up their car with his whips too.

Pepper throws Tony a portable suit folded into a briefcase. Tony opens it, suits up, wraps the whips around his hands, pulls Ivan in and rips the reactor out of his chest. Ivan is arrested.

3-4Talking in prison

Tony visits Ivan in his cell. Ivan says his father and Howard built the reactor together, but Howard had his father sent back home.

Ivan says that if you can make God bleed, people will stop believing in Him. Showing the world that Iron Man can be beaten was enough for him. He also knows that Tony's reactor is killing him.

Part 4: Hammer's scheme

4-1Ivan breaks out

Impressed by Ivan's attack, Hammer fakes Ivan's death in prison and secretly gets him out.

On his private jet, Hammer wines and dines Ivan and asks him to build suits for his company that will outdo Tony's.

4-2He wants his bird

At Hammer's factory, Ivan agrees to the job. In return, he asks for one thing: his pet bird, left behind in Russia.

Ivan decides that unmanned drones are better than suits with people inside, and starts building something different from what Hammer wanted.

4-3Public opinion

The Monaco attack shows the world that technology like Iron Man's exists elsewhere. On TV, people ask whether Tony should have handed the suit over after all.

Tony still can't tell Pepper the truth, and his toxicity keeps rising.

Part 5: The birthday fight

5-1A party out of control

Tony throws a birthday party at his Malibu home. Thinking it may be his last, he gets drunk while wearing his suit.

He blasts bottles and fruit with the repulsors in his palms to show off for the guests. The guests love it, but Pepper is fed up.

5-2Fighting his best friend

Rhodey arrives, puts on another suit kept in the house (the Mark II), and steps in to stop Tony. The two brawl through the house, the guests flee, and the place is wrecked.

The fight ends with neither of them winning.

5-3The suit is taken

Still wearing the suit, Rhodey flies to an Air Force base.

The military seizes it and has Hammer's company load it with weapons. Hammer is thrilled to finally get his hands on a suit.

Part 6: A clue from his father

6-1On top of the donut shop

The next morning, Tony, still in his suit, is sitting inside the giant donut on the roof of a Los Angeles donut shop.

Nick Fury appears and calls him inside. Natalie is waiting there. Her real name is Natasha Romanoff, a spy for Fury's agency, S.H.I.E.L.D., sent in to watch Tony.

6-2His father and S.H.I.E.L.D.

On Fury's orders, Natasha injects Tony in the neck with lithium dioxide, which eases the poison's effects for a while.

Fury reveals that Howard was one of the founders of S.H.I.E.L.D. He explains that Anton Vanko built the reactor with Howard but tried to sell it for profit, so Howard had him deported.

6-3Under watch at home

Fury gives Tony a box of material Howard left behind. S.H.I.E.L.D. agent Coulson arrives to make sure Tony stays home.

Grudgingly, Tony starts going through his father's things in his wrecked house.

6-4The old film

In the box is film of Howard preparing for the Expo years ago.

At first he seems as cold as Tony remembers. But at the end, Howard speaks to young Tony: the technology of his day held him back, Tony will be able to finish what he couldn't, and Tony is his greatest creation. Tony, who always believed his father didn't love him, is moved.

Part 7: A new element

7-1The diagram in the model

Tony has the model of the 1974 Stark Expo grounds, which appears in his father's material, brought to the house.

With J.A.R.V.I.S.'s help, he turns it into a 3D image and sees that the layout of the grounds is the structure of a new element no one has ever made. His father, unable to make it back then, left it for his son.

7-2A machine in the house

Tony breaks through the walls of his home and builds a large particle accelerator himself, even using an old round shield he finds to prop part of it up.

Its beam creates the new element, and Tony puts it into a new triangular reactor in his chest. His toxicity drops, and he is saved.

7-3A call from Ivan

Then Ivan calls. He's alive, and he means to take Tony on again.

Coulson, his minder, has meanwhile been sent on another mission in New Mexico. Realizing Ivan is planning something at the Expo, Tony puts on a new suit and flies there.

Part 8: The battle at the Expo

8-1Hammer's showcase

At the Expo, Hammer unveils rows of drones built for the Army, Navy, Air Force and Marines.

Last comes War Machine, a gray suit loaded with weapons. Rhodey is inside.

8-2Hijacked

Just as Tony lands and tries to warn Rhodey about Ivan, Ivan takes remote control of all the drones and War Machine.

The drones open fire on Iron Man, and Rhodey's suit attacks Tony against his will. Tony flies up and away to keep the crowd out of it, with the drones in pursuit.

8-3Hammer cornered

Natasha and the others confront Hammer at the Expo and work out that Ivan, hidden at Hammer's company, is behind it all. Hammer is arrested for breaking Ivan out of prison.

Natasha and Happy drive to Hammer's facility.

8-4Natasha and Happy

At the facility, Natasha takes down guard after guard as she moves in. Happy slugs it out with one guard and finally beats him.

Ivan has already left in a powerful suit of his own. Natasha gets into the computers and gives Rhodey back control of his suit.

8-5Fighting side by side

Free again, Rhodey fights alongside Tony. Inside a Japanese garden pavilion at the Expo, they gun down the drones surrounding them.

Then Ivan appears in a large armored suit with whips. He is strong, and both of them struggle.

8-6Crossing the beams

Tony and Rhodey fire their repulsor beams at each other, and the collision blows Ivan away. His suit is wrecked and he goes down.

But Ivan's suit and the remaining drones are rigged to explode. Ivan blows up his suit to take them with him and dies, and the drones explode one after another.

8-7A kiss on the rooftop

Pepper is standing near a drone that hasn't exploded yet. Tony scoops her up just in time and sets her down on a nearby rooftop.

Worn out by the job and by Tony hiding that he was dying, Pepper says she quits as CEO. In the middle of their argument, they kiss.

Part 9: Afterward

9-1Fury's assessment

Fury shows Tony the assessment Natasha wrote: Iron Man is a good fit for the hero program, the Avengers Initiative, but Tony Stark himself is not recommended because of his personality.

Even so, Fury asks Tony to help as a consultant.

9-2Medals

In Washington, Tony and Rhodey receive medals from Senator Stern, who can't stand Tony and pins them on grudgingly.

9-3After the credits

The New Mexico desert. Coulson arrives by car and calls Fury: "Sir, we've found it."

In the middle of a crater, a strange hammer is stuck in the ground. (This leads into Thor.)

Summary

Tony's poisoning and the cure

Tony was dying from the palladium in his chest reactor but told no one (Parts 1 and 2). Reckless, he handed the company to Pepper, raced at Monaco and got drunk and destructive at his birthday party (Parts 2, 3 and 5).

Helped by Fury and Natasha, he found a new element in the film and model his father Howard left behind, made it himself and stopped the poisoning (Parts 6 and 7).

What Ivan Vanko did

Blaming Howard for sending his father Anton away, he built a reactor and electric whips from his father's blueprints and attacked Tony at Monaco (Parts 1 and 3).

After Hammer broke him out of prison, he pretended to build drones for Hammer, then hijacked the drones and Rhodey's suit at the Expo to kill Tony (Parts 4 and 8).

Hammer and Rhodey

Hammer, Tony's rival, was humiliated at the hearing (Part 2). He broke Ivan out of prison to build drones and armed Rhodey's suit, but Ivan took it all over at the Expo, and Hammer ended up arrested (Parts 4, 5 and 8).

Rhodey took a suit and gave it to the military to stop the out-of-control Tony (Part 5), but in the end fought beside him and received a medal with him (Parts 8 and 9).

Who died (in order)

- Anton Vanko (Part 1): dies in his Moscow apartment, which sets his son Ivan against Tony.

- Ivan Vanko (Part 8): after Tony and Rhodey defeat him at the Expo, he sets off the bomb in his own suit and dies, hoping to take them with him.

- Tony (Parts 2 to 7): not dead. The palladium was killing him, but the new element saves him.

My takeOpinion

What I like is that a grudge between two fathers becomes a fight between their sons. Tony and Vanko are both geniuses who can build an arc reactor; seen that way, the only difference between them is where they grew up.
