Key terms

The multiverse
The many worlds beyond our own.
The Darkhold
An extremely dangerous book of magic that corrupts those who use it.
The Book of Vishanti
A book of good magic that can counter the Darkhold.
Dreamwalking
A spell that sends your mind into the body of your variant in another world.
Incursion
When two universes collide and both are destroyed.
The Illuminati
A secret council of heroes in another universe.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why does Wanda want America's power?

To be with her children in another world. The Darkhold had corrupted her.

Why does Strange get a third eye?

It's a side effect of the dark magic he used. Where it leads hasn't been shown yet.

Who's in the Illuminati?

Heroes from another world, including some familiar faces from other films.
