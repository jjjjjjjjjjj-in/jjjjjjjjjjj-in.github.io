Part 1: A dream and a wedding

1-1A fight between worlds

In the strange space between universes, a different Strange, his hair tied back, runs with a young girl toward a glowing book, the Book of Vishanti, while a demon chases them.

Deciding they can't escape, Strange tries to take the girl's power for himself. At that moment the demon strikes and mortally wounds him.

Terrified, the girl accidentally opens a star-shaped portal and falls into another universe, taking Strange's body with her.

1-2Waking up, and a wedding

In the New York Sanctum, Strange (the Strange of our world) jolts awake. It seemed like a dream.

It's the wedding day of his ex, Dr. Christine Palmer. At the church he talks with his old colleague Dr. West, who was also gone for five years in the Blip.

At the reception, Strange apologizes to Christine for how he used to act and congratulates her husband Charlie, who turns out to be a fan of his. When Christine asks, "Are you happy?", Strange says yes.

1-3The invisible monster

Hearing screams outside, Strange leaves the reception. Something invisible is tearing through the city and has grabbed a girl.

Strange makes it visible: a giant one-eyed creature with tentacles, Gargantos. The girl it caught is the girl from his dream.

Strange is overpowered, but Wong arrives. Together they kill the creature and save the girl.

1-4America Chavez

The girl introduces herself as America Chavez. She can travel from universe to universe, but she can't do it on purpose; her power only kicks in when she's in danger and scared.

She says all kinds of creatures are hunting her for that power. Strange tells her they have some experience with the multiverse, from a recent business with Spider-Man.

1-5The other Strange's body

America leads them to the corpse of the Strange who fell with her. What Strange saw in his dream really happened, in another universe.

She explains that she and that Strange were looking for the Book of Vishanti, but he turned on her and tried to take her power. Strange buries his other self.

1-6A witch's runes

Strange and Wong take America to Kamar-Taj, the sorcerers' training ground, to keep her safe.

Studying the runes on the creature, they find they aren't sorcery but witchcraft. Strange decides to go and ask Wanda, who knows that kind of magic, for help.

Part 2: The Scarlet Witch

2-1Wanda in the orchard

Since what happened in Westview, Wanda has been living quietly on her own. Strange finds her in an orchard, says he hasn't come about Westview, and asks for help protecting a girl.

But Wanda lets slip America's name, which Strange never told her. Wanda is the one who sent the monsters.

2-2The Darkhold and Wanda's wish

Wanda has been reading the Darkhold, a dangerous book of magic she got in Westview, and has become the Scarlet Witch. Through it she learned that in other universes, her sons Billy and Tommy are real and alive.

She wants America's power so she can go to a world where her children are. She demands that Strange hand the girl over.

The orchard was an illusion of her magic. Strange tries to tell her the children aren't real, and refuses to give her America.

2-3Defending Kamar-Taj

Strange returns to Kamar-Taj and warns them Wanda is coming. Under Wong's command, the sorcerers wrap the temple in a magic shield.

Hamir, an old one-armed sorcerer, stays by America's side.

Wong explains that, according to prophecy, the Scarlet Witch is destined to either rule or destroy the multiverse.

2-4Wanda attacks

Wanda appears and strikes the sorcerers one after another. She takes over the mind of one of them and brings the shield down from inside.

The sorcerers fight with their lives, and many are killed by Wanda.

2-5The Mirror Dimension

Strange traps Wanda in the Mirror Dimension. But she reaches out through her own reflection and escapes.

2-6America's portal

Wanda grabs America. In her terror, America's power fires on its own and a star-shaped portal opens.

America and Strange are pulled through into another universe. Wong, left behind at Kamar-Taj, is captured by Wanda.

Part 3: Earth-838

3-1Through many worlds

In a matter of seconds, the two fall through world after world: one with giant statues, one drawn like a comic, one with dinosaurs, a black-and-white old New York ruled by Hydra, one made of paint.

Then they land in a New York in a new universe. They later learn it is called Earth-838, and their own is Earth-616.

3-2Pizza Poppa

In the unfamiliar city, America grabs a pizza ball from a street stand. She doesn't pay, and the vendor, Pizza Poppa, confronts Strange.

Strange casts a spell that makes the man keep punching himself in the face, and they walk away.

3-3The memory machine

The city has a machine that plays back your memories. When Strange uses it, it shows a date he once had with Christine.

America uses it too. It shows the day her power first appeared and her two mothers were pulled into a star portal and disappeared. Strange comforts her, telling her they're surely still alive somewhere.

3-4A hero's statue, and Mordo

The two head to this world's Sanctum. In front of it stands a statue of this world's Strange, who is honored as a hero who died fighting Thanos.

Waiting inside is this world's Mordo. In Strange's own world, Mordo is a fellow student turned enemy; here he is the Sorcerer Supreme. He greets them like friends and offers them a drink.

But the drink is drugged. They pass out and are taken prisoner.

3-5Dreamwalking

Back at Kamar-Taj on Earth-616, Wanda uses a Darkhold spell called dreamwalking, which lets her mind take over the body of another version of herself in another world.

She enters the body of the Earth-838 Wanda, who lives a happy, ordinary suburban life with her sons Billy and Tommy.

3-6Sara's sacrifice

While Wanda is dreamwalking, Sara, a surviving sorcerer, seizes the Darkhold and destroys it. She is burned to death by the cursed flames that come out of the book.

The dreamwalk breaks, and Wanda is furious. Holding the lives of the remaining sorcerers over him, she forces Wong to lead her to Mount Wundagore, where the Darkhold's power comes from.

3-7Mount Wundagore

Atop Mount Wundagore is a temple with a throne for the Scarlet Witch. Drawing on the power of this place, where the Darkhold's spells came from, Wanda begins dreamwalking again.

Part 4: The Illuminati

4-1This world's Christine

Strange wakes up a prisoner on Earth-838. Working there is this world's Christine, a scientist at a foundation that studies threats from the multiverse. She is wary of him.

4-2Judged by heroes

Strange is brought before this world's secret council of heroes, the Illuminati: Mordo, Captain Carter (Peggy), Black Bolt, Captain Marvel (Maria Rambeau) and Reed Richards, joined by Professor X (Charles Xavier) in his wheelchair.

They reveal that their Strange used the Darkhold in the war against Thanos and caused an incursion that wiped out another universe. Deeming him too dangerous, they had Black Bolt execute him, then told the world he died a hero.

4-3Strange's warning

Mordo argues that the Strange of Earth-616 is just as dangerous. Xavier defends him as a different man.

Strange warns them that the real danger is Wanda, but the members underestimate her.

4-4Wanda arrives

Wanda, inside the body of the Earth-838 Wanda, shows up at the Illuminati headquarters and tears through the guard robots, the Ultron Sentries.

The members of the Illuminati go out to face her.

4-5The Illuminati fall

Black Bolt's voice can destroy things with a whisper. But Wanda magically erases his mouth. Panicking, he screams inside his own head and the blast destroys his brain.

Reed Richards is torn to shreds by Wanda's magic. Captain Carter is cut in half by her own shield, which Wanda sends back at her.

Captain Marvel holds her own for a while, but Wanda drains her power and drops a huge statue on her, killing her.

4-6Xavier's last attempt

Xavier tells Strange where this world's Strange locked away the Book of Vishanti, and urges him to use it to stop Wanda.

Using telepathy, Xavier enters Wanda's mind to free the Earth-838 Wanda trapped inside. But the Earth-616 Wanda catches him and kills him by twisting his head off.

4-7Fighting Mordo, and escape

Strange, still held prisoner, fights Mordo and defeats him.

America wins over this world's Christine. Strange, America and Christine regroup and flee from Wanda to search for the Book of Vishanti. Of the Illuminati, only Mordo survives.

Part 5: The Book of Vishanti and a dead world

5-1Into the space between worlds

The three enter the space between universes, the same place where the film began. There lies the Book of Vishanti, a book of good magic that is the opposite of the Darkhold.

5-2The book destroyed, America taken

But Wanda catches up and destroys the Book of Vishanti.

She takes control of America's mind and uses her power to throw Strange and Christine into another universe. America is carried off to Mount Wundagore.

5-3The Strange of a dead world

They land in a universe destroyed by an incursion, a collision of two worlds.

In that world's Sanctum lives another Strange, corrupted by the Darkhold, with a third eye open on his forehead. He tells Strange that anyone who uses the Darkhold pays a high price.

5-4The battle of music

The two Stranges fight over this world's Darkhold, hurling musical notes at each other with magic.

In the end Strange throws the other out of a window, and he dies impaled on the fence below.

5-5Into his own dead body

Strange decides to use the Darkhold himself and dreamwalk, into the corpse of the Strange from the opening scene, which he buried on Earth-616.

Christine says every Strange in every world is the same. He admits it, but says America needs him right now. He asks her to protect his body from the souls of the damned while he dreamwalks.

5-6Cloaked in damned souls

On Earth-616, the buried corpse rises with Strange's mind inside it. The souls of the damned swarm him, and he is nearly overwhelmed.

Christine, guarding his body in the dead world, tells him to use the souls instead. He takes control of them, wraps them around himself like a cape, and flies to Mount Wundagore.

Part 6: Showdown at Mount Wundagore

6-1The fight at Wundagore

At Mount Wundagore, Wanda is draining America's power. Strange attacks, and Wong, who had been held captive, joins him; together they hold Wanda down.

6-2America's power

Instead of taking America's power, Strange encourages her to believe in herself and use it.

For the first time, America uses her power on purpose. She faces Wanda on her own and opens a portal into the home of the Earth-838 Wanda.

6-3Through her children's eyes

Inside the house, Billy and Tommy see Wanda choking America and run away terrified, crying to their real mother, the Earth-838 Wanda, to protect them from the witch.

Wanda realizes she has become a monster in her children's eyes and breaks down. The Earth-838 Wanda tells her the boys "will be loved."

6-4Wanda's choice

Wanda lets America, Strange and Wong go.

Then she turns the Darkhold's magic against the Darkhold itself, bringing down the temple on Mount Wundagore and destroying every copy of the Darkhold in every universe. She dies under the collapsing mountain.

6-5Strange and Christine

Back in the dead world after the dreamwalk, Strange talks with this world's Christine. He tells her he loves her in every universe, and admits that what held him back was fear.

America opens a star portal and rescues them. She sends Christine home to Earth-838 and returns with Strange to Earth-616.

6-6A new beginning

At Kamar-Taj, the damaged temple is being rebuilt. America stays and begins training in the mystic arts with Wong and the others.

At the Sanctum, Strange repairs the broken watch Christine once gave him, then puts it away.

6-7A third eye

As Strange walks down the street, he is hit by sudden, sharp pain, and a third eye opens on his forehead: the price of using the Darkhold and dreamwalking into a corpse.

6-8Mid-credits

A mysterious woman, Clea, approaches Strange on the street. She tells him his actions have caused an incursion and that he must help fix it.

She slices open a passage into the Dark Dimension with her weapon. Strange opens his third eye and follows her in.

6-9After the credits

On Earth-838, Pizza Poppa is still punching himself under Strange's spell. At last it wears off, and he declares, "It's over!"

Summary (spoilers through the ending)

Who died (in order)

- The other Strange (Part 1): killed by the demon chasing them just as he tries to take America's power. Strange later dreamwalks into his corpse.

- The sorcerers of Kamar-Taj (Part 2): killed by Wanda while protecting America.

- Sara (Part 3): destroys the Darkhold to break Wanda's dreamwalk and is burned to death by the cursed flames from the book.

- Black Bolt (Part 4): Wanda erases his mouth, and his own voice kills him.

- Reed Richards (Part 4): torn apart by Wanda's magic.

- Captain Carter (Part 4): cut in half by her own shield, sent back at her.

- Captain Marvel (Maria Rambeau) (Part 4): drained of her power and crushed under a statue Wanda drops.

- Professor X (Part 4): caught inside Wanda's mind while trying to free the Earth-838 Wanda, and killed when she twists his head off.

- The Darkhold-corrupted Strange (Part 5): in the fight over the Darkhold, Strange throws him out a window and he is impaled on a fence.

- Wanda (Part 6): realizing what she has done, she brings down Mount Wundagore to destroy every Darkhold in every world, and dies beneath it.

* Before the story begins, the Earth-838 Strange had been executed by Black Bolt on the Illuminati's orders, after his use of the Darkhold destroyed another universe.

What Wanda wanted, and how it ended

- Her goal: to take America's power to cross universes and reach a world where her sons Billy and Tommy are alive (Part 2).

- What she did: sent monsters after America (Part 1), attacked Kamar-Taj (Part 2), dreamwalked into her Earth-838 self and nearly wiped out the Illuminati (Part 4), and destroyed the Book of Vishanti (Part 5).

- The end: seeing the Earth-838 children terrified of her, she recognizes her mistakes, destroys every Darkhold, and dies (Part 6).
