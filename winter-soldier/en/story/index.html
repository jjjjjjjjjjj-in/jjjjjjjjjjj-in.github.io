It works less as a superhero film than as a spy thriller where no one can be trusted. "I'm with you till the end of the line" keeps echoing all the way to Civil War.

Part 1: Steve in the present

1-1Morning run

Two years after the battle of New York. Morning in Washington, around the Reflecting Pool by the Lincoln Memorial.

Steve laps the same runner again and again, calling out "On your left" each time. The runner is Sam Wilson, a former Air Force pararescueman who now counsels veterans.

The two get talking on the grass. Sam asks what it's like coming back after waking up from the ice, and recommends an album. Steve adds it to his list of things to catch up on.

Then Natasha pulls up in a car, and Steve heads off on a mission.

1-2The hijacked ship

The Indian Ocean. Pirates led by Batroc have seized the S.H.I.E.L.D. ship Lemurian Star and taken the crew hostage.

Steve, Natasha and the S.T.R.I.K.E. team, led by Rumlow, drop onto the ship by night. Steve jumps without a parachute and takes out the guards on deck one after another.

The team frees the hostages, including agent Sitwell.

1-3Natasha's other mission

Steve fights Batroc one-on-one below deck. Then he finds that Natasha isn't looking for hostages at all: she's copying data from the ship's computers onto a drive.

In the confusion, Batroc gets away. Natasha had separate orders from Fury, and Steve, kept in the dark, is furious.

1-4Project Insight

At the Triskelion, S.H.I.E.L.D.'s headquarters, Steve confronts Fury. Telling a story about his grandfather, Fury takes him down to a vast hangar.

Inside are three new Helicarriers. Linked to satellites launched from the ship, they are meant to find dangerous people anywhere in the world and kill them before they act.

Steve objects: this isn't freedom, it's fear.

1-5The museum and an aged Peggy

In street clothes, Steve visits the Captain America exhibit at a museum, with displays about his wartime self and about Bucky, his best friend who is listed as killed in action.

Then he visits Peggy, now elderly and bedridden. She regrets that he never got to live the life he deserved. Mid-conversation her memory slips, and she weeps with surprise all over again to see him alive.

1-6Sam's group

Steve drops in on the veterans' support group Sam runs.

Afterward, Sam tells him he flew missions in the Air Force with mechanical wings strapped to his back, and that he watched his wingman Riley get shot down. After that, he got out.

Part 2: Fury is attacked

2-1Data he can't open

Fury tries to open the data Natasha brought back, but the system refuses him. Even the director has been locked out.

Suspicious, he meets Alexander Pierce, a senior official on the World Security Council and the man who made him director, and asks him to delay Project Insight.

2-2Ambushed in the streets

On his way to meet Maria Hill, Fury's car is boxed in and shot up by men posing as police. His armored SUV keeps him alive as he tries to break away.

Then a masked assassin with a metal arm, the Winter Soldier, fires a bomb under the car and flips it. When he walks up, the car is empty, with a hole cut in the road beneath it. Fury has escaped underground.

2-3Steve's apartment

Back home, Steve chats with his neighbor Kate, a nurse. Inside his apartment, Fury is sitting in the dark.

Worried the room is bugged, Fury talks about nothing out loud while using his phone screen to tell Steve that S.H.I.E.L.D. is compromised.

Then he is shot through the wall from a building across the street. As he collapses, he presses the flash drive into Steve's hand and tells him to trust no one.

2-4The rooftop chase

Kate bursts in with a gun. She is actually a S.H.I.E.L.D. agent Fury assigned to watch over Steve.

Steve crashes through the window and chases the shooter across the rooftops. He hurls his shield; the assassin catches it with his metal arm, throws it back and vanishes.

2-5Fury's death

At the hospital, Fury goes into surgery and is pronounced dead. Natasha is in tears.

Steve quietly hides the drive inside a vending machine in the hospital corridor.

2-6The elevator fight

Pierce summons Steve and shows him evidence that Fury hired Batroc to hijack the ship. Pierce asks what Fury's last words were. Steve won't tell him.

On the way out, Rumlow and other S.T.R.I.K.E. agents keep filling his glass elevator. Sensing the trap, Steve asks, "Before we get started, does anyone want to get out?" and then fights them all in the cramped space.

With the elevator locked, he smashes through the glass, drops to a lower floor and escapes on his motorcycle, flinging the bike into a pursuing Quinjet to bring it down. He is now a fugitive.

Part 3: The enemy within

3-1Teaming up with Natasha

Back at the hospital, the drive is gone from the vending machine. Natasha has it.

At a shopping mall, they plug it into a store computer and trace the data to a location in New Jersey. To slip past Rumlow's men, they fake a kiss on the escalator.

3-2The Odessa story

On the drive, Natasha tells Steve about the Winter Soldier. Five years ago, he went after an engineer she was protecting near Odessa and shot him straight through her. She still has the scar.

Most people in the spy world, she says, think he's only a ghost story.

3-3Beneath the old camp

It's Camp Lehigh, where Steve trained during the war. He spots a building that shouldn't be there.

A hidden elevator takes them down to an old room-sized computer that holds the mind of Arnim Zola, the former Hydra scientist, preserved as data.

3-4Hydra's secret

Zola explains that after the war, S.H.I.E.L.D. took him on as a scientist, and from inside it he quietly rebuilt Hydra.

For decades Hydra stirred up crises so that people would give up their freedom for security, and it removed anyone in the way, using the Winter Soldier among others, staging deaths to look like accidents. Among the images on screen is a newspaper reporting the deaths of Howard and Maria Stark.

3-5The missile

While Zola keeps them talking, a S.H.I.E.L.D. missile comes in. Steve shields Natasha and drops into a pit under the floor, and they survive the blast that levels the building.

The strike tells them Pierce is the head of Hydra inside S.H.I.E.L.D.

3-6Pierce's house

The Winter Soldier is waiting at Pierce's house. When Pierce's housekeeper, Renata, sees him, Pierce shoots her dead without hesitation.

Pierce gives the Winter Soldier his next targets.

3-7Sam's house

Steve and Natasha turn up at Sam's door, and he takes them in and makes breakfast.

Sam shows them the file on the winged rig he used to fly, the Falcon, and offers to help. It's kept on a heavily guarded base, and they get it out.

Part 4: The Winter Soldier

4-1Breaking Sitwell

Suspecting senior agent Jasper Sitwell, the three grab him on a rooftop. Kicked off the edge and caught in midair by Sam on his wings, Sitwell talks.

Zola built an algorithm that sifts people's records to pick out anyone likely to threaten Hydra in the future. The Insight Helicarriers will shoot millions of those people from the sky at once.

The targets he names include a TV anchor, a high school student in Iowa, Bruce Banner and Stephen Strange.

4-2Attack on the highway

As they drive with Sitwell, the Winter Soldier lands on the roof of the car. He drags Sitwell out through the window and hurls him into oncoming traffic, killing him.

Then he rips out the steering wheel with his metal arm. The three of them ride a torn-off car door down the road and make it out.

4-3The street battle

The Winter Soldier and his team come at them with guns and explosives, and the fight spreads from the highway into the city streets. Natasha is shot in the shoulder.

Sam takes on the gunmen from the air while Steve battles the Winter Soldier with his shield.

4-4Behind the mask

In the fight, the assassin's mask comes off. It's Bucky, the best friend Steve thought died seventy years ago when he fell from a train.

When Steve says his name, Bucky doesn't know him: "Who the hell is Bucky?" Kept alive by Zola's experiments, he has been brainwashed again and again, frozen between missions and used as Hydra's assassin.

4-5Captured

S.T.R.I.K.E. arrives, and Steve, Natasha and Sam are arrested and loaded into a transport.

Inside, one of the guards takes off her helmet: it's Maria Hill. She takes out the guard and gets the three of them out.

4-6Fury lives

At the hideout Hill takes them to, Fury is waiting, alive. He faked his death with a drug that slows the heart almost to a stop.

Fury explains that if they swap new chips into the three Helicarriers, they can change what the ships fire at. Steve says S.H.I.E.L.D. has to go too, not just Hydra, and Fury goes along with it.

4-7Wiped again

Meanwhile, in a Hydra lab, Bucky sits and says he knew the man on the bridge. He is beginning to remember Steve.

Pierce strikes him and orders his mind wiped. Bucky is strapped into the machine and wiped once more.

Part 5: The battle of the Helicarriers

5-1The old uniform

Steve takes his wartime uniform from the museum exhibit, and Sam straps on his wings.

On launch day, members of the World Security Council gather at the Triskelion.

5-2A message to everyone

Steve takes over the building's PA and tells every employee that Hydra has taken over S.H.I.E.L.D., asking them to stand with him.

In the control room, Rumlow holds a technician at gunpoint to force the launch. Sharon, who was "Kate," pulls her gun on him, and the building splits into a gunfight between Hydra and loyal agents. The Helicarriers lift off.

5-3Pierce's room

In the council chamber, Natasha, disguised as one of the council members, disarms Pierce. Then Fury walks in.

Using a scan of his other eye, he forces the database open, and Natasha dumps all of S.H.I.E.L.D.'s and Hydra's secrets onto the internet, including her own past.

After a struggle, Fury shoots Pierce dead.

5-4Two down

Steve and Sam storm the airborne Helicarriers and swap in the new chips one by one.

The first two go fine. On the third, the Winter Soldier is waiting. He wrecks Sam's wings, and Sam is forced back to the ground.

5-5The third carrier

On the third carrier, Steve and Bucky fight hard. Bucky shoots Steve more than once, but Steve gets the chip in.

Hill gives the command, and instead of Hydra's targets, the three Helicarriers fire on each other and start to break apart.

5-6To the end of the line

In the collapsing ship, Steve frees Bucky from under fallen beams. He drops his shield and says he won't fight: Bucky is his friend.

Bucky keeps hitting him, insisting Steve is his mission. Steve tells him to finish it, then, "'cause I'm with you till the end of the line" — the words Bucky once said to him. Bucky's fist stops.

The ship gives way and Steve falls into the Potomac. Bucky goes in after him, drags him out, leaves him unconscious on the bank and walks off into the woods.

5-7The fall of the Triskelion

A Helicarrier crashes into the Triskelion. Rumlow, who was fighting Sam inside, is badly hurt when the building comes down on him.

Fury and Natasha escape from the roof by helicopter and pick up Sam.

Part 6: Afterward

6-1The hospital bed

Steve wakes up in a hospital bed with Sam beside him, and greets him the way he did the morning they met: "On your left."

6-2Testifying before Congress

S.H.I.E.L.D. is finished. Natasha appears before a Senate subcommittee, which wants to blame them, and she answers back that the world still needs them, then walks out.

6-3Fury's grave

At Fury's grave, Fury, still officially dead, says he is going to Europe to hunt down the rest of Hydra.

Natasha hands Steve a file on Bucky, and Steve and Sam decide to go looking for him.

6-4Mid-credits

In a secret Hydra lab, Baron Strucker and his scientists study a glowing scepter (the one Loki used in The Avengers).

Held there are twins given special powers by experiments: the brother moves at incredible speed, and the sister moves things without touching them.

6-5After the credits

At the museum's Captain America exhibit, Bucky stands alone, looking at his own photo and story.

Summary (spoilers through the ending)

Hydra's plan

After the war, Hydra survived inside S.H.I.E.L.D. through Zola and grew for decades (Part 3). Its leader inside S.H.I.E.L.D. was Pierce (Part 3).

Project Insight would use three Helicarriers to shoot, all at once, millions of people that Zola's algorithm marked as future threats to Hydra (Part 4).

Steve and Sam swapped the chips on all three, and the ships destroyed each other. Natasha leaked every secret, and S.H.I.E.L.D. fell along with Hydra (Parts 5–6).

About Bucky

After his fall from the train, Bucky survived thanks to Zola's experiments and was used as Hydra's assassin, the Winter Soldier, his memory wiped again and again (Part 4).

He attacked Fury (Part 2), killed Sitwell and fought Steve's team (Part 4). When he began to remember Steve, he was wiped again (Part 4).

In the end he couldn't kill Steve, who refused to fight; he pulled him from the river and left (Part 5). Then he goes to the museum to look at his own past (Part 6).

Who died or was hurt (in order)

- Fury (Part 2): not killed. Shot through the wall by the Winter Soldier and declared dead in surgery, he had faked his death with a drug that slows the heart (revealed in Part 4).

- Natasha (story told in Part 3): five years ago the Winter Soldier shot through her, and she survived. She is shot in the shoulder in Part 4.

- Renata (Part 3): Pierce's housekeeper. Pierce shoots her dead because she saw the Winter Soldier.

- Sitwell (Part 4): after revealing Hydra's plan, he is dragged out of the car window by the Winter Soldier and thrown into traffic.

- Pierce (Part 5): after his secrets are leaked, Fury shoots him dead following a struggle.

- Rumlow (Part 5): badly hurt when a Helicarrier crashes into the Triskelion and the building collapses on him.

- Steve (Part 5): shot by Bucky and knocked out falling into the river; Bucky pulls him out, and he wakes up in hospital (Part 6).
