StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

1. Steve in the present

In Washington, Steve carries out S.H.I.E.L.D. missions while trying to fit into modern life. On a run, he meets former soldier Sam.

2. Doubts about Insight

Fury shows Steve Project Insight, designed to strike potential threats before they act. Steve objects that it takes away freedom.

3. Fury is attacked

Fury is attacked by the mysterious Winter Soldier and others, and collapses in Steve's apartment, warning him to trust no one.

4. On the run

When Steve holds back what he knows from S.H.I.E.L.D. official Pierce, S.H.I.E.L.D. turns on him. He follows the clues with Natasha.

5. Hydra revealed

At an old base, a computer holding Dr. Zola's mind reveals that Hydra has been hiding and growing inside S.H.I.E.L.D. for years. Insight is meant to wipe out everyone who stands in Hydra's way.

6. Behind the mask

Sam joins them. During a street battle with the Winter Soldier, his mask comes off. He is Bucky, the best friend Steve thought was dead.

7. Stop the carriers

With Fury revealed to be alive, they set out to stop the three carriers as they launch. Natasha releases S.H.I.E.L.D.'s and Hydra's secrets to the world.

8. Fighting his best friend

On the carrier, Steve fights Bucky, then stops and tells him he's with him to the end. The carriers fall, and Bucky pulls Steve out of the river before disappearing.

Key terms

The Winter Soldier
An assassin who has carried out killings for decades without anyone knowing who he is. He has a metal arm.
Project Insight
A S.H.I.E.L.D. plan to use three huge flying carriers to strike anyone deemed a future threat before they act.
Hydra
The secret organization from The First Avenger. It survived by growing inside S.H.I.E.L.D.
S.T.R.I.K.E.
The S.H.I.E.L.D. strike team led by Rumlow.
The Falcon
The mechanical wings Sam wears to fly.
The Triskelion
S.H.I.E.L.D.'s headquarters building in Washington, D.C.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why is the Winter Soldier Bucky?

Bucky survived his fall from the train in The First Avenger. Hydra captured him, wiped his memory and turned him into an assassin.

How did Hydra end up inside S.H.I.E.L.D.?

After the war, some of the scientists who joined S.H.I.E.L.D. were Hydra, and they spent decades quietly recruiting.

Wasn't Fury dead?

He used a drug that slowed his heart to fake his death, because he didn't know who he could trust.

Why was Dr. Zola inside a computer?

Before his body gave out, he transferred his mind into a computer.

Where did Bucky go?

He vanishes after saving Steve. After the credits he visits a museum exhibit about himself, as if his memories are starting to return.

Easy-to-miss details

"On your left"

What Steve says as he laps Sam on a run. It becomes a running line between the two in later films.

The elevator fight

The fight in an elevator packed with enemies is one of the film's best-known scenes.

An older Peggy

Steve visits a much older Peggy. It's a bittersweet reminder of the time that separates them.

Stan Lee's cameo

Marvel Comics legend Stan Lee appears as a museum security guard.
