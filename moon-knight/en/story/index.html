Episode 1: The Goldfish Problem

1-1Harrow and the glass

The story opens on a man named Arthur Harrow. He wraps a glass in cloth, smashes it, pours the shards into his sandals, then puts them on and walks away. He has a tattoo of a set of scales on his arm.

1-2Steven's nights

London. Steven Grant is a timid clerk in a museum gift shop. He knows a lot about ancient Egypt, but when he falls asleep he loses time, and his body seems to wander without him.

So before bed he chains his ankle to the bed, scatters sand around it, and tapes the door, to check whether he left in the night. Sometimes he simply tries not to sleep at all.

He keeps a one-finned goldfish named Gus, and he phones his mother every day.

1-3At the museum

Steven dreams of becoming a tour guide at the museum, but his manager, Donna, keeps scolding him for being late. Outside, he chats about Egypt with Crawley, a street performer who poses as a gold-painted living statue.

1-4Waking in the Alps

That night Steven falls asleep. He comes to in a field near a village in the Austrian Alps, holding a golden scarab.

In the village, Harrow and his followers are judging the villagers one by one. The scales tattooed on his arm swing to decide whether a person will do evil. An old woman judged guilty dies at Harrow's hand.

Harrow asks Steven to give back the scarab. Steven tries, but a voice in his head tells him not to. When Harrow tries to judge Steven, the scales swing wildly and can't reach an answer.

1-5The cupcake-truck escape

Steven runs, pursued by Harrow's followers. He keeps blacking out, and each time he comes to, the men chasing him are lying bloodied. He has no idea what he did.

He flees down a mountain road in a cupcake delivery truck, chased by the followers' cars. The voice in his head helps him, but he blacks out again.

Next thing he knows, he is back in his own bed in London.

1-6Two lost days

Back home, Steven notices things in his flat are off. Gus, his one-finned goldfish, now has two fins, and the pet shop insists it never sold him a one-finned fish.

He goes to a restaurant for a dinner date and even orders a steak, but his date never comes. When he calls, she angrily tells him the date was days ago: two days have passed since he fell asleep.

Hidden in his flat he finds a phone he's never seen and a key to a storage unit. The phone is full of missed calls from a woman named Layla. While he's looking, Layla calls, and she calls him "Marc."

1-7Harrow comes to the museum

The next day Harrow shows up at the museum. He explains that he serves the Egyptian goddess Ammit, who judges people's hearts before they commit evil.

Some of the museum's guards and visitors turn out to be his followers. Steven gets away from him.

To make up for being late, Steven has to stay alone in the closed museum that night to work.

1-8The man in the mirror

In the museum at night, a jackal-like monster summoned by Harrow attacks Steven. It's almost invisible.

Steven flees into the restroom and is cornered. Then his reflection in the mirror moves on its own and speaks to him, asking to take over their body.

Steven agrees. His body becomes a warrior in a white cloak and armor, who beats the jackal to death.

Episode 2: Summon the Suit

2-1Fired

The next day the museum reviews the wrecked restroom. The security cameras show neither the monster nor the white warrior, only Steven rampaging alone after hours. He's fired for the damage.

2-2The storage unit and Marc

Steven uses the key from his flat to open the storage unit. Inside are a bag with camping gear, a pistol, cash and a passport under another name, plus the scarab from the Alps.

His other self speaks to him from a mirror and introduces himself as Marc Spector: an American mercenary, the avatar of the Egyptian moon god Khonshu, and another identity living in the same body.

Marc asks for control of the body. Steven says he'll take it all to the police and see a doctor. Then the towering, bird-skulled Khonshu appears, and Steven flees in terror.

2-3Layla

A woman named Layla El-Faouly finds Steven. She is Marc's wife, and she had no idea that another identity named Steven lived inside him.

Steven didn't even know he was married. The two go to his flat to talk.

2-4Taken by the police

Two police officers, Bobbi and Billy, come to the flat and arrest Steven for stealing the scarab. But they are Harrow's followers.

They take Steven to the place where Harrow's cult gathers.

2-5Harrow explains

Harrow reveals that he was once Khonshu's avatar too, but turned against Khonshu's ways and chose to serve Ammit instead.

He wants to use the scarab to find Ammit's tomb and bring her back. Once she wakes, she will wipe out not only those who have done evil, but those who will. Harrow sees this as ridding the world of evil.

He speaks of the darkness inside Marc and presses Steven to hand over the scarab.

2-6The jackal and Mr. Knight

Layla turns up with the scarab. Furious, Harrow summons another jackal to chase Steven and Layla.

Knocked off a rooftop, Steven summons a suit of his own, different from Marc's battle armor: a gentleman's white three-piece suit and mask. He has become Mr. Knight. Marc is stunned that Steven could call the suit.

But Steven is no fighter, and the jackal overpowers him.

2-7Marc fights

Steven talks it over with Marc and lets him take control. Marc becomes Moon Knight in his armor, fights the jackal in the street and kills it.

But during the fight, the scarab ends up in Harrow's hands.

2-8Khonshu's orders

Khonshu furiously berates Marc for losing the scarab. Marc promises to find Ammit's tomb before Harrow does.

Khonshu threatens to make Layla his next avatar if Marc fails, something Marc refuses to allow.

Khonshu sends him to Egypt. Marc looks out from a Cairo hotel room over a city with the pyramids beyond.

Episode 3: The Friendly Type

3-1Harrow finds the site

In the Egyptian desert, Harrow and his followers use the scarab to locate Ammit's tomb and start digging.

3-2Chasing leads in Cairo

In Cairo, Marc tracks leads on Harrow's whereabouts. But both he and Steven keep losing time. He comes to with Harrow's followers lying defeated, with no idea what happened.

Marc corners one of Harrow's followers to make him talk, but the man refuses and takes his own life. The lead is gone.

3-3The council of the gods

With the lead lost, Khonshu darkens the daytime sky with an eclipse to summon the Ennead, the council of Egyptian gods.

A stone wall in a Cairo alley opens, and Marc walks through to a council chamber inside the Great Pyramid of Giza. Steven is thrilled to be inside it.

There wait the avatars of Hathor, Horus, Isis, Tefnut and Osiris. The gods don't act in the world directly; they speak through their avatars. Osiris's avatar is a man named Selim.

3-4Harrow walks free

Through Marc, Khonshu accuses Harrow of trying to revive Ammit.

Summoned before them, Harrow denies it and tells the gods that Marc is mentally unwell and can't be trusted.

The gods accept Harrow's word and refuse to stop him. Instead, they take issue with how Khonshu uses Marc.

3-5Yatzil's tip

After the council, Hathor's avatar, Yatzil, quietly gives Marc a lead: find the sarcophagus of Senfu, a medjay, an ancient guardian, who knew where Ammit's tomb lay.

3-6Layla and Mogart's estate

Meanwhile, in Cairo, Layla visits a woman she knows who forges passports. Afterward, she finds Marc.

She takes him to the estate of Anton Mogart, a wealthy acquaintance who collects antiquities and owns Senfu's sarcophagus. Mogart is in the middle of practicing an Egyptian horseback sport.

He shows them the sarcophagus. The cloth around it bears a star map that points to the tomb. When Marc touches the cloth, he nearly clashes with Mogart's bodyguard, Bek.

3-7The fight at the estate

Harrow arrives. Drawn by the power of Harrow's cane, Mogart sides with him. Harrow shatters the sarcophagus with a blast from the cane and leaves.

Marc and Layla end up fighting Mogart's men. As Moon Knight, Marc tears through them in the horse arena, brutally enough to horrify Steven. He kills Mogart by running him through the chest with a spear.

Layla gathers the cloth fragments from the wreckage, and the two escape into the desert.

3-8The star map

Night in the desert. Marc can't fit the fragments together, so he reluctantly lets Steven take over. Steven, who knows Egypt so well, quickly assembles them into a star map.

But the map shows the sky of two thousand years ago. The stars don't match today's sky, so the tomb can't be found.

3-9Khonshu turns back the sky

Khonshu uses his power to turn the night sky back to the night on the map. The stars line up, and Steven and Layla learn where the tomb is.

But the gods had forbidden Khonshu to act on his own. The other gods imprison him inside an ushabti, a small stone figure.

Without Khonshu's power, Steven and Marc's body collapses unconscious on the spot.

Episode 4: The Tomb

4-1Layla and the desert camp

Still without Khonshu's power, Steven and Layla head to the spot on the map and find Harrow's camp, deserted. They take supplies from it.

When they find another way into the tomb, Steven and Layla kiss, which angers Marc inside their shared body.

4-2Mummies in the tomb

Inside, Ammit's tomb is a maze shaped like the Eye of Horus. They find Harrow's followers dead, among them Billy, who posed as a police officer.

They were killed by the tomb's guardians, the undead Heka priests. The mummy-like priests rise and attack, and the two are separated. Layla fights the priests and defeats them.

4-3Harrow tells Layla

Layla runs into Harrow in the tomb. He tells her that Marc was among the mercenaries who murdered her father, the archaeologist Abdallah El-Faouly.

4-4Alexander the Great's coffin

Steven reaches the central chamber, where a coffin bears Macedonian writing. He realizes Ammit's last avatar was Alexander the Great.

Reaching down the throat of Alexander's mummy, he pulls out the ushabti, the stone figure imprisoning Ammit.

4-5Marc's confession

Layla arrives and confronts Marc, who has taken over, about her father.

Marc confesses: it was his mercenary partner, Bushman, who killed her father. Marc tried to stop him but was shot and left to die. Khonshu saved him and made him his avatar.

4-6Shot by Harrow

Harrow and his followers arrive. Marc takes down several of them, but Harrow grabs a follower's revolver and shoots Marc twice in the chest. Marc falls, and Harrow takes the ushabti.

4-7The psychiatric hospital

Marc wakes up in a psychiatric hospital. Among the patients are people he and Steven know, such as Crawley and Donna. Marc is groggy, as if drugged.

In an office, Harrow listens to Marc as his doctor. He suggests that the Egyptian gods and Moon Knight are all in Marc's head.

4-8Two sarcophagi and a hippo

Marc escapes the office. The film Tomb Buster is playing somewhere in the hospital.

In one room, Marc finds two sarcophagi. Inside the first is Steven, in a body separate from Marc's. For the first time, they see each other with their own eyes, and they embrace.

Someone else is trapped in the second sarcophagus, struggling, but they leave without opening it.

Then a figure with a hippopotamus's head appears. Marc and Steven scream.

Episode 5: Asylum

5-1Taweret and the boat of the dead

The hippo-headed woman is the Egyptian goddess Taweret; Steven recognizes her.

She tells Marc and Steven they are dead. The "hospital" is really a boat sailing across the sands of the Duat, the Egyptian afterlife.

5-2The scales of the heart

Taweret places their hearts on the Scales of Justice to weigh whether they may enter the Field of Reeds, the paradise of the afterlife. The scales won't balance, because memories are still hidden in their hearts.

She advises them to go through the rooms of the hospital and face those memories together.

5-3Between two worlds

Marc keeps shifting between the boat and Dr. Harrow's office. In the office, Harrow calls a talking hippo goddess nonsense and tries to calm him.

Marc lashes out in the office, is sedated by orderlies, and finds himself back on the boat.

5-4Randall

Steven sees memories from Marc's childhood. Marc had a younger brother, Randall. They both loved the movie Tomb Buster.

One day the brothers went to play in a cave near home. Heavy rain flooded it, and Randall drowned.

Their mother, Wendy, blamed Marc for Randall's death. Lost in grief and drinking, she abused him with words and violence. As a teenager, Marc left home, though his father, Elias, tried to talk him into staying.

5-5Bushman and Khonshu's temple

Marc shows Steven how he became Khonshu's avatar.

As an adult, Marc joined the military, then worked as a mercenary with his former commanding officer, Bushman. On a job at an Egyptian dig site, Bushman, consumed by greed, gunned down the archaeologists, Layla's father Abdallah among them. Marc tried to stop him and was shot.

Dying, Marc dragged himself to a nearby temple of Khonshu and tried to end his own life. Khonshu appeared and offered to save him if he became his avatar. Marc accepted.

5-6Toward the Gates of Osiris

Thinking of Harrow freeing Ammit, the two beg Taweret to let them return to life and stop him. She agrees and steers the boat toward the Gates of Osiris, which lead back to the world of the living.

But they must get there before the gates close.

5-7Why Steven exists

In the office, Dr. Harrow urges Marc to open up to Steven.

Marc shows him the last hidden memory: as a boy who couldn't endure his mother's abuse, Marc unknowingly created Steven to protect his mind from the pain. The name came from the archaeologist hero of Tomb Buster. Steven lived on knowing nothing of their mother's cruelty.

Marc also tells him their mother is dead. The calls Steven made to her every day never really reached her. Marc went home for her shiva but couldn't bring himself to go inside. Steven refuses to believe it at first, then accepts her death, and the two reconcile.

5-8Steven turns to sand

Even so, the scales don't balance. Hostile spirits of the dead attack the boat.

Marc and Steven fight them off, but Steven falls overboard. On the sands of the Duat, he freezes into a figure of sand.

Moments later, the scales balance. Marc finds himself in the Field of Reeds.

Episode 6: Gods and Monsters

6-1Harrow frees Ammit

Layla receives a message from Taweret: free Khonshu, and he can bring Marc back.

Meanwhile, Harrow enters the gods' council chamber in the Great Pyramid and, using Ammit's power, kills the other gods' avatars. Then he frees Ammit from her ushabti, and she chooses him as her avatar.

6-2Layla frees Khonshu

In the pyramid, Layla finds the ushabti imprisoning Khonshu and breaks it. Khonshu asks her to be his avatar, but she refuses.

Khonshu faces the giant Ammit alone and is overpowered.

6-3Marc goes back for Steven

Meanwhile, in the Field of Reeds, Marc refuses to stay in paradise alone and goes back into the Duat to rescue Steven.

He tells the sand-frozen Steven that it was Steven who saved him and let him survive those hard years. Their bond frees Steven from the sand.

With Taweret's help, the two pass through the Gates of Osiris and return to their body.

6-4Bonded again

Khonshu senses Marc and Steven's return and bonds with them once more. His power heals their gunshot wounds and restores their powers as Moon Knight and Mr. Knight.

6-5Layla becomes Taweret's avatar

Layla learns that Ammit can be trapped inside a human body if several gods' avatars work together.

She agrees to become the goddess Taweret's avatar, just for now, and gains the power to fight in a winged suit.

6-6The battle for Cairo

In the streets of Cairo, Harrow, Ammit and their followers begin judging people one after another. Those judged guilty drop dead where they stand.

Moon Knight and Mr. Knight arrive, and the fight with Harrow begins. Marc and Steven switch seamlessly back and forth, fighting in sync.

Above the city, the giant Khonshu clashes with Ammit. Layla saves the people in a van and a young girl.

6-7A blackout

As Ammit's avatar, Harrow is far stronger than before. He uses Marc's trauma to throw him off and overpowers Marc and Steven.

Just as he's about to kill them, they black out. When they come to, Harrow has been brutally beaten. Neither Marc nor Steven knows who did it.

6-8Sealing Ammit

Marc, as Khonshu's avatar, and Layla, as Taweret's, join forces to seal Ammit inside Harrow's body.

Khonshu orders Marc to kill Harrow, and Ammit with him. Marc refuses, and demands that Khonshu release him and Steven from serving as his avatar. Khonshu agrees.

6-9The hospital dream and morning in London

Marc and Steven find themselves again in the hospital office, facing Dr. Harrow. But they reject that world, walk out, and choose to carry on their new life together.

They wake up in Steven's bed in his London flat.

6-10After the credits: Jake Lockley

Harrow, with Ammit sealed inside him, is now in a wheelchair in a psychiatric hospital. Someone comes and takes him out of the hospital to a limousine.

Khonshu appears before Harrow. He is still bonded with a third identity in Marc's body, neither Marc nor Steven: Jake Lockley, who speaks Spanish.

Jake shoots Harrow dead with a silenced pistol.

Summary (spoilers through the finale)

Three identities, one body

- Marc Spector: the original identity. A former soldier turned mercenary, and the moon god Khonshu's avatar (Moon Knight).

- Steven Grant: created by young Marc to shield his mind from his mother's abuse. Works at a London museum; becomes Mr. Knight. Named after the hero of the film Tomb Buster.

- Jake Lockley: a third identity neither Marc nor Steven knew about, who emerged during their blackouts and speaks Spanish. Working with Khonshu, he kills Harrow (Episode 6 mid-credits).

Who died (in order)

- An old woman in the Alpine village (Ep. 1): judged guilty by Harrow's scales and killed by him.

- One of Harrow's followers (Ep. 3): cornered by Marc in Cairo, he takes his own life rather than talk.

- Anton Mogart (Ep. 3): having sided with Harrow, he is speared through the chest by Marc as Moon Knight.

- Billy and other followers of Harrow (Ep. 4): killed by the Heka priests guarding Ammit's tomb.

- Marc and Steven (Ep. 4): shot dead by Harrow in the tomb, they pass into the Duat, and return to life in Episode 6.

- The other gods' avatars (Ep. 6): killed by Harrow with Ammit's power.

- People of Cairo (Ep. 6): those judged guilty by Harrow and Ammit die where they stand.

- Arthur Harrow (Ep. 6 mid-credits): with Ammit sealed inside him, he is shot dead by Jake Lockley, working with Khonshu.

- Before the story: Marc's brother Randall (drowned in a cave), Layla's father Abdallah (shot by Bushman), and their mother Wendy (died about two months before the story).
