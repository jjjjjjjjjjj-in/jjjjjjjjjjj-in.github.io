Episode 1: The Goldfish Problem

Steven's life

Steven Grant, a timid gift-shop clerk at a London museum, loves ancient Egypt. He chains his ankle to the bed so he won't wander at night.

Waking up elsewhere

One morning, Steven wakes up in an Austrian mountain village, clutching a golden scarab.

Arthur Harrow

In the village, a man named Arthur Harrow is judging people's hearts with a set of scales. He demands the scarab.

The voice in the mirror

Back in London, Steven notices a man with his face speaking from the mirror: Marc. When a monster attacks at the museum, Steven lets Marc take over, and Marc transforms into a white-clad hero to defeat it.

Episodes 2–3: Marc and Layla

Who Marc is

Marc Spector is a former soldier and mercenary who serves as the avatar of the moon god Khonshu, punishing evildoers.

Layla

Marc's wife, Layla, visits Steven, who didn't even know he was married.

Mr. Knight

Steven learns to transform too, becoming the white-suited Mr. Knight.

To Egypt

Harrow is searching for the tomb of Ammit, a crocodile-headed goddess who would judge people before they commit any crime. Marc and the others head to Egypt.

The gods' council

Harrow and Marc face the Egyptian gods, who refuse to stop Harrow. Angry, Khonshu rearranges the stars to reveal an ancient night sky, and is imprisoned in stone for it.

Episode 4: The Tomb

Ammit's tomb

Steven and Layla find Ammit's tomb inside a pyramid.

Shot

Inside the tomb, Harrow shoots Marc.

The hospital

Marc wakes up in a psychiatric hospital, where Harrow appears as his doctor. Then the hippo goddess Taweret appears and tells him he's dead.

Episode 5: Asylum

The scales

In the afterlife, their hearts are weighed. They travel through their past memories.

Randall

As a boy, Marc's younger brother Randall drowned in a cave. Their mother blamed Marc and abused him.

How Steven was born

Steven was created to shield Marc's mind from that pain.

Steven's sacrifice

Steven falls into the sands of the afterlife and turns to stone.

Episode 6: Gods and Monsters

Returning together

Marc refuses to leave Steven behind and goes back for him. They return to life together.

Layla transforms

Harrow frees Ammit. Layla becomes Taweret's avatar and fights with new powers.

Battle of the gods

Over Cairo, the giant Khonshu battles Ammit. Marc and Steven fight Harrow, and Ammit is sealed inside his body.

Asking for freedom

Marc and Steven ask Khonshu to release them, and he agrees.

After the credits

Harrow is taken from a hospital and brought before Khonshu. He's shot by a third identity, neither Marc nor Steven: Jake Lockley.
