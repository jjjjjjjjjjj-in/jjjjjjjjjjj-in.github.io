Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

Mostly standalone

You can enjoy it without having seen other MCU titles.

Egyptian gods

Gods from Egyptian mythology appear as real beings.

Two people, one body

The hero has dissociative identity disorder, with multiple identities sharing one body.

Stay through the credits

There's an important scene after the finale.

Key terms

Khonshu
The Egyptian moon god, who empowers Marc to fight as his avatar.
Ammit
A crocodile-headed goddess who would judge people for crimes they haven't yet committed.
The scarab
A small golden beetle that points the way to Ammit's tomb.
The Duat
The afterlife of Egyptian mythology.
Taweret
A hippo-headed goddess who guides the souls of the dead.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

How are Steven and Marc connected?

They're separate identities in the same body. Steven was created within Marc to shield him from childhood trauma.

Is the psychiatric hospital real?

It seems to be a blend of the afterlife and Marc's mind while he's near death. How much is real is left open.

Who is the man after the credits?

A third identity, Jake Lockley.

Easy-to-miss details

The ankle chain

Steven chains his ankle to the bed so he won't wander at night.

Mr. Knight

When Steven transforms, he becomes the white-suited Mr. Knight.

Gus the goldfish
