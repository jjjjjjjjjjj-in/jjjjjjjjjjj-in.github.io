Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

It's set in 1995

It came out in 2019, but the story takes place in 1995, with a young Fury and Coulson. In story order it comes right after Captain America: The First Avenger.

The hero has lost her memory

At first she doesn't know who she is. You discover the truth along with her, piece by piece.

Two powers in space

The Kree empire is at war with the Skrulls, who can change their shape. Which side is in the right is a big part of the story.

There are scenes during and after the credits

The mid-credits scene leads into Avengers: Endgame. Watch to the very end.

StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

1. Vers, a Kree warrior

In the Kree empire, Vers trains as a warrior under her commander, Yon-Rogg. She has no memory of her past and is haunted by dreams of a woman she doesn't know.

2. Captured by the Skrulls

On a mission, Vers is captured by the Skrulls, who search her memories. She escapes and crashes onto Earth, in Los Angeles, in 1995.

3. Meeting Fury

S.H.I.E.L.D. agent Nick Fury comes to investigate. When he learns shape-shifting Skrulls are hiding on Earth, he teams up with Vers.

4. Who is she?

Following clues from her memories, they discover she was once Air Force pilot Carol Danvers. She was believed to have died six years earlier in a crash with her boss, Dr. Lawson.

5. Maria and the truth

Carol reunites with her best friend Maria. Talos the Skrull arrives and explains the Skrulls are refugees who lost their home. The crash recording reveals Dr. Lawson was a Kree scientist building the engine to end the war, and that Yon-Rogg shot her.

6. What really happened

When Carol destroyed the engine, she absorbed its energy and gained her powers. The Kree took her, memory gone, and raised her as one of their warriors.

7. Her true power

In a hidden lab in space, Carol breaks the device the Kree used to hold back her powers and unleashes her full strength. Alone, she drives off the Kree fleet sent to attack Earth.

8. A farewell, and the start of the Avengers

Carol leaves to help the Skrulls find a new home and gives Fury a pager. Fury names his new initiative after her old Air Force call sign, "Avenger."

Key terms

The Kree empire
A large empire in space, ruled by an AI called the Supreme Intelligence. Vers was raised as one of its warriors.
The Skrulls
Aliens who can change into anyone. They've been at war with the Kree for a long time.
Starforce
The elite Kree team Vers belongs to, led by Yon-Rogg.
The Supreme Intelligence
The AI that rules the Kree. It appears to each person as the one they admire most.
Project Pegasus
A secret program run by the U.S. Air Force and Dr. Lawson to build an engine that could travel at the speed of light.
The Tesseract
The blue cube from Captain America: The First Avenger. Dr. Lawson was using its power to run her engine.
Flerken
Looks like a cat, but is actually a different creature with tentacles inside its mouth. That's what Goose really is.
Pager
A small device from the 1990s for receiving short messages. Carol gives Fury one at the end.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why was she called Vers?

Her dog tag was broken in the crash, and only part of "Carol Danvers" was left on it. The Kree used that as her name.

Aren't the Skrulls the bad guys?

In this film the Skrulls are refugees who have lost their home. The Kree were the ones attacking them, so Carol ends up helping the Skrulls.

Why does the Supreme Intelligence look like Dr. Lawson?

It appears as the person you admire most. Even without her memories, Carol still held Dr. Lawson close.

Why does Fury have both eyes?

This is before he loses one. At the end you find out it was Goose the cat who scratched it.

Why doesn't Carol come back to Earth for years?

She went far into space to help the Skrulls find a new home. She left Fury a pager so he could call her in a real emergency.

Why is Coulson here?

In 1995 Coulson is a new S.H.I.E.L.D. agent. It's a younger version of the Coulson from Iron Man and later films.

Easy-to-miss details

Where the name "Avengers" comes from

Fury gets the name for his initiative from Carol's fighter-jet call sign, "Avenger."

1990s details

Video rental stores, pagers and painfully slow computers pop up throughout to set the 1995 mood.

Maria's daughter Monica

Maria's daughter Monica appears as an adult in WandaVision and The Marvels.

Talos and Fury's story continues

Talos and Fury turn up together again in later titles.

Stan Lee's cameo

Marvel Comics legend Stan Lee appears as a passenger reading a script on a train.

The ending and post-creditsSpoilers

After the end credits

Mid-credits: Carol appears at Avengers headquarters (leading into Endgame). Post-credits: Goose the cat coughs up the Tesseract.
