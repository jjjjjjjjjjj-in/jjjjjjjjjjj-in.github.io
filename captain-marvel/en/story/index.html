Part 1: Vers, a Kree warrior

1-1The recurring nightmare

1995, on Hala, capital planet of the Kree Empire. Vers, a member of the elite Kree unit Starforce, wakes from a nightmare about an older woman.

She has no memories from before six years ago. Taken in by the Kree, she lives as a warrior with a special power to blast light from her hands.

1-2Sparring with her commander

Unable to sleep, Vers goes to Yon-Rogg, her commander and mentor, and they spar.

He tells her not to let her emotions take over and to learn to control her power. She ends up firing a blast from her hands anyway.

1-3The Supreme Intelligence

The Kree are ruled by the Supreme Intelligence, an artificial intelligence that appears to each person as the one they respect most.

To Vers it takes the form of the stranger from her nightmares. It, too, urges her to keep her emotions in check.

1-4Starforce deploys

The Kree are at war with the Skrulls, aliens who can take any shape. Starforce heads to the planet Torfa to rescue Soh-Larr, a Kree spy who had infiltrated a group of Skrulls.

Alongside Yon-Rogg, the team includes the sniper Minn-Erva, who resents Vers, plus Korath, Att-Lass and Bron-Char.

Part 2: The ambush on Torfa

2-1An enemy in a friend's face

On Torfa, Vers finds Soh-Larr, the man she came to rescue. Then Yon-Rogg warns her by radio that it's a Skrull ambush.

The "Soh-Larr" in front of her is Talos, the Skrull commander, in disguise. Vers is taken, and the rest of Starforce pulls out.

2-2Memories probed

On the Skrull ship, Talos uses a machine to dig through Vers's memories: the Air Force on Earth, a close friend, the woman from her nightmares, and a plane crash.

What Talos wants is a lead on a light-speed engine.

2-3Escape from the ship

Vers wakes, breaks free of her restraints and fights her way through the ship.

She escapes and falls toward a nearby planet: the one the Kree call C-53, Earth. Talos and his Skrulls follow her.

Part 3: Los Angeles, 1995

3-1Crashing into a video store

Vers crashes through the roof of a Blockbuster Video store in Los Angeles. It's Earth, 1995.

She manages to get in touch with Starforce and tell them she is on Earth.

3-2Fury and Coulson

Investigating the commotion are S.H.I.E.L.D. agent Nick Fury and rookie Phil Coulson.

Vers tells them she's a Kree warrior hunting shapeshifters. Fury doesn't buy it. Then the Skrulls attack.

3-3Chase on the train

Vers chases a Skrull onto a moving train and fights one disguised as an elderly passenger, inside the car and on the roof.

In the end she recovers a crystal holding the memories the Skrulls pulled from her.

3-4The fake Coulson

Fury gives chase by car with Coulson in the passenger seat. Then the real Coulson calls in.

The man beside him is a Skrull posing as Coulson. Fury struggles with him, the car crashes, and the Skrull is killed.

3-5The Skrull autopsy

At a S.H.I.E.L.D. facility, Fury and his boss Keller examine the dead Skrull. Now Fury has to believe in aliens.

Keller orders Fury to work with Vers and keep an eye on her. But "Keller" is really Talos in disguise.

3-6Pancho's Bar

Vers swaps into Earth clothes taken from a store display, gets hold of a motorcycle and rides to Pancho's Bar in the nearby town of Rosamond.

Fury tracks her down there. Now convinced aliens are real, he and Vers question each other to make sure neither is a Skrull in disguise.

3-7An unlikely team

They trade what they know and agree to work together.

Following the clues in her recovered memories, they head for the Project Pegasus facility at a former U.S. Air Force base, home to a secret Air Force research program.

Part 4: Project Pegasus

4-1The cat at the base

They sneak in and look for the records room. Inside, a cat turns up. Its name is Goose, and it belonged to the woman from Vers's nightmares.

4-2What the records show

The records identify the woman from her nightmares as Dr. Wendy Lawson, who was building a light-speed engine.

In 1989 a plane testing the engine crashed, and Lawson and the pilot were declared dead. That pilot was Vers herself.

4-3The fake Keller

Fury tells S.H.I.E.L.D. where they are, and Keller arrives with a team, the real Coulson among them.

Keller calls Fury by a name almost no one uses for him, and Fury realizes Keller is an impostor.

4-4Fight in the records room

The fake Keller (Talos) and his Skrulls close in on Fury, and a fight breaks out in the records room. Vers jumps in to help Fury and fights them off.

4-5Escape by jet

They escape in a prototype jet from the base. Coulson, trusting Fury, lets them go. Goose has stowed away on board.

They fly to Louisiana to find Maria Rambeau, a former Air Force pilot and the last person to see Vers and Lawson alive.

Part 5: Her best friend in Louisiana

5-1Maria and Monica

Maria and her daughter Monica greet them, stunned to see someone they thought was dead.

Vers's real name is Carol Danvers. She was an Air Force pilot, and to Maria and Monica she was like family.

5-2A past she can't recall

Maria tells Carol who she used to be, but Carol still can't remember.

The name "Vers" came from what was left of her dog tag, broken in the crash.

5-3Talos comes unarmed

Talos arrives unarmed, wanting to talk. The Skrulls are refugees whose homeworld the Kree destroyed, searching for a new place to live.

And Dr. Lawson was really Mar-Vell, a Kree scientist who turned against the Kree's war and was building the engine to help the Skrulls escape.

5-4The black box recording

Talos plays a recording from the crashed plane's black box, and Carol remembers that day.

After the crash, Lawson set out to destroy the engine so the Kree couldn't have it, and told Carol to do the same. Yon-Rogg appeared and shot Lawson dead. Carol shot the engine herself and absorbed the energy of the blast.

5-5A warrior made by the Kree

The energy gave Carol her powers but took her memory. Yon-Rogg took her to Hala, saved her life with a transfusion of his own blood, and made her into the Kree warrior Vers.

Faced with the truth, Carol breaks down. Maria and Talos help her through it.

5-6Into space

Lawson's laboratory is hidden, cloaked, in orbit around Earth. Carol decides to go there.

Maria hesitates to leave Monica, but Monica talks her into it, and she joins as co-pilot. With Fury, Talos and Goose, they fly into space.

Part 6: The lab in orbit

6-1The hidden families

The lab hides Skrulls whom Lawson took in, including Talos's wife and daughter.

The power source of her engine is here too: the glowing blue cube called the Tesseract.

6-2Starforce arrives

Yon-Rogg and Starforce arrive and capture Carol and the others.

Carol is hooked up to the Supreme Intelligence.

6-3Carol rises

The Supreme Intelligence, wearing Lawson's face, tells Carol she is only a weak human without what the Kree gave her, and tries to bring her to heel.

Carol remembers all the times since childhood she fell and got back up. She removes the implant the Kree put in to hold her powers back, and her full strength is unleashed.

6-4Taking down Starforce

Glowing all over, Carol tears through the Starforce soldiers in the lab one after another.

6-5What Goose really is

Fury goes to rescue Goose, who turns out to be not a cat but an alien creature called a Flerken. Tentacles burst from its mouth and swallow Kree soldiers.

Goose swallows the Tesseract too, and scratches Fury's face, blinding his left eye.

Part 7: Battle in the sky and space

7-1Maria's dogfight

Maria flies Fury and Talos's family back to Earth. When Minn-Erva gives chase, Maria shoots her down.

7-2Ronan's fleet

At Yon-Rogg's call, the fleet of Kree officer Ronan arrives and prepares a missile strike on Earth to wipe out the Skrulls there.

Carol flies into space, destroys the missiles and smashes through a Kree bomber. Ronan retreats.

7-3Settling things with Yon-Rogg

Down on Earth, Yon-Rogg dares Carol to fight him without her powers. She refuses the challenge ("I have nothing to prove to you") and blasts him with a single shot.

She sends him back to Hala, carrying a warning for the Supreme Intelligence.

Part 8: Farewell, and the start of the Avengers

8-1Leaving for space

Carol decides to leave Earth and help the Skrulls find a new home.

She gives Fury a modified pager so he can call her in a real emergency.

8-2The Avengers name

Having seen the dangers from beyond Earth, Fury starts drafting a plan to find and gather heroes like Carol.

He renames it after Carol's Air Force call sign, painted on her jet in a photo: "Avenger."

8-3Mid-credits

2018. After half of all life in the universe has vanished, Steve, Natasha, Bruce and Rhodey are monitoring the pager Fury left behind.

The signal stops, and Carol appears, asking where Fury is.

8-4After the credits

Back in 1995, Goose coughs up the Tesseract onto Fury's desk.

Summary (spoilers through the ending)

What really happened to Carol

In 1989, Air Force pilot Carol crashed while test-flying the engine built by Dr. Lawson (the Kree scientist Mar-Vell). Yon-Rogg shot Lawson, and Carol destroyed the engine and absorbed its energy (Part 5).

The Kree took her, memory gone, gave her Yon-Rogg's blood, fitted an implant to suppress her power, and raised her for six years as the warrior Vers (Parts 1 and 5).

On Earth, with Fury, Maria and Talos, she recovered who she was, removed the implant and unlocked her full power (Parts 3–6).

The truth about the Skrulls and the Kree

The Skrulls are first shown as enemies, but they are refugees whose home the Kree destroyed, searching for somewhere safe (Part 5).

Talos chased Carol disguised as Keller and Soh-Larr, but his goal was to protect his family and find the engine (Parts 2–5). In the end, Carol left to help the Skrulls find a new home (Part 8).

Who died (in order)

- Dr. Lawson / Mar-Vell (Part 5, in 1989): shot dead by Yon-Rogg while trying to keep the engine from the Kree.

- The Skrull posing as Coulson (Part 3): exposed by Fury and killed when the car crashed during their struggle.

- Minn-Erva (Part 7): shot down by Maria while chasing her plane back to Earth.

- Fury: not dead. Goose's scratch blinded his left eye (Part 6).

My takeOpinion

The late moment where she realises she has nothing to prove is satisfying. The 90s mood and her back-and-forth with a young Fury are highlights, and I think Goose the cat comes out of it best of all.
