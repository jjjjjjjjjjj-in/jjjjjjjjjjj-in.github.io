Key terms

The Temporal Loom
The giant machine at the heart of the TVA that weaves time into a single thread.
Time-slipping
Loki's body jumping to the past or future against his will.
Branches
New worlds that split off from the set timeline, multiplying since the end of Season 1.
Pruning
Erasing a branch of time.
Victor Timely
An inventor living in 1893 who looks exactly like the man in the Citadel from Season 1.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Is Victor Timely the man from the Citadel?

He's a variant of the same person, from another branch, with a very different personality.

What happens to Loki at the end?

He stays at the end of time, holding the branching timelines together with his own hands, giving up his time to protect countless worlds.

What happens to the TVA?

It shifts from pruning branches to watching over them.

Easy-to-miss details

O.B.'s manual

O.B. wrote the TVA's own instruction manual.

The same title

The finale shares its title, Glorious Purpose, with Season 1's first episode. Notice how Loki's purpose has changed.

Sylvie's fast-food job

Sylvie enjoys a quiet life working at a 1980s fast-food restaurant.
