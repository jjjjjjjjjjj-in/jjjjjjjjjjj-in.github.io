Episode 1: Ouroboros

1-1Thrown into the TVA's past

At the end of Season 1, Sylvie pushed Loki through a time door at the Citadel. He arrives back at TVA headquarters, but while staying in the same place, his body keeps jumping between past, present and future.

The first jump takes him to the TVA's past. Nobody there knows him, Mobius included, and they chase him as an intruder.

1-2A recording of He Who Remains

In the past, Loki finds a recording of He Who Remains, the man from the Citadel who created the TVA, thanking Ravonna Renslayer for working with him. The two had been something like partners.

1-3A TVA in chaos

In the present, the Sacred Timeline is branching rapidly because Sylvie killed He Who Remains, and the TVA is in disarray. Ravonna is gone, and Judge Gamble and General Dox have taken charge.

Hunter B-15 and Mobius tell them that everyone working at the TVA is a variant taken from a branched timeline. Gamble is persuaded and orders the TVA to stop wiping out branches.

1-4Loki crashes the meeting

Loki slips into the meeting. He reveals that He Who Remains was behind the TVA and warns Mobius about the danger of He Who Remains' many variants.

Meanwhile, General Dox has many hunters arm themselves heavily, supposedly for a mission to find Sylvie.

1-5O.B. the technician

Loki and Mobius visit Ouroboros, called O.B., a technician who has worked alone for centuries in the department that repairs and builds the TVA's machines.

O.B. calls Loki's condition "time slipping" and suspects the cause is the flood of new branches overloading the Temporal Loom, the giant machine that keeps the flow of time in order.

His plan: Mobius takes a Temporal Aura Extractor to the Loom. Loki prunes himself in the future, which releases him from time, and at that moment the device pulls him out of the time stream.

1-6Mobius on the bridge

Mobius goes out onto the bridge between the TVA building and the Temporal Loom, and manages to hook the Extractor up to the Loom.

1-7The TVA's future

Loki slips into the future, where the Loom is going critical and the TVA is being evacuated.

He hunts for a TimeStick to prune himself and briefly runs into Sylvie. Then, at the last possible second, someone prunes him from behind.

1-8Loki pulled back

In the present, O.B. starts sealing off the Loom to protect the TVA, while Mobius argues they must wait for Loki.

Mobius succeeds in pulling Loki out of the time stream, and the two tumble back to safety as the Loom is sealed.

Loki has stopped slipping. He and Mobius set out to find Sylvie.

1-9Mid-credits

Sylvie arrives in Broxton, Oklahoma, in 1982 on a branched timeline, and walks into a McDonald's.

Episode 2: Breaking Brad

2-1X-5 goes silent

Loki and Mobius are searching for Sylvie. When General Dox and Hunter X-5 stop responding, they suspect X-5 found her and set out to track him down.

2-2London, 1977

X-5 is on the Sacred Timeline in 1977 London, living his real life as famous film actor Brad Wolfe, star of the movie Zaniac.

Brad runs, outpaces Hunter B-15 and overpowers Mobius, but Loki catches him with magic and he is brought back to the TVA.

2-3What Casey finds

TVA receptionist Casey discovers that Ravonna was in contact with Miss Minutes, the TVA's clock-faced AI, before both went missing, and concludes they are working together.

When Casey tells B-15, Loki and Mobius, Loki reveals the recording in which Ravonna and He Who Remains spoke like partners.

2-4O.B. locked out of the Loom

O.B. tries to adjust the Temporal Loom so it can safely take in the growing branches without a meltdown, but finds he can't get into it without Miss Minutes or He Who Remains' temporal aura.

2-5Interrogating Brad

When Loki and Mobius question Brad, he mocks Loki as a loser and needles Mobius for never checking what his own life was like before he joined the TVA. Mobius loses his temper.

Loki then questions Brad alone, using a box-like device that squeezes him, until Brad admits he abandoned Dox's mission and knows where Sylvie is.

2-6Sylvie at McDonald's

Loki, Mobius and Brad travel to Broxton, Oklahoma, on a branched timeline and find Sylvie working the register at McDonald's.

Loki tells her the TVA is in danger and that he met her in the TVA's future, and asks for help working out what happened. She refuses, content with her ordinary life.

When Brad declares they are all in mortal danger, Sylvie enchants him into telling the truth: Dox's real plan is to bomb and destroy every branched timeline at once.

2-7Stopping Dox

Brad is sent back into custody. Loki, Mobius and Sylvie storm Dox's location to stop her operation.

But by then most of the branches have already been destroyed with reset charges and modified TemPads. Dox is captured, though some of her allies escape.

2-8Sylvie leaves

Casey picks up Ravonna's TemPad on one of the surviving branches.

Sylvie declares the TVA rotten for failing to protect the branches, and goes back to her McDonald's in Oklahoma, still holding He Who Remains' TemPad.

Episode 3: 1893

3-1The branches grow back

At TVA headquarters, O.B. finds that the branches Dox destroyed are growing back, threatening to overload the Loom and destroy the TVA. Only Miss Minutes or He Who Remains' temporal aura can open the way to fix it.

3-2Chicago, 1868

Ravonna and Miss Minutes travel to Chicago in 1868 on a branched timeline. As He Who Remains arranged before his death, they secretly leave the TVA Handbook where a young Victor Timely, one of his variants, will find it.

Loki and Mobius, tracking Ravonna's TemPad because she is working with Miss Minutes, go first to 1868 and then to 1893. They never learn about the handbook.

3-3The World's Fair

At the 1893 Chicago World's Fair, Loki and Mobius watch the grown-up Victor Timely present his Loom prototype.

By now Timely has built real prototypes of TVA devices, but he also sells fake inventions to swindle people.

3-4Four groups give chase

Timely ends up chased by four groups:

1) a robber baron and his allies, out for revenge over a scam; 2) Loki and Mobius, who need Timely's temporal aura to fix the Loom; 3) Ravonna and Miss Minutes, who want him to take He Who Remains' place with them at his side; 4) Sylvie, who wants to kill him before he can rise to power.

3-5Loki stops Sylvie

Loki stops Sylvie from killing Timely and uses magic to scare off the robber baron, while Timely escapes with Ravonna and Miss Minutes.

Timely realizes Ravonna gave him the handbook as a child and thanks her, but when she proposes a partnership, he leaves her behind.

3-6The Wisconsin lab

Timely returns to his laboratory in Wisconsin. Miss Minutes, jealous of the attention Ravonna got, says she wishes she had a real body and confesses romantic love for Timely. He switches her off.

3-7Timely begs for his life

Ravonna, Loki, Mobius and Sylvie catch up with Timely at the lab, and Sylvie takes control of the situation.

Timely begs for his life, insisting he won't become He Who Remains and can make his own choices. Sylvie spares him and lets Loki take him to the TVA.

3-8To the Citadel

Sylvie sends Ravonna to the Citadel at the End of Time. Ravonna brings Miss Minutes along and switches her back on, and they see He Who Remains' decaying body.

Miss Minutes tells Ravonna she knows a secret about her that would make her angry.

Episode 4: Heart of the TVA

4-1Ravonna's erased memories

Miss Minutes shows Ravonna an old recording: He Who Remains thanked her for her help in his war and proposed that they lead the TVA together. But when she returned to the TVA, he had Miss Minutes erase her memories and those of other TVA staff.

Ravonna had commanded his army. Miss Minutes proposes that the two of them work together without him.

4-2Timely at the TVA

At the TVA, Loki and Mobius ask Timely to help fix the Temporal Loom, which is close to catastrophic failure because it can't handle the branches.

Timely meets O.B., who reveals that his own ideas came from Timely, while Timely's inventions were inspired by the TVA Handbook that O.B. wrote: a loop with no clear beginning.

Together they build a Throughput Multiplier to fix the Loom.

4-3B-15's offer

Hunter B-15 visits the detained General Dox, her loyalists and Brad, offers to work together to defend the TVA, and leaves.

4-4Ravonna's offer

Then Ravonna and Miss Minutes appear before the detainees, offering a life on the timeline to anyone who helps them take over the TVA.

Only Brad agrees. Dox and her loyalists refuse to help Ravonna, and Miss Minutes crushes them to death.

4-5Timely kidnapped

Brad prunes Hunter D-90 and kidnaps Timely. Loki and Sylvie rush to rescue him.

Miss Minutes takes over the TVA's systems and briefly traps Sylvie.

4-6Who pruned Loki

On the way, Sylvie and Loki run into his past self, still suffering from time slipping. Loki prunes his past self.

The "someone" who pruned Loki from behind in the TVA's future in Episode 1 was Loki himself.

4-7O.B. reboots the system

O.B. reboots the TVA's systems to shut Miss Minutes down, which also turns off the devices that suppress magic inside the TVA.

Now able to use magic, Sylvie enchants Brad and makes him prune Ravonna. Timely is rescued.

4-8The Loom explodes

Timely's temporal aura restores access to the Loom. Loki volunteers to go out and launch the Multiplier, but Timely insists on going because he can fix any problems.

The moment he is exposed to the Loom's heightened temporal radiation, Timely is stretched apart and killed, and the Multiplier goes unused.

The Temporal Loom explodes, and its blast wave spreads toward Loki, Mobius, Sylvie, B-15, Casey and O.B. in the TVA.

Episode 5: Science/Fiction

5-1Loki alone

Loki survives the explosion, but everyone else at TVA headquarters has vanished.

He starts time slipping again, now across both space and time, and barely escapes as the headquarters itself unravels.

5-2Friends back in their old lives

His slips take him to branched timelines where his friends have been reset to the lives they had before the TVA.

Mobius is Don, a jet ski salesman in Cleveland in 2022. B-15 is Dr. Verity Willis, a physician in New York in 2012. Casey is Frank Morris, a prisoner at Alcatraz in 1962. O.B. is Dr. A.D. Doug, a writer and scientist in Pasadena in 1994.

Loki always lands close to them, but none of them know him.

5-3Dr. A.D. Doug

Wanting to slip back to before the explosion, Loki asks Doug for help. Doug believes his story and urges him to learn to control his time slipping.

Loki concentrates on his goal of saving the TVA, but nothing happens.

So Doug suggests another way: gather everyone who was there at the explosion, so their combined temporal aura can send them back to the right time and place. Loki gives Doug the TVA Handbook he kept so Doug can build a TemPad.

5-4Winning over Don

Loki slips to Don's timeline and persuades him to help by appealing to his wish to protect his sons.

Doug arrives using the TemPad he built himself. It took him 19 months, and cost him his job and his wife.

5-5Gathering the group

Using the TemPad, Loki and Doug bring Don, Willis and Frank together in Doug's workshop.

5-6Sylvie's question

Finally Loki finds Sylvie at the McDonald's in Broxton, Oklahoma. Unlike the others, she still has her memories. She refuses to help because she wants to live her own life.

Pressed by her, Loki admits what he really wants: his friends back. He is afraid of being alone.

Sylvie tells him to live his own life too. Loki gives up and says Don, Willis, Frank and Doug should go back to their lives.

5-7The worlds unravel

Then everything in Sylvie's world begins to unravel. Realizing the branches are dying, she changes her mind and heads to Doug's workshop.

But before the group can act, the workshop unravels too, and Frank, Doug, Don, Willis and Sylvie vanish with it.

5-8Rewriting the story

In that moment, Loki finally learns to control his time slipping by focusing on a person instead of an event or a purpose.

He slips back to just before the workshop unravels and tells everyone that the story can be rewritten. Then, focusing on O.B., he slips to the moment before the Loom exploded.

Episode 6: Glorious Purpose

6-1Back before the blast, again and again

Loki slips back again and again to moments before the Loom explodes, using what he learned each time to try something different.

6-2Centuries of study

Over centuries, Loki learns the science of the Throughput Multiplier from O.B. At last he manages a run in which Timely reaches the Loom, activates the Multiplier and returns safely.

But the Loom still explodes. No adjustment can handle infinite branches.

6-3Back to the Citadel

Changing tactics, Loki decides to stop the timeline from branching at all. He slips to the Citadel at the End of Time, just before Sylvie kills He Who Remains.

He tries again and again to stop her, but she says he'll have to kill her to do it. He won't, and every time she kills He Who Remains.

6-4He Who Remains' answer

When Loki asks He Who Remains why he never fights back, He Who Remains freezes Sylvie in time. He recognizes Loki's time slipping and hints that he is the one who laid that path for him.

When Loki shows that he can freeze time too, He Who Remains reveals the truth: the Loom is a fail-safe. If it overloads, the branches are deleted along with the TVA, and only the Sacred Timeline survives.

6-5Two paths

Loki has two options: destroy the Loom and risk an apocalyptic multiversal war waged by He Who Remains' variants, or kill Sylvie to protect He Who Remains and the Sacred Timeline.

He Who Remains suggests killing Sylvie to save the Loom. Loki refuses.

6-6Asking Mobius and Sylvie

Loki visits Mobius and Sylvie at different points in time to ask their advice.

Mobius, from early in Season 1, tells him to accept the burden for the greater good. Sylvie, from the time of Episode 5, urges him to let He Who Remains die so that free will survives.

6-7Loki's choice

Loki slips once more and this time walks to the Loom himself, in Timely's place.

He destroys the Loom, revives the dying branches with magic, gathers them in his hands and reshapes them into a great tree-like structure with himself at its center.

He commits himself to watching over the multiverse's branches alone at the End of Time.

6-8A new TVA

Afterward, the TVA accepts the endless branches and keeps watch on He Who Remains' variants. Mobius reports that one of them has been stopped in Earth-616's "adjacent realm" (the Quantum Realm).

B-15 becomes one of the TVA's leaders. O.B. switches a now-friendly Miss Minutes back on and writes a new TVA Handbook, crediting Timely as co-author.

In one timeline, the young Timely never receives the handbook in 1868.

6-9Where everyone ends up

In the Void, Ravonna wakes near a TVA crest and sees Alioth, the creature that devours time.

Mobius retires from the TVA. He and Sylvie watch his variant Don and Don's sons from afar, while Loki watches over them all from the End of Time.

Summary (spoilers through the finale)

Loki's time slipping

1) Ep. 1: After Sylvie pushes him out of the Citadel, Loki jumps between past, present and future. O.B. and Mobius stop it with the Extractor.

2) Ep. 4: The one who pruned Loki in the future in Episode 1 was Loki himself.

3) Ep. 5: After the Loom explodes, he slips across time and space again, and finally learns to control it by focusing on a person.

4) Ep. 6: He uses that power to replay time again and again, and in the end destroys the Loom and holds the branches together himself.

Friends' original lives (Ep. 5)

- Mobius → Don (Cleveland, 2022; sells jet skis; two sons)

- B-15 → Dr. Verity Willis (New York, 2012)

- Casey → Frank Morris (an Alcatraz prisoner, 1962)

- O.B. → Dr. A.D. Doug (Pasadena, 1994; writer and scientist)

- Only Sylvie kept her memories, living in Broxton at the McDonald's.

Who died or was pruned (in order)

- General Dox and her loyalists (Ep. 4): while detained, they refuse to help take over the TVA, and Miss Minutes crushes them to death.

- Hunter D-90 (Ep. 4): pruned by Brad, now on Ravonna's side, as he kidnaps Timely.

- Loki's past self (Ep. 4): Loki prunes his own time-slipping past self, closing the loop from Episode 1.

- Ravonna (Ep. 4): to rescue Timely, Sylvie enchants Brad into pruning her. She wakes up in the Void in Episode 6.

- Victor Timely (Ep. 4): approaching the Loom with the Multiplier, he is stretched apart by its intense temporal radiation.

- He Who Remains (Ep. 6): no matter how often Loki replays it, Sylvie kills him at the Citadel (the Season 1 finale scene).

Loki, who once wanted a throne, ends by choosing to hold everyone up alone, and it's beautifully done. The longer you've watched since the first Thor, the harder it hits.
