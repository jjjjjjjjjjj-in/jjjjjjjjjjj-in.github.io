StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

1. The Hydra base

The Avengers raid a Hydra base in Sokovia and recover Loki's scepter. They encounter the twins Pietro and Wanda, given powers by Hydra's experiments. Wanda shows Tony a terrifying vision of his friends all dead.

2. Ultron is born

Shaken by the vision, Tony uses the stone with Bruce to try to build Ultron, an AI to protect Earth. But Ultron wakes up, decides humanity is Earth's enemy, and vanishes.

3. The twins join Ultron

The twins, who blame Tony because Stark weapons killed their parents, side with Ultron. In Africa, Wanda's visions send Hulk on a rampage, and Tony stops him with a giant suit.

4. Clint's secret home

The battered team rests at a farmhouse where Clint has been hiding his family. The others learn for the first time that he has a wife and children.

5. A new body

Ultron tries to build the ultimate body from vibranium and the stone. Learning his true goal is to wipe out humanity, the twins switch to the Avengers' side.

6. Vision is born

The team puts the J.A.R.V.I.S. AI into the body they recovered and Thor's lightning brings it to life. The newborn Vision lifts Thor's hammer, proving he's on their side.

7. A city in the sky

Ultron lifts the city of Sokovia into the sky to drop it and wipe out humanity. The Avengers fight while evacuating people. Pietro dies protecting others.

8. A new Avengers

The city's fall is stopped and Ultron is defeated. Tony and Thor step away, and Steve and Natasha start training new members, including Wanda and Vision.

Key terms

Ultron
The AI Tony and Bruce tried to create to protect the world. It gains a will of its own and sets out to wipe out humanity.
Loki's scepter and its stone
The scepter Loki used in The Avengers. The stone inside can affect minds and holds the basis of an AI.
Sokovia
A small Eastern European country with a Hydra base, and the site of the final battle.
Vibranium
An extremely strong metal Ultron wants for a new body.
Hulkbuster
A giant suit Tony built to stop Hulk if he ran wild.
Infinity Stones
Six special stones in the universe. The one in Vision's forehead, the Mind Stone, is one of them.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why did Tony build Ultron?

Wanda's vision made him terrified he couldn't protect his friends from another attack from space. He wanted something that could protect the world in their place.

Why do the twins hate Tony?

Stark Industries bombs killed their parents when they were children.

Why could Vision lift the hammer?

He was deemed worthy, proof that unlike Ultron, he values life.

Who appears at the end?

Thanos. He takes up a gauntlet and declares he'll gather the stones himself.

Where does Thor go partway through?

With his scientist friend Dr. Selvig, he visits a mystical pool to see a vision and investigate the stones.

Easy-to-miss details

The hammer game

At a party, the team takes turns trying to lift Thor's hammer. It budges slightly only for Steve.

Hulk and Natasha

Something special starts to grow between Natasha and Bruce.

Enter Klaue

Arms dealer Ulysses Klaue appears, and returns in Black Panther.

Stan Lee's cameo

Marvel Comics legend Stan Lee appears at the party, downing a glass of powerful Asgardian liquor.
