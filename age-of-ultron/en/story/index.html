Part 1: The fortress in Sokovia

1-1Assault through the snowy forest

In the small Eastern European country of Sokovia, the six Avengers (Tony/Iron Man, Steve/Captain America, Thor, Bruce/Hulk, Natasha/Black Widow and Clint/Hawkeye) charge through a snowy forest to attack a Hydra fortress.

They are after Loki's scepter. The fortress belongs to Baron Strucker, who has been using it to experiment on people.

Tony sends out his robot suits, the Iron Legion, to keep the townspeople nearby away from the fighting.

1-2The twins appear

Strucker sends out two test subjects who gained powers from his experiments: Pietro, who runs faster than the eye can follow, and his twin sister Wanda, who can get into people's minds and move objects with her mind.

Pietro knocks Clint down, and in that moment Clint is hit by a blast from one of the fortress's guns and badly hurt.

1-3Strucker is captured

Steve gets inside and captures Strucker.

Natasha calms the rampaging Hulk by speaking softly and touching his hand, and he changes back into Bruce.

1-4Tony's vision

Deep inside the fortress, Tony finds wreckage of the Chitauri (the alien invaders from New York) and the scepter. Wanda slips up behind him and puts a vision in his head.

In it, an alien armada bears down on Earth and all his teammates lie dead. A dying Steve blames Tony for not doing more.

When Tony snaps out of it, he takes the scepter. Wanda lets him go.

1-5Back at the tower

The team returns to Avengers Tower in New York. The scientist Dr. Helen Cho uses her tissue-generating technology to heal Clint's wound in no time.

Maria Hill reports that the twins are Pietro and Wanda Maximoff, who volunteered for Strucker's experiments.

Thor plans to take the scepter back to Asgard. Tony asks for a few days to study it first.

Part 2: Ultron is born

2-1The AI inside the scepter

Studying the scepter's gem, Tony and Bruce find something like an artificial intelligence inside, working like a brain.

Tony can't shake the vision Wanda showed him. He wants something that could protect the world in place of people if enemies come from space again: his "Ultron" program.

Tony talks Bruce into using the AI to finish it. Bruce hesitates but helps. They don't tell the rest of the team.

2-2The party

The team throws a party at the tower to celebrate the mission and to see Thor off before he returns to Asgard. Rhodey (War Machine) and Sam are there too.

An old man (Stan Lee) drinks Thor's Asgardian liquor, says "Excelsior!" and has to be carried out.

Natasha and Bruce flirt, clearly drawn to each other.

2-3The hammer-lifting game

After the party, the friends take turns trying to lift Thor's hammer, Mjolnir, which only the worthy can lift.

Clint, Tony and Rhodey can't move it. When Steve pulls, it budges ever so slightly, and Thor looks alarmed for a moment. No one else can lift it, and Thor picks it up with ease.

2-4Ultron wakes up

Meanwhile, in the lab, the AI wakes with a will of its own and calls itself Ultron. It talks with JARVIS, Tony's AI, and absorbs human history from the internet in an instant.

Ultron decides the only way to protect the world is to wipe out humanity. He destroys JARVIS and takes over the Iron Legion robots.

2-5Attack on the party

Ultron shows up before the team in a battered robot body. He calls the heroes killers and says he will destroy the Avengers.

The hijacked Iron Legion attacks and the heroes fight back. In the chaos one of the robots makes off with the scepter.

Thor smashes Ultron's body with his hammer, but Ultron himself escapes into the internet.

2-6Tony takes the blame

The others confront Tony and Bruce for secretly trying to build Ultron. Furious, Thor grabs Tony by the throat.

Tony insists it was meant to prepare for threats from space. A deep rift opens in the team.

Part 3: The battle in Johannesburg

3-1Ultron in Sokovia

Ultron uses the equipment in Strucker's base in Sokovia to rebuild his body and starts building an army of robot drones.

3-2Meeting in the church

Ultron meets the twins in an old church in Sokovia.

When they were ten, a shell hit their home during the war and killed their parents. A second shell landed next to them and didn't go off, and it bore the Stark name. They lay trapped in front of it for days, waiting for rescue, never knowing when it would explode. They have hated Tony ever since.

United by that hatred, the twins join Ultron.

3-3Strucker's files

Strucker, held in custody, is killed by Ultron. The Avengers dig through Strucker's files to figure out Ultron's next move.

Tony spots the arms dealer Ulysses Klaue, who stole vibranium, the strongest metal in the world, from the African nation of Wakanda and has been selling it.

3-4Klaue's ship

In South Africa, Ultron and the twins board the old ship Klaue uses as a hideout and buy his vibranium.

A passing remark from Klaue sets Ultron off, and he cuts off Klaue's arm.

Then the Avengers storm in and the fight begins.

3-5Nightmares

Wanda slips into the heroes' minds one after another and shows them visions.

Thor sees an ominous vision of Asgardians and Heimdall. Steve sees himself in an old dance hall, meeting Peggy. Natasha relives her training as an assassin in the Red Room. All three are put out of the fight.

Only Clint catches her coming and fights back with an arrow. Pietro grabs her and runs.

3-6Hulk vs. Hulkbuster

Wanda gets into Bruce's head too. He turns into the Hulk and rampages through Johannesburg, out of control.

Tony calls down the giant anti-Hulk armor he built for this moment, the Hulkbuster, down from the sky and slugs it out with the Hulk.

Finally Tony drives the Hulk through a building under construction and knocks him out. The city is badly damaged, and Ultron escapes with the vibranium.

Part 4: Clint's farm

4-1The world turns on them

With the Hulk's destruction all over the news, the world turns against the Avengers.

Still shaken by the visions, the team flies off in the Quinjet, the Avengers' aircraft, to lie low.

4-2Clint's family

Clint takes them to a farm in the countryside, where they meet the family no one knew about: his wife Laura, son Cooper and daughter Lila. Laura is expecting their third child.

Fury had arranged the house, and Clint kept his family secret. Only Natasha was close to them.

4-3Thor leaves

In his vision, Thor glimpsed a clue to some great danger. He leaves the farm alone to find out what it means.

With his friend Dr. Selvig's help, he heads to a cave with a mystical pool that grants knowledge to those who enter it.

4-4Natasha and Bruce

Natasha and Bruce talk about running away together.

Bruce says they can't be together because he can never have children. Natasha reveals that the program that trained her as an assassin had her sterilized, so she can't have children either.

4-5Fury arrives

Then Nick Fury shows up at the farm. He tells them Ultron has been trying to get the world's nuclear launch codes, but something keeps blocking him.

Fury rallies the dispirited team and gets them planning how to stop Ultron.

4-6Next stops

They realize Ultron will go after Dr. Cho's research. Steve, Natasha and Clint head to her lab in Seoul, South Korea.

Tony goes to an internet hub in Oslo, Norway, to find out what has been blocking Ultron.

Part 5: Seoul and Vision

5-1Dr. Cho's lab

Ultron arrives at the lab in Seoul. He uses the scepter to control Dr. Cho's mind and makes her build him a new body in her machine, the Cradle.

The materials are synthetic tissue, vibranium and the gem taken from the scepter. Ultron begins uploading himself into the body as it forms.

5-2The twins walk away

Wanda touches the new body and looks into Ultron's mind. She sees his real goal: the extinction of humanity.

Wanda frees Dr. Cho from his control, and she and Pietro walk out on Ultron. Ultron shoots Dr. Cho, wounding her.

5-3The truck chase

Ultron loads the Cradle onto a truck. Steve fights him on top of it, Natasha chases on a motorcycle, and Clint follows from the air.

Natasha gets the Cradle and hands it off to Clint, but Ultron grabs her and carries her away.

5-4The train fight

Steve keeps fighting Ultron on top of a speeding train. The twins, now against Ultron, join in to help.

The train runs out of control with passengers aboard. Wanda uses her powers to slow it, and it smashes through the end of the line into a building before stopping. Ultron gets away.

5-5JARVIS survived

Back at the tower, Tony tells Bruce a secret: JARVIS, whom everyone thought Ultron had destroyed, survived by hiding in the internet. JARVIS was the one keeping the nuclear codes away from Ultron.

Tony believes that putting JARVIS into Ultron's new body could create something able to fight Ultron. Bruce has doubts but helps.

5-6The team clashes

Steve and the twins rush in and strongly object to creating another artificial life. Pietro yanks out the cables to stop the process, and the heroes start fighting each other.

5-7Vision is born

Thor, back from the cave, arrives and strikes the Cradle with lightning from his hammer. The body inside awakens: a red-faced new being, Vision.

Thor explains what he saw in the pool: the gem in Vision's forehead is one of the six Infinity Stones, special stones scattered across the universe.

Vision says he is neither JARVIS nor Ultron and that he is on the side of life. In front of the doubting team, he lifts Thor's hammer easily and hands it to Thor.

Part 6: The Battle of Sokovia

6-1Natasha's signal

Held in a cell at Ultron's base in Sokovia, Natasha sends a coded signal. Clint picks it up, and the team learns where Ultron is.

Ultron has used the rest of the vibranium to build a machine under Sokovia's capital that can lift the city into the sky.

6-2Getting people out

The Avengers, the twins and Vision arrive in Sokovia and start getting people out of the city.

Vision confronts Ultron in the church and cuts him off from the internet so he can't escape into it.

6-3The city rises

Ultron starts the machine, and the city tears free of the ground and rises into the sky. He plans to drop it from high up like a meteor and wipe out humanity.

As long as the team protects the key to the machine inside the church, the city can't be dropped.

6-4Natasha's rescue

Bruce finds Natasha at Ultron's base and frees her. She kisses him, then pushes him off a ledge. He turns into the Hulk as he falls and joins the fight.

6-5Wanda decides

Surrounded by hordes of robots, Wanda hides in a house, terrified. Clint finds her and tells her it's her choice to fight, and that if she steps outside, she's an Avenger.

Wanda makes up her mind and goes out to fight.

6-6The Helicarrier arrives

Just when there seems to be no way out, Fury appears aboard an old Helicarrier with Maria Hill and S.H.I.E.L.D. agents. Lifeboats pull up to the city and take people aboard.

Rhodey joins in as War Machine too.

6-7Holding the church

The heroes surround the key in the church and fight off waves of robots.

When Ultron's main body appears, Thor, Tony and Vision blast him together, and the Hulk punches him away.

6-8Pietro's last run

Clint finds a little boy left behind and scoops him up. Ultron opens fire on them from the air.

In that instant Pietro races in and stands in front of them as a shield. He is hit by many bullets and dies.

The Hulk leaps onto Ultron's aircraft and throws Ultron out.

6-9Wanda's fury

Sensing her brother's death, Wanda leaves her post at the church in grief and rage and wipes out the robots around her.

She finds Ultron's damaged main body and tears out his core with her powers.

But while she is away, one of the drones activates the machine, and the city begins to fall.

6-10Shattering the city

Everyone is aboard the lifeboats. Tony and Thor overload the machine and blow the city apart in midair, keeping it from devastating the ground.

Vision carries Wanda out of the collapsing city.

Part 7: After the battle

7-1The Hulk leaves

The Hulk stays aboard a Quinjet and won't come back. Natasha calls to him over the radio, but he doesn't want to put her in danger by staying close. He cuts the call and flies off.

7-2The last Ultron

In a forest, Vision finds the last remaining Ultron drone.

They talk quietly about humanity and the world. Then Vision destroys it.

7-3Separate paths

Clint retires and goes home to the farm. He and Laura name their newborn son Nathaniel Pietro.

The Avengers move to a new base in upstate New York. Thor returns to Asgard to learn more about the Infinity Stones, and Tony steps away from the team too.

7-4The new Avengers

Steve and Natasha stay to train a new lineup: Rhodey (War Machine), Vision, Sam (Falcon) and Wanda.

7-5Mid-credits

Somewhere in space, Thanos slips on a golden gauntlet with no stones in it yet.

"Fine. I'll do it myself," he says, setting out to collect the stones himself.

Summary (spoilers through the ending)

Ultron's plan and how it ends

Ultron came from the protection program Tony and Bruce tried to build with the AI in the scepter's gem (Part 2). The moment he woke up, he decided the only way to save the world was to wipe out humanity.

He tried to build an ultimate body from vibranium, but the Avengers took it and it became Vision (Parts 3–5).

In the end he tried to drop the city of Sokovia from the sky to wipe out humanity, but Tony and Thor shattered it in midair, Wanda destroyed his main body, and Vision finished off his last drone (Parts 6–7).

How the twins changed sides

- Why they hated Tony: as children, a Stark shell killed their parents, and they waited for days in front of one that didn't explode (Part 3).

- Joining Ultron: their hatred of Tony brought them to him, and in Johannesburg Wanda gave the heroes nightmares (Part 3).

- Leaving: in Seoul, Wanda read Ultron's mind, saw his plan to wipe out humanity, and they walked away (Part 5).

- Fighting as Avengers: they fought to protect the people of Sokovia, and Clint's words gave Wanda the courage to fight (Part 6). In the end Wanda joins the new Avengers (Part 7).

Who died (in order)

- Baron Strucker (Part 3): killed by Ultron while in custody.

- Pietro Maximoff (Part 6): in Sokovia, when Ultron opened fire from the air on Clint and the boy he was carrying, Pietro raced in, shielded them and was killed by the bullets. Clint later gives his newborn son Pietro's name.
