Part 1: The parents' plane

1-1The video message

Years earlier, Richard Parker, a scientist who has left Oscorp, records a video explaining why he is disappearing.

He and his wife Mary hurriedly board a private jet. Their son Peter has been left with Richard's brother Ben and his wife May.

1-2An assassin on board

Aboard the jet is a man sent to kill Richard. He kills the pilot and grapples with Richard.

In the middle of the fight, Richard finishes uploading his research data from a laptop. But with no one flying it, the plane crashes, killing both Richard and Mary.

Part 2: Graduation day

2-1The plutonium truck

Time has passed since his fight with the Lizard, and Peter is still fighting crime in New York as Spider-Man, with a remade suit and improved web-shooters. Wisecracking through his fights, he has become popular with the city.

Aleksei Sytsevich, a loud, boastful member of the Russian mob, steals an Oscorp truck carrying vials of plutonium. Spider-Man chases him through traffic.

2-2Saving Max

During the chase, Oscorp electrical engineer Max Dillon freezes in the truck's path. Spider-Man saves him, gives him a few encouraging words, and swings back into the chase.

For Max, who has always been overlooked, the moment becomes unforgettable.

2-3Catching Aleksei

Catching the plutonium vials as they spill from the truck, Spider-Man stops Aleksei and leaves him for the police.

All the while, Peter has been on the phone with Gwen. It's graduation day, and she is giving the valedictorian speech.

2-4The captain's face

While talking to Gwen, Peter sees a vision of her late father, Captain Stacy. In the previous film, the dying captain made Peter promise to keep Gwen out of danger.

Peter is tormented by the fact that he hasn't kept that promise.

2-5Graduation

At the ceremony, Gwen gives her valedictorian speech. After dealing with Aleksei, Peter rushes over and just makes it in time for his own graduation.

2-6The breakup

After graduation, the two talk. Peter feels he must stay away from Gwen to keep his promise to her father, and can't fully commit.

Tired of his hesitation, Gwen breaks up with him.

Part 3: Harry and Max

3-1Harry comes home

A few months later, Peter's childhood friend Harry Osborn returns to New York. He was sent to a boarding school in Europe as a boy and has been away for years. His father Norman, the head of Oscorp, is dying.

Norman tells him the illness, retroviral hyperplasia, has run in the Osborn family for generations, and that Harry is now at the age when it first appears. He gives Harry a small device he says holds his life's work.

3-2The young CEO

Norman dies, and 20-year-old Harry becomes the new head of Oscorp.

Vice president Donald Menken and the rest of the board resent the young CEO. They knew about Norman's secret biogenetic projects for foreign militaries. At a board meeting, Harry accuses them of trying to take the company from the Osborns and humiliates them.

Harry keeps Felicia, his father's assistant, as his own.

3-3Old friends

After Norman's death, Peter visits Harry. They talk like old times, and Peter rekindles their friendship.

3-4Max's birthday

Max is a gifted engineer who designed much of the city's power grid. But he is timid and easy to overlook, ignored at work and living alone.

Ever since Spider-Man saved him, Max has convinced himself they are best friends.

It's his birthday. Running late at Oscorp, he drops a paper, and Gwen, who works there, brings it to him at the elevator. Max is thrilled that she knows his name, and Gwen learns he is obsessed with Spider-Man.

3-5The eel tank

That night, Max's boss leaves him alone to fix the wiring in a lab.

While working, he is electrocuted and falls into a tank of genetically modified electric eels. They attack him, and his body mutates into a living source of electricity.

3-6Clues about Dad

Peter has covered his bedroom wall with articles and papers about his father, trying to learn why his parents vanished. In his father's briefcase he finds the word "Roosevelt."

Aunt May, who still lives with him, insists on doing Peter's laundry herself.

Part 4: Times Square

4-1Just friends

Hoping they can still be friends, Peter meets up with Gwen.

She tells him she has applied for a scholarship to Oxford, and if she gets it, she'll move to England.

4-2The electric man

Before they can talk it through, trouble breaks out. Max, now made of electricity, wanders into Times Square looking for power, drains it, and causes a blackout.

Spider-Man arrives and tries to calm him down. At first, Max is thrilled to meet his hero.

4-3Betrayed

A police sniper fires at Max. Furious, Max decides Spider-Man set him up and lashes out.

As Spider-Man rescues people, all the attention goes to him, and Max's anger grows.

4-4The fire truck

Using water from a fire truck, Spider-Man shorts out Max's power and subdues him.

Gwen realizes the man behind it all is Max, the Oscorp engineer.

4-5Ravencroft

Max is taken to the Ravencroft Institute, which has ties to Oscorp, where the German scientist Dr. Kafka studies and tortures him.

Locked up, Max begins calling himself "Electro." His rage at Spider-Man doesn't fade, and he starts dreaming of using his power to control everything.

Part 5: Harry's plea

5-1The first symptoms

Harry starts to show the same symptoms as his father.

Going through the files in the device Norman gave him, he learns about Oscorp's spider research and concludes that Spider-Man's blood could save his life.

5-2Asking Peter

Peter has been selling photos of Spider-Man to the Daily Bugle. Knowing this, Harry asks him to help find Spider-Man.

Unsure what the blood might do to Harry, Peter tries to talk him out of it.

5-3Spider-Man's answer

Peter appears to Harry as Spider-Man. But fearing another disaster like Dr. Connors in the first film, he refuses to give his blood.

Feeling abandoned, Harry comes to hate Spider-Man.

5-4Max's erased records

Gwen searches Oscorp's files for Max. Realizing the company is covering up the accident, she is marked as a threat by Menken, and men come after her.

Peter distracts them so she can get to the elevator and escape.

5-5Menken's trap

Menken and the board frame Harry for covering up Max's accident, oust him as CEO, and take control of the company.

Felicia, who knew all of Norman's projects, tells Harry that equipment and a serum that might save him are stored inside the company.

Part 6: Dad's secret and the Goblin

6-1A deal with Electro

Harry visits Electro at Ravencroft and makes a deal: he will free him if Electro gets him back inside the Oscorp building. Electro agrees.

Once free, Electro is given a new suit to control his powers. United by their hatred of Spider-Man, the two become allies.

6-2The spider venom

Back inside Oscorp, Harry finds armor and weapons his father had made, along with venom from the genetically altered spiders. He forces Menken to inject him with it.

Instead of curing him, the venom twists his body and mind, transforming him into a goblin-like creature. The armor's emergency system kicks in and keeps him alive. Harry now has his father's armor and a flying glider.

With Harry's help, Electro sets out to take over the city's power grid, the very system he helped design.

6-3Talking with May

Peter presses May about his parents. Through tears, she tells him about the time his parents left him behind.

6-4Tokens in the calculator

Peter discovers a stash of subway tokens hidden in his father's calculator, stamped "Roosevelt."

Remembering May saying his father often rode the subway, he researches the name and finds there is an abandoned subway station called Roosevelt.

6-5The hidden lab

Peter enters the abandoned station and puts a token in the turnstile. A train car rises from the tracks: his father's secret lab.

Inside is the video his father recorded at the start of the film.

6-6Dad's video

In the video, Richard explains that he had to leave New York because he refused to let Norman Osborn turn their work into biological weapons.

He also reveals that he used his own DNA in the spiders, so only someone of his bloodline could take on their power. That is why Peter survived the bite and became the only successful result.

Part 7: The power station and the clock tower

7-1Gwen's voicemail

Peter gets a voicemail from Gwen: she has won the Oxford scholarship and is leaving for the airport earlier than planned.

7-2Choosing Gwen

Peter races to catch up with Gwen and tells her he loves her. He says he will go wherever she goes, and they agree to move to England together.

7-3Citywide blackout

Just then, Electro seizes the power station whose grid he helped design, and the whole city goes dark, putting all of New York in danger.

Peter heads off to fight. To keep Gwen out of danger, he webs her to a police car and leaves her behind.

7-4Working together

At the power station, Spider-Man battles Electro. Gwen arrives in a police car and rams Electro. She tells Peter he can't keep her away from danger; the choice is hers.

Spider-Man repairs the damage and Gwen restarts the grid, so that together they overload Electro with his own electricity.

7-5Electro's end

Overloaded with more power than he can hold, Electro explodes. Max dies, and the lights come back on across the city.

7-6The monster from the sky

The transformed Harry arrives in armor, riding a glider. Seeing Gwen there, he realizes Spider-Man is Peter.

Swearing revenge for being denied the blood, he grabs Gwen and flies off with her.

7-7The clock tower

Harry takes Gwen to the top of a clock tower. Among its giant turning gears, Spider-Man and Harry fight fiercely. Spider-Man fights while trying to protect Gwen. In the end, Spider-Man subdues him.

7-8Gwen's death

During the fight, Gwen falls down through the tower. Spider-Man shoots a web line and catches her, but it stretches too far, and she hits the ground. She dies.

Part 8: Rising again

8-1Five months

Crushed by his failure to save Gwen, Peter quits being Spider-Man. As the seasons pass, he visits her grave almost every day.

Five months go by with no sign of Spider-Man in New York.

8-2Harry at Ravencroft

Harry is locked up at Ravencroft, recovering from his transformation. Gustav Fiers comes to see him: the mysterious man who visited Dr. Connors in his cell at the end of the first film.

Harry orders Fiers to break Aleksei Sytsevich out of prison and equip him with Oscorp weapons.

8-3The Rhino

Broken out of prison, Aleksei is fitted with a rhino-shaped mechanical suit built by Oscorp, with machine guns on its arms and missile launchers on its shoulders. Calling himself "the Rhino," he starts tearing up the streets.

8-4Gwen's speech

Peter watches the video of Gwen's graduation speech again. Inspired by her words, he decides to be Spider-Man once more.

8-5Spider-Man returns

A little boy in a Spider-Man costume steps up to face the Rhino. Then the real Spider-Man returns and goes to face the Rhino. The film ends here.

Summary

Peter and Gwen (in order)

- The promise (Part 2): Peter is haunted by his promise to the late Captain Stacy to keep Gwen out of danger and can't commit. Gwen breaks up with him.

- Just friends (Part 4): They meet again, and Gwen says she may go to Oxford.

- The confession (Part 7): Hearing she's leaving for the airport, Peter catches her, tells her he loves her, and decides to go to England with her.

- The end (Parts 7–8): Gwen chooses to join the fight at the power station and helps defeat Electro, but dies in the clock tower fight with Harry. Peter quits, then returns, moved by her speech.

How Harry changes

- The illness (Part 3): Norman tells him the family disease will strike him too.

- Refused (Part 5): He believes Spider-Man's blood can cure him, is turned down, and comes to hate him. Menken then frames him and ousts him as CEO.

- Transformed (Part 6): He teams up with Electro to get back into Oscorp and injects the spider venom, becoming a goblin-like creature.

- After (Parts 7–8): Having worked out who Spider-Man is, he takes Gwen for revenge. Defeated and locked in Ravencroft, he has Fiers free Aleksei as the Rhino.

Who died (in order)

- The pilot (Part 1): killed by the man sent to assassinate Richard.

- Richard and Mary (Part 1): Peter's parents. Attacked by the assassin, they die when the pilotless plane crashes.

- Norman Osborn (Part 3): Harry's father, who dies of the hereditary illness.

- Max Dillon / Electro (Part 7): at the power station, Spider-Man and Gwen overload him with electricity and he explodes.

- Gwen Stacy (Part 7): taken to the clock tower by Harry for revenge on Spider-Man, she falls during the fight. Peter's web reaches her, but she hits the ground and dies.
