Part 1: The parents' plane

A recorded message

Years earlier, Peter's father Richard records a video about his research and boards a private plane with Peter's mother, Mary.

Struggle in the sky

An assassin attacks them aboard the plane. Richard finishes uploading his data, but the plane goes down.

Part 2: Graduation day

The truck heist

In the present, Spider-Man chases Aleksei Sytsevich through the city after he steals a truck of dangerous cargo. Along the way he saves Oscorp engineer Max Dillon.

Just in time

Peter barely makes it to graduation, in time to hear Gwen's valedictorian speech.

The captain's face

Peter keeps seeing Gwen's late father. Haunted by his promise, he breaks up with her.

Part 3: An old friend returns

Harry and Norman

Peter's childhood friend Harry returns to his dying father, Norman, who tells him the family illness will claim him too before he dies.

The young CEO

Harry takes over Oscorp. From his father's research, he comes to believe Spider-Man's blood could cure him.

Max's birthday

Max, now Spider-Man's devoted fan, is left alone fixing equipment at Oscorp on his birthday. An accident sends him into a tank of electric eels.

Part 4: Electro

Times Square

Turned into living electricity, Max drains power in Times Square. Spider-Man tries to calm him, but police fire and the giant screens make Max feel betrayed.

Electro

Subdued by Spider-Man, Max is locked up at Ravencroft and experimented on. He starts calling himself Electro.

Harry's plea

Harry begs Spider-Man for his blood. Unsure what it might do to him, Peter refuses, and Harry is crushed.

Gwen's news

Gwen tells Peter she's been offered a place at Oxford in England.

Part 5: Dad's secret

The subway lab

Following clues his father left, Peter finds a hidden lab in an abandoned subway station.

Dad's video

In the video, Richard explains that Oscorp wanted to turn his work into weapons. He refused, and because he'd used his own DNA in the spiders, no one else could use them.

Harry and Electro

Forced out of Oscorp, Harry frees Electro from Ravencroft and joins forces with him. He then injects himself with Oscorp's spider venom and is transformed.

Part 6: The power plant and the clock tower

Choosing Gwen

Peter catches up with Gwen and tells her he'll follow her to England.

The power plant

Electro seizes the power station and blacks out the city. Working together, Peter and Gwen overload him and stop him.

Harry transformed

The transformed Harry arrives on a glider, works out Spider-Man's identity and snatches Gwen.

The clock tower

Peter defeats Harry in the clock tower, but when his web catches the falling Gwen, it's too late. Gwen dies.

Part 7: Rising again

Five months

Devastated, Peter gives up being Spider-Man and visits Gwen's grave every day.

Harry at Ravencroft

A mysterious man visits Harry at Ravencroft, and Harry tells him to start assembling a team of special people.

Gwen's words

Rewatching Gwen's graduation speech, Peter finds the strength to carry on.

The Rhino

Aleksei rampages through the streets in a rhino-shaped battle suit. As a little boy in a Spider-Man costume stands up to him, the real Spider-Man returns.
