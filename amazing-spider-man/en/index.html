StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

Part 1: The vanished parents and Dad's briefcase

After his father Richard's study is ransacked, young Peter's parents leave him with Uncle Ben and Aunt May and vanish.

As a teenager, Peter finds his father's briefcase in the basement and learns that Richard worked at Oscorp with Dr. Curt Connors.

Part 2: The Oscorp spiders

Posing as another student, Peter gets into Oscorp, where Gwen is leading the interns, and is bitten by a genetically modified spider in a restricted room.

On the subway home he discovers sticky hands and astonishing strength and speed.

Part 3: Uncle Ben

Peter gives Connors his father's equation and, absorbed in the work, forgets to pick up May.

After arguing with Ben, Peter storms out and lets a store robber get away. Ben, out looking for Peter, tries to stop that robber and is shot dead.

Part 4: Spider-Man is born

Peter makes a mask, a suit and web-shooters and starts catching criminals while hunting Ben's killer.

At dinner he argues with Gwen's father, Captain Stacy, who sees Spider-Man as a menace, then reveals his secret to Gwen and they kiss.

Part 5: The Lizard

Fired after refusing to rush human trials, Connors injects himself with the unfinished serum. His arm grows back, but he becomes the Lizard.

Spider-Man saves people on the bridge, yet the police hunt them both. From a camera Peter drops in the sewers, Connors learns who he is.

Part 6: A plan for the city

After attacking the school, the Lizard plans to spray the serum from Oscorp Tower and turn the whole city into reptiles. Gwen makes an antidote.

Cornered by police, Peter is unmasked by Captain Stacy and shot in the leg, but the father of the boy he saved rallies crane operators to make a path to the tower.

Part 7: The tower and the promise

With the captain's help, Peter swaps in the antidote and cures the city and Connors, but the Lizard fatally wounds the captain, who makes Peter promise to keep Gwen out of it.

Peter pulls away from Gwen, but in the last class he hints he may not keep that promise.

Key terms

Oscorp
A big New York science company where Peter's father and Dr. Connors did their research.
Cross-species genetics
Research into healing the human body with genes from other animals. Dr. Connors hopes lizard DNA can regrow his missing arm.
Web-shooters
Wrist devices Peter builds himself to fire web lines. In this version the webs come from the gadgets, not his body.
The Lizard
A huge, reptilian monster.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Is it connected to the Tobey Maguire films?

No. It's a separate series that retells Spider-Man from the start. The two Peters do eventually meet in Spider-Man: No Way Home.

Who is the Lizard?

Dr. Connors. Pressured by Oscorp to rush human trials, he injects himself with the unfinished serum and becomes the monster.

What was the promise to Captain Stacy?

As he dies, the captain makes Peter promise to keep Gwen out of danger. Peter pulls away from her, but the final scene hints he may not keep that promise.

Who's the man in the mid-credits scene?

A shadowy figure visits Connors in his cell and asks whether he told Peter the truth about his father. His identity isn't revealed in this film.

Easy-to-miss details

The crane run

When Peter is injured, crane operators line up their cranes to give him a path to the tower.

Stan Lee's cameo

Stan Lee plays a school librarian who, headphones on, completely misses the fight going on behind him.

A wisecracking hero

This Spider-Man cracks jokes mid-fight and is handy on a skateboard.
