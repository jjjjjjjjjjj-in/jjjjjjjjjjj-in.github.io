Part 1: The vanished parents and Dad's briefcase

1-1A ransacked study

Young Peter is playing hide-and-seek at home when he finds his father Richard's study broken into and ransacked.

Richard goes pale at the sight and hurriedly gathers papers he had hidden away.

1-2Left with his aunt and uncle

That night Richard and Peter's mother, Mary, leave him with Richard's brother Ben and Ben's wife May.

His parents drive off without saying where they're going, and Peter never sees them again.

1-3Peter in high school

Years later, Peter is a student at Midtown Science High School in New York. He's smart but awkward, loves photography and skateboards to school.

He has a crush on his classmate Gwen Stacy.

1-4Beaten by Flash

At school, the bully Flash dangles another student upside down and tells Peter to take a picture. Peter refuses and tells him to put the kid down, and Flash beats him up.

Gwen steps in and sends Flash off by bringing up the homework he hasn't done. She's impressed that Peter stood up to him.

1-5The briefcase in the basement

The basement floods, and while cleaning up, Ben comes across Richard's old briefcase.

Hidden inside are research papers and a photo of Richard with Dr. Curt Connors. Ben explains that Connors was Richard's friend and research partner at Oscorp.

Part 2: The Oscorp spiders

2-1Posing as an intern

Wanting to learn about his father, Peter goes to Oscorp Tower, uses another student's name badge to pose as him.

The interns' guide turns out to be Gwen, who works at Oscorp as an intern herself. She sees Peter and knows the name is wrong.

2-2Dr. Connors's research

Dr. Connors, who is missing his right arm, is trying to give humans a lizard's ability to regrow lost limbs, using genes from other species, work he once did with Richard.

He tells the interns about his dream of a world without weakness, but a key piece of the puzzle is still unsolved.

2-3The spider room

Peter slips away from the tour into a restricted room where countless genetically modified spiders are spinning strong cable.

Spiders drop onto him. On his way out, Gwen catches him and takes back the badge, and just then a spider bites him on the back of the neck.

2-4Powers on the subway

On the subway home, Peter dozes off. When a passenger presses a cold bottle to his face, he jolts awake and ends up stuck to the ceiling.

His hands stick to everything, a scuffle breaks out with angry men, and with sudden speed and strength he knocks them all down. Frightened, he flees the train.

2-5Changes

At home Peter devours food at an astonishing pace, to Ben and May's surprise.

The next morning he smashes his alarm clock and breaks the bathroom faucet. He can't control his strength yet.

Part 3: Uncle Ben

3-1The basketball incident

At school Flash challenges Peter on the basketball court, and Peter beats him easily, but he's so strong that he shatters the backboard.

Ben switches his work shift to come to the school for Peter, and asks Peter to pick up May after work that night.

3-2At Connors's home

Peter visits Dr. Connors at home and reveals he is Richard's son.

He shows Connors an equation from his father's papers that calculates how fast living cells decay, the missing piece of Connors's research. Connors is thrilled and invites Peter to his lab at Oscorp.

3-3The lab mouse

In the Oscorp lab, Peter and Connors run the equation and inject a three-legged mouse with the formula. Later the mouse regrows its missing leg.

Caught up in the work, Peter completely forgets his promise to pick up May.

3-4The fight with Ben

When Peter gets home, Ben scolds him. He tells Peter that Richard believed that if you could do good things for other people, you had a moral obligation to do them.

Stung at the mention of his father, Peter snaps back that his father walked out on him, and storms out. Ben goes out into the night to look for him.

3-5The store robbery

Peter tries to buy milk at a convenience store but is a little short, and the clerk won't sell it to him.

Moments later another customer robs the register and runs. The clerk asks Peter to stop him, but Peter says it's not his problem and lets him go.

3-6Ben is shot

Ben, still searching for Peter, tries to grab the fleeing robber. In the struggle, Ben is shot with the robber's gun.

Peter runs up, and Ben dies in front of him. The robber gets away.

Part 4: Spider-Man is born

4-1Hunting the killer

Grieving and furious, Peter takes the police sketch and roams the streets at night, roughing up men who match the description.

But he never finds the real killer. Gwen comforts Peter after Ben's death.

4-2The idea for a mask

Chased by a gang, Peter crashes into an old building, where he sees a poster of a masked wrestler and gets the idea of hiding his face.

4-3Suit and web-shooters

Peter makes a spider-patterned suit out of stretchy spandex. He orders Oscorp's spider cable and builds web-shooters that fire it from his wrists.

He practices swinging between buildings on his webs.

4-4The car thief

One night a car thief climbs into a car only to find Peter, now in costume, waiting in the back seat. The thief pulls a knife, but Peter toys with him and wraps him up in webbing.

Word spreads of a masked man catching criminals, and Gwen's father, police Captain George Stacy, sees him as a dangerous vigilante.

4-5Asking Gwen out

In the school hallway, Peter awkwardly asks Gwen out. She invites him to dinner at her family's apartment.

4-6Dinner at the Stacys'

At dinner, Captain Stacy calls Spider-Man a menace who ignores the law. Peter counters that Spider-Man helps people in ways the police can't, and the two argue.

Peter leaves the table and heads out on an awkward note.

4-7Telling Gwen

Gwen follows Peter outside. Up on the roof, he reveals to her that he is Spider-Man.

Shocked but accepting, she kisses him for the first time.

Part 5: The Lizard

5-1Pressure to test

Oscorp executive Ratha demands that Connors begin human trials at once, for the sake of the company's dying head, Norman Osborn.

Connors refuses to put innocent people at risk. Ratha fires him and leaves, saying he'll test the serum at a veterans' hospital instead.

5-2Testing on himself

Desperate, Connors injects himself with the unfinished serum in his lab. He passes out and wakes to find that his missing right arm has grown back.

But his body begins slowly turning reptilian.

5-3The Williamsburg Bridge

Connors lies in wait on the Williamsburg Bridge to stop Ratha on his way to the hospital. There he turns into a huge humanoid reptile, the Lizard, and starts hurling cars off the bridge.

Ratha's car is among those sent over the edge. Spider-Man arrives and catches car after car with his webs, saving the people inside.

5-4The boy in the car

One car dangles from the bridge with a boy, Jack, trapped inside. The car catches fire, and he's too scared to move.

Spider-Man puts his own mask on the boy to give him courage. The boy climbs up the web and is reunited with his father. The Lizard disappears.

5-5Both are hunted

The Lizard escapes into the sewers, where he changes back into Connors.

Unable to explain what happened, the police launch a citywide manhunt for both the Lizard and Spider-Man.

Thinking of the lizard-gene research and the regrown arm, Peter starts to suspect that the Lizard is Dr. Connors.

5-6An ambush in the sewers

Peter strings webs through the sewers and waits for the Lizard. When it attacks, he barely escapes, badly hurt.

He drops his camera, which has his name on it. Connors finds it and learns that Spider-Man is Peter.

5-7Warning the captain

Peter comes home covered in injuries, and May worries about him.

He goes to the police and tells Captain Stacy that the Lizard is Dr. Connors, but the captain doesn't take him seriously.

Part 6: A plan for the city

6-1The school is attacked

Having tracked Peter down from the name on the camera, the Lizard attacks his high school, tearing through the halls and fighting Peter as Spider-Man.

Right behind them, the school librarian (Stan Lee) listens to music on headphones, completely oblivious. The Lizard escapes into the sewers.

6-2The sewer lab

Peter follows and finds the makeshift lab Connors has set up in the sewers.

From Connors's own recordings, he learns the plan: to spray the serum over the city from a device atop Oscorp Tower and turn everyone into reptiles like himself.

6-3Gwen makes the antidote

Peter calls Gwen at Oscorp and tells her to get out. Instead, using Connors's research, Gwen makes an antidote that can turn the reptile changes back.

The Lizard arrives at Oscorp and takes the device up to the top of the tower.

6-4The officers in the sewers

Police officers who went into the sewers after the Lizard are turned into reptiles by him.

6-5The captain learns the truth

As Spider-Man races to the tower, police officers surround him. Captain Stacy pulls off the mask and discovers that Spider-Man is Peter.

Peter tells him Gwen is at Oscorp. Stacy lets him go, but another officer shoots Peter, wounding his leg.

6-6The crane run

With his wounded leg, Peter can't swing well between buildings.

The father of Jack, the boy he saved on the bridge, is a crane operator. He calls on his fellow operators, and they swing their cranes out along the avenue, forming a path to Oscorp Tower. Peter swings along it to the tower.

Part 7: The tower and the promise

7-1Battle on the roof

On the roof of the tower, Peter fights the Lizard while trying to load Gwen's antidote into the device.

Captain Stacy arrives and shoots at the Lizard with his gun, buying Peter time.

7-2The swap

Peter swaps the antidote into the device. The cloud it releases spreads over the city, turning the Lizard back into Dr. Connors and curing the officers who had been changed.

But just before that, the Lizard's claws had mortally wounded Captain Stacy.

7-3Connors saves Peter

Exhausted, Peter nearly falls from the edge of the tower. Connors, human again, grabs him and pulls him to safety.

7-4The captain's request

Before he dies, Captain Stacy gives Peter back his mask. He warns that Peter will make enemies and that the people around him will get hurt, and makes him promise to leave Gwen out of it.

Then he dies. Connors is taken into custody.

7-5Pulling away from Gwen

To keep his promise, Peter keeps his distance from Gwen. When he tells her they can't be together, she realizes her father made him promise.

Meanwhile, Spider-Man keeps helping people across New York at night.

7-6The last class

Back at school, Peter walks into class late. When the teacher says not to make promises you can't keep, Peter quietly remarks that those are the best kind.

Hearing that, Gwen smiles.

7-7The man in the cell (mid-credits)

Partway through the credits, a mysterious man steps out of the shadows in Connors's prison cell.

He asks whether Connors told the boy the truth about his father. Connors says he didn't and tells him to leave the boy alone. Lightning flashes, and the man is gone.

Summary

Key threads

- The parents' mystery (Parts 1 and 7): when Peter was small, his parents took papers from the ransacked study, left him with Ben and May and vanished. His father's equation advanced Connors's research and led to the Lizard. At the end, a mystery man asks Connors about the truth of Richard.

- Peter's responsibility (Parts 3–4): he let a robber go, and that robber shot Ben. Peter starts out hunting the killer and grows into Spider-Man, who helps people.

- Dr. Connors (Parts 5–7): pushed to rush trials, he injected the unfinished serum and became the Lizard. He tried to turn the whole city into reptiles, but Gwen's antidote restored him, and in the end he saved Peter from falling.

- The promise (Part 7): Peter promised the dying Captain Stacy to keep Gwen out of it and pulled away from her, but in the last class he hints he may not be able to keep that promise.

Who died or was hurt (in order)

- Ben Parker (Part 3): searching for Peter after he stormed out, Ben tries to grab the robber fleeing the store and is shot in the struggle.

- Peter (Parts 5–6): badly hurt by the Lizard in the sewers, and shot in the leg by a police officer on his way to the tower.

- The officers in the sewers (Part 6): turned into reptiles by the Lizard, then cured by the antidote cloud from the tower.

- Captain George Stacy (Part 7): helps Peter fight the Lizard on the tower, is gored by its claws and dies after making Peter promise.

- Peter's parents (Part 1): they vanished after leaving him; this film doesn't reveal what happened to them.
