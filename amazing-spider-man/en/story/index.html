Part 1: The vanished parents

A ransacked study

Young Peter comes home to find his father Richard's study ransacked. Richard gathers his papers, and he and Peter's mother leave the boy with Uncle Ben and Aunt May before disappearing.

Teenage Peter

Years later, Peter is a high schooler into photography and science, picked on by Flash Thompson and quietly drawn to classmate Gwen Stacy.

Dad's briefcase

Clearing up a leak in the basement, Peter finds his father's old briefcase, with research papers and a photo of Richard alongside Dr. Curt Connors.

Part 2: The Oscorp spiders

Into Oscorp

Peter sneaks into Oscorp posing as an intern, only to find Gwen leading the interns. Dr. Connors, missing his right arm, researches healing the body with genes from other species.

The spider room

Peter wanders into a room where countless spiders spin cable. On the subway home, he realizes one has bitten his neck.

New powers

On the train, Peter is startled by his body: his hands stick to things, and he's suddenly strong and fast.

Dad's equation

Peter visits Connors at home and shows him an equation from his father's notes. Connors is thrilled, and his research leaps forward.

Part 3: Uncle Ben

The argument

Peter shows off his strength against Flash and gets in trouble. He also forgets to pick up Aunt May, argues with Uncle Ben and storms out.

The store robbery

Snubbed by a store clerk, Peter lets a fleeing robber go. Uncle Ben, out looking for Peter, tries to stop the man and is shot.

Hunting the killer

Grieving and angry, Peter roams the streets at night looking for men who match the killer's description.

Part 4: Spider-Man is born

Mask and suit

Peter makes a mask, then a homemade suit and web-shooters loaded with Oscorp's spider cable.

Dinner at the Stacys'

Invited to Gwen's home, Peter clashes with her father, Captain George Stacy, over the masked vigilante. That night he reveals his secret to Gwen.

Rushed trials

Oscorp executive Ratha demands human trials on behalf of the dying Norman Osborn. Connors refuses, is fired, and injects himself with the unfinished serum.

The Lizard

Connors's arm grows back, but he transforms into a giant reptile, the Lizard. He attacks Ratha on a bridge, and Spider-Man saves a boy trapped in a dangling car.

Part 5: Oscorp Tower

Sewers and school

Suspecting Connors is the Lizard, Peter finds his hidden lab in the sewers. Connors learns who Peter is from the name on a camera he left behind and attacks him at school.

A plan for the city

Connors plans to spread the serum from Oscorp Tower and turn all of New York into lizards. At Oscorp, Gwen prepares an antidote.

The crane run

Peter has been shot in the leg by police. The father of the boy he saved and other crane operators swing their cranes into a path to the tower.

Battle on the tower

Captain Stacy joins the fight, and Peter swaps in the antidote to save the city. Connors returns to normal, but the captain is fatally wounded. Dying, he makes Peter promise to keep Gwen out of it.

Part 6: A promise he can't keep

Pulling away

To keep his promise, Peter distances himself from Gwen, who realizes it's because of her father.

The last class

When a teacher warns against making promises you can't keep, Peter murmurs that those are the best kind, and Gwen smiles.

The man in the cell

Midway through the credits, a mysterious man appears in Connors's cell and asks whether he told Peter the truth about his father. Connors tells him to leave the boy alone, and the man vanishes.
