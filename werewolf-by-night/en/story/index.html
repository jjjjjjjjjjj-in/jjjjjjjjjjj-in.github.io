Part 1: The Bloodstone estate

A fallen leader

Legendary monster hunter Ulysses Bloodstone has died. His widow, Verussa, summons top hunters from around the world to the estate.

The estranged daughter

Among them is Ulysses's estranged daughter, Elsa.

The rules

A monster has been released in the maze with the Bloodstone fixed to its back. Whoever kills it and claims the stone will be the new leader.

Part 2: The hunt

Hunters vs. hunters

Inside the maze, the hunters turn on each other too.

Jack and Elsa

Hunter Jack Russell teams up with Elsa and admits he doesn't want to kill the monster.

Ted

The monster in the maze is Ted, the Man-Thing, Jack's friend. Jack came to rescue him.

Part 3: The werewolf

Captured

Jack and Elsa are captured. Verussa turns the Bloodstone's light on Jack.

The transformation

Jack transforms into a werewolf and tears through the hunters.

Elsa and the werewolf

Elsa speaks to the werewolf, and Jack doesn't harm her.

Verussa's end

Ted touches Verussa and burns her.

Part 4: Separate ways

A new head of the house

Elsa claims the Bloodstone and becomes the head of the house.

Leaving with a friend

Back in human form, Jack leaves the estate with Ted.
