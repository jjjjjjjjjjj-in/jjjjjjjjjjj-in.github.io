Part 1: Bloodstone Manor

1-1Monsters and monster hunters

The film opens in black and white. A narrator explains that besides heroes, the world also holds monsters living in secret, and secret hunters who track them.

Their greatest figure was legendary hunter Ulysses Bloodstone, who used a red gem with power over monsters, the Bloodstone, to kill many of them. Now Ulysses is dead.

1-2Hunters gather at the manor

At the call of Ulysses's widow, Verussa, five skilled hunters from around the world arrive at Bloodstone Manor: Jovan, Azarel, Liorn, Barasso and Jack Russell, strangers to one another until tonight.

Jack wears face paint, which he says is to honor his ancestors. Among the grand surroundings, he seems a little out of place.

1-3Ulysses's funeral

Ulysses's funeral is held at the manor. His corpse stands in an upright coffin, rigged to move and speak.

Verussa presides over the ceremony. With her husband gone, she means to take his place at the head of the hunters.

1-4Elsa, the estranged daughter

Then Ulysses's daughter Elsa arrives. She never fit her family's monster-hunting ways, never got along with her stepmother Verussa, and has been away for years.

Verussa coldly scorns her for abandoning her father's legacy and warns her not to join the hunt. Elsa enters anyway, to win the Bloodstone.

1-5The rules of the hunt

Verussa announces the rules. A captured monster will be released into the great maze on the grounds, with the Bloodstone embedded in its body.

Whoever kills it and claims the stone inherits the Bloodstone and becomes the hunters' new leader.

Part 2: The hunt in the maze

2-1Into the maze

The hunt begins. Armed, the hunters enter the maze at night.

Inside, Jack runs into Elsa. After a brief encounter, they go their separate ways.

2-2Elsa and Liorn

Elsa clashes with one of the hunters, Liorn. She wins the fight, and Liorn dies.

2-3The monster is Ted

Jack finally finds the monster, a huge swamp creature.

But instead of attacking, he calls it by name, Ted, and speaks to it. Ted is the friend Jack has been searching for. Jack posed as a hunter to rescue him.

Jack leaves Ted for the moment and goes to carry out his escape plan.

2-4A pact in the mausoleum

Jack stumbles on Elsa again, hiding in a mausoleum.

She admits she's sick of her father's monster-hunting legacy. They agree to work together: Elsa helps free Ted, and in return the Bloodstone in Ted's body goes to her.

2-5Ted and Jovan

The hunter Jovan attacks Ted. At Ted's touch, Jovan burns to death.

When Ted learns Elsa is with Jack, he leaves her alone.

2-6Out of the maze

Jack breaks through the maze's outer wall to open an escape route.

Elsa removes the Bloodstone from Ted's body, and Ted flees into the forest.

2-7The stone reacts to Jack

But when Jack touches it, the Bloodstone reacts violently: Jack, too, is a monster.

Just then Verussa and the surviving hunters catch up.

Part 3: The werewolf

3-1Caged together

Verussa captures Jack and Elsa and locks them together in a cage inside the manor.

She plans to turn Jack into a monster and let him kill Elsa, ridding herself of her stepdaughter.

3-2The transformation

Verussa turns the Bloodstone's power on Jack, forcing him to transform.

Jack's body becomes a werewolf, shown through Elsa's terrified face and his shadow on the wall.

3-3The werewolf breaks loose

But the werewolf doesn't attack Elsa. It bursts out of the cage and tears through Verussa's guards.

Verussa uses the Bloodstone's power to pin the werewolf down.

3-4Elsa fights back

Elsa also escapes the cage. She fights the two remaining hunters, Azarel and Barasso, and kills them both.

Then she stops Verussa from killing the werewolf.

3-5Elsa and the werewolf

Elsa tries to approach the werewolf. It lunges at her, but recognizes her and backs off without hurting her.

The werewolf leaves the manor.

3-6Verussa's end

Furious, Verussa tries to shoot Elsa. Ted returns and burns her to death.

Ted leaves to find Jack.

Part 4: After the night

4-1The new head of the house

Elsa takes possession of the Bloodstone and the manor, and becomes its new head.

At this moment, the picture shifts from black and white to color.

4-2Morning in the forest

The next morning, Jack wakes in the forest in human form, with Ted watching over him.

Jack is glad to learn Elsa is safe.

Summary

What Jack did

Posing as a hunter, he freed his captured friend Ted from the maze (Part 2).

He is a werewolf. Forced to transform by Verussa, he spared Elsa, cut down the guards and left the manor (Part 3).

What Elsa did

Estranged from the family, she returned for her father's funeral and joined the hunt (Part 1).

She teamed up with Jack to free Ted, and in the end inherited the Bloodstone and the manor (Parts 2–4).

Who died (in order)

- Ulysses (before the story): the legendary hunter, already dead when it begins.

- Liorn (Part 2): dies after fighting Elsa in the maze.

- Jovan (Part 2): attacks Ted and is burned to death by his touch.

- The guards (Part 3): killed by the werewolf after it breaks out of the cage.

- Azarel and Barasso (Part 3): killed by Elsa after she escapes the cage.

- Verussa (Part 3): burned by Ted as she tries to shoot Elsa.
