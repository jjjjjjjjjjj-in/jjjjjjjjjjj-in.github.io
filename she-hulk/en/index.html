Key terms

She-Hulk
Jen's huge, super-strong form. Unlike Bruce, she keeps her own mind when she changes.
The superhuman law division
A new department at a law firm that represents superpowered clients.
Intelligencia
A mysterious online group spreading hate about Jen.
K.E.V.I.N.
An AI that runs Marvel's storytelling, seen in the finale.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why does Jen stay herself when she changes?

As she explains, she's spent her life managing anger and fear. Unlike Bruce, she doesn't become a separate personality.

Who's behind Intelligencia?

An online group that hates female heroes, led by a man named Todd who wants Jen's blood.

What happens in the finale?

Unhappy with the ending, Jen steps outside the show and argues with the AI running it to change the ending.

Easy-to-miss details

A Captain America question

In Episode 1, Jen asks Bruce a cheeky question about Captain America.

Wong and Madisynn

Wong becomes friends with party girl Madisynn.

At the end of the finale, Bruce brings his son home.
