Episode 1: A Normal Amount of Rage

1-1Rehearsing for court

Jennifer "Jen" Walters, a lawyer at the Los Angeles district attorney's office, rehearses an important closing argument with her best friend and coworker Nikki Ramos. Nikki jokes that she can always Hulk out if things go badly.

Jen turns to the viewers and starts explaining, from a few months earlier, how she became a Hulk.

1-2The road trip and the spaceship

A few months earlier, Jen is on a road trip with her cousin Bruce Banner (Smart Hulk). She keeps pestering him about whether Captain America (Steve) died a virgin.

A spaceship from the planet Sakaar suddenly appears in front of them. The car flies off the road and crashes.

As Jen pulls the injured Bruce from the wreck, his blood drips into a cut on her arm.

1-3Jen turns green

Bruce's blood transforms Jen into a huge green form, and she runs off.

That night she stumbles into a bar. In the women's restroom, a group of women help her out and look after her.

Outside, a group of men catcall her. Her anger flares and she transforms again, threatening them, until Bruce catches up and subdues her.

1-4The secret lab in Mexico

Bruce takes Jen to his secret lab in Mexico, which Tony Stark built for him during the Blip.

He explains that they share a rare genetic trait that lets their bodies process gamma radiation, which is why his blood gave her the same powers.

He insists she stay with him until she can master her powers and control her anger.

1-5Training

Bruce teaches Jen how to transform and how to calm herself down. But Jen stays herself when she changes and breezes through his training.

She explains that women spend their lives holding their anger in so they aren't judged, so she is already used to managing it.

Bruce warns her that the world sees Hulks as monsters, and that she must be careful or she will be seen that way too.

1-6No superhero life

Bruce thinks Jen should become a superhero too. Jen refuses: that would mean giving up her normal life and her career, and she wants to go back to being a lawyer.

When she tries to leave, Bruce tries to stop her, and the two fight in their Hulk forms.

Afterward, Bruce reluctantly accepts her choice and says goodbye as she returns to her old life.

1-7Titania in court

Back in the present, Jen is giving her closing argument when Titania, a super-strong social media influencer fleeing her own trial, crashes through the courtroom wall and attacks the bailiffs.

Jen reluctantly transforms and beats Titania with ease.

Back to normal, she finishes her closing argument as if nothing happened.

1-8Mid-credits

Bruce finally answers Jen's question: Steve lost his virginity in 1943, to a woman he met while on the USO tour.

Episode 2: Superhuman Law

2-1"She-Hulk" and getting fired

After beating Titania, Jen is dubbed "She-Hulk" by the public, a name she dislikes.

The trial is declared a mistrial because her fight may have influenced the jury, and the district attorney's office fires her.

2-2Job hunting

Jen tries other law firms, but none will hire her: a famous superhero practicing law is seen as a distraction.

2-3Family dinner

Jen goes to dinner at her parents' house. Her father Morris, her mother Elaine, her cousin Ched and the rest of the family already know she was fired, and they pepper her with questions.

Only her father really listens to her troubles.

2-4An offer from GLK&H

The offer comes from GLK&H (Goodman, Lieber, Kurtzberg & Holliway), the firm on the other side of her old case. Jen impulsively accepts, on the condition that she can bring Nikki as her paralegal.

On her first day, top partner Holden Holliway tells her she will lead the firm's new superhuman law division, but as She-Hulk, not as Jennifer Walters. Her coworker Augustus "Pug" Pugliese is also on the team.

2-5Her first client: Abomination

Her first case is to represent Emil Blonsky (Abomination) at his parole hearing. Because he once tried to kill Bruce, Jen refuses at first.

She visits him in a Department of Damage Control supermax prison, where powers are not allowed, so she has to change back. Blonsky says trying to kill the Hulk wasn't personal, and he seems genuinely sorry for his crimes.

2-6Bruce on the spaceship

Jen calls Bruce to ask if he's okay with her taking the case. He believes Blonsky has changed and gives his blessing.

Bruce is aboard the Sakaaran ship from the first episode, heading off into space.

2-7The news footage

Right after Jen tells Holliway she'll take the case, the news shows leaked footage of Blonsky, supposedly in prison, fighting as Abomination in an underground fight club.

2-8Mid-credits

She-Hulk holds up a car while Ched and her father chat and change the tire.

Episode 3: The People vs. Emil Blonsky

3-1Blonsky's side of the story

Jen confronts Blonsky about the fight club footage. He explains that the Sorcerer Supreme, Wong, took him from his cell by magic, and that he returned to prison willingly afterward.

Jen tries to reach Wong.

3-2Online backlash

News that She-Hulk is representing Abomination spreads, and the internet floods with nasty comments about her.

3-3Bukowski's case

Dennis Bukowski, Jen's former colleague at the DA's office, comes to GLK&H. His "girlfriend," the rapper Megan Thee Stallion, was really Runa, a shape-shifting Light Elf from New Asgard, who defrauded him.

The case goes to Pug.

3-4Wong arrives

Wong comes to see Jen. He confirms he took Blonsky out of his cell and agrees to testify at the parole hearing.

3-5The parole hearing

At the hearing, Wong and other witnesses speak in Blonsky's favor.

To answer whether he might lose control, Blonsky transforms into Abomination on the spot, stays calm and rational, and easily changes back.

He is granted parole but forbidden to transform again, and must wear an inhibitor device made by Bruce.

3-6Pug's case

Jen helps Pug with Bukowski's case. The real Megan Thee Stallion appears, Pug wins, and Runa is sanctioned by the court for trying to impersonate the judge.

3-7The interview and the attack

As She-Hulk, Jen gives a TV interview to tell her side of the story.

On her way home, the Wrecking Crew, four thugs armed with Asgardian construction tools stolen from New Asgard, attack her. They were hired by an anonymous client to take a sample of her blood.

But the needle breaks on her skin. Jen remembers she can change, becomes She-Hulk and drives them off.

3-8Mid-credits

She-Hulk and Megan Thee Stallion twerk together at the office.

Episode 4: Is This Not Real Magic?

4-1Donny Blaze's show

Donny Blaze, a magician who trained at Kamar-Taj but was kicked out for misusing the Mystic Arts, now performs at the Mystic Castle.

During a show, he sends audience member Madisynn King through a portal to another dimension. Confused, she ends up making a deal with a demon before landing in Wong's home at Kamar-Taj.

4-2Wong's case

Wong asks Jen to sue Donny, who broke a core rule of the Mystic Arts: never use them for personal gain or without proper safeguards.

Seeing a chance to set a new legal precedent, Jen takes the case against Donny and the Mystic Castle's owner, Cornelius P. Willows.

4-3The dating app

Jen signs up for a dating app to broaden her social life. Nikki helps, but her profile as Jen gets almost no attention.

When she switches to a She-Hulk profile, the matches pour in.

4-4The magic hearing

In court, Jen argues that Donny shouldn't practice the Mystic Arts without the Sorcerer Supreme's permission, and Madisynn testifies.

4-5The date with Arthur, and the demons

As She-Hulk, Jen goes on a date with Arthur, a match from the app, and things go well.

Then Wong steps through a portal asking for help: Donny has used magic at another show and unleashed a swarm of demons.

Jen and Wong send the demons back and scare Donny and Willows into obeying a cease-and-desist order. Jen goes back to Arthur.

4-6The morning after, and Titania's lawsuit

The next morning, Arthur finds Jen in her normal form, is clearly disappointed she isn't She-Hulk, and awkwardly leaves. Jen is crushed.

Then she learns Titania is out of jail and suing her: Titania has trademarked the name "She-Hulk" and claims Jen's use of it is infringement.

4-7Mid-credits

Madisynn is back at Wong's place, and the two hang out watching TV like old friends.

Episode 5: Mean, Green, and Straight Poured into These Jeans

5-1Losing her name

Titania has launched a line of beauty products under the name "She-Hulk," and her billboards are all over Los Angeles. Jen is furious.

Holliway tells her to deal with it fast and assigns the firm's lawyer Mallory Book to represent her. Mallory resents Jen for getting to lead the superhuman law division.

5-2Tailor Luke Jacobson

Since Jen's clothes rip every time she transforms, Nikki and Pug plan to get her an outfit from Luke Jacobson, an exclusive tailor who secretly makes suits for heroes.

Struggling to get a meeting, they lie and claim Titania sent them.

5-3The countersuit

Jen and Mallory countersue, arguing that Titania is illegally profiting off She-Hulk's fame.

But the fact that Jen once said "She-Hulk" isn't her name works against her.

5-4Todd, and the dating record

Jen is annoyed to learn that Todd, one of her failed dates, is also a client of GLK&H.

But it gives her an idea: on the dating app, she had been calling herself She-Hulk before Titania ever registered the trademark.

5-5The dates testify

In court, her past dates are called one after another and testify that she introduced herself as She-Hulk.

Jen wins. A humiliated Titania is ordered to stop selling every product bearing the She-Hulk name.

5-6Common ground with Mallory

After the trial, Jen and Mallory find common ground as women in the same world and warm to each other a little.

5-7The new suit

Jacobson agrees to design business wear that works for both Jen and She-Hulk, plus a hidden superhero suit.

His workshop also holds a suit for another hero: Daredevil, who appears later.

Episode 6: Just Jen

6-1Lulu's wedding

Jen is asked to be a bridesmaid at her old friend Lulu's wedding and shows up to the reception as She-Hulk.

But Lulu wants her there as just Jen, not She-Hulk, and loads her with pre-wedding chores. Disappointed, Jen helps out in her normal form.

6-2Titania is there too

Titania is at the wedding too, invited because she is dating one of the groomsmen.

6-3Meeting Josh

Jen hits it off with Josh Miller, a friend of the groom.

6-4The fight with Titania

Titania attacks Jen and demands a fight. Jen transforms and fights back.

Before it can become a big brawl, Titania falls on her face and breaks her veneers. Humiliated, she storms off.

6-5Mister Immortal's divorce

Back at the firm, Nikki and Mallory handle a divorce case for Mister Immortal, a superhuman who can't die. He has faked his death again and again to get out of his marriages.

When the website Intelligencia posts a video of his apparent death, his former spouses learn the truth and eight of them sue. Nikki works out a compensation deal for all eight.

6-6The Intelligencia posts

Nikki and Mallory find Intelligencia's message board full of hateful posts and death threats aimed at She-Hulk, run by someone called "HulkKing."

Nikki leaves Jen a voicemail warning her.

6-7Scientists watching

Somewhere, scientists are spying on Jen and planning another attempt to get her blood for "HulkKing."

Episode 7: The Retreat

7-1Josh goes quiet

Jen goes on several dates with Josh, and they sleep together. Then he stops answering. Jen keeps waiting for a text.

7-2A call from the parole officer

Blonsky's parole officer, Chuck Donelan, calls: Blonsky's inhibitor seems to be broken, and he wants Jen along to check on him, in case She-Hulk is needed.

7-3Blonsky's retreat

Blonsky runs a spiritual retreat, Summer Twilights. His inhibitor only glitched, and Chuck fixes it.

After Chuck leaves, two guests, Man-Bull and El Águila, wreck Jen's car in a scuffle, so she has to stay until a tow truck can take it away.

7-4No signal

The retreat has no internet or cell service, but Jen keeps nervously hunting for a signal in case Josh replies.

7-5The group session

Jen joins Blonsky's group therapy session with Man-Bull, El Águila, Porcupine, Saracen (who says he's a vampire), and Wrecker of the Wrecking Crew, who attacked her in Episode 3.

Jen is angry when she recognizes Wrecker, but the group calms her down, and Wrecker apologizes.

Jen admits she feels people like She-Hulk more than her, and that she wants to be liked as Jen. Encouraged by the group, she decides to delete Josh's number and let him go.

7-6In the tow truck

Riding back to town in the tow truck, Jen deletes Josh's contact.

7-7Three days earlier

Three days earlier: after sleeping with Jen, Josh secretly cloned her phone and took a sample of her blood while she slept. He was working for HulkKing.

Episode 8: Ribbit and Rip It

8-1Leap-Frog's failure

Self-styled hero Leap-Frog (Eugene Patilio), in a frog-like suit, tries to fight some thugs, but his rocket boots fail in mid-air.

Eugene, son of GLK&H client Vincent Patilio, wants to sue the suit's maker, Luke Jacobson. Jen has to take the case against the man who makes her own clothes.

8-2In court with Matt Murdock

Jacobson's lawyer is Matt Murdock, a blind attorney from New York.

Matt gets Eugene to admit he ignored Jacobson's instructions and put jet fuel in his boots, and wins the case.

8-3Talking at the bar

After the trial, Jen and Matt meet at a bar. He buys her a drink.

He tells her she's in a unique position to help people, as a lawyer and as She-Hulk. They connect, but Matt leaves suddenly.

8-4Fighting Daredevil

That night Eugene calls Jen for help against an unknown attacker. She arrives as She-Hulk and fights him.

The attacker is Matt, in his vigilante identity as Daredevil. He reveals that Eugene kidnapped Jacobson to fix his suit and make suits for his followers.

8-5Rescuing Jacobson

Jen and Matt storm Leap-Frog's lair, take down his followers, the Tadpoles, and rescue Jacobson. As Matt fights in a hallway, She-Hulk crashes down through the ceiling.

Leap-Frog tries to escape by leaping out the window, shouting "Ribbit and rip it!", but falls and hurts himself. The Tadpoles are arrested and he is carried off on a stretcher.

That night, Jen and Matt sleep together at her place.

8-6The awards gala

The next evening, Jen attends the Southern California Law Awards and accepts the Female Lawyer of the Year award, shared with five other women lawyers.

8-7The Intelligencia video

As Jen gives her speech, Intelligencia hijacks the screen and plays secretly recorded footage of her in bed with Josh.

Losing control, she transforms, smashes the screen and the stage, and grabs an Intelligencia member who was filming her. But Department of Damage Control agents stop her, and the man gets away.

Episode 9: Whose Show Is This?

9-1No more She-Hulk

Jen is released from Damage Control custody but must wear an inhibitor that stops her from transforming. She loses her job at GLK&H and moves back in with her parents.

9-2Hunting for HulkKing

Jen can't find anything on Intelligencia or its creator, HulkKing, so she heads to Blonsky's retreat for advice.

Meanwhile, Nikki manages to get an invitation to HulkKing's private gathering and sends Pug in undercover.

9-3Who's behind it

The gathering is at Blonsky's retreat too. Pug learns that HulkKing is Todd Phelps, one of Jen's old dates.

Blonsky is there as Abomination, working as a motivational speaker, unaware of what Intelligencia really wants.

9-4Todd becomes a Hulk

Jen stumbles onto the gathering and confronts Todd, who injects himself with a serum made from her blood and becomes a Hulk.

Then Titania and Bruce, back from space, show up. Titania fights the Intelligencia members and Bruce fights Blonsky, and it all turns to chaos.

9-5Leaving the show

Fed up with the chaos, Jen smashes her inhibitor, transforms, and talks to the viewers.

She climbs through the Disney+ menu into Marvel Studios: Assembled and storms the show's writers' room. The writers tell her that "Kevin" decides the ending.

9-6K.E.V.I.N.

Inside Marvel Studios, Jen meets K.E.V.I.N., an artificial intelligence that claims to make every story decision in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

She complains about formulaic big-fight endings, brings up how many heroes have "daddy issues," asks when the X-Men are coming, and even pitches ideas for a second season.

K.E.V.I.N. reluctantly agrees to rewrite the climax.

9-7The rewritten ending

Back in the show, Todd never becomes a Hulk and is arrested with the rest of Intelligencia. Jen tells him she'll see him in court.

Titania gives a TV interview, and Blonsky agrees to go back to prison for ten years for violating his parole.

9-8Family gathering

Jen celebrates at home with her family and Matt, who is brought back because she asked K.E.V.I.N. for him.

Then Bruce arrives, back from Sakaar, and introduces his son, Skaar.

9-9Lawyer and hero

Some time later, cleared of all charges and back at her job, Jen heads to her case against Todd and Intelligencia.

When a reporter asks whether she'll be a lawyer or a superhero, She-Hulk answers "both" and walks into the courthouse.

9-10Mid-credits

Wong opens a portal into Blonsky's cell, breaks him out of prison, and takes him to Kamar-Taj.

Summary (spoilers through the finale)

Jen's cases (in order)

- Titania's case (Ep. 1): as a prosecutor. Beating Titania in court causes a mistrial and gets Jen fired.

- Blonsky's parole (Eps. 2–3): won with Wong's testimony and Blonsky's calm transformation.

- Bukowski's fraud case (Ep. 3): Pug's case, won with Jen's help.

- Donny Blaze's magic (Ep. 4): on Wong's behalf; Donny is forced to obey a cease-and-desist.

- The She-Hulk name (Ep. 5): won back from Titania with her dating records and her dates' testimony.

- Mister Immortal's divorce (Ep. 6): Nikki and Mallory's case, settled with compensation for eight former spouses.

- Leap-Frog's lawsuit (Ep. 8): lost to Matt Murdock; afterward they rescue Jacobson together.

- The case against Todd and Intelligencia (Ep. 9): where Jen is headed at the end.

The plot to get Jen's blood

1) Ep. 3: the Wrecking Crew, hired by an anonymous client, try to take her blood on her way home, but the needle breaks.

2) Ep. 6: HulkKing runs Intelligencia's message board full of hate for She-Hulk, while scientists spy on Jen.

3) Ep. 7: after sleeping with Jen, Josh clones her phone and takes her blood.

4) Ep. 8: Intelligencia plays the Josh footage at the gala, provoking Jen into a public rampage. She is banned from transforming.

5) Ep. 9: HulkKing is Todd. He becomes a Hulk with her blood, but Jen gets K.E.V.I.N. to rewrite the ending, and Todd is arrested without ever becoming one.

What happens to Blonsky

He becomes Jen's client in Ep. 2 and is paroled in Ep. 3 on condition he never transforms. By Ep. 7 he runs a retreat. In Ep. 9, having appeared as Abomination at the Intelligencia gathering, he goes back to prison for ten years, until Wong breaks him out and takes him to Kamar-Taj.

No one dies in this series. Mister Immortal, who comes back to life, had been faking his deaths.

My takeOpinion

The silliest series in the MCU, talking straight to the audience. The finale splits opinion, but I think committing to it that fully is refreshing.
