Episode 1: A Normal Amount of Rage

The crash

Los Angeles lawyer Jen Walters is driving with her cousin Bruce when a spaceship forces them off the road. Bruce's blood gets into Jen's wound.

She-Hulk

Jen transforms into the huge, green She-Hulk, but unlike Bruce, she stays fully herself.

Training in Mexico

Bruce tries to train Jen at his house in Mexico, but Jen wants to be a lawyer, not a superhero.

Titania in court

In the middle of a trial, the super-strong Titania bursts into the courtroom. Jen stops her as She-Hulk, and the footage goes viral.

Episodes 2–4: A new job

The superhuman law division

Jen loses her job but gets an offer to lead a big firm's new superhuman law division, on the condition she works as She-Hulk.

Abomination's parole

Her first client is Emil Blonsky, Abomination, who once fought the Hulk. Jen argues for his parole.

Wong's testimony

Blonsky had been taken out of prison by Wong, and Jen asks Wong to testify.

Episodes 4–7: Love and work

Dating apps

Jen tries dating apps as She-Hulk and gets plenty of matches, but they're only interested in She-Hulk.

A fight over a name

Titania trademarks the name 'She-Hulk' for her products, and Jen sues to get it back.

The wedding

At a friend's wedding, Jen tries not to transform, until Titania crashes it.

Blonsky's retreat

Jen attends a wellness retreat run by Blonsky and talks with the other guests.

Episode 8: Ribbit and Rip It

Matt Murdock

Jen faces off against blind lawyer Matt Murdock in court.

Daredevil and She-Hulk

That night, she meets Matt as Daredevil. After fighting side by side, they hit it off.

A trap at the gala

At an awards gala, Intelligencia plays a private video of Jen, and she transforms in anger in front of everyone.

Episode 9: Whose Show Is This?

Power-dampening

Jen is forced to wear a bracelet that blocks her transformation and loses her job.

Intelligencia unmasked

Intelligencia is led by a rich man named Todd, who injects himself with Jen's blood and becomes a Hulk-like creature.

Breaking out

As the finale descends into chaos, Jen talks to the audience and walks out of the show.

K.E.V.I.N.

At Marvel Studios, she meets K.E.V.I.N., the AI that decides the stories, and asks for a better ending.

A family dinner

Back in the show, Jen hosts a family dinner, and Bruce arrives with his son, Skaar.
