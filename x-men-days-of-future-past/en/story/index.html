Part 1: A dying future

The age of Sentinels

In the future, mutant-hunting robots called Sentinels have captured or killed mutants and the humans who helped them. Cities lie in ruins.

Ambush in Russia

Kitty, Bobby, Colossus, Blink, Bishop and others are attacked in their hideout. The Sentinels adapt to each power and overwhelm them.

A few days back

Just before they're wiped out, Kitty sends Bishop's mind into his body a few days earlier. He warns everyone, and they escape before the attack. That's how they've survived.

The temple in China

At a mountain temple, the elderly Professor X and Magneto, Storm and Logan join them. Professor X says it all began in 1973.

Part 2: Back to 1973

The turning point

In 1973, Mystique, Raven, killed Bolivar Trask, a scientist studying mutants. She was captured, and her shape-shifting DNA was used to make the Sentinels unstoppable.

Why Logan

Sending a mind back decades would destroy it. Only Logan, who heals from anything, can survive. His mind is sent into his 1973 body.

Morning in 1973

Logan wakes up in his own bed in 1973. His skeleton isn't yet coated in metal; his claws are still bone.

The empty school

Logan visits the school, but it's closed and neglected. He finds young Hank McCoy and a drunken young Charles. Charles takes a serum that lets him walk but robs him of his telepathy.

Convincing Charles

Logan shares what the future Charles told him. Wanting Raven back, Charles agrees to help.

Part 3: Breaking out Erik

Under the Pentagon

Young Erik, Magneto, is imprisoned deep beneath the Pentagon, accused of involvement in President Kennedy's assassination, in a cell without any metal.

Peter

Logan and the others ask for help from Peter Maximoff, a teenager who can move at incredible speed.

The kitchen

During the escape, the guards open fire. Listening to music, Peter zips through the seemingly frozen bullets, redirects them and takes out the guards in an instant.

Old friends

Erik and Charles reunite, but quickly argue. Charles blames Erik for taking Raven away.

Part 4: Paris

The peace talks

In Paris, talks to end a war are underway. Raven slips in disguised as an officer. She's learned Trask has been experimenting on mutants and plans to kill him.

Erik's choice

As Logan and the others step in, Erik decides killing Raven would guarantee the future and fires at her. She's wounded but escapes.

Caught on camera

The chaos is filmed, and the world sees mutants in action. Trask's people collect a sample of Raven's blood.

Erik goes his own way

Erik leaves on his own, preparing to fight humanity his way.

Part 5: Two Charleses

Logan's nightmares

Old memories haunt Logan, and his link to the future grows unstable. In the future, Kitty struggles to hold his mind in place.

His future self

Charles tries Cerebro, but the serum has dulled his power. Through Logan's mind, he speaks with his older self, who tells him not to fear feeling others' pain.

Reaching Raven

Off the serum, Charles regains his power and reaches out to Raven's mind.

Part 6: The battle in Washington

Unveiling the Sentinels

In Washington, the President unveils Trask's Sentinels. Erik has laced them with steel so he can control them.

A stadium around the White House

Erik lifts an entire stadium, drops it around the White House and turns the Sentinels loose.

Logan in the river

Logan and Hank go after Erik, but Erik impales Logan with steel beams and drops him into the river.

The last stand in the future

At the same moment, the Sentinels find the future temple. One by one, the mutants fall, buying time for the past to change.

Part 7: Raven's choice

The President's face

Raven, disguised as the President, shoots Erik to stop him. Then she turns her gun on Trask.

Charles's words

Charles tells Raven she doesn't have to do it. She lowers the gun, spares Trask and walks away. The world watches a mutant save a human life.

The future changes

In the future, the deadly battle simply fades away.

Part 8: A new future

Morning at the school

Logan wakes up at the school in the future. It's full of students, with Storm, Bobby and Kitty, and Jean and Scott are alive.

The Professor

Logan asks Professor X to tell him everything that's happened since 1973.

Pulled from the river

In 1973, Colonel Stryker pulls Logan out of the river, but his eyes flash yellow: it's Mystique in disguise.

After the credits

In ancient Egypt, crowds chant the name of an immensely powerful mutant as he raises a pyramid with his mind, watched by four horsemen.
