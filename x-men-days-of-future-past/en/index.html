Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

Two timelines

The film cuts between the future (around 2023) and 1973, with both the original and younger casts.

The young trio

Young Charles, Erik and Raven are at the heart of it. They first met in X-Men: First Class (2011).

Changing history

Changing the past erases some of what happened in the original trilogy.

Stay through the credits

A scene sets up the next film.

Key terms

Sentinels
Giant robots built to hunt mutants. In the future, they can adapt to any power.
Bolivar Trask
The scientist who created the Sentinels, convinced mutants are a threat.
The Paris Peace Accords
The 1973 talks to end a war, where one event decides the future.
Cerebro
Professor X's device for reaching minds anywhere in the world.
Hank's serum
A drug that lets young Charles walk, at the cost of his telepathy.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why Logan?

Sending a mind back decades tears it apart. Only Logan, who heals from anything, can survive it.

Why does Raven matter so much?

If she kills Trask, she's captured and studied. Her shape-shifting DNA makes the Sentinels adaptable and unstoppable.

Who rescues Logan at the end?

He looks like Stryker, but his eyes flash yellow: it's Mystique in disguise.

What about the original trilogy?

Because the past changed, Logan wakes up in a future where Jean and Scott are alive. The tragedies of The Last Stand never happened.

Easy-to-miss details

The kitchen scene

Peter, Quicksilver, zipping through frozen bullets while listening to music is one of the film's most famous scenes.

Two Professors

Through Logan's mind, young Charles talks with his older self.

Lifting a stadium

Erik lifts an entire stadium and drops it around the White House.
