Part 1: A grave in the snow, and six years earlier

1-1Logan's grave

In a snowy wasteland stands Logan's grave. The Logan of Wade's world (Earth-10005) died in Logan.

In his Deadpool suit, Wade talks to the audience as he digs up the grave and pulls out Logan's remains. Logan's skeleton is laced with adamantium, an almost unbreakable metal.

1-2Fighting with bones

Soldiers of the TVA, the organization that watches over time, arrive to take Wade in.

Wade swings Logan's metal skeleton like a weapon and cuts through them one after another, practically dancing to a 1990s pop hit as the opening credits roll.

Afterward, Wade starts telling the audience how he got here.

1-3The Avengers interview

At the end of Deadpool 2, Wade used Cable's time machine to save his girlfriend Vanessa.

Then, in 2018, Wade leaves his own world for the Avengers' world (Earth-616) and asks Happy Hogan to let him join the Avengers. Happy turns him down, and Wade goes back home.

1-4Six years later, a retired hero

Six years later, Wade has quit being Deadpool and broken up with Vanessa. He now sells used cars with his friend Peter, a former member of X-Force.

1-5A birthday party

Wade's friends throw him a birthday party at his place. Vanessa, Blind Al, Dopinder, Colossus, Negasonic and others are there.

Then TVA soldiers burst in and take Wade away.

Part 2: The TVA, and hunting for a Wolverine

2-1Paradox's offer

Wade is brought before TVA agent Mr. Paradox, who explains that the TVA exists outside of time and watches over the Sacred Timeline and the wider multiverse.

Paradox offers Wade a future as a hero on the Sacred Timeline. Wade accepts and is given a new suit and weapons.

2-2Why his world is dying

But then Paradox reveals that Wade's world is coming to an end.

Every world has an "anchor being" that holds it together. The anchor of Wade's world was Logan, and since Logan died, the world is slowly falling apart.

2-3The Time Ripper

Paradox has no intention of waiting for Wade's world to fade on its own. He plans to speed things up with the Time Ripper, a machine that erases a world all at once.

That would mean the end of everyone Wade loves: Vanessa, Peter, Al and the rest.

2-4Stealing the TemPad

Wade refuses Paradox's offer, grabs his TemPad, the TVA's travel device, and escapes.

Hoping that bringing Logan back to life will save his world, Wade uses the TemPad to go to Logan's grave.

2-5At the grave

We're back at the snowy opening. Logan's remains, of course, don't come back to life, and the TVA soldiers sent after Wade are dealt with as seen at the start.

Then he has a new idea: bring in a Logan from another universe to serve as a replacement anchor.

2-6So many Wolverines

With the TemPad, Wade hops from universe to universe looking for a Wolverine.

He meets a short Wolverine, one fighting the Hulk, one strung up on a giant X, an old one, one played by Henry Cavill and more, but none of them work out.

2-7The Wolverine in the bar

At last, Wade finds a Logan drinking himself into a stupor in a bar, one who still has a yellow X-Men suit.

Logan refuses to come along, so Wade drags him to the TVA with the TemPad.

2-8The worst Wolverine

At the TVA, Paradox says an anchor can't be replaced, least of all by this Logan, whom the TVA considers the worst Wolverine in the multiverse because he failed to save his fellow X-Men.

2-9Sent to the Void

Wade realizes that Paradox is acting without his superiors' knowledge and threatens to tell on him.

Paradox sends Wade and Logan to the Void, a wasteland at the end of time.

Part 3: The Void and Cassandra

3-1Fighting in the wasteland

The Void is a desert where everything the TVA erases ends up. The ruins of the 20th Century Fox logo lie half-buried in the sand.

Wade and Logan trade insults and then fight. To end it, Wade promises that the TVA can change what happened in Logan's world. Logan believes him and agrees to work together.

3-2Johnny Storm

A man walks up to them. Wade thinks he's Captain America, but the man bursts into flame: he's Johnny Storm of the Fantastic Four.

3-3Captured

The three are attacked and captured by a gang of mutants who round up people in the Void.

They are taken to the lair of Cassandra Nova, ruler of the Void, built inside the corpse of a giant Ant-Man.

3-4Cassandra Nova

Logan realizes that Cassandra is the twin of Charles Xavier (Professor X), leader of the X-Men.

She has terrifying powers: she can reach into a person's head to read their mind and move their body at will. She has made a deal with the TVA to keep order in the Void.

3-5Johnny's end

Wade tells Cassandra that Johnny was badmouthing her before they arrived.

Enraged, she uses her powers to strip away Johnny's skin and flesh, killing him.

3-6Inside Wade's head

Cassandra reaches into Wade's head too. She shows him false memories of Vanessa rejecting him, trying to make him believe he doesn't matter.

3-7Alioth, and an escape

Then Alioth, a giant creature that devours everything in its path, closes in on the lair.

Wade and Logan escape before it can swallow them.

Part 4: The resistance

4-1Nicepool and Dogpool

The pair meet two other Deadpools: Nicepool, an unscarred and polite variant, and Dogpool, a dog in a Deadpool suit.

Nicepool lends them a car and points them toward a resistance group that has been fighting Cassandra.

4-2Fighting in the car

On the drive, Wade lets slip that he doesn't actually know whether the TVA can change Logan's past.

Feeling lied to, Logan tears into Wade's immaturity and failures. They fight savagely inside the car until both are knocked out.

4-3Laura

The two are found by a grown-up Laura (X-23), the daughter of Wade's world's Logan, the girl who was with him when he died in Logan. She brings them to the resistance's hideout.

4-4The resistance

Besides Laura, the hideout holds Elektra, Blade and Gambit, all sent to the Void by the TVA and still fighting Cassandra.

Wade proposes that they team up to defeat Cassandra and escape the Void. But Logan refuses to join.

4-5Logan's past

Logan finally opens up about his world. After joining the X-Men, he went out drinking one night, and while he was gone, mutant hunters killed his teammates.

He has never forgiven himself for not being there. He kept the yellow suit to remember them.

4-6Talking with Laura

Laura tells him about the Logan she knew, who died a hero.

The conversation changes Logan's mind. He quietly climbs into the car and joins them.

Part 5: Showdown with Cassandra

5-1Storming the lair

Wade, Logan, Laura, Elektra, Blade and Gambit attack Cassandra's lair.

While the resistance holds off her henchmen, Wade and Logan go after Cassandra herself.

5-2Logan's memories

Cassandra reaches into Logan's head, freezing him in place, and looks into his past.

5-3Juggernaut's helmet

Wade puts Juggernaut's helmet, which blocks mind powers, on Cassandra's head. Her powers are cut off.

5-4Pyro's betrayal

Then Pyro, one of Cassandra's own followers, shoots her. He has been working for Paradox, planted to kill her.

Logan knocks Pyro out.

5-5Taking off the helmet

Cassandra is dying. Thinking of what Charles would do, Logan persuades Wade to take off the helmet and save her.

Once it's off, Cassandra heals herself.

5-6A portal home

In return, Cassandra uses a sling ring she took from a Doctor Strange of another universe to open a portal, sending Wade and Logan back to Wade's world (Earth-10005).

Part 6: The Time Ripper

6-1The machine is ready

Back in Wade's world, the two go after Paradox. The Time Ripper is already complete.

6-2Cassandra follows

Cassandra gets Pyro to tell her about the Time Ripper, then kills him. She comes through a portal herself and seizes Paradox.

She plans to use the Time Ripper to destroy every world, leaving only the Void.

6-3The Deadpool Corps

Cassandra summons the Deadpool Corps, a huge crowd of Deadpools from the Void, to attack Wade and Logan.

Logan pulls on the mask of his yellow suit and fights beside Wade, but the Deadpools keep healing no matter how often they go down.

6-4Nicepool's end

Nicepool sides with Wade and Logan, but unlike the other Deadpools, he has no healing factor. He is killed in the battle with the Deadpool Corps.

6-5Peter arrives

Then Peter shows up wearing Wade's old Deadpool suit. Distracted by him, the Deadpools stop fighting.

6-6The only way

Paradox tells them how to stop the Time Ripper: someone has to disrupt its flow of energy with their own body. Whoever does it will die.

6-7Stopping it together

Wade and Logan join hands, take the energy together and destroy the Time Ripper.

Cassandra is caught in the collapsing machine and torn apart.

Because they shared the burden, both Wade and Logan survive.

Part 7: A new place to belong

7-1B-15 and Paradox

TVA Hunter B-15 arrives and arrests Paradox. She tells Wade and Logan that thanks to them, Wade's world is no longer falling apart.

7-2Wade's requests

Wade asks B-15 to rescue the resistance members left in the Void, and, keeping his promise to Logan, to change the past of Logan's world.

B-15 says Logan's past can't be changed: it's what made him the hero he is now. Instead, she lets Logan stay in Wade's world.

7-3A new family

Wade brings Logan, the rescued Laura and Dogpool home to his friends. As they all gather around the table with Al and the others, Logan encourages Wade to make things right with Vanessa.

7-4During the credits

During the end credits, a montage of behind-the-scenes footage from the 20th Century Fox Marvel films, including the X-Men series, plays.

7-5After the credits

Wade shows footage of Johnny Storm on the way to Cassandra's lair. Johnny really was badmouthing her.

Summary (spoilers through the end)

How Logan finds his way back

1) Drunk in a bar (Part 2): when his X-Men were killed, he was out drinking and wasn't there. The TVA calls him the worst Wolverine.

2) Wade's lie (Parts 3–4): he teams up believing Wade's promise that the TVA can change his past, then learns it was a lie and they fight.

3) Talking with Laura (Part 4): hearing about a version of himself who died a hero, he decides to fight again.

4) Saving Cassandra (Part 5): thinking of what Charles would do, he saves her life.

5) Saving the world (Part 6): he and Wade stop the Time Ripper together. His past stays as it is, but he finds a new home in Wade's world (Part 7).

Who died (in order)

- TVA soldiers (Part 1): sent to take Wade at Logan's grave, they're cut down by Wade, using Logan's bones among other things.

- Johnny Storm (Part 3): after Wade tells Cassandra that Johnny insulted her, she strips away his skin and flesh with her powers.

- Pyro (Part 6): he shot Cassandra as Paradox's plant. She kills him after getting him to tell her about the Time Ripper.

- Nicepool (Part 6): he sides with Wade and Logan, but has no healing factor and is killed in the battle with the Deadpool Corps.

- Cassandra (Part 6): she tries to erase every world, but when Wade and Logan destroy the Time Ripper, she is caught in the machine and torn apart.

Note: the X-Men of Logan's world were killed by mutant hunters before the film begins (told in Part 4).
