Part 1: Digging up a grave

A grave in the snow

In a snowy wasteland, Deadpool is digging up the grave of Logan, who died in Logan. TVA soldiers arrive.

Fighting with bones

Deadpool fights them using Logan's metal skeleton as a weapon, then dances through the opening titles to a 1990s pop hit.

Part 2: Six years earlier

Wanting to be an Avenger

Six years earlier, Wade asks Happy Hogan for a spot on the Avengers. He's told he's not ready.

Hanging up the suit

Crushed, Wade quits being Deadpool, splits from his girlfriend Vanessa and sells used cars with his friend Peter.

A birthday party

Friends and Vanessa gather for Wade's birthday. Then the TVA shows up and takes him away.

Part 3: The TVA

Paradox's offer

TVA agent Paradox explains that Wade's world is slowly dying because its anchor, Logan, is dead. He offers to bring Wade alone into the main timeline.

The Time Ripper

Wade learns Paradox doesn't plan to wait; he'll erase the world all at once with a machine.

A replacement Wolverine

Wade figures that if he brings in a new Logan, his world might be saved. He steals a TemPad and runs.

Part 4: Hunting for a Wolverine

So many Logans

Wade jumps between universes visiting Wolverine after Wolverine, without luck.

The worst Wolverine

He finally finds a Logan drunk in a bar, the one the TVA calls the worst Wolverine, a man who failed to save the X-Men in his world.

Into the Void

Wade brings Logan to the TVA, but Paradox mocks them and dumps them into the Void.

Part 5: The Void

The ruler of the wasteland

The Void is a desert of everything the TVA erased. The pair are captured by its ruler, Cassandra Nova, Professor X's twin.

Forgotten heroes

They meet heroes stranded in the Void: Elektra, Blade, Laura (X-23) and Gambit, plus Dogpool, a dog dressed as Deadpool.

Fighting in the car

Wade and Logan have a brutal fight in a car. Afterward, Logan begins to open up about what happened in his world.

Attacking Cassandra

The heroes storm Cassandra's base but fall to her power. Wade and Logan use the moment to escape the Void.

Part 6: Saving the world

Back to the TVA

They return to the TVA to stop the machine, but Cassandra follows them.

Cassandra's ambition

Cassandra plans to use the machine to destroy not just one world, but all of them.

Inside the machine

Wade and Logan take the machine's energy head-on. Logan, the 'worst Wolverine,' becomes a hero again.

The world is saved

Cassandra is destroyed by the machine, and Paradox is arrested by TVA Hunter B-15. Wade's world is saved.

Part 7: A new family

A Logan in Wade's world

Logan settles into Wade's world, welcomed by his friends, and finds a new place to belong.

During the credits

Behind-the-scenes footage from the Fox-era Marvel films plays.

After the credits

A short bonus scene features one of the heroes who fell in the Void.
