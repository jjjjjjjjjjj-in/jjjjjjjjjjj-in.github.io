Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

Third in the Deadpool series

It follows Deadpool (2016) and Deadpool 2 (2018), and connects to the MCU.

The TVA and the multiverse

The TVA from Loki appears. Knowing there are many universes helps.

After Logan

In Deadpool's world, Logan died in Logan. This Wolverine comes from a different universe.

Violent and crude

It's rated R (R15+ in Japan), with plenty of gore and crude jokes.

Watch through the credits

There's a moving montage during the credits and a bonus scene at the end.

Key terms

The TVA
The Time Variance Authority, which watches over the flow of time, first seen in Loki.
Anchor being
The most important person holding a universe together. When they die, the world slowly fades. In Deadpool's world, it was Logan.
The Void
The wasteland at the end of time where the TVA dumps whatever it erases.
The Time Ripper
A machine that can wipe out an entire universe at once.
TemPad
The TVA's device for traveling through time and between universes.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why is Deadpool's world dying?

Its anchor, Logan, died in Logan. Without its anchor, a world slowly fades away.

Who is this Wolverine?

A Logan from another universe, haunted by failing to save the X-Men in his world.

Who is Cassandra Nova?

Professor X's twin, a powerful telepath whom the TVA banished to the Void, where she now rules.

What was Paradox up to?

A TVA agent who wanted to speed up the end of Deadpool's world with the Time Ripper, to advance his own career.

Easy-to-miss details

After the first fight, Deadpool dances his way through the opening titles to a 1990s pop hit.

Dogpool

A dog dressed like Deadpool whom they meet in the Void.

The yellow suit
