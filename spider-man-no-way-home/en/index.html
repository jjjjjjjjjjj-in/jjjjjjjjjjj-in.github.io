StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

Part 1: The Day He Was Exposed

A video Mysterio left behind reveals to the world that Spider-Man is Peter Parker. Peter, MJ, Ned, and May are questioned by Damage Control; lawyer Matt Murdock gets the charges dropped, but the public stays hostile. Peter and May move into Happy's apartment. Then Peter, MJ, and Ned are all rejected by MIT.

Part 2: The Spell Goes Wrong

Peter asks Doctor Strange for a spell to make the world forget his identity. But mid-spell he keeps changing the terms (MJ, then Ned, then May), and the spell goes haywire. Strange seals it in a box. Then, on a highway bridge, Peter is attacked by Doctor Octopus and the Green Goblin: the spell has pulled in people from other worlds who know who he is.

Part 3: Visitors from Other Worlds

Peter and his friends capture Electro and Sandman, and bring in Norman Osborn, whom May had been helping at F.E.A.S.T. The visitors realize they were taken just before dying in their fights with Spider-Man. Strange wants to send them home, but Peter argues they should be cured first to change their fates. He traps Strange in the Mirror Dimension and takes the spell box.

Part 4: Happy's Apartment

At Happy's apartment, with Norman's help, Peter restores Octavius to the kind scientist he was. But Norman's other self, the Goblin, takes over and turns the other villains against Peter. In the fight, the Goblin badly wounds May. She tells Peter that with great power comes great responsibility, and dies.

Part 5: Three Peters

Ned discovers he can open magic portals with Strange's ring. Through them come two Peter Parkers from other worlds (Peter-Two and Peter-Three). They share their own losses, Uncle Ben and Gwen, to support the grieving Peter-One. Together, the three Peters build cures for the villains in the school lab.

Part 6: Battle at the Statue of Liberty

At the Statue of Liberty, under construction, the Peters cure the Lizard and Sandman, then Electro with help from the returning Octavius. The Goblin destroys the spell box, and the walls between worlds start to tear. Peter-Three, who once failed to save Gwen, catches MJ as she falls. Peter-Two stops a raging Peter-One from killing the Goblin, and they cure Norman instead.

Part 7: No One Remembers

To save the multiverse, Peter-One asks Strange to erase Peter Parker from everyone's memory. He promises MJ and Ned he'll find them, says goodbye, and the visitors return to their worlds. Two weeks later, Peter visits MJ's coffee shop but leaves without a word, not wanting to put them in danger. Living alone, he keeps being Spider-Man in a homemade suit.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why do villains from other worlds show up?

The botched spell pulled in everyone across the multiverse who knew Spider-Man's identity.

What does Strange do at the end?

To stop the multiverse from breaking, he erases Peter Parker from everyone's memory.

What happens to Peter after?

No one remembers him. He lives alone and keeps being Spider-Man.

My takeOpinion

The more of the earlier series you've seen, the more happy surprises there are. And yet the ending, where Peter loses everything, is I think the most grown-up in the series.
