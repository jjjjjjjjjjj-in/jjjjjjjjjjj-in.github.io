Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why do villains from other worlds show up?

The botched spell pulled in everyone across the multiverse who knew Spider-Man's identity.

What does Strange do at the end?

To stop the multiverse from breaking, he erases Peter Parker from everyone's memory.

What happens to Peter after?

No one remembers him. He lives alone and keeps being Spider-Man.
