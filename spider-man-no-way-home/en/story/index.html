Part 1: The Day He Was Exposed

1-1Mysterio's video

It picks up right where Far From Home ended. Peter and MJ are in the city when a giant screen shows J. Jonah Jameson's online news show, The Daily Bugle.

It airs a doctored video Mysterio (Quentin Beck) left before he died: it claims Spider-Man caused the London attack and killed Beck, and ends with Beck revealing that Spider-Man is Peter Parker.

Everyone turns to stare. Peter grabs MJ and swings away between the buildings.

1-2A crowd at the door

Peter swings MJ back to the apartment he shares with Aunt May, where MJ meets May for the first time.

Crowds gather outside, helicopters circle overhead, and the news is all about Spider-Man. In an instant, their lives have changed.

1-3Questioned by Damage Control

Peter, MJ, his best friend Ned, and May are taken in and questioned by the Department of Damage Control, the agency that handles the aftermath of super-powered incidents. Peter is suspected in Beck's death.

With help from lawyer Matt Murdock, the charges against Peter are dropped. But Jameson's show and Beck's supporters keep the hostility going.

For their safety, Peter and May move into Happy Hogan's apartment.

1-4Back at school

Peter and his friends go back to school, where some cheer Spider-Man and others blame him. Wherever Peter goes, all eyes are on him.

Ned and MJ become famous too, as Spider-Man's friends.

1-5Rejected by MIT

Peter, MJ, and Ned had all applied to MIT, hoping to go together.

All three are rejected because of the Spider-Man controversy. Peter feels he has wrecked his friends' futures, and blames himself.

Part 2: The Spell Goes Wrong

2-1Visiting Strange

Peter visits Doctor Strange at the New York Sanctum. Wong is there too; he is now the Sorcerer Supreme, the highest rank among the sorcerers.

Peter asks if there's any way to make the world forget he's Spider-Man. Strange offers a spell that will erase the knowledge of Spider-Man's identity from everyone's minds. Wong warns that it's dangerous, but Strange agrees to do it.

2-2Changing the terms

Strange starts the spell. But midway, Peter asks for MJ to keep her memory, then Ned, then Aunt May, changing the terms again and again.

Each change throws the spell off until it spins out of control. Strange stops it and seals the unstable spell inside a small box, the Macchina di Kadavus.

Strange suggests that before turning to magic, Peter should go talk to someone at MIT himself.

2-3On the bridge

Peter tracks down an MIT administrator, caught in traffic on a highway bridge, the Alexander Hamilton Bridge, to beg her to reconsider MJ and Ned.

A man with four mechanical arms on his back appears and starts hurling cars: Otto Octavius, Doctor Octopus. Peter suits up and saves the people caught in the chaos.

2-4Octavius's arms

Octavius attacks Peter, thinking he's the Spider-Man he knows. But seeing his face, he says this isn't Peter Parker.

In the fight, his mechanical arms tear nanotech from Peter's Iron Spider suit. The nanotech bonds to the arms, which lets Peter take control of them, and he stops Octavius.

2-5The Green Goblin appears

Then the Green Goblin swoops in on his glider, throwing pumpkin bombs. Octavius recognizes him as his friend Norman Osborn, who should be dead.

At that moment, Strange magically pulls Peter and Octavius back to the Sanctum.

2-6The cells below

Strange locks Octavius in a cell beneath the Sanctum. The cell next door holds the Lizard (Curt Connors), whom Strange had already caught.

2-7Visitors from the multiverse

Strange explains: the corrupted spell pulled people from other universes in the multiverse who know that Spider-Man is Peter Parker.

Left alone, the walls between worlds will crack further. Strange orders Peter, MJ, and Ned to find and capture the other visitors.

Part 3: Visitors from Other Worlds

3-1Electro and Sandman

The search leads to a military research facility, where Electro (Max Dillon), who controls electricity, is on a rampage. Sandman (Flint Marko), who can turn his body into sand, appears too.

Peter captures Electro. Sandman comes along willingly, hoping Peter can get him home to his daughter. Both are locked in the Sanctum's cells.

3-2Norman and May

Norman Osborn regains control from his split Green Goblin personality and smashes the Goblin mask, trying to be rid of it.

Lost and frightened, he ends up at F.E.A.S.T., a center that helps people in need, where May works. She takes care of him.

Peter comes to get Norman and brings him to the Sanctum.

3-3Talk in the cells

In the cells, the visitors compare their fights with Spider-Man. Sandman tells Norman and Octavius that they died fighting Spider-Man.

Talking it over, Norman, Octavius, and Electro realize they were pulled here just before they died fighting Spider-Man. Octavius angrily refuses to believe it at first, until he recalls that his last memory is that fight.

3-4Sending them home

Strange prepares to reverse the spell sealed in the box and send the visitors back to their universes.

Peter objects that sending them back means sending them to their deaths. If they could be cured first, he argues, their fates might change. Strange refuses: their fates belong to their own worlds.

3-5Chase through the Mirror Dimension

Peter grabs the box and runs. Strange traps him in the Mirror Dimension, a mirror copy of reality, and gives chase as buildings and streets fold and twist around them.

Peter figures out that the Mirror Dimension follows mathematical patterns and uses that to outwit Strange. He takes Strange's sling ring, which opens portals, and leaves Strange trapped in the Mirror Dimension.

3-6The box goes to Ned and MJ

Peter leaves Strange's ring and the spell box with Ned and MJ, and takes the visitors out of the Sanctum.

Part 4: Happy's Apartment

4-1Getting ready to cure them

Peter brings the visitors to Happy's apartment. May backs his plan and helps. The place has equipment from Tony Stark's company, Stark Industries.

Peter plans to cure them one at a time.

4-2Octavius restored

Octavius is first. His arms once had a small chip to stop them from taking over his mind, but it was broken. With Norman's help, Peter builds a new one.

With the new chip in place, Octavius is no longer controlled by his arms and becomes the kind scientist he once was. He thanks Peter, returns the stolen nanotech to the suit, and offers to help cure the others.

4-3The Goblin wakes

But Norman, who was to be cured next, is taken over again by his other self, the Goblin.

The Goblin talks the uncured Electro, Sandman, and Lizard into turning on Peter.

4-4The fight in the apartment

A fight breaks out inside. The Goblin beats Peter brutally, summons his glider, and blows up the apartment.

In the chaos, Octavius flees. Electro, Sandman, and the Lizard escape as well.

4-5May's last words

May is badly wounded when the Goblin attacks her with his glider, and the Goblin escapes.

Outside, Peter holds his wounded aunt. May tells him, "With great power, there must also come great responsibility," and dies in his arms. Happy arrives moments later.

Part 5: Three Peters

5-1Peter is gone

Jameson's show blames Spider-Man for the disaster. After losing May, Peter disappears.

At Ned's grandmother's house, Ned and MJ worry about him.

5-2Ned's magic

MJ suggests Strange's ring might help them find Peter. Ned spins it while thinking hard about Peter, and a portal actually opens: Ned has a knack for magic.

But the Peter Parker who steps through is someone they've never seen. Ned tries again, and yet another Peter Parker arrives. Both were pulled here from other worlds by the spell.

Later, the three Peters call this world's Peter "Peter-One," Tobey Maguire's Peter "Peter-Two," and Andrew Garfield's Peter "Peter-Three."

5-3Peter on the rooftop

They find Peter-One on the school rooftop. Crushed by May's death, he is ready to send the villains home to die.

MJ and Ned comfort him and introduce the two other Peters.

5-4What the two other Peters went through

Peter-Two talks about losing his Uncle Ben, and about carrying that grief while staying Spider-Man.

Peter-Three admits he couldn't save his girlfriend, Gwen Stacy, and that afterward he fought out of anger and grew bitter. He doesn't want Peter-One to end up like him.

Peter-One tells them May's words about great power and great responsibility, which the other two know well. For May's sake, he chooses again to try to save the villains.

5-5In the school lab

In the school's science lab, the three Peters build cures for each villain: the Lizard, Electro, Sandman, and the Goblin.

Comparing notes on their webs (Peter-Two's come straight from his body) and Peter-Two's bad back, they start to bond like brothers.

Part 6: Battle at the Statue of Liberty

6-1To the Statue of Liberty

The Peters set out to lure the villains to the Statue of Liberty, which is under construction to have Captain America's shield added.

MJ and Ned go too, carrying the spell box through a portal Ned opens.

6-2Out of sync

Electro, Sandman, and the Lizard arrive. The Lizard goes after Ned and MJ when Ned can't close his portal, and Peter comes to their rescue; the two escape up onto the statue. The Peters fight, but at first they get in each other's way.

They regroup, share what they know about each villain, and start fighting as a team.

6-3The Lizard and Sandman cured

Peter-One and Peter-Two work together to cure the Lizard and Sandman. Connors and Marko return to human form.

6-4Octavius returns

As Electro overpowers them, Octavius arrives. He reunites with Peter-Two and is moved to see his world's Peter all grown up.

With Octavius's help, the Peters cure Electro. Max returns to normal and talks with Peter-Three.

6-5Strange is back

Up on the statue, Ned opens another portal, but it leads to where Strange is trapped, and he accidentally frees Strange from the Mirror Dimension.

6-6The box is destroyed

The Goblin shows up and destroys the spell box with a pumpkin bomb. The sealed spell breaks loose, the walls between worlds tear open, the sky cracks, and countless people who know Peter's identity begin to be pulled in.

Strange struggles to hold the rift closed.

6-7MJ falls

An explosion knocks MJ off the scaffolding. Peter-One tries to save her but the Goblin gets in his way.

Peter-Three dives after her. He once failed to catch the falling Gwen; this time he catches MJ and sets her down safely, tears in his eyes.

6-8Facing the Goblin

Consumed by rage over May, Peter-One pummels the Goblin and raises the glider to finish him off.

Peter-Two steps in and stops him, and the Goblin stabs Peter-Two in the back. The wound is not fatal.

Peter-One and Peter-Three inject the Goblin with his cure. Norman is freed of the Goblin and his mind returns. Seeing the wounded Peter-Two, he is horrified at what he has done.

Part 7: No One Remembers

7-1Peter's decision

The rift keeps widening. Peter-One realizes there's only one way to protect the multiverse: make everyone forget Peter Parker entirely.

Strange warns him that no one, not even MJ or Ned, will remember him. Peter asks him to do it anyway.

7-2Goodbyes

Peter-One promises MJ and Ned that he'll find them and make them remember. He and MJ tell each other how they feel and kiss.

Peter-Two and Peter-Three hug him goodbye.

7-3The forgetting spell

Strange reluctantly casts the spell, and the visitors return to their own worlds.

No one in this world, Strange included, remembers Peter. People still know Spider-Man exists, but no one knows he is Peter Parker.

7-4At MJ's coffee shop

Two weeks later, Peter visits the coffee shop where MJ works. Ned comes in too; both have been accepted to MIT.

Peter means to tell them the truth, but decides not to put them in danger again and leaves without a word.

7-5May's grave

Peter is at May's grave when Happy arrives. Happy doesn't remember him either.

Happy, talking to Peter as a stranger, shares memories of May. The conversation gives Peter the strength to carry on.

7-6Peter alone

Peter moves into a small apartment on his own and studies for his GED, the high-school equivalency test.

He sews himself a new red-and-blue suit and swings out into a snowy New York to protect the city as Spider-Man again.

7-7During and after the credits

Mid-credits: Eddie Brock (Venom), pulled into this world by the first spell, sits at a tropical bar while the bartender fills him in on this world's Iron Man, Spider-Man, and Thanos's snap. Suddenly he vanishes in a flash of light, sent home by the final spell. A small piece of the Venom symbiote is left behind.

After the credits: a preview of Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness plays.

Summary (spoilers through the ending)

The visitors, and what became of them

- Doctor Octopus (Otto Octavius, from Tobey's world): restored at Happy's apartment with a new chip built by Peter-One and Norman (Part 4). At the Statue of Liberty he helps stop Electro.

- The Lizard (Curt Connors, from Andrew's world): cured back to human by Peter-One and Peter-Two at the statue (Part 6).

- Sandman (Flint Marko, from Tobey's world): returned to human at the statue (Part 6).

- Electro (Max Dillon, from Andrew's world): returned to normal at the statue with Octavius's help (Part 6).

- The Green Goblin (Norman Osborn, from Tobey's world): after killing May, he is injected with his cure by Peter-One and Peter-Three at the statue, and his sanity returns (Part 6).

- Peter-Two (Tobey Maguire) and Peter-Three (Andrew Garfield): help Peter-One and return home at the end.

- Eddie Brock: brought over by the first spell, sent home by the last. Only a bit of Venom is left behind (mid-credits).

Who died

- May Parker (Part 4): Peter-One's aunt. She was helping Peter cure the visitors when, in the fight at Happy's apartment, the Goblin wounded her with his glider. Outside, she tells Peter that with great power comes great responsibility, and dies.

What Peter-One lost

- May (Part 4).

- His place in everyone's memory, including MJ, Ned, and Happy (Part 7): to save the multiverse, he had Strange make the world forget him.

- His home and school: he lives alone in a small apartment, studies for his GED, and keeps being Spider-Man in a homemade suit (Part 7).

My takeOpinion

The more of the earlier series you've seen, the more happy surprises there are. And yet the ending, where Peter loses everything, is I think the most grown-up in the series.
