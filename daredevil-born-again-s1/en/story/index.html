Episode 1: Heaven's Half Hour

1-1Cherry's retirement party

Hell's Kitchen, New York. Blind lawyer Matt Murdock runs a law firm with his best friend Foggy Nelson and their friend Karen Page.

The three go to Josie's Bar to celebrate the retirement of Cherry, an NYPD friend who has helped them for years. Lawyer Kirsten McDuffie is there too, and the mood is warm.

1-2The call, and Bullseye's attack

Foggy gets a call from his client Benny, who says he's in danger. Matt suits up as Daredevil and goes to save him.

It's a trap to draw Matt away. While he's gone, his old enemy, the assassin Bullseye (Benjamin "Dex" Poindexter), attacks the bar and shoots Foggy. Two off-duty officers who rush out of the bar are shot dead too.

As Karen reaches for her gun, Daredevil returns, tackles Dex, and the two crash through the bar's window.

1-3The rooftop fight, and Foggy's death

Daredevil and Dex fight their way up the building to the roof. Meanwhile Foggy dies in Karen's arms.

Learning that Foggy is gone, Matt loses control and throws Dex off the roof, meaning to kill him.

Cherry sees Matt's face and learns that Matt is Daredevil. Dex survives the fall.

1-4One year later

A year passes. Matt has hung up the mask and quit being Daredevil. The old firm is gone; he has started a new one, Murdock & McDuffie, with Kirsten.

Karen has moved to San Francisco and they barely speak.

Matt testifies at Dex's trial, and Dex is sentenced to life without parole.

1-5Meeting Heather

Kirsten sets Matt up with her friend Heather Glenn, a therapist, telling each of them the meeting is about something else. They hit it off, and Matt and Heather start dating.

1-6Fisk runs for mayor

Wilson Fisk, Kingpin, who vanished to recover after being shot (in Hawkeye and Echo), returns to New York. While he was away, his wife Vanessa ran his criminal empire.

Fisk runs for mayor of New York, promising to crack down on masked vigilantes.

1-7Matt and Fisk face off

Matt asks Fisk to meet, and they sit down face to face. Fisk says he had nothing to do with Foggy's death.

Matt warns that if Fisk steps out of line, he will stop him. Fisk answers that there will be consequences if Matt goes back to being a vigilante.

1-8Election night

Fisk wins the election and becomes mayor of New York.

That night he tells Vanessa he knows she had an affair with a man named Adam while he was away. She makes him promise not to kill Adam.

Episode 2: Optics

2-1The subway incident

In a subway station, two men are beating up a young man, Nicky Torres. Passerby Hector Ayala steps in, and Nicky runs off.

In the struggle, one of the men, Kel Shanahan, trips off the platform and is killed by a train. The other, Connor Powell, reveals they're police officers and arrests Hector for murder.

2-2Defending Hector

At the precinct, Matt uses his heightened hearing to listen in on the interrogation and believes Hector's story. He takes Hector's case and tells him not to worry about the cost.

Kirsten and Cherry warn that it means taking on the NYPD. Cherry now works as the firm's investigator. Bail is denied and Hector is sent to jail.

2-3White Tiger's secret

Fetching a suit for Hector to wear in court, Cherry finds a White Tiger costume and amulet at his home. Hector is the White Tiger, a masked hero who has been helping people on the streets.

Matt confronts Hector in jail. Hector says he wasn't wearing the suit or the amulet that night in the subway.

Believing it would unfairly bias the jury, Matt persuades the judge to keep Hector's identity out of the trial.

2-4Mayor Fisk's first days

Newly in office, Fisk works on his image. His adviser Sheila Rivera suggests putting Vanessa at his side more often.

With help from Daniel Blake, an eager young staffer who admires Fisk, young reporter BB Urich gets an interview. She reveals that popular Police Commissioner Gallo is about to resign in protest of Fisk's election.

Fisk calls Gallo in and hints that he knows about Gallo's infidelity, scaring him out of resigning.

2-5Couples therapy

Fisk and Vanessa start couples therapy. Their therapist is Matt's girlfriend, Heather.

2-6Saving Nicky

Matt tails Powell and overhears a call telling him where Nicky is hiding. When Powell shoves him against a wall, Matt notices a Punisher skull tattoo on his arm.

Realizing the officers mean to silence the only witness, Matt gets there first and helps Nicky escape.

Powell and another corrupt cop come in, beat Matt and put a gun to his head. Without the Daredevil suit, Matt fights back and takes them both down.

Episode 3: The Hollow of His Hand

3-1Trouble in the underworld

Now that Fisk is mayor, he and Vanessa stay away from their old criminal business. Meanwhile, gang bosses Luca and Viktor start fighting each other.

Fisk thinks they should let the gangs destroy each other, but Vanessa is frustrated to see her work coming undone.

She sends Fisk's right-hand man, Buck Cashman, to order Luca to pay Viktor $1.8 million in restitution.

3-2Powell's testimony

Hector's trial begins. Powell testifies that only he and Shanahan were on the platform when Hector attacked them out of nowhere.

3-3Nicky's lie

Cherry brings Nicky to testify. But frightened by the police in the courtroom, Nicky says he was at home that night and was never assaulted.

Matt can hear he's lying but can do nothing about it.

3-4Revealed in court

With no other options, Matt reverses his own strategy and reveals in court that Hector is the White Tiger. Kirsten and Hector are stunned.

The judge and District Attorney Benjamin Hochberg sharply criticize Matt for exposing the very secret he had asked to keep out of the trial.

3-5Not guilty

Matt and Kirsten bring in witness accounts from people the White Tiger saved, and police records describing his heroic deeds, to show that Hector acts to help people.

The jury finds Hector not guilty, and he walks free.

3-6Fisk pushes back

In an interview with BB, Fisk denounces the verdict and vows to keep up his anti-vigilante stance.

3-7White Tiger's death

Matt had warned Hector to stop being the White Tiger. Even so, Hector suits up and goes out on patrol.

On the street, a man wearing the Punisher's skull symbol appears and shoots Hector in the head, killing him.

Episode 4: Sic Semper Systema

4-1A man carried underground

A figure in a white mask carries an unconscious man into the subway tunnels.

4-2Angela at the morgue

At the morgue, it's clear Hector was shot at close range. His niece, Angela del Toro, storms in.

She believes the police killed her uncle. Matt comforts her, promising the killer will be brought to justice.

4-3Confronting Powell

Matt confronts Powell, suggesting he killed Hector. Powell shoves Matt against a wall and denies it.

Hearing Powell's steady heartbeat, Matt concludes he is telling the truth about this.

4-4A small case

Matt defends Leroy Bradford, arrested for stealing snacks from a store, and gets his sentence cut from 30 days to 10.

Leroy lost his benefits after missing an appointment during his last jail stay. Matt sees how the system punishes people more than it helps them.

4-5Daniel's leak

Fisk is pushing a plan to rebuild the Red Hook port, but red tape and gang fighting keep getting in the way.

Then Daniel Blake goes out drinking with BB and, drunk, lets details slip. BB reports that Fisk is a union buster.

Fisk explodes and demands to know who leaked. Daniel owns up and swears his loyalty. Fisk keeps him on, but warns him there won't be another chance.

4-6Therapy, and Adam

In counseling, Fisk and Vanessa talk about her affair with Adam. Both say they don't know where Adam is.

Alone with Vanessa, Heather asks whether she feels safe around Fisk. Vanessa laughs off the idea.

4-7The skull casing

Matt searches the spot where Hector was killed and finds a bullet casing marked with the Punisher's skull.

4-8Frank Castle again

Matt tracks down Frank Castle, the Punisher. Frank says one of his imitators did it, not him. Matt tells him he should take responsibility for the people misusing his symbol.

Frank throws it back at Matt: Dex, who killed Foggy, is still alive, and Matt did nothing. Frank says Matt really just wants permission to put the mask back on.

Matt punches him, then apologizes, and tearfully says Foggy was the kindest person he knew.

4-9Night falls

That night, Matt takes his Daredevil billy clubs from a hidden room on his roof and practices with them.

Fisk eats dinner next to a cage where he is secretly holding Adam. Technically, he has kept his promise not to kill him.

Underground, the man from the opening wakes up as the masked serial killer Muse begins draining his blood.

Episode 5: With Interest

5-1The bank on St. Patrick's Day

It's St. Patrick's Day. Matt goes to New York Mutual Bank to ask for a loan for his firm.

He meets assistant manager Yusuf Khan, father of Jersey City hero Ms. Marvel (Kamala Khan). Yusuf proudly talks up Ms. Marvel, but turns the loan down because the firm keeps taking clients who can't pay.

5-2The robbery

Just after Matt leaves, a crew led by Devlin storms the bank and takes everyone hostage.

They work for gang boss Luca, and are there to steal something worth the $1.8 million he owes Viktor.

Matt hears the commotion outside, walks back in, and becomes one of the hostages.

5-3Negotiator Kim

Police surround the bank, and hostage negotiator Detective Angie Kim talks with Devlin by phone.

5-4In the vault

The robbers force Yusuf to open the vault. Not knowing how, he stalls for time.

Matt quietly takes out two of the robbers, then opens the vault by listening to its mechanism.

Inside a safe-deposit box is the rare diamond the robbers are after.

5-5The switch

As the police move in, Matt pretends to hand the diamond to Devlin.

Devlin passes the package to an accomplice hiding among the hostages, but she later finds there's no diamond inside.

5-6Catching Devlin

Devlin disguises himself as a police officer and walks out. Matt hides his face with a robber's balaclava, chases him down, and breaks his leg.

5-7Returning the diamond

Matt had hidden the diamond in the candy jar on Yusuf's desk. After he leaves, Yusuf spots it among the candy.

Matt has secretly returned the diamond. Yusuf invites him to dinner with his family, and Matt accepts.

Episode 6: Excessive Force

6-1Luca defies Fisk

After the failed robbery, Luca visits Fisk and refuses to pay Viktor the $1.8 million.

Fisk raises it to $2.8 million and gives him one week.

6-2Paintings in blood

The city's sanitation department finds that murals appearing around the city are made with human blood. It's Muse's work.

Police believe there are at least 60 victims. Then Muse leaves the bodies of two victims beside a new mural, making his crimes public.

6-3The fundraiser

Fisk hears about the bodies during a fundraiser with the city's elite.

Socialite Jack Duquesne and the others there oppose his Red Hook plans and refuse to back them.

6-4The Anti-Vigilante Task Force

To deal with Muse, Fisk sets up the Anti-Vigilante Task Force (AVTF), a police unit with extra powers.

He fills it with notorious officers like Powell and former detective Cole North. Members don't have to wear body cameras, and Buck equips them with new gear and heavy weapons. It will go after masked vigilantes like Daredevil as well as Muse.

6-5Angela's request

Angela comes to Matt's office. She says Hector was looking into disappearances before he died, believes he was on Muse's trail, and asks Matt to continue his work.

When Matt refuses, she storms out, saying she'll do it herself.

6-6Heather's book

Heather is planning a book on the psychology of vigilantes and asks Matt, who knows the Punisher and Daredevil, for his view. The gap between their outlooks begins to show.

6-7Angela taken

Angela goes to investigate the subway and is captured by Muse, who begins draining her blood.

When Hector's widow calls to say Angela is missing, Matt finally puts on the Daredevil suit.

6-8Fighting Muse

Daredevil finds Muse's lair and they fight brutally.

Then he hears Angela's heart stop. He lets Muse go and does chest compressions until she revives. Muse escapes.

6-9Fisk and Adam

At the same time, Fisk hands Adam an axe and challenges him to fight. Fisk beats him and drags him back to his cell.

Episode 7: Art for Art's Sake

7-1Daredevil is back

Recovering in hospital, Angela tells the police that Daredevil saved her and points them to Muse's lair.

Fisk is furious to hear that Daredevil has returned.

7-2The suspect, Bastian

Detective Kim tells Fisk that the prime suspect for Muse is a troubled young man named Bastian Cooper.

7-3Luca's offer

Luca asks Vanessa to go back to their criminal business behind Fisk's back.

7-4Heather and Bastian

Heather notices bruises on Matt's body and worries, but he brushes it off.

Later, in her office, her patient Bastian thanks her for helping him embrace his true self.

Heather realizes he is Muse. When she tries to get away, he knocks her unconscious.

7-5Heather's portraits

Matt and the AVTF each find paintings of Heather in Muse's lair. Realizing she's the next target, both head for her.

7-6The end of Muse

Daredevil crashes in through the window, rescues Heather, and fights Muse.

As the AVTF is about to enter, Heather shoots Muse dead to save her own life, then faints.

Daredevil stabilizes her wounds and slips away before the AVTF comes in.

7-7Credit to the AVTF

Fisk credits the AVTF with stopping Muse and publicly calls them heroes.

Daniel threatens BB into removing eyewitness mentions of Daredevil from her blog.

7-8The voice that said her name

Recovering in hospital, Heather tells Matt she heard Daredevil say her name.

7-9Luca's death

Vanessa tells Luca where Fisk is dining alone, and Luca goes to kill him.

It's a trap. Buck Cashman is waiting and shoots Luca dead.

Episode 8: Isle of Joy

8-1Dex is moved

On Fisk's orders, Dex is moved from solitary confinement into the prison's general population.

8-2Adam's death

Fisk reveals to Vanessa that he has been holding Adam prisoner, and leaves the choice to her.

Adam begs for his life, and Vanessa shoots him dead. Fisk holds her and promises never to leave her again.

8-3Daniel's promotion, and an invitation

Fisk promotes Daniel to Deputy Mayor of Communications.

He also invites Heather to his Black & White Ball, a fundraiser for the Red Hook project.

8-4Matt and Heather drift apart

Seeing the invitation, Matt realizes Fisk is Heather's patient.

Heather leans toward Fisk's anti-vigilante views. Matt argues against them, and their relationship grows strained.

8-5What Matt learns at Josie's

At Josie's Bar, Matt learns that Foggy was celebrating an upcoming court win the night he died.

Matt suspects Fisk hired Dex to silence Foggy.

8-6Visiting Dex, and his escape

Matt visits Dex in prison and demands to know who hired him. Dex offers to tell if Matt gets him out. Matt snaps and slams Dex's head into the table again and again.

Afterwards, Dex spits his loose tooth at a guard hard enough to knock him down, kills the doctor who treated him and a guard, and escapes from prison.

8-7The ball

At the Black & White Ball, Fisk reveals that he knows Jack Duquesne is the sword-wielding vigilante Swordsman, and blackmails him and other members of the elite into backing the Red Hook project.

Reporter BB, who knows Fisk was involved in the murder of her uncle Ben Urich, asks Commissioner Gallo to help bring down Fisk and the AVTF.

8-8The real client

When word comes that Dex has escaped, Matt hears Vanessa's heartbeat change. It was Vanessa, not Fisk, who hired Dex to kill Foggy.

Matt confronts her.

8-9Taking the bullet

Fisk begins to tell Heather that Matt is Daredevil.

At that moment Dex, who has slipped into the ball, fires at Fisk. Matt jumps in front of him and takes the bullet.

Episode 9: Straight to Hell

9-1Behind Foggy's death

A flashback shows that before Foggy's death, Vanessa met Dex and hired him to kill Foggy, because a case Foggy was handling threatened to expose her criminal business.

9-2Hospital, and the blackout

Dex escapes the ball, and the wounded Matt is taken to hospital.

When he wakes, Matt tells Kirsten that Vanessa ordered Foggy's killing and asks her to find Foggy's case files.

Fisk cuts the power to all of New York. In the dark, the AVTF can act freely, and the city falls into chaos.

Fisk orders Buck to kill Matt. Before Buck reaches his room, Matt slips out of the hospital and makes it back to his apartment.

9-3Teaming up with Frank

Frank Castle is waiting in the apartment. Then AVTF officers attack.

As Daredevil, Matt fights them alongside Frank. In the fight it's revealed that AVTF member Cole North killed Hector. Frank urges Matt to kill North, but Matt won't.

9-4Reunited with Karen

A grenade goes off and the two jump out the window. Karen is waiting with a car and picks them up. When she heard Dex had escaped, she asked Frank to protect Matt.

9-5Foggy's files

Matt and Karen track down Foggy's case files.

They reveal that Red Hook is a free port outside normal law, and that the Fisks plan to turn it into their own city-state.

9-6Frank is captured

Frank storms Red Hook alone, calling out the AVTF officers for using his skull symbol as he fights them. In the end, he is captured.

9-7Gallo's death

Fisk learns that Commissioner Gallo is plotting against him.

In front of AVTF officers, he crushes Gallo's skull with his bare hands.

9-8Martial law

Fisk turns the power back on and declares martial law. Vigilantism is outlawed, and Duquesne, Frank, and other dissidents are locked up at Red Hook.

Fisk offers Heather the post of Mental Health Commissioner, and she accepts.

9-9To take the city back

Matt gathers Cherry, Detective Kim, and other honest police officers to stand up to Fisk and take back New York.

9-10(Mid-credits) Frank's escape

Caged at Red Hook, Frank tricks a young guard into getting close, setting up his escape.

Summary

The truth behind Foggy's death

Dex (Bullseye) killed Foggy. Vanessa hired him, because a case Foggy was handling threatened to expose her criminal business (shown in Ep. 9). Matt figures it out in Ep. 8 from Vanessa's heartbeat.

Who killed Hector

The White Tiger (Hector) was shot by police officer Cole North, who later joins the AVTF, wearing the Punisher's skull (killed in Ep. 3, revealed in Ep. 9). It wasn't Frank Castle himself.

Who died (in order)

- Foggy Nelson (Ep. 1): shot by Dex, hired by Vanessa. Two off-duty officers at the bar are also shot by Dex.

- Kel Shanahan (Ep. 2): a cop beating Nicky. Struggling with Hector, who stepped in, he falls off the platform and is hit by a train.

- Hector Ayala (Ep. 3): after his acquittal, shot in the head on patrol by a man wearing the skull (Cole North).

- Muse's victims (Eps. 4-6): drained of blood for his murals.

- Muse / Bastian Cooper (Ep. 7): shot by Heather, defending herself when he attacks her.

- Luca (Ep. 7): ambushed and shot by Buck when he goes to kill Fisk.

- Adam (Ep. 8): Vanessa's former lover, held prisoner by Fisk. Vanessa shoots him.

- A prison doctor and a guard (Ep. 8): killed by Dex as he escapes.

- Commissioner Gallo (Ep. 9): Fisk crushes his skull with his bare hands for plotting against him.

How Season 1 ends

Fisk rules New York under martial law, and vigilantes are outlawed. Matt is Daredevil again and begins organizing with Karen, Cherry, Detective Kim, and honest police officers to fight back. Bullseye is still at large and Frank is about to break out as the story heads into Season 2.

My takeOpinion

Dark and bruising by MCU standards. I like how Matt and Fisk are set side by side: the harder each tries to keep up his public face, the more the other one shows.
