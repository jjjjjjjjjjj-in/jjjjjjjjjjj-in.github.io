Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why does Fisk run for mayor?

At the end of Echo, he decided to run. He wants to rule New York through legitimate power.

Why does Matt quit?

Devastated by his friend Foggy's murder, he decides to focus on law.

What was Vanessa up to?

While Fisk was away, his wife Vanessa ran his criminal empire.
