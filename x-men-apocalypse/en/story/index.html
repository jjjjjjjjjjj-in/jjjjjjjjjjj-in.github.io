Part 1: Ancient Egypt

1-1The man called a god

Egypt, 3600 BC. En Sabah Nur, the first mutant, rules the land and is worshipped as a god for his immense power.

Whenever his body grows old, he moves his mind into another mutant's body, and so he has lived for ages and gathered many powers. Four horsemen always serve at his side.

1-2The pyramid ritual

Inside a pyramid, a ritual transfers En Sabah Nur's mind into the body of a man with a healing power.

As it goes on, conspirators who fear him rise up and break the supports holding the pyramid, to bring the whole structure down on him.

1-3Sleep beneath the sand

The four horsemen give their lives protecting their master, fighting the conspirators as they fall.

In his new body, En Sabah Nur plunges deep underground as the pyramid collapses, and is sealed in the rubble in a long sleep.

Part 2: 1983

2-1Scott's eyes

1983. It has been ten years since the world learned that mutants exist.

American high schooler Scott Summers suddenly starts firing uncontrollable beams from his eyes. His older brother Alex takes him to Charles Xavier's school for gifted youngsters, hoping Charles and Hank McCoy can teach him to control it.

2-2The tree and Jean

When his blindfold comes off, Scott's blast destroys the oldest tree on the school grounds, one that had belonged to Charles's family for generations.

Scott is given special glasses that hold back his beams. At the school he meets Jean Grey, a telepath, and the two slowly become friends.

2-3Beneath Cairo

Cairo, Egypt. CIA agent Moira MacTaggert is following a group of worshippers of an ancient god.

They hold a ritual underground, sunlight pours in, and En Sabah Nur wakes beneath the rubble. The force he unleashes as he breaks free shakes the ground, and Moira sees it happen.

2-4Jean's nightmare

The shock is felt around the world. At the school, Jean wakes from a nightmare about the end of the world, and her power shakes the whole building. Charles calms her.

Using Cerebro, Charles learns that Moira knows what happened in Cairo. She once fought alongside him.

2-5Moira at the CIA

Charles visits Moira at CIA headquarters with Alex. He erased her memories long ago, so she does not remember him.

Moira tells them the ancient legend of En Sabah Nur and how he always kept four horsemen at his side.

Part 3: The god awakens

3-1Meeting Storm

Walking the streets of Cairo, En Sabah Nur meets Ororo Munroe, a young thief who can control the weather.

She helps him and takes him home, where he absorbs the languages and events of the modern world through television. Deciding humanity has lost its way, he resolves to remake the world.

3-2The first horseman

En Sabah Nur amplifies Ororo's power. As Storm, she becomes his first horseman.

3-3Erik in Poland

Communist Poland. Erik Lehnsherr has taken a new name and lives quietly with his wife Magda and daughter Nina, working at a steel factory.

When the earthquake caused by En Sabah Nur's awakening hits, a coworker's life is in danger. Erik saves him with his power, but the people who see it learn who he is.

3-4Tragedy in the forest

Police take Nina hostage in the forest to force Erik to surrender. He agrees to give himself up in exchange for her release.

Then Nina's power to communicate with animals awakens, and a flock of birds attacks the officers. In the confusion one officer looses an arrow from his bow, and the single arrow pierces both Nina and Magda, killing them.

Overcome with grief and rage, Erik uses his power on his daughter's metal locket and kills the officers.

Part 4: Berlin and the four horsemen

4-1The East Berlin cage fight

East Berlin. Raven, Mystique, has been seen as a mutant hero since the events of ten years ago. She comes to an underground fight club.

In a caged ring, Angel, a mutant with huge wings, is made to fight Kurt Wagner, a blue teleporting boy. Raven overloads a generator to cause chaos and rescues Kurt. Angel escapes too.

4-2Caliban's place

Raven asks the black marketeer Caliban to get Kurt to America. Caliban tells her that Erik has lost his wife and daughter.

To save Erik, Raven rushes to Charles's school with Kurt's help.

4-3Psylocke and Angel

After they leave, En Sabah Nur arrives at Caliban's with Storm, demanding to know about powerful mutants.

Caliban's bodyguard Psylocke, who wields psychic blades, has her power amplified, becomes a horseman and leads him to Angel. En Sabah Nur remakes Angel's damaged wings in metal and makes him his third horseman.

4-4Auschwitz

En Sabah Nur appears before the grieving Erik with his horsemen. He takes Erik to Auschwitz, where Erik was torn from his parents as a boy, and amplifies his power.

Erik tears the camp apart and joins him as Magneto, the fourth horseman.

Part 5: The school explodes

5-1Peter sets out

In America, speedster Peter Maximoff has learned that Erik is his biological father, and it weighs on him.

He heads for Charles's school, hoping to find Erik.

5-2The mall and the movies

At the school, Scott, Jean, Kurt and Jubilee sneak out to the mall and see Return of the Jedi.

5-3Cerebro hijacked

Raven reaches the school and tells Charles what happened to Erik. Charles uses Cerebro to reach Erik's mind and try to talk him down.

But En Sabah Nur, at Erik's side, turns Cerebro around and seizes Charles's power. Soldiers around the world are controlled, and every nation's nuclear missiles are launched into space, so no one can interfere.

Alex blasts Cerebro to break the connection.

5-4Charles is taken

En Sabah Nur and his horsemen appear at the school and take Charles.

Alex fires to stop them, but his blast hits the engine of a prototype jet at the mansion, setting off a huge explosion.

5-5Peter's rescue

Peter arrives just in time and races around at super-speed in the split second as the blast spreads, carrying students, teachers, Hank, Raven, Moira, even a dog and a goldfish to safety.

But Alex, closest to the blast, could not be saved. Scott returns and breaks down when he learns his brother is dead.

Part 6: Alkali Lake

6-1Stryker's raid

Colonel William Stryker's forces arrive by helicopter and knock everyone out with a special weapon. Stryker believes Charles is behind the missile launches.

They take Hank, Raven, Peter and Moira. Scott, Jean and Kurt, who were farther away, are unharmed.

6-2Stowing away

Kurt teleports the three of them onto the helicopter, but a device that blocks mutant powers keeps them from using their abilities inside.

They ride along to Stryker's base at Alkali Lake in Canada and decide to free their friends there.

6-3Charles's message

Meanwhile, En Sabah Nur amplifies Charles's power to send his words into the minds of everyone on Earth, ending with a call for mutants to attack humans.

Instead, Charles urges them to protect the weak. And he secretly tells Jean that he is in Cairo.

6-4Weapon X

Distracted by the message, Jean gets the three of them spotted. She opens the cell of a man held in the base.

Fitted with a device on his head and stripped of his memories, he is Logan, the test subject of the army's Weapon X program. With his metal claws, he cuts through the soldiers.

6-5Jean and Logan

When Logan confronts them in a corridor, Jean reaches into his mind and restores part of his lost memories. Calmed, he disappears into the snowy woods.

The three free the four captives, and all seven fly a large jet from the base to Cairo.

Part 7: The pyramid in Cairo

7-1The world falls apart

En Sabah Nur tears Cairo apart and raises a huge pyramid over it.

Erik, more powerful than ever, shifts the Earth's magnetic field and pulls in metal from all over the world, wrecking cities everywhere.

7-2The transfer ritual

Charles is carried into the pyramid, and the ritual to move En Sabah Nur's mind into Charles's body begins, so he can use Charles's power to control every mind on Earth.

As the ritual goes on, Charles's hair falls out.

7-3Splitting up

In Cairo the group splits up. Raven and Peter go to reason with Erik, while Hank, Scott, Jean and Kurt go after Charles. Moira stays with the jet.

Storm and Psylocke block the way outside the pyramid, and Kurt, teleporting inside, is attacked by Angel.

7-4Rescuing Charles

Just before the ritual is complete, Kurt reaches Charles and teleports him out. The ritual is cut short.

With everyone aboard, Moira takes off in the jet.

7-5Angel's end

Angel and Psylocke attack the jet in flight, and it goes down.

Kurt teleports his friends to the ground. Psylocke leaps clear and escapes, but Angel is killed when the jet crashes.

7-6Erik wavers

Raven, still trying to reach Erik, sees the jet go down and runs to the others with Peter.

Left alone, Erik remembers Charles telling him long ago that there is good in him, not only pain and anger.

Part 8: The final battle

8-1Peter's attack

With the ritual failed, En Sabah Nur comes out of the pyramid to take Charles back.

Peter strikes at him again and again at super-speed, but En Sabah Nur reads his movements, catches him and hurts him badly.

8-2Raven's strike

Psylocke appears to finish Peter off, but it is Raven in disguise. She slashes En Sabah Nur's throat with a sword.

The wound heals at once, and he chokes her with one hand. Her disguise falls away as her life hangs in the balance.

8-3The battle in the mind

To save Raven, Charles takes on En Sabah Nur in the mental plane. But his mind is overwhelmingly strong, and Charles is pushed to the brink.

8-4Erik turns

In the real world, a steel girder flung by Erik stops En Sabah Nur from reaching Charles. Asked whether he is betraying him, Erik turns on him and keeps attacking.

Scott and Hank join in, but En Sabah Nur is too strong, and they are driven back.

8-5Jean's fire

On the verge of defeat in the mind, Charles calls on Jean to unleash all of her power.

Jean awakens a vast, fiery power that has been sleeping inside her. Cornered, En Sabah Nur tries to escape, but Storm, moved by the sight of her hero Raven, joins the attack.

Realizing his end has come, En Sabah Nur is burned away by Jean's power.

8-6After the battle

Back in the real world, Charles returns the memories he erased from Moira, and the two reconcile.

Psylocke, who watched the fight, has quietly vanished.

Part 9: The new X-Men

9-1Rebuilding the school

Erik and Jean work together to rebuild the school. Charles asks Erik to stay and teach with him, but Erik declines. They part with a smile, as friends, and Erik leaves.

Peter, still not having told Erik he is his son, decides to stay at the school. Storm stays too.

9-2A new team

Raven and Hank begin training Scott, Jean, Storm, Kurt and Peter as the new X-Men.

In the training room, the five face Sentinel robots in their first exercise.

9-3After the credits

Men in suits arrive at the Alkali Lake base as it is being cleared out. They collect Weapon X research materials and vials of blood and pack them into a case marked Essex Corp.

Summary

Who died

- En Sabah Nur's four horsemen (3600 BC): fall fighting to protect him from the conspirators.

- Nina and Magda (Erik's daughter and wife): pierced by a single arrow loosed by a police officer in the chaos as police try to capture Erik.

- The Polish police officers: killed by Erik, mad with grief, using his daughter's locket.

- Alex Summers (Scott's brother): his own blast, fired to stop the horsemen taking Charles, hits a jet engine, and he is caught in the explosion.

- The soldiers at Alkali Lake: cut down by Logan (Weapon X) after Jean frees him.

- Angel: attacks the X-Men's jet and dies when it crashes.

- En Sabah Nur: fails to take over Charles's body and is burned away by the power Jean unleashes.

Where everyone ends up

- Erik: turns against Apocalypse, helps rebuild the school, then parts from Charles as a friend.

- Peter: stays at the school without telling Erik he is his father.

- Storm: turns against Apocalypse and joins the X-Men.

- Psylocke: disappears after the battle.

- Logan: regains part of his memory and vanishes into the snowy woods.

- Moira: Charles gives her back the memories he erased.

- Charles: loses his hair in the ritual. Raven and Hank begin training Scott, Jean, Storm, Kurt and Peter as the new X-Men.
