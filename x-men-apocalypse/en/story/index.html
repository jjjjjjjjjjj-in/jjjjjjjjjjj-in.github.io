Part 1: Ancient Egypt

The man called a god

Egypt, 3600 BC. En Sabah Nur is worshipped as a god. By moving his mind into other mutants' bodies, he has lived for ages and collected many powers.

Betrayal

During the ritual to move into a new body, rebels bring the pyramid down. Some of his horsemen die shielding him, and he's sealed deep underground, asleep.

Part 2: 1983

Waking in Cairo

1983. CIA agent Moira MacTaggert is investigating a cult in Cairo. An underground ritual lets the light in, and Apocalypse awakens.

Charles's school

Charles is teaching young mutants at his school. Jean Grey is plagued by nightmares about the end of the world.

Scott's eyes

High schooler Scott Summers suddenly fires uncontrollable beams from his eyes. His brother Alex brings him to the school.

Erik in Poland

Erik lives quietly in Poland under a new name, working in a factory with a wife and daughter.

Part 3: The Four Horsemen

Storm

Ororo, Storm, a young thief on the streets of Cairo, meets Apocalypse. He amplifies her power, and she becomes his first horseman.

Erik's tragedy

Erik uses his power to save a coworker. Police come for him, and in the struggle an arrow kills his wife and daughter. In fury, Erik kills the officers.

Psylocke and Angel

Apocalypse recruits Psylocke, who wields psychic blades, and Angel, a winged mutant forced to fight in a Berlin cage match. Angel's damaged wings are remade in metal.

Magneto

Apocalypse finds the grieving Erik and amplifies his power at the site of Auschwitz. Erik becomes the fourth horseman.

Part 4: Berlin and the school

Nightcrawler

Raven, Mystique, rescues Kurt, a blue teleporting boy, from the Berlin cage fights and brings him to Charles's school.

A day out

Scott, Jean, Kurt and Jubilee sneak off to the mall and the movies.

Cerebro hijacked

Charles uses Cerebro to find Erik, but Apocalypse hijacks the connection and uses Charles's power to launch the world's nuclear missiles into space.

Charles is taken

Apocalypse and his horsemen appear at the school and take Charles away.

Part 5: The explosion

The blast

As Charles is taken, Alex's power goes off near Cerebro, and the school explodes.

Peter's rescue

Peter, who has come looking for Erik, races through the explosion carrying students to safety, but Alex can't be saved.

Stryker strikes

Colonel Stryker arrives by helicopter and takes Hank, Raven, Moira and Peter. Scott, Jean and Kurt secretly follow.

Part 6: Alkali Lake

Weapon X

At the Alkali Lake base, the three find a captive man with a device on his head: Logan, stripped of his memories. He tears through the soldiers.

Jean's touch

Jean reaches into Logan's mind, restores a piece of his memory and calms him. He disappears into the snowy woods.

Freeing the others

The three free their captured friends and fly Hank's jet to Cairo.

Part 7: Showdown in Cairo

The world falls apart

Apocalypse has Erik tear at the world's metal, wrecking cities, and begins the ritual to move into Charles's body.

The X-Men fight

Raven, Hank, Peter, Scott, Jean and Kurt battle the horsemen. Angel falls fighting Kurt.

The battle within

Charles fights Apocalypse in the mental plane. Outside, Peter is beaten by Apocalypse without ever telling Erik he's his son.

Changing sides

Moved by Raven and Charles, Erik and Storm turn against Apocalypse and join the X-Men.

Jean's fire

Urged by Charles to let go, Jean unleashes a fiery power sleeping inside her. Apocalypse is consumed by it.

Part 8: The new X-Men

Moira remembers

Charles gives back the memories he once erased from Moira.

Rebuilding the school

Erik and Jean rebuild the school together. Erik then says goodbye to Charles and leaves.

A new team

Raven and Hank begin training Scott, Jean, Storm, Kurt and Peter as the new X-Men.

After the credits

Men in suits arrive at the Alkali Lake base. They take a vial of blood labeled Weapon X and place it in a case marked Essex Corp.
