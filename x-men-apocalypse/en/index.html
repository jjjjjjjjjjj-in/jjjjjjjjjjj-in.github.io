Key terms

Apocalypse (En Sabah Nur)
The first mutant, worshipped as a god in ancient Egypt. By moving into other mutants' bodies, he gathered many powers.
The Four Horsemen
The four mutants Apocalypse always keeps at his side, with their powers amplified.
Cerebro
Professor X's device for reaching minds worldwide, which Apocalypse wants to use.
Weapon X
Colonel Stryker's secret program using mutants, based at Alkali Lake.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

What did Apocalypse want?

To destroy a world he thought was ruled by the weak, leaving only the strong. He also planned to move into Charles's body to control every mind on Earth.

Why does Erik join him?

After his powers expose him in Poland, he loses his family. Consumed by grief and rage, he accepts Apocalypse's offer.

What's the link between Peter and Erik?

Peter knows Erik is his father, but he doesn't tell him in this film.

Who defeats Apocalypse?

Jean, by unleashing a tremendous power that had been sleeping inside her.

Easy-to-miss details

Peter saves the school

Peter carrying students out of the exploding school, set to music, rivals the kitchen scene from the last film.

Leaving the movies

Jean, Scott, Kurt and Jubilee joke about third movies in a series after leaving a theater.

Charles's hair

By the end, Charles takes on his familiar look.
