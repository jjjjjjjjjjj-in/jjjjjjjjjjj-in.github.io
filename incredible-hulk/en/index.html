Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

Bruce has a different actor here

Edward Norton plays him in this film. From The Avengers on, Mark Ruffalo takes over. Same Bruce Banner.

How he became Hulk is shown quickly

The opening montage briefly explains the lab accident that created Hulk. Don't miss it.

General Ross matters later

General Ross, who hunts Bruce, becomes an important figure in much later films.

Tony Stark shows up at the end

Iron Man's Tony appears in the final scene, a sign that the films are connected.

StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

1. The accident and the escape

Scientist Bruce Banner is left by a lab accident with a body that turns into Hulk when he's angry. After hurting the woman he loves, Betty, he disappears.

2. Life in Brazil

Bruce works at a factory in Brazil, practices breathing to control his anger, and searches for a cure with an online contact called Mr. Blue. Then something gives away his location to the army.

3. The army closes in

General Ross sends in a team that includes Blonsky. Cornered, Bruce turns into Hulk, overpowers them and escapes. Having seen Hulk's power, Blonsky wants more strength for himself.

4. Reunited with Betty

Back in the U.S. to find his research, Bruce reunites with Betty. The army attacks again, the enhanced Blonsky fights Hulk, and Hulk escapes carrying Betty.

5. Who Mr. Blue is

In New York, Bruce and Betty meet Mr. Blue, Dr. Sterns. The treatment seems to work, but Bruce is captured by the army.

6. The Abomination

Blonsky forces Dr. Sterns to use Bruce's blood on him, becomes the monster Abomination, and goes on a rampage in the city.

7. Hulk vs. Abomination

Only Hulk can stop him. Bruce chooses to jump from a helicopter and become Hulk, then fights and defeats the Abomination.

8. Separate paths

Bruce disappears again, but the final shot hints he's learning to control his power. Meanwhile Tony Stark visits General Ross and mentions a team he's putting together.

Key terms

Hulk
The huge green monster Bruce turns into when he gets angry or his heart races. Incredibly strong, and very hard to control.
Gamma radiation
The radiation Bruce was exposed to in the accident that made him Hulk.
Heart-rate monitor
The watch Bruce wears to track his pulse. If it climbs too high, he turns into Hulk.
The super-soldier program
Research into creating stronger soldiers that dates back to World War II. General Ross revives it and uses it on Blonsky.
Abomination
The monster Blonsky becomes from the super-soldier formula combined with Bruce's blood.
Mr. Blue
The online name of the unknown ally Bruce has been talking to. He turns out to be Dr. Sterns.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why does Bruce look different in The Avengers?

The role went to Mark Ruffalo after this film. Different face, same Bruce Banner, and the story continues.

How did Bruce become Hulk?

An accident during a military gamma radiation experiment. General Ross wanted to use the research to create stronger soldiers.

Why does General Ross chase Bruce?

He wants Hulk's power as a military weapon, even over his daughter Betty's feelings.

Why did Blonsky become a monster?

He hated getting older and weaker and wanted more power. He took the super-soldier formula, then used Bruce's blood and became the Abomination.

What does Bruce's last look mean?

As he meditates, his eyes flash green and he smiles slightly, suggesting he's starting to call up Hulk's power on his own terms.

Why does Tony show up at the end?

He tells General Ross he's putting a team together, a hint of the Avengers to come.

Easy-to-miss details

Stan Lee's cameo

Marvel Comics legend Stan Lee drinks a bottle contaminated with Bruce's blood, which is how the army tracks Bruce down.

A nod to the old TV Hulk

The actor who played Hulk in the old TV series appears as a university security guard.

What happens to Dr. Sterns

At the end, Bruce's blood gets into a wound on Dr. Sterns's head and something starts to change. That thread is picked up much later.

A Captain America connection

The formula General Ross gives Blonsky traces back to the World War II super-soldier research.

The ending and post-creditsSpoilers

After the end credits
