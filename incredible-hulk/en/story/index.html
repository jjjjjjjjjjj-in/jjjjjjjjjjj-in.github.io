Part 1: The accident (opening)

1-1The experiment at Culver University

The film opens with a short montage under the credits. At Culver University in Virginia, scientist Bruce Banner is working with his girlfriend and colleague Betty on an experiment for her father, General Ross. Officially, it's meant to make people resistant to gamma radiation.

In truth, it's Ross's attempt to revive a World War II-era "super soldier" program.

1-2The Hulk is born

Bruce tests it on himself and is exposed to gamma radiation. The experiment fails, and he turns into a huge green monster, the Hulk. From then on, anger or a racing heart can set off the change.

The Hulk wrecks the lab and injures Betty, General Ross and others nearby.

1-3On the run

Bruce flees and becomes a fugitive. General Ross, who wants to turn the Hulk's power into a weapon, devotes himself to catching him. The montage flashes through military files and news reports tracking Bruce.

Part 2: Hiding in Brazil

2-1Rocinha, Rio

Time has passed. In Rocinha, a hillside neighborhood packed with houses in Rio de Janeiro, Bruce lives quietly in hiding. He hasn't transformed in months.

He wears a heart-rate monitor on his wrist and is always careful not to let his pulse climb too high.

2-2Breathing lessons

Bruce trains with a martial arts teacher, learning to calm himself through breathing. Controlling his anger is his best defense against becoming the Hulk.

2-3Messages with Mr. Blue

Online, Bruce trades messages with a scientist who calls himself "Mr. Blue," without either revealing who they are. Bruce goes by "Mr. Green."

Together they're searching for a cure. Bruce has been sending Mr. Blue samples of his blood, but nothing has worked yet. Mr. Blue says he needs the detailed data from the original experiment.

2-4The bottling plant

Bruce works at a soda bottling plant. His coworker Martina is kind to him, but a gang of men there harass him because he can't speak Portuguese well.

2-5One drop of blood

One day Bruce cuts his finger at the plant. He hurries to clean up the blood, but a single drop falls into a bottle.

The soda is shipped to the U.S., where an elderly man in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, drinks it and comes down with gamma sickness.

2-6Ross finds him

At the Pentagon, General Ross hears about the case and traces the bottle back to the factory in Brazil. Knowing Bruce is there, he moves to send in a team.

Part 3: The chase in Rio

3-1Blonsky's team

At a military base in Florida, an elite team is assembled. At its center is Emil Blonsky, a Russian-born British Royal Marine, highly skilled but no longer young.

Without telling them exactly what they're facing, General Ross sends Blonsky's team to Brazil.

3-2The raid at night

At night, the team surrounds Bruce's home. He notices, slips out a window and runs across the rooftops while the soldiers chase him through the neighborhood.

3-3Cornered in the factory

Bruce hides in the factory, but the men who always harass him find him there and beat him. The soldiers close in, and his heart rate keeps rising.

3-4The Hulk emerges

At last Bruce turns into the Hulk. In the dark factory he hurls the men and the soldiers aside, and the team is helpless against him.

Blonsky survives and watches the Hulk smash through a wall and escape. He becomes fascinated by the creature he couldn't beat.

3-5Waking up in Central America

Back to normal, Bruce wakes up in the jungle in Guatemala, his clothes in tatters. He hitches a ride on a passing truck, and in a market in Mexico he is given a little money and buys new clothes.

He decides to head back to the U.S. for the data from the original experiment.

Part 4: Back to Culver

4-1Ross reveals the secret

General Ross tells Blonsky that the monster is Bruce, the product of a failed super-soldier experiment.

Blonsky, who wishes he could fight with a younger, stronger body, agrees to be injected with a serum derived from that research.

4-2Blonsky enhanced

The serum makes Blonsky far faster, stronger and more agile than any ordinary soldier.

4-3Stanley's pizzeria

Bruce returns to the town around Culver University. Stanley, a pizzeria owner who has known Bruce and Betty for years, quietly lets Bruce stay at his place.

4-4Sneaking into the lab

Bruce bribes a campus security guard with a pizza to get into the building. He borrows a student's computer to look for his old research, but the data has been erased.

4-5Reunited with Betty

Betty comes to Stanley's restaurant with her new boyfriend, psychiatrist Leonard Samson. She spots Bruce leaving and runs after him in the rain. They see each other for the first time in five years.

Overjoyed that he's alive, Betty takes him to stay at her house.

4-6The data Betty kept

Betty has kept a copy of Bruce's research records and gives it to him. Bruce sends the data to Mr. Blue.

Meanwhile, Leonard realizes Bruce is staying with Betty and contacts General Ross.

Part 5: Battle on campus

5-1The army surrounds the campus

The next day, General Ross arrives at the university with his troops and Blonsky. Bruce, who is with Betty, is spotted and cornered.

Betty pleads with her father to stop, but Ross won't listen.

5-2Sonic cannons and armored vehicles

Bruce transforms inside a building. The army attacks with vehicles carrying sonic cannons and with armored trucks, but the Hulk tears through them one after another.

Students film the battle on their phones.

5-3Blonsky takes him on

Enhanced by the serum, Blonsky charges at the Hulk alone and mocks him. The Hulk kicks him into a tree, breaking nearly every bone in his body.

5-4Saving Betty

A helicopter gunship fires on the Hulk, nearly killing Betty in the crossfire. The Hulk shields her and pulls the helicopter down.

Then he carries Betty away.

5-5A stormy night in the hills

On a night of rain and thunder, the Hulk takes Betty to a rocky shelter in the mountains. He roars at the thunder, and Betty gently calms him. He grows quiet beside her.

5-6Blonsky heals

Meanwhile, the badly injured Blonsky heals astonishingly fast thanks to the serum, and he wants even more power.

Part 6: Who Mr. Blue is

6-1On the road together

In the morning, back to normal, Bruce goes on the run with Betty. At a motel, the moment grows tender, but his heart rate climbs and they have to stop.

They buy a used truck. When Bruce contacts Mr. Blue, he urges them to come to New York.

6-2A New York taxi

In New York, they catch a taxi with a reckless driver. Seeing Bruce get agitated, Betty tells the driver off.

6-3Dr. Sterns

Mr. Blue turns out to be Dr. Samuel Sterns, a cellular biologist at Grayburn College. Thrilled to meet Bruce, he says he has made a possible antidote.

6-4A risky test

To test it, they have to trigger a transformation on purpose and inject the antidote mid-change. Bruce is strapped to a table and begins to turn into the Hulk.

Betty talks to the struggling Hulk to calm him while Sterns injects the antidote, and he turns back into Bruce.

6-5Not necessarily cured

The test seems to work. But Sterns warns that the antidote may only reverse that one transformation, not cure Bruce for good.

6-6A stockpile of blood

Sterns has used the blood samples Bruce sent from Brazil to synthesize a large supply. He wants to apply its "limitless potential" to medicine.

Fearing the Hulk's power could fall into the wrong hands, Bruce wants the supply destroyed.

6-7Bruce is captured

Blonsky and Ross's forces arrive and take Bruce into custody. Bruce and Betty are flown away in a helicopter.

Part 7: Showdown with the Abomination

7-1Blonsky's demand

Blonsky stays behind in the lab and orders Sterns to inject him with Bruce's blood, because he covets the Hulk's power. Sterns does as he is told.

7-2The Abomination is born

Blonsky's body mutates into the Abomination, a monster bigger than the Hulk, with bone jutting from its body.

The Abomination knocks Sterns aside. Some of Bruce's blood gets into a cut on Sterns's forehead, and his head begins to swell.

7-3Rampage in Harlem

The Abomination tears through Harlem, smashing cars and buildings and attacking soldiers, roaring as if to call the Hulk out.

He shouts, "Give me a real fight!"

7-4Jumping from the helicopter

In the helicopter, Bruce convinces General Ross that only the Hulk can stop the Abomination. He can't control the change, but he might be able to aim it.

Ross lets him go, and Bruce leaps out. He slams into the street and rises as the Hulk.

7-5Monster against monster

The Hulk and the Abomination clash in the streets. The Abomination is powerful and knocks the Hulk down again and again. The Hulk rips a police car in half and uses the pieces like boxing gloves.

In the middle of the fight, he protects Betty and General Ross from danger.

At one point he roars, "Hulk smash!"

7-6The chain

Finally the Hulk wraps a heavy chain around the Abomination's neck and nearly strangles him to death. But when he hears Betty's plea, he loosens his grip and spares his life.

He tosses the beaten Abomination aside for Ross's forces to arrest.

7-7Betty's name

The Hulk stands before Betty and says her name, "Betty." Then he leaves New York behind.

Ross captures the Abomination, but Bruce gets away again.

Part 8: Afterward

8-1The cabin in Canada

A month later, in Bella Coola, British Columbia, Bruce sits in a cabin with his eyes closed.

His heart-rate monitor climbs higher and higher. When he opens his eyes, they glow green, and he smiles. He seems to be learning to control the change.

8-2Tony Stark at the bar

Later, General Ross, his program shut down, is drinking alone in a bar when Tony Stark walks in.

Tony says he hears Ross has an unusual problem, and that the super-soldier program was put on ice for a reason. Then he asks, "What if I told you we were putting a team together?" Ross asks, "Who's 'we'?"

Summary

What Bruce did

Exposed to gamma radiation in General Ross's experiment, he became the Hulk whenever angered. He hurt Betty and others and fled (Part 1).

In Brazil he practiced breathing and searched for a cure with Mr. Blue (Part 2). Found by the army, he fled back to the U.S. for his data and reunited with Betty (Parts 3 and 4).

In New York he tried Sterns's antidote, but it was unclear whether he was cured (Part 6). In the end he chose to become the Hulk and stopped the Abomination (Part 7).

Betty and General Ross

Betty stood by Bruce even against her father and calmed the Hulk (Parts 5 and 6). In the end, the Hulk spared the Abomination when he heard her voice (Part 7).

General Ross kept hunting Bruce to turn the Hulk into a weapon, and had Blonsky injected with the serum (Part 4). In the end his program is shut down and Tony approaches him (Part 8).

Blonsky and Sterns

After failing to beat the Hulk, Blonsky craved power and took the super-soldier serum (Part 4). Then he had Sterns inject him with Bruce's blood and became the Abomination (Part 7). He rampaged through Harlem but was beaten by the Hulk and captured.

Sterns had been stockpiling Bruce's blood (Part 6). When Blonsky struck him, Bruce's blood got into a cut on his forehead and his head began to swell (Part 7).

Who lives and dies

None of the main named characters die in this film. Blonsky was badly hurt by the Hulk on campus but healed thanks to the serum (Part 5), and in Harlem the Hulk spared his life and he was captured (Part 7). Sterns, too, survives being struck (Part 7).
