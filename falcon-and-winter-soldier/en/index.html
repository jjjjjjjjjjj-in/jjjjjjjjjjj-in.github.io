StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

Episode 1: New World Order

Sam pulls off an Air Force rescue, but gives Steve's shield to a museum, feeling it isn't his. At home, he and his sister Sarah try to save the family boat, but the bank won't lend them money.

Bucky, pardoned by the government, is in therapy and making amends to people he hurt while brainwashed. He can't bring himself to tell his friend Yori that he killed Yori's son.

Finally, the government unveils soldier John Walker as the new Captain America.

Episode 2: The Star-Spangled Man

In Germany, Sam and Bucky fight the Flag Smashers, a group that wants a world without borders. Leader Karli and her people have taken stolen super-soldier serum, and even with Walker's help, they can't win.

Bucky introduces Sam to Isaiah, a Black soldier secretly given the serum long ago. Through police mistreatment and a joint therapy session, the two start opening up. To trace the serum, they decide to see Zemo in prison.

Episode 3: Power Broker

Bucky breaks Zemo out, and they go to the criminal haven of Madripoor. With help from fugitive Sharon Carter, they find Dr. Nagel, who recreated the serum. He made 20 vials, which Karli stole, and then Zemo shoots him dead.

Meanwhile, Karli blows up a depot of the GRC (the council running the post-Blip world) with people inside, killing three. Finally, the Wakandan warrior Ayo arrives to demand Zemo.

Episode 4: The Whole World Is Watching

Sam tries to understand Karli, but Walker barges in and ruins it. Zemo destroys nearly all the serum, but Walker pockets the last vial and takes it.

In a fight, Karli knocks Walker's best friend Lemar into a pillar and he dies. Enraged, Walker beats Nico, a Flag Smasher who didn't kill Lemar, to death with the shield in front of a crowd. Footage of the bloody shield spreads worldwide.

Episode 5: Truth

Sam and Bucky take the shield back from Walker, who is stripped of the title. Zemo is handed over to Wakanda.

Isaiah tells Sam how he was experimented on and, after rescuing his comrades, imprisoned for 30 years. At home, Sam fixes the family boat, trains with the shield, and becomes true friends with Bucky, who gives him a new suit from Wakanda.

Episode 6: One World, One People

Karli's group attacks the GRC in New York, and Sam, in his new suit, saves the hostages as Captain America. Sharon is secretly the Power Broker: she shoots Batroc when he blackmails her, and shoots Karli as Karli comes at Sam. Karli dies in Sam's arms. The remaining Flag Smashers are killed by a bomb planted by Zemo's butler.

Sam urges the officials to listen to people who lost their homes. Bucky finally tells Yori the truth and completes his amends.

Key terms

The Flag Smashers
A group fighting for a world without borders, angry at how the world changed after the Blip. Some have taken the super-soldier serum.
The super-soldier serum
The same kind of serum that made Steve strong, secretly recreated.
The GRC
The global council handling resettlement after the Blip.
Madripoor
An island nation in Asia full of criminals.
The Power Broker
The mysterious figure who runs Madripoor's underworld.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why does Sam give up the shield at first?

He wasn't sure he could replace Steve, or that the country would accept a Black man as its symbol.

Who is Isaiah Bradley?

A Black soldier secretly given the serum in the Korean War era. Instead of being honored, he was imprisoned for decades.

What happens to John Walker?

He's stripped of the title, but a mysterious woman named Valentina recruits him as U.S. Agent.

What happens to Sam?

He takes up a new suit from Wakanda and becomes the new Captain America.

Easy-to-miss details

Zemo's dance

Zemo's brief dance in a club in Episode 3 became a fan favorite.

Bucky's notebook

Bucky keeps a notebook of the people he hurt and is making amends one by one.

Sam's family

The family boat of Sam's sister Sarah and her sons in Louisiana is an important setting.
