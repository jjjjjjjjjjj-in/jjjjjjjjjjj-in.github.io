Episode 1: New World Order

1-1Rescue in the sky

Over Tunisia, a U.S. Air Force plane has been hijacked by the LAF, a group led by Batroc, who are taking Captain Vassant. Sam must rescue him before the plane crosses into Libyan airspace and causes an international incident.

On the ground, Air Force Lt. Torres supports Sam by radio as Sam flies after the plane.

Two enemy helicopters appear and open fire on Sam. He dodges and fights them in mid-air.

Sam rescues Captain Vassant. Batroc jumps out and escapes by parachute.

1-2Torres's report, and the shield goes to a museum

After the mission, at a café in Tunisia, Torres tells Sam about a group that organizes online and believes the world was better during the Blip: the Flag Smashers.

At a ceremony in Washington, Sam hands Steve's shield to the government to be displayed in a museum exhibit about Steve.

Afterward, his friend Rhodey (War Machine) asks if he's sure. Sam says it feels like the shield still belongs to Steve.

1-3Bucky's nightmare and therapy

Bucky wakes from a nightmare of his Winter Soldier days: in a hotel room he killed a target, then shot a young man who happened to see it.

As a condition of his pardon, Bucky sees a therapist, Dr. Raynor, who has set three rules for his amends: 1) break no laws, 2) hurt no one, 3) tell the person he is no longer the Winter Soldier and is making amends.

She asks about his nightmares and his lack of friends, but he doesn't open up much.

1-4Amends #1: Senator Atwood

Bucky's notebook lists people tied to his brainwashed past. One is Senator Atwood. Bucky slips into her car as she orders a rival congressman silenced, and he has recorded the conversation.

She is arrested and no one is hurt. Bucky crosses her name off his list.

1-5Yori and Leah

Every week Bucky has lunch with an elderly neighbor, Yori Nakajima. Yori talks about his son RJ, killed in a hotel; the killer was never found.

The young man in Bucky's nightmare was RJ: he witnessed a Winter Soldier assassination and Bucky killed him. Bucky can't bring himself to tell Yori.

Encouraged by Yori, Bucky goes on a date with the waitress Leah. She also talks about RJ's death, and Bucky's face clouds. He has been ignoring Sam's texts, too.

1-6Sam's family and the boat

Sam goes home to Delacroix, Louisiana. His sister Sarah, raising two sons, is struggling to keep the family fishing business going and plans to sell the boat. Sam objects: it's their family's history.

Hoping his fame will help, Sam goes with Sarah to apply for a loan. The banker is thrilled to meet him but turns them down, because the business makes little money and Sam had no income for the five years he was gone.

1-7The Flag Smashers in Switzerland

In Switzerland, Torres investigates the Flag Smashers and sees masked members rob a bank. He confronts one of them, but is thrown with inhuman strength and knocked unconscious.

1-8A new Captain America

Torres reports what he found to Sam.

Sam is watching the news with his sister Sarah. The government unveils a new Captain America: a soldier named John Walker, holding the shield Sam gave away. Sam is stunned.

Episode 2: The Star-Spangled Man

2-1John Walker

In a locker room at his old high school, Walker gets ready for a public appearance. His wife Olivia comes in to encourage him.

Walker runs onto his old school's football field as the new Captain America while a marching band plays a new arrangement of "The Star-Spangled Man," the song from Steve's 1940s shows. He gives an interview to Good Morning America there, calls Steve a brother, and says he wants to live up to Steve's legacy. His best friend Lemar is at his side.

2-2Bucky is angry

In his bare apartment, Bucky watches Walker on TV and grows angry.

Bucky confronts Sam: why did he hand the shield to the government?

Still arguing, they team up to chase the Flag Smashers.

2-3The fight in Munich

Without explaining his plan, Sam jumps from a plane. Bucky follows without a parachute and lands badly. Using footage from Sam's drone Redwing, Bucky finds him.

In Munich, the Flag Smashers are smuggling medicine by truck. Sam spots what looks like a young female hostage, who turns out to be their leader, Karli.

Sam and Bucky fight on top of the trucks. Every member has super strength, and the two are overpowered.

Walker and Lemar join in, but the Flag Smashers still escape. Sam's drone Redwing is destroyed.

Afterward, Walker and Lemar invite them to help the GRC, but Sam and Bucky refuse.

2-4Karli's side

Karli and her group deliver the stolen supplies to refugee camps, where people see them as saviors.

Karli receives a threatening message from someone called the Power Broker.

2-5Isaiah Bradley

Bucky takes Sam to see Isaiah, an old man in Baltimore. Isaiah is a super soldier who fought Bucky, then the Winter Soldier, in 1951 during the Korean War.

Sam is shocked to learn a Black super soldier ever existed. But Isaiah, bitter after 30 years of imprisonment and experiments, refuses to help with the new serum and sends them away.

2-6The police and therapy

Outside, Sam confronts Bucky for keeping a Black super soldier secret, and they argue. Police arrive and demand ID from Sam only, because he's Black. They change their tune when they recognize him, but Sam is left fuming.

Then Bucky is arrested for skipping therapy. Walker and Lemar get him released.

In return, Sam and Bucky do a session together with Dr. Raynor. Bucky's real fear comes out: if Steve was wrong about Sam, who gave up the shield, maybe Steve was wrong about Bucky too.

2-7Walker's offer, and Zemo

Walker asks them again; they refuse again, and he warns them to stay out of his way.

Meanwhile in Slovakia, the Power Broker's men close in on the Flag Smashers. One member stays behind to hold them off while Karli and the others escape.

To find where the serum came from, Bucky suggests asking a man who knows super soldiers: Zemo, in prison in Berlin.

Episode 3: Power Broker

3-1Zemo breaks out

Sam and Bucky visit Zemo in a Berlin prison to ask about the Flag Smashers.

When Zemo agrees to help, Bucky secretly arranges a prison riot so Zemo can escape. Sam is furious but reluctantly goes along.

3-2Zemo's jet

Zemo is rich, with a private jet and a butler, Oeznik.

On the jet, Zemo picks up Bucky's notebook and notes that the name Nakajima seems important.

Zemo explains that the world is in danger as long as super soldiers exist.

3-3Madripoor and Selby

They fly to Madripoor, a criminal haven run by the Power Broker. Zemo has Bucky act as the Winter Soldier, and Sam poses as a gangster who frequents the city.

They meet Selby, a powerful criminal. Using the Winter Soldier to get her attention, Zemo learns that the serum was recreated by Dr. Nagel, a Hydra scientist hired by the Power Broker.

Sam's sister Sarah calls him by his real name and blows his cover. Selby orders her men to kill them, but before they can act, a shot from outside kills Selby.

With a bounty on their heads, every bounty hunter in the city comes after them.

3-4Reunion with Sharon

Sharon Carter rescues them. A fugitive since the events of Civil War, she has been living in Madripoor.

Using her connections, she finds Dr. Nagel's lab and takes them there.

3-5Dr. Nagel

Dr. Nagel once worked on the serum for Hydra. After Hydra fell, the CIA hired him to continue the work using blood from an American given the serum long ago.

He says he recreated 20 doses, and Karli stole all of them.

The moment he finishes, Zemo shoots him dead: anyone who can make the serum will create more super soldiers.

Bounty hunters attack and the lab is destroyed. The three escape. Sharon chooses to stay in Madripoor, and Sam promises to get her a pardon so she can come home.

3-6Walker's pursuit, and Karli's bombing

Walker and Lemar arrive in Berlin and figure out that Sam and Bucky helped Zemo escape.

Meanwhile, Karli's group raids a GRC supply depot in Lithuania. To send a message, Karli blows up the building with people still inside.

3-7Latvia and a Wakandan warrior

The three head to Latvia to look for Karli.

Bucky spots Wakandan tracking devices in the street, leaves the others, and confronts whoever is following them: Ayo, a Wakandan warrior, who demands he hand over Zemo.

Episode 4: The Whole World Is Watching

4-1Wakanda, and eight hours

Six years earlier in Wakanda, Ayo reads the trigger words to test whether Shuri's deprogramming worked. Nothing happens, and Bucky weeps. He is finally free.

In the present, Ayo demands Zemo, who killed Wakanda's King T'Chaka. Bucky asks for eight hours, and she agrees.

Sam and the others learn that Karli's group bombed a GRC depot, killing three people.

4-2Donya's funeral

The three look into a refugee camp in Latvia, where Flag Smasher supporters house and teach people displaced by the Blip.

Handing out Turkish delight to the children, Zemo gets a girl to tell him where Donya Madani's memorial will be.

Donya raised children with nowhere else to go; she was Karli's adoptive mother. She died in the camp. Karli's grief turns to anger.

4-3Sam and Karli

Walker and Lemar show up. Sam convinces them to let him talk to Karli first, alone.

Karli says she only wants to save people who lost their homes. Sam understands but argues that hurting people is wrong.

An impatient Walker barges in and ruins it. Karli decides Sam is on the other side, and a fight breaks out.

4-4Destroying the serum

In the fight, Zemo shoots Karli. The serum keeps her alive, but she drops her vials.

Zemo smashes them one after another until Walker takes him down. Walker picks up the one vial Zemo missed and quietly pockets it.

Karli escapes.

4-5The Dora Milaje and Walker

When the eight hours are up, Ayo and the Dora Milaje come for Zemo. Walker refuses to hand him over, and they fight.

Walker and Lemar are utterly outmatched. Sam and Bucky step in so the Dora Milaje don't kill them. While everyone is fighting, Zemo quietly slips away.

Humiliated, Walker later talks with Lemar about the serum. Lemar says he'd take it, and that Walker would make the right call.

4-6Karli's trap

Karli plans to divide them. She calls Sam's sister Sarah and, with a veiled threat to her and her sons, lures Sam and Bucky to a meeting.

Meanwhile Walker and Lemar attack other Flag Smashers. Sam and Bucky rush to find them, and another fight breaks out, in which Sam realizes Walker has taken the serum.

4-7Lemar's death

Karli follows Sam and Bucky and joins the fight. In the chaos, she knocks Lemar away with her super strength; he slams into a stone pillar and dies. She never meant to kill him.

4-8Nico's death in the square

Consumed by rage, Walker chases Nico, the Flag Smasher who held him back from saving Lemar. Nico cries that he didn't kill Lemar.

Walker beats him to death with the shield anyway, in a crowded square. Sam, Bucky and Karli can only watch in horror.

People film it, and images of Captain America holding a bloody shield spread worldwide in moments.

Episode 5: Truth

5-1Taking back the shield

After killing Nico, Walker flees to a factory. Sam and Bucky catch up and demand the shield.

He refuses and they fight. Walker wrecks Sam's wings.

Working together, they get the shield back; Bucky breaks Walker's arm to make him let go.

Sam leaves his damaged wings with Torres and asks him to keep looking for the Flag Smashers.

5-2Handing over Zemo

Bucky finds Zemo at the memorial for those who died in Sokovia.

Bucky aims his gun and pulls the trigger, but he had already removed the bullets.

Zemo goes quietly and is handed to the Dora Milaje, to be sent to the Raft, a high-security prison at sea.

Ayo tells Bucky not to come to Wakanda for a while after working with Zemo, but agrees to ask Wakanda for a favor on his behalf.

5-3Walker's punishment and Valentina

At a hearing, Walker is stripped of the Captain America title and pushed out of the military in disgrace.

A mysterious woman named Valentina approaches him on a bench. She says taking the serum and killing the Flag Smasher were the right calls, and that she'll be in touch.

5-4The truth Isaiah tells

Sam goes back to see Isaiah in Baltimore, who tells him everything.

In the Korean War era, he and other Black soldiers were experimented on with the serum, and many died. When fellow soldiers who had also been experimented on were held captive, Isaiah went alone to rescue them. Instead of honoring him, the country imprisoned him for 30 years and kept experimenting. His family was told he had died.

Isaiah tells Sam a Black man will never be allowed to be Captain America, and shouldn't want to be.

5-5The boat and the shield in Louisiana

Sam takes the shield home to Louisiana. Sarah couldn't bring herself to sell the boat after all. Sam starts fixing it with Sarah and his nephews and calls on neighbors his parents once helped, who pitch in one after another.

Bucky arrives and hands Sam a case sent from Wakanda. He helps with the boat and trains with Sam, throwing the shield.

Bucky admits that when Sam gave the shield away, it felt like Steve's faith was thrown out, and that he had nothing left.

Sam tells him that to truly make amends, he has to be honest with the people he hurt. They agree to move past their pasts and work together.

5-6Lemar's parents

At home, Walker complains to his wife Olivia that no one understands what being Captain America takes. She tells him to start by visiting Lemar's family.

Walker visits Lemar's parents. When Lemar's father asks if the man he killed was the one who killed Lemar, Walker lies and says yes. Lemar's mother says Lemar was proud when Walker became Captain America. Lemar's sister looks skeptical.

In truth, Nico, the man Walker killed, was not the one who killed Lemar.

5-7Karli's next plan

Karli's group plans to strike a GRC conference in New York, where a plan to send refugees back to their old countries will be decided.

Batroc, Sam's foe from Episode 1, joins them. He was released from prison by Sharon, and he wants to kill Sam.

5-8The case from Wakanda

Torres calls: the Flag Smashers have been spotted in New York. Sam decides to act and opens the Wakandan case. Inside is a new suit made in Wakanda.

5-9Mid-credits

Walker builds a new shield of his own from scrap metal and his Medal of Honor.

Episode 6: One World, One People

6-1Attack on the council

The GRC meets in New York to vote on the Patch Act, which would send refugees back to their old countries.

The building's power goes out and tear gas sets off a panic. Karli's goal isn't to get in but to drive everyone out.

A disguised figure approaches Bucky and reveals herself: Sharon Carter. She joins the fight.

6-2The new Captain America

Then Sam arrives from the sky with the shield, wearing his new Wakandan suit and wings.

While Sam fights Batroc, the Flag Smashers take council members hostage.

Sam chases a helicopter carrying hostages and, with a rebuilt Redwing drone, saves them in mid-air.

6-3The truck and Walker

Bucky goes after trucks carrying hostages. Karli sets a vehicle on fire to distract him and get away, but Walker arrives with his homemade shield.

Bucky frees the hostages from the burning truck and fights the Flag Smashers alongside Walker, but is knocked down.

Karli drives the last truck to the edge of a drop. Walker chooses to save the truck rather than chase her for revenge.

Karli's group attacks him and they all fall to the ground below. Sam flies in, holds the truck, and the hostages are saved.

6-4Sharon's secret and Batroc's death

Once the hostages are freed, Batroc sets off smoke grenades so the Flag Smashers can escape.

Sharon corners Karli. In truth, Sharon is the Power Broker, the boss of Madripoor's underworld, and she is angry that Karli betrayed her.

Batroc tries to blackmail her for more money. Sharon shoots him dead on the spot. In the shootout, Sharon is also wounded by Karli.

6-5Karli's end

Sam tries to reason with Karli, but she won't listen and attacks him. He won't fight back and keeps telling her there is another way.

When Karli holds Sam at gunpoint, the wounded Sharon shoots her. Tearful, Karli dies in Sam's arms, saying she's sorry. Sam carries her body out.

6-6Sam's speech

In front of the cameras, Sam tells the GRC officials they decide millions of lives from their desks and must listen to the people who lost their homes.

He convinces the GRC to postpone the vote and help those people instead. As a Black Captain America, he shows in his own words what the shield means.

6-7The transport explodes

The surviving Flag Smashers are arrested, to be sent to the Raft. On the way, a bomb planted by Zemo's butler Oeznik blows up their transport, killing them all.

In his cell on the Raft, Zemo hears the news.

6-8U.S. Agent

Valentina gives Walker a new suit and dubs him U.S. Agent.

6-9Isaiah's statue

Sam brings Isaiah and his grandson Eli to the museum's Captain America exhibit, which now features a statue of Isaiah and his true story. Isaiah is moved to tears.

6-10Telling Yori

Bucky visits Yori and finally tells him: as the Winter Soldier, he killed Yori's son. Tearful, Yori asks why. Bucky says he didn't have a choice. Whether Yori forgives him isn't shown.

Bucky crosses off every other name on his list and hands the notebook to Dr. Raynor. His amends are finished.

6-11Celebration in Louisiana

In Louisiana, neighbors gather for a cookout by the restored boat. Bucky is invited and laughs with Sam's family.

The title card changes to Captain America and the Winter Soldier.

6-12Mid-credits

Sharon receives a pardon and is reinstated to her old CIA position. Afterward, she tells someone on the phone that she now has access to government secrets.

Summary (spoilers through the finale)

Bucky's amends (the notebook)

1) Senator Atwood (Ep. 1): he helped put her in office as the Winter Soldier. He recorded her ordering a hit and got her arrested.

2) Yori Nakajima (Eps. 1–6): Bucky killed Yori's son RJ, who witnessed a hit in a hotel. They became weekly lunch friends, but Bucky couldn't tell him until Episode 6. Yori is left in tears.

Finally, he crosses off every other name and hands Dr. Raynor the notebook (Ep. 6).

Who died (in order)

- Selby (Ep. 3): a Madripoor crime boss, shot from outside by an unseen shooter just after ordering Sam and Bucky killed.

- Dr. Nagel (Ep. 3): the scientist who recreated the serum. Zemo shoots him so no more can be made.

- Three people at the GRC depot (Ep. 3): Karli blew it up with people inside.

- Donya (Ep. 4): Karli's adoptive mother, who died in the refugee camp (seen at her memorial).

- Lemar (Ep. 4): Walker's best friend. Karli knocks him into a pillar during the fight, without meaning to kill him.

- Nico (Ep. 4): a Flag Smasher. Though he didn't kill Lemar, an enraged Walker beats him to death with the shield in a square.

- Batroc (Ep. 6): Sharon (the Power Broker) shoots him when he tries to blackmail her.

- Karli (Ep. 6): Sharon shoots her when she holds Sam at gunpoint. She dies in Sam's arms.

- The remaining Flag Smashers (Ep. 6): killed by a bomb planted by Zemo's butler Oeznik on the way to the Raft.
