Episode 1: New World Order

1-1Rescue in the sky

Over North Africa, a U.S. Air Force plane has been seized by a criminal group led by Batroc, who are taking Captain Vassant. The military asks Sam to rescue him.

On the ground, Air Force Lt. Torres supports Sam by radio as Sam flies after the hijacked plane.

Two enemy helicopters appear to guard the plane and open fire on Sam. He dodges and fights them in mid-air.

Sam rescues Captain Vassant. Batroc jumps out and escapes by parachute.

1-2Torres's report, and the shield goes to a museum

After the mission, Torres tells Sam about a group that organizes online and believes the world was better during the Blip. Sam is asked to keep an eye on them.

At a ceremony at a museum in Washington, Sam hands over the shield Steve gave him.

After the ceremony, Sam's friend Rhodey (War Machine) asks if he's sure. Sam says it feels like it belongs to someone else.

1-3Bucky's nightmare and therapy

Bucky wakes from a nightmare of his Winter Soldier days: in a hotel room he killed a target, then shot a young man who happened to see it.

Bucky sees a therapist, Dr. Raynor, who has set three rules for his amends: 1) break no laws, 2) hurt no one, 3) tell the person he is no longer the Winter Soldier and is making amends.

She asks about his nightmares and his lack of friends, but he doesn't open up much.

1-4Amends #1: Senator Atwood

Bucky's notebook lists people tied to his brainwashed past. One is Senator Atwood. Bucky slips into her car as she orders a rival congressman silenced, and he has recorded the conversation.

She is arrested and no one is hurt. Bucky crosses her name off his list.

1-5Yori and Leah

Every week Bucky has lunch with an elderly neighbor, Yori Nakajima. Yori talks about his son, killed in a hotel; the killer was never found. He was just in the wrong place at the wrong time, Yori says.

Bucky tries to say something to Yori but can't.

Yori plays matchmaker between Bucky and the waitress Leah. Bucky enjoys their date, but his face clouds when Yori comes up.

1-6Sam's family and the boat

Sam goes home to Louisiana. His sister Sarah, raising two sons, plans to sell the fishing boat their parents left them. Sam objects: it's their family's history.

Sam and Sarah apply for a bank loan. The banker is thrilled to meet an Avenger but turns them down: Sam had no income for the five years he was gone.

1-7The Flag Smashers in Switzerland

In Switzerland, Torres watches the masked Flag Smashers. When he tries to stop them, their leader throws him like a rag doll and breaks the bone around his eye. That strength isn't normal.

1-8A new Captain America

Sam is watching the news with his sister Sarah. The government unveils a new Captain America. Holding the shield Sam gave away is a soldier named John Walker. Sam is stunned.

Episode 2: The Star-Spangled Man

2-1John Walker

In a locker room at his old high school, Walker gets ready for a public appearance. His wife Olivia comes in to encourage him.

At the rally, Walker is introduced as the new Captain America and gives a TV interview, calling Steve a brother. His best friend Lemar is at his side.

Walker admits it's hard to be compared to Steve.

2-2Bucky is angry

In his bare apartment, Bucky watches Walker's interview and grows angry.

Bucky confronts Sam: why did he give up the shield?

Still arguing, they team up to chase the Flag Smashers.

2-3The fight in Germany

Without explaining his plan, Sam jumps from a plane. Bucky follows without a parachute and lands badly. Using footage from Sam's drone Redwing, Bucky finds him.

In Germany, the Flag Smashers drive trucks loaded with vaccines and medicine. Sam and Bucky jump on top and fight.

They find that every member has super strength. Even Bucky gets thrown around.

Walker and Lemar arrive by helicopter, but even four of them can't win. Afterward, Walker's team picks Sam and Bucky up.

Sam's drone Redwing is destroyed, and the group escapes.

2-4Karli's side

Leader Karli and her group deliver the stolen supplies to refugee camps, where people see them as saviors.

Karli's phone fills with threatening messages from someone called the Power Broker, demanding something back.

2-5Isaiah Bradley

Bucky takes Sam to see Isaiah, an old man in Baltimore. He was a super soldier in the Korean War era who was sent by the government to fight the Winter Soldier.

Sam is shocked to learn a Black super soldier ever existed. But Isaiah, bitter toward his country, sends them away.

2-6The police and therapy

As they argue outside, police demand ID from Sam only, because he's Black. They change their tune when they recognize him, but Sam is left fuming.

Bucky is arrested for skipping therapy. Walker gets him released.

In return, Sam and Bucky do a session together with Dr. Raynor. Facing each other, Bucky's real fear comes out: if Steve was wrong about Sam, who gave up the shield, maybe Steve was wrong about Bucky too.

2-7Walker's offer, and Zemo

Walker asks them to team up. They refuse, and he warns them to stay out of his way.

To find where the serum came from, Bucky suggests asking a man who knows super soldiers: Zemo, in prison.

Episode 3: Power Broker

3-1Zemo breaks out

Bucky visits Zemo in a Berlin prison and asks for his help.

During a riot, Zemo walks out with Bucky's help. Sam objects but reluctantly goes along.

3-2Zemo's jet

Zemo is rich, with a private jet and a butler, Oeznik.

On the jet, Zemo picks up Bucky's notebook and notes that the name Nakajima seems important.

Zemo explains that the world is in danger as long as super soldiers exist.

3-3Madripoor and Selby

They fly to Madripoor, a haven for criminals. Sam poses as a rich crook and Bucky as the Winter Soldier.

They go to the club of Selby, an information dealer. Zemo pretends to offer her control of the Winter Soldier in exchange for information about the serum. Selby says Dr. Nagel recreated it.

A call from Sam's sister nearly blows their cover.

Then Selby is shot dead through the window by an unseen shooter.

With a bounty on their heads, the whole city comes after them.

3-4Reunion with Sharon

Sharon Carter rescues them. Still a fugitive, she lives in Madripoor dealing in art.

Sam promises to push for her pardon, and she leads them to Dr. Nagel's lab.

3-5Dr. Nagel

Dr. Nagel once worked on the serum for Hydra. After Hydra fell, the CIA hired him, and he remade the serum from the blood of an American given it long ago.

He made 20 vials while working for the Power Broker, and Karli stole all of them.

The moment he finishes, Zemo shoots him dead: anyone who can make the serum will create more super soldiers.

The lab is then blown up, and the group barely escapes.

3-6Karli's depot bombing

Meanwhile, Karli's group raids a GRC supply depot in Lithuania and takes the supplies.

Karli blows up the depot with people still trapped inside. Three people die. Some in her group feel she's gone too far.

3-7Riga and a Wakandan warrior

Following Zemo's lead, they head to Riga, Latvia.

That night, the Wakandan warrior Ayo appears before Bucky and demands Zemo.

Episode 4: The Whole World Is Watching

4-1Wakanda, and eight hours

Six years earlier in Wakanda, Ayo reads the trigger words that used to turn Bucky into an assassin. Nothing happens, and he weeps. He is finally free.

In Riga, Ayo demands Zemo. Bucky asks for eight hours, and she agrees.

Sam and the others learn that Karli's group bombed a GRC depot, killing three people.

4-2Donya's funeral

In the refugee camp, Zemo hands out Turkish delight to the children and gets a girl to tell him about Donya and where her funeral will be. This is how he learns Karli will be there.

Donya raised children with nowhere else to go; to Karli she was like a mother. She died in the camp. Karli's grief turns to anger.

4-3Sam and Karli

Sam meets Karli alone. She says she only wants to save people who lost their homes. Sam understands but argues hurting people is wrong.

Walker barges in and ruins the talk. Karli concludes Sam is working with the other side, and escapes.

Karli calls Sam's sister Sarah and, with a veiled threat to her and her sons, has her pass a meeting place to Sam.

4-4Destroying the serum

A fight breaks out at the meeting place. Zemo shoots Karli. The serum keeps her alive, but her bag of vials spills out.

Zemo smashes the vials one after another.

The last vial is picked up by Walker, who quietly pockets it.

4-5The Dora Milaje and Walker

Walker asks Lemar if he would take the serum. Lemar says yes.

When the eight hours are up, Ayo and the Dora Milaje come for Zemo. Walker and Lemar try to stop them and are utterly outmatched.

Humiliated that Captain America can't win, Walker injects himself with the vial.

4-6Lemar's death

That night the fight with Karli's group resumes.

In the chaos, Karli strikes Lemar with her super strength, slamming him into a stone pillar. He dies. She never meant to kill him.

Zemo slips away in the confusion.

4-7Nico's death in the square

Consumed by rage, Walker chases Nico, one of the fleeing Flag Smashers. Nico cries that he didn't kill Lemar.

Walker beats him to death with the shield anyway, in a crowded square. Sam, Bucky and Karli can only watch in horror.

People film it, and images of Captain America holding a bloody shield spread worldwide in moments.

Episode 5: Truth

5-1Taking back the shield

Walker hides in a factory. Sam and Bucky catch up and demand the shield.

Walker refuses and they fight. He tears off Sam's wings.

They work together. Bucky breaks Walker's arm to make him let go, and Walker is knocked out. They take the shield back.

Sam leaves his broken wings with Torres and tells him to keep them.

5-2Walker's punishment and Valentina

At a hearing, Walker is stripped of the title and pushed out of the military in disgrace.

Meeting Lemar's parents, Walker lies that he got the man who killed their son.

A mysterious woman named Valentina visits Walker, says taking the serum was right, promises him work, and leaves.

5-3Handing over Zemo

Bucky finds Zemo paying respects at the memorial for those who died in Sokovia.

Bucky aims his gun and pulls the trigger, but he had already removed the bullets.

Zemo goes quietly and is handed to the Dora Milaje, who send him to the Raft, a high-security prison at sea.

Bucky asks Ayo for a favor, for Sam.

5-4The truth Isaiah tells

Sam goes back to Isaiah, who begins to tell him everything.

In the Korean War era, he and other Black soldiers were given the serum without being told. Many of them died from it.

When captured comrades were about to be abandoned, Isaiah went alone to save them. Instead of honoring him, the country imprisoned him for 30 years and kept experimenting. His family was told he had died.

Isaiah tells Sam the country will never accept a Black Captain America.

5-5The boat and the shield in Louisiana

Sam takes the shield home to Louisiana and decides to fix the boat instead of selling it.

Sam works on the boat with Sarah and Bucky, and neighbors who have known him for years pitch in.

Bucky arrives, helps with the boat, and trains with Sam, throwing the shield.

Bucky admits that when Sam gave the shield away, it felt like Steve's faith was thrown out, and that he had nothing left.

Sam tells him that to truly make amends, he has to be honest with the people he hurt. They finally become real friends.

5-6Karli's next plan

Karli's group plans to strike a GRC meeting in New York that will decide to send refugees back to their old countries. Batroc, Sam's foe from Episode 1, has joined Karli.

5-7The case from Wakanda

Bucky leaves Sam a case from Wakanda. Sam opens it: inside is a new suit made in Wakanda.

5-8Mid-credits

In a metal shop, Walker begins forging a shield of his own.

Episode 6: One World, One People

6-1Attack on the council

The GRC meets in New York to vote on the Patch Act, which would send refugees back to their old countries.

The building's power goes out and tear gas sets off a panic. Karli's goal isn't to get in but to drive everyone out. The Flag Smashers try to carry off council members as hostages.

A disguised figure approaches Bucky and reveals herself: Sharon Carter. She joins the fight.

6-2The new Captain America

Then Sam arrives from the sky with the shield, in a new red, white and blue suit with wings.

Sam chases a helicopter carrying hostages and, with a rebuilt Redwing drone, saves them in mid-air.

A van full of hostages is about to fall from a height. Walker, with his homemade shield, chooses the hostages over revenge on Karli, drops his shield and tries to pull the van back. Sam flies in and holds it with his wings, and the hostages are saved.

6-3Sharon's secret and Batroc's death

Sharon catches up with Karli and Batroc. In truth, Sharon is the Power Broker, boss of Madripoor's underworld, and both of them know it.

Batroc threatens to expose her unless she pays far more. Sharon shoots him dead on the spot.

6-4Karli's end

Sam comes to stop Karli. She attacks him, but he won't fight back and keeps telling her there is another way.

As Karli comes at Sam, Sharon shoots her. Karli dies in Sam's arms, saying she's sorry.

Sam carries her body out.

6-5Sam's speech

In front of the cameras, Sam tells the GRC officials they decide millions of lives from their desks and must listen to people who lost their homes. As a Black Captain America, he shows in his own words what the shield means.

6-6The transport explodes

The surviving Flag Smashers are arrested and taken to prison. On the way, a bomb in their vehicle explodes, killing them all. Watching from a nearby car is Oeznik, Zemo's butler.

In his cell on the Raft, Zemo hears the news.

6-7U.S. Agent

Valentina gives Walker a new black and red suit and a new name, U.S. Agent. He will now work for her.

6-8Telling Yori

Bucky visits Yori and finally tells him: as the Winter Soldier, he killed Yori's son. Tearful, Yori asks why. Bucky says he didn't have a choice. Whether Yori forgives him isn't shown.

Bucky visits Dr. Raynor and hands her the notebook, every name crossed off. His amends are finished.

6-9Isaiah's statue and the celebration

Sam brings Isaiah and his grandson Eli to the museum. The Captain America exhibit now includes a statue of Isaiah and his true story. Isaiah is moved to tears.

In Louisiana, neighbors gather for a cookout by the restored boat. Bucky is invited and laughs with Sam's family.

The title card changes to Captain America and the Winter Soldier.

6-10Mid-credits

Sharon receives a full pardon and is offered her old government job. Afterward, she tells someone on the phone she'll now have access to government secrets and weapons.

Summary (spoilers through the finale)

Bucky's amends (the notebook)

1) Senator Atwood (Ep. 1): he helped put her in office as the Winter Soldier. He recorded her ordering a hit and got her arrested.

2) Yori Nakajima (Eps. 1–6): Bucky killed Yori's son, who witnessed a hit in a hotel. They became weekly lunch friends, but Bucky couldn't tell him until Episode 6. Yori is left in tears.

Finally, he hands Dr. Raynor the notebook with every name crossed off (Ep. 6).

Who died (in order)

- Selby (Ep. 3): the information dealer, shot through a window in Madripoor by an unseen shooter.

- Dr. Nagel (Ep. 3): the scientist who recreated the serum. Zemo shoots him so no more can be made.

- Three people at the GRC depot (Ep. 3): Karli blew it up with people trapped inside.

- Donya (Ep. 4): Karli's mother figure, who died in the refugee camp (seen at her funeral).

- Lemar (Ep. 4): Walker's best friend. Karli knocks him into a pillar during the fight, without meaning to kill him.

- Nico (Ep. 4): a Flag Smasher. Though he didn't kill Lemar, an enraged Walker beats him to death with the shield in a square.

- Batroc (Ep. 6): Sharon (the Power Broker) shoots him when he tries to blackmail her.

- Karli (Ep. 6): Sharon shoots her as she comes at Sam. She dies in Sam's arms.

- The remaining Flag Smashers (Ep. 6): killed by a car bomb planted by Zemo's butler Oeznik while being taken to prison.
