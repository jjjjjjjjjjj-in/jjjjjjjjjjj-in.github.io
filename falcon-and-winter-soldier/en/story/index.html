Before you start: Who's who

Sam Wilson (Falcon)

A former U.S. Air Force pararescueman who flies with mechanical wings, and an Avenger. At the end of Endgame, an elderly Steve (Captain America) handed him the shield.

But Sam isn't sure he can replace Steve, or whether the country would accept a Black man as its symbol. His sister Sarah and her sons AJ and Cass live in Louisiana.

Spoiler-free note

Sam first appeared in Captain America: The Winter Soldier, where he met Steve while running and became his close friend.

Bucky Barnes (the Winter Soldier)

Steve's childhood friend. Captured and brainwashed by the evil organization Hydra, he was used for about 70 years as an assassin called the Winter Soldier. His left arm is metal.

Now free of the brainwashing, he has been pardoned by the U.S. government, on the condition that he attend therapy. He is trying to make amends to the people he hurt while brainwashed.

Spoiler-free note

It was Wakanda, the African nation from Black Panther, that freed him. His current metal arm was also made there.

John Walker

A U.S. Army soldier with three Medals of Honor. He works alongside his best friend Lemar Hoskins, called Battlestar. His wife is Olivia.

Spoiler-free note

The Medal of Honor is the U.S. military's highest award. Receiving three is extremely rare.

The Flag Smashers and Karli

A group of young people who want a world without borders. Their leader is a young woman, Karli Morgenthau.

Spoiler-free note

Background: during the five years after the Snap (when Thanos wiped out half of all people), those left behind helped each other across borders and moved into empty homes. When the vanished came back, the global council GRC put them first, and many who had settled were pushed into refugee camps. Karli's group acts for those people.

Zemo

A baron from the Eastern European country of Sokovia, who used Bucky to split the Avengers apart in Civil War. He is now in a German prison.

Spoiler-free note

Sokovia is the country whose city was lifted into the sky and destroyed in Age of Ultron. Zemo lost his family there and came to hate the Avengers.

Others

Sharon Carter: a former CIA agent, now a fugitive because she helped Steve in Civil War.

Ayo: a warrior of the Dora Milaje, the women who guard the king of Wakanda.

Isaiah Bradley: an old man living in Baltimore.

Joaquin Torres: an Air Force lieutenant who helps Sam on missions.

The Power Broker: the unknown boss of the underworld in Madripoor, an island run by criminals.

Spoiler-free note

Sharon is a relative of Peggy Carter, Steve's old love. Wakanda's former king T'Chaka died in the bombing Zemo caused in Civil War.

Episode 1: New World Order

1-1A mission in the sky

Over North Africa, a U.S. Air Force plane has been seized by a criminal group led by Batroc, who are taking Captain Vassant. The military asks Sam to rescue him.

Spoiler-free note

Batroc is the mercenary Steve fought on a ship at the start of The Winter Soldier.

1-2Lt. Torres helps

On the ground, Air Force Lt. Torres supports Sam by radio as Sam flies after the hijacked plane.

Spoiler-free note

Torres is a new character in this series.

1-3Dogfight with helicopters

Two enemy helicopters appear to guard the plane and open fire on Sam. He dodges and fights them in mid-air.

1-4Saving the captain

Sam rescues Captain Vassant. Batroc jumps out and escapes by parachute.

1-5Torres's report

After the mission, Torres tells Sam about a group that organizes online and believes the world was better during the Blip.

Spoiler-free note

The Blip is the five years people were gone. Their return has caused chaos over homes and jobs.

1-6The shield goes to a museum

At a ceremony at a museum in Washington, Sam hands over the shield Steve gave him.

Spoiler-free note

This is the shield an elderly Steve gave Sam at the end of Endgame. The museum (the Smithsonian) has an exhibit on Steve.

1-7Talking with Rhodey

After the ceremony, Sam's friend Rhodey (War Machine) asks if he's sure. Sam says it feels like it belongs to someone else.

Spoiler-free note

Sam isn't sure he can replace Steve, or that he'd be accepted as the nation's symbol.

1-8Bucky's nightmare

Bucky wakes from a nightmare of his Winter Soldier days: in a hotel room he killed a target, then shot a young man who happened to see it.

Spoiler-free note

Bucky still clearly remembers what he did while brainwashed, and it haunts him.

1-9Three rules for amends

Bucky sees a therapist, Dr. Raynor, who has set three rules for his amends: 1) break no laws, 2) hurt no one, 3) tell the person he is no longer the Winter Soldier and is making amends.

Spoiler-free note

Attending therapy is a condition of his government pardon.

1-10Talking with Dr. Raynor

She asks about his nightmares and his lack of friends, but he doesn't open up much.

1-11Amends #1: Senator Atwood's car

Bucky's notebook lists people tied to his brainwashed past. One is Senator Atwood. Bucky slips into her car as she orders a rival congressman silenced, and he has recorded the conversation.

Spoiler-free note

As the Winter Soldier, Bucky helped Atwood get into office. She kept abusing her power after Hydra fell.

1-12The senator's arrest

She is arrested and no one is hurt. Bucky crosses her name off his list.

Spoiler-free note

Hydra secretly planted its people inside the U.S. government (The Winter Soldier).

1-13Lunch with Yori

Every week Bucky has lunch with an elderly neighbor, Yori Nakajima. Yori talks about his son, killed in a hotel; the killer was never found. He was just in the wrong place at the wrong time, Yori says.

Spoiler-free note

Compare Bucky's nightmare with Yori's story.

1-14Bucky can't say it

Bucky tries to say something to Yori but can't.

1-15A date with Leah

Yori plays matchmaker between Bucky and the waitress Leah. Bucky enjoys their date, but his face clouds when Yori comes up.

Spoiler-free note

Bucky hasn't lived a normal life in about 70 years.

1-16Sam's family and the boat

Sam goes home to Louisiana. His sister Sarah, raising two sons, plans to sell the fishing boat their parents left them. Sam objects: it's their family's history.

Spoiler-free note

While Sam was gone for five years, Sarah held everything together alone.

1-17Turned down at the bank

Sam and Sarah apply for a bank loan. The banker is thrilled to meet an Avenger but turns them down: Sam had no income for the five years he was gone.

Spoiler-free note

The Avengers don't get a government salary.

1-18The Flag Smashers in Switzerland

In Switzerland, Torres watches the masked Flag Smashers. When he tries to stop them, their leader throws him like a rag doll and breaks the bone around his eye. That strength isn't normal.

Spoiler-free note

Masks with red handprints are the Flag Smashers' symbol.

1-19A new Captain America

On TV, the government unveils a new Captain America. Holding the shield Sam gave away is a soldier named John Walker. Sam is stunned, and Bucky watches too.

Spoiler-free note

Sam thought the shield would be displayed in the museum.

Episode 2: The Star-Spangled Man

2-1Enter Walker

Walker is introduced as the new Captain America at a rally at his old high school, with his best friend Lemar at his side.

Spoiler-free note

The title is the song Steve was made to perform on stage in Captain America: The First Avenger.

2-2Walker's honesty

Walker admits it's hard to be compared to Steve.

Spoiler-free note

Even a three-time Medal of Honor winner finds Steve's role a heavy one.

2-3Bucky is angry

Bucky confronts Sam: why did he give up the shield?

Spoiler-free note

To Bucky, the shield was Steve's trust in Sam.

2-4Teaming up

Still arguing, they team up to chase the Flag Smashers.

2-5Fight on the trucks

In Germany, the Flag Smashers drive trucks of supplies. Sam and Bucky jump on top and fight.

2-6All of them are super

They find that every member has super strength. Even Bucky gets thrown around.

Spoiler-free note

Steve and Bucky got their strength from the super-soldier serum. So many super-strong people means someone remade it.

2-7Walker and Lemar join

Walker and Lemar arrive by helicopter, but even four of them can't win.

2-8They escape

Sam's drone Redwing is destroyed, and the group escapes.

2-9Karli's side

Leader Karli and her group deliver the stolen supplies to refugee camps, where people see them as saviors.

Spoiler-free note

Karli's group is helping people who lost their homes. The show wants you to wonder who is right.

2-10The Power Broker's threats

Karli's phone fills with threatening messages from someone called the Power Broker, demanding something back.

2-11Isaiah's house

Bucky takes Sam to see Isaiah, an old man in Baltimore. In the Korean War, Isaiah was a super soldier who fought Bucky when he was the Winter Soldier.

Spoiler-free note

The Korean War began in 1950. The public never knew anyone but Steve got the serum.

2-12Isaiah's anger

Sam is shocked to learn a Black super soldier ever existed. But Isaiah, bitter toward his country, sends them away.

2-13The police and Sam

As they argue outside, police demand ID from Sam only, because he's Black. They change their tune when they recognize him, but Sam is left fuming.

Spoiler-free note

This shows racial profiling, and connects to Isaiah's story.

2-14Bucky arrested

Bucky is arrested for skipping therapy. Walker gets him released.

Spoiler-free note

As Episode 1 showed, therapy is a condition of his pardon.

2-15Joint therapy

In return, Sam and Bucky do a session together with Dr. Raynor. Facing each other, both reveal how much they miss Steve.

Spoiler-free note

It's funny, but also the first time they're honest with each other.

2-16Walker's offer

Walker asks them to team up. They refuse, and he warns them to stay out of his way.

Spoiler-free note

Walker isn't a villain so much as someone desperate to be accepted like Steve.

2-17Going to Zemo

To find where the serum came from, Bucky suggests asking a man who knows super soldiers: Zemo, in prison.

Spoiler-free note

In Civil War, Zemo dug into Hydra's super-soldier program.

Episode 3: Power Broker

3-1Zemo in prison

Bucky visits Zemo in a Berlin prison and asks for his help.

Spoiler-free note

Zemo is a Sokovian baron who used Bucky to split the Avengers in Civil War.

3-2Zemo breaks out

During a riot, Zemo walks out with Bucky's help. Sam objects but reluctantly goes along.

Spoiler-free note

Rule one of Bucky's amends is to break no laws, and he knowingly breaks it.

3-3Zemo's jet

Zemo is rich, with a private jet and a butler, Oeznik.

3-4The notebook

On the jet, Zemo picks up Bucky's notebook and notes that the name Nakajima seems important.

Spoiler-free note

It's the amends Bucky hasn't made yet.

3-5Zemo's belief

Zemo explains that the world is in danger as long as super soldiers exist.

Spoiler-free note

By his logic, super soldiers like Bucky and Steve are dangerous too.

3-6Undercover in Madripoor

They fly to Madripoor, a haven for criminals. Sam poses as a rich crook and Bucky as the Winter Soldier.

Spoiler-free note

Madripoor is a fictional island nation from Marvel comics.

3-7Selby the dealer

They meet Selby, an information dealer, who says Dr. Nagel recreated the serum.

3-8A call from Sarah

A call from Sam's sister nearly blows their cover.

3-9Selby is shot

Then Selby is shot dead through the window by an unseen shooter.

Spoiler-free note

Madripoor is ruled by the Power Broker.

3-10A bounty

With a bounty on their heads, the whole city comes after them.

3-11Reunion with Sharon

Sharon Carter rescues them. Still a fugitive, she lives in Madripoor dealing in art.

Spoiler-free note

In Civil War, Sharon secretly returned Sam's wings and Steve's shield. She's been on the run since, and resents that no one helped.

3-12Sam's promise

Sam promises to push for her pardon, and she leads them to Dr. Nagel's lab.

3-13Dr. Nagel's story

Dr. Nagel once worked on the serum for Hydra. After Hydra fell, the CIA hired him, and he remade the serum from the blood of an American given it long ago.

Spoiler-free note

The CIA is a U.S. intelligence agency, so the U.S. government was trying to remake the serum.

3-14Twenty vials

He made 20 vials, he says, and Karli stole them.

3-15Dr. Nagel's death

The moment he finishes, Zemo shoots him dead: anyone who can make the serum will create more super soldiers.

Spoiler-free note

Zemo's belief from the jet becomes action.

3-16The lab explodes

The lab is then blown up, and the group barely escapes.

3-17Karli raids a depot

Meanwhile, Karli's group raids a GRC supply depot in Lithuania and takes the supplies.

Spoiler-free note

The GRC is the global council deciding where returned people live.

3-18The depot bombing

Karli blows up the depot with people still trapped inside. Three people die. Some in her group feel she's gone too far.

3-19To Riga

Following Zemo's lead, they head to Riga, Latvia.

3-20A Wakandan warrior

That night, the Wakandan warrior Ayo appears before Bucky and demands Zemo.

Spoiler-free note

In Civil War, Zemo caused the bombing that killed Wakanda's King T'Chaka.

Episode 4: The Whole World Is Watching

4-1A memory of Wakanda

Six years earlier in Wakanda, Ayo reads the trigger words that used to turn Bucky into an assassin. Nothing happens, and he weeps. He is finally free.

Spoiler-free note

In Civil War, Zemo used these words to control Bucky.

4-2Eight hours

In Riga, Ayo demands Zemo. Bucky asks for eight hours, and she agrees.

Spoiler-free note

Ayo helped free Bucky.

4-3Tracking the funeral

Zemo learns Karli will attend the funeral of Donya Madani in the refugee camp.

4-4Donya

Donya raised children with nowhere else to go; to Karli she was like a mother. She died in the camp. Karli's grief turns to anger.

Spoiler-free note

Karli feels the world has abandoned the camps.

4-5Sam talks with Karli

Sam meets Karli alone. She says she only wants to save people who lost their homes. Sam understands but argues hurting people is wrong.

Spoiler-free note

Sam tries to listen before fighting.

4-6Walker barges in

Walker barges in and Karli escapes.

4-7A threat to Sarah

Karli calls Sam's sister Sarah and, with a veiled threat to her and her sons, has her pass a meeting place to Sam.

Spoiler-free note

Threatening his family changes things for Sam.

4-8The meeting turns violent

A fight breaks out at the meeting place. Zemo shoots Karli. The serum keeps her alive, but her bag of vials spills out.

4-9Smashing the serum

Zemo smashes the vials one after another.

Spoiler-free note

There were 20 in all; this bag held what was left.

4-10The last vial

The last vial is picked up by Walker, who quietly pockets it.

4-11A question for Lemar

Walker asks Lemar if he would take the serum. Lemar says yes.

4-12The Dora Milaje

When the eight hours are up, Ayo and the Dora Milaje come for Zemo. Walker and Lemar try to stop them and are utterly outmatched.

Spoiler-free note

The Dora Milaje are the women warriors who guard Wakanda's king.

4-13Walker takes the serum

Humiliated that Captain America can't win, Walker injects himself with the vial.

4-14The night fight

That night the fight with Karli's group resumes.

4-15Lemar's death

In the chaos, Karli strikes Lemar with her super strength, slamming him into a stone pillar. He dies. She never meant to kill him.

Spoiler-free note

Walker has just taken the serum, magnifying his strength and emotions.

4-16Zemo slips away

Zemo slips away in the confusion.

4-17Chasing Nico

Consumed by rage, Walker chases Nico, one of the fleeing Flag Smashers. Nico cries that he didn't kill Lemar.

4-18In the square

Walker beats him to death with the shield anyway, in a crowded square. Sam, Bucky and Karli can only watch in horror.

4-19Seen around the world

People film it, and images of Captain America holding a bloody shield spread worldwide in moments.

Spoiler-free note

This is what the episode title refers to.

Episode 5: Truth

5-1Walker in the factory

Walker hides in a factory. Sam and Bucky catch up and demand the shield.

Spoiler-free note

Having taken the serum, Walker is about as strong as Steve or Bucky.

5-2A brutal fight

Walker refuses and they fight. He tears off Sam's wings.

5-3Taking back the shield

Working together, they get the shield back.

5-4Wings for Torres

Sam leaves his broken wings with Torres and tells him to keep them.

Spoiler-free note

Torres has helped Sam since Episode 1.

5-5Walker's punishment

At a hearing, Walker is stripped of the title and pushed out of the military in disgrace.

5-6Lemar's parents

Meeting Lemar's parents, Walker lies that he got the man who killed their son.

Spoiler-free note

As Episode 4 showed, Nico wasn't Lemar's killer.

5-7Valentina

A mysterious woman named Valentina visits Walker, says taking the serum was right, promises him work, and leaves.

Spoiler-free note

Valentina appears for the first time. Who she works for isn't revealed.

5-8His own shield

Walker begins forging a shield of his own.

5-9The Sokovia memorial

Bucky finds Zemo paying respects at the memorial for those who died in Sokovia.

Spoiler-free note

Sokovia was destroyed in Age of Ultron; Zemo lost his family there.

5-10An empty gun

Bucky aims his gun and pulls the trigger, but he had already removed the bullets.

5-11To the Raft

Zemo goes quietly and is handed to the Dora Milaje, who send him to the Raft, a high-security prison at sea.

Spoiler-free note

The Raft is where Sam and others were locked up in Civil War.

5-12A favor from Ayo

Bucky asks Ayo for a favor, for Sam.

5-13Back to Isaiah

Sam goes back to Isaiah, who begins to tell him everything.

5-14The experiments

In the Korean War era, he and other Black soldiers were given the serum without being told. Many of them died from it.

Spoiler-free note

This ties to the American Dr. Nagel mentioned in Episode 3.

5-15Punished for a rescue

When captured comrades were about to be abandoned, Isaiah went alone to save them. Instead of honoring him, the country imprisoned him for 30 years and kept experimenting. His family was told he had died.

5-16Isaiah's words

Isaiah tells Sam the country will never accept a Black Captain America.

Spoiler-free note

"Truth" refers in part to Isaiah's hidden past.

5-17Home to Louisiana

Sam takes the shield home to Louisiana and decides to fix the boat instead of selling it.

Spoiler-free note

This picks up the boat story from Episode 1.

5-18Fixing the boat

Neighbors who have known Sam for years pitch in one after another.

5-19Shield practice

Bucky arrives, helps with the boat, and trains with Sam, throwing the shield.

5-20Bucky opens up

Bucky admits that when Sam gave the shield away, it felt like Steve's faith was thrown out, and that he had nothing left.

5-21Sam's advice

Sam tells him that to truly make amends, he has to be honest with the people he hurt. They finally become real friends.

Spoiler-free note

Sam's advice points to Yori.

5-22Karli's next plan

Karli's group plans to strike a GRC meeting in New York that will decide to send refugees back to their old countries. Batroc, Sam's foe from Episode 1, has joined Karli.

Spoiler-free note

Batroc is a mercenary working for money, not belief.

5-23The case from Wakanda

Bucky leaves Sam a case from Wakanda. Sam opens it: inside is a new suit made in Wakanda.

Spoiler-free note

This is the favor Bucky asked of Ayo.

Episode 6: One World, One People

6-1The GRC meeting

The GRC meeting begins in New York.

Spoiler-free note

The title is the Flag Smashers' slogan.

6-2Attack on the council

The Flag Smashers get inside and try to carry off council members as hostages. Bucky and Sharon move in.

6-3The new Captain America

Then Sam arrives from the sky with the shield, in a new red, white and blue suit with wings.

Spoiler-free note

It's the suit from the Wakandan case.

6-4Helicopter rescue

Sam chases a helicopter carrying hostages and saves them in mid-air.

6-5The truck and Walker

A truck full of hostages is about to fall off a ledge. Walker, with his homemade shield, chooses the hostages over revenge on Karli, drops his shield and tries to pull the truck back. The hostages are saved.

Spoiler-free note

Walker made this shield himself in Episode 5.

6-6Sharon's secret

Sharon catches up with Karli and Batroc. In truth, Sharon is the Power Broker, boss of Madripoor's underworld, and both of them know it.

Spoiler-free note

The Power Broker sent Karli threats in Episode 2.

6-7Batroc's death

Batroc threatens to expose her unless she pays far more. Sharon shoots him dead on the spot.

6-8Karli and Sam

Sam comes to stop Karli. She attacks him, but he won't fight back and keeps telling her there is another way.

Spoiler-free note

Sam tried to talk first in Episode 4 too.

6-9Karli's end

As Karli comes at Sam, Sharon shoots her. Karli dies in Sam's arms, saying she's sorry.

6-10Carrying Karli out

Sam carries her body out.

6-11Sam's speech

In front of the cameras, Sam tells the GRC officials they decide millions of lives from their desks and must listen to people who lost their homes. As a Black Captain America, he shows in his own words what the shield means.

Spoiler-free note

This is Sam's answer to what Isaiah said in Episode 5.

6-12The transport explodes

The surviving Flag Smashers are arrested and taken to prison. On the way, a bomb in their vehicle explodes, killing them all. Watching from a nearby car is Oeznik, Zemo's butler.

Spoiler-free note

In Episode 3, Zemo said super soldiers should not exist.

6-13Zemo on the Raft

In his cell on the Raft, Zemo hears the news.

6-14U.S. Agent

Valentina gives Walker a new black and red suit and a new name, U.S. Agent. He will now work for her.

Spoiler-free note

This is the work she promised in Episode 5.

6-15Telling Yori

Bucky visits Yori and finally tells him: as the Winter Soldier, he killed Yori's son. Yori is left speechless, tears in his eyes. Whether he forgives Bucky isn't shown.

Spoiler-free note

The young man in the Episode 1 nightmare was Yori's son. Bucky follows Sam's advice.

6-16The notebook

Bucky visits Dr. Raynor and hands her the notebook, every name crossed off. His amends are finished.

Spoiler-free note

The Bucky who wouldn't open up now goes to see her himself.

6-17Isaiah's statue

Sam brings Isaiah and his grandson Eli to the museum. The Captain America exhibit now includes a statue of Isaiah and his true story. Isaiah is moved to tears.

Spoiler-free note

It's the museum from Episode 1.

6-18Celebration in Louisiana

In Louisiana, neighbors gather for a cookout by the restored boat. Bucky is invited and laughs with Sam's family.

6-19A new title

The title card changes to Captain America and the Winter Soldier.

Spoiler-free note

It shows Sam has truly become Captain America.

6-20Mid-credits

Sharon receives a full pardon and is offered her old government job. Afterward, she tells someone on the phone she'll now have access to government secrets and weapons.

Spoiler-free note

This fulfills Sam's promise from Episode 3.

Summary (spoilers through the finale)

Bucky's amends (the notebook)

1) Senator Atwood (Ep. 1): he helped put her in office as the Winter Soldier. He recorded her ordering a hit and got her arrested.

2) Yori Nakajima (Eps. 1–6): Bucky killed Yori's son, who witnessed a hit in a hotel. They became weekly lunch friends, but Bucky couldn't tell him until Episode 6. Yori is left in tears.

Finally, he hands Dr. Raynor the notebook with every name crossed off (Ep. 6).

Who died (in order)

- Selby (Ep. 3): the information dealer, shot through a window in Madripoor by an unseen shooter.

- Dr. Nagel (Ep. 3): the scientist who recreated the serum. Zemo shoots him so no more can be made.

- Three people at the GRC depot (Ep. 3): Karli blew it up with people trapped inside.

- Donya (Ep. 4): Karli's mother figure, who died in the refugee camp (seen at her funeral).

- Lemar (Ep. 4): Walker's best friend. Karli knocks him into a pillar during the fight, without meaning to kill him.

- Nico (Ep. 4): a Flag Smasher. Though he didn't kill Lemar, an enraged Walker beats him to death with the shield in a square.

- Batroc (Ep. 6): Sharon (the Power Broker) shoots him when he tries to blackmail her.

- Karli (Ep. 6): Sharon shoots her as she comes at Sam. She dies in Sam's arms.

- The remaining Flag Smashers (Ep. 6): killed by a car bomb planted by Zemo's butler Oeznik while being taken to prison.
