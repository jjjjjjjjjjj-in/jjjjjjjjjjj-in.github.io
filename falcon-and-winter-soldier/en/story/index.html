Before you start: Who's who

Sam Wilson (Falcon)

A former U.S. Air Force pararescueman who flies with mechanical wings, and an Avenger. At the end of Endgame, an elderly Steve (Captain America) handed him the shield.

But Sam isn't sure he can replace Steve, or whether the country would accept a Black man as its symbol. His sister Sarah and her sons AJ and Cass live in Louisiana.

Spoiler-free note

Sam first appeared in Captain America: The Winter Soldier, where he met Steve while running and became his close friend.

Bucky Barnes (the Winter Soldier)

Steve's childhood friend. Captured and brainwashed by the evil organization Hydra, he was used for about 70 years as an assassin called the Winter Soldier. His left arm is metal.

Now free of the brainwashing, he has been pardoned by the U.S. government, on the condition that he attend therapy. He is trying to make amends to the people he hurt while brainwashed.

Spoiler-free note

It was Wakanda, the African nation from Black Panther, that freed him. His current metal arm was also made there.

John Walker

A U.S. Army soldier with three Medals of Honor. He works alongside his best friend Lemar Hoskins, called Battlestar. His wife is Olivia.

Spoiler-free note

The Medal of Honor is the U.S. military's highest award. Receiving three is extremely rare.

The Flag Smashers and Karli

A group of young people who want a world without borders. Their leader is a young woman, Karli Morgenthau.

Spoiler-free note

Background: during the five years after the Snap (when Thanos wiped out half of all people), those left behind helped each other across borders and moved into empty homes. When the vanished came back, the global council GRC put them first, and many who had settled were pushed into refugee camps. Karli's group acts for those people.

Zemo

A baron from the Eastern European country of Sokovia, who used Bucky to split the Avengers apart in Civil War. He is now in a German prison.

Spoiler-free note

Sokovia is the country whose city was lifted into the sky and destroyed in Age of Ultron. Zemo lost his family there and came to hate the Avengers.

Others

Sharon Carter: a former CIA agent, now a fugitive because she helped Steve in Civil War.

Ayo: a warrior of the Dora Milaje, the women who guard the king of Wakanda.

Isaiah Bradley: an old man living in Baltimore.

Joaquin Torres: an Air Force lieutenant who helps Sam on missions.

The Power Broker: the unknown boss of the underworld in Madripoor, an island run by criminals.

Spoiler-free note

Sharon is a relative of Peggy Carter, Steve's old love. Wakanda's former king T'Chaka died in the bombing Zemo caused in Civil War.

Episode 1: New World Order

1-1Rescue in the sky

Over North Africa. The Air Force asks Sam to rescue one of its officers, Captain Vassant, whose plane has been seized by a criminal group led by Batroc.

Sam catches the plane with his wings and fights off enemy helicopters in mid-air to save the captain, with Lt. Torres helping from the ground. Batroc escapes by parachute.

Spoiler-free note

Batroc is the mercenary Steve fought on a ship at the start of The Winter Soldier. The little machine on Sam's wings is his scouting drone, Redwing.

1-2Torres's report

After the mission, Torres tells Sam about a group that organizes online and believes the world was better during the Blip.

Spoiler-free note

The Blip is the five years people were gone. Their return has caused chaos over homes and jobs.

1-3The shield goes to a museum

At a ceremony at a museum in Washington, Sam hands over the shield Steve gave him.

His friend Rhodey (War Machine) asks if he's sure. Sam says it feels like it belongs to someone else.

Spoiler-free note

This museum (the Smithsonian) has an exhibit on Steve. It's where he took back his old uniform in The Winter Soldier.

1-4Bucky's nightmare

Bucky wakes from a nightmare of his Winter Soldier days: in a hotel room he killed a target, then shot a young man who happened to see it.

Spoiler-free note

Bucky still clearly remembers what he did while brainwashed, and it haunts him.

1-5Therapy with Dr. Raynor

Bucky sees a therapist, Dr. Raynor, who has set three rules for his amends: 1) break no laws, 2) hurt no one, 3) tell the person he is no longer the Winter Soldier and is making amends.

She asks about his nightmares and his lack of friends, but he doesn't open up much.

Spoiler-free note

Attending therapy is a condition of his government pardon. Skipping it could put that pardon at risk.

1-6Amends #1: Senator Atwood

Bucky's notebook lists people tied to his brainwashed past. The first name he crosses off is Senator Atwood. As the Winter Soldier, he helped her get into office, and she kept abusing her power after Hydra fell.

Bucky slips into her car and records her ordering a rival congressman silenced. She is arrested, and no one is hurt.

Spoiler-free note

Hydra secretly planted its people inside the U.S. government (The Winter Soldier). Senator Atwood was one of them.

1-7Lunch with Yori

Every week Bucky has lunch with an elderly neighbor, Yori Nakajima. Yori talks about his son, who was killed in a hotel; the killer was never found. He was just in the wrong place at the wrong time, Yori says.

Bucky tries to say something but can't.

Spoiler-free note

Compare Bucky's nightmare with Yori's story. It hints at why Bucky befriended him.

1-8A date with Leah

Yori plays matchmaker between Bucky and the waitress Leah. Bucky has a nice time on their date, but his face clouds when Yori comes up.

Spoiler-free note

Bucky hasn't lived a normal life in about 70 years and is still learning how.

1-9Sam's family and the boat

Sam goes home to Louisiana. His sister Sarah, raising two sons, plans to sell the fishing boat their parents left them. Sam objects: the boat is their family's history.

Spoiler-free note

During the five years Sam was gone, Sarah held the family and the boat together alone.

1-10Turned down at the bank

Sam and Sarah apply for a bank loan. The banker is thrilled to meet an Avenger but turns them down: Sam had no income during the five years he was gone.

Spoiler-free note

The Avengers don't get a government salary. Being a hero doesn't pay the bills.

1-11The Flag Smashers in Switzerland

Torres tracks the Flag Smashers in Switzerland. When he tries to stop the masked group after a bank robbery, their leader throws him around like a rag doll and breaks the bone around his eye. The leader's strength isn't normal.

Spoiler-free note

The masks with red handprints are the Flag Smashers' symbol.

1-12A new Captain America

On TV, the government unveils a new Captain America. Holding the shield Sam gave away is a soldier named John Walker. Sam is stunned, and Bucky watches the same broadcast.

Spoiler-free note

Sam gave the shield to be displayed in the museum. He had no idea the government would hand it to someone else.

Episode 2: The Star-Spangled Man

2-1Walker and Lemar

Walker is introduced as the new Captain America at a rally at his old high school, with his best friend Lemar at his side. He admits it's hard to be compared to Steve.

Spoiler-free note

The episode title is the song Steve was made to perform on stage in Captain America: The First Avenger.

2-2Bucky is angry

Bucky confronts Sam: why did he give up the shield? Still arguing, they team up to go after the Flag Smashers.

Spoiler-free note

To Bucky, the shield was Steve's vote of trust in Sam. He can't accept that Sam gave it away.

2-3Fight on the trucks

In Germany, the Flag Smashers are driving trucks of supplies. Sam and Bucky jump onto them and find that every member has super strength.

Walker and Lemar join by helicopter, but even four of them can't win. Sam's drone Redwing is destroyed, and the group escapes.

Spoiler-free note

Steve and Bucky got their strength from the super-soldier serum. So many people with that strength means someone has recreated it.

2-4Karli's side

Leader Karli and her group deliver the stolen supplies to refugee camps, where people see them as saviors.

Meanwhile, Karli's phone fills with threatening messages from someone called the Power Broker, demanding something back.

Spoiler-free note

Karli's group aren't simple criminals; they're helping people who lost their homes. The show wants you to wrestle with who is right.

2-5Isaiah Bradley

Bucky takes Sam to see Isaiah, an old man in Baltimore. Back in the Korean War, Isaiah was a super soldier who fought Bucky when he was the Winter Soldier.

Sam is shocked to learn a Black super soldier ever existed. But Isaiah, bitter toward his country, sends them away.

Spoiler-free note

The Korean War began in 1950. The public was never told that anyone besides Steve had been given the serum.

2-6The police and Sam

As they argue outside, police officers come up and demand ID from Sam only, because he's Black. They change their tune when they recognize him, but Sam is left fuming.

Spoiler-free note

The scene depicts racial profiling, a real problem in the U.S., and connects to Isaiah's story.

2-7Joint therapy

Bucky is arrested for skipping therapy. Walker gets him released, but in return Sam and Bucky must do a session together with Dr. Raynor. Facing each other, both reveal how much they miss Steve.

Spoiler-free note

It's funny, but it's also the first time the two are honest with each other.

2-8Walker's offer

Walker asks them to team up. They refuse, and he warns them to stay out of his way.

Spoiler-free note

Walker isn't a villain so much as someone desperate to be accepted the way Steve was.

2-9Going to Zemo

To find where the serum came from, Bucky suggests asking a man who knows all about super soldiers: Zemo, in prison.

Spoiler-free note

In Civil War, Zemo dug into Hydra's super-soldier program, which is why he knows about the serum.

Episode 3: Power Broker

3-1Zemo breaks out

Bucky visits Zemo in a Berlin prison. During a riot, Zemo walks out with Bucky's help. Sam objects but reluctantly goes along.

Spoiler-free note

Rule one of Bucky's amends is to break no laws, and he knowingly breaks it here.

3-2Zemo's jet

Zemo is rich, with a private jet and a butler, Oeznik. On the jet, Zemo picks up Bucky's notebook and notes that the name Nakajima seems important.

Zemo explains his belief that the world is in danger as long as super soldiers exist.

Spoiler-free note

By Zemo's logic, super soldiers like Bucky and Steve are dangerous too. His goals differ from Sam and Bucky's.

3-3Undercover in Madripoor

They fly to Madripoor, a haven for criminals. Sam poses as a rich crook and Bucky as the Winter Soldier to meet Selby, an information dealer.

Spoiler-free note

Madripoor is a fictional island nation from Marvel comics.

3-4What Selby says

Selby says Dr. Nagel recreated the serum. A call from Sam's sister nearly blows their cover, and then Selby is shot dead through the window by an unseen shooter.

With a bounty on their heads, the whole city comes after them.

Spoiler-free note

Madripoor is ruled by the Power Broker.

3-5Reunion with Sharon

Sharon Carter rescues them. Still a fugitive, she lives in Madripoor dealing in art. Sam promises to push for her pardon, and she leads them to Dr. Nagel's lab.

Spoiler-free note

In Civil War, Sharon secretly returned Sam's wings and Steve's shield after they were confiscated. She has been on the run ever since, and resents that no one helped her.

3-6Dr. Nagel's story

Dr. Nagel once worked on the serum for Hydra. After Hydra fell, the CIA hired him, and he recreated the serum from the blood of an American given it long ago. He made 20 vials, and Karli stole them.

Spoiler-free note

The CIA is a U.S. intelligence agency, which means the U.S. government itself was trying to remake the serum.

3-7Dr. Nagel's death

The moment he finishes talking, Zemo shoots him dead: anyone who can make the serum will create more super soldiers. The lab is then blown up, and the group barely escapes.

Spoiler-free note

Zemo's belief from the jet becomes action here.

3-8Karli's bombing

Meanwhile, Karli's group raids a GRC supply depot in Lithuania. After taking the supplies, Karli blows it up with people still trapped inside. Three people die.

Some in her group feel she's gone too far.

Spoiler-free note

The GRC is the global council deciding where returned people live. Karli sees it as the side making refugees suffer.

3-9A Wakandan warrior

Following Zemo's lead, they head to Riga, Latvia. That night, the Wakandan warrior Ayo appears before Bucky and demands Zemo.

Spoiler-free note

In Civil War, Zemo caused the bombing that killed Wakanda's King T'Chaka. To Wakanda, he is the king's murderer.

Episode 4: The Whole World Is Watching

4-1A memory of Wakanda

Six years earlier in Wakanda, Ayo reads the trigger words that used to turn Bucky into an assassin. Nothing happens, and he weeps. He is finally free.

Spoiler-free note

In Civil War, Zemo used these words to control Bucky. Bucky is seen in Wakanda at the end of Black Panther.

4-2Eight hours

In Riga, Ayo demands Zemo. Bucky asks for eight hours, and she agrees.

Spoiler-free note

Ayo helped free Bucky, which is why she grants his request just this once.

4-3Donya's funeral

Zemo learns Karli will attend the funeral of Donya Madani, a woman in the refugee camp who raised children with nowhere else to go. To Karli she was like a mother. She fell ill in the camp and died.

Spoiler-free note

Karli is angry because she feels the world has abandoned the camps. Donya's death deepens that anger.

4-4Sam talks with Karli

Sam meets Karli alone. She says she only wants to save people who lost their homes. Sam understands her but argues that hurting people is wrong.

Walker barges in and Karli escapes.

Spoiler-free note

Sam tries to listen before fighting. That sets him apart from both Steve and Walker.

4-5A threat to Sarah

Karli calls Sam's sister Sarah and, with a thinly veiled threat to her and her sons, has her pass along a meeting place to Sam. Sam is enraged that his family has been targeted.

Spoiler-free note

Sam had sympathized with Karli, but threatening his family changes things.

4-6Zemo destroys the serum

A fight breaks out at the meeting place. Zemo shoots Karli. The serum keeps her alive, but her bag of vials spills out, and Zemo smashes them one after another.

The last vial, though, is picked up by Walker, who quietly pockets it.

Spoiler-free note

There were 20 vials in all. This bag held what was left after Karli's group took their doses.

4-7The Dora Milaje vs. Walker

When the eight hours are up, Ayo and the Dora Milaje come for Zemo. Walker and Lemar try to stop them and are utterly outmatched.

Humiliated that Captain America can't win, Walker later injects himself with the vial he took.

Spoiler-free note

Walker and Lemar once talked about whether they'd take the serum if they could. Walker had been wrestling with it.

4-8Lemar's death

That night the fight with Karli's group resumes. In the chaos, Karli strikes Lemar with her super strength, slamming him into a stone pillar. He dies.

She never meant to kill him, but Walker has just watched his best friend die. Zemo slips away in the confusion.

Spoiler-free note

Walker has just taken the serum, magnifying both his strength and his emotions.

4-9Nico's death in the square

Consumed by rage, Walker chases Nico, one of the fleeing Flag Smashers. Nico cries out that he wasn't the one who killed Lemar.

Walker beats him to death with the shield anyway, in a crowded square. People film it on their phones, and images of Captain America holding a bloody shield spread around the world in moments. Sam, Bucky and Karli can only watch in horror.

Spoiler-free note

This is the scene the episode title refers to. The shield is also the symbol of the nation.

Episode 5: Truth

5-1Taking back the shield

Sam and Bucky find Walker hiding in a factory and demand the shield. The fight with the serum-powered Walker is brutal, and he tears off Sam's wings. Together, they still get the shield back.

Spoiler-free note

Walker took the serum in Episode 4, so he is now about as strong as Steve or Bucky.

5-2Wings for Torres

Sam leaves his broken wings with Torres and tells him to keep them.

Spoiler-free note

Torres is the Air Force lieutenant who has helped Sam since Episode 1.

5-3Walker's punishment

At a military hearing, Walker is stripped of the Captain America title and pushed out of the military in disgrace.

When he meets Lemar's parents, he lies that he got the man who killed their son.

Spoiler-free note

As Episode 4 showed, Nico, the man Walker killed, was not the one who killed Lemar.

5-4Valentina

A mysterious woman named Valentina visits Walker. She tells him taking the serum was the right call, says she'll have work for him, and leaves. Walker then begins forging a shield of his own.

Spoiler-free note

Valentina appears for the first time here. Who she works for isn't revealed yet.

5-5Handing over Zemo

Bucky finds Zemo paying respects at the memorial for those who died in Sokovia. Bucky aims his gun and pulls the trigger, but he had already removed the bullets.

Zemo goes quietly and is handed to the Dora Milaje, who send him to the Raft, a high-security prison at sea. Bucky asks Ayo for a favor, for Sam.

Spoiler-free note

The Raft is the prison where Sam and others were locked up in Civil War.

5-6The truth Isaiah tells

Sam goes back to see Isaiah, who finally tells him everything.

In the Korean War era, he and other Black soldiers were given the serum in experiments without being told. Many of them died from it. When captured comrades were about to be abandoned, Isaiah went alone to rescue them. Instead of honoring him, the country imprisoned him for 30 years and kept experimenting on him. His family was told he had died.

Isaiah tells Sam the country will never accept a Black Captain America.

Spoiler-free note

The title "Truth" refers in part to Isaiah's hidden past. It also ties to the American Dr. Nagel mentioned in Episode 3.

5-7Fixing the boat at home

Sam takes the shield home to Louisiana. Instead of selling the family boat, he fixes it with help from neighbors who have known him for years.

Spoiler-free note

This picks up the boat story from the bank scene in Episode 1.

5-8Training, and making up

Bucky comes to help and trains with Sam, throwing the shield. He admits that when Sam gave the shield away, it felt like Steve's faith was thrown out, and that he had nothing left. Sam tells him that to truly make amends, he has to be honest with the people he hurt. They finally become real friends.

Bucky leaves Sam a case sent from Wakanda.

Spoiler-free note

Sam's advice points to Yori, the person Bucky still hasn't been honest with.

5-9Karli's next plan

Karli's group plans to strike a GRC meeting in New York, where a plan to send refugees back to their old countries will be decided. Batroc, Sam's foe from Episode 1, has joined Karli.

Spoiler-free note

Batroc is a mercenary who works for money, not because he shares Karli's beliefs.

5-10The case from Wakanda

Sam opens the case. Inside is a new suit made in Wakanda. He decides to carry the shield.

Spoiler-free note

This case is the favor Bucky asked of Ayo.

Episode 6: One World, One People

6-1Attack on the council

The GRC meets in New York. The Flag Smashers get inside and try to carry off council members as hostages. Bucky and Sharon move in.

Spoiler-free note

The title "One World, One People" is the Flag Smashers' slogan.

6-2The new Captain America

Then Sam arrives from the sky with the shield, wearing a new red, white and blue suit with wings: the new Captain America.

Spoiler-free note

This is the suit from the Wakandan case in Episode 5. Using both wings and shield is Sam's own style.

6-3Rescues in the air and on the ground

Sam chases a helicopter carrying hostages and saves them in mid-air.

On the ground, a truck full of hostages is about to fall off a ledge. Walker, carrying his homemade shield, gives up chasing Karli and holds the truck up with Bucky to save them.

Spoiler-free note

Walker's shield is the one he began forging himself in Episode 5.

6-4Batroc's death

Sharon catches up with Karli and Batroc. In truth, Sharon is the Power Broker, the boss of Madripoor's underworld, and both of them know it.

Batroc threatens to expose her unless she pays him far more. Sharon shoots him dead on the spot.

Spoiler-free note

The Power Broker is the one who sent Karli threatening messages in Episode 2.

6-5Karli's end

Sam comes to stop Karli. She attacks him, but he won't fight back and keeps telling her there is another way.

As Karli comes at Sam, Sharon shoots her. Karli dies in Sam's arms, saying she's sorry. Sam carries her body out.

Spoiler-free note

Sam tried to talk with Karli before fighting in Episode 4 too, and he holds to that to the end.

6-6Sam's speech

In front of the cameras, Sam addresses the GRC officials: they decide millions of lives from their desks, and they need to listen to the people who lost their homes.

As a Black Captain America, Sam shows in his own words what carrying the shield means.

Spoiler-free note

In Episode 5, Isaiah said the country would never accept a Black Captain America. This is Sam's answer.

6-7The last Flag Smashers die

The surviving Flag Smashers are arrested and transferred to prison. On the way, a bomb planted in their vehicle explodes, killing them all.

Watching from a nearby car is Oeznik, Zemo's butler. In his cell on the Raft, Zemo hears the news.

Spoiler-free note

In Episode 3, Zemo said super soldiers should not exist. Oeznik is the butler from Episode 3.

6-8Walker's new name

Valentina gives Walker a new black and red suit and a new name: U.S. Agent. He will now work for her.

Spoiler-free note

In Episode 5, Valentina told Walker she would have work for him.

6-9The last amends: telling Yori

Bucky visits Yori and finally tells him the truth: as the Winter Soldier, he killed Yori's son.

Yori is left speechless, tears in his eyes. Whether he forgives Bucky isn't shown, but Bucky faced him with the truth.

Spoiler-free note

The young man shot in the Episode 1 nightmare was Yori's son. Bucky follows Sam's Episode 5 advice to be honest.

6-10The notebook goes to Dr. Raynor

Bucky visits Dr. Raynor and hands her the notebook, every name now crossed off. His list of amends is finished.

Spoiler-free note

The Bucky who wouldn't open up in Episode 1 now goes to see her on his own.

6-11Isaiah's statue

Sam brings Isaiah and his grandson Eli to the museum. The Captain America exhibit now includes a statue of Isaiah and his true story. Isaiah, long forgotten by his country, is moved to tears.

Spoiler-free note

It's the same museum where Sam gave up the shield in Episode 1.

6-12Celebration in Louisiana

In Louisiana, neighbors gather for a cookout by the restored family boat. Bucky is invited and laughs with Sam's family.

The title card changes to Captain America and the Winter Soldier.

Spoiler-free note

The changed title shows that Sam has truly become Captain America.

6-13Mid-credits

Sharon receives a full pardon and is offered her old government job. Afterward, she makes a phone call: she'll now have access to government secrets and weapons. The Power Broker's next scheme is about to begin.

Spoiler-free note

This fulfills Sam's promise in Episode 3 to push for Sharon's pardon.

Summary (spoilers through the finale)

Bucky's amends (the notebook)

1) Senator Atwood (Ep. 1): he helped put her in office as the Winter Soldier. He recorded her ordering a hit and got her arrested.

2) Yori Nakajima (Eps. 1–6): Bucky killed Yori's son, who witnessed a hit in a hotel. They became weekly lunch friends, but Bucky couldn't tell him until Episode 6. Yori is left in tears.

Finally, he hands Dr. Raynor the notebook with every name crossed off (Ep. 6).

Who died (in order)

- Selby (Ep. 3): the information dealer, shot through a window in Madripoor by an unseen shooter.

- Dr. Nagel (Ep. 3): the scientist who recreated the serum. Zemo shoots him so no more can be made.

- Three people at the GRC depot (Ep. 3): Karli blew it up with people trapped inside.

- Donya (Ep. 4): Karli's mother figure, who fell ill and died in the refugee camp (seen at her funeral).

- Lemar (Ep. 4): Walker's best friend. Karli knocks him into a pillar during the fight, without meaning to kill him.

- Nico (Ep. 4): a Flag Smasher. Though he didn't kill Lemar, an enraged Walker beats him to death with the shield in a square.

- Batroc (Ep. 6): Sharon (the Power Broker) shoots him when he tries to blackmail her.

- Karli (Ep. 6): Sharon shoots her as she comes at Sam. She dies in Sam's arms.

- The remaining Flag Smashers (Ep. 6): killed by a car bomb planted by Zemo's butler Oeznik while being taken to prison.
