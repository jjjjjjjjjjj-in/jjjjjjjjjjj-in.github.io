Episode 1: New World Order

Rescue in the sky

On a military mission, Sam flies after terrorist Batroc's aircraft and rescues a hostage.

The shield goes to a museum

Sam donates Steve's shield to a museum, saying it belongs to Steve.

Bucky's amends

In therapy, Bucky tries to make amends to the people he hurt while brainwashed. He befriends the father of a young man he once killed, but can't bring himself to tell him.

Sam's family

In Louisiana, Sam tries to help his sister Sarah save the family boat, but the bank won't give them a loan.

A new Captain America

On TV, the government unveils a new Captain America. Holding the shield is a soldier Sam doesn't know: John Walker.

Episode 2: The Star-Spangled Man

Fight on the trucks

Sam and Bucky chase the Flag Smashers in Germany. Fighting on top of trucks, they realize their foes all have super strength. Walker joins in, but they're outmatched.

Isaiah Bradley

Bucky takes Sam to see an old man, Isaiah, a Black soldier secretly given the serum long ago, then imprisoned and experimented on for 30 years.

Going to Zemo

To find out where the serum came from, Bucky suggests asking the imprisoned Zemo for help.

Episode 3: Power Broker

Zemo breaks out

Bucky breaks Zemo out of prison, and the three travel on Zemo's private jet.

Madripoor

In the criminal haven of Madripoor, they go undercover to find the serum's source, and run into Sharon Carter, now a fugitive.

The serum's creator

They find the doctor who recreated the serum, but Zemo shoots him.

A Wakandan warrior

The Wakandan warrior Ayo arrives, hunting for Zemo.

Episode 4: The Whole World Is Watching

Karli's cause

Karli's group is fighting for people displaced when the vanished returned. Sam thinks they might be able to talk.

The last vial

Zemo destroys nearly all the remaining serum, but one vial ends up in Walker's hands.

Something unforgivable

Walker takes the serum. When his partner is killed, he kills a man with the shield in front of a crowd. Images of him holding the bloody shield spread worldwide.

Episode 5: Truth

Taking back the shield

Sam and Bucky fight Walker and take the shield back. Walker is stripped of the title.

Isaiah's words

Sam talks with Isaiah again, who insists the country will never accept a Black Captain America.

Training at home

Back home, Sam fixes the family boat with neighbors' help and practices with the shield. Bucky brings him a case from Wakanda.

Valentina

A mysterious woman named Valentina visits Walker with a job offer.

Episode 6: One World, One People

The new Captain America

Karli's group attacks a council meeting in New York. Sam arrives with the shield, wearing a new suit and wings made in Wakanda.

Helicopter rescue

Sam saves hostages being carried off by helicopter.

Karli's end

Sam tries to reach Karli, but she's shot and killed, by Sharon.

Sam's speech

Sam speaks to the press and officials, urging them to listen to the people.

Afterward

Bucky finally tells the grieving father the truth. Sam has Isaiah honored at the museum and celebrates with his family as the boat sets out.

Mid-credits

Pardoned, Sharon returns to government work. But secretly, she's the Power Broker, ruler of the underworld.
