Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Who is Laura?

A girl created by the company from Logan's DNA. Like him, she has claws and a healing factor.

What happened to Charles?

In old age, a brain disease has left him unable to control his powers. His seizures paralyze everyone around him.

How does it fit with the other X-Men films?

It's set in the far future of Fox's X-Men world, though the exact connections are left vague.

Easy-to-miss details

X-Men comics

Laura carries X-Men comic books and believes Eden is real because of them.

Shane

Scenes and lines from the classic Western Shane appear at key moments.

The cross and the X

At the end, Laura tilts the cross on a grave to make an X.
