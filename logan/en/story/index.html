Part 1: The border, 2029

1-1Waking up in the car

2029. Not a single new mutant has been born in 25 years, and mutants are nearly gone.

Near the US–Mexico border, Logan wakes in the back of his limo to find a gang trying to steal its wheels. He tells them to stop; they shoot him.

He pops his claws and cuts them down. But his wounds now heal far more slowly than they used to, and his body is worn out.

1-2The limo driver

Logan works as a limo driver in El Paso, Texas, under his birth name. His eyes are failing, and he needs glasses to read.

The adamantium in his body has been slowly poisoning him for years. He is saving money to buy a boat and live quietly out at sea.

1-3Approached at a cemetery

While Logan waits at a cemetery to drive mourners home, a woman named Gabriela comes up to him. She knows who he really is and desperately begs for help.

Logan wants nothing to do with it and turns her down.

1-4Pierce shows up

Later, a man with a cybernetic arm, Pierce, finds Logan. He too knows who Logan really is. He says he's looking for a certain woman and asks for his help.

Logan brushes him off and leaves.

1-5The abandoned plant across the border

Logan gets hold of medicine for Charles on the sly and crosses the border into the Mexican desert. Their hideout is an abandoned smelting plant.

There, Caliban, a mutant who can sense other mutants, helps care for Charles. Sunlight burns Caliban's skin, so he can't go outside during the day.

1-6Charles in the fallen tank

Charles, now in his nineties, lives inside a huge toppled tank. A brain disease has left him unable to control his power: when he has a seizure, his mind paralyzes everyone nearby. Logan keeps the seizures down with drugs.

Years ago, one of his seizures killed several X-Men and other people.

Charles insists a new mutant has appeared, but Logan doesn't believe him.

Part 2: Gabriela and Laura

2-1Gabriela's job

Logan ends up hearing Gabriela out. She used to be a nurse for a company called Transigen.

She asks him to drive her and an 11-year-old girl, Laura, to a place called Eden in North Dakota, and offers him a lot of money. Laura says nothing at all.

Logan needs the money for his boat, and reluctantly agrees.

2-2Gabriela is dead

When Logan comes to pick them up, Gabriela has been murdered, by Pierce. Laura is nowhere to be seen.

Logan drives back to the plant alone.

2-3Pierce at the plant

Pierce arrives at the plant and demands that Logan hand over the girl. Laura had stowed away in Logan's limo and come with him.

Pierce leads the Reavers, a squad of soldiers with cybernetic bodies.

2-4Laura's claws

Surrounded, Laura pops two claws from each hand and one from each foot and cuts through the soldiers one after another. She has the same powers as Logan.

Logan gets Charles into the limo and flees. A section of fence snagged on the front of the car takes out the Reavers chasing them on motorcycles, and Laura dives in through the sunroof of the moving car.

2-5Caliban is taken

The three of them get away, but Pierce's men capture Caliban.

Pierce tortures him with sunlight to force him to track Laura.

Part 3: Who Laura is

3-1The video on the phone

Logan and Charles watch a video left on Gabriela's phone.

At Transigen's lab in Mexico, children were made from mutant DNA and raised as weapons. But they proved hard to control, so the company switched to a new project and decided to kill them. Gabriela and other nurses helped some escape.

3-2Logan's daughter

Laura was created from Logan's DNA. Charles tells Logan she is his daughter.

The three head north to get Laura to Eden.

3-3The hotel in Oklahoma

The three stay at a casino hotel in Oklahoma City. Charles and Laura watch the old Western Shane on the room's TV.

Logan finds that Eden appears in an X-Men comic book Laura carries. He tells her comics are made up, and Eden isn't real.

3-4The hotel seizure

The Reavers arrive at the hotel. Just then, Charles has a seizure that freezes everyone in the building.

Only Logan and Laura can move. They kill the soldiers, and Logan fights his way to Charles and injects him with his medicine.

3-5Dr. Rice arrives

The three flee the hotel.

Meanwhile, Transigen's head, Dr. Rice, arrives to help Pierce.

Part 4: A night with the Munsons

4-1Horses on the road

On the highway, horses have gotten loose and a farm family's truck is stuck. Charles secretly uses his power to help round up the horses.

To thank them, the father, Will Munson, invites the three to dinner at their home.

4-2A warm dinner

Dinner with Will, his wife Kathryn and their son Nate. Charles enjoys a peaceful, family-like evening for the first time in years.

Will explains that a big corporate farm nearby keeps shutting off their water to drive them out.

4-3Trouble at the water pump

Logan goes with Will to fix the water. Men from the corporate farm show up and point a gun at them; Logan takes the gun, snaps it in half and drives them off.

4-4Charles's words

Before bed, Charles talks to Logan about how much life and family matter, then goes to sleep.

4-5X-24

That night, while Logan is away from the house, another "Logan" appears: X-24, a mindless clone Transigen grew from Logan's DNA in his prime, built as their ultimate weapon.

Mistaking X-24 for Logan, Charles confesses that he now remembers the people his seizure killed, and apologizes. X-24 stabs him in his bed with its claws.

X-24 also kills Kathryn and Nate, and captures Laura.

4-6Caliban's end

The captive Caliban is in one of Pierce's vehicles. Learning that Charles has been killed, he sets off the soldiers' grenades, killing himself and several Reavers. Pierce is only injured.

Caliban gives up his life so he can no longer be used to hunt Laura.

4-7Will's end and Charles's death

Logan returns and fights X-24, but he is no match for his younger, stronger clone. Will pins X-24 with his truck, then dies of his wounds.

Charles dies, murmuring the name of the boat he and Logan were going to buy: the Sunseeker. Logan and Laura escape with his body.

Part 5: The road north

5-1A grave by the lake

Logan buries Charles by a lake. He tries to say a few words of farewell but can't get them out.

Then the truck breaks down, and in helpless rage he smashes it with a shovel.

5-2Laura speaks

Around this time, Laura, who hasn't said a word, suddenly bursts out in rapid-fire Spanish. She can speak English too.

She refuses to give up on going to Eden in North Dakota.

5-3To a doctor

Logan finally collapses. Laura gets him to a doctor, and he partly recovers.

He insists the place in North Dakota isn't Eden, but Laura won't let it go. Logan agrees to take her there.

Part 6: Eden

6-1The children

At the spot in the North Dakota woods, there really are children waiting: Rictor and the other kids who escaped Transigen.

They are getting ready to cross the border into Canada.

6-2Rictor's serum

Rictor gives Logan a serum. It boosts his strength and healing for a while, and he's told to use only a little at a time.

6-3The adamantium bullet

Laura finds the adamantium bullet Logan has carried since he escaped the Weapon X facility. He once thought of using it to kill himself.

Logan says he won't go with the children. Laura is devastated.

6-4The children set out

In the morning, the children set off through the forest toward the border. Logan stays behind.

But the Reavers catch up and start capturing the children.

6-5The whole dose

Logan injects himself with all of the serum at once and races into the forest.

With his strength back, he and Laura kill most of the soldiers. Then the serum wears off.

Part 7: The last fight

7-1Dr. Rice's confession

Pierce holds Rictor at gunpoint. Years ago at the Weapon X facility, Logan killed Rice's father.

Then Rice reveals that no new mutants have been born in the US because Transigen spread genetically engineered crops through the food supply.

Logan, who has found a gun, shoots Rice dead and wounds Pierce.

7-2The children fight back

X-24 attacks Logan. Meanwhile, the children combine their powers to kill Pierce and the remaining soldiers.

Rictor uses his power to flip a truck onto X-24.

7-3The branch

But X-24 frees himself and impales Logan on a large tree branch, mortally wounding him.

Laura loads Logan's revolver with the adamantium bullet and shoots X-24 in the head, killing him.

7-4Logan's end

Dying, Logan tells Laura not to become the weapon they made her to be. In tears, Laura calls him Daddy.

"So this is what it feels like," Logan says, and he dies peacefully in her arms.

7-5The cross and the X

The children bury Logan in the woods and set up a wooden cross. As a eulogy, Laura recites the closing speech from Shane, the film she watched with Charles at the hotel.

Before the children head for the border, Laura tilts the cross on its side to make an "X."

Summary

The journey to protect Laura (in order)

- The start (Parts 1–2): former Transigen nurse Gabriela asks Logan to take Laura to Eden in North Dakota. Pierce kills Gabriela, and Logan reluctantly flees with Laura and Charles.

- Who Laura is (Part 3): Laura was made from Logan's DNA as a weapon, and Charles calls her Logan's daughter.

- Eden (Parts 5–6): Logan thought it was made up, but the escaped children really are gathered there.

- The end (Part 7): Logan fights to protect the children and dies telling Laura not to become a weapon. The children head for Canada.

Who died (in order)

- The would-be wheel thieves (Part 1): they shoot Logan and he cuts them down.

- Gabriela (Part 2): the former nurse who helped Laura escape. Pierce kills her while hunting Laura.

- Charles (Part 4): at the Munsons' house, X-24, whom he mistakes for Logan, stabs him in bed. He dies saying the name of the boat.

- Kathryn and Nate (Part 4): Will's wife and son, killed by X-24.

- Caliban (Part 4): so he can't be used to hunt Laura, he sets off grenades and dies with the soldiers around him.

- Will (Part 4): after pinning X-24 with his truck, he dies of his wounds.

- Dr. Rice (Part 7): after revealing why mutants stopped being born, he is shot dead by Logan.

- Pierce and the Reavers (Parts 6–7): killed by Logan, Laura and the children fighting together.

- X-24 (Part 7): after impaling Logan, he is shot in the head by Laura with the adamantium bullet.

- Logan (Part 7): dies of the wounds X-24 gave him, in Laura's arms.

My takeOpinion

Less a superhero film than a western about an old man's last journey. As an ending for Logan, I don't think it could be bettered.
