Part 1: An aging Logan

2029

2029, near the US–Mexico border. No mutants have been born in 25 years, and they're nearly gone. Logan works as a limo driver.

A failing body

Logan's healing is slowing, and the metal in his body is slowly poisoning him.

Caring for Charles

In an abandoned plant in Mexico, Logan and the mutant Caliban care for Charles, now in his nineties. A brain disease makes his seizures paralyze everyone nearby.

Part 2: The girl

Gabriela's request

A nurse named Gabriela begs Logan to take a girl, Laura, to a place in the north called Eden.

The Reavers

The Reavers, soldiers working for a company, come after Laura. Their leader, Pierce, captures Caliban.

Laura's claws

Surrounded, Laura pops claws from her hands and feet and cuts through the soldiers. She was created from Logan's DNA.

Part 3: On the run

The hotel seizure

The three stop at a casino hotel in Oklahoma. Charles has a seizure that freezes everyone, and Logan struggles to give him his medicine.

The company's secret

A video Gabriela left reveals the company was turning children into weapons.

Part 4: A night at the farm

A family dinner

The three are invited to dinner by a farm family they helped. Charles enjoys a rare peaceful evening.

X-24

That night, the company's clone of Logan, X-24, attacks and kills Charles and the family.

Caliban's end

The captive Caliban sacrifices himself with grenades.

Charles's grave

Logan buries Charles by a lake.

Part 5: Eden

The children

Logan and Laura reach Eden in North Dakota, where the other children who escaped the company are waiting.

The final battle

As the children head for the border, the soldiers and X-24 catch up. Logan fights with everything he has left.

Logan's end

Mortally wounded by X-24, Logan dies in Laura's arms, murmuring, 'So this is what it feels like.'

The cross and the X

Laura says a line from Shane as a eulogy, then tilts the cross on his grave into an X.
