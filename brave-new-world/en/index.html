StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

Part 1: The new Captain America

Five months into Ross's presidency, Sam, the new Captain America, and Joaquin, the new Falcon, stop the mercenary Sidewinder's crew from selling stolen items in Mexico.

Sam has doubts about taking on dangerous missions without super powers.

Ross invites them to a White House summit, and Sam gets Isaiah, a super soldier the government once imprisoned, invited too.

Part 2: The attack at the White House

Ross proposes a treaty to share adamantium, a metal found on Celestial Island.

Mid-speech, the song "Mr. Blue" plays, and Isaiah and four other men suddenly open fire.

Arrested, Isaiah remembers nothing. Sam, sure his friend was controlled, starts digging.

Part 3: Hunting the mastermind

Sam captures Sidewinder after an ambush and traces his phone to a secret site, Camp Echo One.

The mastermind is Samuel Sterns, a scientist Ross has kept locked up for years, who controls minds with technology and a song.

Sam and Joaquin team up with Ross's aide Ruth.

Part 4: Crisis in the Indian Ocean

Japanese and American fleets face off over Celestial Island. Ross confesses to Sam that he is dying of heart failure and has been kept alive by Sterns's pills.

Sterns takes control of American pilots to attack the Japanese fleet. Sam and Joaquin stop a war just in time, but Joaquin is badly hurt.

Part 5: Red Hulk

Sterns kills Sam's friend Dunphy, who uncovered the truth about the pills, then gives himself up.

When his ties to Sterns come out at a press conference, Ross loses control and, because of the gamma radiation built up by the pills, becomes a red Hulk.

Losing a wing in the fight, Sam reminds Ross of the cherry blossoms he saw with Betty and brings him back.

Part 6: Afterward

Isaiah is exonerated and the treaty is ratified. Ross resigns, has himself locked in the Raft, and reunites with Betty.

Sam decides to restart the Avengers and makes Joaquin his first recruit. After the credits, Sterns warns of an attack from other worlds.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why did Isaiah attack the President?

Not by choice. He'd been programmed to be controlled when he heard a certain song.

Who's the mastermind?

Samuel Sterns, the scientist from The Incredible Hulk. Exposed to Bruce's blood, he gained a super-intelligent mind, and Ross kept him locked up for years.

Why does Ross become Red Hulk?

Sterns had been secretly dosing him through his medication. When his anger peaks, he transforms.

Does Sam have super powers?

No. He never took the super-soldier serum. That he fights anyway is central to the film.

Easy-to-miss details

Betty returns

Ross's daughter Betty appears at the end.

Ruth Bat-Seraph

The President's aide Ruth was trained in the same program as the Black Widows.

Cherry blossoms

The climax takes place during Washington's cherry blossom festival, among the trees given by Japan.
