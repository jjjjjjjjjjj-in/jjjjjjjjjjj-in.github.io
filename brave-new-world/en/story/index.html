Part 1: The new Captain America

Mission in Mexico

Sam Wilson and the new Falcon, Joaquin Torres, raid a deal in Mexico to recover a stolen secret, run by the mercenary Sidewinder's crew.

A call from the President

Newly elected President Ross calls Sam and invites him to the White House. Ross says he wants to rebuild the Avengers.

Bringing Isaiah

Sam brings Isaiah Bradley, a super soldier the government once imprisoned for decades, to the event.

Part 2: The attack

Celestial Island

Ross tells world leaders he wants to share the adamantium found on Celestial Island. Japan's prime minister is among them.

A sudden attack

Mid-ceremony, a certain song plays, and Isaiah and several others suddenly open fire on the President. Sam and Joaquin protect him.

Isaiah in custody

Isaiah is arrested and says he remembers nothing. Sam is convinced his friend was controlled.

Part 3: Following the trail

Sidewinder

Sam and Joaquin track down Sidewinder, who admits someone hired him.

Ruth

The President's aide Ruth Bat-Seraph investigates too. She's a former spy trained in the same program as the Black Widows.

The mastermind

Behind it all is Samuel Sterns. Exposed to Bruce Banner's blood, he gained a brilliant mind, and Ross kept him locked away for years to exploit it.

Part 4: Crisis at sea

Battle in the Indian Ocean

American and Japanese fleets face off near Celestial Island. Sterns takes control of pilots to make them fire on both sides.

Sam and Joaquin

Sam and Joaquin fly through the battle stopping missiles and jets. Joaquin is badly hurt.

Stopping a war

Sam's desperate efforts stop the war just in time.

Part 5: Red Hulk

Sterns's plan

Sam finds Sterns's hideout. Sterns reveals he's been dosing Ross for years, so that rage will transform him.

The cherry blossom festival

At Washington's cherry blossom festival, Sterns's trap is sprung. Ross's anger boils over, and he becomes the Red Hulk.

Battle at the White House

The Red Hulk tears through Washington. Sam, with no super powers, takes him on in his new suit.

Calming the rage

Under the cherry trees, Sam talks to Ross. Thinking of his daughter Betty, Ross calms down and turns back into a man.

Part 6: Afterward

Ross's decision

Ross resigns as President and goes to the Raft prison. His daughter Betty comes to see him.

Isaiah is free

Isaiah is cleared and set free.

A new Avengers

Sam starts thinking about forming a new Avengers.

After the credits

Sam visits Sterns in the Raft. Sterns warns that danger is coming from beyond this world.
