Part 1: The new Captain America

1-1Ross becomes President

At a bar in Baltimore, Sam Wilson watches a TV broadcast of a rally for Thaddeus Ross, who has just won the presidential election.

Ross once hunted super-powered people as an Army general, and later tried to put the Avengers under government control with the Sokovia Accords.

1-2The mission in Mexico

About five months into Ross's presidency, he sends Sam, the new Captain America, and Joaquin Torres, the new Falcon, to Oaxaca, Mexico.

The mercenary Sidewinder and his crew, Serpent, are about to sell classified items they stole. Sam and Joaquin are there to stop the sale and get the items back.

1-3Fighting Serpent

Sam takes on the Serpent crew. Copperhead, one of them, underestimates Sam and gets the upper hand for a moment, but Sam turns it around and leaves him beaten and hurt.

Working together, Sam and Joaquin recover the items, but Sidewinder gets away.

1-4Joaquin and Sam

Joaquin is thrilled to have taken over Sam's old name, Falcon.

But Sam hesitates to bring him on dangerous missions, because unlike Steve, the former Captain America, neither of them has super powers.

1-5Training with Isaiah

After the mission, Sam takes Joaquin to a gym in Baltimore to meet Isaiah Bradley, a super soldier whom the government once imprisoned and experimented on for decades.

The three of them train together.

1-6An invitation from the President

Ross calls: he wants Sam and Joaquin at a summit with world leaders at the White House.

Sam agrees, on the condition that Isaiah is invited too.

Part 2: The attack at the White House

2-1The President's request

At the White House, Ross asks Sam to help him rebuild the Avengers.

He also wants the new Captain America to be the face of the treaty he is about to propose.

Overseeing Ross's security is Ruth Bat-Seraph, who, like Natasha, was trained as a Black Widow.

2-2The adamantium treaty

Ross addresses the world leaders. After the events of Eternals, the giant body of the Celestial Tiamut was left in the Indian Ocean, and it is now called Celestial Island. A new metal, adamantium, has been found there.

The items Sam recovered in Mexico were the first refined samples, stolen from a Japanese mining operation.

To avoid an arms race, Ross proposes a treaty governing how adamantium is mined and shared.

2-3Mr. Blue

As Ross speaks, an old song, "Mr. Blue," plays.

Suddenly Isaiah and four other men open fire on Ross and the other dignitaries. The room descends into chaos.

2-4Chasing Isaiah

Ruth's team subdues the shooters while Sam goes after Isaiah, who has fled.

Sam watches as Isaiah is arrested. The men come back to their senses and say they have no memory of the attack.

2-5Stay out of it

Sam returns to the White House bunker, where Ross tells him not to investigate.

The Japanese government blames Ross both for the theft of their adamantium and for the attack, and the treaty is in danger.

2-6Isaiah in prison

Sam visits Isaiah in prison. Isaiah has no memory of what he did.

Convinced his friend was being controlled, Sam decides to find the truth himself.

Part 3: Hunting the mastermind

3-1Sidewinder's ambush

After Sam leaves the prison, Sidewinder ambushes him.

Sam fights back, captures Sidewinder, and takes his phone.

3-2Where the call went

Back at their base, Sam and Joaquin trace the last call on the phone to a hidden black site in West Virginia: Camp Echo One.

3-3Ross realizes

Ross, meanwhile, realizes who is behind the attack: the scientist Samuel Sterns.

In The Incredible Hulk, during the Abomination's rampage through Harlem, Sterns was exposed to Bruce Banner's blood and gained a vastly superior intellect.

Ross locked him up at Camp Echo One, publicly blamed him for the Abomination's rampage, and promised to free him if he helped Ross win the presidency.

3-4The President's orders

Ross heads to Japan to talk to its prime minister about the treaty. On the way, Secret Service agent Leila Taylor tells him that Sam and Joaquin have gone off-grid, and Ross orders them brought in.

Then Sterns calls Ross. He admits he was behind the attack and threatens to make Ross's daughter, Betty, hate him even more.

Ross sends Ruth to Camp Echo One.

3-5Camp Echo One

Sam and Joaquin infiltrate Camp Echo One. In a lab-like room they find photos of Isaiah and the other attackers, and Ross's medical records.

Then Sterns appears, his skin turned green and his head swollen.

3-6How the mind control works

Sam and Joaquin learn how Sterns controls minds, using technology and the song "Mr. Blue" as the trigger that set Isaiah and the others in motion.

Sterns turns the base's soldiers against them, and slips away while they fight.

3-7Teaming up with Ruth

Ruth, sent by Ross, finds Sam and Joaquin at the base and helps them.

After the three escape, Sam and Joaquin are taken into custody by the FBI. But the agents fall under Sterns's control too, and the three break free.

Part 4: Crisis in the Indian Ocean

4-1A friend in Norfolk

The three go to Norfolk, Virginia, to see Sam's military friend Dennis Dunphy, who is holding Sidewinder.

Sam gives Dunphy a box of pills and asks him to look into them.

4-2What Sterns wants

Sam questions Sidewinder, who has known Sterns since his unit pulled him from the rubble in Harlem.

From what he learns, Sam works out Sterns's plan: to destroy Ross's reputation.

They also learn that Ross is on a ship in the Indian Ocean.

4-3Japan's prime minister

In Japan, Ross meets Prime Minister Ozaki, who refuses to uphold the treaty and accuses Ross of sending Serpent to steal the adamantium.

When that intel is proven true, the two countries race to claim the adamantium, and Ross leads the U.S. military to Celestial Island.

4-4Ross on the ship

Sam, Joaquin and Ruth arrive on Ross's ship to stop him from advancing on the island, now that they know Sterns is behind everything.

Ross confesses that he is dying of heart failure and that pills made by Sterns have kept him alive. He refused to free Sterns for fear the pills would stop, and that is what drove Sterns to revenge.

Ross also says he still wants to mend things with Betty.

4-5Pilots under control

Another call from Sterns leaves Ross seething.

Sterns takes control of two American pilots and sends them to attack the Japanese fleet. A war between Japan and the U.S. is about to begin.

4-6The battle in the sky

Sam and Joaquin fly out to intercept the two jets. They stop the planes and convince the Japanese to stand down.

War is averted at the last moment, but Joaquin is badly injured in the fight.

4-7A call to Betty

With the conflict over, Ross calms down. Back in Washington, he calls his estranged daughter Betty,

and the two share a heartfelt conversation.

Part 5: Red Hulk

5-1Sam and Bucky at the hospital

At Walter Reed, Sam watches over Joaquin in the treatment room, blaming himself for not taking the serum and putting his partner in danger.

His friend Bucky Barnes shows up and comforts him: Steve gave Sam the shield because of who Sam is.

5-2Dunphy's death

Investigating Ross's pills, Dunphy discovers that they have been building up gamma radiation in Ross's body, the same radiation that made Bruce Banner the Hulk.

But Sterns kills him.

5-3Sterns surrenders

When Sam steps outside the hospital for some air, Sterns appears and gives himself up.

5-4The press conference

Ross is holding a press conference at the White House when Sterns's arrest, and his connection to Ross, become public.

Hounded by reporters, Ross loses control of his anger. Because of the gamma radiation built up by the pills, he transforms into a giant red Hulk.

5-5Battle at the White House

The red Hulk tears apart part of the White House and the monuments and landscape around it.

Sam arrives to fight him, but without super powers he can't overpower him.

5-6The fight among the cherry trees

Sam draws the fight to Washington's cherry blossom trees, where Ross used to go with Betty.

Sam narrowly dodges some of the blows, but is finally caught, and one of his vibranium wings is ripped off.

5-7One last strike, and words

Using the energy stored in his remaining wing, Sam wounds the red Hulk badly, and an explosion knocks both of them out for a moment.

Injured, Sam talks to Ross and reminds him of visiting the cherry blossoms with Betty.

Ross calms down and turns back into a man.

Part 6: Afterward

6-1Isaiah cleared, treaty signed

Afterward, Isaiah is exonerated.

The adamantium treaty is ratified.

6-2Ross at the Raft

Ross takes full responsibility for his actions, resigns as President, and has himself locked up in the Raft, the prison at sea.

Sam visits him there, and so does Betty. Father and daughter are reunited.

6-3Joaquin and the Avengers

Sam goes back to the hospital to see Joaquin, who is recovering. Joaquin tells Sam how much he means to him, and Sam acknowledges him as the Falcon.

Sam says he has decided to restart and lead the Avengers, and makes Joaquin his first recruit. They talk about a new Falcon suit.

6-4After the credits

Sam visits Sterns, now imprisoned in the Raft.

Sterns tells him this is not the only world, and warns that an attack is coming from other worlds that Sam will have to defend this one against.

Summary (spoilers through the ending)

Sterns's revenge

After Harlem, Ross locked Sterns away, blamed him, and promised to free him if he helped Ross become President. But Ross broke that promise to keep the life-saving pills coming (Parts 3–4).

So Sterns used "Mr. Blue" to make Isaiah and others attack Ross (Part 2), took over pilots to nearly start a war between Japan and the U.S. (Part 4), and used the pills to build up gamma radiation so Ross would turn into a red Hulk in public (Part 5). His goal was to destroy Ross's reputation.

Where everyone ends up

- Isaiah: controlled into joining the attack, he is exonerated (Part 6).

- Ross: resigns and has himself imprisoned in the Raft, where he reunites with Betty (Part 6).

- Joaquin: badly hurt in the Indian Ocean battle, he recovers and becomes the first member of the new Avengers (Parts 4 and 6).

- Sterns: gives himself up and is held in the Raft, where he warns of an attack from other worlds (Parts 5–6).

- Sam: decides to restart and lead the Avengers (Part 6).

Who died

- Dennis Dunphy (Part 5): Sam's military friend. He discovered that Ross's pills were building up gamma radiation, and Sterns killed him.
