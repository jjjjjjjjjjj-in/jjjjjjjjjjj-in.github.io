Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

It follows Thor and The Avengers

Thor and Jane's relationship, and why Loki is locked up, make more sense if you've seen both.

Watch Thor and Loki

The estranged brothers are forced to team up again. Their back-and-forth is a highlight.

"Infinity Stones" are named for the first time

This film reveals there are several special stones in the universe, the start of a much bigger story.

There are scenes during and after the credits

The mid-credits scene leads into Guardians of the Galaxy.

My takeOpinion

It isn't highly rated, but the middle stretch where Thor and Loki team up is in a different class. How each reacts to losing their mother Frigga shows the brothers' characters clearly.

StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

Part 1: The Dark Elves and the Aether

Long ago, the Dark Elf king Malekith tried to use a force called the Aether to return the universe to darkness. The army of Bor, Odin's father, stopped him, took the Aether and hid it where no one could find it.

Malekith fled with his lieutenant Algrim and a few followers and went into a long sleep.

Part 2: Where everyone is now

Loki is locked in Asgard's dungeons for life for attacking New York. Thor and his friends bring peace back to the Nine Realms, winning the battle on Vanaheim.

But Thor's heart is with Jane on Earth. Heimdall tells him the Convergence, when the Nine Realms line up, is near.

Part 3: Jane in London

Investigating an abandoned factory in London where objects vanish and float, Jane is pulled into another world and the hidden Aether enters her body. This wakes Malekith.

Thor comes looking for Jane after she vanishes from Heimdall's sight, sees something is wrong with her, and takes her to Asgard.

Part 4: Tragedy in Asgard

Algrim, transformed into the monster Kurse, frees the prisoners in the dungeon and brings down Asgard's shield. Malekith attacks, and Thor's mother Frigga is stabbed to death by Kurse while protecting Jane.

Asgard sends Frigga off on a burning boat.

Part 5: A plan against the king

Odin means to make his stand in Asgard, but Thor decides to take Jane to the Dark Elves' world. Heimdall, Sif and the others help him, knowing it is treason.

Thor teams up with the imprisoned Loki, who knows a secret way out, to avenge their mother.

Part 6: Into the dark world

On the Dark Elves' world, Loki pretends to betray Thor so that Malekith will draw the Aether out of Jane. But Thor's lightning can't destroy it, and it enters Malekith.

Loki kills Kurse but is run through himself and goes still in Thor's arms.

Part 7: Showdown at Greenwich

Back on Earth, Thor and Jane join Dr. Selvig and head to Greenwich in London, the center of the Convergence. Thor and Malekith's fight tumbles through holes in space from world to world.

In the end, Jane's team's devices send Malekith to his own world, where his ship crushes him.

Part 8: Afterward

Thor tells Odin he won't be king and chooses Earth. But the Odin on the throne is Loki, alive and in disguise.

In the mid-credits scene, the Aether is left with the Collector. Finally, Thor returns to Jane in London.

Key terms

The Dark Elves
A race from the darkness before the universe began, who want to return it to darkness. Their leader is Malekith.
The Aether
A red, fluid-like force of enormous power. The Dark Elves want to use it to plunge the universe into darkness. It's later revealed to be an Infinity Stone.
The Convergence
Once every few thousand years, the Nine Realms line up and the boundaries between them grow thin.
The Nine Realms
Nine worlds, including Asgard and Earth (Midgard), linked like the branches of a tree.
Kurse
The monster the Dark Elf warrior Algrim becomes through a special power.
The Collector
A figure who collects rare things from across the universe. He appears mid-credits.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

What is the Aether?

A force powerful enough to return the universe to darkness. At the end it's revealed to be one of the special stones called Infinity Stones.

Did Loki really die?

It looks that way, but at the end he's on Asgard's throne disguised as Odin, one of his trademark tricks.

What happened to the real Odin?

This film doesn't say. That thread continues in Thor: Ragnarok.

Why give the Aether to the Collector?

Asgard already holds the Tesseract, and keeping two such powerful stones in one place seemed too dangerous.

Why is Dr. Selvig acting strange?

He's still shaken from having his mind controlled by Loki in The Avengers. Even so, his devices are key in the final battle.

Easy-to-miss details

Frigga and Loki

Their mother Frigga still loved Loki despite everything he'd done. She meant a lot to him.

Loki does Captain America

In one scene Loki uses magic to turn into Captain America to mock Thor.

Stan Lee's cameo

Marvel Comics legend Stan Lee appears as a patient at the same hospital as Dr. Selvig.

The final tag

After the credits, a creature from another realm left behind in London runs loose.
