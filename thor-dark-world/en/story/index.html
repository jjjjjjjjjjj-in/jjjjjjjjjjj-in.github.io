Part 1: The legend of the Dark Elves

An ancient war

Before the universe began, the Dark Elves ruled in darkness. Their king Malekith tried to use a red, fluid power called the Aether to plunge the worlds back into darkness. Odin's father Bor stopped him and hid the Aether where no one could find it. Malekith and a few followers slipped away into a long sleep.

Part 2: Where everyone is now

Loki in prison

For his attack on New York, Loki is sentenced to life in Asgard's dungeons. Only his mother Frigga visits him.

Peace in the Nine Realms

Thor and his friends restore peace across the Nine Realms. Odin wants Thor to become king, but Thor's heart is with Jane on Earth.

Jane in London

In London, Jane, still waiting for Thor, is on a date when Darcy interrupts with news of strange readings.

Part 3: The Aether

The strange factory

In an abandoned factory, objects vanish and float in midair. Jane slips through into another place and the hidden Aether flows into her body.

Malekith wakes

The Aether's release wakes Malekith from his sleep.

To Asgard

Thor finally reappears. Realizing something is wrong with Jane, he brings her to Asgard. Odin explains that the power inside her is ancient and dangerous.

Part 4: Tragedy in Asgard

The prison break

One of Malekith's followers gets into the prison disguised as a captive, transforms into a monster and breaks out. Loki points him the way.

Frigga's death

Malekith attacks the palace. Frigga takes up a sword to protect Jane and holds off Malekith, but his monstrous follower stabs her from behind.

The funeral boats

Asgard sends Frigga off on a burning boat. In his cell, Loki grieves alone.

Thor's decision

Odin plans to fortify Asgard whatever the cost. Thor defies him and decides to take Jane away, and frees Loki to help.

Part 5: The brothers' journey

The escape

With help from Heimdall, Sif and the Warriors Three, Thor, Loki and Jane steal a Dark Elf ship and escape Asgard. Loki teases Thor the whole way.

The dark world

On the Dark Elves' world, Loki pretends to betray Thor to get close to Malekith. Malekith draws the Aether out of Jane, Thor strikes, but the Aether enters Malekith.

Loki's death?

When the monstrous follower attacks Thor, Loki takes him down, but is run through himself and goes still in Thor's arms.

Part 6: Showdown at Greenwich

The Convergence

The Nine Realms align. At Greenwich in London, Malekith prepares to unleash the Aether and plunge everything into darkness. Jane and Dr. Selvig fight back with scientific gadgets.

A fight across worlds

Thor and Malekith's battle tumbles through portals from one world to another, with Mjolnir chasing Thor through each of them.

Malekith's end

Jane's devices send Malekith back to his own world, where his giant ship falls on him.

Part 7: Afterward

The throne

Thor tells Odin he won't be king. After he leaves, Odin's form shifts into Loki. Loki is alive, and sitting on the throne in disguise.

Mid-credits

Sif and Volstagg decide it's unwise to keep two Infinity Stones in one place and leave the Aether with a man called the Collector. "One down, five to go," he says.

After the credits

Thor returns to Jane in London and they kiss. Behind them, a creature that wandered in from another world during the battle is still running loose.
