Part 1: The Dark Elves and the Aether

1-1Darkness before the universe

The film opens with Odin's narration. Before the universe was born there was only darkness, and from that darkness came the Dark Elves.

Their king, Malekith, planned to use a terrible red, fluid-like force called the Aether to plunge the universe back into darkness during the Convergence, when the Nine Realms line up.

1-2War with Bor's army

On the Dark Elves' world, Svartalfheim, an Asgardian army led by Odin's father Bor battles the Elves, stops their ritual and seizes the Aether.

Seeing he has lost, Malekith flees in his ship with his lieutenant Algrim and a handful of followers, leaving many of his people behind. They go into a long sleep.

1-3The Aether is hidden

The Aether cannot be destroyed, so Bor hides it where no one will ever find it.

In time, Asgard comes to believe the Dark Elves are gone for good.

Part 2: Where everyone is now

2-1Loki on trial

Present-day Asgard. Loki, who attacked New York in The Avengers, is brought before Odin in chains, with his mother Frigga looking on.

Loki shows no remorse. Odin tells him the only reason he is still alive is Frigga, and sentences him to the dungeons for the rest of his life.

2-2The battle of Vanaheim

With the Bifrost rebuilt using the Tesseract, Thor has been bringing peace back to the Nine Realms. On Vanaheim, Sif and the Warriors Three, Volstagg, Fandral and Hogun, are fighting marauders.

Thor arrives with his hammer, Mjolnir, and smashes the marauders' strongest fighter, a huge rock creature, with a single blow. The rest surrender.

2-3Hogun stays home

Vanaheim is Hogun's homeland. Thor tells him to stay with his people, and Hogun remains behind.

The captured marauders are sent to the dungeons of Asgard.

2-4A mother's visit

Frigga visits Loki in his cell as a magical projection and brings him books. She wants him to face up to what he has done.

But Loki coldly tells her she is not his real mother, and they get nowhere.

2-5The feast and the observatory

Asgard throws a victory feast, but Thor's mind is elsewhere.

Thor slips away to the observatory of Heimdall, the gatekeeper who sees all the worlds. Heimdall says the Convergence is near and the boundaries between the realms are growing thin. Thor asks him how Jane is doing on Earth.

Part 3: Jane in London

3-1An interrupted date

London. Astrophysicist Jane, who has waited two years for Thor, is on a date with a man named Richard at a restaurant.

Her assistant Darcy bursts in with her own intern, Ian. Jane's equipment is picking up the same kind of readings they saw in New Mexico.

3-2The strange factory

The readings lead to an abandoned factory, where objects vanish and float in midair against all the laws of physics.

Wandering off alone, Jane is pulled through a rift into a dark, rocky place. There she finds the hidden Aether, and it flows into her body.

3-3Jane vanishes from Heimdall's sight

Because Jane has crossed into another world, she disappears even from Heimdall's all-seeing gaze. When Heimdall tells Thor, he heads to Earth.

Meanwhile, the Aether's release wakes Malekith and Algrim from their sleep somewhere out in space.

3-4Reunion with Thor

When Jane comes back to the factory, hours have passed and Darcy has called the police. Then Thor appears.

Jane slaps him for disappearing without a word. Thor explains that the bridge had been destroyed and that he has been fighting across the Nine Realms ever since. Jane saw on TV that he had been in New York.

3-5To Asgard

When the police try to take Jane away, an invisible force bursts out of her and throws them back.

Realizing something is wrong with her, Thor holds Jane and rides the Bifrost's beam to Asgard.

Part 4: Tragedy in Asgard

4-1What the Aether is

Healers in Asgard examine Jane. Odin is not pleased to have a mortal in Asgard.

But when the force inside her lashes out, Odin shows Thor and Jane an old book and tells them about the Dark Elves, the Aether and the Convergence. The Aether, he warns, will kill Jane, and its return foretells disaster.

4-2Algrim becomes a monster

Malekith learns that the Aether is in Asgard. Algrim takes the power of a special stone into his body and becomes Kurse, an immensely strong monster.

Kurse lets himself be captured among the Vanaheim marauders and is taken into Asgard's dungeons.

4-3The prison break

Inside the dungeon, Kurse reveals his true form, smashes the cells and frees the prisoners. Thor rushes in to fight the rioting inmates.

Passing Loki's cell, Kurse leaves him locked in. Loki tells him which way to go to get up into the palace.

4-4The shield falls

Kurse destroys the device in the palace that powers Asgard's invisible shield.

With the shield down, Malekith and the Dark Elf ships attack. Heimdall leaps onto one of the ships and brings it down single-handedly.

4-5Frigga's stand

Frigga hides Jane in her chambers. When Malekith and Kurse arrive, she takes up a sword and fights Malekith.

When Malekith reaches for Jane, she vanishes; it was an illusion cast by Frigga. Frigga refuses to tell him where Jane is.

4-6Frigga's death

Kurse stabs Frigga, and she dies, killed for protecting Jane.

Thor arrives at that moment and blasts Malekith in the face with lightning from his hammer, burning half of it. Malekith and Kurse escape in their ship. In his cell, Loki hears of his mother's death from a guard.

4-7The funeral boats

At night, Asgard gathers to send Frigga's body out on a boat over the water. Archers fire flaming arrows, and the burning boat rises into the sky.

Thor, Odin and Jane watch in silence. Loki is not there.

Part 5: A plan against the king

5-1Father against son

Odin believes that as long as Jane stays in Asgard, Malekith will come back, and he means to meet him there whatever the cost.

Thor argues they should take Jane to the Dark Elves' world and face Malekith far from Asgard, but Odin forbids it.

5-2His friends' promise

Thor secretly gathers Heimdall, Sif, Volstagg and Fandral and tells them his plan. Defying the king is treason.

Even so, they all promise to help. Heimdall, too, agrees knowing what it will cost him.

5-3Loki's cell, unmasked

Only Loki knows a secret way out of Asgard to the Dark Elves' world. Thor goes to his cell.

The cell looks calm, but that is an illusion. When it drops, the room is wrecked and Loki sits there disheveled. Thor promises him revenge for their mother, and they join forces.

5-4Loki the shapeshifter

Thor takes Loki out of his cell. Walking the palace halls, Loki uses magic to turn into various people to needle Thor, even Captain America.

Meanwhile, Heimdall distracts Odin by confessing his own treason.

5-5Jane meets Loki

Sif helps Jane slip out of the palace and join the brothers.

Jane slaps Loki across the face for what he did to New York.

Part 6: Into the dark world

6-1Stealing a Dark Elf ship

Volstagg holds off the guards while Thor, Jane and Loki steal a small Dark Elf ship left behind. Thor can barely fly it; the ship bangs through the palace and knocks the head off a statue.

Outside, Fandral stops the soldiers chasing them.

6-2The secret path

Dodging the Asgardian ships on their tail, they dive into a narrow crack between mountains. It is Loki's secret pathway, and they come out on Svartalfheim, the Dark Elves' world.

The brothers bicker the whole way. Thor warns Loki that if he betrays them, he will kill him.

6-3The gray wasteland

Svartalfheim is a gray wasteland, scarred by the ancient war. Malekith's huge ship approaches.

Thor and Loki briefly speak of their grief for their mother.

6-4Loki's betrayal?

In front of Malekith, Loki suddenly stabs Thor, kicks him down and cuts off his hand. He offers Jane to Malekith.

Malekith draws the Aether out of Jane's body, and then Thor rises, hammer in his unharmed hand. The severed hand was Loki's illusion, part of the brothers' plan to get the Aether out of Jane.

6-5The Aether cannot be destroyed

Thor blasts the floating Aether with lightning. It seems to shatter, but the pieces gather again and flow into Malekith's body.

With the Aether inside him, Malekith boards his ship and heads for Earth.

6-6Loki's last moments

Kurse throws a black hole grenade at Loki, and Thor saves him. Kurse then overpowers Thor.

Loki attacks Kurse but is run through by Kurse's blade. He has already set off one of the grenades on Kurse's belt, though, and Kurse is pulled into the black hole and vanishes.

Loki collapses in Thor's arms. When Thor says he will tell their father what Loki did, Loki answers that he didn't do it for him, and goes still.

6-7The cave portal

Thor and Jane find a portal in a nearby cave. Passing through it, they come out in London, on Earth.

Part 7: Showdown at Greenwich

7-1Dr. Selvig

In London, Jane's mentor Dr. Selvig had been shown on TV running naked around Stonehenge and was put in a hospital, still suffering from Loki's mind control in The Avengers.

Now out, he is at Jane's flat with Darcy and Ian. He had noticed the Convergence and has been studying it on his own.

7-2Planning at Jane's flat

Thor gently hangs his hammer on Jane's coat rack.

Selvig works out that the very center of the Convergence is Greenwich in London. Malekith means to release the Aether there and plunge the universe into darkness. Jane and Selvig prepare devices that can bend space to fight back.

7-3The ship lands at Greenwich

Malekith's huge ship sets down among the buildings of the Old Royal Naval College in Greenwich, and he begins preparing to unleash the Aether.

Thor takes him on while Jane and the others plant their devices around Greenwich.

7-4A fight across worlds

Because the boundaries between worlds are thin, Thor and Malekith keep falling through holes in space into other realms. They even land in icy Jotunheim, and a monster from there gets through to Earth.

Mjolnir flies after Thor from world to world. Ending up elsewhere in London, Thor takes the Underground back to Greenwich.

7-5The devices at work

Jane and Selvig's devices send the nearby Dark Elf soldiers off to other places.

The Nine Realms line up, and the Aether begins pouring out of Malekith.

7-6Malekith's end

Using the devices, Thor and Jane's team send Malekith to Svartalfheim.

His ship, no longer held up, topples toward the exhausted Thor. At the last moment, Selvig sends it to Svartalfheim too, where it falls on Malekith and crushes him. The Convergence passes, and the universe is saved.

Part 8: Afterward

8-1Thor turns down the throne

Thor returns to Asgard and stands before Odin on the throne. Odin offers him the crown.

Thor declines. He tells Odin that Loki died with honor protecting him, and is allowed to go to Earth to be with Jane.

8-2Who sits on the throne

After Thor leaves, the figure of Odin on the throne changes, and it is Loki.

Loki is alive and ruling Asgard disguised as Odin. What happened to the real Odin is not revealed in this film.

8-3Mid-credits

Sif and Volstagg bring the Aether, sealed in a container, to a man called the Collector, who lives in a place in space called Knowhere.

Asgard already holds the Tesseract, and they think keeping two Infinity Stones in one place is too dangerous, so they leave it with him. Once they are gone, the Collector says, "One down, five to go."

8-4After the credits

Thor comes back to Jane's flat in London, and they embrace and kiss.

Outside, the monster that wandered in from Jotunheim during the battle is still roaming the streets of London.

Summary (spoilers through the ending)

Where the Aether went

Long ago, Bor took it from the Dark Elves and hid it where no one could find it (Part 1). It entered Jane's body when she strayed into another world in London (Part 3), and Malekith drew it out and took it into himself on Svartalfheim (Part 6).

After Malekith fell, Sif and Volstagg left it with the Collector, since keeping it in the same place as the Tesseract seemed too dangerous (Part 8).

Thor and Loki

To stop Malekith, who killed their mother, Thor defied his father and teamed up with the imprisoned Loki (Part 5). Loki faked a betrayal to trick Malekith into drawing out the Aether, killed Kurse to save Thor, and seemed to die (Part 6).

Thor turned down the throne and chose Earth. But the Odin on the throne was Loki, alive and in disguise (Part 8).

Who died (in order)

- The Dark Elves left behind (Part 1): abandoned by the fleeing Malekith in the ancient war.

- Frigga (Part 4): fought Malekith to protect Jane and refused to reveal where she was; Kurse stabbed her to death.

- Kurse (Algrim) (Part 6): ran Loki through, but was sucked into a black hole by the grenade Loki had set off.

- Loki (Part 6): not dead. Run through by Kurse, he went still in Thor's arms, but the ending shows him on the throne disguised as Odin (Part 8).

- Malekith (Part 7): sent to Svartalfheim by the devices at Greenwich and crushed by his own ship, which was sent there too.
