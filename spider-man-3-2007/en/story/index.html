Part 1: Good times

1-1The city's favorite hero

After an opening that looks back on the first two films, the story begins.

Spider-Man is now loved all over New York. Peter is keeping up with college, and he and MJ have been together for a year. Everything seems to be going right.

1-2MJ's Broadway debut

MJ makes her Broadway debut in a lead role, and Peter watches her sing from the audience.

Meanwhile Harry, once his best friend, still believes Spider-Man, that is, Peter, killed his father Norman, and he has never stopped hating him.

1-3A meteorite in the park

That night, Peter and MJ lie in a web hammock in Central Park, looking up at the night sky.

A small meteorite lands nearby. A black, oozing creature crawls out and, unnoticed, attaches itself to Peter's moped. It rides all the way back to his apartment.

1-4Aunt May's ring

Peter visits Aunt May and tells her he plans to propose to MJ.

May gives him the engagement ring Uncle Ben once gave her.

1-5The New Goblin attacks

Riding home from May's, Peter is attacked by a masked man on a flying board. It is Harry. Using his father's strength-boosting gas and Goblin weapons, he wants to avenge Norman.

They fight furiously while flying between buildings. Peter tells him he didn't kill Norman, that Norman died by his own weapon, but Harry won't believe it.

1-6Harry loses his memory

At the end of the fight, Harry falls from his board, hits his head and is knocked out. Peter takes him to the hospital.

When Harry wakes, he has lost part of his memory of the last few years. He has forgotten Peter's secret and his quest for revenge, and welcomes Peter and MJ as his best friends again. Having also forgotten taking his father's formula, he is surprised by how quick his own reflexes have become. Peter does not tell him the truth about Norman's death.

Part 2: Sandman is born

2-1Marko on the run

Flint Marko has escaped from prison, and the police are after him.

He goes to see Emma and his sick daughter Penny at their apartment. Penny's treatment costs money. Emma tells him to leave, and then the police arrive. Marko escapes across the rooftops.

2-2A body of sand

Fleeing, Marko falls into a physics test site where a particle experiment is under way. The machine starts up, and his body is fused with sand.

Marko struggles to pull himself together out of the sand and stands up. Sandman, who can reshape his sandy body at will, is born.

2-3MJ's bad review

A newspaper runs a bad review of MJ's performance. She is crushed, but Peter can't really connect with how she feels. Instead of listening, he keeps talking about himself and Spider-Man's popularity.

2-4A rival at the Bugle

At the Daily Bugle, where Peter sells his photos, a photographer named Eddie Brock shows up.

Editor J. Jonah Jameson says there is one staff photographer job, and it will go to whoever brings in a photo proving Spider-Man is a criminal. Peter and Eddie are now competing for it.

2-5Saving Gwen

In Peter's college physics class, his lab partner is Gwen Stacy, daughter of police Captain George Stacy. She also works as a model.

While she is at a photo shoot high in a skyscraper, a runaway construction crane smashes into the building. Gwen falls, and Spider-Man catches her in mid-air.

2-6The news ticker

A big screen in the city announces that Spider-Man is to be honored by the city. As Peter looks up at it, an old man passing by (Stan Lee) remarks that one person can make a difference, and walks off.

Part 3: The real killer

3-1The ceremony and Gwen's kiss

Before a huge crowd, the city holds a ceremony for Spider-Man, and Gwen, whose life he saved, hands him the key to the city.

Playing to the crowd, Spider-Man hangs upside down and kisses Gwen, the same kind of kiss he once shared with MJ. MJ, watching from the crowd, is deeply hurt.

3-2Sandman robs an armored car

During the ceremony, Marko, now Sandman, attacks an armored car. He wants the money for his daughter.

Spider-Man rushes in and fights him, but his punches pass right through the sand. Marko escapes as a cloud of sand.

3-3The proposal that never happens

At a fancy French restaurant, Peter tries to propose to MJ, with the maître d' helping him set up the ring.

But MJ is still angry about the kiss. Peter can't see how she feels, and she walks out. The ring stays in his pocket.

3-4Captain Stacy's news

Captain Stacy comes to see Peter and Aunt May.

He tells them that the man who shot Uncle Ben was not the man they caught before, but the escaped Flint Marko; the first man, Dennis Carradine, was only his accomplice. May is devastated, and Peter is consumed by rage.

3-5The black suit

That night, still seething, Peter falls asleep in his Spider-Man suit. The black creature from the meteorite seeps into his suit while he sleeps.

He wakes on top of a building, his suit now completely black. He is stronger and faster than before, and he enjoys it.

3-6Dr. Connors's warning

Peter has his physics professor, Dr. Connors, look at a sample of the black substance.

Connors says it is a symbiote, something that needs a host to survive, and warns that it amplifies its host's traits, especially aggression.

Part 4: Peter changes

4-1The subway fight

In the black suit, Peter finds Marko in a subway station and fights him savagely.

Realizing water weakens his sand body, Peter rips open a pipe and floods him. Marko turns to mud and is washed away. Peter believes he has killed him.

4-2Aunt May's words

Peter happily tells Aunt May that Spider-Man has finished off Marko.

May isn't glad. She says Uncle Ben would never have wanted this, and that revenge is like a poison that eats you up. Peter is thrown.

4-3MJ and Harry

Fired from her show after the bad reviews and drifting apart from Peter, MJ visits Harry at his mansion. They cook together and dance to music and have a great time.

In the moment, they kiss. MJ regrets it right away and hurries out.

4-4Harry remembers

After MJ leaves, a vision of Harry's dead father Norman appears to him. Harry remembers everything: that Peter is Spider-Man, and that he wanted revenge.

The vision urges him to make Peter suffer by attacking his heart, not his body.

4-5MJ breaks up with Peter

Harry threatens MJ: if she wants Peter to live, she has to break up with him.

To protect Peter, MJ ends things with him on a bridge in the park, lying that she loves someone else. Peter is stunned.

4-6Harry at the café

Harry meets the heartbroken Peter at a café and tells him he is MJ's new man. Realizing Harry planned it to take MJ from him, Peter burns with anger.

4-7The fight at the mansion

Wearing the black suit, Peter storms the Osborn mansion and fights Harry hand to hand. He beats Harry and taunts him cruelly about his father.

When Harry throws a pumpkin bomb, Peter flings it back. It goes off by Harry's face and scars the right side of it. Peter walks away from the beaten Harry.

Part 5: Losing the black suit

5-1The faked photo

Eddie brings in a photo that seems to show Spider-Man committing a crime and lands the staff job.

But Peter recognizes it as a doctored version of a picture he took earlier, and exposes it in front of Jameson. Jameson fires Eddie and makes Peter the staff photographer.

Eddie begs Peter to let it go, since no paper in town will hire him now, but Peter coldly brushes him off.

5-2Peter struts

Under the suit's influence, Peter grows more and more full of himself. With his hair combed down and dressed in black, he struts down the street.

5-3The jazz club

Peter takes Gwen to a jazz club where MJ has started working as a singing waitress. To make MJ jealous, he dances flashily with Gwen.

Gwen realizes what he is doing, apologizes to MJ and leaves. Peter brawls with the club's bouncers, and when MJ steps in, he accidentally knocks her down.

Seeing her face on the floor, Peter realizes how much the black creature has changed him.

5-4Eddie prays

Having seen Peter with Gwen, Eddie feels he has lost everything and goes to a church. There he prays to God to kill Peter Parker.

5-5Tearing off the suit in the bell tower

Peter climbs into the bell tower of that same church and tries to tear off the black suit, but the creature won't let go.

Then the great church bell rings right beside him. Seeing that the loud clang of metal hurts the creature, he rings the bell again and again and finally rips it off his body.

5-6Venom is born

The creature falls onto Eddie, who is below the bell tower. Eddie has learned that Peter is Spider-Man.

The creature covers Eddie's body, and Venom, a monster with sharp fangs, is born.

Part 6: The final battle

6-1Venom and Sandman

Marko survived being washed away and has pulled his sand body back together. Venom finds him and proposes they kill Spider-Man together. Marko agrees.

6-2MJ is kidnapped

Venom and Marko kidnap MJ and trap her in a taxi hanging in a web high on a building under construction. It is a trap to lure Spider-Man.

Crowds and TV crews gather, and it plays out live on the news.

6-3Asking Harry for help

Peter goes to Harry's mansion and asks him to fight with him, since he can't beat both villains alone. Harry, his face scarred, refuses, and Peter leaves alone.

After he goes, the butler Bernard tells Harry the truth: when he tended Norman's body, he could see Norman had been killed by his own glider, not by Spider-Man. Harry realizes Peter was telling the truth.

6-4Peter fights alone

Peter puts on his red suit and heads to the building. He fights Venom and a giant Sandman at once, but they corner him and nearly kill him.

Then MJ, from above, drops a heavy cinder block on Venom and saves him.

6-5Harry arrives

Harry flies in on his glider and fights side by side with Peter. Together they bring down the giant Sandman and rescue MJ.

6-6Harry takes the blow

Venom knocks Harry off his glider and tries to impale Peter with its blade. Harry leaps in front of Peter and is impaled instead, badly wounded.

6-7Venom's end

Peter remembers how the bell weakened the creature. He sets up metal pipes and bangs them to make a huge clanging noise, and the creature starts to pull away from Eddie. Peter drags Eddie out.

Peter throws a pumpkin bomb at the creature. But Eddie, who can't let go of its power, runs toward it and is killed along with it in the explosion.

6-8Sandman's confession

Back to human size, Marko talks to Peter. He never meant to shoot Uncle Ben: while he was trying to take Ben's car, his partner startled him and the gun went off. Everything he has done, he says, was to help his daughter.

Peter forgives him, and Marko drifts away on the wind as sand.

6-9Harry's last moments

Peter and MJ rush to Harry's side. Peter apologizes for hurting him, and Harry forgives him. They agree they are best friends, always.

Harry dies with Peter and MJ beside him.

6-10The funeral, and one more dance

Peter and MJ attend Harry's funeral.

Some time later, Peter goes to the jazz club where MJ sings. The two dance quietly, without a word.

Summary

Peter's three foes (in order)

- Harry (Parts 1–6): attacks Peter to avenge his father and loses his memory. When it returns he uses MJ to hurt Peter, but after his butler tells him the truth, he comes to Peter's rescue in the end.

- Sandman (Parts 2–6): a fugitive who needs money for his daughter's treatment. He confesses that Uncle Ben's death was an accident, and Peter forgives him.

- The symbiote and Venom (Parts 1–6): it arrives on a meteorite and feeds Peter's anger and arrogance. After Peter tears it off with the bell, it bonds with Eddie and becomes Venom.

Peter and MJ (in order)

- Getting ready to propose (Part 1): Aunt May gives Peter Uncle Ben's ring.

- Drifting apart (Parts 2–3): he doesn't listen to her troubles, kisses Gwen at the ceremony, and never gets to propose.

- The breakup (Part 4): threatened by Harry, MJ lies and leaves Peter to protect him.

- The jazz club (Part 5): Peter knocks MJ down and decides to get rid of the black suit.

- The end (Part 6): after Harry's funeral, the two dance quietly.

Who died (in order)

- Eddie Brock (Part 6): when Peter throws a pumpkin bomb at the symbiote, Eddie, unwilling to lose its power, leaps toward it and dies with it in the blast.

- Harry (Part 6): he shields Peter from the glider blade Venom aims at him, is badly wounded, and dies with Peter and MJ beside him.

- Uncle Ben (earlier): shot in the first film. This film reveals Marko fired the shot, and that it was an accident when his partner startled him.
