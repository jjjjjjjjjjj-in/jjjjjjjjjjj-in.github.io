Key terms

The symbiote
An alien substance that arrives with a meteorite. It boosts its host's strength but makes them aggressive.
The black suit
The black Spider-Man suit formed when the symbiote bonds with Peter.
Sandman
A man fused with sand who can reshape and grow his body.
Venom
The monster born when the symbiote bonds with another human.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

What does Sandman have to do with Uncle Ben?

The film reveals that Flint Marko, Sandman, was the one who shot Uncle Ben. In the end, it turns out the gun went off by accident.

Who becomes Venom?

Eddie Brock, a rival photographer at the Bugle who blames Peter for ruining his career.

How does Peter get rid of the symbiote?

He discovers it can't stand loud sound, and uses the ringing of a church bell to tear it off.

Easy-to-miss details

The jazz club dance

Under the symbiote's influence, a cocky Peter shows off on the dance floor of a jazz club.

Gwen Stacy

Peter's classmate Gwen Stacy appears.

Sandman is born

Flint Marko's first moments as Sandman play out in a quiet scene with no dialogue.
