Part 1: Falling apart

Pizza delivery

Busy being Spider-Man, Peter is late with pizza deliveries and gets fired. He's always late for class, too.

MJ's play

MJ is starring in a play. Peter promises to come but gets caught up in a crisis and misses it. MJ starts dating astronaut John Jameson.

Harry's grudge

Harry now runs his father's company. Still convinced Spider-Man killed his father, he resents Peter for photographing him.

Part 2: Doctor Octopus

Dr. Octavius

Peter meets Otto Octavius, a scientist funded by Harry's company. Otto is devoted to his wife Rosie and kind to Peter.

The experiment fails

His public fusion demonstration spirals out of control, killing Rosie. The four thinking mechanical arms become permanently fused to his body.

The broken chip

With the chip controlling the arms destroyed, Otto, swayed by the arms, resolves to rebuild the experiment.

The bank robbery

Otto robs a bank for funding. Aunt May, who happens to be there, is taken hostage, and Spider-Man saves her.

Part 3: Spider-Man no more

Fading powers

Peter's powers start failing. He falls off walls and can't shoot webs.

The suit in the trash

Peter decides to quit and live a normal life, and throws his suit in the trash. It ends up at the Daily Bugle.

A normal life

His grades improve and he finally sees MJ's play. But crime in the city rises.

Confession to May

Peter tells Aunt May that Uncle Ben's death was his fault. She's devastated.

A hero in all of us

Later, May tells Peter there's a hero in everyone, and people need heroes.

The burning building

Hearing a child is trapped in a burning building, Peter rushes in without his powers and saves her.

Part 4: The train

Harry's deal

Otto needs tritium. Harry agrees to supply it if Otto brings him Spider-Man.

MJ is taken

Believing Peter can reach Spider-Man, Otto snatches MJ from a café where she's talking with Peter.

Powers return

Desperate to save MJ, Peter's powers come back. He reclaims his suit and becomes Spider-Man again.

The runaway train

Fighting atop a train, Otto wrecks the brakes. Spider-Man stops the train just in time by anchoring webs with all his strength.

The passengers

The passengers carry the unconscious Peter. They see his face, promise to keep his secret and hand back his mask.

Part 5: Final choices

Harry's shock

Otto delivers Spider-Man to Harry. Harry unmasks him and finds his best friend Peter.

The riverside lab

Peter storms Otto's lab, where the experiment is again spiraling out of control and threatening the city.

Otto's choice

Peter takes off his mask and reminds Otto who he really is. Otto commands his arms to obey and sinks with the reactor into the river.

MJ knows

MJ learns Peter is Spider-Man. He still pushes her away to keep her safe.

Part 6: Afterward

Father in the mirror

Harry sees a vision of his father in a mirror and discovers the Green Goblin's hidden room behind it.

The runaway bride

On the day of her wedding to John, MJ runs to Peter's apartment in her wedding dress.

Go get 'em

Hearing sirens, Peter leaps out the window, and MJ watches him go.
