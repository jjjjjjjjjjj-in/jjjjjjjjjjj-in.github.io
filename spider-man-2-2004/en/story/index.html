Part 1: A life falling apart

1-1Pizza delivery, and fired

Two years after the battle with the Green Goblin, Peter attends university while protecting the city as Spider-Man.

At his job at a pizza place, his boss Mr. Aziz gives him a big stack of pizzas to deliver on time. It's his last chance.

Peter swings across the city as Spider-Man, but arrives late, and the receptionist refuses to pay. Back at the shop, Aziz tells him he's a nice guy but just not dependable, and fires him.

1-2Short on money

Peter goes to the Daily Bugle to sell photos of Spider-Man. Editor J. Jonah Jameson, who paints Spider-Man as a menace in the paper, pays him very little and refuses him an advance.

At home, his landlord Mr. Ditkovich demands his overdue rent.

1-3Falling behind at school

At university Peter studies physics, but being Spider-Man makes him late for class and behind on his papers. Dr. Connors warns him he is close to failing.

1-4The birthday party

Aunt May throws Peter a birthday party at her house, and he sees MJ and Harry for the first time in a while.

May is struggling with money and may lose her home.

MJ is now an actress. They have feelings for each other, but Peter, afraid of putting her in danger, hides his and keeps his distance.

1-5Harry's grudge

Harry now runs the research division at his father Norman's company, Oscorp. He believes Spider-Man killed his father and hates him for it.

Since Peter always photographs Spider-Man, Harry resents that Peter won't tell him where to find him.

Harry offers to introduce Peter to Otto Octavius, a scientist Oscorp is funding. Octavius is Peter's scientific hero.

Part 2: Octavius's experiment

2-1The Octaviuses

Peter visits Octavius and meets his wife Rosie. Otto is warm and kind, and the couple are devoted to each other.

Otto tells Peter that intelligence is not a privilege but a gift, to be used for the good of mankind. He takes a liking to Peter and becomes a mentor to him.

2-2The mechanical arms

The next day, Octavius stages a public demonstration of fusion power using a fuel called tritium. Harry and Peter are in the audience. Success would give Oscorp the rights to a new energy source, and Harry has staked the company's future on it.

To handle the dangerous reactor, Otto straps on a harness with four mechanical arms. The arms have artificial intelligence, and an inhibitor chip at the back of his neck keeps them from controlling his mind.

2-3The accident

It starts well, but the reaction destabilizes. A powerful magnetic pull drags metal toward the reactor and the room begins to tear apart. Harry demands he shut it down; Otto refuses.

Rosie is killed by shattered glass. Peter becomes Spider-Man and shuts the reactor down.

Otto is knocked unconscious, the harness fused to his body and the inhibitor chip destroyed.

2-4The operating room

Otto is taken to a hospital, where surgeons prepare to saw the arms off.

Without the inhibitor chip, the arms act on their own and kill the surgical team. Otto wakes to the carnage and flees.

2-5The harbor warehouse

Otto hides in a derelict warehouse at the harbor. The arms work on his mind, urging him to try the experiment again.

Grieving Rosie and now under the arms' influence, Otto resolves to rebuild the machine.

2-6The missed play

Peter promises to see MJ in The Importance of Being Earnest, but arrives after curtain, and the usher won't let him in.

MJ sees his empty seat and is hurt. She has been dating astronaut John Jameson, the son of Peter's editor.

Part 3: Doctor Octopus

3-1At the bank

Peter goes with Aunt May to the bank to plead for a loan so she can keep her house. They are turned down.

Then Otto bursts in. He has come to rob the vault to pay for rebuilding his machine.

3-2May is taken hostage

Peter suits up and fights. Otto seizes Aunt May as a hostage.

In the course of the battle Spider-Man rescues her, but Otto escapes with the stolen money.

The Daily Bugle dubs the man with the mechanical arms "Doctor Octopus," or "Doc Ock."

3-3Harry's slap

At a party Jameson throws in honor of his son John, Peter works as the photographer.

Harry, who has been drinking heavily since the failed experiment, turns on Peter: Peter was his father's favorite, Peter protects Spider-Man to keep selling his photos, Peter even took MJ.

Then Harry slaps him in front of everyone.

3-4MJ's engagement

At the same party, John Jameson announces his engagement to MJ. Peter has to keep taking pictures.

3-5Powers fading

Peter's powers start failing. Swinging across town, his webs give out and he falls. He can't cling to walls.

Part 4: Spider-Man no more

4-1The suit in the trash

Exhausted and longing for a normal life, Peter quits being Spider-Man and leaves his suit in an alley trash can.

A garbage man finds it and brings it to Jameson at the Daily Bugle.

4-2A normal life

Free of Spider-Man, Peter starts doing well at school. He finally sees MJ's play, and their friendship begins to mend.

Meanwhile, crime in the city is rising.

4-3Confession to May

Peter tells Aunt May that Uncle Ben's death was his fault: the robber who shot Ben was the one Peter let get away.

May is shocked at first, but later forgives him.

4-4A hero in all of us

May has to give up her house. As Peter helps her move, she talks about Spider-Man.

People need heroes to give them hope, she says. Sometimes a hero has to give up what he wants most. And she believes there's a hero in all of us.

4-5The burning building

Learning a child is trapped in a burning building, Peter runs inside without his powers.

He carries the child out, but learns that another person in the building died. As Spider-Man, he might have saved them both.

Part 5: The train

5-1Harry's deal

Otto completes his new machine. He needs tritium to run it, and storms into Harry's home to demand it.

Harry offers a deal: the tritium in exchange for Spider-Man. He tells Otto to use Peter Parker, who always photographs Spider-Man, to find him.

5-2MJ's kiss

Kissing John in a way that echoes her kiss with Spider-Man, MJ realizes she doesn't love John.

5-3At the café

MJ meets Peter at a café and asks if he loves her. He lies and says no.

She asks him to kiss her. At that moment Otto attacks the café.

Otto tells Peter to find Spider-Man, and carries MJ off.

5-4Powers return

Desperate to save MJ, Peter feels his powers return. He takes his suit back from Jameson at the Daily Bugle.

5-5Clock tower to train

Spider-Man finds Otto and they fight on a clock tower, then tumble onto a moving elevated train and keep fighting.

Otto wrecks the controls, leaving the train out of control, and leaves. Unless it stops, it will run off the end of the track.

5-6Stopping the train

Standing at the front, Spider-Man fires web after web onto the buildings and holds on with all his strength.

His mask torn off, the train stops just short of the end of the track, and he passes out.

5-7The passengers

The passengers catch Peter as he falls and carry him inside. They see his face, promise to keep his secret and give his mask back.

Then Otto returns. The passengers stand in his way to protect Peter, but he tosses them aside and takes the weakened hero.

Part 6: Final choices

6-1Harry learns the truth

Otto hands Spider-Man over to Harry and leaves with the tritium.

Knife in hand, Harry pulls off the mask and finds his best friend Peter.

Harry is stunned. Peter persuades him that MJ must be saved, and Harry tells him where Otto is.

6-2The harbor lab

At the warehouse, Otto holds MJ captive and has started the new experiment. The reaction is bigger than before and again unstable, threatening to engulf the city.

Spider-Man fights Otto and stuns him with an electric shock.

6-3Otto's choice

Peter takes off his mask and tells Otto who he is. He urges Otto to fight the arms' influence and be himself again.

Coming back to himself, Otto orders the arms to obey and overpowers them.

Choosing to give his life to stop it, Otto pulls the reactor down into the water and drowns with it.

6-4MJ knows

MJ sees Peter unmasked and learns he is Spider-Man. He rescues her.

Peter admits he loves her, but says they can't be together because it would put her in danger.

6-5Father in the mirror

Harry sees his father Norman in a mirror, demanding revenge.

Unwilling to hurt his friend, Harry refuses and throws a knife that shatters the mirror. Behind it is a hidden room full of Norman's Green Goblin equipment.

6-6The runaway bride

On her wedding day, MJ runs out on the ceremony and comes to Peter's apartment in her wedding dress.

She tells him she has chosen to be with him, whatever the risk. They kiss.

6-7Go get 'em, Tiger

Police sirens sound outside. MJ tells him, "Go get 'em, Tiger."

Peter leaps out the window as Spider-Man.

Summary

Peter's choices (in order)

- The start (Part 1): being Spider-Man wrecks Peter's job, school, rent and relationship with MJ.

- Powers fade (Part 3): after Harry slaps him and he learns MJ is engaged, his powers fail.

- Quitting (Part 4): he throws away the suit and lives a normal life, but May's words and the burning building make him rethink his responsibility.

- Spider-Man again (Parts 5–6): his powers return to save MJ; he saves the train passengers and brings Otto back to himself. In the end MJ chooses to be with him.

The Harry–Otto deal

- Otto wants tritium to power his machine.

- Harry wants Spider-Man, whom he blames for his father's death.

- Harry tells Otto to use Peter; Otto kidnaps MJ to force Peter's hand, then delivers Spider-Man to Harry after the train fight. Harry unmasks him and learns the truth.

Who died (in order)

- Rosie (Part 2): Otto's wife, killed by flying glass in the demonstration accident.

- The hospital surgeons (Part 2): killed by the arms, freed of the inhibitor chip, as they try to remove them.

- A person in the burning building (Part 4): died in the building where Peter saved the child.

- Otto Octavius (Part 6): back in his right mind, he drowns with his reactor to stop it.
