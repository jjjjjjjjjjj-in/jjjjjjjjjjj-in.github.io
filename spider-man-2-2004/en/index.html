StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

Part 1: A life falling apart

Two years into being Spider-Man, Peter is fired from his pizza job, falling behind at school and behind on rent. To keep MJ safe, he hides his feelings and keeps his distance.

His best friend Harry blames Spider-Man for his father's death.

Part 2: Octavius's experiment

Scientist Otto Octavius's public experiment, funded by Harry's company, goes wrong and kills his wife Rosie. Four AI-driven mechanical arms are fused to his body.

With their inhibitor chip destroyed, the arms kill the surgeons at the hospital, and Otto, under their influence, resolves to try again.

Part 3: Doctor Octopus

Otto robs a bank for funding and takes Aunt May hostage. Spider-Man saves her, but Otto escapes.

After Harry slaps him at a party and he learns MJ is engaged, Peter's powers start to fail.

Part 4: Spider-Man no more

Peter throws away his suit and lives a normal life. He confesses to May that Uncle Ben's death was his fault.

May tells him people need heroes. After saving a child from a burning building without his powers, Peter rethinks his responsibility.

Part 5: The train

Harry offers Otto tritium in exchange for Spider-Man, and Otto kidnaps MJ.

His powers back, Peter stops a runaway train; the passengers see his face and keep his secret. Exhausted, he is captured and delivered to Harry.

Part 6: Final choices

Harry unmasks Spider-Man and finds his best friend Peter. Peter storms Otto's lab and, unmasked, reaches him.

Back in his right mind, Otto drowns with his reactor. MJ learns Peter's secret, runs from her wedding and chooses him. Harry finds his father's hidden room.

Key terms

The fusion experiment
Octavius's attempt at a new energy source, funded by Harry's company.
The mechanical arms
Four thinking arms Octavius built for his experiment.
Tritium
The rare fuel Octavius needs for his experiment.
The inhibitor chip
The part that keeps the arms from controlling Otto's mind. It's destroyed in the accident.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why does Peter lose his powers?

It's not physical. It's his doubt. When he's worn out and unsure he wants to be Spider-Man, his powers fail him.

Is Otto evil?

He's a kind scientist at heart. The accident destroyed the chip that kept his arms from influencing his mind.

What does Harry find at the end?

After a vision of his father, he finds the Green Goblin's hidden room behind a mirror in his home.

Easy-to-miss details

The suit in the trash

Peter dumping his suit in a trash can recreates a famous comic book image.

Aunt May's speech

Aunt May tells Peter there's a hero in all of us.

The train passengers

After the train stops, the passengers see Peter's face but keep his secret and return his mask.
