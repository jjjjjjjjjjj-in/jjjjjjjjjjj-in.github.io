Episode 1: Generation Why

1-1Kamala's fan video

Kamala Khan, 16, lives in Jersey City, New Jersey, and is a huge fan of the Avengers, especially Captain Marvel.

In her bedroom she narrates a video for her channel, retelling the final battle of Endgame with Captain Marvel as the star, using animated doodles and paper cutouts. Her room is packed with Captain Marvel merchandise.

1-2Failing the driving test

Kamala takes her driving test, but lost in a daydream she reverses without checking the gear and backs into the instructor's car. She fails.

Her mother, Muneeba, tells her to stop daydreaming and focus on what's in front of her. Kamala had hoped to use errands for her brother Aamir's wedding as cover to get to AvengerCon, a fan convention.

1-3School and the counselor

At school, her best friends Bruno and Nakia greet her; they had bet on how the test would go. In the hall Kamala bumps into Zoe Zimmer, a popular influencer and former friend.

The guidance counselor, Mr. Wilson, calls her in. Teachers say she doodles and stares into space in class. He tells her it's time to get serious about her future and to look in the mirror and work out who she is.

1-4Bruno's gloves, and a no from her parents

After school Kamala visits Bruno at the Circle Q convenience store where he works. Bruno, a tech whiz, has finished light-up gloves for her Captain Marvel cosplay, and they play-fight on the roof.

At home, Kamala asks her parents to let her go to AvengerCon, the first convention of its kind, with a Captain Marvel cosplay contest. They say no.

1-5The box from Grandma

A package arrives from Kamala's grandmother Sana in Karachi, Pakistan. Muneeba calls it junk and has Aamir put it away. Kamala is drawn to an old golden bangle inside.

Her parents then agree to let her go, with conditions: her father, Yusuf, drives, they stay two hours, and they wear matching costumes. Yusuf appears dressed as the Hulk, and Muneeba has a green-and-purple outfit ready for Kamala. Kamala refuses to go like that.

1-6Sneaking out

The contest asks entrants to bring something that represents their culture. At Bruno's suggestion, Kamala takes the bangle from the box.

She leaves family dinner early, climbs out her window, meets Bruno, and takes the bus to AvengerCon.

1-7Powers awaken at AvengerCon

At the convention, Zoe is also dressed as Captain Marvel.

Just before the contest Kamala puts on the bangle. It and her eyes glow, and light energy bursts from her hands. She can't control it, and the event is thrown into chaos.

When a large falling prop nearly hits Zoe, Kamala saves her with a construct made of light.

1-8Mom's anger, and Damage Control

Bruno rushes Kamala home. Muneeba is waiting, furious that Kamala lied and snuck out, and grounds her.

[Mid-credits] Agents Cleary and Deever of the government's Department of Damage Control watch footage of the AvengerCon incident and set out to find the person responsible.

Episode 2: Crushed

2-1The "Night Light" buzz

At school nobody knows about Kamala's powers. Zoe calls the mystery hero who saved her at AvengerCon "Night Light," and her story goes viral.

Zoe announces a party and invites Kamran, a British-Pakistani senior who has just transferred in. Kamala decides she's going.

2-2Training with Bruno

Only Bruno knows. He tests Kamala's powers and helps her train, and she learns to shape the light into platforms she can jump across.

Bruno concludes the power didn't come from the bangle: it was already inside her, and the bangle unlocked it.

2-3The mosque and Nakia

Kamala and Nakia go to the mosque. The women's section is run-down and far away, and they can barely hear Sheikh Abdullah.

Nominations for the mosque board open. Frustrated that the board is all men, Nakia has already put her name in.

Nakia tells Kamala about choosing to wear the hijab and how she's been seen for it: too white for some people, too ethnic for others.

2-4Zoe's party, and Kamran

Kamala, Nakia and Bruno go to Zoe's party. Kamala is told her drink has no alcohol, but it does. Police break the party up early.

Kamran gets the three of them out in his car. Over food, Kamala and Kamran bond over Bollywood and find out both their mothers are fans of the same film star. Kamran offers her driving lessons, and she goes home giddy.

After that Kamala chooses Kamran over training, and Bruno gets frustrated. He hasn't told her that he got early admission to Caltech.

2-5A vision at dinner

At a family dinner Yusuf tells the story of Grandma Sana's childhood: in 1947, during the partition of India, little Sana lost her family but found her way back to her father by following a trail of stars. Her mother, Aisha, disappeared that same night.

As he speaks the bangle glows. Kamala sees a vision of an unknown woman and faints in front of the family.

Kamala asks Muneeba about Aisha, the bangle's first owner, but she brushes it off. On the phone Sana says only that the bangle was Aisha's and won't say more.

2-6Damage Control investigates

Deever and Cleary question Zoe about her rescuer. Deever pushes for physical details, and they gather the rescuer is South Asian.

They order a sweep of South Asian communities around the New York area.

2-7A rescue on Eid

On Eid al-Adha, the community gathers at the mosque. Kamala and Bruno help Nakia campaign, and Kamala overhears aunties gossiping darkly about Aisha.

A young boy slips and is about to fall from a height. Hiding her face with her homemade costume and mask, Kamala catches him with her powers. Another vision of the woman makes her lose her grip, but she catches him again and saves him.

2-8Pursuers, and the woman from the visions

Damage Control drones and agents led by Deever chase Kamala through the alleys.

Kamran pulls up in his car and helps her escape. He introduces her to his mother: the woman from Kamala's visions.

Episode 3: Destined

3-1A cave in old India

India in the 1940s, under British rule. Najma, Aisha and their group search a crumbling cave whose walls bear the Ten Rings symbol.

They find an old bangle. When Aisha puts it on her eyes glow, and they believe it might take them home.

British soldiers arrive and the group scatters. Aisha leaves with the bangle and never returns to them.

3-2The Clandestines' story

In the present, Kamran and Najma take Kamala to their home, where she meets the others: Fariha, Aadam and Saleem.

Najma explains that they are Clandestines, called djinn on Earth, exiled from the Noor dimension and stranded here for decades. Aisha was one of them.

The bangle might send them home, and Kamala agrees to help.

3-3Bruno's warning

Excited, Kamala tells Bruno everything. He researches interdimensional travel and concludes that forcing a door open could be extremely dangerous; many people could die.

Bruno also tells her he got into Caltech and has decided to go to California.

Kamala texts Kamran that she needs time to make sure it's safe. Kamran understands, but Najma refuses to wait and decides to force Kamala's hand.

3-4Agents at the mosque

Damage Control agents walk into the mosque with their shoes on, asking about the "enhanced individual." Nakia, newly elected to the mosque board, demands a signed warrant, and they leave empty-handed.

Kamala asks Sheikh Abdullah whether someone like Night Light can be good. He tells her: "Good is not a thing you are, Kamala. It's a thing you do."

3-5Aamir and Tyesha's wedding

Aamir marries his fiancée, Tyesha. At the reception the family surprises the couple with a rehearsed Bollywood-style dance, and Tyesha's parents join in.

Then Kamran arrives to warn Kamala that his mother and the others are coming for the bangle.

3-6The fight at the venue

Kamala pulls the fire alarm to get her family and the guests out. Najma, Fariha, Aadam and Saleem attack. Kamala, Bruno, and Kamran, who sides with Kamala, fight back; Bruno is knocked flying by a chair.

The three are cornered. When Najma grabs the bangle on Kamala's arm, they share a vision of a train on the night Aisha disappeared.

3-7Arrests, and Sana's call

Damage Control arrives and arrests the Clandestines, Kamran included, letting Kamala and Bruno escape.

On the way, Nakia sees Kamala use her powers and realizes she's Night Light. She's hurt that she was left out.

Then Sana calls. She saw the train vision too, and insists that Kamala and Muneeba come to Karachi.

Episode 4: Seeing Red

4-1Family in Karachi

Kamala and Muneeba fly to Karachi, where her cousins and her grandmother Sana welcome them. There's old tension between Muneeba and Sana.

On an outing with her cousins, Kamala is turned away from the restaurant they booked because she's wearing jeans.

4-2Sana and the train

Sana shows Kamala her paintings and the only surviving photo of her father, Hasan.

When Kamala shows her the bangle, Sana says she knows Kamala is djinn: it runs in the family.

Sana tells how in 1947 she was separated from her family and followed a trail of stars to her father on a train, the same train they've both seen in visions. The bangle is trying to tell them something, she says, and Kamala must find out what.

4-3A chase from the station

The next day Kamala, masked, goes to investigate the Karachi train station from the vision. A dagger strikes the wall beside her.

A young man her age, face covered by a red scarf, attacks and chases her through Karachi. He is Kareem. Sensing the Noor in her, he mistook her for a Clandestine.

When he realizes she isn't one, he stops.

4-4The Red Daggers' hideout

Kareem leads Kamala down a secret passage to the hideout of the Red Daggers, who protect people from unseen threats.

Their leader, Waleed, explains that the Clandestines want to break the Veil that separates the Noor dimension from the human world. If it falls, their dimension would spread and take over ours.

4-5The prison break

Meanwhile in America, Najma and the others overpower their guards and break out of Damage Control's maximum-security prison.

Najma orders them to leave her son Kamran behind, because he helped Kamala.

4-6The fight through Karachi

Kamala begins training her powers with the Red Daggers. During a session, Najma, Fariha, Aadam and Saleem attack the hideout, and a chase breaks out through Karachi's crowded streets.

Waleed holds off the pursuers and kills Saleem, but Najma stabs Waleed, and he dies.

Cornered, Kamala and Kareem face Aadam, Fariha and Najma. Kareem kills Aadam.

4-7Into 1947

In her rage Najma's blade strikes Kamala's bangle. A blinding light bursts, and Kamala is thrown into a void.

She lands at a crowded train station in 1947, the year of the partition of India.

Episode 5: Time and Again

5-1Aisha comes to a village

India, 1942, under British rule. Aisha, with nowhere to go, reaches a village. Hasan, a young independence activist, gives her food and shelter.

They fall in love. Hasan shares a favorite line of Rumi: "What you seek is seeking you." They have a daughter, Sana.

5-2Najma arrives

1947, as India is about to be partitioned. As religious tensions grow, life in the village becomes hard for Hasan's Muslim family.

One night Najma, who has hunted for Aisha for years, appears at her door and demands the bangle: it's time to go home.

Aisha leaves the bangle with little Sana and tries to flee with Hasan and Sana to the new nation of Pakistan.

5-3Aisha's last moments at the station

At the packed station, Aisha reveals her true self to Hasan and lights up the bangle.

Najma appears and stabs Aisha for betraying them. In the chaos, Hasan and little Sana are separated.

Kamala, arrived from the future, finds the dying Aisha: her great-grandmother. Aisha repeats Hasan's Rumi line, asks Kamala to guide Sana, and dies.

5-4The trail of stars

Kamala pushes through the crowd, finds little Sana, and comforts her.

She creates a trail of stars and a path of light that leads Sana to her father, Hasan, aboard the departing train. They cross the border together.

Kamala realizes the light in her grandmother's story was her.

5-5The Veil opens

Kamala returns to present-day Karachi, with Kareem, Najma and Fariha nearby. The blade's strike on the bangle has opened the Veil to the Noor dimension, and anyone who touches it is destroyed.

Kamala tries to close it and can't. She shields bystanders with a wall of light and asks Kareem to get people out.

Fariha, calling out to Kamran, tries to pass through and crumbles to nothing.

5-6Najma's choice

Kamala pleads with Najma: Kamran, the son she left behind, still needs her.

Najma sends her power to Kamran, far away in America. Then she sacrifices herself to close the Veil.

5-7What Mom and Grandma saw

Unable to reach Kamala, Muneeba finds her using the family phone-location feature and rushes over with Sana.

Seeing Kamala's powers, Muneeba realizes her mother's old stories were true and accepts her daughter's gift. She picks up Kamala's broken necklace, now shaped like a lightning bolt.

Kamala says goodbye to Kareem.

5-8The blast at Bruno's

Jersey City. With nowhere else to go, Kamran turns up at Bruno's place above the Circle Q. Bruno agrees to hide him.

A Damage Control drone appears outside the window. Kamran blasts it with his new powers, and the explosion destroys the Circle Q store.

Episode 6: No Normal

6-1Bruno and Kamran on the run

With the store destroyed, Bruno and Kamran, who still can't control his powers, go on the run. Damage Control's Deever locks down Jersey City and wants Kamran taken alive.

Deever's agents also storm the mosque, demanding that Kamran be handed over.

6-2The family already knows

Kamala comes home to Jersey City. She tries to tell her family about her powers, but Muneeba has already told Yusuf, Aamir and Tyesha. They accept and support her.

Muneeba has made her a real costume, with the lightning-bolt shape of the broken necklace and the red scarf Kareem gave her in Karachi.

6-3A plan to get Kamran out

Nakia tells Kamala that Bruno's store has been destroyed and he's missing.

In costume, Kamala finds Bruno and Kamran and calls Kareem. If they get Kamran to the docks by midnight, he says, his contacts can smuggle him out of the country.

Bruno and Kamran hide in the empty high school on a Saturday. Kamala, Nakia, Aamir and Zoe join them and set traps to slow the agents down.

6-4Storming the school

Damage Control surrounds the school. Deever's superior, Cleary, orders her to stand down and not go after kids, but she ignores him and sends a team in.

Zoe posts a video begging for help, and the community gathers outside, demanding that Damage Control leave the teens alone.

Even so, the agents arrest everyone except Kamala and Kamran.

6-5Kamran's grief

Kamran learns that his mother is dead. He walks out to face Deever in front of a huge crowd, and she fires at him.

Kamala shields him with her powers and fights off the agents. Overwhelmed by grief and his uncontrolled powers, Kamran rampages and causes heavy damage.

Kamala calms him down and gets him away. When agents move in on her, the community closes around her so she can escape too.

6-6Aftermath

With public opinion turning against Damage Control, Cleary dismisses Deever for disobeying orders.

Kamran reaches Pakistan by boat and is taken in by Kareem. Kamala becomes a beloved figure in the neighborhood.

6-7The name Ms. Marvel

Yusuf explains the meaning of her name: "Kamal" means "perfect" in Arabic and "wonder" or "marvel" in Urdu. After years of hoping for a second child, that's exactly what Kamala was to him and Muneeba.

He tells her she has always been "our own little Ms. Marvel." And so Kamala takes the name Ms. Marvel.

6-8Bruno's discovery, and a swap

A week later, as he prepares to leave for Caltech, Bruno tells Kamala he examined her family's DNA to see if Aamir might have powers too. He found that Kamala alone is different from her family: there's a mutation in her genes.

[Mid-credits] Kamala, in costume, goes up to her room to do homework. The bangle glows and she vanishes. In her place appears Captain Marvel, Carol Danvers, who looks around in confusion at a room covered in her own posters.

Summary (spoilers through the finale)

Kamala's family and the bangle

- Aisha: Kamala's great-grandmother, one of the Clandestines (djinn). She left her group with the bangle they found in a cave in the 1940s, married Hasan in an Indian village, and had a daughter, Sana (Eps. 3 and 5).

- Sana: Kamala's grandmother. Lost in 1947, she followed a trail of stars back to her father. The light was made by Kamala, visiting from the future (Ep. 5).

- Kamala: the bangle unlocked power already inside her. In the end, Bruno finds a mutation in her genes that sets her apart from her family (Ep. 6).

Who died (in order)

- Aisha (Ep. 5, 1947): stabbed by Najma at the station for betraying the group and keeping the bangle. She entrusts Sana to Kamala and dies.

- Saleem (Ep. 4): a Clandestine, killed by Waleed while Waleed holds off the pursuers during the chase through Karachi.

- Waleed (Ep. 4): leader of the Red Daggers, stabbed by Najma during the same chase.

- Aadam (Ep. 4): a Clandestine, killed by Kareem when Kamala and Kareem are cornered.

- Fariha (Ep. 5): a Clandestine. She tries to cross through the open Veil to go home and crumbles away.

- Najma (Ep. 5): the Clandestines' leader. To stop the Veil from swallowing Earth, she sends her power to her son Kamran and sacrifices herself to close it.
