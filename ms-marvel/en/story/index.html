Episode 1: Generation Why

Kamala's life

Jersey City high schooler Kamala Khan is a daydreamer obsessed with Captain Marvel. She hangs out with her best friends Bruno and Nakia and makes Avengers fan videos.

AvengerCon

Kamala wants to go to AvengerCon dressed as Captain Marvel, but her parents say no.

Grandma's bangle

Kamala uses an old bangle her grandmother sent from Pakistan as part of her costume. She sneaks out to the convention, and when she puts it on, light bursts from her hands.

Episodes 2–3: The ClanDestines

Practicing

Kamala practices her powers with Bruno.

Kamran

Kamala is drawn to new student Kamran, whose mother Najma knows about the bangle.

Longing for home

Najma's group, the ClanDestines, come from another dimension and are trapped on Earth. They want to use the bangle to open a door home.

Damage Control

The government agency Damage Control starts hunting Kamala too.

Episodes 4–5: Karachi

Visiting grandma

Kamala and her mother Muneeba visit her grandmother Sana in Karachi.

The Red Daggers

In Karachi, Kamala meets Kareem of the Red Daggers, a group that fights the ClanDestines.

The 1947 train

Kamala is pulled into the past, to a train station during the 1947 partition, where she saves her young grandmother. The trail of light in the sky that day was Kamala herself.

Her great-grandmother

Her great-grandmother Aisha was one of the ClanDestines and gave her life protecting her family.

Najma's choice

Realizing the door home could swallow Earth, Najma sacrifices herself to close it.

Episode 6: No Normal

Kamran on the run

Najma's son Kamran gains powers and is hunted by Damage Control.

Battle at school

Kamala and her friends defend Kamran from Damage Control at their school, with help from their neighbors.

Ms. Marvel

Kamala's father tells her what 'marvel' means, and her mother makes her a costume. Kamala embraces being Ms. Marvel.

Bruno's discovery

Bruno discovers something different in Kamala's genes.

After the credits

The bangle glows and Kamala vanishes from her room. In her place appears Captain Marvel.
