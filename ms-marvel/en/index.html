Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Do Kamala's powers come from the bangle?

The bangle unlocks power already inside her. The finale hints that Kamala carries a mutation.

Who was Kamala's great-grandmother?

Aisha, one of the ClanDestines. That's why the women of Kamala's family are so connected to the bangle.

What happens in the final scene?

The bangle glows, and Captain Marvel suddenly appears in Kamala's room in her place. It leads into The Marvels.

Easy-to-miss details

Doodles on the city

Kamala's daydreams appear as drawings on walls and signs around town.

The name Ms. Marvel

In the finale, Kamala's father explains what the word 'marvel' means to him.
