Good to know before watchingNo spoilers

It builds on everything before it

The events of The Winter Soldier and Age of Ultron are the starting point.

Both sides have a point

Neither Tony nor Steve is simply the villain. Paying attention to each one's reasoning makes it richer.

Two new heroes

Black Panther (T'Challa) and Tom Holland's Spider-Man make their debuts.

There are scenes during and after the credits

Both lead into upcoming films.

StorySpoilers

Read the story (spoilers up to the ending)

1. A mission in 1991

In 1991, Bucky, controlled by Hydra as the Winter Soldier, attacks a car and steals what's inside.

2. The Lagos incident

The Avengers stop a terrorist, but in the process, people are killed by an explosion that Wanda's powers misdirect.

3. The Sokovia Accords

With criticism pouring in, a framework is drawn up for U.N. oversight of the Avengers. Tony, who feels responsible for Sokovia, supports it. Steve refuses.

4. The Vienna bombing

The Accords signing is bombed and the king of Wakanda is killed. Bucky is shown as the bomber, and Prince T'Challa vows revenge.

5. Two teams

The Avengers split between Steve's side, protecting Bucky, and Tony's side, following the Accords to bring him in. Tony recruits a young Spider-Man.

6. The airport battle

At a German airport, the two teams clash. Steve and Bucky escape, but Rhodey is badly injured.

7. The real mastermind

The real bomber was Zemo. Tony learns Bucky is innocent and heads to Siberia, but there Zemo shows him footage revealing Bucky killed his parents.

8. A broken team

Tony and Steve fight bitterly, and Steve leaves his shield behind as he goes with Bucky. T'Challa captures Zemo. Steve breaks his friends out of prison and sends Tony a letter.

Key terms

The Sokovia Accords
An agreement to put the Avengers under U.N. oversight. Signers can't act without U.N. approval.
The trigger words
A conditioning Hydra put in Bucky: hearing a set of words makes him obey orders.
Wakanda
An African nation with highly advanced vibranium technology. T'Challa is its prince.
The Raft
A special prison at sea for holding superpowered people.
The Siberian facility
An old Hydra base where other Winter Soldiers were kept in cryostasis.

Confusing parts, explainedSpoilers

Contains spoilers about the ending.

Why did Tony support the Accords?

He felt deeply responsible for the deaths in Sokovia in Age of Ultron and believed the Avengers needed limits.

Why did Steve refuse?

After Hydra turned out to be inside S.H.I.E.L.D. in The Winter Soldier, he doesn't trust that any institution will always do the right thing.

What was Zemo's goal?

Zemo lost his family in Sokovia. Knowing he couldn't defeat the Avengers from outside, he set out to make them tear each other apart.

Who was in the car in 1991?

Tony's parents, Howard and Maria Stark. Their deaths were thought to be an accident, but Bucky killed them.

Is Bucky to blame?

Hydra controlled his mind, so it wasn't his choice. Even so, it was impossible for Tony to accept.

Easy-to-miss details

Spider-Man's debut

Tom Holland's Spider-Man appears for the first time and steals the show at the airport, even snagging Cap's shield.

Giant-Man

In the airport fight, Ant-Man surprises everyone by growing huge.

Goodbye to Peggy

Peggy Carter passes away, and at her funeral Steve learns Sharon is her niece.

Stan Lee's cameo

Marvel Comics legend Stan Lee appears as a delivery man with a package for Tony.
