Part 1: 1991 and Lagos

1-11991: a Hydra base in Siberia

1991, a Hydra base in Siberia. Bucky is brought out of cryo-sleep.

Hydra officer Karpov reads a list of words from a red book, one by one, in Russian. By the last word, Bucky has become the Winter Soldier, an assassin who simply obeys.

His orders: intercept a certain car and take what it is carrying.

1-2A crash on a dark road

A dark road at night. A car veers off and slams into a tree.

Bucky walks up and takes a case from the trunk: it holds super-soldier serum. The film doesn't yet show clearly who was in the car.

1-3A stakeout in Lagos

The present, about a year after the battle against Ultron in Sokovia. Lagos, Nigeria.

Steve, Natasha, Sam and Wanda are tracking a crew led by Rumlow, the former Hydra agent turned mercenary. As they wait, Steve teaches Wanda to watch the people and exits around her.

1-4The lab is hit

Rumlow's crew smashes a garbage truck through the gate of an infectious diseases institute and steals a biological weapon.

The team splits up. Sam sends his little drone, Redwing, to scout inside; Wanda wraps tear gas in her power and carries it away; Natasha chases down the man running off with the weapon and gets it back.

1-5Rumlow's suicide bomb

In a market, Steve corners Rumlow, who rattles him by bringing up Bucky.

Rumlow then sets off the bomb strapped to his body, hoping to take Steve with him. Wanda wraps the blast in her power and lifts it into the sky.

But it hits a nearby building and blows out the upper floors, killing Wakandan humanitarian workers inside. Rumlow dies too. Wanda is left in shock.

Part 2: The Sokovia Accords

2-1Tony's memory projector

At MIT, Tony shows students a new device that turns memories into 3D projections.

He replays his last day with his parents, Howard and Maria, in December 1991, before they left on a trip. He regrets that he parted with his father on bad terms and never properly said goodbye.

At the end, he announces that he will fund every student's project, and the hall erupts.

2-2A grieving mother

Outside the hall, a woman approaches Tony. Her son Charlie was killed in the battle of Sokovia.

She hands Tony a photo of her son, blames him for his death, and walks away. Tony has no answer.

2-3The Avengers under fire

At the Avengers Compound, the news is full of Lagos, and some blame Wanda. Steve comforts her in her room, telling her they can't save everyone.

Vision phases through the wall to tell them Tony and Secretary Ross have arrived.

2-4Ross makes his case

Ross, now U.S. Secretary of State, shows footage of the battles in New York, Washington, Sokovia and Lagos.

He tells them 117 countries back the Sokovia Accords. Sign, and the Avengers will act only when a U.N. panel approves. Refuse, and they must retire.

He leaves, saying the Accords will be ratified in Vienna.

2-5For and against

The team splits. Rhodey and Vision are for the Accords; Sam is against.

Tony lays Charlie's photo on the table and says they need limits, weighed down by his guilt over creating Ultron.

Steve believes governments aren't always right and that they can't hand off the responsibility of choosing. Natasha thinks signing would at least keep them part of the conversation.

Then Steve gets a message: Peggy Carter has died in her sleep.

2-6Peggy's funeral

Peggy's funeral is held in London. The one who stands up to give the eulogy is Sharon, the S.H.I.E.L.D. agent who once lived next door to Steve. Only now does Steve learn she is Peggy's relative.

Sharon shares what Peggy taught her: "Compromise where you can. Where you can't, don't." Stand firm against what's wrong, even if everyone else disagrees.

Her words settle Steve's mind.

2-7Natasha and Steve

After the funeral, Natasha finds Steve. She tells him she is going to Vienna to sign.

Steve says he can't. Natasha worries about him standing alone.

2-8Zemo and Karpov

Meanwhile, a man named Helmut Zemo tracks down Karpov, the Hydra officer who sent Bucky out in 1991.

Zemo interrogates and kills him, and takes the red book containing the words that control Bucky.

Part 3: The Vienna bombing and Bucharest

3-1The Vienna conference

Vienna, Austria. A U.N. conference is about to ratify the Accords.

King T'Chaka of Wakanda speaks about his citizens who died in Lagos. Natasha talks with his son, Prince T'Challa.

3-2The bombing, and Bucky as suspect

A bomb in a vehicle parked outside goes off and tears through the building. King T'Chaka is killed.

Security footage shows Bucky. Grieving over his father, T'Challa vows to kill Bucky himself.

3-3A tip from Sharon

Sharon, now with the CIA, tells Steve and Sam that Bucky seems to be in Bucharest, Romania, and that the special forces have orders to shoot on sight.

Steve decides to find Bucky himself before anyone else does.

3-4Bucky's apartment

At a Bucharest market, Bucky buys fruit and sees a newspaper naming him as the bomber.

Steve gets into Bucky's small apartment first and finds his notebooks full of memories. When Bucky returns, he tells Steve he wasn't in Vienna and that he doesn't do that anymore.

Then special forces surround the building.

3-5Rooftops and the tunnel

Bucky fights his way past the troops and gets out with Steve. On the rooftops, T'Challa, in a black panther suit, attacks Bucky.

The chase moves into a highway tunnel: Bucky on a motorcycle, T'Challa on foot, Steve in a commandeered car, and Sam from the air.

3-6All four arrested

At the end of the tunnel, Rhodey in his War Machine armor and the police are waiting.

Steve, Sam, Bucky and T'Challa are all taken in. Steve and Sam's shield and wings are confiscated for breaking the Accords.

Part 4: Chaos in Berlin

4-1The counterterrorism center

The four are taken to a counterterrorism center in Berlin, run by the CIA's Everett Ross.

On the way, T'Challa tells Steve that the Black Panther has protected Wakanda for generations.

4-2Tony's offer

Tony asks Steve to sign the Accords, promising to do what he can for Bucky if he does.

But Steve is angered to learn that Wanda has been confined to the Compound on Tony's orders. The talk goes nowhere.

4-3Zemo poses as a psychiatrist

A psychiatrist is due to examine Bucky. The man who comes in posing as him is Zemo.

Zemo cuts the building's power and reads the words from the red book. Bucky becomes the Winter Soldier again.

Zemo orders him to report on his mission of December 16, 1991, and gets his answers.

4-4Bucky's rampage

Under control, Bucky breaks out of his cell and tears through the building. Tony, Natasha, Sharon and T'Challa each try to stop him and fail.

In the chaos, Zemo slips away.

4-5The helicopter and the river

Bucky tries to escape in a helicopter on the roof. Steve grabs it with both arms and drags it back down as it lifts off.

It crashes into the water nearby, and Steve pulls out the unconscious Bucky and gets him away. Sam slips out in the confusion.

4-6Bucky explains

In an abandoned warehouse, Bucky comes back to himself, remembering what he did.

He says the real bomber was that man, Zemo, not him. What Zemo wanted was the location of the Siberian base, where five more Winter Soldiers are kept frozen.

Steve and Sam fear Zemo means to use them. With no time to wait for permission, they decide to stop him themselves.

4-7Ross's ultimatum

Ross orders Tony to bring Steve and the others in; if he can't, others will be sent.

Tony starts gathering a team from those who backed the Accords.

Part 5: Two teams and the airport battle

5-1The kid from Queens

Queens, New York. Tony visits the home of high-schooler Peter Parker, to the surprise of his Aunt May.

In Peter's room, Tony shows him viral videos of a masked spider-powered vigilante and reveals he knows it's Peter.

Peter explains that if he has these powers and does nothing when bad things happen, it's on him. Tony takes him to Germany.

5-2Sharon's help

In Germany, Sharon secretly returns Steve's shield and Sam's wings.

As they part, Steve and Sharon kiss, while Sam and Bucky watch awkwardly from the car.

5-3Steve's team

The retired Clint Barton goes to the Compound to get Wanda out. Vision, who has been keeping watch over her, tries to stop him, but Wanda drives Vision deep down through the floors.

Sam suggests a guy he knows: Scott Lang, Ant-Man. Clint and Wanda pick up Scott and meet Steve's group at Leipzig/Halle Airport in Germany.

5-4Face-off at the airport

As Steve's group heads for the Quinjet to Siberia, Tony's team is waiting: Tony, Rhodey, Natasha, Vision and T'Challa.

Steve tries to tell them about Zemo, but Tony won't listen. At Tony's call, Spider-Man appears in a new suit and webs away Steve's shield.

5-5Heroes against heroes

A shrunken Scott joins in, and the two teams collide. Spider-Man chatters cheerfully even while trading blows with Steve.

Natasha and Clint, still friends, fight while holding back. Wanda shields her teammates and squares off against Vision.

5-6Giant-Man

To get Steve and Bucky to the jet, Scott uses his suit to grow into a giant and draw the other team's fire.

Spider-Man remembers a trick from an old movie and ties up the giant's legs with webbing. Tony and Rhodey hit him, and the giant Scott goes down.

5-7Natasha's choice

Steve and Bucky make for the Quinjet. As T'Challa chases them, Natasha stops him with her electric stingers.

Because Natasha lets them go, the two take off.

5-8Rhodey falls

Tony, Rhodey and Sam give chase in the air. Vision fires a beam at Sam but hits Rhodey by mistake.

With his suit dead, Rhodey plummets. Tony and Sam can't reach him in time, and he slams into the ground, breaking his spine.

Furious, Tony blasts Sam out of the sky.

5-9The captured

Sam, Clint, Wanda and Scott are locked up in the Raft, a special prison at sea.

T'Challa tells Ross that Natasha let Steve and Bucky go, and she has to go into hiding.

Part 6: The truth in Siberia

6-1What Tony learns

Tony digs further and finds evidence that Zemo framed Bucky.

At the Raft, he admits to Sam that he was wrong, and Sam tells him Steve and Bucky have gone to the base in Siberia.

Without telling Ross, Tony flies to Siberia alone. T'Challa secretly follows him.

6-2A truce

At the Siberian base, Tony catches up with Steve and Bucky. They brace for a fight, but Tony says he's there to stop Zemo.

The three call a truce and go inside together.

6-3Soldiers killed in their sleep

The five Winter Soldiers in their cryo-pods have already been shot dead by Zemo.

Zemo speaks to the three from behind thick protective glass. He never meant to use the soldiers at all.

6-4The 1991 footage

Zemo plays security footage from the night of December 16, 1991.

Bucky, on a motorcycle, runs the car off the road. Inside are Tony's parents, Howard and Maria. Bucky beats Howard to death as he begs for help and strangles Maria, then takes the serum and shoots out the camera.

Tony asks Steve, "Did you know?" Steve answers, "Yes."

6-5The three-way fight

In a rage, Tony attacks Bucky. Steve protects him, and it becomes a brutal two-against-one fight.

Steve says Bucky is his friend. Tony answers, "So was I."

In the fight, Tony blasts off Bucky's metal arm.

6-6The shield left behind

Battered but still standing, Steve finally drives his shield into the power source in Tony's chest and shuts down the suit.

On the floor, Tony shouts, "You don't deserve that shield! My father made that shield!"

Steve drops the shield and leaves, holding up the wounded Bucky.

6-7Zemo and T'Challa

Outside, Zemo listens to a voicemail left by his wife. T'Challa finds him.

Zemo reveals that he lost his family in the battle of Sokovia, and that he wanted to destroy the Avengers from within rather than from outside. He has succeeded.

Zemo tries to shoot himself, but T'Challa stops him. Having seen what vengeance did to this man, T'Challa lets go of his own and hands Zemo over alive.

Part 7: Separate paths

7-1Zemo in his cell

In Berlin, Zemo is locked up. Everett Ross mocks him for failing, but Zemo doesn't seem to think he did.

7-2Rhodey learns to walk again

At the Compound, Tony helps Rhodey practice walking with leg braces Tony built for him.

7-3Steve's letter

Tony receives a letter from Steve along with an old flip phone.

Steve writes that he can't agree with the Accords, that he kept quiet about Tony's parents partly to spare him, and that if Tony ever needs them, he'll be there.

7-4Breakout from the Raft

Ross calls Tony about trouble at the Raft. Tony doesn't help.

At the Raft, Steve has slipped in and is freeing Sam, Clint, Wanda and Scott.

7-5Mid-credits

Wakanda. Bucky can't trust his own mind not to be controlled again, so he chooses to go back into cryo-sleep until a cure is found.

Steve thanks T'Challa for taking them in.

7-6After the credits

Queens. Peter, sporting a black eye, dodges Aunt May's questions about the fight.

Playing with the web-shooter Tony gave him, he discovers it can project a glowing red spider signal onto the ceiling.

Summary (spoilers through the ending)

Zemo's plan

Zemo lost his family in the battle of Sokovia and blamed the Avengers (Part 6).

He took the red book from Karpov (Part 2) and framed Bucky for the Vienna bombing (Part 3). Posing as a psychiatrist, he took control of Bucky and learned the Siberian base's location and the details of the 1991 mission (Part 4).

Then he drew Tony and Steve to the base and showed Tony the footage of his parents' deaths, turning them on each other (Part 6). The Avengers broke apart from within, just as he wanted. Zemo is captured and imprisoned (Parts 6–7).

The two teams

- Steve's side (against the Accords): Steve, Bucky, Sam, Wanda, Clint, Scott. After the airport battle, Sam, Wanda, Clint and Scott are sent to the Raft, and Steve breaks them out at the end (Parts 5–7).

- Tony's side (for the Accords): Tony, Rhodey, Natasha, Vision, T'Challa, Peter. Natasha lets Steve and Bucky escape at the airport and has to go into hiding (Part 5). T'Challa learns the truth about Zemo, gives up his revenge, and in the end shelters Steve and Bucky in Wakanda (Parts 6–7).

Who died or was hurt (in order)

- Howard and Maria Stark (Parts 1 and 6): in 1991, Bucky, under Hydra's control and ordered to take the serum, ran their car off the road, beat Howard to death and strangled Maria. The footage is revealed near the end.

- Rumlow and the Wakandans (Part 1): Rumlow blew himself up trying to kill Steve. Wanda lifted the blast skyward, but it hit a nearby building, killing Wakandan humanitarian workers.

- Charlie (Part 2): the son of the mother who blames Tony. He died in the battle of Sokovia before the film begins.

- Peggy Carter (Part 2): dies in her sleep in London.

- Karpov (Part 2): Zemo interrogates and kills him to get the red book.

- King T'Chaka (Part 3): killed by Zemo's bomb at the conference in Vienna.

- Rhodey (Part 5): not killed. Vision's beam hits him by mistake; he falls and breaks his spine. He starts walking again with braces Tony built (Part 7).

- The five Winter Soldiers (Part 6): shot dead by Zemo while still frozen in the Siberian base.
