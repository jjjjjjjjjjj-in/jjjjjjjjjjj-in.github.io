Part 1: A mission in 1991

The assassin wakes

1991, a Hydra base in Siberia. Bucky is brought out of cryo-sleep, and a list of trigger words turns him into the obedient Winter Soldier. On a dark road, he attacks a car and takes something from inside.

Part 2: Heroes under fire

The Lagos incident

Lagos, Nigeria. Steve's team corners Rumlow as he tries to steal a biological weapon. Rumlow blows himself up. Wanda pushes the blast into the air, but it hits a building, killing people including aid workers from Wakanda.

A mother's words

After a speech, Tony is confronted by a woman whose son died in Sokovia. "You murdered my son."

The Sokovia Accords

Secretary Ross presents the Sokovia Accords, which would put the Avengers under the control of a U.N. panel. Guilt-ridden Tony is for it. Steve, who wants to choose for himself whom to help, is against it. The team splits.

Peggy's funeral

Then Peggy Carter dies. At the funeral, Steve learns Sharon is Peggy's niece. Inspired by her eulogy, he decides not to sign.

Part 3: Bucky is framed

The Vienna bombing

A bomb goes off at the signing ceremony in Vienna, killing King T'Chaka of Wakanda. Security footage shows Bucky. T'Chaka's son T'Challa vows revenge.

The chase in Bucharest

Steve finds Bucky hiding in Bucharest. Bucky says he didn't do it. T'Challa, in a black panther suit, joins a wild chase, and they're all arrested.

Zemo's scheme

At a facility in Germany, a man named Zemo, posing as a psychiatrist, reads Bucky the trigger words. Bucky goes on a rampage until Steve stops him. Once himself again, Bucky says more super soldiers are frozen in Siberia.

Part 4: Two teams

Steve's team

Steve gathers Sam and Bucky, plus Clint, Wanda and Scott Lang, the Ant-Man, whom Clint brings in.

Tony's team

Tony has Rhodey, Natasha, Vision and T'Challa, and visits Peter Parker, a high-school student in Queens with spider powers.

The airport battle

At an airport in Germany, the heroes clash head-on. Spider-Man snatches Steve's shield, and Ant-Man grows to giant size.

Rhodey falls

Steve and Bucky escape in a jet, helped by Natasha. Rhodey, chasing them, is hit by a stray beam from Vision and falls, badly injuring his spine.

Part 5: The truth in Siberia

The prison at sea

Sam, Clint, Wanda and Scott are locked in a prison at sea. Tony learns from Sam that Bucky was framed and goes to Siberia on his own.

Zemo's real goal

In the Siberian base, the frozen super soldiers are already dead, killed by Zemo. His real goal was to tear the Avengers apart from within.

The 1991 footage

Zemo plays a video. That night in 1991, the people Bucky killed in the car were Tony's parents, Howard and Maria. Tony asks Steve if he knew. Steve says yes.

Three-way fight

Enraged, Tony attacks Bucky, and Steve and Bucky fight Tony. Bucky's metal arm is blown off and Tony's suit is disabled. Steve drops his shield and leaves, supporting Bucky.

Zemo and T'Challa

Zemo lost his family in Sokovia. When he tries to take his own life, T'Challa stops him and hands him over to Ross.

Part 6: Afterward

Steve's letter

Tony receives a letter and an old phone from Steve, promising he'll be there if Tony ever needs him. Steve breaks his friends out of prison.

Rhodey's recovery

Tony helps Rhodey learn to walk again with new leg braces.

Mid-credits

In Wakanda, Bucky chooses to go back into cryo-sleep until his mind can be fixed. Steve thanks T'Challa for giving them refuge.

After the credits

Peter discovers that his new web-shooter, a gift from Tony, projects a glowing red spider signal.
